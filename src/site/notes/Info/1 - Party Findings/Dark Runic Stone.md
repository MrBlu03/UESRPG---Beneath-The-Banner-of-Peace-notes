# Dark Runic Stone

## Description
A **dark stone** approximately the size of a large egg or small fist, etched with **Daedric runes** that are clearly visible to those with knowledge of such script. The stone **radiates heat** when uncovered and handled. It was found inside a lockbox in the Jarl's private quarters.

## Physical Properties
- **Material**: Unknown dark stone
- **Size**: Small enough to fit in a pouch
- **Runes**: Etched with Daedric script
- **Temperature**: Radiates noticeable heat
- **Weight**: Moderate (stone-like density)

## Discovery Details

### Found By
**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** discovered the stone during her infiltration of the Jarl's Hall in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]].

### Location
- **Room**: The Jarl's private bedroom in the Clan Morvan Longhouse
- **Container**: Inside a **lockbox** on an end table
- **Security**: The room was locked and required lockpicking to access

### Discovery Circumstances
- Kirya was searching the Jarl's quarters during the Guild trade event
- The room appeared to be important sleeping quarters (double bed, fine furnishings)
- The lockbox was the only secured item in the room
- The stone was the **only item** inside the lockbox

## Significance

### Daedric Connection
- The **Daedric runes** suggest direct connection to Oblivion
- The **radiating heat** is characteristic of Daedric artifacts or materials from volcanic regions of Oblivion
- Similar to brimstone gold's warmth, but more intense and sustained

### Jarl's Involvement
- Kept in the Jarl's **most private quarters**
- Stored in a **secured lockbox** - clearly valuable or dangerous
- Presence in the Jarl's room suggests personal involvement with Daedric forces
- May explain the Jarl's strange behavior and magical speech

### Potential Uses
- Could be a **communication device** (like the Daedric speaking stone but more powerful)
- Might be a **power source** for magical effects
- Could be used for **rituals** or **summoning**
- May be connected to the Cult of Clavicus Vile
- Possibly related to "The Grand Architect"

## Party's Handling

### Retrieval
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** wrapped the stone in cloth before touching it
- Placed it in a pouch (the same one containing a rusted key found earlier)
- Did not touch it directly with bare skin
- Carefully relocked the lockbox to delay discovery

### Current Status
- **In party's possession** (carried by Kirya)
- Wrapped in cloth inside a pouch
- Not yet analyzed by magical experts
- Party discussed sending it to the Queen's court mage but decided to hold it for now

### Concerns
- **Dangerous**: Daedric artifacts can be corrupting or cursed
- **Hot**: Radiates heat even when wrapped
- **Tracked**: The Jarl will likely notice it's missing
- **Conspiracy Evidence**: Direct link between the Jarl and Daedric forces
