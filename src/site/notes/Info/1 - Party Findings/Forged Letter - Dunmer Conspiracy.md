# Forged Letter - Dunmer Conspiracy

## Basic Information
**Type:** Fabricated Evidence  
**Created By:** [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]  
**Created:** [[2 -  Sessions/Session 021\|Session 021]]  
**Purpose:** To deceive [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] and create a pretext for investigating the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]  
**Status:** Delivered to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]]; party directed to bring it to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]

## Description
A **partially burned letter** forged by **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** and **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** to create the appearance of a Dunmer conspiracy involving the Guild of the Silverroad and brimstone gold from Oblivion. The letter was deliberately made to look authentic—scorched and damaged to suggest it had been destroyed but partially survived.

## The Deception Plan ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 021\|Session 021]])

### Strategic Purpose
**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** conceived the plan:
- Lie to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] about finding anyone at the guild house
- Create forged evidence of a Dunmer conspiracy
- Use this as a pretext to investigate the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]
- Give the party a reason to "poke their nose where they don't belong"
- Deflect suspicion away from their real investigation

### Creation
**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** and **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** worked together:
- Forged a letter with convincing content
- **Partially burned** it to suggest attempted destruction
- Made it appear authentic and weathered
- Timed to match their supposed investigation at the guild house

## Letter Contents

The forged letter contained the following information:
1. **The ledger** has been sent with "the Dunmer"
2. Warning that people are being sent after them—need to be careful
3. Reference to a **Dunmer caravan** carrying the ledger
4. Mention of **brimstone gold from Oblivion**
5. Implication of the **Guild of the Silverroad's** involvement
6. Deliberately vague to raise questions while seeming genuine

### Strategic Content Choices
- **Dunmer focus**: Deflects from the party's real investigation
- **Ledger reference**: Creates a MacGuffin to chase
- **Brimstone gold mention**: Uses real information to add authenticity
- **Guild of the Silverroad**: Direct connection to get access
- **Caravan detail**: Gives a plausible delivery method

## Delivery to Darius Karo

### The Meeting ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 021\|Session 021]])
The party returned to the [[Info/Locations/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Clan Morvan Longhouse]]:
- Claimed they searched the abandoned [[Blackfoot Merchants Guild House\|Blackfoot Merchants Guild House]]
- Reported finding **no sign of Sadrith** (the Dunmer bounty target)
- Presented the forged letter as evidence found at the location
- Suggested it pointed to a Dunmer conspiracy

### Darius Karo's Reaction
- Stated he was **"very well acquainted with the guild"**
- Seemed **unconcerned** about the letter's contents
- Dismissed it as **"not his concern"**
- Easily accepted the party's story

### Durov's Intervention
**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** suggested:
- The letter should be brought to **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]** for further investigation
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] agreed this made more sense
- Redirected the party to the Trade Advocate

### Payment
- Party convinced [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] they were owed **800 gold each**
- He paid **3,200 gold total** without resistance
- Quick payment suggests either gullibility or ulterior motives

## Current Status

### Next Steps
The party has been directed to deliver the letter to **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]**:
- Trade Advocate for Guildmaster [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Jorvik_Hanison\|Jorvik_Hanison]]
- Handles sensitive guild operations
- This meeting will be extremely dangerous if the forgery is discovered

### Risk Assessment
**HIGH RISK:**
1. **If discovered as forgery:**
   - Party will be exposed as hostile agents
   - Could face arrest, assassination, or worse
   - Entire investigation compromised

2. **If accepted as genuine:**
   - Party gains access to guild operations
   - Can investigate from inside
   - [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] may reveal information while "investigating"

3. **If partially believed:**
   - [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] may test the party
   - Could set a trap
   - Might investigate the party's background

## Strategic Value

### Advantages Created
- **Access**: Legitimate reason to meet [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]
- **Cover**: Appears to be helping guild investigate threat
- **Information**: May learn guild's response to "threat"
- **Payment**: Received 3,200 gold for the deception

### Potential Complications
- The letter creates a false lead guild may pursue
- Innocent Dunmer caravans might be investigated
- Guild might tighten security, making real investigation harder
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]]'s easy acceptance is suspicious

## Connection to Real Evidence

### Truth Mixed with Fiction
The forged letter cleverly incorporates real evidence:
- **Brimstone gold** is genuinely from Oblivion (confirmed by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]])
- **Guild of the Silverroad** is genuinely involved (via [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]])
- **Ledger** references might connect to previous [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] operations
- This mixture makes the forgery more convincing

### Misdirection Elements
The false parts:
- **Dunmer conspiracy** angle (fabricated to deflect suspicion)
- **Caravan** detail (no such caravan exists)
- **The implication** that outsiders (Dunmer) are responsible rather than guild leadership

## Notes
- This is an extremely bold and risky deception
- Success depends on [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s forgery skills
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] may be much harder to fool than [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]]
- The party is walking into a meeting with guild leadership while carrying fabricated evidence
- If [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] knows the truth about the brimstone gold, he may immediately recognize the deception
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]'s suggestion to involve [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] was clever—adds another layer to the infiltration
