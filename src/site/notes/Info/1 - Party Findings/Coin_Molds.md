# Coin Molds

## Description
Multiple **coin molds** discovered inside sealed crates at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District\|Warehouse 7]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. The molds match the distinctive shape of **brimstone gold** — the Daedric-tainted currency the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] has been distributing through [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]]'s economy.

## Physical Properties
- **Type**: Casting molds used for producing metal coins
- **Shape**: Match the distinctive form of brimstone gold coins
- **Material**: Unknown (standard mold material)
- **Quantity**: Multiple molds (exact count not recorded)

## Discovery Details

### Found By
**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** and the party discovered the molds during an investigation of Warehouse 7 in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]].

### Location
- **Building**: Warehouse 7, [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District\|Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District]]
- **Container**: Sealed crates within the warehouse
- **Access**: The crates were sealed but not locked

### Discovery Circumstances
The party was investigating unusual activity in the warehouse district. The sealed crates were opened to find the molds alongside:
- Gemstones (cut and uncut)
- Chalk
- **Black candles**
- Bone, sinew, and pig hearts (listed on any manifest only as "animal parts")

## Significance

### Brimstone Gold Operation
The molds confirm an **active manufacturing or ritual process** involving brimstone gold:
- Previously, brimstone gold appeared to originate from Oblivion (warm to the touch, smells of sulfur)
- Coin molds matching their shape suggest the Guild may be producing their own copies, or that fresh batches are being prepared
- Combined with black candles and ritual animal parts (bone, sinew, hearts), the crates suggest these molds are being used in **Daedric rituals** as part of the coin production

### The Daedric Anchor Connection
A warehouse worker present during the investigation commented that the brimstone gold **"smells like Daedric anchors from the war"** — referring to the Planemeld anchors used by Molag Bal during his invasion of Tamriel. This confirms:
- The smell is recognizable to Planemeld survivors
- The Daedric origin of the gold is not merely theoretical
- Someone with Planemeld-era experience is aware of what this cargo is

### The "Temple Allocation" Ledger Entry
The guild ledger at [[Info/Locations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad_Guildhouse\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad_Guildhouse]] contains an entry: *"Special consignment — temple allocation."* There is **no temple in Kjalgrad**. This entry, combined with the ritual materials found alongside the molds, suggests:
- The molds and materials are destined for (or supplied from) a ritual site
- The "temple" may be a code name for a hidden location — possibly the **northeast marker** on Valen's map

## Party's Handling

### Durov's Action
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** paid **500 gold of his own coin** to the warehouse supervisor to hold the crates
- The supervisor accepted and then **never returned** — he may have been intercepted by Guild agents aware of the investigation
- The crates' current status is uncertain; they may have been moved

### Current Status
- Crates were paid to be held but the supervisor vanished
- Physical possession is unclear — the party did not remove the molds from the warehouse
- The Guild almost certainly became aware of the investigation (guard whispered to Valen mid-meeting)

## Theories

1. **Production Molds**: Used to cast new brimstone gold coins from raw Daedric material
2. **Ritual Casting**: The black candles, bone, and sinew suggest a ritual component to the coin production — possibly a Daedric summoning or consecration ceremony
3. **Counterfeiting**: The Guild may be producing copies of brimstone gold to flood the economy at scale without relying solely on Oblivion supply
4. **Evidence Destruction**: The molds could be part of an operation being wound down — the night transfer from Warehouse 4 to Warehouse 7 suggests things are being consolidated

## Related Evidence
- **Brimstone Gold**: The currency these molds produce; previously distributed via [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]]
- **Black Candles**: Found alongside the molds — ritual use suspected
- **Animal Parts**: Bone, sinew, pig hearts — listed as "animal parts" on manifest
- **[[Info/1 - Party Findings/Valen_Office_Intelligence\|Valen_Office_Intelligence]]**: Ledger and map from Valen's office connecting the warehouses

## Investigation Priorities
1. Determine if the crates were moved after Durov's payment was rejected/ignored
2. Identify what ritual the black candles and animal parts are used for
3. Investigate the northeast marker on Valen's map — possible ritual production site
4. Cross-reference with the "temple allocation" ledger entry
