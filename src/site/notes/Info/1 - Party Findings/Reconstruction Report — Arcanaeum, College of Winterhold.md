**To:** The Fangs of Fjelhorst
**From**: Urag gro-Shub, Master Librarian of the Arcanaeum
**Subject:** Partial Restoration of Burned Correspondence Recovered from the Adrith Velan Camp.

---

To those who entrusted me with the fire-damaged letter:

What little remained was difficult to restore, but not impossible. Several lines were recoverable through ink-lift and arcane spectral rendering. Others were irretrievable, though context allows for partial interpretation.

As before, brackets [] indicate missing words that cannot be confirmed.

---

# RESTORED PASSAGES

> **“…the trouble between Treva’s Bend and the Snow-Shods will spread if stoked properly. Let them quarrel over honor, land, and imagined slights — their pride makes the work easy.”**

> **“…your role in the Rift remains essential. Discord breeds well when guided with a patient hand.”**

> **“…our brothers labor elsewhere, but do not let that embolden you. Each task is weighed, each outcome counted.”**

> **“…the Architect sees all contributions. Deliver your part, brother Velan, and you will have earned His notice.”**

> **“…when the bargains are set in motion, and the [a—k] begins, ensure it coincides with the change of g[u]ard. No loose eyes. No delays.”**  
> _(The fragment ‘bargains’ is intact; the word beginning with ‘a’ is too damaged to confirm — could be act, ask, attack, or anything similar. Context is inconclusive.)_

> **“…you know well what failure cost [Si—]. Do not follow his example.”**

---

# ADDITIONAL NOTES

1. **ON “THE ARCHITECT”**  
    Unknown title. Capitalization suggests a figure of authority — religious, criminal, or ideological.
    
2. **THE “BARGAINS” SECTION**  
    This is the most intriguing intact word. Whatever these “bargains” are, they appear to be coordinated with some planned event (the damaged “a—k” word).  
    The phrasing implies deals, exchanges, or agreements made behind closed doors. I cannot determine whether financial, political, or otherwise.

3. **THE TONE**  
    Cold. Controlled. Whoever authored this letter commands subordinates and expects obedience.

4. **THE HANDFUL OF CONTEXTUAL IMPRESSIONS**

    - Intent to provoke disputes between settlements.
    - References to coordinated “tasks” across the hold.
    - Someone named "Si—" met a negative end.
    - The word “brother” denotes membership in a group, order, or cult.

None of this is enough to identify the sender nor the group with certainty.

---

# Closing Comments

Be mindful — when correspondences burn, often the most important words are those that _don’t_ survive the fire.  
Should you uncover more scraps, bring them intact this time. Charcoal and ash do not make pleasant company.

**—Urag gro-Shub**  
Master Librarian of the Arcanaeum  
College of Winterhold
