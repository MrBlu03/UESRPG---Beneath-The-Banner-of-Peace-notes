# Bloody Fur Cloak

## Description
An **old fur cloak** discovered in the Jarl's private quarters, heavily stained with **blood**, smelling of **soot**, and giving off a strong odor of **wet, dirty fur**. The cloak was found in a closet with a trail of blood leading from it to a nearby window.

## Physical Properties
- **Type**: Old fur cloak (likely Nordic or traditional style)
- **Condition**: Stained with blood, worn, dirty
- **Smell**: 
  - Strong soot/smoke odor
  - Wet, dirty fur smell
  - Blood
- **Location in Room**: Hidden in a closet

## Discovery Details

### Found By
**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** discovered the cloak during her infiltration of the Jarl's Hall in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]].

### Location
- **Room**: The Jarl's private bedroom in the Clan Morvan Longhouse
- **Specific Location**: Inside a closet
- **Associated Evidence**: Blood trail leading from the closet to the window

### Discovery Circumstances
- Kirya was searching the Jarl's quarters during the Guild trade event
- Found blood trail on the floor leading from closet to window
- Inside the closet: the bloody fur cloak
- Blood smudges on the wall near the window
- Evidence suggests something (or someone) was moved from the closet to the window

## Significance

### Evidence of Violence
- **Blood stains** indicate recent or past violence
- The amount of blood suggests injury or death
- The trail from closet to window suggests concealment or disposal of... something

### Soot Connection
- **Soot smell** suggests:
  - Connection to fire or burning
  - Possible connection to volcanic regions (Oblivion?)
  - Association with forges or industrial processes
  - Link to the brimstone gold operation

### The Jarl's Activities
- Kept in his **most private quarters**
- Hidden in a closet - clearly not meant to be found
- Suggests the Jarl is personally involved in violent or occult activities
- May be connected to disappearances or murders in [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]

### Possible Connections
- **Cult of Clavicus Vile**: May be related to sacrifices or rituals
- **Coiled Serpent**: Could be evidence of the Jarl's involvement
- **The Grand Architect**: May be following orders or participating in conspiracy
- **Dark Runic Stone**: Found in same room - likely connected

## The Blood Trail

### What Was Found
- **Origin**: Closet containing the fur cloak
- **Path**: Across the floor to the window
- **Window**: Blood smudges on the wall nearby
- **Destination**: Trail ends at/near the window

### Interpretations
1. **Body Disposal**: Someone was injured/killed, wrapped in the cloak, and thrown from the window
2. **Transformation**: Someone transformed and escaped through the window
3. **Ritual**: Blood was collected or spilled as part of a ritual
4. **Cleanup**: Evidence of an attempted cleanup after an incident
