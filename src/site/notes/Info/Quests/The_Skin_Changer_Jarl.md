# The Skin-Changer Jarl (Clan Morvan Subversion)

## Quest Log Summary
The party uncovered that the true Jarl of Kjalgrad had been slain and covertly replaced by a Daedric mimicry entity anchored by a dark runic stone, manipulated by Scaafin steward [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]].

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Infiltrate the Jarl's trade banquet and detect unnatural behavior from the Jarl and steward ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]).
- [x] Steal the dark runic stone from the Jarl's bedchamber ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]).
- [x] Deliver the stone to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] to confirm its identity as a Scaafin soul-anchor ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]]).
- [x] Assault the longhouse alongside [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]; burn through the steward's disguise using *Sunbeam* magic ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 028\|Session 028]]).
- [x] Capture and interrogate Darius Caro, extracting full coordinates to the Altan Mesa facility ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 029\|Session 029]]).
