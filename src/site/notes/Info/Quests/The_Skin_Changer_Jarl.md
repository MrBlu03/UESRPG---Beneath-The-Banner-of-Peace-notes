# The Skin-Changer on the Jarl's Throne

**Status**: Active (Session 026) — Degrading but still in place  
**Objective**: Identify and deal with the Mimicry Daedra currently posing as the Jarl of Kjalgrad

## Background
In [[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]], [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] recovered a **dark runic stone** from a locked box in the Jarl's private quarters. The stone was confirmed in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]] by **Milos** (the [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|Vestige]]) to be a **memory anchor** — a specific type of Daedric artifact used by **Mimicry Daedra** (skin-changers) to maintain the identity of a person they are impersonating.

> *"The ritual is... I've only seen something like this once before. This is a ritual of what we call skin-changers. Mimicry Daedra — they don't belong to a clan or a prince, which makes this difficult. But I'd assume they'll be working with the cult of Clavicus Vile in the area."*
> — Milos, Session 025

Milos's assessment: based on the Nordic mannerisms being inconsistent with someone who should have them naturally, and based on the steward being an Imperial (who would not have the same mannerism tells), **the Jarl is almost certainly a skin-changer, and the real Jarl is almost certainly dead**.

## The Memory Anchor
The **Daedric stone** stolen by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] was the skin-changer's memory anchor:
- Periodically transfers memories of the real Jarl (or a stored copy) to the skin-changer
- Allows the creature to convincingly maintain the Jarl's identity over time
- Without it, the creature **loses memories** — gaps appear in its performance

### Current State
- The stone has been missing for approximately **one week**
- The skin-changer is actively **degrading** — wrong mannerisms, small gaps
- It **knows the stone is gone** and is currently searching for it
- The cult is aware the anchor has been taken

## Known Status (Session 026)
From Taria's network (Lower Lodge):
- The skin-changer **has not left the Yarls Hall**
- It has been **more agitated than usual** — consistent with degrading memory without the anchor
- The Lower Lodge network has the building surrounded
- **No secret exits or entrances** detected
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 027\|Session 027]] Update**: Onasha set fire to the Yarls Hall (Clan Morvan Longhouse) during her escape. The current status of the skin-changer Jarl (whether it survived the fire or fled) is unknown.

## Connection to the Main Facility
The skin-changer Jarl and the main facility are part of the **same operation**. Once the facility is dealt with:
- The skin-changer's support structure (the cult, the supply chain, its handlers) will be disrupted
- It will be increasingly isolated
- Milos's plan: deal with the facility first, skin-changer second

## Immunities
Based on Milos's confirmation regarding Mimicry Daedra:
- **Immune to mind-altering magic** — Illusion spells (Calm, Frenzy, Charm) will not work
- Direct confrontation is the only viable approach
