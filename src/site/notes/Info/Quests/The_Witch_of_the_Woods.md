# The Witch of the Woods

## Quest Overview
A bounty posted by a hunter named Bjorn regarding a dangerous "witch of the woods" that has been attacking hunters and their livestock west of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. The creature uses magic and has killed several people, making it a threat to the local hunting community.

## Quest Details
**Given By:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorn_Hunter\|Bjorn]] (hunter)  
**Location:** Western woods outside [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Contact:** Bjorn at the tavern in [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Type:** Bounty/Monster hunt  
**Creature:** [[Info/Quests/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|The_Witch_of_the_Woods]] (hagravenn-like supernatural being)

## Background
Hunters operating west of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] have encountered a dangerous creature that attacks with magic. Bjorn's horse was killed by fire magic during a hunting expedition, and the creature has claimed several lives in recent days. The hunters are not equipped to deal with a magical threat and posted a bounty for adventurers to handle the problem.

## Quest Development

### [[Session 023a\|Session 023a]] - Investigation and Resolution

**Taking the Contract:**
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] found the bounty on the bounty board at [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]
- Met with Bjorn at the tavern to discuss details
- Learned the creature uses fire magic and has killed multiple people
- Received a rough map showing the creature's territorial range

**The Investigation:**
- Traveled west for approximately two hours to investigate
- Found Bjorn's dead horse with severe claw marks and a burn mark
- Located the abandoned hunter camp
- Tracked humanoid footprints northeast to a small clearing

**The Encounter:**
- Discovered the creature is a hagravenn-like being (fusion of hag and bird)
- Creature immediately attacked with frost bolts
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] took 10 damage total from two frost bolts
- Broke line of sight and called for parlay

**The Negotiation:**
- Successfully negotiated a truce with the creature
- Witch agreed to cease attacks on hunters and livestock
- In exchange: monthly offerings of spell components (hearts, eyeballs, gems)
- Established a buffer zone around the witch's territory

## Quest Status: COMPLETED (Diplomatic Resolution)

**Outcome:**
- Resolved through negotiation rather than combat
- Peaceful coexistence established between the witch and local hunters
- No further attacks on hunters or livestock (pending tribute)
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] gained a potential source of magical knowledge

**Resolution Method:**
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] convinced the witch that peaceful coexistence benefits both parties
- Explained that organized human retaliation would eventually overwhelm the creature
- Offered regular tribute in exchange for peace
- Witch tentatively agreed with a "Maybe" conditional on humans upholding their end

## Significance
This quest demonstrates that not all supernatural threats require violent solutions. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s diplomatic approach established a sustainable peace that benefits both the local hunting community and the witch. The resolution also provides [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] with a potential mentor for magical studies, showing the value of understanding and negotiation over combat.

## Future Considerations

- The witch may become a source of magical knowledge for [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]
