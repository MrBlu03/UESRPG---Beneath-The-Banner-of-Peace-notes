# Blackfeather Court Investigation

## Quest Log Summary
Throughout their journeys, the party repeatedly encountered cryptic, telepathic Daedric crows belonging to the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]]. In Kjalgrad, the crows delivered a complex riddle (*"When the Silver Gate fed the wandering road..."*) that tasked the party with tracking an unmapped path to uncover the sanctuary of their master, the Crowfather.

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Encounter the Court of Crows and receive protective black feathers in Riften ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]]).
- [x] Receive the Blackfeather riddle in Kjalgrad ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]]).
- [x] Follow the frozen [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Widows_River\|Widow's River]] south through the mountain valleys ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]]).
- [x] Locate [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Three_Brothers\|The_Three_Brothers]] standing stones on the mountain ridge ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]]).
- [x] Enter the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Barrow_of_the_Oath-Breaker_King\|Barrow_of_the_Oath-Breaker_King]] and establish contact with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]]).
