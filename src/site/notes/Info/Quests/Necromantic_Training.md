# Necromantic Training

## Quest Summary
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] has begun formal necromantic training under the tutelage of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]] in [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]]. This represents a significant step in her magical development and understanding of the darker arts.

## Quest Details

### Background
Following her experiences with failed necromancy attempts and growing interest in the art, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sought out formal instruction from the knowledgeable [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]].

### Objectives
- [ ] Study "Mortgren's First Necromantic Tome" 
- [ ] Complete initial necromantic exercises
- [ ] Progress through Mortgren's curriculum
- [ ] Develop proper understanding of necromantic principles

### Key Events
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 012a\|Session 012a]]**: 
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] approached [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]] requesting necromantic instruction
  - Mortgren agreed to teach her after confirming her motivations and dedication
  - Established formal teacher-student relationship
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] received "Mortgren's First Necromantic Tome" as introductory study material

### Quest Items
- **Mortgren's First Necromantic Tome**: Introductory text on necromantic principles and practices

### Current Status
