# Necromantic Training (Periwyn)

## Quest Log Summary
Pursuing formal mastery over life, death, and soul manipulation beyond standard school dogma, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] engaged in clandestine necromantic study under royal court mage [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]] and planar scholar [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]].

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Accept private instruction and receive a black leather necromancy grimoire from Archmagus Mortgren in Riften ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 012a\|Session 012a]]).
- [x] Study corpse dissection, bone wards, and soul-energy pathways ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 012a\|Session 012a]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]).
- [x] Receive planar instruction from The Vestige at the mountain barrow ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 030\|Session 030]]).
- [x] Master the *Bone Shards* spell after rigorous trial castings ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 030\|Session 030]]).
- [x] Successfully execute the first trial of *Weight of Death* pulse detection at the mountain lake ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 030\|Session 030]]).
- [ ] Complete remaining trials to fully master *Weight of Death*.
