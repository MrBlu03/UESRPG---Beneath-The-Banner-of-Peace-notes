# The Economic Aftermath of Tainted Gold

## Quest Log Summary
With the destruction of the Daedric counterfeit mint at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan_Mesa]], the magical bonding anchoring the 37,000 circulating brimstone Septims will begin to decay, threatening to dissolve the coins into sulfur ash and trigger a catastrophic market panic across eastern Skyrim.

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Slay [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|The_Xivilai]] and smash the counterfeit minting crucibles at Altan Mesa ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]).
- [ ] Return to Kjalgrad to observe how the destruction of the ritual affects circulating coins ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]).
- [ ] Coordinate with the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild]] and city bankers to replace vanishing currency with authentic gold.
- [ ] Mitigate public riots and commercial collapse among merchants holding worthless brimstone coin.
