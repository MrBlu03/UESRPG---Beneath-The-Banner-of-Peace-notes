# Infiltrate Guild of the Silverroad

## Quest Log Summary
Acting on guidance from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]], the party infiltrated the heavily guarded headquarters and warehouse network of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] to expose their circulation of tainted counterfeit gold and ties to the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]].

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Gather intelligence on Silverroad trade monopoly and guard bribes in Kjalgrad ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 021\|Session 021]]).
- [x] Infiltrate the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Silverroad_Guildhouse\|Silverroad_Guildhouse]] during the merchant gala ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]).
- [x] Break into [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Warehouse_Seven\|Warehouse_Seven]] using Camonna Tong disguises to seize master ledgers ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 024\|Session 024]]).
- [x] Sabotage and incinerate the guild's primary warehouse depots during the market strike ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]]).
- [x] Breach the Clan Morvan Longhouse and unmask the guild's puppet administration ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 028\|Session 028]]).
