# Track the Camonna Tong East Pass

## Quest Log Summary
During the search of the abandoned mountain camp outside Kjalgrad, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain_Irlav]] discovered heavy wagon tracks and footprints leading east toward the secluded mountain passes into Morrowind, indicating the smugglers relocated their primary supply corridor.

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Locate the abandoned mountain camp and search tents for supply manifests ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 028\|Session 028]]).
- [x] Identify fresh wagon tracks and boot prints heading east into the foothills ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 029\|Session 029]]).
- [ ] Track the caravan trail through the eastern mountain pass.
- [ ] Intercept the relocated Camonna Tong camp before they establish new border strongholds.
