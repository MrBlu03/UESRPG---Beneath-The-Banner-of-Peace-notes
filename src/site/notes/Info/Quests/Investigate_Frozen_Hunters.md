# Investigate Frozen Hunters

## Quest Log Summary
Upon arriving in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], the party was summoned by huscarl [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga_Cairn-Shield]] to investigate reports of experienced hunters freezing solid in the woods during clear weather, and the suspicious disappearance of hunter [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nilan\|Nilan]].
