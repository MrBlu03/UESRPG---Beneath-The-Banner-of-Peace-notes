# Investigate Frozen Hunters

## Quest Overview
Investigation into the mysterious deaths of hunters who were found frozen in the [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]] near [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. What initially appeared to be a supernatural hunting accident revealed itself to be part of a larger vampire conspiracy involving field testing of sun-blocking magic.

## Quest Details
**Given By:** Community concern / [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Grieving_Brother\|Grieving_Brother]]  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] and [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]]  
**Primary Contact:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]] (Huscarl)  
**Type:** Investigation and Intelligence Gathering

## Background
Rumors circulated in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] about hunters who had been mysteriously frozen while hunting in the northern forests. The incident initially appeared to be a supernatural hunting accident, with one survivor ([[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]]) reportedly having escaped the fate that befell his companions.

## Investigation Process

### Initial Information Gathering
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Grieving_Brother\|Grieving_Brother]]**: Provided initial information about the incident and directed party to official sources
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]]**: Huscarl provided official details about the supposed survivor [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]]
- **Community Sources**: General rumors and concern about the supernatural threat

### [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]]'s House Investigation
- Discovered [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]]'s true fate as vampire thrall
- Found evidence of necromantic experimentation in hidden basement
- Recovered alchemy equipment and mysterious vials
- Determined [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]] had been killed by his vampire master

### [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]] Investigation
- Located the actual site where hunters were frozen
- Found evidence of supernatural combat and territorial creature activity
- Followed necromantic emanations to [[Info/Locations/Ruined_Hunting_Lodge\|Ruined_Hunting_Lodge]]
- Discovered [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Vampire_Survivor\|Vampire_Survivor]] conducting experiments

## Key Discoveries

### Vampire Conspiracy Revealed
- Frozen hunters were victims of sun-blocking magic field test
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Vampire_Survivor\|Vampire_Survivor]] from [[Info/Locations/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] incident responsible
- Part of larger conspiracy involving [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn]]'s organization
- Alliance with [[Poisoned_Dunmer\|Poisoned_Dunmer]] and other supernatural agents

### Territorial Creature
- Unknown supernatural entity controlling [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]]
- Has injured the vampire survivor
- Driven away all natural wildlife from the area
- Represents potential ally against vampire conspiracy

### Intelligence Gathered
- Vampire conspiracy goals: spread fear, test sun-blocking magic
- Preparation for world under blocked sun with enslaved populations
- Use of [[Molag_Bal\|Molag_Bal]] narrative as misdirection
- Field agent network operating throughout region

## Quest Resolution
The investigation successfully uncovered the truth behind the frozen hunters incident, revealing it as part of a field test for supernatural weapons. The party's non-hostile approach to the vampire allowed them to gather crucial intelligence about the broader conspiracy threatening the region.
