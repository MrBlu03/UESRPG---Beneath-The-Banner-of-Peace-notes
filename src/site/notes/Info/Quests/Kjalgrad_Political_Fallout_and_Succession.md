# Kjalgrad Political Fallout & Succession

## Quest Log Summary
Following the exposure and burning of the Daedric mimicry Jarl in the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Clan_Morvan_Longhouse]] and the destruction of the Silverroad warehouses, [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] has entered a state of intense political upheaval. The party and the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Lower_Lodge_Network\|Lower_Lodge_Network]] must navigate the city's power vacuum, armed worker protests, and the response of Eastmarch crown authorities.

- [ ] Manage the arrival and investigation of royal inspectors and crown authorities from Windhelm.
