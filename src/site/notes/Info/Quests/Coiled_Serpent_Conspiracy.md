## Session 018 Updates
### House Hlaalu Connection Discovered
Interrogation of Dunmer prisoners in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] revealed:
- Smugglers worked for **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Garen_Hlaalu\|Garen_Hlaalu]]** who leads Skyrim operations
- They were **contacted by** the Coiled Serpent (not voluntary members)
- Paid **2,000 gold in advance** to transport the ledger
- **Delivery Destination**: [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjallgrad]] (the Rift)
- **Contact**: Individual wearing **Ouroboros pendant**
- **Origin of Ledger**: Received from Temple Guard-like figure in [[Info/Locations/Fultheim\|Fultheim]]
  - The contact smelled of **sulfur** (volcanic regions?)

### Harrowstorm Engine Core Revelation
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle_Benele]] revealed:
- The Ritual Orb is a **Harrowstorm Engine Core** from the [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey_Host]]
- Grey Host were vampires/werewolves planning undead hybrids
- The core is **dormant** but could theoretically be reactivated
- Now secured by [[Info/Organizations/Mages_Guild\|Mages_Guild]] and en route to [[Info/Locations/Eyevea\|Eyevea]]
