# Coiled Serpent Conspiracy

## Quest Log Summary
A multi-hold overarching investigation into the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]], a subversive occult syndicate marked by the triple Ouroboros symbol. The organization infiltrates legitimate institutions, finances rogue banditry, cooperates with Daedric cults, and floods Skyrim with tainted currency to destabilize the realm.
