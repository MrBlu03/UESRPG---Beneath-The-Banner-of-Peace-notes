# Investigate the Jarl's Dark Stone

## Quest Log Summary
While infiltrating the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Clan_Morvan_Longhouse]] during the merchant gala, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] discovered an eerie, cold runic stone inscribed with Daedric glyphs hidden inside Jarl Morvan's private chambers.

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Sneak into the Jarl's private quarters and recover the dark runic stone ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]).
- [x] Keep the stone shielded from Daedric detection in insulated pouches ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]]).
- [x] Deliver the stone to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] at the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Barrow_of_the_Oath-Breaker_King\|Barrow_of_the_Oath-Breaker_King]] ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]]).
- [x] Learn from the Vestige that the stone acts as an arcane anchor allowing a Scaafin mimic to impersonate the slain Jarl ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]]).
