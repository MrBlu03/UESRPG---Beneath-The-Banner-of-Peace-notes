# Infiltrate Greenwall

## Quest Log Summary
With the siege forces of The Companions decimated outside Fort Greenwall, Harbinger [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod_Wraithbane]] and the party coordinated an infiltration strategy to breach the fortress through forgotten Three Banners War smuggling tunnels while trebuchets staged a diversionary bombardment.
