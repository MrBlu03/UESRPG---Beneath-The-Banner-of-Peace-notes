# Cure the Priest of Stendarr (Father Aldric)

## Quest Log Summary
During the assault on the cult facility at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan_Mesa]], **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Father_Aldric\|Father_Aldric]]** (a pious Breton Priest of Stendarr and alteration dabbler hired by Periwyn) attempted to unravel the secondary entrance wards, resulting in a catastrophic arcane backlash that hurled him off the 4-meter balcony and locked him in a comatose, vegetative mental prison. Following the battle, the party transported him to the Vestige's hideout and committed to ensuring his full recovery.

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Hire [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Father_Aldric\|Father_Aldric]] at the Frost Gate Hall to assist with ward dispelling ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 030\|Session 030]]).
- [x] Catch the unconscious priest as he is flung from the balcony during the ward backlash ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 030\|Session 030]]).
- [x] Transport the comatose priest through the night to the Vestige's Dwemer ruin infirmary ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]).
- [x] [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] writes a signed letter of apology bearing her family crest and the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Knights_of_Arkay\|Knights_of_Arkay]] seal, placing it in his robes ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]).
- [x] [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] summons specialized healer mages via messenger crow to treat his shattered psyche ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]).
- [ ] Await the arrival of the healer mages and monitor Father Aldric's recovery from the mental void.
