# Woad Training (Onasha)

## Quest Log Summary
Intrigued by ancient Nordic shamanic rites, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] sought tutelage under Shaman [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] in Riften and elder Laws-speaker [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] in Falderstad to learn the sacred art of body-painting magical woads.

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Receive initial spiritual guidance and woad concepts from Shaman Adda at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall_of_Ysmir]] ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]]).
- [x] Study ancient herbal pigments and ritual binding under Laws-speaker Torik in Falderstad ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]).
- [x] Master the *Fox's Guile* and *Hunter's Scent* woad patterns, enhancing her stealth and tracking ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]).
