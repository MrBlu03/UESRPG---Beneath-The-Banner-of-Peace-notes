# Undead Werewolf Threat & Grey Host Connection

## Quest Log Summary
Following the discovery of the soul-harvesting ritual orb at Fort Greenwall, the party investigated ancient First Era lore to determine whether the ancient **[[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey_Host]]** was resurfacing in Skyrim.

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Extract the mysterious soul orb from the Greenwall ritual chamber ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 006\|Session 006]]).
- [x] Consult Archmagus Mortgren and Mages Guild texts regarding ancient Harrowstorms ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 012\|Session 012]]).
- [x] Meet Antiquarian [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle_Benele]] in Falderstad to perform arcane analysis ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]).
- [x] Confirm that the orb was manufactured by the ancient Grey Host and seal it with anti-Daedric wards ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]).
