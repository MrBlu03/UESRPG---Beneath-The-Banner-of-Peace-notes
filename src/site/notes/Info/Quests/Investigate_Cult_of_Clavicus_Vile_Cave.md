## Evidence Collected

### The Brimstone Gold
- Currency originating from **Oblivion**
- Warm to the touch
- Smells of sulfur/brimstone
- Being distributed through normal commerce in [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]] paid **far more** than items were worth
- Sample obtained by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] from merchant [[Nielfa\|Nielfa]]

### Daedric Speaking Stone
- Confiscated by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] from [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]]
- Different from normal speaking stones
- Etched with **Daedric runes**
- Used for one-way communication with cult leadership
- The entity in the projection appeared humanoid but inhuman
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 021\|Session 021]]**: Retrieved from sewers; [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] confirmed it **links to Oblivion itself**
- Connection is weak not from degradation, but because it bridges realms

### Varren's Confession & Status
- Works for [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]
- Started as simple provisioning work
- Gold was warm from the beginning
- After weeks, told to meet contact in sewers
- Mentioned **"The Grand Architect"** - said "he would be pleased"
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 021\|Session 021]]**: Severely **traumatized** by interrogation
  - Can still speak but not mentally stable enough to act as double agent
  - Guild would immediately notice something wrong
  - Recovering with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Adda\|Adda]] in the Crows' Wood
  - Double agent plan failed
