# Haunted Lodge Contract

## Quest Information
- **Type**: Contract Quest
- **Status**: Completed
- **Client**: [[Info/Characters/Hadvar\|Hadvar]] of Clan Cold-Moor
- **Reward**: 400 gold each (doubled from original 200)
- **Location**: [[Haunted Hunting Lodge\|Haunted Hunting Lodge]]

## Quest Description
Clear the haunting at [[Info/Characters/Hadvar\|Hadvar]]'s inherited hunting lodge east of [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]], near [[Khaarus Hollow\|Khaarus Hollow]].

## Haunting Phenomena
- Doors opening on their own
- Hearth lighting itself
- Whispers from the rafters
- Bear rug growling
- Bed moving across the floor at midnight
- Cold air and strange noises
- Well that never dries up, even during frost

## Key Discoveries
- **Dragon Cult Connection**: The lodge contained an ancient barrow with Dragon Cult ritual chambers
- **[[Sven\|Sven]]'s True Nature**: Was under control of a Dragon Priest, not a simple spirit
- **[[Info/Characters/Lagetha\|Lagetha]]'s Fate**: Ancient spirit bound by pain and rage for centuries
- **Dragon Priest**: Ancient entity controlling [[Sven\|Sven]], destroyed by [[Info/Characters/Adda\|Adda]]

## Resolution
- **[[Sven\|Sven]] and [[Info/Characters/Lagetha\|Lagetha]]**: Finally put to rest after centuries of torment
- **Dragon Priest**: Destroyed by [[Info/Characters/Adda\|Adda]]'s spiritual magic
- **Domain Protection**: [[Cult of Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]] will ensure the area remains undisturbed
- **Dragon Cult Mask**: Retrieved by [[Info/Characters/Kirya\|Kirya]] for study

## Rewards
- **Gold**: 400 each (doubled from original 200 through [[Info/Characters/Durov\|Durov]]'s intimidation)
- **Items**: Dragon Cult mask, various alchemical ingredients, soul gem fragments
- **Recognition**: Permanent welcome at [[Cult of Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]] hall

## Notes
- The haunting was more complex than initially believed
- Dragon Cult activity revealed the true nature of the spirits
- [[Info/Characters/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] was abducted during the quest but successfully rescued
- The lodge area is now permanently protected from future disturbances
