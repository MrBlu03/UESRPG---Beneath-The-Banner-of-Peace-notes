# White Stag Hunt

## Quest Overview
A hunter met at a roadside shrine to the old gods has invited the party to join his hunting group in tracking a legendary white stag once the heavy snows clear in the [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] area.

## Quest Details
**Given By:** Unnamed Hunter at roadside shrine  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] area  
**Contact:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Denskar\|Denskar]] at [[Info/Locations/The_Wounded_Boar\|The_Wounded_Boar]] inn  
**Requirements:** Wait for snow to clear, coordinate with hunting party

## Background
During their journey to [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], the party encountered a hunter giving tribute to the old gods (the fox and the hawk) at a roadside shrine. When [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] inquired about his hunting plans, he revealed he and his friends were planning to hunt a white stag - a creature of legendary status and potential magical significance.

## Current Status
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] offered to assist the hunting party
- Meeting arranged through [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Denskar\|Denskar]] at [[Info/Locations/The_Wounded_Boar\|The_Wounded_Boar]] inn in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
- Hunt is planned for after the heavy snows clear
- Party has arrived in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] and can now make contact

## Objectives
- [ ] Contact [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Denskar\|Denskar]] at [[Info/Locations/The_Wounded_Boar\|The_Wounded_Boar]] inn
- [ ] Coordinate with the hunting party
- [ ] Wait for favorable weather conditions
- [ ] Participate in the white stag hunt
- [ ] Investigate any magical properties of the white stag

## Notes
- White stags are often associated with supernatural or divine significance in Nordic culture
- The hunter's reverence at the shrine suggests this is more than ordinary hunting
- Success could provide valuable connections in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
- May yield rare materials or magical components
