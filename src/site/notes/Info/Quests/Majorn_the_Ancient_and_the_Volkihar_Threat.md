# Majorn the Ancient & the Volkihar Threat

## Quest Log Summary
While the party successfully liberated Fort Greenwall and slew commander [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Thrysnr_the_Unsullied\|Thrysnr_the_Unsullied]], captured vampire thralls in Falderstad confirmed that the Greenwall massacre was merely a field experiment conducted by **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]]**, who remains at large building a rogue coven in eastern Skyrim.

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Uncover Volkihar vampire involvement in the Greenwall blizzard ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]).
- [x] Slay vampire commander Thrysnr and recover the Harrowstorm Engine Core ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 006\|Session 006]]).
- [x] Interrogate the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vampire_Survivor\|Vampire_Survivor]] in Falderstad, learning of Majorn the Ancient's overarching invasion plans ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]]).
