# The Fake Currency Crisis

## Quest Log Summary
Upon entering Kjalgrad, the party noticed widespread economic unrest caused by millions of lightweight, brimstone-scented counterfeit Septims flooding the market, designed to vanish into ash once their Daedric bonding expired.

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Examine counterfeit gold coins and detect brimstone residue and Daedric enchantments ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]]).
- [x] Interrogate moneychangers and merchant victims in the Grand Market ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 021\|Session 021]]).
- [x] Raid [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Warehouse_Seven\|Warehouse_Seven]] to seize master ledgers tracking the counterfeit distribution ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 024\|Session 024]]).
- [x] Track the minting operation north to the subterranean Dwemer vaults of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan_Mesa]] ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 029\|Session 029]]).
- [x] Destroy the coin molds, smash the magical melting crucibles, and slay the Xivilai overseer ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]).
