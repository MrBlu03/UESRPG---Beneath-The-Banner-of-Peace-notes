# Assault on the Main Facility (Altan Mesa)

## Quest Log Summary
Following intelligence gathered from Silverroad ledgers and the interrogation of Scaafin steward [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]], the party discovered that the primary operational base for the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile]] was situated within the subterranean Dwemer and Nordic ruins of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan_Mesa]], north of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. The cult used the facility as a counterfeit coin mint and sacrificial altar powered by abducted citizens.
