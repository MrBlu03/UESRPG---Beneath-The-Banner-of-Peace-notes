# Jean-Pierre LaRoche: Missing

**Status**: Active (Session 026)  
**Objective**: Find Jean-Pierre LaRoche, a silk merchant missing since the Jarl's banquet

## Background
During the market protest in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]], [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Lady_Aine\|Lady_Aine]] (LaRoche's wife) approached [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] in his capacity as a Guild complaints officer to report her husband missing.

**Jean-Pierre LaRoche** — His Illustrious Magnificence, Marquis of the Sapphire Coast, Baron of the Nine Rooms, Keeper of the Royal Cocoon Seals, and Patriarch of House LaRoche — is a Breton silk merchant with a distinctive curling moustache. He disappeared over a week ago, last seen leaving home to open his market stall.

His stall is still standing in the market with **frozen fabrics** abandoned on display. Lady Aine believes he would never voluntarily abandon his silks.

## Connection to the Conspiracy
Jean-Pierre LaRoche's name appears in the **warehouse ledger** recovered from the northeast warehouse in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 024\|Session 024]], listed under **"selection cargo"** — a category cross-referencing names of the city's disappeared, specifically merchants and guests at the Jarl's events.

He was at the **Jarl's banquet** shortly before he disappeared. His wife noted he had been mildly absent-minded since the banquet.

He is almost certainly being held at the **main facility north of Kjalgrad** — the destination of the "selection cargo" shipments.
