# Rescue Onasha (Longhouse Jailbreak)

## Quest Log Summary
Following the chaotic warehouse fires, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] was cornered and detained by city guards, locked deep within the holding cells of the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Clan_Morvan_Longhouse]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] teamed with the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Lower_Lodge_Network\|Lower_Lodge_Network]] to execute a daring sewer breach and rescue her.
