# Fangs of Fjelhorst Formation

## Quest Log Summary
Following the successful resolution of the Treva's Bend water crisis and the scouting of the Harmugstahl disturbance, Queen Freidr of Riften formally recognized the party's valor, christening them **The Fangs of Fjelhorst** after Mount Fjelhorst and commissioning them as royal problem-solvers.

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Resolve the Snow-Shod water dispute and recover the poison evidence ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 001\|Session 001]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]).
- [x] Report findings to Queen Freidr at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil_Keep]] ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]).
- [x] Conclude the Harmugstahl investigation and receive 400 gold reward from Rigi ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]]).
- [x] Receive official royal charter, writs, and titles from Queen Freidr ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]]).
