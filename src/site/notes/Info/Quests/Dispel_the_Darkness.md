# Dispel the Darkness

## Quest Log Summary
Fort [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] was shrouded in an unnatural, impenetrable magical eclipse and freezing blizzard generated by a rogue Volkihar vampire coven. The party was tasked with infiltrating the fortress and disrupting the dark ritual mechanism maintaining the permanent night.

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Infiltrate the fortress interior through the Three Banners War smuggler tunnel ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 006\|Session 006]]).
- [x] Locate the ritual chamber in the upper battlements ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 006\|Session 006]]).
- [x] Disrupt the ritual apparatus and slay the Volkihar ritualists ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 006\|Session 006]]).
- [x] Dispel the supernatural darkness and restore daylight to the Rift pass ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 006\|Session 006]]).
