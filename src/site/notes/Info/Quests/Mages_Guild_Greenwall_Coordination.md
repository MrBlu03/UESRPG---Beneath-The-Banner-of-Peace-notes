# Mages Guild Greenwall Coordination

## Quest Log Summary
Following the liberation of Fort Greenwall, the party coordinated with royal court mage [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]] and Mages Guild antiquarian [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle_Benele]] to study, ward, and safely contain the powerful Daedric orb used by the vampires.

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Consult Archmagus Mortgren regarding the unnatural blizzard at Greenwall ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]).
- [x] Recover the dark ritual orb from the battlements of Greenwall ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 006\|Session 006]]).
- [x] Meet Antiquarian [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle_Benele]] in Falderstad to evaluate the artifact ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]).
- [x] Identify the relic as an authentic First Era **Harrowstorm Engine Core** forged by the [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey_Host]] ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]).
- [x] Apply anti-Daedric wards and ship the core via Winterhold to [[Info/Locations/Other Realms/Eyevea\|Eyevea]] for permanent containment ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]).
