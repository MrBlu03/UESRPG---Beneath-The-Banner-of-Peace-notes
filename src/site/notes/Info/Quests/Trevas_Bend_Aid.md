# Treva's Bend Aid

## Quest Log Summary
Commissioned by Queen Freidr and Alderman Torgir, the party investigated rising hostilities between the agrarian settlement of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Trevas_Bend]] and [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod_Estate]] over an abrupt river stoppage.

## Objectives & Progression
- [x] Travel to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Trevas_Bend]] and meet with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Alderman_Torgir]] ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 001\|Session 001]]).
- [x] Inspect the dried riverbed and trek upstream to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Dam\|Snow-Shod_Dam]] ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]).
- [x] Discover that the dam floodgate was deliberately jammed with an ungreased lever ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]).
- [x] Clear the stoppage, restore water flow, and locate the poisoned corpse of Dunmer saboteur [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith_Velan]] ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]).
- [x] Return to Treva's Bend to deliver the good news and receive 50 gold from Alderman Torgir ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]).
