# Stryder's Favor

## Quest Overview
A criminal operation orchestrated by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]] to intercept a [[Dunmer\|Dunmer]] merchant caravan suspected of smuggling illegal goods. The quest involved [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] paying off a debt by providing muscle and combat support for the operation, ultimately revealing connections to the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] conspiracy.

## Quest Details
**Given By:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]]  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] and surrounding roads  
**Meeting Point:** [[Info/Locations/Abandoned_Guild_House\|Abandoned_Guild_House]]  
**Type:** Criminal interception and intelligence gathering

## Background
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]] called in a favor owed by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]], requesting "muscle" and someone capable of close combat for a special operation. The target was a [[Dunmer\|Dunmer]] merchant caravan operating along trade routes near [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], suspected of illegal activities.

## Quest Development

### Initial Contact
- Arrow message delivered to party's door
- Meeting arranged at [[Info/Locations/Abandoned_Guild_House\|Abandoned_Guild_House]] on NE side of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] brought [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] as requested combat support

### Mission Briefing
- **Target**: [[Dunmer\|Dunmer]] trader caravan cutting off road guards
- **Objective**: Inspect caravan for suspicious or useful items
- **Intelligence**: 6 guards, lightly armed, 1 Nord driver, rest [[Dunmer\|Dunmer]]
- **Support**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s men positioned in trees as backup
- **Payment Symbol**: Silver coin with triple ouroboros eating crown (dragon, eagle, lion)

### Operational Execution
Located caravan south of city along river:
- Well-maintained two-wagon convoy
- 6 [[Dunmer\|Dunmer]] guards (3 per side) plus Nord driver
- 2 guards in darker red showing nervous behavior

#### Authority Assertion
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] claimed inspection rights on behalf of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]]:
- Presented as official security check for illegal goods
- Merchants allowed inspection after initial cooperation
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] conducted physical search while [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] maintained security

#### Discovery Phase
**First Wagon**: Cloth, mead, fish - legitimate trade goods
**Second Wagon**: Spices, herbs (surface), moonsugar (hidden)

#### Interrogation
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] separated nervous guard for questioning
- Guard initially claimed legitimacy with proper paperwork
- Documents revealed as forged with incorrect house signatures
- Under pressure, guard admitted to moonsugar hidden under floorboards

#### Combat Resolution
- Interrogated guard's yelling alerted companions
- Guard attacked [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] with sword
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] tackled, disarmed, and killed attacker
- Cooperative guard produced smuggling ledger revealing [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] connections

### Evidence Recovered
- **Moonsugar**: Illegal contraband in sealed waxed leather
- **Smuggling Ledger**: Multiple shipments with "payment, serpent mark confirmed"
- **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] Emblem**: On final page of ledger
- **Forged Documents**: Evidence of systematic document fraud
- **Red Gem**: Found with silks in first cart
- **Additional Evidence**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] planted extra contraband to justify executions

## Quest Resolution

### Legal Proceedings
- Prisoners brought to [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] gates
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]] the huscarl summoned for sentencing
- Execution options offered: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] executes them or gladiatorial combat
- Surviving prisoners held in dungeons pending final execution

### Final Coordination
- Return to [[Info/Locations/Abandoned_Guild_House\|Abandoned_Guild_House]] with seized goods
- Meeting with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hooded_Figure\|Hooded_Figure]]
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hooded_Figure\|Hooded_Figure]] claimed to be "no one of consequence"
- Coordination of contraband distribution and criminal network activities

## Intelligence Gathered

### [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] Operations

- Payment structure with serpent mark confirmation system
- Systematic document forgery for official legitimacy
- Connection between criminal networks and larger conspiracy

### Criminal Network Structure
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s organization coordinates with court-connected figures
- Understanding between criminals and local authorities
- Sophisticated operational planning and backup support
- Integration of legitimate authority with criminal activities
