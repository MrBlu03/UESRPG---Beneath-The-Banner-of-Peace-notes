# Durov's Lost Past

## Quest Log Summary
[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], a heavily armored Colovian warrior, suffers from severe retrograde amnesia, retaining flawless martial instincts and military discipline while having virtually no memory of his past campaigns, family, or former comrades.
