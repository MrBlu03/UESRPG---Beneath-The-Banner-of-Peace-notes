# Hunt Forest Creature

## Quest Overview
An organized expedition to hunt the territorial supernatural creature that has been affecting the [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]] and killed the white stag. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] is organizing community hunters to address the supernatural threat that has driven away normal wildlife and created dangerous conditions in the forest.

## Quest Details
**Given By:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]] and [[Info/Locations/Cursed_Grove\|Cursed_Grove]]  
**Contact:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] organizing expedition  
**Type:** Monster hunting and supernatural threat elimination

## Background
Following the tragic conclusion of the [[Info/Quests/White_Stag_Hunt\|White_Stag_Hunt]], where the stag was found dying with five deep claw marks and the hunting party was chased by an unseen territorial creature, [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] recognized the need for organized community action to address the supernatural threat.

## Threat Assessment

### Territorial Creature Characteristics
- **Location**: Controls [[Info/Locations/Cursed_Grove\|Cursed_Grove]] area within [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]]
- **Behavior**: Territorial, attacks intruders in supernatural domain
- **Capabilities**: Massive claws capable of creating deep wounds
- **Impact**: Has driven away all natural wildlife from the area
- **Supernatural Nature**: Operates in area described as "not Kyne's forest"

### Evidence of Activity
- **White Stag Death**: Five long, deep claw marks across the creature's side
- **Carved Totems**: Tree trunks marked with claw damage throughout grove
- **Environmental Corruption**: Area exists outside natural order
- **Pursuit Behavior**: Chased hunting party when they intruded on territory
- **Supernatural Howl**: Distinctive vocalization before pursuit

The Hunt Forest Creature quest represents the community's organized response to supernatural threats affecting their environment and livelihood. Success would eliminate a major threat to the [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]] and demonstrate the effectiveness of coordinated action against supernatural dangers.
