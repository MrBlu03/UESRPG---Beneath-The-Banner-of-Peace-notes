# Hunt Forest Creature

## Quest Overview
An organized expedition to hunt the territorial supernatural creature that has been affecting the [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]] and killed the white stag. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] is organizing community hunters to address the supernatural threat that has driven away normal wildlife and created dangerous conditions in the forest.

## Quest Details
**Given By:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]] and [[Info/Locations/Cursed_Grove\|Cursed_Grove]]  
**Contact:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] organizing expedition  
**Type:** Monster hunting and supernatural threat elimination  
**Creature Identified:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Skinstealer\|Skinstealer]] (Ragged Lord aspect of Hircine)  
**Key Weakness:** Vulnerable to fire, especially Kyne's flame

## Background
Following the tragic conclusion of the [[Info/Quests/White_Stag_Hunt\|White_Stag_Hunt]], where the stag was found dying with five deep claw marks and the hunting party was chased by an unseen territorial creature, [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] recognized the need for organized community action to address the supernatural threat.

## Threat Assessment

### The Skinstealer (Ragged Lord)
**Identified in Session 016 by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]**

**Identity:**
- Ragged Lord: Aspect of Hircine (Daedric Prince of the Hunt)
- Associated with mockery and hunter's madness
- Active for 3-4 weeks in the region

**Physical Characteristics:**
- 45 cm ebony-sharp claws
- Immense strength (can fling armored warriors 2 meters)
- Materializes out of thin air
- Commands pack of 3 compelled wolves

**Supernatural Abilities:**
- **Voice Mimicry**: Perfectly replicates human voices to separate prey
- **Spatial Manipulation**: Creates infinite forest loops to trap victims
- **Track Erasure**: Removes all footprints and evidence of passage
- **Throat-Cutting Efficiency**: Kills swiftly and displays bodies
- **Environmental Control**: Corrupts atmosphere, amplifies sounds

**Territory:**
- Claims the land through "inheritance" (Othala rune)
- Controls [[Info/Locations/Cursed_Grove\|Cursed_Grove]] area
- Has created grotesque shrine of bones and human skins
- Marks territory with Othala runes on trees
- Hundreds of iron nails driven around streams

**Critical Weakness:**
- **Vulnerable to Fire**: Gets hurt by fire easily
- **Kyne's Flame**: Specifically fears sacred flame of Kyne
- Recoils from fire salt rituals
- Forest screams in pain when exposed to Kyne's fire

### Evidence of Activity
- **White Stag Death**: Five long, deep claw marks across the creature's side
- **Bjorr's Death**: Woodsman killed during Session 016 hunt (throat cut, suspended in trees)
- **Carved Totems**: Tree trunks marked with claw damage throughout grove
- **Othala Runes**: Nordic runes meaning "heritage/inheritance" carved on tree backs
- **Environmental Corruption**: Area exists outside natural order
- **Pursuit Behavior**: Chased hunting party when they intruded on territory
- **Supernatural Howl**: Distinctive vocalization before pursuit
- **Grotesque Clearing**: Bone monolith and human skin shrine discovered
- **Iron Nails**: Hundreds driven into trees around half-frozen streams
- **Blood Leakage**: Warm supernatural blood leaking from burned totems

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]] - The Organized Hunt

#### Pre-Hunt Preparation
- **Dawn Rally**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] tolled hunting bell at 5:00 AM in market
- **Hunting Party Assembled**: Six experienced hunters plus adventuring party
  - [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] (mage from Winterhold)
  - [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]] (woodsman)
  - [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Ingrin\|Ingrin]] (huntress who found bone clearing)
  - [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]] (huntress)
  - [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]] (hunter and meat seller)
  - [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]] initially present but convinced to stay safe
- **Strategic Briefing**: Compiled all known information about the creature
- **Rune Analysis**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] identified Othala rune meaning and theories
- **War Paint**: Traditional woad applied to all participants

#### The Hunt
- **Journey**: Ventured deep into [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]] for several hours
- **Environmental Progression**: Air became wrong and empty, footsteps unnaturally loud
- **Stream Discovery**: Found half-frozen stream with iron nails, burned totem, blood stains
- **Clearing Reached**: Arrived at grotesque bone shrine around 11 AM-noon
- **Identification**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] recognized Ragged Lord markings from Winterhold texts
- **Weakness Discovered**: Learned creature vulnerable to fire

#### The Supernatural Maze
During retreat:
- **Track Erasure**: All footprints vanished
- **Spatial Distortion**: Forest created infinite loops
- **Voice Mimicry Attack**: Creature copied [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Ingrin\|Ingrin]]'s voice
- **Bjorr's Death**: Woodsman separated and killed
- **Torik's Ritual**: Fire salt ceremony broke the enchantment
- **Forest Reaction**: Screamed in pain, silhouette recoiled
- **Final Attack**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Skinstealer\|Skinstealer]] struck at [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] at forest edge, blocked by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]

#### Post-Hunt Organization
- **Casualty Report**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]] informed of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]]'s death
- **Bell Tolled**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] signaled hunt's end, regrouping tomorrow
- **Guard Mobilization**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] coordinated with sister to muster eastern gate guards
- **Fire Preparations**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]] ordered to prepare:
  - Fire arrows with extra grease
  - Defensive bonfires
  - Guards to shoot anything from forest
- **Alchemical Work**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]], [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]], and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] to prepare fire-based weapons
  - Materials: Fox whiskers and blood for "fox gale"

## Quest Status: COMPLETED

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]] - Final Confrontation and Victory

**The Battle:**
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] successfully performed summoning ritual to force manifestation
- Ragged Lord (Anorak) appeared as 8-9m tall horror of stitched hides and antlers
- Battle progressed through three distinct phases:
  - **Phase One**: Seven spectral wolves eliminated by combined party efforts
  - **Phase Two**: Ragged Lord manifested, grievously wounded [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]] (12 damage + grievous wound), but her fire-varnished counterattack permanently burned him
  - **Phase Three**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]'s interception and counterstrikes (13+ and 17 damage) triggered transformation, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] severed right arm (17 damage), [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] delivered killing blow by splitting her own arrow in his kneecap (11 damage), [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] drove silver blade through his heart

**Casualties:**
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]]**: Killed in Session 016 (one fatality total)
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]]**: Grievously wounded but survived with medical intervention
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]]**: Wounded, contracted rock joint, recovered with treatment
- **All other hunters survived**

**Aftermath:**
- Ragged Lord burned to ash, whispering "Hunter..." as he died
- [[Kyne\|Kyne]]'s peace immediately returned to the forest
- Oppressive atmosphere lifted completely
- Totems vanished from trees
- Natural sounds and wildlife returned
- Sun broke through clouds for first time in weeks

**Rewards:**
- **500 gold each** from Jarl [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Jorrun\|Jorrun]]'s personal funds (2000 gold total)
- **Permanent free use of Dylin's house** in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
- Community celebration and hero status
- Restored hunting grounds and forest access

**Community Impact:**
- [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] erupted in celebration
- Aurora returned to the sky
- New hawk totem raised in gratitude (not fear)
- Children wearing celebratory masks
- Hunter's bell tolled in victory
- Economic benefits from restored hunting grounds
