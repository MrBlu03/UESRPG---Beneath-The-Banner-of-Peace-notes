# Hunt Forest Creature (The Ragged Lord)

## Quest Log Summary
After woodsman [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and local hunters encountered a horrific shapeshifting beast in the snowbound woods, huntress [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]] and Laws-speaker [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] organized a full expeditionary force to track and destroy the Daedric predator **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Nohrak, the Ragged Lord]]** in the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]].
