# Warehouse Sabotage (Kjalgrad)

## Quest Log Summary
To cripple the Guild of the Silverroad and support the city-wide market strike, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] executed a synchronized sabotage mission, planting alchemical deathbell traps and incinerating the primary guild warehouses.
