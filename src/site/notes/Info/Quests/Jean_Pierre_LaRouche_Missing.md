# The Disappearance of Jean-Pierre LaRouche

## Overview
**Status:** Completed  
**Quest Type:** Investigation  
**Assigned Session:** [[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]]  
**Completed Session:** [[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]  
**Reward:** House LaRouche Alliance, Safe Conduct Map  

## Objective
Locate and rescue the eccentric Breton silk merchant **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jean-Pierre_LaRouche\|His Illustrious Magnificence Jean-Pierre LaRouche]]**, reported missing by his wife **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lady_Elaine\|Lady_Elaine]]** after disappearing following the Jarl's banquet in Kjalgrad.

## Timeline & Progression
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]]**: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lady_Elaine\|Lady_Elaine]] approached [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] during the town square protest, reporting that her husband vanished over a week ago, leaving his frozen market stall and precious silks behind. Ledger entries confirmed he had been marked as "selection cargo."
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 027\|Session 027]]**: Scraps from merchant journals pointed the investigation toward the cult facility under [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan_Mesa]].
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]**: The party infiltrated the Central Ritual Chamber beneath Altan Mesa, finding Jean-Pierre bound and unconscious as a sacrificial captive. After decapitating [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|The_Xivilai]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] revived him with smelling salts, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] provided an escape map back to Kjalgrad.
