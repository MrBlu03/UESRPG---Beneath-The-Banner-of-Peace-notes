# Black Feather

## Description
A long, black feather faintly humming with energy, given to Periwyn by a mysterious cloaked figure in her dream/vision during Session 9.5. The feather is connected to crows, the Court of Crows/Blackfeather Court, and possibly the Daedric Prince Nocturnal. It may have magical properties and is also carried by Onasha.

## First Appearance
- Session 9.5: Periwyn's Slumber (Other Realm)
