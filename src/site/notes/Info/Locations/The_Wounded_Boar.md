# The Wounded Boar

## Basic Information
**Type:** Inn  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Region:** Eastern Skyrim  
**Status:** Active  
**Proprietor:** Gruff unnamed man

## Description
The Wounded Boar is the primary inn in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], recommended by city guards for traveler accommodations. The establishment serves as both lodging and a social hub for visitors and locals, offering a warm refuge from the harsh eastern Skyrim climate.

## Key Features
- **Accommodations:** Guest rooms for travelers
- **Common Area:** Meeting space for guests and locals
- **Contact Hub:** Serves as coordination point for local activities
- **Cold Weather Refuge:** Provides shelter from harsh winter conditions
