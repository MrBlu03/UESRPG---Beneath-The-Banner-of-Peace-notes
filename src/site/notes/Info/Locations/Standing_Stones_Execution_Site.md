# Standing Stones (Execution Site)

## Basic Information
**Type:** Ritual/Execution Site  
**Location:** Outside [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] (approximately one hour's walk)  
**Region:** Eastern Skyrim  
**Status:** Active sacred site

## Description
A circle of ancient standing stones half-buried in frost, located outside [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. This site serves as the traditional location for Nordic executions and related ceremonies. The location holds deep religious significance and is prepared with specific ritual elements for each execution.

## Physical Features
- **Standing Stones**: Ancient stones arranged in a circle, partially buried by centuries of frost
- **Braziers**: Placed at each cardinal direction, burning with thick grey smoke during ceremonies
- **Fire Pit**: Central area where [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Tarik\|Tarik]] creates ritual circles of ash
- **Surrounding Area**: Snow-covered ground with woods nearby

## Ritual Significance
The Standing Stones serve as a liminal space between the mortal world and Sovngarde, where:
- The gods ([[Shor\|Shor]] and [[Kyne\|Kyne]]) bear witness to justice
- The condemned face their final moments
- Blood debts are paid according to Nordic law

## Key Events

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]] - The Dunmer Execution (11th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600)

#### Preparations
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Tarik\|Tarik]] completed a circle of ash around the fire pit
- Braziers burned at each cardinal direction with thick grey smoke
- The crowd formed a ring around the circle
- Ravens circled overhead

#### The Procession
Participants walked from [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] for nearly an hour:
1. Jarl [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gorm\|Gorm]]
2. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Tarik\|Tarik]] (Speaker for the Dead)
3. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]], [[Sonja\|Sonja]], and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Agni\|Agni]] (Skalds)
4. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valor\|Valor]]
5. [[The Fangs of Fjelhorst\|The Fangs of Fjelhorst]]
6. Citizens of Falderstad

#### The Executions
- Five Dunmer smugglers were executed by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Tarik\|Tarik]] conducted the ritual invocations
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]] chanted after each execution
- The first four prisoners received honorable deaths and were burned on a pyre
- The fifth (defiant) prisoner was executed from behind and his body left at the edge of the woods

#### Aftermath
- Bodies (except the disgraced one) burned on a distant pyre
- Blue smoke rose above [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Tarik\|Tarik]] traced a spiral rune to close the ceremony

## Ceremonial Elements

### The Ritual Words
**Opening** ([[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Tarik\|Tarik]]):
> *"Before the gods and the blood of men, the way to Sovngarde is called. Those who conspired against the clans pay the price they owe."*

**Post-Execution Chant** ([[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]]):
> *"Blood for blood, oath for oath. Shor's law holds, some flesh is loath. Kyne bear the wind and the dead man's breath, to Sovngarde or to Orkey's death."*

**Closing** ([[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Tarik\|Tarik]]):
> *"The deed is done. Let none speak of vengeance or guilt. The blood is paid and the gods are sated."*

### Honorable vs. Dishonorable Death
- **Honorable**: Face the executioner, receive mead, body burned on pyre
- **Dishonorable**: Executed from behind, denied mead, body left in woods
