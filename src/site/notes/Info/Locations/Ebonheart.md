# Ebonheart

## Basic Information
**Type:** City/Port  
**Region:** Morrowind (Stonefalls)  
**Status:** Active  
**Known For:** Trade hub, moon sugar smuggling origin point

## Description
Ebonheart is a major city in Morrowind, serving as a significant trade hub. According to interrogation of Dunmer smugglers in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], it serves as a source location for moon sugar being smuggled into Skyrim through [[Info/Organizations/House_Hlaalu\|House_Hlaalu]] operations.

## Criminal Activity

### Moon Sugar Smuggling ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]])
Information obtained from interrogating Dunmer prisoners in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]:
- Smugglers operating for [[Info/Organizations/House_Hlaalu\|House_Hlaalu]] obtain moon sugar from Ebonheart
- The moon sugar is then transported from Morrowind into Skyrim
- This trade operates outside of legitimate Khajiit caravan networks
- A crate of moon sugar is worth approximately **300 gold**

## Trade Significance
- **Moon Sugar Source**: Supplies smuggling operations into Skyrim
- **Morrowind Trade**: Part of inter-provincial commerce (legitimate and illicit)
- **House Hlaalu Operations**: The Great House has established smuggling networks here

## Related Organizations
- [[Info/Organizations/House_Hlaalu\|House_Hlaalu]]: Uses Ebonheart as source for moon sugar smuggling
- **Khajiit Caravans**: NOT involved in the House Hlaalu smuggling (per interrogation)
