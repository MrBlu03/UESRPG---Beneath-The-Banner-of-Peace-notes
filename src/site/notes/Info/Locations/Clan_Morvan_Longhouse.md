# Clan Morvan Longhouse

## Basic Information
**Type:** Jarl's Longhouse / Seat of Government  
**Location:** [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], across a small bridge from the main city  
**Region:** The Rift, Skyrim  
**Status:** Active  
**Controlled By:** [[Clan Morvan\|Clan Morvan]] (nominal), [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] (effective)

## Description
The Clan Morvan Longhouse is the seat of power in [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], serving as the Jarl's residence and the center of government for the mining town. The building is ostentatiously decorated - described as "tiresomely showy" - reflecting the wealth generated by [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]'s mining operations.

## Architecture and Features

### Exterior
- Located across a small bridge from the main city
- Separated from the market and guild hall districts
- Traditional Nordic longhouse design with expensive embellishments

### Interior
- **Décor**: Extravagantly decorated, almost gaudy in its wealth display
- **Jarl's Chair**: The throne sits empty most of the time
- **Mammoth Skull**: A great mammoth skull hangs above the Jarl's chair
- **Steward's Position**: Space beside the throne where [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] typically stands

## Political Significance

### The Power Dynamic
The longhouse represents a façade of traditional Nordic rule:
- The **Jarl's chair** sits empty during official meetings
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]]** (Imperial Steward) conducts actual governance
- The Jarl is essentially a figurehead
- Real power lies with the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]

### Symbolic Elements
- The mammoth skull suggests traditional Nordic might
- The empty chair symbolizes the absent/ineffective Jarl
- The excessive decoration reflects guild-funded wealth
- An Imperial steward in a Nordic longhouse shows outside influence
