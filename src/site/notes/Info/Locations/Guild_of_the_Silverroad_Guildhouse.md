# Guild of the Silverroad Guildhouse

## Basic Information
**Type:** Guild Headquarters / Office  
**Location:** [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Region:** The Rift, Skyrim  
**Status:** Active  
**Controlled By:** [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]  
**Primary Occupant:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] (Trade Advocate)

## Description
The [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] maintains a guildhouse in [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] that serves as the official seat of guild operations in the city. The building includes formal meeting rooms for conducting business with potential trade partners, as well as private offices. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]'s personal office is located here and contains significant intelligence on guild operations.

## Valen Marr's Office

### Furnishings and Layout
- Meeting table with chairs — used for formal business discussions
- Wine service (Valen offered [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] a glass during the meeting)
- A **map on the wall** — showing regional geography with notable markings
- A **locked cabinet** behind or near the desk
- A **lockbox** accessible within the office

### The Wall Map
The map in Valen's office bore several marked locations:
- **Two red circles in the warehouse district** of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] — almost certainly [[Warehouse 4\|Warehouse 4]] and [[Warehouse 7\|Warehouse 7]]
- **One mark northeast of Kjalgrad**, outside the city walls — location unknown; possible field site, meeting point, or hidden operation

### The Ledger
A ledger on or near the desk contained entries from the last few weeks:
- *"Transfer warehouse 4 to warehouse 7 — escorted shipment, night delivery"* — night transfer of cargo between guild warehouses
- *"Special consignment — temple allocation"* — notable because there is **no temple in Kjalgrad**
- *"Coin distribution — market circulation"* — consistent with the brimstone gold operation

### The Locked Cabinet
Contents observed when the cabinet was briefly accessible (while Valen stepped out):
- A **slightly burnt letter** reading: *"Jarl remains cooperative though his condition requires continued supervision"*
  - Confirms the Jarl is being actively managed by the Guild or Coiled Serpent
  - The word "condition" implies the Jarl may be unwell, controlled, or otherwise compromised
  - The letter was partially burnt — possibly an attempt to destroy it, or fire damage from another source

### The Lockbox
Contents of the lockbox observed during the same window:
- **Silver Road contracts** — standard guild business documentation
- **Bribe payments to Riften officials** — dated within the last two weeks, suggesting active political corruption outside Kjalgrad
- **Letter fragments** referencing **"the Coming Correction"** — a phrase not previously encountered; implies a planned event of significant scale

## Key Events

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]] — Meeting with Valen Marr
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] met with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] at the guildhouse on behalf of the [[Fangs of Fjelhorst\|Fangs of Fjelhorst]]:

**The Partnership Offer:**
- Valen offered Periwyn an **8% guild rate** (compared to the standard 15% available to independent merchants)
- Proposed **three trade routes**:
  1. Kjalgrad → Morrowind
  2. Rift routes (unspecified)
  3. Rift → Falkreath → Bruma / Cyrodiil
- Offered a **permanent warehouse in southeast Skyrim**
- In exchange: carry Guild cargo on her routes, and report on rival merchants

**The Interruption:**
- Partway through the meeting, a guard whispered something to Valen
- Valen excused himself and stepped out — almost certainly informed about the warehouse incident (the party had been investigating Warehouse 7 and paid 500 gold to hold suspicious crates)
- This gave the party a brief window to examine the office

**The Pendant Question:**
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] asked Valen directly about his **Ouroboros pendant**
- Valen gave a prepared cover story: it is a keepsake from a fellow **Planemeld survivor**, representing the three alliances (Ebonheart Pact, Aldmeri Dominion — griffon symbol, Daggerfall Covenant — lion symbol) from the siege of the White Gold Tower
- He claims to know **one other pendant-wearer in Bruma**
- This is his public explanation for wearing the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] symbol openly

**Periwyn's Deceive Roll:**
- Valen noticed [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] had not touched her wine
- She narrowly passed a Deceive check to cover her hesitation

## Strategic Importance

The guildhouse is one of the most intelligence-rich locations the party has accessed:
- The map reveals operational geography (warehouse district circles, northeast marker)
- The ledger documents financial and logistical crimes
- The cabinet letter **directly confirms the Jarl is under Guild supervision**
- The lockbox reveals bribery of Riften officials and references "the Coming Correction"
