# Harmugstahl

## Description
[[Info/Locations/Harmugstahl\|Harmugstahl]] is a military keep and training ground for [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]]'s scouts that mysteriously went silent. The entire garrison was found frozen solid in an supernatural incident, with soldiers trapped mid-action but still alive within the ice.

## Key Features
- Military training facility for Riften scouts
- Completely frozen by supernatural magic
- Contains barracks, dungeons, and overseer's quarters
- Well in courtyard (frozen solid to the bottom)
- Stables (abandoned, no horses found)

## Key Events

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]] - The Frozen Keep Investigation
**Initial Discovery:**
- Keep went silent three days prior to investigation
- Scouts sent to investigate never returned
- Hunter [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Eydis\|Eydis]] reported eerie stillness before being found frozen to death in his home
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]] tasked the party with investigating

**Journey:**
- Followed by mysterious crows ([[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron_Jangleplume]])
- Baron Jangleplume issued cryptic warnings: "You're too late," "The keep is Hollow," "He waits beneath the bones"

**Investigation Findings:**
- No footprints except the party's own
- Unnaturally cold throughout
- Frostbitten severed hand found in well bucket
- Water frozen solid to bottom of well
- No signs of horses in stables

**Barracks:**
- Soldiers frozen mid-action with eyes still moving
- Some drawing swords, some kneeling in prayer, some writing letters
- Torches lit but giving dim light and no heat
- Mysterious voices heard:
  - "Gareth's little stray, still chasing ghosts?" (directed at [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]])
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] threw knife at frozen soldier, shattering it
- Presence felt in the room
- Frozen soldiers turned in their places

**Overseer's Quarters:**
- Logbook found with last entry: "It is inside now"
- Mysterious shadowy [[Khajiit_Figure\|Khajiit_Figure]] glimpsed through window reflection
- Force rattled shutters closed
- Smell of fresh blood filled the air
- Breath visible in darkest corner, but no form visible

**Dungeon:**
- Air felt like it was pushing against the party
- Trail of black feathers led to cracked mirror in last cell
- [[Khajiit_Figure\|Khajiit_Figure]] appeared in mirror (only [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] could see)
- Figure said: "They left them for you"
- All torches suddenly extinguished
- Two symbols appeared as if drawn by invisible hand:
  - [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] symbol (seen before)
  - Variant [[Info/Organizations/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan_Volkihar]] symbol (splinter faction)
- Second gust of wind restored torches
- Presence departed
- Frozen soldiers upstairs shattered into fine dust like metal shavings
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] collected dust sample

## Supernatural Elements
- Advanced freezing magic of unknown origin
- Soldiers trapped but alive within ice
- Supernatural presence with ability to manipulate environment
- Connection to [[Info/Organizations/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan_Volkihar]] and [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]]

- [[Khajiit_Figure\|Khajiit_Figure]] appearances (only visible to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]])
