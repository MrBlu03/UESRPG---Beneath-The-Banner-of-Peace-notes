# Ruined Hunting Lodge

## Basic Information
**Type:** Abandoned Lodge  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]] near [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Region:** Eastern Skyrim  
**Status:** Ruined/Abandoned  
**Current Occupant:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Vampire_Survivor\|Vampire_Survivor]]

## Description
A run-down hunting lodge deep in the northern forests outside [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], now serving as a refuge for the [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Vampire_Survivor\|Vampire_Survivor]] from the [[Info/Locations/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] incident. The lodge has become a center for necromantic experimentation and vampire operations as part of [[Majorn\|Majorn]]'s conspiracy.

## Condition and Atmosphere
- **Structural State:** Run-down and deteriorating
- **Odors:** Strong scents of dust, rot, and blood throughout
- **Ambience:** Oppressive supernatural presence
- **Accessibility:** Remote location requiring forest travel to reach

## Supernatural Elements
- **Necromantic Presence:** Powerful death magic detected by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]
- **Vampire Lair:** Current residence of active vampire entity
- **Experimental Site:** Evidence of ongoing supernatural research
- **Dark Magic:** Connected to sun-blocking field tests and vampire operations

## Current Occupant

### [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Vampire_Survivor\|Vampire_Survivor]]
- Sole survivor from [[Info/Locations/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] incident
- Currently injured by territorial creature in the forests
- Using lodge as base of operations for vampire conspiracy activities
- Conducting necromantic experiments related to sun-blocking magic
- Working as field agent for [[Majorn\|Majorn]]'s organization

## Operations and Activities
- **Field Testing:** Sun-blocking magical techniques
- **Intelligence Gathering:** Monitoring local populations
- **Thrall Management:** Controlled [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]] from this location
- **Necromantic Research:** Ongoing magical experimentation
- **Alliance Coordination:** Working with [[Poisoned_Dunmer\|Poisoned_Dunmer]] and other agents

## Strategic Importance
- **Remote Location:** Hidden from casual discovery
- **Central Position:** Accessible to [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] for operations
- **Supernatural Nexus:** Concentrated necromantic energy
- **Communication Hub:** Coordination point for conspiracy activities

## Threats and Dangers
- **Active Vampire:** Intelligent undead creature in residence
- **Necromantic Contamination:** Dangerous magical emanations
- **Territorial Creature:** Unknown entity that injured the vampire
- **Conspiracy Center:** Hub for larger supernatural threat network

## Party Interaction
- Initially approached with caution due to supernatural detection
- Surprised vampire by not immediately attacking
- Gained extensive intelligence through conversation
- Left vampire alive, potentially for future information gathering

## Environmental Context
- Located in area with no natural wildlife (driven away by territorial creature)
- Connected to network of claw marks and supernatural activity in forest
- Part of larger supernatural disturbance affecting northern forest ecosystem

## Significance
The Ruined Hunting Lodge serves as a critical intelligence source and represents the infrastructure supporting the vampire conspiracy. Its remote location demonstrates how supernatural threats establish hidden bases of operation while remaining connected to population centers they target.

## Future Considerations
- Vampire remains active threat despite current injuries
- Location could be staging ground for larger operations
- Territorial creature represents additional unknown threat
- Lodge may contain additional evidence or resources for conspiracy activities
