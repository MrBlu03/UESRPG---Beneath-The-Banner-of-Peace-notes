# Nilan's House

## Basic Information
**Type:** Residential House  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Region:** Eastern Skyrim  
**Status:** Abandoned (Owner deceased)  
**Owner:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]] (Deceased)

## Description
The former residence of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]], a hunter who was supposedly the sole survivor of the frozen hunter incident. The house became a crime scene when the party discovered [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]]'s true fate during their investigation in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]]. The property contains evidence of vampire thrall activity and necromantic experimentation.

## Layout and Features
- **Main Floor:** Standard Nord house with kitchen, common area
- **Bedroom:** Contains hidden entrance to secret basement
- **Hidden Basement:** Accessed through unlocked hatch beneath rug in bedroom
- **Perimeter:** Undisturbed grounds with spoiled food visible through windows

## Key Discoveries

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]] Investigation
- **Entrance:** Bow hanging incorrectly by door (unusual for seasoned hunter)
- **Kitchen:** Searched but yielded no useful information
- **Bedroom:** [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sensed death magic beneath rug covering hatch

### The Hidden Basement
Critical evidence discovered:
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]]'s Body:** Found dead with bite marks, signs of vampire thrall transformation
- **Nightshade Evidence:** Single flower at feet, scent throughout area
- **Alchemy Station:** Recently used equipment with nightshade residue
- **Mysterious Vials:** Two unique containers, one with liquid "black as darkness"
- **Necromantic Presence:** Strong death magic emanations detected by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]

## Supernatural Elements
- Death magic concentrated in basement area
- Evidence of recent necromantic experimentation
- Vampire thrall transformation site
- Alchemy equipment used for unknown supernatural preparations

## Investigation Timeline
1. Party approached after learning of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]]'s supposed survival
2. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] detected death magic before entry
3. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] found perimeter undisturbed but noted spoiled food
4. No response to knocking, party entered unlocked house
5. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] noticed incorrectly hung bow
6. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] located hidden basement through magical detection
7. Full investigation revealed vampire conspiracy evidence

## Evidence Recovered
- **Hunting Trophies:** Retrieved for proper disposition
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]]'s Body:** Removed for funeral pyre
- **Alchemy Vials:** Mysterious potions for further analysis
- **Intelligence:** Evidence of vampire thrall operations

## Current Status
- House secured and locked after investigation
- Evidence gathered and body removed
- Basement served as temporary location for [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s necromancy training
- Property likely to remain abandoned given supernatural contamination

## Significance
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]]'s House represents a critical breakthrough in understanding the vampire conspiracy affecting [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. The discovery of the basement operation revealed the true scope of supernatural infiltration in the community and provided evidence linking local incidents to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn]]'s broader conspiracy.

## Security Notes

- No signs of forced entry or struggle on main floor
- Hidden basement suggests longer-term vampire operations
- Property may require cleansing given necromantic contamination
