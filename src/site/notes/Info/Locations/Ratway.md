# The Ratway

## Description
[[Info/Locations/Ratway\|The Ratway]] is the underground tunnel system beneath [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]], serving as shelter for the city's poorest residents, criminals, and outcasts. The network includes old cisterns, tunnels, and passages that connect to ancient catacombs.

## Features
- **Underground Network**: Damp, torch-lit tunnels beneath [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]]
- **Criminal Haven**: Refuge for outlaws, beggars, and those avoiding authority
- **Ancient Connections**: Passages leading to old catacombs and the [[Info/Locations/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall_of_the_Dead]]
- **Multiple Entrances**: Hidden access points throughout the city

## Key Areas

### [[Info/Locations/The_Ragged_Flagon\|The_Ragged_Flagon]]
- **Description**: Large tavern-like structure in a converted cistern chamber
- **Atmosphere**: Smoky fires, rough tables, broken chairs, smell of cheap ale and rot
- **Clientele**: Criminals, information brokers, and desperate folk
- **Notable Features**: Warped sign, multiple corridors leading deeper

### The Warrens
- **Description**: Side passages leading deeper into the tunnel system
- **Connection**: Links to ancient catacombs and [[Info/Locations/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall_of_the_Dead]]
- **Recent Events**: Site of deliberate arson attack in Session 012
- **Damage**: Burned archways with [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] markings

## NPCs
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Toren_the_Fence\|Toren_the_Fence]]**: Information broker and fence operating in the tunnels
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Yannick_the_Brawler\|Yannick_the_Brawler]]**: Drunk troublemaker spreading conspiracy theories
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Brooding_Corner_Patron\|Brooding_Corner_Patron]]**: Respected Dunmer with valuable information
- **Barman**: Unnamed proprietor of [[Info/Locations/The_Ragged_Flagon\|The_Ragged_Flagon]]
- **Various Refugees**: Displaced residents living in the tunnels

## Recent Events (Session 012)

### The Fire
- **Date**: Two nights before Session 012 (16th of Frostfall)
- **Location**: Started in the Warrens near the catacombs
- **Method**: Deliberately set using oil accelerants
- **Purpose**: Create chaos to cover theft from [[Info/Locations/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall_of_the_Dead]]
- **Perpetrators**: Three masked figures in black leathers with Daedric runes
- **Evidence**: [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] mark found on burned archways

### Investigation Results
- **Rumor Control**: Guards blamed for the fire, but investigation proved [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] cult involvement
- **Theft Confirmed**: Poisoned Dunmer body and documentation stolen from [[Info/Locations/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall_of_the_Dead]]
- **Trust Issues**: Tension between surface authorities and underground population

## Entrances
- **Canal Entrance**: Near the docks, through market area manholes
- **Outlaw's Refuge**: Hidden stairway behind boathouse near where Khajiit caravans stop

## Notes
- The [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] serves as a crucial information network for criminal activities
- Recent events have heightened tensions between residents and [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] authorities
- The connection to ancient catacombs makes it strategically important for cult activities
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]] seeks to maintain trust with underground population rather than use force
