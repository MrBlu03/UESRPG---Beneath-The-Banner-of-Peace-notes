# The Honed Edge

## Description
A high-end weaponsmith and fletcher in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] specializing in precision bladed weapons, composite bows, and specialized ammunition.

## Features
- Display cases featuring masterwork steel, silvered blades, and tempered hunting axes.
- Fletching workbench producing silver-tipped and bodkin arrows.

## NPCs
- Weaponsmith

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]]**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] purchased 50 silver arrows in preparation for hunting undead and suspected vampire threats in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]].
