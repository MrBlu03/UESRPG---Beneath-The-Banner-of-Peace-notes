# Widow's River

## Overview
A winding, ice-locked mountain stream cutting through the rocky valleys south of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], named in ancient lore for a Jarl's grieving wife who walked into its waters.

## Description
A frozen, snow-draped stream bed bordered by pine woods, frozen ponds, and treacherous rocky slopes.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **Wispmother**: Ethereal spirit encountered near a frozen pond along the river.

## Notable Events
- **Tracking the Crows' Riddle ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]]):** The party followed the frozen bed of the Widow's River to bypass patrolled roads, encountering an ethereal Wispmother and arriving at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Three_Brothers\|The_Three_Brothers]] standing stones.
