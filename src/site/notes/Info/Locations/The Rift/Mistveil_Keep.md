# Mistveil Keep

## Description
The fortified palace and royal seat of power in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], serving as the residence and administrative center for [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]] and her court.

## Features
- Grand stone throne room adorned with banners of the Rift.
- Tower laboratory used by Court Wizard [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Speaks-Spells-Gently\|Speaks-Spells-Gently]] for magical research and alchemy.
- Heavily guarded barracks and subterranean dungeon complex beneath the keep.
