# The Ratway

## Description
The Ratway is the damp, labyrinthine network of ancient stone sewers, drainage sluices, and sunken vaults running beneath the streets and canals of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. Originally built as city foundations, it has long served as a haven for thieves, outlaws, black market dealers, and clandestine organizations operating out of sight of the city guard. It features murky waterways, suspended timber walkways linking hidden cellars, and a thriving black market hub.
