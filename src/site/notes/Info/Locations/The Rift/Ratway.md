# The Ratway

## Description
The Ratway is the damp, labyrinthine network of ancient stone sewers, drainage sluices, and sunken vaults running beneath the streets and canals of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. Originally built as city foundations, it has long served as a haven for thieves, outlaws, black market dealers, and clandestine organizations operating out of sight of the city guard.

## Features
- **Vaulted Brick Sewers**: Murky waterways and suspended timber walkways linking hidden cellars.
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ragged_Flagon\|The Ragged Flagon]]**: A sunken subterranean tavern built around a central reservoir.
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Outlaws_Refuge\|Outlaws_Refuge]]**: A thriving black market hub where fences barter in illicit goods and stolen secrets.

## NPCs
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Toren_the_Fence\|Toren_the_Fence]]**: Underworld broker and black market dealer.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Brooding_Corner_Patron\|Brooding_Corner_Patron]]**: Enigmatic information broker in the Ragged Flagon.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Yannick_the_Brawler\|Yannick_the_Brawler]]**: Pit fighter in the subterranean arena.
