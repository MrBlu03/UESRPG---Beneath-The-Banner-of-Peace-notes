# The Ratway

## Overview
The vast, multi-level labyrinth of ancient drainage aqueducts, flooded tunnels, forgotten crypts, and criminal warrens extending beneath the streets of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Description
A dank, crumbling subterranean maze featuring iron portcullises, wooden walkways over stagnant water, hidden trapdoors, and secret connections to the city catacombs and [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall_of_the_Dead]].

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Toren_the_Fence\|Toren_the_Fence]] & [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Brooding_Corner_Patron\|Brooding_Corner_Patron]]**: Underworld figures.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]] & [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Harvold\|Captain_Harvold]]**: Attempt to monitor and police the upper levels.

## Notable Events
- **Cult Arson & Corpse Theft ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 012\|Session 012]]):** Three masked operatives in Daedric leathers set fire to the side warrens as a diversion, allowing them to breach the adjoining catacombs to steal [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith Velan]]'s corpse and autopsy records.
- **Diplomatic Investigation ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 012\|Session 012]]):** The party peacefully navigated the tunnels using food baskets provided by chef [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Brann\|Brann]], uncovering the cult conspiracy.
