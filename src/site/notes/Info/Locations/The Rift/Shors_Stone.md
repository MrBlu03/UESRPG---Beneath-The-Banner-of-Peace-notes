# Shor's Stone

## Description
A small, industrious mining village located north of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], famous for the rich ebony and iron veins of Redbelly Mine.

## Features
- Mine entrance built directly into the village square.
- Smelters billowing gray smoke over rustic timber homes.

## NPCs
- Miners
- Village Elders

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 019\|Session 019]]**: Passed during party transit, noting trade shipments heading toward [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
