# Outlaw's Refuge (Riften)

## Description
A subterranean black market and safe haven hidden in the brick foundations beneath [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], accessible through secluded grates and cellar trapdoors.

## Features
- Shrouded alcoves where fences buy stolen goods, lockpicks, and illicit substances.
- Underground fighting ring and gambling dens.
- Direct tunnel connections to the lower harbor canal.
