# Outlaw's Refuge (Riften)

## Overview
The subterranean sanctuary, black market trade hub, and criminal haven hidden within the deep stone vaults of the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ratway\|Ratway]] beneath [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Description
A dimly lit, vaulted brick undercroft filled with contraband stalls, smuggler caches, temporary bedrolls, and discreet transaction tables protected by unwritten underworld laws.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Toren_the_Fence\|Toren_the_Fence]]**: Prominent information broker and black market dealer.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Brooding_Corner_Patron\|Brooding_Corner_Patron]]**: Respected Dunmer elder and mediator.
- **Underworld Refugees**: Displaced smugglers and vagrants who fled guild purges in Windhelm.

## Notable Events
- **The Ratway Investigation ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 012\|Session 012]]):** The party brought a food donation basket from royal chef [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Brann\|Brann]], gained the trust of the residents, and purchased crucial intelligence from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Toren_the_Fence\|Toren_the_Fence]] regarding the cultists who set the catacomb fires.
