## Description
A hidden underground haven located beneath the city of Riften. It serves as a refuge for fences, smugglers, and those looking to operate outside the law. 

## Notable NPCs
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Toren_the_Fence\|Toren]]**: A contact located within the refuge.

## Key Events
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 012\|Session 012]]**: The party was directed to Toren in the Outlaw's Refuge by an amused cabbage-seller in the market after they asked strange questions regarding a fire.
