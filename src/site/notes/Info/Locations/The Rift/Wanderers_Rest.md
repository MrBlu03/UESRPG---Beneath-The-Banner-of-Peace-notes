# Wanderer's Rest

## Overview
A comfortable, well-managed inn and tavern in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], owned and operated by the jovial innkeeper [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] and his huntress daughter [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]].

## Description
A welcoming two-story wooden inn featuring hearty hearth fires, private upstairs bedrooms, and a lively common room popular with travelers and hunters.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]]**: Innkeeper and expedition leader.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]]**: Huntress, server, and local informant.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Stayed and socialized here during their time in Riften.

## Notable Events
- **Base in Riften ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]]):** Provided lodging and warm meals for the party as they took on early bounties.
- **Expedition Staging ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]):** Bjorin and Astrid staged their forest stag-hunting expedition from the inn.
