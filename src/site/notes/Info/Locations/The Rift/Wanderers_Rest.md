# Wanderer's Rest

## Description
A cozy, welcoming tavern and inn in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] frequented by travelers, honest merchants, and adventurers seeking respite.

## Features
- A warm circular central hearth with roasting spits.
- Comfortable second-story sleeping quarters overlooking the street.
- A popular hub for trading rumors, news of the roads, and local job postings.

## NPCs
- Barkeep
- Town Laborers
- Traveling Merchants

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]**: The party celebrated their return from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]] with mead and roasted game, unwinding after their first successful mission.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]-**[[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]**: Served as the party's primary gathering place and planning hub in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].
