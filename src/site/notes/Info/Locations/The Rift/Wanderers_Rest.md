# Wanderer's Rest

## Description
Wanderer's Rest is a cozy, welcoming tavern and inn in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] frequented by travelers, honest merchants, and adventurers seeking respite. It features a warm circular central hearth with roasting spits, comfortable second-story sleeping quarters, and is a popular hub for trading rumors and news.

## Notable NPCs
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]]**: Local friend of the party.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]]**: The innkeeper's daughter, who prepared hearty breakfasts.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonya]]**: Skald twins who occasionally performed here.
