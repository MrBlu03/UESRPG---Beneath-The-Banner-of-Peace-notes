## Description
Ysmir's Hall is a prominent shrine and meeting place in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] dedicated to [[Info/Lore/Shor\|Shor]] and Ysmir. It is operated by the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult_of_Ysmir]], preserving the sacred traditions of Nordic warrior-kings. The hall features runed pillars, fire pits, and votive offerings.

## Notable NPCs
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**: A young, blindfolded shaman adorned with antlers and feathers who leads the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult_of_Ysmir]] at the hall.
