# Ysmir's Hall (Hall of Ysmir)

## Overview
The grand chapterhouse and temple of the **[[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]]** in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], led by Harbinger [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]] and Shaman [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]. *(See also: [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall_of_Ysmir]])*

## Description
A solemn stone sanctuary featuring great fire trenches, prayer halls, sparring yards, and private chambers for cult shamans and honored guests.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]]**: Harbinger of Ysmir.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**: High Shaman and seer.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Resided here during their campaigns across the Rift.

## Notable Events
- **Cult Sanctuary & Guidance ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]] – [[2 -  Sessions/Session 013\|Session 013]]):** Served as the primary sanctuary and residence for the party in Riften, providing arcane consultations, woad training, and shelter.
