# Sweaty Steel

## Description
A bustling blacksmith workshop and armory in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], echoing constantly with the ring of hammer on anvil.

## Features
- Large stone charcoal forge, heavy quenching troughs, and sharpening wheels.
- Racks of steel and iron swords, shields, chainmail, and hunting daggers.

## NPCs
- Master Smith

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]**: Visited by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] to sharpen blades, repair armor, and evaluate weapon values.
