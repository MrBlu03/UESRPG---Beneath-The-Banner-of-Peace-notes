# Nimalten

## Description
A quiet timber-milling town situated in the western Rift along the river, known for its strategic bridge and watermills.

## Features
- Waterwheel sawmill processing timber from the surrounding birch and pine forests.
- Fortified bridge house guarding the road toward Ivarstead.

## NPCs
- Local Guards
- Woodcutters
