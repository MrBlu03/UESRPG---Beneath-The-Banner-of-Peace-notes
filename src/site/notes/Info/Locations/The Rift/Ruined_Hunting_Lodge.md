# Ruined Hunting Lodge

## Description
A crumbling, partially burned hunting lodge located deep in the border forest between the Rift and [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].

## Features
- Charred timber beams and collapsed roof trusses overgrown with wild mountain ivy.
- Claw marks on the doorframes and dried bloodstains on the floor.

## NPCs
- Vampire Survivor

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]]**: The party discovered an injured vampire survivor taking shelter in the ruins, obtaining crucial information about the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Skinstealer]]'s hunting patterns.
