# Kjall's Crossing

## Overview
A fertile agricultural crossroad and rural farming hamlet situated along the Kjall River north of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

## Description
A scenic valley dotted with vegetable fields, root cellars, and timber farmsteads known for producing cold-hardy crops.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Cabbage_Guy\|Olaf (Cabbage Guy)]]**: Local merchant and farmer who manages extensive cabbage and potato fields here.

## Notable Events
- **Produce Supply Lines ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]):** Sourced the fresh winter vegetables and cabbages sold in the Grand Markets of Riften and Kjalgrad.
