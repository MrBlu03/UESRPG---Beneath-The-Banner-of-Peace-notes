# The Rift

## Description
The Rift is one of the Old Holds of southeastern Skyrim, renowned for its golden autumn birch forests, fertile river valleys, and the vast expanse of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Lake_Honrich\|Lake_Honrich]]. Ruled by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]] from the fortified capital of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], the hold controls critical trade corridors connecting Skyrim with Morrowind and Cyrodiil.

## Features
- **Autumn Aspen Valleys**: Golden-leafed forests stretching from the foothills of the Jerall Mountains to the Velothi border.
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Lake_Honrich\|Lake_Honrich]] & River Treva**: The central waterways supporting fishing, watermills, and regional transport.
- **Fortified Bastions & Sanctuaries**: Ancient strongholds including Fort [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]], [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Fallowstone_Hall\|Fallowstone_Hall]], and the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall_of_Ysmir]].

## Settlements & Locations
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**: Hold capital and royal seat of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil_Keep]].
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Trevas_Bend]]**: River fishing community downstream from the dam.
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod_Estate]] & [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Dam\|Snow-Shod_Dam]]**: Ancestral farmlands and river irrigation works of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Snow-Shod\|Clan_Snow-Shod]].
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]**: Strategic military fortress guarding the northern highway.
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Fallowstone_Hall\|Fallowstone_Hall]]**: Revered sanctuary and mead hall of the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]].
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mount_Fjelhorst\|Mount_Fjelhorst]]**: Iconic snow-capped mountain peak on the eastern horizon.
