# Mount Fjelhorst

## Overview
A towering, snow-capped mountain peak dominating the western skyline of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The_Rift]]. It harbors ancient First Era [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Dragon_Cult\|Dragon Cult]] ruins and is the namesake of **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**.

## Description
A rugged, treacherous mountain featuring jagged granite crags, deep ravines, and ancient stone ruins populated by frost trolls and slumbering draugr.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **Ancient Draugr & Frost Trolls**: Inhabit the ancient ruins atop the summit.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Christened by Queen Freidr in honor of the mountain's enduring strength.

## Notable Events
- **Legends of the Old Hold ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]):** Hunter [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eydis\|Eydis]] recounted ancient tales of Dragon Cult ruins atop Mount Fjelhorst and the dangerous frost trolls roaming its slopes.
