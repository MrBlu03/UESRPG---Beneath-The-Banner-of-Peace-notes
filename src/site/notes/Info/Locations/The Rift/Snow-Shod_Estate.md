# Snow-Shod Estate

## Description
The prosperous ancestral manor, farmsteads, and fertile agricultural lands owned by [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Snow-Shod\|Clan_Snow-Shod]] in the river valley west of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Features
- Sprawling wheat fields, windmill grain mills, and apple orchards.
- A fortified Nord manor house with private guard towers.
