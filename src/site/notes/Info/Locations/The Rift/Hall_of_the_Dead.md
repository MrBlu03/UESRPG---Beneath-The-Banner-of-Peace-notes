# Hall of the Dead (Riften)

## Description
The subterranean consecrated crypts and mortuary of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], overseen by the mortician [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sigrid\|Sigrid]] to prepare deceased citizens for burial according to the rites of [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]].

## Features
- Cool stone slab tables for examinations, autopsies, and embalming.
- Extensive burial niches and catacombs holding generations of Nord families.
- Consecration braziers burning scented herbs to ward against necromancy.

## NPCs
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sigrid\|Sigrid]]
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Ralvard\|Ralvard]]
