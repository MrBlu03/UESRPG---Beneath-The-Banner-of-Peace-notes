# Amber Keg

## Overview
A lively, popular tavern and gathering hall situated in the bustling heart of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], frequented by city guards, merchants, sellswords, and common folk.

## Description
A boisterous wooden drinking hall with multiple roaring fireplaces, wide trestle tables, private alcoves, and a stage for traveling bards.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Grelka\|Grelka]]**: Notable local patron and trader.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Celebrated victories and toasted fallen comrades here.

## Notable Events
- **Evening Socials ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]]):** The party relaxed here with drinks following their return from Harmugstahl, meeting local contacts.
- **Toasts to the Fallen ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]):** Following the bloody liberation of Greenwall, the party joined a seasonal tavern celebration, raising mugs in solemn remembrance for fallen Companions [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen the Mer-Killer]].
