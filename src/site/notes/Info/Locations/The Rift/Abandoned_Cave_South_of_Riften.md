# Abandoned Cave South of Riften

## Description
A hidden limestone cave tucked into the rocky cliffs south of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], used by the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna_Tong]] to store smuggled contraband.

## Features
- Concealed entrance masked by hanging brush.
- Crates of illicit Morrowind goods, moon sugar, and forged coins.

## NPCs
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tharen_Duleri\|Tharen_Duleri]]
- Smugglers

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]**: The party cleared out the cave, defeated the smugglers, and secured critical ledger evidence.
