# Fort Greenwall

## Description
A formidable military fortress strategically positioned north of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] along the main road toward Eastmarch. Holds deep historical importance from the Three Banners War.

## Features
- Imposing stone ramparts, catapult platforms, and reinforced iron gates.
- A subterranean smuggler's tunnel network beneath the foundation dating to the Second Era.
- A corrupted ritual site discovered deep within the tunnels featuring a dark Crimson Orb connected to [[Info/Lore/Molag_Bal\|Molag Bal]].
