# The Shadow Stone

## Description
An ancient, mystical standing stone situated south of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], carved with constellations of the Shadow.

## Features
- Monolithic carved granite obelisk radiating subtle magical resonance.
- Grants the blessing of unseen movement and stealth to attuned travelers.

## NPCs
- Various local residents, travelers, and guards.

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]**: Investigated by [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] to study ancient constellation magic.
