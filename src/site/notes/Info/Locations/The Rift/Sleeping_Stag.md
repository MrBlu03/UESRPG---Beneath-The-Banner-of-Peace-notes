# The Sleeping Stag

## Overview
A rustic wayside tavern, drinking hall, and hunters' rest situated at a woodland fork along the road east of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], near [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Kahrus_Hollow\|Kahrus_Hollow]].

## Description
A low-ceilinged timber tavern featuring rough-hewn benches, game trophies on the walls, and a warm hearth where hunters and travelers gather to trade wilderness gossip.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eydis\|Eydis]]**: Hunter and bartender knowledgeable about local history and ancient ruins.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]] & [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonya]]**: Twin skalds who performed ancient ballads here.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hadvar\|Hadvar]]**: Cold-Moor noble who met the party here to commission the lodge investigation.

## Notable Events
- **The Cold-Moor Contract & Ballad ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]]):** Hadvar hired the party here to cleanse his ancestral hunting lodge; the twin skalds Senya and Sonya performed the ancient saga of Clan Cold-Moor, revealing the tragic love and murder of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]].
