# The Sleeping Stag

## Description
A rustic wayside inn and tavern along the road north of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], catering to woodsmen, trappers, and pilgrims.

## Features
- Mounted stag head above the bar.
- Warm pine interior smelling of roasted venison and spiced cider.

## NPCs
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eydis\|Eydis]]
- Local Hunters
