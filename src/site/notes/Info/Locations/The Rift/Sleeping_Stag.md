## Description
The Sleeping Stag is a rustic wayside inn and tavern along the road north of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], featuring a warm pine interior smelling of roasted venison and burning pine. It caters to woodsmen, trappers, and pilgrims, serving as a gathering place for locals and travelers alike.

## Notable NPCs
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eydis\|Eydis]]**: The bartender who knows local lore and rumors.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonya]]**: Twin skalds who perform here.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hadvar\|Hadvar]]**: A nobleman of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Cold-Moor\|Clan_Cold-Moor]] who frequented the tavern.
