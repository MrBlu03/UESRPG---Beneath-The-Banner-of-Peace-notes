# Riften

## Description
Riften is the fortified capital of The Rift in southeastern Skyrim, ruled by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]] from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil_Keep]]. Built around [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Lake_Honrich\|Lake Honrich]] and canal waterways, the city is a vibrant trade hub known for timber, mead, and fishing, but also notorious for criminal undercurrents and political rivalries.

## Features
- High stone walls and grand wooden gates guarding eastern and southern entrances.
- Canal networks and bustling lower docks supporting freshwater fishing and shipping.
- [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil_Keep]] overlooking the central marketplace and artisan quarter.
- The subterranean Ratway network beneath the streets housing outlaws and black markets.
