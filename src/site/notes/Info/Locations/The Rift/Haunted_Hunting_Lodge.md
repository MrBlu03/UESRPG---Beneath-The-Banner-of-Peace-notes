# Haunted Hunting Lodge

## Overview
An ancient, ancestral timber lodge standing eerily intact amidst the ruined foundations of a forgotten First Era village in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Kahrus_Hollow\|Kahrus_Hollow]], east of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. Built upon blood-soaked ground where a tragic clan massacre occurred centuries ago.

## Description
A sturdy, weathered timber hall with a never-freezing courtyard well, cold drafty corridors, a locked master bedroom, and an ancient burial mound behind the estate.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hadvar\|Hadvar]]**: Noble heir of Clan Cold-Moor who inherited the property and hired the party.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]]**: Ancient ghost of Clan Red-Grain who haunted the estate.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven Cold-Moor]]**: Undead draugr bound by an ancient grief curse.

## Notable Events
- **The Cold-Moor Contract ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]]):** The party explored the lodge, encountering the spirit of Lagertha in the upper window and Sven Cold-Moor emerging from the barrow. Sven moved the heavy wardrobe to reveal his journal and pointed to the well.
- **Consecration & Rest ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 009\|Session 009]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]):** [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] retrieved Lagertha's skull and floral bone necklace from the well; the party consecrated the grounds with holy water and laid both souls to rest in a joint funeral pyre.
