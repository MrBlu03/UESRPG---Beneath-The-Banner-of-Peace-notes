# Western Woods

## Description
Dense, rugged woodlands west of the main settlements, such as [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] and [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]. It is a wild area where forgotten tunnels and abandoned hunter camps can be found.

## Notable NPCs
None specific to this location.

## Key Events
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 006\|Session 006]]**: The party was briefed on a mission to infiltrate [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] through a forgotten smugglers' tunnel. They were instructed that any survivors found during the mission should be sent through the tunnel and into the safety of the western woods.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 023a Periwyns witch hunt\|Session 023a Periwyns witch hunt]]**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] traversed the western woods outside [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] during her hunt, finding an abandoned hunter camp.
