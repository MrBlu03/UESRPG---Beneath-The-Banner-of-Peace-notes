# Western Woods

## Overview
The dense, rugged woodlands stretching across the western frontiers outside settlements in eastern and western Skyrim.

## Description
A vast forest of pine and rocky crags, home to wild beasts, old hunter trails, and isolated clearings including the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Witchs_Clearing\|Witch's Clearing]].

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Quests/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|The_Witch_of_the_Woods]]**: Inhabits a secluded clearing in the western woods of Kjalgrad.

## Notable Events
- **Hagraven Truce ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 023a Periwyns witch hunt\|Session 023a Periwyns witch hunt]]):** [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] tracked horse attacks into the western woods outside Kjalgrad, negotiating a territorial truce with the Witch of the Woods.
