# Snow-Shod Dam

## Overview
A heavy stone and timber river dam situated along the Treva River near [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod_Estate]], regulating water flow downstream toward [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Trevas_Bend]].

## Description
A rugged hydraulic structure of stone piers and heavy timber floodgates, flanked by moss-covered stones and drooping willows.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **Snow-Shod Guards**: Stationed to protect the dam.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith_Velan]]**: Dunmer agent who sabotaged the mechanism.

## Notable Events
- **The Dam Sabotage Investigation ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]):** The party discovered the dam had been deliberately jammed with an ungreased lever mechanism bearing the carved serpent insignia of the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]. Upstream, they found the poisoned corpse of Dunmer agent [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith_Velan]], exposing a conspiracy to inflame regional feud.
