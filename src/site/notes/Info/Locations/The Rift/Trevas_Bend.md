# Treva's Bend

## Overview
A low-lying agricultural riverbank settlement located along the Treva River downstream from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod_Estate]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The_Rift]], led by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Alderman Torgir]].

## Description
A humble farming village featuring timber cabins, muddy pathways, millraces, and riverside vegetable fields dependent on the Treva River's flow.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Alderman Torgir]]**: Respected civic leader and alderman.
- **Riverbank Farmers**: Local populace dependent on irrigation.

## Notable Events
- **The Water Stoppage Crisis ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 001\|Session 001]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]):** Suffered severe water shortages threatening crops; the party resolved the dispute by clearing the sabotaged Snow-Shod Dam and uncovering the assassin's plot, earning a 50 gold reward from Alderman Torgir.
