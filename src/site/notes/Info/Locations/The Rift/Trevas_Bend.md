# Treva's Bend

## Description
A low-lying fishing village nestled along a bend in the river southwest of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. Highly dependent on seasonal river fish runs, the settlement features muddy paths, wooden docks, drying racks for fishing nets, smokehouses, and modest thatched-roof timber cottages.

## Notable NPCs
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Alderman Torgir]]**: The cautious and suspicious leader of Treva's Bend.
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Eldar]]**: A local fisherman.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith Velan]]**: A Dunmer provocateur working for the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]].
