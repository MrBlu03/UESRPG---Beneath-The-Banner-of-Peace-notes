# Hall of Ysmir

## Description
A sacred stone temple and shrine dedicated to Ysmir (the Dragon of the North), where Nord pilgrims gather to honor ancient heroes and the Thu'um.

## Features
- A colossal carved stone statue of Ysmir wreathed in ceremonial braziers.
- Inscribed stone tablets recording ancient sagas and Dragon War chronicles.
