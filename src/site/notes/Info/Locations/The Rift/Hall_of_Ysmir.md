# Hall of Ysmir

## Description
A sacred stone temple and shrine dedicated to Ysmir, the Dragon of the North, where Nord pilgrims gather to honor ancient heroes and the Thu'um. It features a colossal carved stone statue of Ysmir wreathed in ceremonial braziers, pilgrim benches, meditation alcoves, and inscribed stone tablets recording ancient sagas and Dragon War chronicles.
