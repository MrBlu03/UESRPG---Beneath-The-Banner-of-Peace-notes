# Hall of Ysmir (Ysmir's Hall)

## Overview
The grand stone temple, chapterhouse, and sacred sanctuary of the **[[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]]** located in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. Led by Harbinger [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]] and Shaman [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]], it serves as a center for warrior training, ancient Nordic rites, and spiritual sanctuary.

## Description
A towering, solemn stone hall featuring grand firepits, sparring arenas, an ancient shrine to Ysmir, alchemical and woad preparation rooms, and upper living quarters for cult shamans and honored guests.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]]**: Harbinger and martial leader of the cult.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**: Blindfolded seer and high shaman.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vidir\|Vidir (Víðir)]]**: Ancient mystic warrior and temple guardian.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Resided here during their operations across the Rift.

## Notable Events
- **Base of Operations ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 009\|Session 009]]):** The party stayed here while investigating the Haunted Hunting Lodge, receiving cryptic guidance and spiritual aid from Shaman Adda.
- **Dock Conspiracies ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]):** [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torvald_Black-Foot\|Torvald_Black-Foot]] visited the hall in a torrential downpour to recruit the party to protect Argonian dock workers from Camonna Tong harassment.
- **Departure for Falderstad ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 013\|Session 013]]):** [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] returned sacred texts to Adda before the party embarked on their northern journey.
