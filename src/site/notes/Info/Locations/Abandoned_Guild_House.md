# Abandoned Guild House

## Basic Information
**Type:** Abandoned Guild Building (Former Tannery)  
**Location:** Northeast side of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Region:** Eastern Skyrim  
**Status:** Abandoned/Derelict  
**Current Use:** Clandestine meeting location

## Description
A former tannery that has been abandoned and fallen into disrepair, now serving as a meeting location for [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s criminal operations. The building provides privacy and discretion for illegal activities while remaining accessible to those who know of its use.

## Structural Condition
- **Roof**: Collapsed due to heavy snow accumulation
- **Main Floor**: Mostly empty with minimal remaining infrastructure
- **Basement**: Accessed via narrow staircase, more intact than upper level
- **Construction**: Damp shale rock basement construction
- **Atmosphere**: Horrible smell throughout, particularly in basement

## Layout and Features

### Main Floor
- Large empty space with evidence of former tannery operations
- Collapsed sections from roof damage
- Narrow staircase at far end leading to basement
- Minimal remaining equipment or furnishings

### Basement Level
- **Construction**: Shale rock walls, damp conditions
- **Size**: Wider at base than staircase suggests
- **Furnishing**: Table with map of the Rift
- **Usage**: Meeting room for criminal coordination
- **Security**: Remote location, minimal foot traffic
- **Equipment**: Basic furniture for operational planning

## Criminal Operations

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]] Usage
- Meeting location for [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hooded_Figure\|Hooded_Figure]]
- Coordination point for caravan interception operation
- Planning location with detailed regional maps
- Exchange point for payment and operational symbols

### Operational Security
- Remote location in abandoned district of city
- Difficult to observe or approach unnoticed
- Basement provides additional concealment
- Previous legitimate use provides cover for activity

## Strategic Value
- **Privacy**: Abandoned status ensures minimal witnesses
- **Accessibility**: Located within city but in unused area
- **Planning Space**: Basement suitable for operational coordination
- **Concealment**: Derelict appearance discourages casual investigation
- **Centrality**: Northeast location accessible from multiple directions

## Environmental Conditions
- **Odor**: Strong unpleasant smells from former tannery operations
- **Moisture**: Damp conditions, particularly in basement
- **Lighting**: Limited natural light, requires artificial illumination
- **Temperature**: Cold conditions typical of abandoned structures
- **Structural Integrity**: Compromised but basement remains usable

## Criminal Network Infrastructure
- Part of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s operational network
- Suitable for meetings with court-connected figures like [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hooded_Figure\|Hooded_Figure]]
- Provides map storage and planning capabilities
- Safe location for exchange of criminal payment and symbols

## Significance
The Abandoned Guild House represents the infrastructure that supports organized criminal activity in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. Its use by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hooded_Figure\|Hooded_Figure]] demonstrates how criminals adapt abandoned urban infrastructure for their operational needs while maintaining proximity to legitimate activities.

## Security Considerations
- Isolated location reduces chance of observation
- Multiple exit routes available through ruined structure
- Basement provides additional layer of concealment
- Abandoned status makes regular surveillance unlikely

## Future Use Potential
- Likely to continue serving as criminal meeting location
- Could be used for storage of contraband or illegal goods
- May serve as safe house or temporary hideout
- Potential site for future criminal operations coordination

## Notes
- Former tannery operations explain persistent odors
- Roof collapse suggests building abandoned for extended period
- Basement preservation makes it valuable for criminal use
