# Western Woods (Kjalgrad)

## Basic Information
**Type:** Forest Region  
**Region:** The Rift, Skyrim (west of [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]])  
**Status:** Active  
**Significance:** Hunting grounds; home to supernatural threats

## Description
The western woods outside [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]] serve as hunting grounds for local hunters. The area is cold and wild, with dense forest cover that makes tracking difficult. Recently, the woods have become dangerous due to supernatural activity.

## Notable Locations
- **Abandoned Hunter Camp:** Left behind after the witch attack
- **Dead Horse Site:** Where Bjorn's horse was killed by the witch
- **Witch's Clearing:** The creature's territorial center

## Key Events

### [[Session 023a\|Session 023a]] — Witch Activity
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] traveled through these woods to investigate the witch bounty:
- Found Bjorn's dead horse with claw marks and burn marks
- Discovered the abandoned hunter camp
- Tracked footprints to the witch's clearing
- Negotiated a truce with the supernatural creature

## Dangers
- **The Witch of the Woods:** A hagravenn-like creature residing in a clearing
- **Territorial Creature:** Attacks hunters and livestock that enter its domain
- **Harsh Weather:** -18°C temperatures during [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s investigation

## Significance
These woods demonstrate that supernatural threats exist close to settled areas like [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]], requiring hunters and adventurers to deal with creatures that don't fit into normal understandings of wildlife.

## Session 026 — The Truce Becomes Plot-Critical
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|Milos]] (the Vestige) departed the [[Info/Locations/Barrow_of_the_Oath_Breaker_King\|Barrow_of_the_Oath_Breaker_King]] for the western woods to retrieve a **hag raven feather** from the Witch of the Woods:
- The feather is a required ingredient for the **Ward-dissolving varnish** needed to breach the secondary entrances of the main facility (see [[Info/Quests/Assault_Main_Facility\|Assault_Main_Facility]])
- Milos indicated he would either negotiate or take what he needs
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s prior diplomatic truce (Session 023a) gave him context to work from
- His reaction on learning Periwyn had brokered a deal with a hagraven: *"I swear. Bretons and hag-ravens."*

The monthly tribute arrangement (hearts, eyeballs, gems) may affect whether the Witch cooperates willingly. **Status: outcome not yet known.**
