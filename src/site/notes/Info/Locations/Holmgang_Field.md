# Holmgang Field

## Basic Information
**Type:** Ritual/Execution Site  
**Location:** Outside [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] (approximately one hour's walk)  
**Region:** Eastern Skyrim  
**Other Names:** The Standing Stones, Execution Site  
**Status:** Active sacred site

## Description
A circle of ancient standing stones half-buried in frost, located outside [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. The Holmgang Field serves as the traditional location for Nordic executions, duels, and related ceremonies. The location holds deep religious significance and is prepared with specific ritual elements for each execution.

The name "Holmgang" refers to an ancient Nordic tradition of resolving disputes through formal combat or judgment.

## Physical Features
- **Standing Stones**: Ancient stones arranged in a circle, partially buried by centuries of frost
- **Braziers**: Placed at each cardinal direction, burning with thick grey smoke during ceremonies
- **Fire Pit**: Central area where [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] creates ritual circles of ash
- **Surrounding Area**: Snow-covered ground with woods nearby

## Ritual Significance
The Holmgang Field serves as a liminal space between the mortal world and Sovngarde, where:
- The gods ([[Shor\|Shor]] and [[Kyne\|Kyne]]) bear witness to justice
- The condemned face their final moments
- Blood debts are paid according to Nordic Law

## Key Events

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]] - The Dunmer Execution (11th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600)

#### Preparations
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] (Lawspeaker) completed a circle of ash around the fire pit
- Braziers burned at each cardinal direction with thick grey smoke
- The crowd formed a ring around the circle
- Ravens circled overhead

#### The Procession
Participants walked from [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] for nearly an hour:
1. Jarl [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gorm\|Gorm]]
2. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] (Lawspeaker)
3. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]], [[Sonja\|Sonja]], and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Agni\|Agni]] (Skalds playing "Suurin")
4. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]
5. [[The Fangs of Fjelhorst\|The Fangs of Fjelhorst]]
6. Everyone else

#### The Ritual
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] served as **Lawspeaker**, reciting the ritual words and calling upon the gods
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] served as the executioner in full ceremonial garb
- The three condemned were executed with a **Stalhrim blade** from the time of Ysgramor
- The blood oath chant concluded the ceremony: *"Today the Law takes blood. Today the debt is paid. Shor witness. Kyne carry them home. The blood oath is sealed."*

#### Post-Execution
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Garen_Hlaalu\|Garen_Hlaalu]] defiantly shouted: *"You've won nothing. Skyrim will remember Morrowind's blood spilled on your snow. The serpent coils. The serpent waits. Skyrim will burn."*
- A feast was held at [[Info/Locations/The_Soggy_Beaver\|The_Soggy_Beaver]] with **Boar's Blood Mead**
