# Arkayfell

## Description
A location linked to Periwyn's past and her former home of North Point. It was the site where Periwyn killed Sir Gareth and appeared prominently within her visionary dream-realm.

## Notable NPCs
- None

## Key Events
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)\|Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)]]**: The chapel transformed into Arkayfell within Periwyn's dream, where she saw herself younger, kneeling beside Sir Gareth's dying body.
