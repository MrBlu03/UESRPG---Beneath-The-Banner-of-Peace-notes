# Arkayfell

## Overview
A sacred mountain fortress, monastery, and chapel of the **[[Info/Organizations/Religious/Knights_of_Arkay\|Knights of the Order of Arkay]]** located in the rugged mountains of western Skyrim. It served as the childhood home and training sanctuary of [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] under her mentor [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sir_Gareth\|Sir_Gareth]].

## Description
A stone redoubt and cloister featuring training yards, scriptoriums, and a grand chapel dedicated to Arkay, encircled by wild alpine roses.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sir_Gareth\|Sir_Gareth]]**: Knight Commander and combat instructor who wielded his signature warhammer.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Raised and trained here as a squire before the sanctuary fell.

## Notable Events
- **The Vampire Siege & Tragedy:** Overrun by a deadly vampire incursion; during the siege, Sir Gareth was infected and mercy-killed by Periwyn, while Periwyn lost her left ear and fled with Gareth's warhammer.
- **Spiritual Absolution ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)\|Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)]]):** Recreated in Periwyn's dreamscape where the spirit of Sir Gareth manifested to grant her emotional peace and drive off the vampire [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lady_Elaris\|Lady_Elaris]].
