# Arkayfell

## Description
A solemn mountain stronghold and sanctuary dedicated to [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]], deeply intertwined with the ancestral history of [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s lineage, House Lunclaire.

## Features
- High stone chapel warded with consecration runes against necromancy and undead.
- Family crypts of the Lunclaire knights who fell fighting supernatural abominations.
