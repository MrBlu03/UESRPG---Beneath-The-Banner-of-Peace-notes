# Harmugstahl

## Description
An isolated, spider-infested dungeon and natural cavern network situated in the rugged cliffs of The Reach, infamous for unnatural alchemical breeding experiments.

## Features
- Caverns choked with thick cobwebs and aggressive frostbite spider nests.
- Ancient lever-operated gate mechanisms and locked alchemical holding pens.

## NPCs
- Missing Alchemist

- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]]**: Subject of an investigative bounty regarding missing scholars and strange arachnid mutations.
