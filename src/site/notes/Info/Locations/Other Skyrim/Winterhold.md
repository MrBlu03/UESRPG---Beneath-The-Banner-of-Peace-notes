# Winterhold

## Overview
The ancient, wind-swept coastal capital of northern Skyrim and the renowned home of the **College of Winterhold**, Tamriel's premier institution for arcane scholarship, elemental magic, and ancient history.

## Description
A historic city perched high on coastal sea cliffs, dominated by the towering stone bridges, arcane spires, and grand library vaults of the College.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **College Mages & Scholars**: Researchers in runology, restoration, and elemental magic.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]**: Elder mage of Falderstad who completed his arcane education at the College.

## Notable Events
- **Arcane Reference Point ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]]):** Elder mage [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] utilized knowledge preserved in College archives to decipher the Othala rune and prepare consecrated fire salts against the Ragged Lord.
- **Artifact Transit Hub ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]):** Served as the overland transit point receiving the Harrowstorm Engine Core from Greenwall before its final sea passage to the vaults of Eyevea.
