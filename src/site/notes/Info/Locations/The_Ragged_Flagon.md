# The Ragged Flagon

## Description
[[Info/Locations/The_Ragged_Flagon\|The_Ragged_Flagon]] is a makeshift tavern located in the depths of the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]], converted from an old cistern chamber. It serves as the main gathering place for [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]]'s criminal underworld and desperate residents.

## Features
- **Converted Cistern**: Large underground chamber with arched ceilings
- **Rough Establishment**: Broken chairs, rough tables scattered around smoky fires
- **Criminal Hub**: Meeting place for information brokers, fences, and outlaws
- **Warped Sign**: Wooden sign marking the establishment

## Atmosphere
- **Lighting**: Torch-lit with dancing shadows on stone walls
- **Smells**: Cheap ale, sweat, rot, and dampness
- **Sounds**: Clatter of mugs, muffled laughter, occasional arguments echoing off stone
- **Clientele**: Criminals, refugees, information brokers, and those avoiding surface authority

## Notable NPCs
- **Barman**: Unnamed proprietor who cleans mugs and serves drinks
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Toren_the_Fence\|Toren_the_Fence]]**: Information broker operating from a corridor with gates
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Yannick_the_Brawler\|Yannick_the_Brawler]]**: Drunk troublemaker who spreads conspiracy theories
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Brooding_Corner_Patron\|Brooding_Corner_Patron]]**: Respected Dunmer who maintains order and has valuable information

## Layout
- **Main Chamber**: Central tavern area with tables and fire pits
- **Corridors**: Multiple passages leading deeper into the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]]
- **Fencing Operation**: Gated corridor where [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Toren_the_Fence\|Toren_the_Fence]] conducts business
- **Corner Areas**: Various seating areas where patrons gather

## Recent Events (Session 012)
- **Information Hub**: Served as central location for investigating the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] fires
- **Tension**: Heightened due to accusations against [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] guards
- **Cooperation**: Ultimately provided crucial intelligence about [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] involvement

## Services
- **Drinks**: Ale and other alcoholic beverages (including Sujama from Morrowind)
- **Information Brokerage**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Toren_the_Fence\|Toren_the_Fence]] provides intelligence for gold
- **Fencing**: Illegal goods trading through [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Toren_the_Fence\|Toren_the_Fence]]
- **Sanctuary**: Refuge for those avoiding surface authorities

## Location
- **Access**: Hidden entrance behind boathouse near Khajiit caravan stop
- **Depth**: Deep within the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] tunnel system
- **Connections**: Multiple passages leading to other areas of the underground network

## Notes
- Represents the heart of [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]]'s criminal underworld
- Important location for gathering intelligence about underground activities
- Demonstrates the delicate balance between surface authority and underground autonomy
