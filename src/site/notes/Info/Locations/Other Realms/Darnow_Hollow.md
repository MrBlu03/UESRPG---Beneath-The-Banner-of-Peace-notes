# Darnow Hollow

## Overview
A spectral, crumbling yet regal estate standing atop a misty hill within [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s psychological dreamscape during her comatose slumber.

## Description
A grand, weathered country manor marked by an old wooden signpost reading *"Darnow Hollow"*, overlooking a surreal landscape that shifted between rural memories and traumatic battlefields.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Navigated the estate during her inner trial of guilt, grief, and conscience.

## Notable Events
- **The Road to Darnow Hollow ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)\|Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)]]):** Served as the entry threshold into Periwyn's spiritual confrontation, leading from the crumbling manor toward the chapel of roses where she faced [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lady_Elaris\|Lady_Elaris]] and received absolution from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sir_Gareth\|Sir_Gareth]].
