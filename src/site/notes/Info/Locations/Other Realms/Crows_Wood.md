# Crow's Wood

## Description
A shadowy, surreal pocket realm of the Evergloam ruled by the sentient corvids of the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather_Court]], under the overarching domain of [[Info/Lore/Nocturnal\|Nocturnal]].

## Features
- Perpetual twilight gloom beneath twisted, leafless purple-black trees.
- Ruined, spectral architecture reflecting twisted memories of mortal sanctuaries.
- Swirling flocks of talking crows and raven courts acting as judges and tricksters.
