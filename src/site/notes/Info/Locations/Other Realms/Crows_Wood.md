# Crow's Wood

## Overview
An eerie, twilight pocket realm situated within the outer reaches of [[Evergloam\|Evergloam]], Nocturnal's Daedric domain. Described by its master as a secluded refuge created away from prying eyes, it is populated by intelligent Daedric crows of the [[Blackfeather_Court\|Blackfeather Court]].

## Description
A perpetually moonlit forest of twisted, dark-barked trees, whispering winds, and ancient stone ruins bathed in violet and midnight hues.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige / Crowfather]]**: Oversees the pocket realm and commands its crow sentries.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron_Jangleplume]] & [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Knave_of_Rooks\|Knave_of_Rooks]]**: Avian courtiers of the Blackfeather Court.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**: Cult shaman who accesses the realm for spiritual healing.

- **Periwyn's Slumber Vision ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)\|Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)]]):** [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] entered Crow's Wood during her comatose state, meeting a cloaked crow figure who granted her a humming black feather of protection.
- **Sanctuary for Varran ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 021\|Session 021]]):** Shaman [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] brought the traumatized Imperial courier [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] to Crow's Wood to recover from severe psychological trauma away from the mortal plane.
