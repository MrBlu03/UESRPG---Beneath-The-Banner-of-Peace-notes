# Boran's Bullwarks

## Description
Boran's Bullwarks is a shop in [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] owned by Boran, specializing in cold weather gear and fur trading. The shop offers group discounts for bulk purchases and serves as both a supplier of winter equipment and a buyer of pelts and furs.

## Services
- **Cold Weather Gear**: Bear cloaks and winter protection equipment
- **Fur Trading**: Purchases pelts and furs from hunters and trappers
- **Group Discounts**: Reduced prices for bulk purchases

## Party History

### Session 013 - Equipment Purchase
- Party purchased 3 bear cloaks at 75 gold each (25% discount from standard 100 gold price)
- Sold 100 gold worth of bear fur to the shop
- Sold additional small fur worth 10 gold
- Demonstrated Boran's willingness to offer fair deals for bulk transactions

- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Boran\|Boran]]**: Owner and proprietor of the shop, known for fair dealing and group discounts
