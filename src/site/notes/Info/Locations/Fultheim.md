# Fultheim

## Basic Information
**Type:** Town/Settlement  
**Region:** Skyrim  
**Status:** Active  
**Significance:** Location where Coiled Serpent ledger was handed off

## Description
Fultheim is a settlement in Skyrim where Dunmer smugglers received the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] ledger from a mysterious contact.

## Key Events

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]] - The Ledger Handoff
According to interrogation of Dunmer prisoners in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]:
- The smugglers met their contact in **Fultheim's market**
- The contact resembled a **Temple Guard**
- He smelled of **sulfur** (possibly from proximity to Morrowind's volcanic regions)
- The ledger bearing the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] symbol was given to them
- The smugglers departed Fultheim the next day
- They were ultimately captured before completing delivery to [[Kil_Great_White\|Kil_Great_White]]

## Investigative Value
- **Temple Guard Connection**: The contact's appearance suggests possible Tribunal Temple or Dunmer religious connections
- **Sulfur Smell**: May indicate travel from Morrowind (near Red Mountain/volcanic areas)
- **Market Location**: Public meeting suggests confidence or cover story
