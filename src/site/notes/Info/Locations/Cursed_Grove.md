# Cursed Grove

## Basic Information
**Type:** Supernatural Grove / Ragged Lord Territory  
**Location:** Deep within [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]] east of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Region:** Eastern Skyrim  
**Status:** Active supernatural threat area  
**Nature:** Cursed/corrupted natural area, claimed by Skinstealer
**Entity**: Territory of the [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Skinstealer\|Skinstealer]] (Ragged Lord aspect of Hircine)

## Description
A cursed section of the [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]] that exists outside the natural order, described by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] as "not Kyne's forest." The area exhibits supernatural corruption with carved totems, supernatural creatures, and an oppressive atmosphere that affects both animals and humans who enter.

## Environmental Characteristics

### Atmospheric Changes
- **Air Quality**: Heavy, oppressive atmosphere
- **Sound**: Reduced animal sounds, unnatural quiet
- **Smell**: Sweet rot permeating the area
- **Temperature**: Supernatural chill beyond normal forest conditions
- **Visibility**: Dense tree growth creating shadowed environment

### Terrain Features
- **Tree Density**: Trees grow closer together than natural forest
- **Ground Level**: Dips down creating hollow-like depressions
- **Clearing**: Central hollow where supernatural events occur
- **Approach**: Accessible only through specific woodland paths

## Supernatural Elements

### The Othala Rune
- **Appearance**: Carved on back of trees throughout the territory
- **Meaning**: Heritage, inheritance, bloodline, burden (identified by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]])
- **Significance**: The [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Skinstealer\|Skinstealer]] claims this land as its inheritance
- **Theory**: The creature has inherited this land and its sins
- **Timeline**: Markings added over 3-4 weeks of activity

### Carved Totems
- **Appearance**: Carved into tree trunks throughout the area
- **Design**: Human-like but "not quite" human features
- **Damage**: Cut marks from massive claws visible on totems
- **Distribution**: Some with holes, various designs scattered through grove
- **Purpose**: Territorial markers, possible ritual objects, or mockery of human culture
- **Freshly Added**: New totems continue to appear

### Half-Frozen Stream
- **Location**: En route to the grotesque clearing
- **Iron Nails**: Hundreds driven into trees around the bank
- **Burned Totem**: One totem half-turned to ash, as if struck by lightning
- **Blood Stains**: Snow stained red with strange sap
- **Skinstealer's Blood**: Warm blood-like substance leaking from totems
- **Ritual Site**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]] performed successful cleansing ritual here

### The Grotesque Clearing (Ragged Lord's Shrine)
**Located several hours into territory, reached around 11 AM-noon**

**Ground Features:**
- No snow, no grass - supernaturally barren
- Described as "wrong" and outside natural order

**Center Piece:**
- Monolith of bones fused together with permafrost
- All kinds of bones arranged in concentric circles
- Pattern recognized by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] from old Winterhold texts
- **Identified as**: Markings of the Ragged Lord, aspect of Hircine

**Hides and Skins:**
- Human skins hanging around clearing like banners
- First time party saw human remains displayed
- Patched together in grotesque tapestry
- Layered like a mosaic, like shards to itself
- **Purpose**: Shrine to itself, mockery of victims

**Daedric Association:**
- Ragged Lord: Associated with mockery and hunter's madness
- Aspect of Hircine (Daedric Prince of the Hunt)
- Represents twisted, mad side of hunting

### Supernatural Boundaries
- **Recognition**: Experienced locals like [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] can identify the boundary
- **Warning Signs**: Environmental changes indicate transition into cursed area
- **Animal Behavior**: Hunting dogs and wildlife refuse to enter
- **Spiritual Awareness**: Described as outside Kyne's (natural) domain

- Howl sounded very close behind them

- Creature howled and disappeared after display of power

## Supernatural Threats

### The Skinstealer (Ragged Lord)
- **Presence**: Invisible but audible through distinctive howl
- **Behavior**: Pursues intruders who violate grove boundaries
- **Territory**: Controls access to cursed grove area
- **Danger Level**: Capable of chasing away armed hunting party
- **Connection**: Responsible for claw marks on totems and stag's wounds
- **Abilities**:
  - Voice mimicry (perfectly copies human voices)
  - Spatial manipulation (creates infinite forest loops)
  - Track erasure (removes all footprints)
  - Teleportation (materializes suddenly)
  - Throat-cutting efficiency in kills
- **Weakness**: Vulnerable to fire, specifically Kyne's flame
- **Wolf Pack**: Commands 3 compelled wolves
- **Physical**: 45 cm ebony-sharp claws, immense strength

### Environmental Hazards
- **Psychological Impact**: Causes fear and disorientation
- **Animal Rejection**: Natural creatures avoid or flee the area
- **Supernatural Corruption**: Area exists outside natural order
- **Pursuit Risk**: Intruders may be chased by territorial entities

## Significance
The Cursed Grove represents the heart of supernatural corruption affecting the [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]]. Its existence outside the natural order and the Skinstealer's presence as a Ragged Lord aspect of Hircine suggest a significant Daedric threat. The grove serves as both territory and shrine for the creature, where it displays its victims and performs its mockery of the hunt. The discovery of the Skinstealer's fire weakness provides hope for reclaiming the land.
