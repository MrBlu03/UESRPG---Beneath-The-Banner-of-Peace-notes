# Falderstad

## Basic Information
**Type:** City  
**Region:** Eastern Skyrim  
**Status:** Active  
**Climate:** Harsh winter conditions (-14°C during party's arrival)

## Description
Falderstad is a Nordic city in eastern Skyrim, characterized by severe winter weather and well-defended gates. The city maintains active guard patrols who question visitors about their business before granting entry. Despite the harsh climate, it serves as a waypoint for travelers and offers accommodations for those journeying through the region.

## Key Features
- **City Gates:** Well-guarded entrance with active security protocols (eastern gate reinforced with fire defenses)
- **Guard Posts:** Manned by guards who question visitors' intentions  
- **Accommodations:** Directs travelers to [[Info/Locations/The_Wounded_Boar\|The_Wounded_Boar]] inn
- **Stables:** Facilities for horse grooming and care
- **Market Square:** Central gathering point with new hawk totem (erected Session 017)
- **Dylin's House:** Permanent safe house granted to [[The Fangs of Fjelhorst\|The Fangs of Fjelhorst]]
- **Hunter's Bell:** Used to rally hunters for expeditions and signal victories

## Establishments
- **[[Info/Locations/The_Wounded_Boar\|The_Wounded_Boar]]**: Inn recommended by city guards for visitor accommodations
