# Kjalgrad Warehouse District

## Basic Information
**Type:** Warehouse and Docking District  
**Location:** [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Region:** The Rift, Skyrim  
**Status:** Active  
**Controlled By:** [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] (effective)

## Description
The warehouse district of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] handles the storage and distribution of goods flowing through the city's guild-controlled trade network. The district has been the site of suspicious activity: night deliveries, sealed crates with no manifest explanation, and workers reporting "less than reputable" individuals asking questions around the premises.

## Notable Locations

### Warehouse 7
The party investigated Warehouse 7 in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]] after receiving intelligence about unusual activity:

**Contents of Sealed Crates:**
- **[[Info/1 - Party Findings/Coin_Molds\|Coin_Molds]]** — matching the shape of brimstone gold coins
- Gemstones (cut and uncut)
- Chalk
- **Black candles**
- Bone, sinew, and pig hearts — listed on any manifest only as "animal parts"

**The Brimstone Gold Connection:**
A warehouse worker commented that the brimstone gold **"smells like Daedric anchors from the war"** — a reference to the Planemeld and the Daedric anchors that brought Coldharbour to Tamriel during Molag Bal's invasion. This suggests the worker has direct experience with Daedric artifacts and recognizes the smell.

**The Coin Molds:**
The molds found match the distinctive shape of brimstone gold. Their presence in a guild warehouse alongside black candles and ritual components (bone, sinew, hearts) points to an active manufacturing or ritual process — either producing counterfeit brimstone gold or performing ceremonies related to the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]]'s Daedric activities.

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]'s Actions:**
- Durov paid **500 gold of his own coin** to the warehouse supervisor to hold the crates
- The supervisor accepted the payment and then **never returned**
- This unusual disappearance may indicate the supervisor was intercepted by Guild agents, or was himself part of the operation

**The [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Warehouse_Contact\|Warehouse_Contact]]:**
- A suspiciously well-dressed individual was present, incongruous with the labor setting
- Made contact with [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and offered assistance via "friends in low places"
- Identity and affiliation unknown

### Warehouse 4
Referenced in the ledger found at [[Info/Locations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad_Guildhouse\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad_Guildhouse]]:
- *"Transfer warehouse 4 to warehouse 7 — escorted shipment, night delivery"*
- Cargo was transferred between these two warehouses under escort, after dark
- The nature of that cargo is unknown but likely connected to what was found in Warehouse 7

## Broader Activity

### Guild Control
City guards — who are on the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]'s payroll — have reportedly been "less than reputable people" asking questions around the warehouse district. This suggests the Guild is actively monitoring movement in and out of these buildings.

### Citizens Disappearing
The broader pattern of citizens vanishing after speaking out against the Guild's tariff increases may be connected to the warehouse district. The map in [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]'s office showed **two red circles in the warehouse district** — these likely correspond to Warehouses 4 and 7, but could indicate processing or holding locations for disappeared citizens.

### The Northeast Marker
The same map also showed a mark **northeast of Kjalgrad outside the city walls**. This may represent:
- A secondary distribution point
- A ritual site for the brimstone gold / coin mold operation
- A meeting point for Coiled Serpent operatives
