# Kjalgrad Sewers

## Basic Information
**Type:** Underground Sewer System  
**Location:** Beneath [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Region:** The Rift, Skyrim  
**Status:** Active  
**Significance:** Meeting point for [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] agents

## Description
The sewer system beneath [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] serves as a clandestine meeting location for agents of the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] to communicate with their mysterious superiors. The tunnels are accessible via grates in the city's alleyways.

## Access Points
- Sewer grates in various alleyways throughout [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]] accessed via an alley grate when tailed by the party

## Layout
- Network of tunnels beneath the city
- Contains dead-end passages suitable for private meetings
- Connected to the city drainage system

## Daedric Activity

### Communication Hub ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]])
The sewers serve as a secure location for cult communications:
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]] met his contact in a dead-end tunnel
- Used a **Daedric speaking stone** etched with runes
- The stone projected an image of his superior
- The projection appeared humanoid but inhuman ("weird")

### The Encounter
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]], and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] followed [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]] into the sewers
- Observed him reporting on his gold distribution activities
- Confronted him at the dead-end
- A **Daedric crocodile creature** attacked from behind
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] appeared and killed the creature

## Dangers

### Daedric Creatures
- At least one bipedal Daedric crocodile creature inhabited the sewers
- May be guardians for the communication point
- Killed by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] with a thrown dagger

### Cult Operatives
- [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] agents use these tunnels
- Communication with Daedric entities occurs here
- Additional guardians or agents may be present

## Strategic Value
- **Secrecy**: Underground location away from public eyes
- **Communication**: Secure point for Daedric speaking stone usage
- **Escape Routes**: Multiple tunnels for evading pursuit
- **Defense**: Narrow passages create natural chokepoints

## Key Events

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]] - Following Varren
1. Party spotted [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]] in the market
2. Tailed him through the city for 30 minutes
3. He entered a sewer grate in an alleyway
4. Party followed him through the tunnels
5. Found him at a dead-end using a speaking stone
6. Confronted him after his communication ended
7. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] paralyzed him to prevent escape
8. Daedric crocodile creature attacked
9. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] appeared and killed the creature
10. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]] was interrogated and turned
11. A crow guided the party out
