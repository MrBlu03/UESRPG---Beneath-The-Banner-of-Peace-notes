# Barrow of the Oath Breaker King

## Basic Information
**Type:** Nordic burial barrow  
**Region:** Mountains south of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], near the Three Brothers standing stones  
**Status:** Active — occupied by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] (Milos)  
**First Visited:** Session 025  

## Description
An ancient Nordic barrow built into a cliff face, externally difficult to distinguish from natural rock. The entrance consists of a plain metal door that opens inward (pull, not push — Durov's first instinct was wrong). Inside, the ruin is pitch black and extensive, featuring multiple chambers, traps, flooded sections, and a lever puzzle.

The barrow is the **tomb of the Oath Breaker King**, a Nordic ruler who was sealed away by three brothers who subsequently turned themselves into standing stones in his memory. The Three Brothers standing stones outside serve as the barrow's exterior landmark.

The [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|Vestige]] (Milos) has made this his base of operations during the [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] investigation.

## How to Find It
Follow the riddle given by the Blackfeather Court:
1. Exit through the **south gate** of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]
2. Follow the **Widow's River** (a small frozen stream named for a Jarl's wife who walked into it in grief after her husband died in war)
3. At the fork, take the **right branch**
4. Pass the **Three Brothers** standing stones (on a gentle slope to the left, barely-visible crown carvings)
5. A Blackfeather Court crow will lead you from the stones to the barrow entrance

## Interior Layout

### Entry Hall
- Skeleton near the far wall — contains Bone Meal (alchemical)
- Gate on the left (lever-operated) leading to a corridor
- A door ahead

### Corridor and Log Trap
- A concealed log trap suspended from the ceiling
- Evade test required to avoid 2d10 crushing damage
- Spotted by Subterfuge (Observe)

### Round Room
- Pots around the perimeter
- Another skeleton (examined by Onasha, nothing recovered)

### Several Locked Wooden Doors
- Durov broke most of these open with an axe rather than waiting for lockpicking

### Lever Puzzle Room
- Four levers on the wall, each with an animal symbol
- Levers: **Hawk**, **Moth**, **Bear**, **Whale** — two carved markers had crumbled
- The door was marked with a **Hawk** and a **Bear**
- Hawk was the leftmost; Durov flipped a coin for Bear and was correct
- Opens a mechanism in the wall

### Chest (behind lever door)
- Wooden chest, broken open by Durov
- Contents: an unknown spell book (ciphered/unfamiliar script), six lockpicks, strange old coins matching those found in a previous ruin

### The Water Room
- Flooded section with a submerged area
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] dove in (can breathe underwater): found a skeleton with a ring on the bottom

### The Final Chamber (Throne Room)
- Large reinforced wooden door, barred from the other side
- Two voices heard through the door
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] knocked; Milos opened it
- Contains: the **sarcophagus of the Oath Breaker King** (large, sealed stone coffin) — Milos had been sitting/standing on it
- Features: fire, bedrolls, Milos's equipment and notes

### The Shortcut
- A direct corridor connecting the entry to the throne room — the party had taken the long way around entirely without knowing

### Exit Trap
- A closed door near the exit that Durov bashed open without looking
- Failed Evade: took **15 crushing damage** from a massive flail trap (2d10)

## Facilities
Milos has equipped the barrow with:
- **Alchemy lab**: Available for [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s use
- **Enchanting table**: Offered freely to the party
- Bedrolls, a fire in the central room, and provisions

## Historical Significance
The Oath Breaker King was sealed away by three brothers who then became the standing stones outside — a Nordic legend referenced in *On the Early Years of the Rift*. The barrow has been sealed for an indeterminate time and was undisturbed before Milos established his base here.
