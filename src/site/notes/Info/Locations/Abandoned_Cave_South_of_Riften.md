# Abandoned Cave South of Riften

## Overview
An abandoned cave/grotto entrance located at the foot of mountains south of [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]], discovered during [[Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]. This location served as a temporary hideout for Camonna Tong criminals before their ship-based operations.

## Geographic Location
- Located south of [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] in mountainous terrain
- Situated in a small clearing at the foot of the mountains
- Accessible after 2-3 hours of walking from [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]]
- Near water access (ship mast visible from location)

## Notable Features
- Cave/grotto entrance suitable for hiding criminal operations
- Open clearing area for camping and equipment storage
- Recently abandoned with signs of recent habitation
- Strategic position for monitoring maritime activities

## Current Status
- **Abandoned**: Vacated only hours before [[The Fangs of Fjelhorst\|The Fangs of Fjelhorst]] arrival
- Previously used as Camonna Tong criminal hideout
- Likely no longer operationally significant after elimination of local criminals

## Evidence of Use
**[[Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]] Discovery:**
- Extinguished campfire (cold for only a few hours)
- Food scraps scattered near walls
- Impressions where gear and equipment had been placed
- Clear signs of hasty abandonment

## Connections
- **[[Info/Organizations/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna_Tong]]**: Used as operational base by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Tharen_Duleri\|Tharen_Duleri]]'s criminal network
- **[[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] Docks**: Connected to harassment operations against merchant activities
- **Sea of Ghosts Ships**: Potential staging area for maritime criminal activities
- **Morrowind**: Served as waypoint for return routes to criminal syndicate homeland

## Related Events
- **[[Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]**: Investigated by [[The Fangs of Fjelhorst\|The Fangs of Fjelhorst]] during pursuit of Camonna Tong criminals
- Used as meeting point and base for lockbox recovery operation
- Abandoned before final ship-based confrontation

## Strategic Importance
- Remote location away from [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] authority
- Access to both land and water routes
- Suitable for criminal operations requiring secrecy
- Now compromised and likely unusable by criminal elements

## Notes
- Discovery led party to identify ship location and final confrontation
- Represents broader criminal network infrastructure in the region
- Abandonment suggests organized retreat rather than panicked flight
