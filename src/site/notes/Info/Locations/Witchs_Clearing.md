# Witch's Clearing

## Basic Information
**Type:** Forest Clearing  
**Region:** The Rift, Skyrim (west of [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]])  
**Status:** Active (Territorial)  
**Controlled By:** The Witch of the Woods

## Description
A small clearing in the western woods outside [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]] that serves as the territorial center for a supernatural creature known as the Witch of the Woods. The clearing is the creature's home and the center of its domain. It appears inconspicuous from a distance but holds danger for those who enter uninvited.

## Features
- **Open Space:** Small clearing surrounded by dense forest
- **Ice Formation:** Natural ice present in the area
- **Old Trees:** Dense old growth surrounding the clearing
- **Territorial Markers:** The witch considers this her domain

## Key Events

### [[Session 023a\|Session 023a]] - Negotiation
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] tracked the witch to this clearing:
- The witch immediately attacked with frost bolts when [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] entered
- After a brief combat exchange, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] called for parlay
- The two negotiated a truce establishing peaceful coexistence
- The witch agreed to cease attacks in exchange for monthly offerings

**The Agreement:**
- Hunters will avoid the clearing and surrounding territory
- Monthly offerings of spell components (hearts, eyeballs, gems) will be provided
- The witch will cease attacks on hunters and livestock
- Both parties agreed to maintain the peace

## Dangers
- **Territorial Defense:** The witch attacks intruders immediately
- **Frost Magic:** The creature can cast damaging frost bolts
- **Isolation:** Located deep in the woods, far from help

## Significance
The clearing represents a successful diplomatic resolution to a supernatural threat. Rather than destroying the creature, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] established a treaty that protects both the local hunters and the witch's right to exist. The location may become a site for future magical learning if the truce holds.

## Notes
- The witch appears to have been residing here for some time
- The clearing's inconspicuous appearance belies its dangerous occupant
- Humanoid footprints lead to this location from the abandoned hunter camp
