# Northeast Warehouse / Main Facility

## Overview
Two related sites northeast of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] that form the logistical and ritual backbone of the [[Cult of Clavicus Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]] and [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] operation.

---

## Site 1: The Northeast Warehouse

### Basic Information
**Type:** Fortified warehouse  
**Location:** ~3 hours northeast of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], on foot through forest  
**Status:** Partially cleared (guards dead, contents stripped, workers freed)  
**First Visited:** Session 024  

### Description
A large block-shaped building backed against a cliff face, with rudimentary fortifications and a cart outside. No windows. The roof can be accessed from the cliff above (roughly 1.5m clearance). The party reached it by travelling ~2.5–3 hours northeast through increasingly dense, snowy forest. They entered in [[Info/Organizations/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] disguises to frame the Tong for the break-in.

### Contents Discovered

#### Enslaved Workers
Several workers inside bore the marks of shackle use. They were not willing employees — sourced through the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] Guildhouse. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] directed them to the city sewers to seek out **Strider** for safe harbour. One worker was briefly under compulsion from the Scathing but became cooperative once the Daedra left.

#### The Warehouse Ledger
Tucked behind a crate — **critical intelligence**:
- Standard inventory entries (cover)
- **Ritual shipment confirmations**: matching the crates [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] had flagged at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District\|Warehouse 7]] — coin molds, chalk, gemstones, bone and sinew
- **"Selection cargo" entries**: names of the city's disappeared — merchants and guests at the Jarl's events (including [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Jean_Pierre_LaRoche\|Jean-Pierre LaRoche]])
- **Payment records to bandits**: confirming the Guild financed the eastern road bandit operation cleared in Session 23b — the mage with the horned mask symbol was on their payroll
- **Direction to the main facility**: *"Several hours further north"* — the primary ritual site

#### Scathing's Equipment (Looted)
- **Scathing's armor**: Composition unclear, taken for identification
- **Bronze sword**: Standard weapon, taken by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]
- **One orichalcum arrow**: Found on the body
- **One Scathing horn**: Successfully extracted (other broke during extraction)

#### Other Loot
- Medical supplies (replenished party kits)
- Silks and provisions (taken to simulate a robbery)
- Wooden crossbow (taken by Kirya as a long-term training weapon)

### The Scathing
A tall, horned humanoid Daedra emerged after combat — the warehouse's manager and a servant of [[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]]. It calmly allowed the party to search freely in exchange for its release, then departed. See [[Info/Lore/Mimicry_Daedra\|Mimicry_Daedra]] and [[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus_Vile]] for details.

### Current Status
- Guards dead; workers freed; Scathing departed
- The party chose **not to burn it** — [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] argued for preserving the supply chain as an intelligence trail. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] accepted reluctantly, extracting a promise that she gets to burn the main facility with the person responsible inside.
- Camonna Tong cover may have held — no survivors inside to contradict the framing.

## Site 2: The Main Facility (Nordic Ruin)

### Basic Information
**Type:** Ancient Nordic ruin (repurposed as cult ritual site)  
**Location:** Several hours north of the warehouse, up by the edge of the mesa  
**Status:** Active threat — cult operations ongoing  
**First Scouted:** Session 026 (by the Vestige, overnight solo)  

### Description
The remnants of an old Nordic ruin in the northern mountains. Sporadically guarded — which is manageable. The serious problem is the **secondary entrances**: the cult has Warded them with powerful glyphs that the Vestige cannot dispel alone.

> *"It is guarded sporadically. That is not the main problem. Normally, ruins like that have several entrances apart from the main one scattered about. It seems that the cult has Warded them quite well. Powerful Warding glyphs that I, unfortunately, with my limited capabilities in conjuration, cannot dispel."*
> — The Vestige, Session 026

### Known Intelligence (from Vestige's scout)
- Sporadically guarded — primary entrance is passable
- Secondary entrances Warded shut with powerful glyphs (cult-placed)
- The Vestige returned bloody from scouting — suggests some contact or hazard

### What's Inside (Inferred from Ledger)
- **"Selection cargo"** (enslaved people from the city — merchants, Jarl's guests)
- **Ritual supplies**: coin molds, chalk, gemstones, bone and sinew (confirmed by ledger)
- **The cult's primary ritual site** for whatever operation the [[Cult of Clavicus Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]] is running
- Likely contains **the person responsible** for the overall operation
- The Vestige plans to **distract the top-tier target** while the party handles everyone else and frees the enslaved

### The Ward Problem
Two solutions being pursued simultaneously:
1. **Alchemical varnish** — requires pearl (purchased from Julian for 8 gold) + hag raven feather (the Vestige is retrieving this from the Witch of the Woods). [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] will brew it.
2. **Father Aldric** — [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Father_Aldric\|Breton priest of Stendarr]], met in the Grand Library; alteration dabbler; agreed to help dispel the Wards

### Assault Plan (Pending Final Briefing)
The Vestige's plan:
- He **distracts the main target**
- Party **handles the guards and frees the enslaved people**
- Final briefing comes once the Ward problem is resolved and the Vestige has returned
