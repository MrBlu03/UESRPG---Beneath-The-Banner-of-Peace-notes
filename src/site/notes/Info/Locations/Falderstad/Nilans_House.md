# Nilan's House

## Description
A modest hunter's timber cabin on the forest verge of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], belonging to the missing master tracker [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nilan\|Nilan]].

## Features
- Trophy racks holding antlers, cured hides, and bowstrings.
- Cold hearth with a pot of spoiled stew indicating a sudden, hurried departure.
- Faint residual scent of nightshade and wolfbane in the storage chests.

## NPCs
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nilan\|Nilan]]
