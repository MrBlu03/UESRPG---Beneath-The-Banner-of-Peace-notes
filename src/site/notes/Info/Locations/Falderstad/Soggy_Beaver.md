# The Soggy Beaver

## Description
A rowdy, low-ceilinged dive tavern in the lower quarter of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], popular among loggers, fur trappers, and river workers.

## Features
- Smoky interior smelling of cheap ale, fried fish, and wet wool.
- A popular hub for underground rumors, dice games, and raw gossip.

## NPCs
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]]
- Local Laborers
