# Falderstad

## Description
Falderstad is a rugged frontier settlement nestled in eastern Skyrim near the dense northern forest. Ruled by the ailing [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl_Gorm]], the town relies heavily on timber, hunting, pelt trade, and brewing. It features a central timber longhouse, bustling marketplaces, and is surrounded by log palisades and watchtowers. The town recently suffered terror from supernatural beasts and the predation of the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]].
