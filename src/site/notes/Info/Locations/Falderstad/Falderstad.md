# Falderstad

## Overview
A rugged, fortified Nordic settlement located in the borderlands between the Rift and Eastmarch. Governed by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl Gorm]] from his great timber longhouse and protected by Huscarl [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga Cairn-Shield]], Falderstad is a resilient hunting and trapping community rooted deeply in ancient Nordic law, sacred customs, and ancestor traditions.

## Description
Surrounded by a high wooden palisade with guarded gatehouses, Falderstad features a lively central marketplace, the Hunter's Bell podium, two distinct taverns ([[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Wounded_Boar\|The Wounded Boar]] and [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]]), [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Thistle_Branch\|The Thistle Branch]] apothecary, and traditional burial grounds and dueling fields ([[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Holmgang_Field\|Holmgang Field]]) outside the walls.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl Gorm]]**: The stalwart, tradition-abiding Jarl of Falderstad.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga Cairn-Shield]]**: Huscarl and commander of the town guard.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]**: Respected master huntress and leader of local trackers.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]**: Elder mage, Lawspeaker, and owner of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Thistle_Branch\|The_Thistle_Branch]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]] & [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Denskar\|Denskar]]**: Barkeeps of the town's prominent taverns.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Celebrated heroes granted citizenship and permanent ownership of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Nilans_House\|Nilans_House]].
