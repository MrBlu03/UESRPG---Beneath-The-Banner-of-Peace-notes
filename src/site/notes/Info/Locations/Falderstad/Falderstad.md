# Falderstad

## Description
Falderstad is a rugged frontier settlement nestled in eastern Skyrim near the dense [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]]. Ruled by the ailing [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl_Gorm]], the town relies heavily on timber, hunting, pelt trade, and brewing, but recently suffered terror from supernatural beasts and the predation of the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]].

## Features
- Central timber longhouse and town hall ruled by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl_Gorm]].
- Bustling marketplace and tanneries catering to forest hunters.
- Surrounding palisades and watchtowers guarding against wilderness predators.
