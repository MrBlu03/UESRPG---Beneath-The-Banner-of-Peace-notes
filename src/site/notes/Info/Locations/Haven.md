# Haven

## Basic Information
**Type:** City/Port  
**Region:** Valenwood (Aldmeri Dominion)  
**Status:** Active  
**Known For:** Trade hub, moon sugar smuggling origin

## Description
Haven is a location in Valenwood, the forest province of the Aldmeri Dominion inhabited primarily by Bosmer (Wood Elves). It serves as a trade hub and, according to interrogation of Dunmer smugglers, a source location for moon sugar being smuggled into Skyrim.

## Criminal Activity

### Moon Sugar Smuggling ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]])
Information obtained from interrogating Dunmer prisoners in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]:
- Smugglers operating for [[Info/Organizations/House_Hlaalu\|House_Hlaalu]] obtain moon sugar from Haven
- The moon sugar is then transported from Valenwood into Skyrim
- This trade operates outside of legitimate Khajiit caravan networks
- A crate of moon sugar is worth approximately 300 gold

## Trade Significance
- **Moon Sugar Source**: Supplies smuggling operations into Skyrim
- **Valenwood Trade**: Part of inter-provincial commerce (legitimate and illicit)

## Related Organizations
- [[Info/Organizations/House_Hlaalu\|House_Hlaalu]]: Uses Haven as source for moon sugar smuggling
- **Khajiit Caravans**: NOT involved in the House Hlaalu smuggling (per interrogation)
