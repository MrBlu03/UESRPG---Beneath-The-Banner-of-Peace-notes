# Northern Forest

## Basic Information
**Type:** Wilderness Area  
**Location:** North of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Region:** Eastern Skyrim  
**Status:** Active but Disturbed  
**Access:** Through northern gate of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]

## Description
A forested wilderness area north of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] that has become the center of supernatural disturbances affecting the region. The forest serves as both hunting ground and battleground for various supernatural entities, making it dangerous for normal wildlife and human visitors.

## Environmental Conditions
- **Wildlife:** Notably absent due to territorial supernatural creature
- **Atmosphere:** Oppressive feeling of being watched
- **Terrain:** Mixed forest with clearings and abandoned structures
- **Climate:** Cold northern conditions with winter weather effects

## Key Features

### Supernatural Disturbances
- **Territorial Creature:** Unknown entity with massive claws driving away natural wildlife
- **Necromantic Emanations:** Concentrated around [[Info/Locations/Ruined_Hunting_Lodge\|Ruined_Hunting_Lodge]]
- **Vampire Activity:** Active undead presence affecting local ecosystem
- **Claw Marks:** Evidence of large supernatural predator throughout area

### Points of Interest
- **[[Info/Locations/Ruined_Hunting_Lodge\|Ruined_Hunting_Lodge]]:** Vampire lair and necromantic experimentation site
- **Frozen Hunter Site:** Clearing where hunters were killed by supernatural freeze
- **Battlefield Area:** Scene of conflict with broken branches and claw damage
- **High Tree Markers:** Animal hide hanging impossibly high (supernatural placement)

- **Blue Eyes:** Glowing blue eyes spotted watching from high in trees

## Threats and Dangers
- **Territorial Creature:** Unknown supernatural predator with massive claws
- **Vampire Activity:** Active undead presence conducting experiments
- **Necromantic Contamination:** Dangerous magical effects throughout area
- **Frozen Death Magic:** Supernatural freezing effects that killed hunters
- **Unknown Watchers:** Multiple entities observing visitors

## Investigation Significance
The Northern Forest represents the epicenter of supernatural threats affecting the [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] region. The concentration of vampire activity, unknown territorial creatures, and necromantic experimentation makes it a critical area for understanding and combating the broader conspiracy.

## Environmental Impact
- Natural wildlife driven away or killed
- Ecosystem disrupted by supernatural presence
- Hunting grounds rendered dangerous for normal use
- Area contaminated by necromantic magic

## Strategic Considerations
- Multiple supernatural factions appear to be in conflict
- Territorial creature may be natural force opposing vampire conspiracy
- Area serves as testing ground for supernatural weapons and techniques
- Remote location ideal for undetected supernatural operations

## Notes
- Forest serves as natural barrier and hideout for supernatural threats
- Multiple unknown entities suggest complex supernatural ecosystem
- Territorial creature's opposition to vampire may represent opportunity for alliance
- Area requires continued monitoring due to ongoing supernatural activity
