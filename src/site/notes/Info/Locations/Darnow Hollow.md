# Darnow Hollow

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Darnow Hollow
- **Type:** Abandoned Manor Estate
- **Status:** Deserted and sealed off
- **Location:** Atop a hill, accessible by a familiar road
- **Significance:** Former contract location for [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], site of her transformative dream vision
- **First Appearance:** [[2 -  Sessions/Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)\|Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)]]

## Description
- **Overall:** Once grand manor, now crumbling yet retaining regal bearing
- **Condition:** Half sunk into stone like it's trying to bury itself
- **Atmosphere:** Cliff side breathes salt and rot
- **Vegetation:** Ivy chokes the walls but can't mask the scent of blood clinging to the air
- **Size:** Bigger than even the Luneclaire estate back in [[Info/Locations/North_Point\|North Point]]

## Estate Grounds

### The Road & Entrance
- **Signpost:** Reads "Darnow Hollow"
- **Approach:** Familiar road leading to an old contract location
- **Status:** Place appears deserted and sealed off
- **Hill Position:** Manor sits atop a hill, commanding the surrounding area

### The Front Garden
- **Flowers:** White flowers blooming pure and defiant
- **Symbolism:** Reminiscent of [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s mother's flowers back in [[Info/Locations/North_Point\|North Point]]
- **Contradiction:** Flowers shouldn't be growing in soil this dark
- **Significance:** Beauty persisting in a place of tragedy

### The Barn
- **Condition:** Door split open by enormous claw marks
- **Interior:** 
  - Hay soaked with blood
  - Pile of bones neatly stacked in the corner
  - Small glint reveals the [[Info/Items/Child's Doll\|Child's Doll]] wrapped in cloth
- **Evidence:** Suggests violent attack by large creature
- **Tragedy:** Remnants of innocent victims

### The Grain Mill
- **Activity:** Soft humming coming from within
- **Occupant:** [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Sybille\|Sybille]] with straw-colored hair
- **Warning:** Girl warns about "a lady in white from the manor who lives in the chapel"
- **Danger:** Lady asks for names and makes people forget them
- **Atmosphere:** Deceptively peaceful despite the ominous warning
- **Interaction:** [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] returns the [[Info/Items/Child's Doll\|child's doll]] to [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Sybille\|Sybille]]

### The Chapel of Stendarr
#### Exterior:
- **Lighting:** Red light pulsing from stained glass windows
- **Appearance:** Like a wound in the forest
- **Evidence:** Bear footprints leading into the chapel
- **Graves:** To the left of entrance - marked "Cerulian and Saphirelle"
- **Emotional Impact:** [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] has a sad moment at the graves

#### Interior:
- **Atmosphere:** Once sacred air gone stagnant like water trapped in a crypt
- **Lighting:** Blood candles flicker on every surface, casting shadows that twist into shapes of kneeling men
- **Altar:** 
  - Symbol of Stendarr carved out/defaced
  - Beneath it, faintly scratched: [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]]'s seal
  - [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] recognizes her own seal, made years ago but doesn't remember when
  - Later holds a black soul gem pulsing with unseen veins
- **Occupant:** Figure kneeling motionless at the end of the aisle behind the pulpit

## The Manor Interior

### The Foyer
- **Flooring:** Marble underfoot
- **Walls:** Blackened portraits line the walls
- **Lighting:** Grand chandelier swings as if from some unseen breath
- **Memory:** Place remembers feasting
- **Scale:** Larger than the Luneclaire estate

### The Ballroom
- **Layout:** Grand space with far end featuring a throne
- **Throne:** Made of withered roses where [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Lady Elaris\|Lady Elaris]] sits
- **Atmosphere:** Dominated by [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Lady Elaris\|Lady Elaris]]'s seductive and threatening presence
- **Significance:** Site of the major confrontation between [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Lady Elaris\|Lady Elaris]]

## Historical Significance

### Past Contract
- **Connection:** Old contract location familiar to [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]
- **Nature:** Unknown what the original contract involved
- **Tragedy:** Site of some catastrophic event involving violence
- **Victims:** Children and families lived here before the disaster

### The Tragedy
- **Evidence:** Claw marks, blood, bones suggest monster attack
- **Victims:** Child's doll indicates young casualties
- **Scope:** Entire household appears to have been wiped out
- **Aftermath:** Manor abandoned and sealed

- **Inner Journey:** Represents [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s exploration of her own psyche
