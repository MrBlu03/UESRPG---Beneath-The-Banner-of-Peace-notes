# Riften

## Description
[[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] is a major city that serves as the central hub for the party's activities. It's ruled by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]] and houses numerous establishments, political figures, and quest givers.

## Features
- Seat of power for [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]
- Royal residence and court
- Multiple shops and establishments
- Center of regional governance

## Establishments
- **[[Info/Locations/Scent_of_Serenity\|Scent of Serenity]]**: Alchemy shop run by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alara\|Alara]] and her son [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hafjorg\|Hafjorg]]
- **[[Info/Locations/Sweaty_Steel\|Sweaty Steel]]**: Blacksmith shop operated by siblings [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svana\|Svana]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorn\|Bjorn]]
- **[[Info/Locations/The_Honed_Edge\|The Honed Edge]]**: Weapon shop that sells silver weapons
- **[[Info/Locations/The_Sleeping_Stag\|The Sleeping Stag]]**: Establishment run by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Grelka\|Grelka]]
- **[[Info/Locations/Borans_Bullwarks\|Boran's Bullwarks]]**: Shop owned by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Boran\|Boran]], likely selling shields/armor
- **[[Info/Locations/Whispwood_Remedies\|Whispwood Remedies]]**: Shop owned by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Ralvard\|Ralvard]]
- **[[Info/Locations/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]]**: Likely an inn or tavern
