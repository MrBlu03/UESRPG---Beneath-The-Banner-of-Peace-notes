# Abandoned Hunter Camp

## Basic Information
**Type:** Campsite  
**Region:** The Rift, Skyrim (west of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]])  
**Status:** Abandoned  
**Last Used:** Prior to [[Session 023a\|Session 023a]]

## Description
A temporary hunting camp established by Bjorn and his hunting party in the western woods outside [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. The camp was abandoned after the hunters were attacked by a supernatural creature, forcing them to flee and leave their equipment behind.

## Features
- **Burnt-out Campfire:** Still has logs covered in snow
- **Discarded Equipment:** Left behind during the hasty retreat
- **Rock Formations:** Large rocks nearby where supplies were hidden

## Items Found (Session 023a)
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] discovered the following items at the camp:
- **Iron Short Sword:** Standard hunting weapon
- **Hunting Bow:** Left behind by fleeing hunters
- **Dried Provisions:** Food supplies preserved by the cold, tucked in rocks
- **Rope:** Small game trap materials

## Key Events

### [[Session 023a\|Session 023a]] - Investigation
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] visited the abandoned camp while tracking the witch:
- Found humanoid footprints leading northeast toward the witch's clearing
- Collected the abandoned equipment
- Used the camp as a reference point for tracking

## Significance
The abandoned camp serves as evidence of the witch's threat and provides a starting point for tracking the creature. The items left behind suggest the hunters fled in haste, underscoring the danger the creature poses.
