# Darnell Hollow

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Darnell Hollow
- **Type:** Abandoned manor estate
- **Status:** Deserted and sealed off
- **Location:** On a hill, accessible by a familiar road
- **Significance:** Appears in [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s dream/vision as an old contract location

## Description
- **Exterior:** Crumbling yet regal manor atop a hill
- **Condition:** Deserted and sealed off from the outside world
- **Atmosphere:** Dark and foreboding, with a sense of ancient history
- **Access:** Sign post reads "Darnell Hollow"

## Structures
### The Manor
- **Style:** Once grand estate, now half-sunk into stone
- **Garden:** White flowers blooming pure and defiant
- **Interior:** Long foyer with marble underfoot, blackened portraits
- **Ballroom:** Grand chandelier, throne of withered roses
- **History:** Place remembers feasting, bigger than Luneclaire estate

### The Barn
- **Condition:** Door split open by enormous claw marks
- **Contents:** Hay soaked with blood, pile of bones neatly stacked
- **Discovery:** Child's doll wrapped in cloth found next to bones

### The Grain Mill
- **Atmosphere:** Soft humming coming from within
- **Inhabitants:** Small girl with straw-colored hair
- **Warning:** Girl warns about lady in white from manor who lives in chapel

### The Chapel
- **Name:** Chapel of Stendarr
- **Appearance:** Like a wound in the forest
- **Windows:** Red light pulsing from stained glass
- **Graves:** Cerulian and Saphirelle to the left of entrance
- **Interior:** Blood candles, defaced altar with Arkay's seal beneath
- **Significance:** Site of [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s confrontation with [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Gareth\|Gareth]]

## Key Events
### Session 9.5 (Dream/Vision)
- **[[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s Arrival:** Finds herself on familiar road leading to the manor
- **Exploration:** Discovers the barn, grain mill, and chapel
- **Confrontation:** Faces [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Gareth\|Gareth]] in the chapel for philosophical battle
- **Transformation:** Chapel shifts to become Arkfell during the confrontation
- **Return:** Later returns to manor for confrontation with Loris

## Inhabitants (Dream)
- **The Elderly Woman:** Cryptic figure with cloudy eyes and sharp pain
- **The Small Girl:** Straw-colored hair, warns about the lady in white
- **The Lady in White:** Dreamlike figure who asks for names and makes people forget them
- **Loris:** Mysterious figure attempting to seduce [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] into darkness
- **[[Info/Characters/NPC's/Gareth\|Gareth]]:** [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s former mentor, appears as spirit
- **Vampire Priest:** Former Arkay priest who succumbed to vampirism
