# Kjalgrad

## Basic Information
**Type:** city
**Region:** The Rift, Skyrim  
**Status:** Active  
**Controlled By:** [[Clan Morvan\|Clan Morvan]]  
**Significance:** Prosperous mining settlement and [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] delivery destination

## Description
Kjalgrad is a fortified town in the Rift, more prosperous and secure than [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. The town is controlled by **Clan Morvan**, whose purple banners bearing a gold coin emblem fly from the stone palisades. The town has a diverse, trade-focused population and serves as an important waypoint on the road to Shor's Stone and Riften.

## Physical Features

### Fortifications
- **Walls**: Solid stone construction, far more fortified than Falderstad's wooden walls
- **Gates**: Guarded entrances with professional security
- **Banners**: Purple banners with gold coin emblems (Clan Morvan's colors)
- **Western Gate**: Primary entrance where the party arrived

### Population
The main streets are bustling with diverse inhabitants:
- **Nords** (majority population)
- **Argonians** (merchants and traders)
- **Bretons**
- **Khajiit**
- **Dunmer**

### Atmosphere
- Prosperous and well-maintained
- Strong merchant presence
- Active trade hub
- Professional guard force

## Notable Locations

### [[Frost Gate Hall\|Frost Gate Hall]]
- Upscale inn near the western gate
- Features stables for horses
- Polished oak beams and high ceilings
- Silk drapes and fur rugs
- Glass windows (luxury feature)
- Innkeeper: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Orgnar\|Orgnar]]

## Clan Morvan

The town is controlled by **Clan Morvan**, identified by:
- **Colors**: Purple and gold
- **Emblem**: Gold coin
- **Symbol Meaning**: Likely represents wealth from mining operations
- The clan appears to maintain order and prosperity in the settlement

## Coiled Serpent Connection

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]] - Interrogation Intel
Information obtained from Dunmer prisoners:
- The smugglers were paid **2,000 gold in advance** by the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] to transport a ledger
- **Delivery Destination**: Kjallgrad
- **Contact**: An individual wearing an **Ouroboros pendant** was to meet them
- The delivery was never completed due to the smugglers' capture

## Investigation Priority
This location should be investigated to:
- Identify the Coiled Serpent contact wearing the Ouroboros pendant
- Understand the organization's operations in the Rift
- Trace connections between the Coiled Serpent and [[Info/Organizations/House_Hlaalu\|House_Hlaalu]]
