# Kjalgrad

## Basic Information
**Type:** Mining Town  
**Region:** The Rift, Skyrim  
**Status:** Active  
**Controlled By:** [[Clan Morvan\|Clan Morvan]] (nominal), [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] (effective)  
**Significance:** Prosperous mining town; center of Daedric cult activity

## Description
Kjalgrad is a prosperous mining town in the Rift, controlled by [[Clan Morvan\|Clan Morvan]]. The town's wealth comes from its mining operations, making it an attractive location for merchants and travelers. However, beneath its prosperous surface lies corruption: the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] effectively controls the city, and a **Cult of Clavicus Vile** has infiltrated its commerce.

## Political Structure

### Official Government
- **Jarl**: Ceremonial figurehead, rarely present at governance
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]]**: Imperial Steward, handles actual governance
- **[[Clan Morvan\|Clan Morvan]]**: Traditional ruling clan

### Actual Power ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]])
According to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]]:
- The [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] **bankrolls the guards**, not the Jarl
- The guild controls **prices, tolls, and passages**
- The Jarl is essentially a figurehead
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] does most of the actual talking/governing
- **Implication**: The steward is likely in the guild's pocket

### Why Khajiit Caravans Don't Visit
- The city doesn't have enough independent money to attract them
- The [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] controls all commerce
- Caravans wouldn't profit under guild-controlled tolls

## Daedric Activity

### Cult of Clavicus Vile ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]])
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] revealed a Daedric cult operating in the city:
- **Brimstone Gold**: Tainted currency from Oblivion being distributed
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]]**: Agent distributing gold through the market
- **Daedric Speaking Stones**: Used for communication with cult leadership
- **Daedric Creatures**: At least one crocodile creature in the sewers

### Coiled Serpent Connection
- Someone **high-ranking** in the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] wears an **Ouroboros necklace**
- This was the delivery destination for the [[Info/Organizations/House_Hlaalu\|House_Hlaalu]] ledger
- The [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] has infiltrated city leadership

- Party searched Valen's office during his absence; found wall map (warehouse district circles + **northeast marker outside city**), ledger, cabinet letter confirming Jarl supervision, and lockbox with Riften bribes and "Coming Correction" fragments
- A **[[Info/1 - Party Findings/Blackfeather_Court_Note\|Blackfeather Court crow]]** delivered a note to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] at [[Info/Locations/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost_Gate_Hall]]
