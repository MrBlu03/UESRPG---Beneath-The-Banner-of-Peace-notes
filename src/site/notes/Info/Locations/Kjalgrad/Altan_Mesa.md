# Altan Mesa (Main Facility)

## Description
Altan Mesa, also referenced as the Northeast Warehouse and Main Facility, was a heavily fortified secret stronghold and staging ground utilized by the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile]] outside the urban walls of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Disguised as an isolated logistics complex, it housed sacrificial altars, summoning circles, and holding cells.

## Features
- **Heavily Guarded Perimeter**: Patrolled by mercenary guards, cult fanatics, and bound Daedric overseers.
- **Subterranean Ritual Chambers**: Deep chambers carved into the rock, outfitted with blood channels, dark runic stones, and Daedric summoning circles.
- **Holding Cells**: Dungeons used to hold kidnapped citizens and captives intended for ritual sacrifices.

## NPCs
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|The_Scathing]]**: Horned Daedric overseer stationed at the facility.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]]**: Dremora steward who coordinated operations at the facility.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|The_Xivilai]]**: High-ranking Daedric champion summoned during the climactic assault.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|Milos / The Vestige]]**: Assisted the party in storming and securing the facility.
