# Altan Mesa (The Main Facility)

## Overview
An ancient, fortified subterranean complex combining Dwemer architecture and ancient Nordic stonecraft situated deep within the mountain crags several hours north of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. It was repurposed by Trade Advocate [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]] and the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]] as their primary regional stronghold and ritual counterfeiting facility.

## Description
A vast underground facility secured by heavy bronze doors, magical wards, ventilation grates, and defensive traps. It houses barracks, ritual workshops, holding cells for abducted citizens, and a grand central chamber featuring a massive arcane forge and sacrificial circle.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]]**: Cult mastermind and Silverroad advocate who staged his base here after the warehouse fires.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|The_Xivilai]]**: Daedric warlord who commanded the central ritual chamber.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] & The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Infiltrated and breached the stronghold.
- **Captive Citizens**: Missing Kjalgrad townsfolk (including noble merchant [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jean-Pierre_LaRouche\|Jean-Pierre_LaRouche]]) held in trance-like slumber to power the dark ritual.

- **The Grand Assault & Decapitation of the Xivilai ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]):** [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] melted the entrance wards with hagraven-feather tincture; the party navigated Dwemer vents, neutralized sentries with paralysis bombs, executed [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|The_Xivilai]], and liberated the captive townspeople, halting the counterfeit coin production.
