# Warehouse Seven

## Overview
A high-security storage depot in the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District\|Kjalgrad Warehouse District]], operated by the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]] as a covert staging facility for ritual supplies and tainted currency.

## Description
A cavernous stone warehouse containing stacked shipping crates, locked ledger offices, and holding areas for coerced laborers. Inscribed with covert Daedric markings.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|The_Scathing]]**: Horned mimicry Daedra overseer.
- **Enslaved Workers**: Chained commoners forced to sort cargo.
- **Silverroad Sentries**: Armed guards stationed at every doorway.

## Notable Events
- **Undercover Infiltration & Ledgers ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 024\|Session 024]]):** The party infiltrated in Camonna Tong disguise; [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] analyzed shipping crates with Clairvoyance, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] threw a frenzy bomb, and the group secured the master shipping ledger pointing directly to the northern cult facility at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan_Mesa]].
- **Arson & Destruction ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]]):** Targeted during the synchronized city strike; [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] hurled kindling and oil into the crates, burning the facility to the ground.
