# Warehouse Seven

## Description
A commercial storage building situated in the Kjalgrad warehouse district. It has been used for suspicious shipments, including coin molds for minting Daedric brimstone gold.

## Notable NPCs
- **[[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Caravan_Master]]**: The merchant overseeing the caravan at the warehouse.

## Key Events
- **[[3 - The Fjelhorst Chronicles/Chapter 023\|Chapter 023]]**: Durov and Captain Irlav visited the warehouse in the morning.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]]**: Durov carried out an inspection of a caravan's cargo at the warehouse. He found suspicious sealed crates containing coin molds, Daedric materials, and animal parts, and paid the Caravan Master 500 gold to hold them. A ledger was also reviewed here linking to a northeast marker.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 024\|Session 024]]**: The party investigated a northeast warehouse and found ritual supplies matching the shipments Durov flagged at Warehouse 7.
