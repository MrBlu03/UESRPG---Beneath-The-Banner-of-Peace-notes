# Clan Morvan Longhouse

## Description
The [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Morvan\|Clan Morvan]] Longhouse is the massive timber and stone seat of government in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], perched atop the highest elevation of the city. It serves as the primary residence and court of the Jarl of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], housing the council chambers, treasury, administrative archives, and private guard quarters.

## Features
- **Great Hearth & Throne Room**: Centerpiece of the longhouse where the Jarl receives petitions, holds feasts, and conducts formal diplomatic business.
- **Steward's Office**: Administrative suite filled with trade contracts, tax rolls, and city ledgers. Contains secret compartments used by the false steward to conceal dark pacts.
- **Subterranean Cells**: Secure dungeon holding cells beneath the barracks.

## NPCs
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Morvan\|Jarl of Kjalgrad]]**: Ruler residing within the longhouse.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]]**: Former steward whose office was raided by the party.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain_Irlav]]**: Commander of the longhouse guard.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Proventus\|Proventus]]**: Guard stationed in the administrative wing.
