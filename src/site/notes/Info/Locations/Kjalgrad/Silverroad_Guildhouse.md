## Description
The Silverroad Guildhouse is the opulent, fortified headquarters of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. It stands as a testament to the guild's massive financial and political influence over trade in eastern Skyrim, housing high-vaulted trading floors and luxurious guildmaster chambers.
