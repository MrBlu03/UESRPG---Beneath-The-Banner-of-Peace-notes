# Silverroad Guildhouse

## Overview
The imposing, lavish grand headquarters of the **[[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]]** in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], functioning as the true economic and administrative powerhouse of the city.

## Description
A fortress-like stone complex boasting polished granite floors, expensive tapestries, private meeting parlors, secure vaults, and heavily armed private guards in polished armor.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jorven_Halliksson\|Jorven_Halliksson]]**: Guildmaster of the Silverroad.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin_Marr]]**: Trade Advocate and Coiled Serpent mastermind.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain_Irlav]]**: Guild guard commander.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Hired as an auxiliary guard and muscle.

- **Mercenary Recruitment & Bounties ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]]):** [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] attended meetings here with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin_Marr]], receiving mercenary employment and instructions to spy on Sadrith Indoril.
- **Guard Briefing & Alibi Management ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 028\|Session 028]]):** Served as the staging ground where Durov checked in with Captain Irlav before departing for the northern facility.
