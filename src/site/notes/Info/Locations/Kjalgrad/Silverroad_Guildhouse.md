# Silverroad Guildhouse (Kjalgrad)

## Description
The [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Silverroad]] Guildhouse is the opulent, heavily fortified headquarters of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Featuring fine stone masonry, ornate ironwork, and polished timber, it stands as a testament to the guild's massive financial and political influence over trade throughout eastern Skyrim and across the border into Morrowind.

## Features
- **Grand Trading Floor**: High-vaulted hall where lucrative caravan contracts, import licenses, and debt notes are negotiated.
- **Guildmaster's Chambers**: Luxurious upper office where Guildmaster [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jorven_Halliksson\|Jorven_Halliksson]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin_Marr]] conduct high-level affairs.
- **Reinforced Vaults**: Heavily warded subterranean strongrooms holding currency, precious gems, and sensitive merchant ledgers.

## NPCs
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jorven_Halliksson\|Jorven_Halliksson]]**: Guildmaster of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Silverroad]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin_Marr]]**: Trade advocate and silver-tongued diplomat.
- **Guild Enforcers & Mercenaries**: Armed private soldiers wearing the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Silverroad]] colors.
