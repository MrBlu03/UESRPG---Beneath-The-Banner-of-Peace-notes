# Kjalgrad Warehouse District

## Overview
The heavily fortified industrial and commercial storage quarter located on the northeastern perimeter of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], controlled entirely by the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]].

## Description
A dense district of high-walled stone and timber storage warehouses, loading docks, wagon ramps, and security checkpoints patrolled by guild mercenaries.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Warehouse_Seven\|Warehouse 7]]**: Central hub holding tainted coins and ritual shipments.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|The_Scathing]]**: Mimicry Daedra overseer.
- **Laborers & Guards**: Oppressed workers and heavily armed guild sentries.

## Notable Events
- **Warehouse Seven Infiltration ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 024\|Session 024]]):** Infiltrated by the party in Camonna Tong disguise, recovering shipping ledgers and confronting [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|The_Scathing]].
- **Warehouse Sabotage & Fires ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]]):** Synchronized with the market strike; [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] planted traps and ignited the warehouses with kindling and oil, burning multiple depots and sending massive smoke plumes over Kjalgrad.
