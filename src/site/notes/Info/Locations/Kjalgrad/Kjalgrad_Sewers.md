# Kjalgrad Sewers

## Description
The Kjalgrad Sewers form a damp, sprawling subterranean drainage and canal system running beneath the streets of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Originally built during the First Era, the brick and stone waterways have become a haven for smugglers, thieves, and the resistance network known as the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|Lower_Lodge]]. Recently, they have been infested by the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile]].

## Notable NPCs
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tabitha\|Tabitha]]**: Skilled local guide and sewer scout for the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|Lower_Lodge]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]**: Navigated the tunnels and hunted Daedra here.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]]**: Used the sewers to communicate with Daedric forces via a speaking stone.
