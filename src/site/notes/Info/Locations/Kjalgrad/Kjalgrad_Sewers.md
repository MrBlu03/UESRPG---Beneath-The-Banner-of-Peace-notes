# Kjalgrad Sewers

## Description
The [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] Sewers (frequently referred to as the Ratway of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]) form a damp, sprawling subterranean drainage and canal system running beneath the streets, warehouses, and longhouse of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Originally built during the First Era, the brick and stone waterways have become a haven for smugglers, thieves, exiled guild members, and dark cultists.

## Features
- **Vaulted Brick Canals**: Murky waist-deep water channels accessible via storm drains and basement iron grates throughout the city.
- **Hidden Smuggler Platforms & Docks**: Wooden boardwalks and storage coves used to move contraband directly to the river without paying harbour duties.
- **Daedric Infiltration Points**: Secret passages breaking through old masonry into hidden subterranean shrines.
