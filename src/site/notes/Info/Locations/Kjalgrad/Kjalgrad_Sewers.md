# Kjalgrad Sewers

## Overview
A sprawling, ancient subterranean sewer network and forgotten ruin system extending beneath the streets of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Used extensively by smugglers, the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|Lower Lodge]] resistance, and Daedric infiltrators.

## Description
A damp, vaulted labyrinth of stone aqueducts, drainage channels, crumbling brick walls, and hidden maintenance corridors. Many key junctions are inscribed with the covert **diamond-circle symbol** marking safe passage.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|Lower Lodge Resistance]]**: Operates concealed safehouses within the deeper vaults.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]**: Navigated the tunnels to assist the party.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]]**: Used the sewers as a clandestine drop route for tainted gold.

## Notable Events
- **Ambush of Varran ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]]):** The party tracked courier Varran into the sewers, overhearing his conversation via a Daedric speaking stone and defeating a Daedric crocodile beast.
- **Gala Infiltration Route ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]):** [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] navigated the sewer grates to enter the Jarl's Hall unobserved.
- **Jailbreak Passage ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 027\|Session 027]]):** [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tabitha\|Tabitha]] guided [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] through the sewer ruins to access the hidden dungeon evidence room and liberate [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]].
