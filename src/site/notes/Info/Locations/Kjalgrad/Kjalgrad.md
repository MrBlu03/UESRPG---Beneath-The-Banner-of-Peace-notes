# Kjalgrad

## Description
Kjalgrad is a fortified, bustling commercial port and trade hub situated in eastern Skyrim, known as the City of Coin. Ruled by the Jarl of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Morvan\|Clan_Morvan]], the city controls critical land and maritime trade corridors between eastern Skyrim, Cyrodiil, and Morrowind. Underneath its thriving merchant façade, the city has become a hotbed of guild rivalries, criminal cartels, and covert Daedric infiltration led by the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]].

- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]]**: Trade advocate and [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] liaison.
