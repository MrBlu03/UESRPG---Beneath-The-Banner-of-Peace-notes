# Kjalgrad

## Overview
A prosperous, fortified mountain mining city situated in [[Info/Lore/Eastmarch\|Eastmarch]]. Renowned for its rich iron mines and booming trade, the city is nominally ruled by [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Clan Morvan]] but is economically and politically controlled by the powerful [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]].

## Description
Built across ascending terraces against the snowy mountainside, Kjalgrad is divided into distinct quarters: the bustling Grand Market, the heavily fortified Warehouse District, the lower artisan quarter, the sprawling subterranean sewer ruins ([[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Sewers\|Kjalgrad_Sewers]]), and the elevated Upper District housing the Jarl's Hall.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **Clan Morvan**: The historic ruling house of Kjalgrad.
- **[[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]]**: Dominant merchant syndicate led by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jorven_Halliksson\|Jorven_Halliksson]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin_Marr]].
- **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|Lower Lodge Network]]**: Underground resistance movement led by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tarja\|Tarja]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] & [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]**: Covert operators working from the nearby barrow.
