# Kjalgrad

## Description
Kjalgrad is a fortified, bustling commercial port and trade hub situated in eastern Skyrim. Ruled by the Jarl of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Morvan\|Clan_Morvan]], the city controls critical land and maritime trade corridors between eastern Skyrim, Cyrodiil, and Morrowind. Underneath its thriving merchant façade, the city has become a hotbed of guild rivalries, criminal cartels, and covert Daedric infiltration.

## Features
- **High Stone Walls and Fortified Gates**: Guards inspect caravans and travelers entering from the mountain passes and river routes.
- **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Grand_Market_Kjalgrad\|Grand_Market_Kjalgrad]]**: The economic heart of the city where spices, silks, pelts, and weapons are traded.
- **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Clan_Morvan_Longhouse]]**: Seat of the Jarl, dominating the upper tier of the settlement.
- **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District\|Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District]]**: Sprawling docks and storage facilities along the waterways, heavily monitored by the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]].
- **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Sewers\|Kjalgrad_Sewers]]**: A dark, extensive subterranean network linking the harbor, warehouse basements, and hidden outlaw dens.
