# Frost Gate Hall

## Description
Frost Gate Hall is a warm, prominent tavern and inn located immediately inside the primary gates of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Known for its robust mead, roaring firepits, and steady influx of travelers, merchants, and mercenaries, it served as the main operations base for [[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]] during their stay in the city. It features a bustling common room, secure upstairs quarters, and courtyard stables.

## Notable NPCs
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Orgnar\|Orgnar]]**: Gruff yet dependable Nord innkeeper and barkeep.
- **Unnamed Priest of Stendarr**: A Breton mage hired by [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]].
