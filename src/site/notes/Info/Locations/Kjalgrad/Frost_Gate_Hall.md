# Frost Gate Hall

## Overview
A respectable, warm, two-story timber tavern and inn located near the southern entrance of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], owned and operated by the hospitable Nord innkeeper [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Orgnar\|Orgnar]].

## Description
A clean, welcoming tavern featuring roaring hearths, heavy wooden tables, upper-floor guest chambers, and a reputation for hearty food—most notably Orgnar's signature **Kjalgrad Sweet Pork**.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Orgnar\|Orgnar]]**: Proprietor, chef, and innkeeper.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Maintained rooms and staged operations here during their stay in Kjalgrad.

## Notable Events
- **Arrival in Kjalgrad ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 019\|Session 019]]):** The party secured lodging here after their five-day winter journey from Falderstad; visited at their window by a murder of Daedric crows led by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron_Jangleplume]].
- **Base of Operations ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]] – [[2 -  Sessions/Session 024\|Session 024]]):** Provided meals, rest, and private planning quarters for the party throughout their investigations into the Guild of the Silverroad.
- **Farewell & Recipe Gift ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 030\|Session 030]]):** [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Orgnar\|Orgnar]] shared a heartfelt farewell with [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], gifting him the secret sweet pork recipe before departing the inn to join the civic protests.
