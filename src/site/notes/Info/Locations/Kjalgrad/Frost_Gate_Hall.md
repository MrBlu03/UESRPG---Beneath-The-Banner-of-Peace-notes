# Frost Gate Hall

## Description
Frost Gate Hall is a warm, prominent tavern and inn located immediately inside the primary gates of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Known for its robust mead, roaring firepits, and steady influx of travelers, merchants, and mercenaries, it served as the main operations base for [[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]] during their stay in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

## Features
- **Central Hearth & Timber Tables**: A bustling common room filled with hunters, traders, and local guards sharing rumors.
- **Upstairs Quarters**: Secure rooms rented by travelers, providing views over the main thoroughfare and market entrance.
- **Courtyard & Stables**: Stabling facilities where party mounts ([[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|The Horses]]) were sheltered.

## NPCs
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Orgnar\|Orgnar]]**: Gruff yet dependable Nord innkeeper and barkeep.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tarja\|Tarja]]**: Resistance operative and scout who frequently met the party here.
- **Visiting Merchants & Guards**: Regular clientele sharing city whispers.
