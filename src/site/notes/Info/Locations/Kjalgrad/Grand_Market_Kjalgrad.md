## Description
The Grand Market is the vibrant commercial square of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], situated between the lower docks and the upper longhouse. Vendors from various regions congregate here to sell goods under the watchful eyes of both city guards and guild inspectors.

## Notable NPCs
- **[[Nielfa\|Nielfa]]**: A merchant who trades in the market.
