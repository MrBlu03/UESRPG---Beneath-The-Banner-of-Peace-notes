# Grand Market of Kjalgrad

## Overview
The bustling commercial center of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], lined with permanent stalls, artisan workshops, and trading posts. It is the primary venue where merchants, miners, and travelers trade goods across Eastmarch.

## Description
An expansive cobblestone plaza featuring produce stands, meat vendors, weapon smiths, clothiers, and apothecary stalls. Guarded by Silverroad-funded city sentries.

## Key Inhabitants & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Nielfa]]**: Active merchant trading goods in the market.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Cabbage_Guy\|Olaf (Cabbage Guy)]]**: Produce merchant selling cabbages and root vegetables.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jean-Pierre_LaRouche\|Jean-Pierre LaRouche]]**: High-society silk merchant whose stall was found abandoned and frozen.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lady_Elaine\|Lady_Elaine]]**, **Hana**, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Yelena\|Yelena]]**: Protesters and complainants during civil unrest.
