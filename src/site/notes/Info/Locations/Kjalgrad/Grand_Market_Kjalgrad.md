# Grand Market (Kjalgrad)

## Description
The Grand Market is the vibrant commercial square of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], situated between the lower docks and the upper longhouse. Vendors from Morrowind, Cyrodiil, and western Skyrim congregate here to sell rare herbs, textiles, weapons, foodstuffs, and smuggled goods under the watchful eyes of both city guards and [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Silverroad guild]] inspectors.

## Features
- **Canopied Merchant Rows**: Dozens of colorful stalls organized by trade goods, from Dunmeri silks to Nordic cured meats.
- **Central Well & Public Board**: A stone fountain and notice post displaying official proclamations, trade tariffs, and guild bounties.
- **Side Alley Apothecaries & Tanners**: Workshop stalls including the tanner [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Yelena\|Yelena]] and potion vendors.
