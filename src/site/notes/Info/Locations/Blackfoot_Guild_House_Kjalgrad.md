# Blackfoot Guild House (Kjalgrad)

## Basic Information
**Type:** Abandoned Guild House  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Region:** The Rift, Skyrim  
**Status:** Abandoned  
**Former Owner:** [[Info/Organizations/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild]]  
**Current Use:** Base of operations for [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]

## Description
An abandoned guild house in [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]] that formerly belonged to the [[Info/Organizations/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild]] before they left the city. The building now serves as a temporary base for [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] (operating under the alias "Sadrith Indoril") during his investigation of the **Cult of Clavicus Vile**.

## Physical Description
- **Appearance**: Similar aesthetic to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s place in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
- **Banner**: Torn banner at the front displaying the Blackfoot Guild symbol
- **Front Door**: Appears closed but hangs off its hinges
- **Windows**: Boarded up
- **Interior**: Mostly empty

## Interior Features

### Main Room
- Small light source (candle or lamp)
- **Table**: Where [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] spread his investigation materials
- **Board**: Behind the table, likely with notes and connections
- Otherwise largely empty

### Investigation Materials
When [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] examined the table after [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] departed:
- Notes on the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]
- Information about the **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]]**
- Record of someone wearing an **Ouroboros necklace** - high up in the guild

## History

### As a Guild House
- Originally operated by the [[Info/Organizations/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild]]
- Guild departed [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]] at some point (reasons unknown)
- Building fell into disuse

### Current Usage ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]])
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] set up operations here
- Using it as a base for investigating Daedric activity
- His presence was noticed, leading to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] placing a bounty

## Key Events

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]] - Meeting the Vestige

**Reconnaissance:**
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] peeked inside to scout
- Observed the Dunmer ([[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]) standing over a table
- Noted the board with information behind him
- Confirmed he was alone

**The Approach:**
- Party decided to knock rather than ambush
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] (as Sadrith Indoril) explained his investigation
- Revealed the brimstone gold and Cult of Clavicus Vile

**The Reveal:**
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] saw through [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s deception
- Lunged at her, then retreated
- Called [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] "little flower"
- Summoned his crows and revealed his true identity

**After the Sewer Mission:**
- Party returned here around dusk
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] was already seated at the table
- Discussed "The Grand Architect"
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] provided the reconstructed letter
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] agreed to pay for a week's lodging
