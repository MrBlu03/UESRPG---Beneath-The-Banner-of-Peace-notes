# By Word of Queen Freidr the Patient

Matriarch of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Volng\|Clan Volng]], Jarl of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], Queen of the Rift,
Shield of the Eastmarch Border, Bear-Mother of the Rift,
Keeper of the Watchfires, Blood-Daughter of Ysgramor

---

**Let it be known to all Jarls, Thanes, Clans, and free folk of the Rift:**

The bear watches, the wolf hunts, the hawk strikes true. So do these, the chosen hands of my hall, carry my will beyond the walls of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

The bearers of this writ act under **my seal and my voice**, as **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|Fangs of Fjelhorst]]**, sworn to safeguard our hold from serpent, shadow, and strife. Their word is to be weighed as my own in matters of protection, judgement, and defense of the people.

Aid in their passage. Head their questions as you would mine. Any who hinder them hinder me, and any who raise steel against them raise steel against the Rift itself.

Know this also: their charge is not endless war, but **truth, watchfulness, and the rooting out of hidden threat**. They walk where rumor coils, and where the serpent strikes unseen.

By my hand and by my oath, let no man or woman doubt their right to stand in my name.

---

**Given under the roof of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil Keep]], in the 600th year of the Second Era.**

**Freidr Volngsdottir**
*Queen of the Rift*

**(Here, the document bears her personal seal: a bear's head, jaws open in defiance, ringed by the knotwork of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Volng\|Clan Volng]]. Beneath it, the royal seal of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]: the crossed keys of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil Keep]].)**

## History and Significance

### Confiscation and Recovery ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 027\|Session 027]])
- During the arrest of [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], the writ was confiscated along with [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s journal by city guards and taken to the evidence room of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]].
- The corrupt steward [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Karo]] discovered the writ, using it to question [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] about her association with the Queen of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]], a guild guard who holds respect for the Queen of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] but not for the corrupt Jarl or steward of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], recovered the writ from the steward's possession and handed it to [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]].
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] hid the writ in his armor and successfully returned it to [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] during the jailbreak before the longhouse was set on fire.
