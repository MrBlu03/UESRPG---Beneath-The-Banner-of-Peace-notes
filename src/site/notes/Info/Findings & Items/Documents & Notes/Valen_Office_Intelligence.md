# Valen's Office Intelligence

## Description
During [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]], while [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] stepped out of his office at the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Silverroad_Guildhouse\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad_Guildhouse]] (after being informed by a guard — likely about the warehouse incident), the party had a brief window to examine his office. Three pieces of significant intelligence were gathered:

1. **The Wall Map** — marked locations including the warehouse district and an unknown site northeast of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]
2. **The Ledger** — documented warehouse transfers, a "temple allocation," and coin distribution
3. **The Locked Cabinet and Lockbox** — contained a damning letter about the Jarl and evidence of bribery and a mysterious planned event

## The Wall Map

### Details
A regional map hanging on Valen's wall with three notable markings:

- **Two red circles in the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District\|Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District]]** — almost certainly Warehouses 4 and 7 (the same warehouses referenced in the ledger and where the party had just investigated)
- **One mark northeast of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], outside the city walls** — the purpose of this location is unknown

### The Northeast Marker
The mark outside the city is the most significant unknown. Possible interpretations:
- A field meeting point for [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] operatives
- A ritual site (connected to the "temple allocation" ledger entry)
- A secondary storage or distribution facility
- A hidden location related to the Jarl's "condition" or supervision

## The Ledger

### Entries Observed
All entries are from within the last few weeks:

| Entry | Content | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer entry | "Transfer warehouse 4 to [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Warehouse_Seven\|warehouse 7]] — escorted shipment, night delivery" | Cargo moved covertly between guild warehouses after dark |
| Consignment entry | "Special consignment — temple allocation" | **No temple exists in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]** — code name or secret ritual site |
| Distribution entry | "Coin distribution — market circulation" | Consistent with the brimstone gold flooding operation |

### Analysis
The "temple allocation" entry is particularly alarming. The only temples that would require a "special consignment" allocation in this context are:
- A hidden Daedric shrine within the city
- A ritual site outside the city (the northeast marker?)
- A coded reference to a [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] meeting location

## The Locked Cabinet — Burnt Letter

### Contents
A **slightly burnt letter** found inside the locked cabinet read:

> *"Jarl remains cooperative though his condition requires continued supervision."*

### Significance
This is the most damaging piece of intelligence gathered in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]]:
- The Jarl of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] is being **actively managed** by the Guild or [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]
- The word "condition" implies the Jarl may be: unwell, magically controlled, under compulsion, or otherwise compromised
- The need for "continued supervision" confirms this is an ongoing arrangement, not a one-off event
- This connects directly to the [[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Dark Runic Stone\|Dark Runic Stone]] found in the Jarl's quarters ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]) and his unnaturally magical speech at the trade event

## The Lockbox

### Contents
Three items observed in the lockbox:

1. **Silver Road contracts** — standard [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]] business documentation
2. **Bribe payments to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] officials** — dated within the last **two weeks**; confirms the Guild is actively purchasing political influence in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] right now
3. **Letter fragments referencing "the Coming Correction"** — a new phrase; implies a planned event

### "The Coming Correction"
This phrase has not appeared before in the party's investigation. It suggests:
- A major Guild/[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] operation is being planned or is imminent
- The word "correction" implies a punitive or restorative action — possibly against political enemies, citizens who resist, or rivals
- Combined with the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] bribery (active within two weeks) and the Jarl being "supervised," the Coming Correction may be a political or military event affecting the entire region

## Combined Intelligence Picture

Taken together, these findings paint a clear picture of the Guild's current operations:

| Finding | Implication |
|---|---|
| Two warehouse circles on map | Guild knows exactly what's in Warehouses 4 and 7 — these are monitored operation sites |
| Northeast marker | Active operation or ritual site outside city walls — investigate |
| Night warehouse transfer | Covert movement of sensitive cargo (coin molds, ritual materials) |
| "Temple allocation" | Ritual site receiving "special consignment" cargo |
| Jarl "cooperative, condition requires supervision" | The Jarl is under Guild/[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] control |
| [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] bribes (last 2 weeks) | Guild is actively expanding political reach toward [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] right now |
| "The Coming Correction" | Major event planned — scope and target unknown |

## Party's Handling
- Intelligence was **observed and memorized** — nothing physical was taken from the office
- Valen returned to the meeting and appeared unaware of what the party had seen
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] narrowly passed a Deceive check when Valen noticed she hadn't touched her wine

## Investigation Priorities
1. **Northeast marker**: Identify the location and investigate it
2. **The Coming Correction**: Gather more information — timeline, target, nature of the event
3. **The Jarl's "condition"**: Cross-reference with the [[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Dark Runic Stone\|Dark Runic Stone]] and [[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Bloody Fur Cloak\|Bloody Fur Cloak]]
4. **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] bribes**: Determine which officials are compromised and what they have done in exchange
