# Blackfeather Court Note

## Description
A short written note delivered by a [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] crow to **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** at the window of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost_Gate_Hall]] in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] traded an apple to the crow in exchange for the note.

## Contents

The note read:

> *"Apple note from Master. Thank you."*

## Delivery Method

- A **crow** appeared at the inn window at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost_Gate_Hall]]
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] offered the crow an apple
- The crow delivered the note in exchange

## Analysis

### Literal Interpretation
The note appears to be a **receipt or acknowledgment** from the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] — specifically from its leader, referred to as "Master" (i.e., **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]**, also known as "The Crowfather").

The phrase "Apple note from Master" is ambiguous:
- It could mean: *"A note about the apple, from the Master"* — the crow is formally logging the transaction
- Or it could be an entirely characteristic piece of [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] communication — whimsical, cryptic, and seemingly trivial on the surface

### The Crow's Behavior
The exchange reinforces what is known about the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]]'s Daedric crows:
- They are **intelligent and communicative**
- They maintain contact with the party on behalf of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]
- They appear at inns and windows — tracking the party's location
- The apple-for-note exchange suggests the crows have their own system of currency and transaction

### Significance
The note itself contains no operational intelligence. However, the delivery confirms:
1. **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] is aware of the party's current location** — they are staying at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost_Gate_Hall]] and the crows found them
2. **The [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] remains in contact** even during the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] investigation
3. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] is the point of contact** for this particular crow — the crow came to her specifically

## The Vestige's Current Whereabouts
[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] was last known to be in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] but is not currently travelling with the party. The crow's continued contact suggests he is monitoring the situation, potentially from a distance, and may intervene if the party's mission becomes critical.

## Notes
- The note is mundane in content but significant as a sign of the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]]'s continued surveillance and interest
- The apple as "payment" echoes the established pattern of the crows collecting small items (see: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron Jangleplume]]'s fake soup spoon prank with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle_Benele]])
