# Autopsy Report of Adrith Velan

**Deceased**: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith Velan]] (Dunmer, Male)
**Examiner**: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sigrid\|Sigrid]], Head Priestess of the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall of the Dead]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]
**Transcribed from Memory**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]

---

## External Examination

- Prior to death, all visible wounds had been healed; scars had fully formed along the body.
	
	- Additional trauma observed: bruising and gashes consistent with **dragging across stone or earth**. These injuries were confirmed by witnesses to have been inflicted by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] while moving the deceased to the examiner.
	
	- The face was **caved in after death**, blunt force trauma consistent with deliberate post mortem damage. (Attributed to [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], who struck the corpse to ensure [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith]] had perished.)
	
- The body was discovered at the base of a tree. Drag marks behind the heels indicate **placement post mortem** rather than collapse at the scene.

---

## Toxicological Findings

- A pervasive odor of poison detected upon close inspection.
	
- **No puncture wounds or lesions** suggesting injection or forced administration. Lack of internal tissue rupture **suggests ingestion** rather than inhalation or magical application.
	
- The concentration of **Nightshade Extract** was particularly strong. This poison carries such potency that its scent is **unmistakable, even when masked.**

**Expert Consultation ([[Info/Characters/NPCs/Ralvard\|Ralvard]], Poison Specialist)**
[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Ralvard\|Ralvard]] separated the remaining toxins into their base components:

- **High Concentration**: Concentrated Nightshade Extract
	
- **Trace Elements**:
	
	- Shadow Ichor (derived exclusively from Daedric Hell Hounds).
		
	- Vampire Dust.
		
	- Twilight Poison (harvested from the stinger of a Winged Twilight, Daedric).

---

## Conclusions

- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith Velan]] **ingested** the poison **willingly**, as no signs indicated forced administration.
	
- Shadow Ichor and Vampire Dust were **deliberately introduced to mask** the pungent odor of Nighshade and bitterness of Twilight Poison.
	
- Over time, **these masking agents dissipated**, leaving only the distinctive scent of Nightshade once it had reacted with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith]]'s blood.
	
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith Velan]]'s body was **relocated after death**, his final resting place chosen with intent rather than chance.
