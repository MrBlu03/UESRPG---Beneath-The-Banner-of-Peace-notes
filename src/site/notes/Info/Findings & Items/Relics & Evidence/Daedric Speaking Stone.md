# Daedric Speaking Stone

## Basic Information
**Type:** Communication Device  
**Origin:** Oblivion (Daedric)  
**Obtained:** [[2 -  Sessions/Session 021\|Session 021]]  
**Obtained By:** [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]  
**Current Holder:** [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]

## Description
A speaking stone that differs from normal Tamrielic communication devices. Unlike standard speaking stones that facilitate one-way communication, this device is etched with **Daedric runes** and connects to entities in or associated with Oblivion.

## Physical Properties
- Stone material (specific type unknown)
- Etched with **Daedric runes**
- Used for one-way communication
- Projects an image of the recipient

## How It Was Obtained
Confiscated from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varren]] in the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Sewers\|Kjalgrad_Sewers]] during [[2 -  Sessions/Session 021\|Session 021]] after he retrieved it for us:
1. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varren]] used it to report to his superior at a dead-end in the sewers
2. The party observed the communication
3. After [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varren]] was captured and interrogated, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] took the stone

## Known Usage
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varren]] used it to communicate with his superior in the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]
- Reports included: goods purchased, amounts of gold distributed
- The entity in the projection appeared **humanoid but inhuman**
- One-way communication device (recipient controls when to respond)

## The Projection Entity
When [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varren]] used the device:
- The projection showed a humanoid figure
- Described as "not entirely human" and "weird"
- Entity appeared to be giving orders regarding brimstone gold distribution
- May be a Daedric servant or a transformed mortal

## Connection to Cult of Clavicus Vile
- Device links to [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] leadership
- Used in conspiracy involving brimstone gold
- "The Grand Architect" was mentioned during these communications
- May provide a way to trace the cult's command structure

## Potential Uses
1. **Intelligence**: Could potentially intercept communications
2. **Tracing**: May be possible to trace where communications go
3. **Evidence**: Proves Daedric involvement in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] affairs
4. **Bait**: Could be used to lure cult members
5. **Analysis**: [[Info/Organizations/Other/Mages_Guild\|Mages Guild]] might be able to study its properties

## Risks
- Using it may alert the cult that [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varren]] has been compromised
- The entity on the other end may be able to detect who holds it
- Could be tracked or traced back to the party
- Unknown magical properties could be dangerous

## Investigation Value
This device is crucial evidence connecting:
- [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] → Daedric entities
- Brimstone gold operation → Organized conspiracy
- [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] corruption → [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]]

## Notes
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] did not take the stone; it was left with the party
- May need expert analysis (possibly from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle_Benele]] or [[Info/Organizations/Other/Mages_Guild\|Mages_Guild]])
- The Daedric runes could provide clues about which Daedric Prince is involved
- Should be handled carefully to avoid unintended activation

## The Vestige's Analysis ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 021\|Session 021]])
After retrieving the stone from the sewers, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] brought it to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] for examination. His findings:
- The connection is weak, but **not due to degradation**
- The stone is functioning properly
- The weakness is because it **links to a different realm**
- Almost certainly connects to **Oblivion** itself
- This confirms direct communication between [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] agents and Daedric entities
