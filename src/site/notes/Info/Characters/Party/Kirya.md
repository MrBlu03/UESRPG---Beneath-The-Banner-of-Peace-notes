# Kirya (Kirya Ma'Tael)

## Overview
- **Player:** Kirya (Full name: Kirya Ma'Tael)
- **Race:** Khajiit
- **Class:** Wilderness Scout / Precision Ranger / Skald
- **Background:** A wandering Khajiit seeking her lost sister across Skyrim. Traveled through the Rift and Eastmarch honing her archery, music, and stealth abilities.

## Personality Traits
- **Empathetic & Caring:** Deeply concerned for the well-being of her companions (*"I care about you. Both of you. I didn't think I would, but I do"*).
- **Observant & Cautious:** High situational awareness, spotting covert symbols, tracks, and traps before others.
- **Passionate Musician:** Loves playing the lute, studying ancient Nordic sagas and instrumental techniques under twin skalds Senya and Sonya.

## Abilities and Skills
- **Specialized Archery:** Highly proficient with precision, smoke, fire, and slowing ammunition.
- **Stealth & Infiltration:** Infiltrated Jarl's private quarters and sewer labyrinths undetected; trained in advanced blade combat and stealth under [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]].
- **Musical Mastery:** Plays the lute and performs traditional instrumental pieces.

## Inventory
- Composite hunting bow and quiver of specialized ammunition (fire, smoke, slow arrows).
- Finely crafted traveler's lute.
- Ancient Dragon Priest mask trophy (powerless relic kept as a memento).
- Black feather token granted by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]].
- Insulated bear-fur cloak from Boran's Bulwarks.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal:** Track down and rescue her missing sister across Skyrim (departed east with best friend [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jzargo\|Jzargo]] in Session 026 on a fresh lead).
- **Secondary Goal:** Master traditional instrumental music and support the Fangs of Fjelhorst.

## Backstory
Left her homeland in search of her lost sister, arriving in Riften where she was recruited into Queen Freidr's royal party. Her keen senses and empathy have repeatedly preserved group morale and uncovered hidden conspiracies.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Her core companions in the Fangs of Fjelhorst.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jzargo\|Jzargo]]**: Her closest childhood friend and confidant who assists in her sister search.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]] & [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonya]]**: Twin skalds who became her musical mentors in Falderstad.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]**: Fellow stealth operative who provided advanced dagger and evasion training.

## Achievements
- Uncovered critical evidence in the Jarl's quarters including the bloody fur cloak and heated dark runic stone.
- Landed the vital slow arrow that pinned the Ragged Lord during the climactic boss battle.
- Successfully tracked and located missing alchemist [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] in the southern wilderness.
- Returned the stolen emerald pendant to barkeep [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Halla\|Halla]] in Kjalgrad.
