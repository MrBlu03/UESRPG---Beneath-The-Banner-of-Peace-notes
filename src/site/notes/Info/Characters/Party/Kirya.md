# Kirya

![Screenshot799.png](/img/user/Z%20-%20other%20bullshit/Z%20-%20Image%20Assets/Screenshot799.png)

## Overview
- **Player:** Player Character
- **Race:** Khajiit
- **Class:** Rogue / Scout / Sharpshooter
- **Background:** Baandari Wanderer
- **Status:** Departed in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]] (Traveling with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jzargo\|J'zargo]])

## Description
Kirya is a nimble, sharp-eyed Khajiit archer, scout, and rogue of [[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]. Possessing exceptional agility, keen senses, and a sharp wit, she served as the eyes, ears, and marksman of the company. Behind her playful optimism and love of warmth lies a deeply protective soul shaped by early grief over the loss of her sister.

## Background & Motivations
- Lost her sister at a young age, leaving her with profound trauma and a deep dread of losing those she cares about.
- Values transparency, honesty, and mutual trust among [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|companions]] above all else.
- Struggles in the biting northern cold, seeking comfort in sunny clearings, roaring hearths, and campfire music.
- In [[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]], after helping uncover the cult network in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], she departed with her friend [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jzargo\|J'zargo]] from the College of [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Winterhold\|Winterhold]] to pursue her own path across Skyrim, carrying the protective black feather gifted by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]].

## Personality & Quirks
- **Protective & Vulnerable:** Openly admitted her deep care for her [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|companions]] while demanding honesty in return.
- **Musical & Warm:** Plays the lute around evening campfires, raising spirits after harrowing battles.
- **Skilled Opportunist:** Adept at lockpicking, foraging rare flora, wagering on tavern brawls, and catching fish with bare paws.
- **Non-Lethal Precision:** Frequently employs specialized paralysis blowdarts to incapacitate foes without unnecessary bloodshed.

## Abilities & Skills
- **Marksman Mastery:** Deadly precision with composite hunting bows and silvered arrows.
- **Stealth & Infiltration:** Silent movement, lockpicking, trap disarming, and urban reconnaissance.
- **Survival & Foraging:** Wilderness tracking, identifying alchemical herbs, and gathering game.

## Mount
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses#Sunsnow\|Sunsnow]]**: A dappled cream mare with a soft golden mane. Playful, curious, and light-footed, reflecting Kirya's warmth and optimistic spirit in the dark northern wilderness.
