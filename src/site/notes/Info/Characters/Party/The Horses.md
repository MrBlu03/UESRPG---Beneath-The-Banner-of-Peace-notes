Profiles for the horses given to the Fangs of Fjelhorst during [[2 -  Sessions/Session 013\|The Road to Falderstad]].

# Rimefang

The horse of [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov Vostroyan]]

- **Appearance:** A massive **iron-grey stallion**, with a thick neck and feathered legs. His coat catches light like steel, mane almost black. Taller even than most Nord mounts — a true giant.
    
- **Temperament:** Calm, steady, almost statuesque in demeanor. Doesn’t spook, doesn’t shy, but when he moves, the ground seems to shudder beneath his hooves. He meets Durov’s gaze without flinching, as if weighing him in silence.
    
- **Symbolic tie:** Just like Durov, Rimefang is reserved but commanding in presence — a quiet power. Where Durov hides behind his helm, the horse hides behind stillness. Together, they make a pair that others underestimate until forced to reckon with their strength.

---

# Sunsnow 

The horse of [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]

- **Appearance:** A **dappled cream mare**, her coat pale as sand with patches of white, like snow melting on stone. Her mane is long, golden, and unusually soft, braided by the stablehands.
    
- **Temperament:** Light-footed, curious, quick to nudge pockets for treats. She seems to prefer warmth, standing in the brightest patches of sun. Though playful, she moves with surprising grace, threading through gaps the others would avoid.
    
- **Symbolic tie:** Sunsnow is warmth in the cold — a mirror of Kirya’s helpful optimism despite loss. Where Kirya hates the cold, her mare seems like a spark of summer that carries it away. Both are survivors who find light and laughter in the shadows.

---

# Mossback

The horse of [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]

- **Appearance:** A **deep chestnut gelding**, broad but not tall, with a coat that shines almost bronze in the sun. His mane is tangled with little burrs and a hint of green moss clings near his fetlocks from standing in river shallows.
    
- **Temperament:** Gentle, cautious, steady of step. He nudges with his nose softly at hands, but startles easily at loud voices — yet recovers quickly, trusting his rider to guide him. Loves water, often wading knee-deep into rivers.
    
- **Symbolic tie:** Mossback reflects Onasha’s quiet pragmatism and her survivor’s caution. He isn’t bold, but he endures, careful on the path where others stumble. Both horse and rider live in a balance of fear and care — and both know that patience, not pride, is what ensures survival.

---

# Ashen

The horse of [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn Lunclaire]]

- **Appearance:** A **coal-black mare**, leaner than the others, with a single pale white streak across her muzzle like a scar. Her eyes are sharp and restless, scanning everything around her.
    
- **Temperament:** Fierce and fiery — not cruel, but quick to anger, quick to surge forward. She pulls at the reins, restless for movement, impatient with hesitation.
    
- **Symbolic tie:** Ashen mirrors Periwyn’s grief and fury — not tamed, but channeled. She’s not the most “peaceful” horse, but she is a warhorse in spirit: relentless, headstrong, and fearless in the face of threat. Only a rider who knows pain and refuses to yield could hold her.
