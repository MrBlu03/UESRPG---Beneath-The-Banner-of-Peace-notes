# The Horses of Fjelhorst

Profiles for the loyal warhorses and mounts granted to [[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]] during [[2 -  Sessions/Session 013\|Session 013]] prior to their long expedition to [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] and [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

---

## Rimefang
**Mount of [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**

- **Appearance:** A massive **iron-grey gelding** with a thick neck and heavily feathered legs. His coat catches light like burnished steel, with a mane almost pitch black. Taller even than most Nord draft mounts—a true powerhouse.
- **Temperament:** Calm, steady, and statuesque. Doesn't spook or shy at battle cries, moving with ground-shuddering power. Meets [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s gaze without flinching, weighing his rider in silent trust.
- **Symbolic Tie:** Reserved, quiet power. Where [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] hides behind his helmet, Rimefang hides behind stillness. Together, they form an immovable vanguard.

---

## Sunsnow
**Mount of [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**

- **Appearance:** A **dappled cream mare**, her coat pale as sand with white snow-like patches. Her long golden mane is unusually soft and carefully braided.
- **Temperament:** Light-footed, curious, and playful, constantly nudging pockets for sweet treats. Prefers bright sun and threads gracefully through rugged terrain.
- **Symbolic Tie:** Warmth in the cold—a mirror of [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s cheerful optimism and resilience. A spark of summer enduring in the northern frost.

---

## Mossback
**Mount of [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**

- **Appearance:** A broad **deep chestnut gelding** whose coat shines like bronze in the sunlight. His mane carries little forest burrs, and moss often clings near his fetlocks from standing in river shallows.
- **Temperament:** Gentle, cautious, and fond of water. Startles at sudden shouts but recovers swiftly under [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s calm guidance. Loves wading through rivers and marshes.
- **Symbolic Tie:** Reflects [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s quiet pragmatism and survivor's caution. Enduring and patient, moving safely where bolder steeds stumble.

---

## Ashen
**Mount of [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**

- **Appearance:** A lean, muscular **coal-black mare** with a stark white blaze across her muzzle resembling a battle scar. Her eyes are sharp and restless.
- **Temperament:** Fierce, spirited, and eager to charge forward. Pulls at the reins, impatient with hesitation, yet deeply devoted to her rider.
- **Symbolic Tie:** Mirrors [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s channeled fury and warrior spirit. A warhorse at heart: relentless, fearless, and forged for battle.
