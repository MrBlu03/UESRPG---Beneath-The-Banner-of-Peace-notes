# Durov

## Overview
- **Player:** Durov
- **Race:** Imperial (Colovian heritage, estimated under 30 years of age)
- **Class:** Heavy Frontline Warrior / Mercenary Tank
- **Background:** Former Legionnaire with amnesia regarding his life prior to mercenary work. Wears an enclosed heavy steel helm almost constantly out of paranoia of head wounds. Bears an eagle crest on his shield matching ancient Colovian noble houses of Bruma.

## Personality Traits
- **Pragmatic & Blunt:** Direct, no-nonsense soldier who values straightforward solutions, hearty meals, and cold logic.
- **Deeply Loyal:** Acts as the impenetrable shield of the party, unhesitatingly placing himself between his allies and lethal threats.
- **Dislikes Pretension:** Disdains stiff aristocratic protocol and rigid formality, preferring honesty and camaraderie.
- **Pastimes:** Enjoys playing Solitaire with cards, entering drinking contests, and eating traditional dishes like Kjalgrad Sweet Pork.

## Abilities and Skills
- **Frontline Tanking & Defense:** High physical endurance, heavy armor mastery, and shield blocking.
- **Unrelenting Talent:** Signature defensive ability that prevents massive foes from disengaging or trampling allies.
- **Brawling & Executions:** Brutal close-quarters combatant capable of ripping the spine and head from Daedric warlords (*The Xivilai*).
- **Infiltration & Espionage:** Posed as a guard for the Guild of the Silverroad and Trade Advocate Vaelin Marr, gaining insider clearance into fortified installations.

## Inventory
- Heavy plate armor and full enclosed steel helmet.
- Reinforced broad shield emblazoned with the eagle **Crest of Bruma**.
- Steel war axe and enchanted silver weapons.
- Inscribed bone totem from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonya]] commemorating participation in Nordic Law.
- Stamped Imperial Legion identification tag (number zero) from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]].
- Authentic recipe for Kjalgrad Sweet Pork gifted by innkeeper [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Orgnar\|Orgnar]].

## Goals
- **Short-Term:** Aid the party in dismantling the Cult of Clavicus Vile and securing Eastmarch.
- **Long-Term:** Recover his lost memories and uncover the truth of his identity and Colovian heritage.

## Backstory
Found wandering with little recollection of his past prior to his mercenary days. Joined the adventuring party in Riften under royal summons from Queen Freidr, where his tactical discipline and martial resilience quickly established him as the party's defensive anchor.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Trusted comrades in the Fangs of Fjelhorst.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain_Irlav]]**: Fellow Colovian Imperial and guard captain with whom he shares soldierly camaraderie and homeland heritage.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]**: Respected ally with whom he shared deep philosophical conversations regarding mortality and soul-shriven existence.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl_Gorm]]**: Earned the Jarl's deep respect and 500 gold bounty after the slaying of the Ragged Lord, engaging in a memorable longhouse drinking contest.

## Achievements
- Slew the Daedric Xivilai commander in the Dwemer ruins by tearing off its head and spine.
- Prevented the Ragged Lord's escape in the Cursed Grove with his Unrelenting defensive stance.
- Served as the official executioner in Falderstad's traditional Nordic justice ritual.
- Secured full free deed and perpetual ownership of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Nilans_House\|Nilans_House]] for the party.
