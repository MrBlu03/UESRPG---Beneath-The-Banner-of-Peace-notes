# Durov

![Screenshot800.png](/img/user/Z%20-%20other%20bullshit/Z%20-%20Image%20Assets/Screenshot800.png)

## Overview
- **Player:** Player Character
- **Race:** Imperial (Colovian)
- **Class:** Heavy Vanguard / Shield-Bearer
- **Background:** [[Info/Organizations/Military/Imperial_Legion\|Imperial Legion]] Veteran (Colovian Nobility of Bruma)
- **Status:** Active ([[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The Fangs of Fjelhorst]])

## Description
Durov is the stalwart, heavily armored vanguard and shield of [[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]. Stoic, disciplined, and possessing unyielding martial resolve, he is famously known for never removing his helmet in public—a habit born from a combination of amnesia, military paranoia about headshots, and deep personal reserve. Beneath his steel plate lies a warrior haunted by a lost past, seeking truth in the frozen valleys of Skyrim.

## Backstory & The Lost Past
- Suffers from amnesia regarding his previous life before awakening in Skyrim.
- Carries an ancient, heavy Colovian kite shield emblazoned with the **Bruma Crest**: a two-headed eagle, with one head looking forward toward the future and one head looking back with its eye closed (representing the tragic failure to learn from the past).
- In [[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014a]]/015, tracked down ex-Legionnaire [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gaius\|Gaius]] outside [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], recovering an [[Info/Organizations/Military/Imperial_Legion\|Imperial Legion]] identification tag marked with a zero.
- Connections to the traumatic Planemeld Crisis (circa 2E 582), during which a Dark Anchor fell into the Bruma market square in front of the Cathedral of Saint Lucia.
- Plagued by battlefield memories of fallen comrades and a snowy Colovian fortress.

## Personality & Traits
- **Stoic & Resolute:** Speaks sparingly, preferring direct action and defensive readiness over boastful words.
- **Unshakable Loyalty:** Fiercely protective of his party members ([[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]), placing himself between danger and his allies in every engagement.
- **Private Habits:** Eats meals behind his tent or apart from crowds to avoid exposing his face; sleeps with his helmet nearby.
- **Pragmatic Mercenary:** Possesses a sharp eye for bargaining, weapon values, and salvage.

## Abilities & Combat Style
- **Heavy Shield Mastery:** Employs defensive shield-bashes, protective bulwarks, and doorway-blocking chokepoints.
- **Heavy Plate Armor:** High physical resilience and poise against heavy strikes and beast claws.
- **Tactical Discipline:** Trained in standard [[Info/Organizations/Military/Imperial_Legion\|Imperial Legion]] infantry tactics, weapon maintenance, and perimeter security.

## Mount
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses#Rimefang\|Rimefang]]**: A massive iron-grey gelding with feathered legs and a steel-like coat. Quiet, powerful, and unyielding, perfectly matching Durov's reserved strength.
