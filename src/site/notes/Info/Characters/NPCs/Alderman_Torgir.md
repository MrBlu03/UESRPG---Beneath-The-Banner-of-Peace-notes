# Alderman Torgir

## Description
The earnest, weary village elder and spokesman for the fishing community at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Trevas_Bend]]. Driven by desperate concern for his starving people during the water crisis, he initially fell victim to the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]'s deceptive agitation before working with the party to restore peace with [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Snow-Shod\|Clan Snow-Shod]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith_Velan]]**: Secretly met with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith]] under the guise of a 'concerned kinsman' before realizing he was being manipulated.
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Snow-Shod\|Clan_Snow-Shod]]**: Reconciled with the clan after the dam sabotage was exposed.
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]**: Rewarded the party with gold for restoring the river and resolving the crisis.

## Location
- Resides in the elder's hall at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Trevas_Bend]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]**: Met with the party upon their arrival at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]]. Confirmed river starvation and admitted to clandestine meetings near the dam. Once the party cleared the sabotage and delivered [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith]]'s corpse, Torgir promised to pursue reconciliation with [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Snow-Shod\|Clan Snow-Shod]] and pledged a reward of 50 gold per member once the fishing nets filled again.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: Acknowledged the sustained recovery of river trade and honored his commitments to the party.
