# Alderman Torgir

## Description
The alderman and head of the riverside settlement of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]]. A pragmatic and cautious leader devoted to defending the livelihood of his village's fishermen.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Snow-Shod\|Clan Snow-Shod]]**: Neighboring clan upstream; Torgir sought fragile reconciliation after the dam dispute was cleared.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]**: Acknowledged royal jurisdiction when the party invoked the Queen's authority.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Negotiated the town's compensation with Durov, paying the exact agreed-upon reward of 50 gold per person once trade resumed.

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]]**

## Notes
- **Water Dispute Crisis ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]):** Greeted the party with suspicion before revealing that the stoppage at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Dam\|Snow-Shod Dam]] had ruined local fishing. Admitted to meeting a suspicious "concerned kinsman" upstream under cover of night. Promised to pay the party 50 gold each once nets were filled again.
- **Debt Fulfilled ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]):** Paid the promised 50 gold per party member when the group stopped in Treva's Bend on their hunting trip, correctly rejecting [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s humorous attempt to claim 60 gold.
