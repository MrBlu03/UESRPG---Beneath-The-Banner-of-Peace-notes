# Alderman Torgir

## Description
The earnest, weary village elder and spokesman for the fishing community at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Trevas_Bend]]. Driven by desperate concern for his starving people during a manufactured water crisis, he works hard to maintain his village's survival amidst political manipulation.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith_Velan]]**: Secretly met with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith]] under the guise of a 'concerned kinsman' before realizing he was being manipulated.
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Snow-Shod\|Clan_Snow-Shod]]**: Pledged to pursue reconciliation with the clan after the true sabotage was exposed.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Negotiated the party's compensation for resolving the crisis.

## Location
- Resides in the elder's hall at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Trevas_Bend]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]**: Greeted the party with deep suspicion until they invoked [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]'s name. He admitted to holding clandestine meetings with a manipulator near the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod]] dam. After the party exposed the sabotage, he promised reconciliation with [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Snow-Shod\|Clan Snow-Shod]] and agreed to [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s demand for 50 gold per member once the river recovered.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: Met with the party during their travels and honored his commitment, paying exactly 50 gold to each member despite [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s attempt to extort 60.
