# The Witch of the Woods

## Description
A grotesque, bloodthirsty hagraven nesting in the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Western_Woods\|Western_Woods]] who performed dark sacrifices and terrorized wilderness hunters.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Slain by [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] in a holy duel.

## Location
- Lair located at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Witchs_Clearing\|Witchs_Clearing]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 023a Periwyns witch hunt\|Session 023a]]**: Tracked down and slain by [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] during her solo expedition, purging her vile blood altar and ending her curse over the woods.
