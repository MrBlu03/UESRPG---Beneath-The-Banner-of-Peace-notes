# Olen the Mer-Killer

## Description
A lean, hawkish, battle-hardened Nord veteran of [[The_Companions\|The Companions]] and the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]]. Renowned for his martial ferocity and deep experience against elves and supernatural foes.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]]**: Fought alongside the Harbinger on multiple campaigns.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild the Unwavering]]**: Close companion-in-arms who fought and died beside him at Greenwall.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Shared wagon reins, war stories, and tactical discussions on the march to Greenwall.

## Location
- Camped outside and assaulted **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]**.

## Notes
- **The March to Greenwall ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]):** Marched in the frontline vanguard, helping [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and Gjellir cremate the 17 roadside execution victims.
- **Heroic Death in Battle ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 006\|Session 006]]):** Slain during the fierce storming of Greenwall fortress while fighting the occupying vampire coven.
- **Funeral Pyre & Memory ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]):** Given a warrior's cremation on a smoking funeral pyre outside the fortress, and remembered with solemn toasts at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Amber_Keg\|Amber Keg]] in Riften.
