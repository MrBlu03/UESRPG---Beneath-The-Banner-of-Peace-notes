# Tarja (Strider)

## Description
A courageous, sharp-shooting Nord ranger, scout, and coordinator for the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Lower_Lodge_Network\|Lower_Lodge_Network]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. A fierce protector of the city's common folk against cultist tyranny and [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Silverroad]] corruption.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]**: Key ally and resistance coordinator in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Orgnar\|Orgnar]]**: Trusted tavern contact.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tabitha\|Tabitha]]**: Fellow scout.

## Location
- Operates out of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|The_Lower_Lodge]] and [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost_Gate_Hall]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
