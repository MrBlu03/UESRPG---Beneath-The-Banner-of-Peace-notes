# Bjorn (Hunter)

## Description
A seasoned Nord hunter based in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. He wears studded leather and furs, sporting a distinctive goatee and mustache combo.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Posted a bounty and briefed Periwyn regarding the supernatural threat terrorizing the western woods.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|The_Witch_of_the_Woods]]**: The hagraven-like entity that ambushed him and killed his horse.

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]** (Tavern and surrounding western woods).

## Notes
- **Witch of the Woods Bounty ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 023a Periwyns witch hunt\|Session 023a Periwyns witch hunt]]):** Posted a bounty in Kjalgrad after his hunting horse was brutally attacked with fire and clawed to death west of the city. Provided [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] with a hand-drawn map of the creature's territorial boundary and the location of their abandoned campsite.
