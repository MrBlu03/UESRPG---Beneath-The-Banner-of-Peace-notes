# Bjorn Hunter

## Description
A hunter based near [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] who wears studded leather and fur and sports a distinctive goatee and mustache combo. 

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Provided her with intel and a map regarding a creature bounty.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|The_Witch_of_the_Woods]]**: The creature that killed his horse and several other people.

## Location
- Frequents taverns in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] and hunts in the woods to the west.

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 023a Periwyns witch hunt\|Session 023a]]**: He posted a bounty for "The Witch of the Woods" after his horse was attacked and killed by fire magic while hunting west of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. When [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] inquired about the bounty at the tavern, he explained the situation and provided her with a rough map showing the creature's territorial range and an abandoned hunter camp. Later, it was planned for [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] to inform him of a negotiated truce with the witch.
