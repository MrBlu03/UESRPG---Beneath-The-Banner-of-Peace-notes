# Nohrak (The Ragged Lord / Skinstealer)

## Description
A terrifying ancient monster of primal dread—a towering, stag-skulled predator corrupted by dark blood rites and [[Info/Lore/Hircine\|Hircine]]'s curse. Known as the Skinstealer and Ragged Lord, it terrorized the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Northern_Forest\|Northern Forest]] by leaving flayed deer in deliberate spiral patterns.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]**: Summoned him using the ancient Old Nordic hunt-nymic chant and burned him with fire magic.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Cleaved off his arms with her sword during the final battle.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Delivered the killing blow by shooting an arrow through his kneecap.

## Location
- Lurked in the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Cursed_Grove\|Cursed_Grove]] deep within the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]]**: Stalked the woods around Falderstad, carving distorted Nordic runes into trees and hunting prey with surgical precision.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]]**: Trapped the hunting party in a supernatural maze, killed [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorr\|Bjorr]], and ambushed [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] at the forest edge before being blocked by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]].
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]]**: Was summoned in the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Cursed_Grove\|Cursed Grove]] by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]'s ritual. Fought a brutal battle against the party, using teleportation and a hide-whip. After taking severe fire damage, its stag skull split to reveal a scarred human face before being slain by the party, leaving behind only ash and a hunter's token.
