# Nohrak (The Ragged Lord / Skinstealer)

## Description
An ancient, terrifying Daedric aspect of **[[Info/Lore/Hircine\|Hircine]]** (Prince of the Hunt), known locally as **The Skinstealer** or **The Ragged Lord** (Anorak). Embodying hunter's madness, cruelty, and spatial manipulation, it wears a mantle of stitched skins and a stag skull concealing a scarred, hollow-eyed face. It wields skin whips, commands corrupted wolf packs, mimics human voices, and twists forest geometry. It is severely vulnerable to silver and the sacred fire of [[Info/Lore/Kyne\|Kyne]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Lore/Hircine\|Hircine]]**: Divine Daedric patron whose aspect of madness and savagery it represents.
- **The Hunters of Falderstad**: Terrorized, murdered, and carved totems depicting local hunters (including [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorr\|Bjorr]]).
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Tracked, summoned, and destroyed the beast in a climactic battle.

## Location
- The **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Cursed_Grove\|Cursed Grove]]** in the **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Northern_Forest\|Northern Forest]]** north of **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]**.

## Notes
- **Supernatural Terror & Spatial Maze ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]]):** Terrorized the region for weeks. Trapped the hunting party in a reality-bending spatial maze where it assassinated woodman [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorr\|Bjorr]] and mimicked [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Ingrin\|Ingrin]]'s voice. Repelled at the forest boundary when [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] used fire salts.
- **The Ritual Summoning & Epic Battle ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]]):** Summoned at its grotesque bone altar when [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] recited its ancient nymic. After seven corrupted wolves were slain, the Ragged Lord materialized. It critically wounded [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]] before [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] severed its right arm, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] blocked its teleportation attacks, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] fired the killing silver arrow through its kneecap. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] tore off its head and spine, reducing the monster to ash and recovering an antler charm.
