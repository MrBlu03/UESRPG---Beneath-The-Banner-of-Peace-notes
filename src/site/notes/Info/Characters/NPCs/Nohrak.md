# Nohrak (The Ragged Lord / Skinstealer)

> *"A primal shadow crowned in bone—the beast that wears the flesh of men and stalks the northern woods in Hircine's dread name."*

## Description
A horrifying entity of primal terror and ancient curse—a towering, stag-skulled predator born of twisted Nordic blood rites and the hunting sphere of [[Info/Lore/Hircine\|Hircine]]. Known across the hold as the Skinstealer and the Ragged Lord, it terrorized the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Northern_Forest\|Northern Forest]] surrounding [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], leaving flayed victims and severed deer in deliberate, ritualistic spiral formations. Capable of weaving spatial mists, displacing itself through the trees, and wearing the faces and flesh of its victims, its death in the Cursed Grove revealed the tragic truth: beneath the stag skull lay the agonizingly altered remains of an ancient hunter cursed to hunger eternally.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]**: Lawspeaker and runic scholar of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] who broke its spatial maze, chanted its ancient true-nymic to force a physical manifestation, and immolated it with fire magic.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Stalked and ambushed her at the forest edge; in the final clash, she cleaved through its hide-whip arm with her greatsword.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Shielded the hunting company from its psychic ambushes and held the line against its brutal teleportation strikes.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Slid an alchemical arrow through its kneecap to cripple its escape, allowing the party to finish it off and claim its enchanted hunter's token.

## Location
- Lurked within the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Cursed_Grove\|Cursed Grove]] deep within the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]], ranging as far as the perimeter outposts of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].
