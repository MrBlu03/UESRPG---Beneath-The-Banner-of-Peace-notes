# Helga Cairn-Shield

## Description
The formidable, disciplined Huscarl to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl Gorm]] of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] and the elder sister of huntress [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]. She commands the town guard, oversees garrison security, and manages defense operations with strict military authority.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl Gorm]]**: Serves as his loyal huscarl, personal protector, and military commander.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]**: Her younger sister, whom she deeply cares for and worries about during dangerous hunts.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Collaborated on city defense, anti-smuggling raids, and prisoner handling.

## Location
- Longhouse and Garrison in **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]**.

## Notes
- **Smuggler Crackdown ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]]):** Coordinated the arrest of the Dunmer smuggling caravan flying false Hlaalu banners, providing options for their incarceration and justice.
- **Defense Against the Skinstealer ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]]):** Organized town defenses, arming guards with fire arrows and setting bonfires. Personally thanked the party for returning her sister [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]] alive from the hunt.
- **Smuggler Ledger & Interrogation ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]):** Led the party to the basement cells to interrogate the captured Dunmer smugglers and provided their confiscated ciphered ledger bearing the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] emblem.
- **Departure Farewell ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 019\|Session 019]]):** Bid farewell to the party alongside Svala as they departed Falderstad for Kjalgrad.
