# Helga Cairn-Shield

## Description
A stern, resolute Nord matriarch and shield-thane of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. With [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl Gorm]] incapacitated by bone rot, Helga assumed administrative leadership of the town, fiercely defending her people against forest horrors.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl_Gorm]]**: Serving as acting protector and advisor during the Jarl's severe illness.
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]**: Commissioned the hunt for the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]**: Coordinated town defense and hunting logistics.

## Location
- Governs from the longhouse in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].
