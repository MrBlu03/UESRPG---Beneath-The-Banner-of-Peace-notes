# Colleux

## Description
A Colovian blacksmith residing in [[Info/Lore/Bruma\|Bruma]], Cyrodiil. He is the father of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain Irlav]] and husband to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Leona\|Leona]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain Irlav]]**: His son, who serves as guard captain in Skyrim.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Leona\|Leona]]**: His wife in Bruma.

## Location
- **[[Info/Lore/Bruma\|Bruma]]**, **[[Info/Lore/Cyrodiil\|Cyrodiil]]**.

## Notes
- **Family History Recalled ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 028\|Session 028]]):** Mentioned by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain Irlav]] during a night ride with [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], recounting his family background, his father's smithing trade, and their survival during the Daedric Planemeld crisis in Bruma.
