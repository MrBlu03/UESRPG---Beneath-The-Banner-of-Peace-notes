# Alara

## Description
Alara is an adventurous young Nord alchemist based in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. Known for venturing deep into wilderness areas to forage rare herbs and fungi, she frequently provides local adventurers and herbalists with quality reagents.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svana\|Svana]]**: Close friend who sought the party's aid when Alara went missing in the woods.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Fellow alchemist who regularly trades reagents, salt, and bomb supplies with her.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Noticed [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s shield crest, remarking that it reminded her of a Colovian companion from her travels.

## Location
- Usually found at her apothecary workbench and herb gardens in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]**: Went missing while foraging in the woods outside [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. The party tracked her down and rescued her from wilderness predators alongside a protective spriggan.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]**: Offered alchemical supplies and fire salts to [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] to aid in crafting bombs for the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] expedition.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]**: The party visited her shop to confirm her family's safety following the liberation of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Fort Greenwall]].
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 009\|Session 009]]**: Supplied [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] with purified alchemical salts for protective rituals.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: Joined the party, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]], and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] on a relaxing camping and hunting trip into the Rift forests.
