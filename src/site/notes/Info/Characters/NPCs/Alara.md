# Alara

## Description
An experienced herbalist, alchemist, and proprietor of the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Scent_of_Serenity\|Scent of Serenity]] apothecary in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. She frequently travels into the southern woods to forage rare ingredients and has maintained a unique, peaceful friendship with a wild spriggan for over twenty years. She is also the owner of John, a loyal husky dog.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hafjorg\|Hafjorg]]**: Her son, who assists at the alchemy shop and was deeply distraught when she went missing.
- **John**: A stray husky dog she rescued and adopted three years ago.
- **Wild Spriggan**: A forest creature with whom she has shared a gentle, protective companionship for two decades.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst ([[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]])**: Rescued her in the southern wilderness ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]) and later accompanied her on a hunting expedition ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]).

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Scent_of_Serenity\|Scent of Serenity]]** alchemy shop in **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**.
- Occasionally found foraging in the **Southern Woods** near Riften.

## Notes
- **Missing Alchemist Contract ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]):** Vanished for 2-3 days on an herb-gathering expedition, taking shelter in a southern cave under the gentle guard of her spriggan companion. Provided a dark blue ribbon as proof of life when located by the party.
- **Fire Salts Offer ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]):** Offered crafting materials for firebombs/fire salts to assist with the threat at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]].
- **Tragedy in Greenwall ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]):** Devastated by the news delivered by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] that none of her relatives in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] survived the vampire massacre, closing the shop to mourn in private.
- **Hunting Expedition ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]):** Joined the party, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]], and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] on a stag-hunting and camping excursion north of Riften with her husky John, discussing her spriggan bond.
