# Ingvar Snow-Shod

## Description
Ingvar Snow-Shod is the proud, influential patriarch and overseer of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Snow-Shod\|Clan_Snow-Shod]] at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod_Estate]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The Rift]]. Fiercely defensive of his clan's honor, agricultural holdings, and ancient land rights, he was initially defensive when accused of maliciously starving the river during the regional water crisis.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Alderman_Torgir]]**: Feuded bitterly over water rights before reconciling when the sabotage was exposed.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]]**: Loyal subject of the Rift crown.
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]**: Vindicated his clan by uncovering the true saboteur [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith_Velan]].

## Location
- Usually found at the manor house on [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod_Estate]].
