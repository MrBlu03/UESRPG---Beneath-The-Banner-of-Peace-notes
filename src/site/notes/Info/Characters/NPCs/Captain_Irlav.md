# Captain Irlav

## Description
The honorable commander of the city guard in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Despite finding himself caught in the corrupt web of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Silverroad]] and the false steward, he remained dedicated to the defense of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]'s people.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Morvan\|Jarl of Kjalgrad]]**: Ruler of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Morvan\|Clan Morvan]] whom he faithfully protects.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]]**: Clashed with the false steward over security protocols.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Respected [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s military discipline and shared guard intelligence.

## Location
- Headquartered at the guard post in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Clan_Morvan_Longhouse]].
