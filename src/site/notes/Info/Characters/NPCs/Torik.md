# Torik

## Description
A wise, venerable Nord master hunter, herbalist, and shamanic practitioner in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], proprietor of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Thistle_Branch\|The_Thistle_Branch]]. Possesses deep knowledge of the Old Ways, [[Info/Lore/Kyne\|Kyne]]'s sacred balance, and ancient hunt rituals.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]**: Loyal hunting companion.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Shared herbalism and bone totem crafting wisdom.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Provided the Old Nordic summon nymic to draw out the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]].

## Location
- Operates [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Thistle_Branch\|The_Thistle_Branch]] in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].
