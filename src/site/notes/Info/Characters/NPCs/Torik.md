# Torik

## Description
A wise, clever elder Nord mage and scholar educated at the College of [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Winterhold\|Winterhold]]. He owns and operates **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Thistle_Branch\|The Thistle Branch]]** apothecary in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] and serves as the town's respected Lawspeaker. He is deeply versed in ancient Nordic runes, woad painting, and fire magic.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]**: Tactical partner in organizing the expedition against the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Skinstealer]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]]**: Mentors young Hoki in herbalism and potion-making.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Taught Onasha the *Fox's Guile* woad and gifted her the hunter's antler charm recovered from the Ragged Lord.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Analyzed Durov's rune carvings and assisted with tactical preparations.

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Thistle_Branch\|The Thistle Branch]]** in **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]**.

## Notes
- **Breaking the Spatial Maze ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]]):** Identified the Othala rune carved on Durov's bracer as meaning "heritage" and used consecrated fire salts to shatter the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Skinstealer]]'s reality-twisting forest maze.
- **Summoning the Ragged Lord ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]]):** Performed the ancient ritual at the bone shrine, reciting the true nymic of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Nohrak, the Ragged Lord]] and casting devastating fireballs during the battle. Recovered the stag-shaped antler sliver from the ashes.
- **Woad Training & Apprenticeship ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]):** Taught [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] the *Fox's Guile* woad for 30 gold, explained the history of the hunter's token, and welcomed [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]] as his alchemy apprentice.
