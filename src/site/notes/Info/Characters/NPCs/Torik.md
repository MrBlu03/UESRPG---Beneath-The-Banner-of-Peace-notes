# Torik

## Description
A wise older mage, scholar of the Clever Craft from [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Winterhold\|Winterhold]], and Lawspeaker of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. He operates [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Thistle_Branch\|The_Thistle_Branch]] and possesses deep knowledge of runes, alchemy, and the Old Ways.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]**: Organized the hunt which he helped lead.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Taught her the Fox's Guile woad and gifted her an antler charm.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]]**: Took him on as an alchemy student.

## Location
- [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Thistle_Branch\|The_Thistle_Branch]] in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].
