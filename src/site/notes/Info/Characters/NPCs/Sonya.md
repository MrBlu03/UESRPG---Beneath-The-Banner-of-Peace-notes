# Sonya (Sonja)

## Description
A gifted twin skald traveling with her sister [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]]. A master of the lute, flutes, and old Nordic battle chants.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]]**: Her twin sister and musical partner.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Gifted [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] a sacred Nordic bone totem in honor of his valor.

## Location
- Performs in taverns across [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The Rift]] and [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]]**: Sang the ancient sagas of the Broken Jarls at the wayside inn.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: Accompanied the party on their decompression retreat in the Rift.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]**: Gifted [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] a sacred Nordic bone totem in recognition of his courage, and played *Bonden og Kragen* during the victory feast in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].
