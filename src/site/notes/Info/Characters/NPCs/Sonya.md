# Sonya (Sonja)

## Description
A gifted Nord skald (warrior-poet) and musician who travels across Skyrim with her twin sister [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]]. She plays the Tagelharpa and is well-versed in ancient Nordic law, oral histories, and ceremonial sagas.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]]**: Her twin sister and performance partner.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Agni\|Agni]]**: Fellow skald who joined them in musical performance in Falderstad.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Agreed to provide music and instrumental lessons to Kirya.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Presented Durov with an inscribed bone totem commemorating his role in Nordic law.

## Location
- Taverns across **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]** and **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]**.

## Notes
- **Saga of Clan Cold-Moor ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]]):** Recited the tragic tale of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] at The Sleeping Stag in Riften.
- **Falderstad Law & Performance ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]):** Traveled to Falderstad to provide ceremonial music for the traditional execution. Presented [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] with a carved bone totem with Nordic runes, entered the party's names into the Book of Falderstad, and agreed to stay extra days to teach musical techniques to [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]].
