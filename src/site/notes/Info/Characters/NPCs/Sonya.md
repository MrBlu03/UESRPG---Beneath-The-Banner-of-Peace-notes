# Sonya (Sonja)

## Description
A gifted twin skald traveling with her sister [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]]. She collects names for the Book of Falderstad and performs ancient Nordic sagas.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]]**: Her twin sister and musical partner.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Gifted him a carved bone totem in recognition of his role in Nordic Law.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Arranged to stay and teach her local instruments.

## Location
- Performs in taverns across the Rift and [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], such as [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]].

- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: Set up her instruments alongside her sister at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderers_Rest]].
