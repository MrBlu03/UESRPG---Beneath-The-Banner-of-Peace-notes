# Archmagus Mortgren

## Description
A renowned Breton Archmagus of legendary renown who served High King Emeric during the height of the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Daggerfall_Covenant\|Daggerfall Covenant]]. His scholarly treatises on necromancy, planar conjuration, and bone magic are considered masterworks of arcane theory. He is far older than he appears, having survived for centuries.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]]**: Corresponded on matters of planar rifts and ancient curses.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Mentored her in defensive necromantic arts, providing her a conjured tome.
- **High King Emeric**: Served historically as High Arcane Advisor in Wayrest.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]]**: Identified his connection to a rogue vampire of [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]].

## Location
- Historically based in [[Info/Lore/High_Rock\|High_Rock]]; currently traveling Skyrim.
