# Archmagus Mortgren

## Description
A renowned Breton Archmagus of legendary renown who served High King Emeric during the height of the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Daggerfall_Covenant\|Daggerfall Covenant]]. His scholarly treatises on necromancy, planar conjuration, and bone magic are considered masterworks of arcane theory.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]]**: Corresponded on matters of planar rifts and ancient curses.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: His arcane treatises on necromancy and bone preservation directly guided [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s studies.
- **High King Emeric**: Served historically as High Arcane Advisor in Wayrest.

## Location
- Historically based in [[Info/Lore/High_Rock\|High_Rock]]; manuscripts archived in [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Winterhold\|Winterhold]].
