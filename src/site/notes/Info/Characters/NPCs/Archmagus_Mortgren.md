# Archmagus Mortgren

## Description
The Court Mage of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil Keep]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], serving [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]. An imposing, highly knowledgeable, and calculating sorcerer with profound mastery over esoteric magic, ancient artifacts, and necromantic arts.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]**: Serves as her trusted royal advisor and chief magical authority in the Rift.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Mentor in necromantic arts. Identified Periwyn's latent magical gift, formally agreed to train her in necromancy in secret, and provided her with instructions, rituals, and magical tomes.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]**: Associated through older historical events and recognized in connection with planar mysteries.

## Location
- Royal Quarters and Laboratory in **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil Keep]]**, **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**.

## Notes
- **Vampire Threat Analysis ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]):** Examined the severed head of the vampire brought from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]], identifying it as an ancient rogue member of [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]] and analyzing the ominous blood-scrying orb.
- **Mentorship of Periwyn ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 012\|Session 012]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 012a\|Session 012a]]):** Established a formal secret training agreement with Periwyn after evaluating her potential. Provided her with necromantic research texts and instructions on ritual preparation (including the treatment of bones and channeling necrotic energy).
- **Subsequent Applications ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]]):** Periwyn utilized Mortgren's precise necromantic techniques during her field investigations, including detecting lingering death pulses and boiling ritual fox bones.
