# Astrid

> *"A steady hand on the bow and a keen eye for who walks through the inn door at sundown."*

## Description
The capable, sharp-eyed daughter of innkeeper [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. Equally skilled in maintaining tavern accounts and stalking deer through the southern birch forests, Astrid is a reliable huntress and quiet confidante to the party.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]]**: Her father, with whom she manages the tavern's logistics and lodging.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Bonded during a hunting expedition in the woods north of Riften, sharing tracking tips.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Appreciated Durov's respectful demeanor and shared trail provisions.

## Location
- [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].
