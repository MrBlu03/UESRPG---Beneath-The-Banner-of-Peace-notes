# Astrid

## Description
The innkeeper's daughter at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderers_Rest]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. She is a skilled hunter who keeps secret records of interesting tavern visitors.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]]**: Her father, who raised her after her mother died in a war.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Tracked and hunted a stag with her during a camping trip.

## Location
- [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderers_Rest]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]**: Prepared a hearty breakfast for the party before their departure.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: Joined the party's camping trip where she demonstrated her hunting skills and revealed her secret records, including an entry about a man who spoke of dragons returning 14 years ago.
