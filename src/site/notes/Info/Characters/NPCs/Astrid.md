# Astrid

## Description
The cheerful, spirited daughter of the innkeeper at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderers_Rest]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. An adventurous soul who enjoys archery and hunting outside the city walls.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Hunting partner who ventured into the woods with her.
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]] Innkeeper**: Her father, whom she assists in managing the tavern.

## Location
- Works at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderers_Rest]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]**: Served breakfast to the party and shared morning tavern rumors before their march to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]].
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: Joined the party on their decompression camping trip in the Rift forests, successfully hunting a stag alongside [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and harvesting valuable antlers.
