# Astrid

## Description
A capable Nord huntress and the daughter of innkeeper [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. She is skilled in wilderness tracking and archery, possessing combat and survival experience from past wartime service.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]]**: Her father, who runs Wanderer's Rest and is fiercely protective of her.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Established a friendly rapport during camp hunts; later recruited as Onasha's trusted informant in Riften.

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]]** in **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**.

## Notes
- **Morning at the Inn ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]):** Prepared breakfast for the adventuring party prior to their departure toward Greenwall.
- **Hunting Expedition ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]):** Joined the party, her father [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]], and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] on a stag hunt north of Riften. Demonstrated sharp hunting and tracking skills, sharing stories of her wartime background and keeping tabs on travelers visiting the inn.
- **Intelligence Contact ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]):** Serves as [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s primary contact and informant in Riften as part of Onasha's growing regional network.
