# Senya

## Description
A talented Nord skald (warrior-poet) and skald performer who travels across Skyrim alongside her twin sister [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonya/Sonja]]. She sings traditional sagas and plays the lute with great artistic skill.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonya/Sonja]]**: Her twin sister and musical partner.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Agni\|Agni]]**: Fellow skald who performed with them in Falderstad.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Bonded over lute playing and music at The Soggy Beaver tavern.

## Location
- Taverns across **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]** and **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]**.

## Notes
- **Saga of Clan Cold-Moor ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]]):** Performed at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Sleeping_Stag\|The Sleeping Stag]] in Riften, recounting the ancient tragedy and murder of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]].
- **Falderstad Execution & Festival ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]):** Arrived in Falderstad for the traditional execution ritual, performing *"Bonden og Kragen"* and *"Suurin"* with Agni, and inviting [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] to share the stage with her lute.
