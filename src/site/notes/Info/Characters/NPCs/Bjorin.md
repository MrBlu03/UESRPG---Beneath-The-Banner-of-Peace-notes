# Bjorin

## Description
The hearty innkeeper of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] and an experienced hunter. He is protective of his daughter [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] and skilled in preparing trail provisions and organizing northern hunting treks.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]]**: His daughter, whom he fiercely loves and watches over.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Frequently hosts the party at his inn, supplying food and lodgings.

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]]** in **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**.

## Notes
- **Host & Provisioner ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]):** Provided meals and oversaw logistics and trail rations for travelers and Companions departing from Riften.
- **Hunting Expedition Leader ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]):** Proposed and guided a stag-hunting expedition to a clearing a few hours north of Riften, sharing camp craft and hunting expertise with the party.
