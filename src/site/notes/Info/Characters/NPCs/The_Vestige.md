# The Vestige (Sadrith Indoril / Crow Father)

## Description
The Vestige is an extraordinary, legendary, and multifaceted figure operating across Tamriel. Connected to planar entities and the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather_Court]] as the Crow Father, he also moves among mortals under the identity of the noble Dunmer **Sadrith Indoril**. Wielding immense magical prowess and ancient authority, he works from the shadows to dismantle Daedric plots threatening the mortal plane.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]**: Trusted operative ally who coordinates shadow strikes and intelligence gathering.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Gifted her an enchanted black feather of protection and saw her off upon her departure.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Fought shoulder-to-shoulder in the sewers beneath [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] against a rampaging Daedroth.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Assisted [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] with arcane insights into planar rifts and dragon scrolls.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Coordinated strategic moves to expose the cult in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron_Jangleplume]]**: High-ranking noble in his corvid court.
