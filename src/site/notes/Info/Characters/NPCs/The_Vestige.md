# The Vestige (Sadrith Indoril / Crow Father)

## Description
The Vestige is an extraordinary, legendary figure operating across Tamriel. Connected to planar entities and the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] as the Crow Father, he also moves among mortals under the identity of the Dunmer Sadrith Indoril. Wielding immense magical prowess and ancient authority, he works from the shadows to dismantle Daedric plots threatening the mortal plane.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]**: Trusted Argonian operative and assassin ally who coordinates shadow strikes and intelligence gathering.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Gifted her an enchanted black feather of protection and saw her off upon her departure.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Coordinates strategic moves and shares a familiarity from her visions, calling her "little flower."
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Fought shoulder-to-shoulder in the sewers beneath [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] against a rampaging Daedroth.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Assisted her with arcane insights into planar rifts and Daedric memory anchors.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron_Jangleplume]]**: High-ranking noble in his corvid court.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]]**: Maintains contact with the ancient Breton mage regarding supernatural threats.

## Location
- Frequently found at the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Barrow_of_the_Oath-Breaker_King\|Barrow_of_the_Oath-Breaker_King]] or roaming [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] and planar borders.
