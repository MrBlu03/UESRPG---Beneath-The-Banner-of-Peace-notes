# Olvfrost

## Description
A wealthy, genial, and influential Nord lumber baron whose vast logging operations are based near the Fort Morven Scott region in Eastmarch.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Reconnected with Periwyn under her Lunclaire merchant lineage, establishing lucrative trade agreements.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jorven_Halliksson\|Jorven Halliksson]]**: Longtime business associate within the merchant networks of the Guild of the Silverroad.

## Location
- Timber estates near **Fort Morven Scott** and the Grand Market in **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]**.

## Notes
- **Lumber Trade Agreement ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]):** Attended the prestigious trade gala in Kjalgrad's Jarl's Hall. Based on his mutual respect for Guildmaster Halliksson and the Lunclaire merchant legacy, he signed a highly profitable commercial contract granting [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] exclusive rights over a lumber trade route running from his mills to [[Whiterun\|Whiterun]].
