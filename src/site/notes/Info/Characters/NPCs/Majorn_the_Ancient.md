# Majorn the Ancient

## Description
An ancient, powerful master vampire originally hailing from Lord Harkon's court in [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]]. Growing disdainful of the court's isolationism, he broke away to establish his own aggressive coven across eastern Skyrim. Believed to have been slain twenty years ago during the Planemeld, he has covertly resurfaced.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]]**: Warns that Majorn's name commands terrifying dread in occult circles and should not be uttered lightly.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]**: Historical adversary who previously combated his brood in Eastmarch.
- **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent Syndicate]]**: Allied in occult experiments and regional destabilization.

## Location
- Hidden covens across **[[Info/Lore/Eastmarch\|Eastmarch]]** and the mountain borders of the Rift.

## Notes
- **Vampire Incursion Origins ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 012\|Session 012]]):** Archmagus Mortgren revealed that the vampire commander at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] was directly sired by Majorn the Ancient.
- **Greenwall as a Field Test ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]]):** A captured vampire survivor in Falderstad confessed that the horrific slaughter at Greenwall was merely an experimental test conducted by Majorn's coven to evaluate mortal military responses prior to broader operations.
