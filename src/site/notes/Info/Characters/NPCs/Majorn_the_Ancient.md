# Majorn the Ancient

## Description
A terrifying, ancient vampire patriarch of [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]] blood who rejected Harkon's isolationism. Believed to have been killed twenty years ago during the Planemeld, his name still carries immense dark weight among scholars and hunters as he orchestrates new blood plagues across the mountains.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]]**: Studied his lineage and warned the party of his return.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Investigating the resurging threat of his coven.

## Location
- Operating secretly, with his coven's influence spreading through [[Info/Lore/The_Reach\|The Reach]] and [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 012\|Session 012]]**: Identified by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]] as the true sire of the vampire head recovered by the party. His survival from the Planemeld twenty years ago was revealed, signaling a dangerous escalation in regional vampire activity connected to the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] conspiracy.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]]**: Bragged about by a surviving vampire in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], who claimed to serve Majorn's Coven. The vampire revealed that the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] incident was merely a "field experiment" ordered by Majorn to test mortal strength.
