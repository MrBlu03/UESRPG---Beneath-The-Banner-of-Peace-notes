# Majorn the Ancient

## Description
A terrifying, ancient vampire patriarch whose name is recorded in ancient Breton and Nordic chronicles detailing blood plagues across the western mountains.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Sworn enemy of his undead lineage.

## Location
- Lurks in deep subterranean fastnesses in [[Info/Lore/The_Reach\|The Reach]].
