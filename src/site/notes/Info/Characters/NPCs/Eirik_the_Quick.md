# Eirik the Quick

## Description
A famously agile, light-footed Nord courier, acrobat, and rogue in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. Renowned for his uncanny balance, rapid reflexes, and constant energetic movement.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Subject of a recurring, humorous rivalry wherein [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] repeatedly attempts (and hilariously fails) to trip him.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]]**: Tavern companion at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]].

## Location
- Usually found darting between the market stalls or resting at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderers_Rest]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]**: Introduced as a swift courier carrying messages between the Queen's steward and local wardens.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]**: Danced and bantered with tavern patrons at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]].
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] attempted to trip him during a tavern evening, failing spectacularly as Eirik effortlessly hopped over her foot.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] failed to trip him twice more during dockside banter.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 012\|Session 012]]-**[[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]**: Continued to provide swift courier services and lighthearted camaraderie for the party in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].
