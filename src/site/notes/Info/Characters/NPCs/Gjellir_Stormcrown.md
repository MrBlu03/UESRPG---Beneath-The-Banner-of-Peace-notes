# Gjellir Stormcrown

## Description
Gjellir Stormcrown is the towering Harbinger of the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult_of_Ysmir]] stationed at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall_of_Ysmir]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. A seasoned leader and repository of lore, he is knowledgeable about dragon traditions, the ancient Nord heritage, and military strategy. He actively collaborates with the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] and assisted the party in their efforts against [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan_Volkihar]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Wuunferth\|Wuunferth]]**: Identified him after finding him tortured at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]].
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Traveled with Durov to Greenwall, sharing tales of past battles and noting the resemblance of Durov's shield crest to old Bruma noble lines.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen the Mer-Killer]]** and **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild the Unwavering]]**: Members of the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult_of_Ysmir]] who flanked and traveled with him.

## Location
- Officiates at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall_of_Ysmir]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].
- Led a convoy to the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] war camp.
