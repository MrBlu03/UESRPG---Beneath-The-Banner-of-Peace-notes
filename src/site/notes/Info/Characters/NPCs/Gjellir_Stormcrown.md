# Gjellir Stormcrown

> *"By the Dragon of the North and the breath of the mountain! Steel answers blood, and Ysmir watches over the brave!"*

## Description
A towering, silver-bearded Nordic titan and high priest who presides as the revered Harbinger of the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult_of_Ysmir]] at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Ysmir's Hall]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. Imbued with fierce warrior-zeal, booming charisma, and ancient Nordic theology, Gjellir commands immense respect from both hold nobility and common soldiers. A staunch defender of Skyrim against Daedric corruption, he wields a massive waraxe with divine ferocity.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]]**: Trusted martial and spiritual advisor to the crown of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod_Wraithbane]]**: Respected comrade-in-arms with the Harbinger of the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]], coordinating joint military campaigns against supernatural threats.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Welcomed her warmly into the mead hall, discussed ancient vampire legends, and blessed her crusader hammer.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**: Relies on Priestess Adda to tend the wounded and maintain the hearth-fires while he directs temple warriors.

## Location
- Presides over the great hearth in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall_of_Ysmir]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].
