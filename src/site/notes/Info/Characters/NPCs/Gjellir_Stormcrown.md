# Gjellir Stormcrown

## Description
The towering, heavily armored Harbinger and martial chieftain of the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. A formidable Nord veteran of immense physical stature—standing even taller than [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]—he commands deep respect through his stoic leadership, combat mastery, and devotion to Ysmir.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]]**: Supreme martial leader and Harbinger based at the Hall of Ysmir in Riften.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**: Entrusts the Hall of Ysmir to Shaman Adda during his military deployments.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild_the_Unwavering]] & [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen_the_Mer-Killer]]**: Veteran warriors who marched under his command at Greenwall.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod Wraithbane]]**: Allies with the Companions of Fallowstone Hall against regional supernatural threats.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Shared command and tactics on the road to Greenwall, noting that Durov's shield bore similarities to ancient Colovian noble lines of Bruma.

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall of Ysmir]]** in **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**; campaigned at **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]**.

## Notes
- **Greenwall Crisis Briefing ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]):** Warned the party of the unnatural freezing surrounding [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]], describing the disappearance of scouts phasing through ice walls, and offered the Cult's mead hall for training and supplies.
- **The March to Greenwall ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]):** Organized and led the joint expedition to relieve Greenwall, marching at a disciplined military pace. Helped [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] cremate 17 roadside execution victims and shared ancient sagas of Nord warriors fighting into Sovngarde.
- **Identification of Wuunferth ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]):** Following the liberation of Greenwall, identified the tortured victim inside the fortress as [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Wuunferth\|Wuunferth the Hunter]].
