# Gjellir Stormcrown

## Description
A venerable, booming-voiced priest and lorekeeper of the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult_of_Ysmir]] at the temple in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. Deeply versed in the ancient Thu'um, dragon lore, and the heroic traditions of the First Empire.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Wuunferth\|Wuunferth]]**: Fellow temple scholar and hunter.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Discussed Colovian heraldry and Nordic dragon symbolism.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen_the_Mer-Killer]]**: Accompanied [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen]] on temple security tours.

## Location
- Officiates at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall_of_Ysmir]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].
