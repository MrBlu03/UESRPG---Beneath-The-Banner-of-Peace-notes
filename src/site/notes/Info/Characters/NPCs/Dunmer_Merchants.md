# Dunmer Merchants

> *"Skyrim's laws end where Morrowind's coin begins, s'wit. We sell to those who can pay."*

## Description
A syndicate of shrewd, arrogant Dunmer merchants and smugglers operating under the legitimate commercial aegis of [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Hlaalu\|House_Hlaalu]], while clandestinely trafficking contraband for the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna_Tong]]. Specializing in moon sugar, poisoned blades, and unregistered dwemer scrap, they maintain distribution channels running from Mournhold through the Rift into the western holds.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Harvold\|Captain_Harvold]]**: Held them in custody at the Riften barracks, repeatedly threatening them with the execution block.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Interrogated them with blunt Imperial intimidation, extracting shipping routes and guild passwords.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Garen_Hlaalu\|Garen_Hlaalu]]**: Their master factor in Morrowind who coordinated shipments across the Velothi Mountains.

## Location
- The canal warehouses and docks of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], with cells in the town guardhouse.
