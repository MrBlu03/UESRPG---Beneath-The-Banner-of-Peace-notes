# Dunmer Merchants

## Description
A suspicious armed caravan of Dunmer smugglers operating under the guise of legitimate traders flying [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Hlaalu\|House Hlaalu]] colors. The crew consisted of six Dunmer guards and a Nord wagon driver.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent Conspiracy]]**: Smugglers linked to covert syndicate logistics in eastern Skyrim.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga Cairn-Shield]] & [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Intercepted, ambushed, and arrested by Helga and Durov's squad.

## Location
- Roads and forests surrounding **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]**; later detained in the Falderstad guard dungeons.

## Notes
- **Caravan Ambush & Forgery ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]]):** Intercepted near Falderstad carrying forged transit papers lacking official wax seals (*"Do they not have wax where you're from?"* - Durov). When an interrogated guard shouted an alarm in Dunmeri, a fight ensued. The smugglers were subdued, disarmed, and thrown into the dungeons to await judgment by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga Cairn-Shield]].
