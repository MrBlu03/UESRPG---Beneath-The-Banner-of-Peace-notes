# Father Aldric

## Description
A Breton priest of Stendarr in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] who primarily specializes in restoration magic, but also dabbles in alteration and destruction. He is an enthusiastic heavy drinker who is often found with a jug of mead.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Agreed to help her dispel a ward at a cult facility for essentially nothing but a bottle of mead.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jean-Pierre_LaRouche\|Jean-Pierre_LaRouche]]**: Spiritual counselor and friend.

## Location
- Tends the chapel and hospice of Stendarr in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
