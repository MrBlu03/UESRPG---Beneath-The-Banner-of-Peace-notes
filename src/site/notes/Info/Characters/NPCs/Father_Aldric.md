# Father Aldric

## Overview
A Breton priest of Stendarr and restoration mage residing in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. While his primary devotion lies in healing and restoration magic, he possesses practical knowledge of alteration and ward theory. Encountered by Periwyn in the restoration wing of the Grand Library.

## History & Notable Events
- **Recruitment by Periwyn ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 030\|Session 030]]):** Recruited at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost_Gate_Hall]] by [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], who pledged her family name and honor to guarantee payment for assistance with dispelling Daedric wards at the cult facility.
- **Catastrophic Backlash ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 030\|Session 030]]):** Attempted to break the purple Daedric ward at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan_Mesa]] with a channeled beam of restoration light. The ward backlashed violently up his staff, flinging him off the 4-meter walkway. He was caught below by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]].
- **Comatose Convalescence ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]):** Transported to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]'s Dwemer ruin infirmary. The Vestige diagnosed him as trapped in a vegetative mental void. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] tucked a signed letter of apology bearing her family crest and the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Knights_of_Arkay\|Knights_of_Arkay]] emblem inside his robes, while the Vestige summoned experienced healer mages via messenger crow to repair his shattered psyche.
