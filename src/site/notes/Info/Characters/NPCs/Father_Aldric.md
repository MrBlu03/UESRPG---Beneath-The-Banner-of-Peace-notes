# Father Aldric

## Description
A gentle, elderly priest of Stendarr in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] who tends to the poor, heals the sick, and provides spiritual sanctuary to those persecuted by the city's corrupt authorities.

- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jean-Pierre_LaRouche\|Jean-Pierre_LaRouche]]**: Spiritual counselor and friend.
