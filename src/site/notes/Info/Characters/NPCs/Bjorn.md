# Bjorn

## Description
A Nord blacksmith in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] who co-manages and operates the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Sweaty_Steel\|Sweaty Steel]] forge alongside his sister [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svana\|Svana]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svana\|Svana]]**: His sister and blacksmithing partner at Sweaty Steel.

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Sweaty_Steel\|Sweaty Steel]]** smithy in **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**.

## Notes
- **Smithy Operations ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]):** Co-runs the forge where adventurers purchase equipment and trade recovered weapons. Assisted when the party visited the shop to trade a bandit's greatsword and inquire about missing alchemist [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]].
