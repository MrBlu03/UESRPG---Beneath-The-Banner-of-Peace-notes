# Xula

> *"The girl left behind in the mud and the chains—the quiet reason why Onasha strives to be better."*

## Description
A young Argonian girl whom [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] was forced to leave behind in Morrowind when escaping Dunmer slavery seven years ago. At the time of Onasha's flight, Xula was only nine years old; in the present day, she would be around sixteen. Although physically distant and yet to appear in person, Xula exists as Onasha's most profound emotional anchor, personal vulnerability, and moral compass.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Her former fellow captive and sister-figure. Onasha is deeply haunted by guilt and the lingering fear that Xula may never forgive her for escaping alone, yet vows to become someone worthy of returning for her.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Confided in by Onasha during a late-night vigil in Kjalgrad, where Periwyn reassured Onasha that someone as caring as her could never be forgotten.

## Location
- Originates from the slave plantations of southern [[Info/Lore/Morrowind\|Morrowind]] / northern [[Info/Lore/Black_Marsh\|Black_Marsh]]; current exact whereabouts unknown.

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 029\|Session 029]]**: During a private conversation by the hearth with [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] while awaiting the waking of the captured Daedra in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] revealed Xula's name for the first time. She recounted the agonizing memory of escaping slavery when Xula was only nine years old, voicing her deep dread that Xula hated her for leaving. Periwyn gently pushed back, consoling Onasha and affirming that love and memory endure beyond captivity.
