# Xula

## Description
A young Argonian girl whom [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] left behind when Onasha made her desperate escape from House Dres slavery seven years ago. She was 9 years old at the time of Onasha's flight and is now approximately 16.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Regarded as a bond-sister. Onasha is deeply haunted by the fear that Xula has not forgiven her for fleeing alone, driving Onasha's vow to return and rescue her.

## Location
- House Dres territory in **[[Info/Lore/Morrowind\|Morrowind]]**.

## Notes
- **Onasha's Confession ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 029\|Session 029]]):** [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] revealed Xula's name for the first time during an intimate, emotional conversation with [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], sharing her grief and trauma regarding those she left behind in slavery.
