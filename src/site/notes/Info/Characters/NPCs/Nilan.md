# Nilan

## Description
The master tracker and chief huntsman of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. Ventured into the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]] to pursue the mythical white stag, where he was ambushed, slain, and skinned by the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Skinstealer]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]]**: Friend and tavern companion.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]**: Fellow master hunter in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Nohrak]]**: Slain by the monster in the deep woods.

## Location
- Formerly lived in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Nilans_House\|Nilans_House]] in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]; remains found in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]]**: His disappearance prompted [[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]] to investigate his cabin, finding spoiled stew and hunting maps.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]]**: The party discovered his torn clothing and grisly remains in the forest, confirming the lethal threat of the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]].
