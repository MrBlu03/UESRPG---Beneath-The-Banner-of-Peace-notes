# Jean-Pierre LaRouche

## Description
His Illustrious Magnificence Jean-Pierre LaRoche, Marquis of the Sapphire Coast, Baron of the Nine Rooms, Keeper of the Royal Cocoon Seals, and Patriarch of House LaRoche. An eccentric, pompous, but harmless Breton silk merchant and noble who became embroiled in the dark conspiracies of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lady_Aine\|Lady_Aine]]**: His aristocratic wife who fiercely lobbied for his rescue.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Revived him with smelling salts after his abduction.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Provided him safe passage instructions to escape the Daedric complex.

## Location
- Lodging in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], running a temporary silk market stall.

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]]**: Reported missing by his wife [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lady_Aine\|Lady_Aine]] after disappearing shortly following the Jarl's banquet. He left his market stall frozen and unattended, marking him as a target of the city's widespread abductions.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]**: Discovered unconscious and bound as a sacrificial captive in the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]]'s ritual chamber beneath Alton Mesa. After being freed by the party, he was revived by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and tearfully began reciting his full titles before being calmed down and given an escape map by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]].
