# Jean-Pierre LaRouche

## Description
An eccentric, flamboyant, and aristocratic Breton clothier and high-end merchant residing in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Known by his grand, self-proclaimed titles—*"Marquis of the Sapphire Coast, Baron of the Nine Looms"*—he is known for extravagant dress and dramatic sensibilities.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/House_LaRouche\|House_LaRouche]]**: Scion and representative of House LaRouche's high-fashion merchant interests.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Rescued him from sacrificial execution inside the Dwemer cult facility.

## Location
- Fashion salon and boutique in **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]**; held captive in the **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Main Facility Ruin]]**.

## Notes
- **Kidnapping by the Cult ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 027\|Session 027]]):** Abducted in Kjalgrad by agents of the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]] alongside other citizens to serve as blood sacrifices sustaining the 37,000 counterfeit Septims flooding the regional market.
- **Ritual Rescue ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]):** Found bound and unconscious beside the glowing sacrificial altar in the Dwemer ruins under guard of a massive Daedric Xivilai. Following the beast's execution, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] revived him with smelling salts. Upon waking, he tearfully recited his noble titles before being calmed, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] gave him a map to navigate back to Kjalgrad.
