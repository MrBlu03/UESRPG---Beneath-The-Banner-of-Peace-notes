# Jean-Pierre LaRouche

> *"His Illustrious Magnificence Jean-Pierre LaRoche, Marquis of the Sapphire Coast, Baron of the Nine Looms, Keeper of the Royal Cocoon Seals, and Patriarch of House LaRoche."*

## Full Title & Aristocratic Honors
- **Formal Styling**: **His Illustrious Magnificence Jean-Pierre LaRouche, Marquis of the Sapphire Coast, Baron of the Nine Looms, Keeper of the Royal Cocoon Seals, and Patriarch of House LaRoche**

## Description
An eccentric, flamboyant, and pompous yet ultimately harmless Breton nobleman and master silk merchant. Instantly recognizable by his aristocratic bearing, high-society attire, and impeccably styled, distinctive curling moustache. Despite his bombastic recitations of his titles, he is deeply devoted to his craft and his family. He arrived in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] to exhibit luxurious textiles, only to become one of the most prominent victims of the city's sinister conspiracy.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lady_Aine\|Lady_Aine]]**: His devoted, high-class aristocratic wife, who wielded her cane and status to aggressively demand a search party when he went missing.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Gently brought him back to reality with smelling salts in the ritual cavern and endured his pompous title recitation with dry tolerance.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Handed him a marked escape map, gave him tactical survival advice for avoiding city unrest, and safely ushered him out of the cult complex.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Father_Aldric\|Father_Aldric]]**: Fellow Breton compatriot and spiritual confidant residing in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

## Location
- Lodges in the upper quarters of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], operating an elite silk pavilion in the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Grand_Market_Kjalgrad\|Grand Market]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]]**: Reported missing by his frantic wife, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lady_Aine\|Lady_Aine]], who lodged an official complaint with [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] during the town square protest. Jean-Pierre had attended the Jarl's banquet at the longhouse a week prior, appeared strangely absent-minded afterward, and subsequently vanished. His outdoor market stall was left abandoned and covered in frost, with bolts of fine, frozen silk untouched—a sure sign of foul play. Guild ledgers later revealed he had been singled out as "selection cargo."
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 027\|Session 027]]**: Remains listed among the highest-priority missing citizens alongside multiple merchants linked to the suspicious Altan Mesa activities.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]**: Found bound and unconscious around the sacrificial circle in the central ritual chamber of the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]] beneath [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan Mesa]]. He was slated to be sacrificed to fuel the Daedric vanishing ritual maintaining 37,000 counterfeit Septims. Following the decapitation of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|The Xivilai]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] revived him with smelling salts. Upon regaining consciousness, he tearfully and reflexively launched into reciting his full titles before being calmed down. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] provided him with an escape map back to [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] and warned him of the riots in the streets.
