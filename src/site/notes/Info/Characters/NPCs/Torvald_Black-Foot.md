# Torvald Black-Foot

## Description
The wealthy, calculating Guildmaster of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Black-Foot Merchant Guild]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. A highly astute businessman who manages merchant caravans across Skyrim and fiercely protects his guild's interests from criminal syndicates and rival factions.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hedrek_Black-Foot\|Hedrek_Black-Foot]]**: Guild kin.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Hired his mercenary company to violently resolve guild problems.

## Location
- Conducts business from the guild offices and docks in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 001\|Session 001]]**: Introduced to the party on the streets of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], possessing a valuable unappraised gemstone the party sought to examine.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]**: Referenced by his brother [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hjar\|Hjar]], who asked the party to pass on a message that he still owed Torvald a bottle of mead.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]**: Hired the party to investigate Dunmer [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] criminals harassing his Argonian dock workers and breaking into his warehouses. After the party violently recovered a stolen lockbox from a hijacked merchant ship, Torvald promised to investigate its dangerous connections to [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Dres\|House Dres]] shipwrecks in the Sea of Ghosts.
