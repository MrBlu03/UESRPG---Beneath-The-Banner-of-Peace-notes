# Torvald Black-Foot

## Description
The wealthy, calculating Guildmaster of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. Highly astute in commerce, he manages merchant caravans across southern and eastern Skyrim.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]**: Hired the party for gem appraisals and recovering stolen lockboxes.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hedrek_Black-Foot\|Hedrek_Black-Foot]]**: Guild kin in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

## Location
- Conducts business from the guild offices in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 001\|Session 001]]**: Introduced with a valuable unappraised gemstone the party sought to examine.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]**: Coordinated caravan supplies following the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] campaign.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]**: Received the recovered lockbox from the party following the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] harbor raid, discussing trade connections to the Sea of Ghosts.
