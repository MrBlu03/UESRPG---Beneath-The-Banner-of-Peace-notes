# Torvald Black-Foot

## Description
A respected Nord clan elder and patriarch of Clan Black-Foot in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], who leads the historical Black-Foot trading interests across the Rift.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]**: Attends royal court proceedings at Mistveil Keep.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Consulted Durov regarding clan alliances and regional developments.

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]** (Mistveil Keep and Hall of Ysmir).

## Notes
- **Royal Audience ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 001\|Session 001]]):** Present at Mistveil Keep during early audiences with Queen Freidr.
- **Hall of Ysmir Meeting ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]):** Entered dripping wet from the rain to consult with [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] at the Hall of Ysmir, discussing clan trade and municipal tensions.
