# Hadring the Unlucky

## Description
An ancient, historical Jarl of the Rift who was betrayed during a winter war.

## Relationships
- **Unnamed Widow**: His widow mourned him by wandering the southern river banks until her death, and the river was named after her by local clans.
- **Three Stone Brothers**: Warriors who were once sworn to Hadring's banner.

## Location
- Historical figure of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The Rift]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]]**: Discovered by the party in ancient lays and texts. The texts revealed he was betrayed during a winter war and noted that his sworn warriors, the Three Stone Brothers, made their final stand beside a ridge south of the river valleys.
