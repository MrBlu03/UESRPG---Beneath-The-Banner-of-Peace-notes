# Gaius

## Description
A grizzled, reclusive Colovian veteran of the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Imperial_Legion\|Imperial Legion]] living in self-imposed exile outside [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. A man of stern military honor who carries memories of the empire's fractured glory.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Former legion comrade; tested [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s spirit before returning his ancestral identification tag.

## Location
- Lives in a fortified homestead on the outskirts of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014a]]**: Tracked down by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]. Gaius slipped Nirnroot into [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s drink to test his alertness and resolve in combat.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]]**: Satisfied by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s unwavering discipline, Gaius handed over an [[Info/Organizations/Military/Imperial_Legion\|Imperial Legion]] tag marked with a zero, unlocking crucial memories of [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s forgotten military past.
