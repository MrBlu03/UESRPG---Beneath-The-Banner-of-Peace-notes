# Gaius

## Description
A grizzled, reclusive Colovian veteran of the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Imperial_Legion\|Imperial Legion]] from Chorrol, living in self-imposed exile outside [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. A man of stern military honor burdened by guilt over his service, carrying memories of the empire's fractured glory and debts that cannot be paid.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Former legion comrade; recognized the crest on his armor and tested his resolve.

## Location
- Lives in a near-abandoned fortified homestead on the snowy outskirts of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]]**: Tracked down by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] to his cottage outside [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. He recognized the Bruma garrison crest on [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s armor, confessed his profound guilt over the war, and slipped Nirnroot into [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s mead "to give him silence." He vanished into the snow before [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] awoke, leaving behind a note and a melted twin-bird pendant.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]]**: It was revealed that he had left a special [[Info/Organizations/Military/Imperial_Legion\|Imperial Legion]] tag marked with a zero with the tavern owner [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]] to be given to [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], unlocking crucial memories of his forgotten military past.
