# Gaius

## Description
A Colovian Imperial and retired veteran of the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Imperial_Legion\|Imperial Legion]], living in quiet retirement in a weathered, near-abandoned residence on the outskirts of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].

- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Consulted by Durov regarding Colovian heritage and Imperial Legion history in eastern Skyrim.
