# Jarl Gorm

## Description
The hearty, generous, and traditional Nord ruler of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], holding court from his grand longhouse. A veteran warrior who values martial prowess, honor, and loyalty to his people.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga Cairn-Shield]]**: His trusted Huscarl, bodyguard, and military commander.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Holds Durov in high esteem, granting him town honors and engaging in a spirited drinking contest.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Hailed the party as heroes of Falderstad, rewarding them lavishly.

## Location
- Jarl's Longhouse in **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]**.

## Notes
- **Recovery & Bounty Reward ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]]):** Having fully recovered from rock joint, Jarl Gorm personally greeted the returning hunters after the slaying of the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]]. He generously awarded 500 gold each (2,000 gold total) from his private treasury and granted the party perpetual free use of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Nilans_House\|Nilan's house]].
- **Stalhrim Execution & Victory Feast ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]):** Presided over the public execution of treacherous elements using a great claymore forged of **Stalhrim**. Hosted a lavish celebration feast in his longhouse featuring roasted stag and Boar's Blood Mead, participating in a heavy drinking contest with [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]].
