# Stryder

## Description
A skilled, solitary wilderness ranger and tracker who patrols the frontier highways between the Rift and [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]**: Contracted the party for scouting and favor exchanges.

## Location
- Ranges across the frontier between [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The Rift]] and [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014a]]-**[[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]]**: Assisted the party with woodland navigation during the hunt for the white stag.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]]**: Supported the party during wilderness extraction following the battle at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Cursed_Grove\|Cursed Grove]].
