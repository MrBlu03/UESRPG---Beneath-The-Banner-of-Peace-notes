# Stryder

## Description
A sharp-witted, cryptic underworld operative and fixer who operates out of abandoned warehouses and tanneries in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] and [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. He speaks concisely, deals in valuable favors, and maintains extensive connections to regional trade networks and shadowy figures.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Called in a personal debt/favor to intercept a smuggling operation.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hooded_Figure\|Hooded Figure]]**: Associated with the enigmatic cloaked operative during clandestine meetings.

## Location
- Abandoned warehouses in **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]** and **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]**.

## Notes
- **Calling in the Favor ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]]):** Pinned a note to the doorframe summoning [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] to an abandoned guildhouse, hiring him as muscle to ambush an illegal Dunmer smuggling caravan carrying forged documents.
- **Cryptic Greetings ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]]):** Materialized after the battle against the Ragged Lord to offer cryptic congratulations before slipping away toward the old tannery.
- **Disorientation & Relocation ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 019\|Session 019]]):** After a heavy drinking contest, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] woke up outside Stryder's warehouse with no memory of his transit as Stryder's network shifted operations toward Kjalgrad.
