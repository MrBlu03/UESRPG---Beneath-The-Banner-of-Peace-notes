# Stryder

## Description
A skilled, solitary wilderness ranger, tracker, and criminal connected to smuggling rings who patrols the frontier highways between the Rift and [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]**: Contracted the party for scouting and favor exchanges.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hooded_Figure\|Hooded_Figure]]**: Mysterious companion with court connections.

## Location
- Ranges across the frontier between [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The Rift]] and [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].
