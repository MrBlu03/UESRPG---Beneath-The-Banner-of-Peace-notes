# Stryder (Tarja)

> *"They own the towers and the ledgers. We own the stones beneath their boots."*

## Description
A battle-hardened, sharp-eyed resistance coordinator and operative who leads the underground network of the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|Lower_Lodge]] beneath [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Known on the surface by various aliases including Tarja and Stryder, she is a master of urban sabotage, sewer navigation, and guerrilla strikes. She serves as the party's primary underground military ally in dismantling the corrupt monopolies of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Respects Durov's Imperial discipline, coordinating street-level protests and sabotage actions with his investigations.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Partnered with Onasha to execute firebombings of Silverroad warehouses and mobilized an armed strike team to rescue her from the longhouse dungeons.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]**: Coordinates intelligence and safehouse logistics with the Crow Father's clandestine operatives.

## Location
- The labyrinthine conduits of the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Sewers\|Kjalgrad Sewers]] and the underground command hub of the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|Lower_Lodge]].
