# Jorven Halliksson

## Description
The influential Guildmaster of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Alongside Trade Advocate [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]], he exercises immense commercial, administrative, and political power over Eastmarch's trade networks.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]]**: Co-leader and high administrator within the Guild of the Silverroad.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius Caro]]**: Coordinates municipal governance and commercial privileges with the Jarl's steward.

## Location
- Guild Headquarters and Grand Market in **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]**.

## Notes
- **Guild Leadership ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 021\|Session 021]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]):** Directs the expansive operations of the Guild of the Silverroad, which bankrolls the city guard, dictates trade tariffs, and effectively manages civic administration in Kjalgrad over the figurehead Jarl.
