# Jorven Halliksson

## Description
The shrewd, ambitious Guildmaster of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Driven by commercial dominance, he leveraged debt contracts, Morrowind connections, and aggressive diplomacy to monopolize eastern trade corridors.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin_Marr]]**: Chief trade advocate and diplomat.
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Morvan\|Jarl of Kjalgrad]]**: Pressured the Jarl for exclusive commercial monopolies.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hedrek_Black-Foot\|Hedrek_Black-Foot]]**: Bitter rival whose guild he ousted from [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

## Location
