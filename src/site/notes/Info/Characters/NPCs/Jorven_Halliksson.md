# Jorven Halliksson

## Description
The shrewd and composed Guildmaster of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. He commands vast wealth and uses his power to maintain the guild's mercantile monopolies.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin_Marr]]**: His Trade Advocate who handles the aggressive and secretive sides of the guild's operations.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Personally invited her to a trade event.

## Location
- The [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Silverroad_Guildhouse\|Silverroad_Guildhouse]] and the Jarl's Hall in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020a]]**: Appeared alongside [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin_Marr]] during negotiations at the grand guildhouse, displaying the composure of a man accustomed to favorable outcomes.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 021\|Session 021]]**: Confirmed as the ultimate authority of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]], although his exact ties to the Cult of Clavicus Vile remain unclear.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]**: Personally invited [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] to a trade gathering at the Jarl's Hall, where he delivered an opening speech on trade, trust, and prosperity.
