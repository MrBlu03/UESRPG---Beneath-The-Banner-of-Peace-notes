# Hedrek Black-Foot

## Description
A resilient, clandestine operative for the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Working in secret following the guild's exile, he gathers evidence of [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Silverroad]] corruption.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torvald_Black-Foot\|Torvald_Black-Foot]]**: Respected guildmaster in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Shared covert intelligence on the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Silverroad]] monopoly.

## Location
- Operates out of hiding near the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Old_Blackfoot_Guild_House\|Old_Blackfoot_Guild_House]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
