# Vidir (Víðir)

## Description
An ancient, stone-faced Nord warrior and mystic companion of Shaman [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall of Ysmir]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. He carries an aura described as "older and colder" than the restless draugr spirits.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**: Close mystic companion and martial protector.

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall of Ysmir]]** in **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]** and the **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Haunted_Hunting_Lodge\|Haunted Hunting Lodge]]**.

## Notes
- **Cleansing the Barrow ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 009\|Session 009]]):** Accompanied Adda and Kirya to the Haunted Hunting Lodge to maintain spiritual balance. Darted into combat with spinning dual axes, effortlessly cutting down rising draugr and helping place [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] to eternal rest.
