# Víðir

## Description
An ancient companion of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] and a member of the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult_of_Ysmir]]. He is described as an older and colder presence, a formidable warrior who wields dual spinning axes. He was specifically brought along to act as a balancing force against [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven_Cold-Moor]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**: His companion in the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult_of_Ysmir]], who trusts him with securing ancient sites.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven_Cold-Moor]]**: Víðir was brought along as a balance against him.

## Location
- Resides at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ysmirs_Hall\|Ysmirs_Hall]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 009\|Session 009]]**: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] summoned Víðir to join the party on a rescue mission to save [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]. Deep in the woods, he helped clear an ancient, overgrown barrow, partially translating scratched runes on the walls and cutting through draugr with ease using spinning axes. After returning to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ysmirs_Hall\|Ysmirs_Hall]], he gently placed the rescued [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] on a stone bed. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] then ordered him and his kin to permanently seal the barrow to ensure the domain of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven_Cold-Moor]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] remained undisturbed.
