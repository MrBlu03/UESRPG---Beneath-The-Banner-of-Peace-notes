# Hafjorg

## Description
A potion brewer and the son of the apothecary [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Scent_of_Serenity\|Scent_of_Serenity]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. He is deeply devoted to his family.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]]**: His mother, whom he feared lost when she disappeared into the woods.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: A regular client who purchases healing draughts and reagents.

## Location
- Operates the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Scent_of_Serenity\|Scent_of_Serenity]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 001\|Session 001]]**: Sold healing potions to the party.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]**: Offered a 200 gold reward when his mother went missing; teared up and busied himself preparing the home when the party delivered her blue ribbon.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]**: Quietly declined an invitation to drinks, choosing to mourn the loss of his family in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] in peace.
