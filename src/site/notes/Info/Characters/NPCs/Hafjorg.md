# Hafjorg

## Description
A talented, soft-spoken apothecary and son of the proprietor of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Scent_of_Serenity\|Scent_of_Serenity]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. He brews exceptional curative potions (2 HP, 6 HP, 10 HP) and maintains an extensive herbarium.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Regular alchemical trading partner and client.

## Location
- Operates the counter at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Scent_of_Serenity\|Scent_of_Serenity]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 001\|Session 001]]**: Supplied the party with their initial batch of healing draughts before their departure for [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]].
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]**: Provided specialized botanical reagents to [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] for experimental bomb testing.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]**: Restocked the party's medical kit following their return from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Fort Greenwall]].
