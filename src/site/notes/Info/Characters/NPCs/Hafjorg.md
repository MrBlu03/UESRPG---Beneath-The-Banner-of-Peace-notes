# Hafjorg

## Description
A devoted, gentle-mannered Nord alchemist in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] and the son of master herbalist [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]]. He helps manage and run the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Scent_of_Serenity\|Scent of Serenity]] alchemy shop.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]]**: His mother, whom he cares for deeply and works alongside at the apothecary.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Trades alchemical potions with the party and posted the bounty to locate his missing mother.

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Scent_of_Serenity\|Scent of Serenity]]** in **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**.

## Notes
- **Alchemical Trade ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 001\|Session 001]]):** Sold essential healing potions to the party during their early travels.
- **Missing Mother Search ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]):** In visible distress when his mother failed to return from foraging, posting a 200 gold bounty. Wept tears of profound relief when the party delivered her ribbon proving she was safe.
- **Grief and Seclusion ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]):** When informed that family members in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] had died during the vampire massacre, he declined social invitations, quietly closing the shop to mourn in peace.
