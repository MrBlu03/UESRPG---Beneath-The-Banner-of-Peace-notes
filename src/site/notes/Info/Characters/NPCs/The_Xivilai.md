# The Xivilai

## Description
A towering, silvery-blue Daedric commander who presided over the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile]]'s counterfeit coin vanishing ritual at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan_Mesa]]. He wielded immense physical power and a massive Orcish Greataxe.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]**: Slain by the party.

## Location
- Encountered in the central ritual chamber of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan_Mesa]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]**: Led a Daedric ritual to vanish counterfeit coins and sacrifice captives. Engaged in a drinking contest with [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] before the party ambushed him. During the fight, he severely wounded [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] with Brutal Cleave. After being disarmed, burned by sunbeams, and pinned by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], he was fatally shot through the neck by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] before [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] tore his head and spine completely off.
