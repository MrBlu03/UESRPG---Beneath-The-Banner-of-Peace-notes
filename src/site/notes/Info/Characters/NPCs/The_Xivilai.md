# The Xivilai

## Description
A towering, devastating Daedric champion summoned during the climactic blood ritual at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan_Mesa]]. Wielding destructive elemental magic and a colossal greatsword, it served as the cult's ultimate weapon.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]**: Slain in the final battle.

## Location
- Summoned at the central ritual altar of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan_Mesa]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]**: Fought and destroyed by [[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]] and their allies in a fierce battle, shattering the portal and liberating the region.
