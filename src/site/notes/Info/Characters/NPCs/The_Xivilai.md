# The Xivilai

## Description
A towering, fearsome Daedric warlord with horned skull, silvery-blue skin, and immense martial prowess. He commanded the central sacrificial chamber within the Dwemer ruins of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan_Mesa]], overseeing the dark ritual powering the 37,000 counterfeit Septims flooding Eastmarch.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]]**: High ritual commander in the Dwemer facility.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Ambushed, outwitted, and slain by the party.

## Location
- Central Ritual Chamber in the **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Main Facility Ruin]]** north of **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]**.

## Notes
- **Drinking Challenge & Execution ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]):** [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] posed as a thirsty sentry and challenged the Xivilai to a half-liter flagon drinking contest to distract him. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] dropped paralysis bombs from the overhead ventilation shafts, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] shot a critical silver arrow through the Daedra's neck, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] drove his hands into the wound, tearing off the Xivilai's head and spine in a brutal execution.
