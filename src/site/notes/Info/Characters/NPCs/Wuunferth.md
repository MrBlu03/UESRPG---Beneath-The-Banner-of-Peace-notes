# Wuunferth

## Description
Wuunferth the Hunter is a veteran vampire hunter who became the target of [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s personal quest to locate him. He tracked his quarry to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Shors_Bastion\|Shor's Bastion]] but ended up trapped in the magically frozen town of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]], where he was captured and severely tortured by the ancient vampires of [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan_Volkihar]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]]**: Identified Wuunferth's tortured body at the Greenwall war camp, recognizing him from his past.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Searched for him as part of her personal quest to find the elusive vampire hunter.

## Location
- Last seen being transported to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistwatch\|Mistwatch]] for medical recovery.
