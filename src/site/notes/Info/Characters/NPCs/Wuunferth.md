# Wuunferth

## Description
A renowned, veteran vampire hunter whose tracking of ancient Volkihar bloodlines formed the primary objective of [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s personal quest.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Periwyn sought him across the Rift to join his hunt and learn vampire lore.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hillevi\|Hillevi]]**: Companion healer who provided life-saving medical care and escorted him to Mistwatch.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod Wraithbane]]**: Coordinated his rescue from the vampires at Greenwall.

## Location
- Found in **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]**; relocated to **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistwatch\|Mistwatch]]** for convalescence.

## Notes
- **The Search for the Hunter ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 002a (downtime)\|Session 002a (downtime)]] – [[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]):** Tracked by Periwyn from Shor's Bastion to Greenwall, where he had become trapped by the vampire blizzard.
- **Torture & Rescue at Greenwall ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 006\|Session 006]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]):** Discovered hanging and horribly tortured inside the fortress keep, barely clinging to life. Healer [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hillevi\|Hillevi]] stabilized his injuries and escorted him to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistwatch\|Mistwatch]] for advanced healing by the Old Gods until the spring thaw.
