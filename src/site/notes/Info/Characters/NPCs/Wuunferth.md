# Wuunferth

> *"A grim veteran of the shadowed hunt—broken by the fangs of Clan Volkihar, yet bearing secrets vital to Skyrim's survival."*

## Description
A seasoned, solitary vampire hunter and battlemage who has dedicated his life to tracking and exterminating the ancient bloodlines of [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan_Volkihar]]. Renowned across the eastern holds for his deep knowledge of hematomancy, sun-runes, and silvercraft, he was captured during a reconnaissance mission at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Fort Greenwall]] and subjected to horrific blood-drain rituals before being rescued by [[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The Fangs]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Central figure in Periwyn's personal quest; she sought him out across the Rift to glean his expertise on vampire eradication.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir_Stormcrown]]**: Old comrade-in-arms who monitored his movements and alerted the party to his disappearance near Greenwall.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hillevi\|Hillevi]]**: Companion healer who personally escorted the broken hunter from Greenwall to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistwatch\|Mistwatch]] to recover in the mountain sanctuary.

## Location
- Convalescing at the mountain fortress of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistwatch\|Mistwatch]] under the care of old healers.
