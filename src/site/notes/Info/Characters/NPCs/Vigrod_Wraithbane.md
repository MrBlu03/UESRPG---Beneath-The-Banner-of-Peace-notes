# Vigrod Wraithbane

## Description
Vigrod Wraithbane is the Harbinger and leader of the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Fallowstone_Hall\|Fallowstone_Hall]]. He is a seasoned Nord commander who led a regiment to rescue the town of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] from a vampire ambush orchestrated by [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan_Volkihar]]. Despite suffering heavy losses, Vigrod remained a determined and inspiring leader, showing great concern for his soldiers and respect for the party.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]]**: A fellow harbinger with whom he collaborated on military strategy at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] war camp.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen]]**: A fallen Companion whom Vigrod mourned and honored.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild]]**: Another fallen Companion whose loss was heavily felt by Vigrod and his remaining troops.

## Location
- Stationed at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Fallowstone_Hall\|Fallowstone_Hall]], but established a war camp near [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]].

## Notes

And may we meet again in Sovngarde."
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]**: Received a message from a hawk regarding reinforcements arriving the next day. Decided the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] would stay behind to secure the keep and the dangerous crimson orb. Warned the party about the dangers of associating with the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark_Brotherhood]], advising them to "Choose your allies with care."
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 009\|Session 009]]**: Appeared in the party's mirror visions as a hollow, soulless figure with blackened, rotting eyes.
