# Vigrod Wraithbane

## Description
The revered Harbinger of [[The_Companions\|The Companions]] of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Fallowstone_Hall\|Fallowstone Hall]] in the Rift. A legendary, resolute Nord warrior who commands supreme respect across Skyrim for his martial prowess, honor, and leadership.

## Relationships
- **[[The_Companions\|The Companions]]**: Supreme Harbinger and military leader of Fallowstone Hall.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]]**: Allied with the Cult of Ysmir against supernatural incursions.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Wuunferth\|Wuunferth]]**: Coordinated the rescue and medical evacuation of the veteran vampire hunter.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Coordinated tactical plans to storm Greenwall fortress.

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Fallowstone_Hall\|Fallowstone Hall]]** and the war camp outside **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]**.

## Notes
- **Greenwall War Camp ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]):** Established a siege camp outside Greenwall after an initial force of 100 soldiers was reduced to five battered veterans by a surprise vampire ambush. Formulated the multi-pronged breach strategy with Gjellir and the party.
- **Securing the Fortress & Harrowstorm Orb ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 006\|Session 006]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]):** Breached the fortress keep, secured the mysterious Harrowstorm ritual orb, and dispatched the party back to Riften with the severed vampire commander's head.
