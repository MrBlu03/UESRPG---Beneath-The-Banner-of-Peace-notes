# Vigrod Wraithbane

## Description
The legendary Harbinger and battle-leader of the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Fallowstone_Hall\|Fallowstone_Hall]]. A mighty Nord warrior of peerless honor, wisdom, and strength who embodies the spirit of Ysgramor and leads his shield-brothers in defense of the realm.

- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen_the_Mer-Killer]]**: Trusted veteran Companion.
