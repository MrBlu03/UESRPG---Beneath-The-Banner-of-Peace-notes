# Bjalfi

## Description
Bjalfi is the proprietor and bartender of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]] tavern in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. He is a reliable source of local information, frequently interacting with travelers and hunters passing through the town. 

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: A frequent patron who played cards with Bjalfi and sought his help finding people in town.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gaius\|Gaius]]**: A Colovian ex-Legionnaire who drank at his tavern.

## Location
- Found serving drinks and food at [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]] in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].
