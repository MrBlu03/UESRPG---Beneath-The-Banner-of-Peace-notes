# Bjalfi

## Description
A boisterous, rough-and-ready Nord trapper and logger in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], known for his loud laughter and deep familiarity with the riverbanks.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nilan\|Nilan]]**: Friend and fellow tracker in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]**: Cooperated with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] during the forest hunt.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Drinking companion at [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]].

## Location
- Frequents [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|The_Soggy_Beaver]] tavern in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].
