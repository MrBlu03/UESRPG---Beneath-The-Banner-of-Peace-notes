# Bjalfi

## Description
The proprietor and bartender of [[The_Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]] tavern in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. A pragmatic tavernkeeper who keeps a watchful eye on travelers and local happenings.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Provided Durov with crucial clues regarding Imperial veterans in town and gave him an Imperial Legion identification tag stamped with zero.
- **[[The_Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver Staff]]**: Employs staff including Elsa to run drinks while he serves food and tends bar.

## Location
- **[[The_Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]]** in **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]**.

## Notes
- **Imperial Inquiries ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]]):** Confirmed the presence of a Colovian in town ([[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gaius\|Gaius]]) and handed [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] a stamped Imperial Legion tag, aiding Durov's search into his forgotten past.
- **Local Intelligence ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]]):** Provided details on recent suspicious visitors and movements around Falderstad to the party.
- **Festival Service ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 019\|Session 019]]):** Managed food and drink service at the tavern during the post-hunt celebrations and continued regular tavern operations during the party's downtime.
