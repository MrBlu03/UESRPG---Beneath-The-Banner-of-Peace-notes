# Alma

## Description
A resilient and direct Nord huntress from [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], later seen residing in or traveling through [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Ingrin\|Ingrin]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Runa\|Runa]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorr\|Bjorr]]**: Fellow hunters in the organized expedition against the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Skinstealer]].
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Saved Alma's life on the battlefield through critical medical treatment after Alma was critically wounded by the Ragged Lord.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige / Sadrith Indoril]]**: Questioned by Sadrith regarding tainted, strange-smelling currency in Kjalgrad.

## Location
- Formerly **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]** ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]]).
- **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]** ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]]).

## Notes
- **Hunt for the Skinstealer ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]]):** Joined [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]'s hunting company into the cursed grove in the Northern Forest.
- **Battle with the Ragged Lord ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]]):** Sustained a grievous chest wound from the creature's claws (12 damage). After taking a healing potion, she landed an attack of opportunity that inflicted permanent burns on the monster. She survived thanks to immediate emergency medical care from [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]].
- **Kjalgrad Intelligence ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]]):** Encountered in Kjalgrad with wounds healing well. She provided vital intel about Sadrith (The Vestige) and detailed how the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]] bankrolls the city guards and effectively controls Kjalgrad's commerce, tolls, and steward over the figurehead Jarl.
