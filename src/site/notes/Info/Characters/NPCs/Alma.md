# Alma

## Description
Alma is a skilled Nord huntress from [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] who traveled to [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] to participate in the dangerous expedition to slay the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Skinstealer]]. She is a brave and capable warrior who survived a near-fatal encounter with the beast and later provided the party with valuable intelligence about the corrupt politics of her home city.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]**, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]**, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Runa\|Runa]]**, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Ingrin\|Ingrin]]**: Fellow members of the hunting band that ventured into the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Northern_Forest\|Northern Forest]].
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Saved Alma's life by stabilizing her grievous wounds during the battle with the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]].

## Location
- Resides in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]]**: Joined [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]'s organized hunt for the supernatural creature terrorizing the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Northern_Forest\|Northern Forest]]. She was noted for her impatience, suggesting they shouldn't sit around for too long.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]]**: Fought bravely against the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]] and his wolves. She shot multiple wolves before the Ragged Lord teleported behind her and slashed her chest, dealing 12 damage and a grievous wound. Despite bleeding out, she landed a crucial attack of opportunity with her fire-varnished blade that permanently burned the creature. After [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] stabilized her, she drank a healing potion and survived the battle.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]]**: Met the party in the market district of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], her wounds healing cleanly. She provided vital intel about Sadrith (a Dunmer asking about "gold that smells weird") and explained how the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]] corruptly throttles trade and controls the city's guards.
