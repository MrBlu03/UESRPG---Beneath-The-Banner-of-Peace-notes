# Brovin

## Description
A loyal and perceptive hunting hound belonging to the hunters of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].

## Relationships
- **Hunters of Falderstad**: Trained hunting dog used for tracking game and supernatural disturbances in the surrounding wilderness.

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]** and the surrounding woods.

## Notes
- **Supernatural Warning ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]]):** During a wilderness scouting expedition into the Northern Forest, Brovin abruptly stopped dead in his tracks, whining and refusing to cross into the cursed territory of the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Skinstealer]], providing an early warning of dark magic.
