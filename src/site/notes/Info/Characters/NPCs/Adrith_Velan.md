# Adrith Velan

## Description
A Dunmer operative and saboteur in the employ of the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]]. Posing as a concerned kinsman in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Trevas_Bend]], he deliberately blocked the flow regulators of the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Dam\|Snow-Shod_Dam]] to incite a violent feud between local fishermen and [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Snow-Shod\|Clan_Snow-Shod]]. Once his task was complete, his masters eliminated him with concentrated nightshade poison.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Alderman_Torgir]]**: Manipulated [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Torgir]] under cover of darkness, posing as a helpful kinsman while secretly orchestrating the water feud.
- **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]]**: His employers who orchestrated regional sabotage and ultimately silenced him with poison.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Discovered his body; struck his skull with a warhammer to ensure he would not rise as an undead abomination.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sigrid\|Sigrid]]**: Conducted the post-mortem toxicology at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall of the Dead]].

## Location
- Found dead near the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Dam\|Snow-Shod_Dam]]; body delivered to the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall_of_the_Dead]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]**: Found dead by the party beneath an oak near the dam, poisoned by concentrated nightshade with a single black nightshade blossom placed on his chest. His recovered journal revealed the sabotage plot and confirmed his ties to the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]].
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]**: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sigrid\|Sigrid]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Ralvard\|Ralvard]] completed the chemical analysis of the poison taken from his corpse, confirming a rare and potent strain.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 012\|Session 012]]**: His autopsy report was stolen from the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall of the Dead]] by cult infiltrators seeking to erase evidence of their involvement.
