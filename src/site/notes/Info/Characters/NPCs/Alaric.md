# Alaric

## Description
A mercenary bandit captain hired by the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] to ambush travelers and blockade the southern road between [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] and [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Trevas_Bend]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Battered his squad into submission with her warhammer on the southern road.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Harvold\|Captain_Harvold]]**: Transferred into [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Harvold\|Harvold]]'s custody at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] barracks.

## Location
- Imprisoned in the guard dungeon beneath [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil_Keep]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 001\|Session 001]]**: Led an ambush against [[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]] on the road south of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. After his squad was routed, he surrendered and revealed written orders stamped with the wax seal of the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]. Handed over to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Harvold\|Captain_Harvold]] for a bounty.
