# Granny Mummu

## Description
A sharp-witted, eccentric elder alchemist in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Beneath her grandmotherly persona lies a master toxicologist who brews some of the most potent salves and poisons in eastern Skyrim.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Mentored [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] in rare shadow reagents and high-grade paralysis venom.

## Location
- Runs an apothecary workshop near the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Grand_Market_Kjalgrad\|Grand_Market_Kjalgrad]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]**: Sold rare reagents to [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and shared warnings regarding strange ingredients being purchased in bulk by the longhouse steward.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]]**: Supplied [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] with refined solvents and venom bases used to craft the paralysis and flame bombs for the longhouse raid.
