# Boran

## Description
A seasoned, muscular Nord master armorer in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], proprietor of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Borans_Bulwarks\|Borans_Bulwarks]]. Specializes in crafting reinforced wooden and iron shields.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Regular customer who consulted him on shield reinforcement.

## Location
- Operates [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Borans_Bulwarks\|Borans_Bulwarks]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 009\|Session 009]]**: Consulted by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] regarding boss reinforcement techniques for his heavy Colovian shield.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 013\|Session 013]]**: Sold armor rivets, leather straps, and repair kits to the party before their long journey to [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].
