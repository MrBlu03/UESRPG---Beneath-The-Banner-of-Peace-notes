# Tharen Duleri

## Description
A haughty Dunmer smuggler and leader of the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna_Tong]] cell operating out of a coastal cave south of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. Involved in illegal trade of moon sugar, stolen goods, and weapons.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Interrogated by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]; had his nose broken after mocking the party.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torvald_Black-Foot\|Torvald_Black-Foot]]**: Smuggled goods compromising Black-Foot merchant routes.

## Location
- Held in the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] guard dungeons after capture.

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]**: His hideout was raided by the party. Captured after an underwater flank attack by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], he was interrogated and yielded ledgers revealing ties between the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] and the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]].
