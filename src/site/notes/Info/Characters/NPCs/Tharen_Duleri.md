# Tharen Duleri

## Description
A leader of the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna_Tong]] operating in the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] area. His base of operations was a small clearing and grotto in the woods south of the city.

## Relationships
- **Unnamed Dunmer Subordinate**: A member of his crew who gave up his name and location under interrogation.

## Location
- His base was an abandoned cave in the woods south of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]**: He was mentioned by a captured Dunmer subordinate during an interrogation by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Harvold\|Captain_Harvold]] at the jail. The subordinate named Tharen as his boss and revealed their hideout's location. Tharen himself was not encountered.
