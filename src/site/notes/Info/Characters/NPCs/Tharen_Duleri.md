# Tharen Duleri

## Description
A high-ranking Dunmer boss and regional syndicate leader within the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] criminal organization operating along the Skyrim-Morrowind border.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]]**: Commands local smuggling cells and mercenary operatives.

## Location
- Borderlands of **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The_Rift]]** and **[[Info/Lore/Morrowind\|Morrowind]]**.

## Notes
- **Syndicate Inquiries ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]):** Named during dungeon interrogations in Riften as the direct syndicate boss orchestrating illegal operations and smuggling caravans in the region.
