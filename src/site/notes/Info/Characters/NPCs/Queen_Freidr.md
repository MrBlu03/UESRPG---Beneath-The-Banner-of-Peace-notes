# Queen Freidr

## Description
The sovereign Queen of The Rift, ruling from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil_Keep]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. A wise, dignified, and burdened ruler seeking to protect her hold from external subversion, internal corruption, and supernatural perils.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]**: Patron monarch who commissioned their investigations and granted them royal authority.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Harvold\|Captain_Harvold]]**: Her loyal hold guard commander.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]]**: Met with him to discuss prophecies and the vampires of [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]].

## Location
- Holds court in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil_Keep]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].
