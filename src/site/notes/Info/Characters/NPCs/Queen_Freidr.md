# Queen Freidr

## Description
The reigning Queen of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] and ruler of the Rift, holding court from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil Keep]]. Resolute, politically shrewd, and dignified, she fiercely defends the sovereignty of eastern Skyrim against external threats, syndicates, and occult conspiracies.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]]**: Her court mage, royal advisor, and chief magical authority.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Harvold\|Captain Harvold]]**: Commander of her royal city guard in Riften.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst ([[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]])**: Granted royal charter, official writs of authority, bounties, and quality warhorses, christening them "The Fangs of Fjelhorst."

## Location
- Throne Room of **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil Keep]]** in **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**.

## Notes
- **Royal Summons & Dam Dispute ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 001\|Session 001]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]):** Summoned the party to Mistveil Keep to investigate the water stoppage between Treva's Bend and Clan Snow-Shod. Received the recovered diary of poisoned Dunmer [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith Velan]].
- **Naming the Fangs & Greenwall Crisis ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]):** Officially bestowed the party with the title **The Fangs of Fjelhorst** and tasked them with the military relief of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]. Personally received the severed vampire head and awarded honors upon the fortress's liberation.
- **Royal Writs & Mounts ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 012\|Session 012]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 013\|Session 013]]):** Granted the party broad royal writs of authority to pursue cultists into Eastmarch and personally oversaw the gifting of sturdy horses at the royal stables.
