# Yannick the Brawler

## Description
A loud, belligerent, and heavily intoxicated Nord brawler who frequents **[[The_Ragged_Flagon\|The Ragged Flagon]]** beneath [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Brooding_Corner_Patron\|Brooding_Corner_Patron]]**: Respected the brooding Dunmer patron, immediately backing down when whistled at.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Backed down when confronted by Onasha's steely gaze.

## Location
- **[[The_Ragged_Flagon\|The Ragged Flagon]]** in the **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ratway\|Ratway]]**, **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**.

## Notes
- **Ratway Confrontation ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 012\|Session 012]]):** Drunkenly screamed guard conspiracy theories regarding the recent fires in the Ratway. When pressed by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], he faltered and stood down completely after the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Brooding_Corner_Patron\|Brooding_Corner_Patron]] whistled from the corner.
