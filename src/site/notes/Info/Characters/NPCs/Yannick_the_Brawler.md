# Yannick the Brawler

## Description
A drunk troublemaker and patron found in the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] Ratway who is known for spreading conspiracy theories.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Pressed him on his claims, causing him to falter.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Brooding_Corner_Patron\|Brooding_Corner_Patron]]**: Silenced Yannick with a whistle.

## Location
- Frequents the taverns and common areas of the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] Ratway.

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 012\|Session 012]]**: While drunk, he loudly blamed the guards for recent fires in the Ratway. He quickly faltered and backed down when pressed by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], and was completely silenced by a whistle from the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Brooding_Corner_Patron\|Brooding_Corner_Patron]].
