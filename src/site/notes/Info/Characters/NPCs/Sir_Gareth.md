# Sir Gareth

## Description
A legendary Breton paladin of the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Knights_of_Arkay\|Knights_of_Arkay]] and House Lunclaire, and the former mentor of [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]. His teachings on mercy and duty remain central to his protege's life, even after his tragic death by her hand.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: His former student, whom he trained and whom he forced to end his life when he turned into a vampire.

## Location
- His memory lives on in [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s mind and the ancestral memory of [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Arkayfell\|Arkayfell]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]]**: A malicious whisper taunted [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] in the nightmare manor, calling her "Gareth's little stray."
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)\|Session 009a]]**: Manifested in [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s coma vision, engaging her in a physical and philosophical duel about mercy versus vengeance, ultimately teaching her to forgive herself.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sketched a drawing of him based on her visions and opened up to [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] about having to kill him.
