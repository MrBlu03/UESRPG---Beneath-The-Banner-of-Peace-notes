# Sir Gareth

## Description
A legendary Breton paladin of the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Knights_of_Arkay\|Order of Arkay]] and House Lunclaire. His noble teachings on chivalry, righteous fury, and eternal vigilance against vampirism form the bedrock of [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s convictions.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Revered mentor and ancestral exemplar of House Lunclaire.

## Location
- His memory and legacy are enshrined at [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Arkayfell\|Arkayfell]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)\|Session 009a]]**: Appeared within [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s visionary dream in [[Info/Locations/Other Realms/Crows_Wood\|Crow's Wood]], debating the nature of vampiric curses, [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Ravenwatch\|House Ravenwatch]], and reminding her of her sacred oath to eradicate the undead.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: Remembered in solemn prayers as [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] reaffirmed her knightly vows.
