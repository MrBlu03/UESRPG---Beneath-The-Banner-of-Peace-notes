# Sir Gareth

## Description
A noble Knight of the Order of Arkay and the beloved combat mentor of [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] during her youth. A master warrior who wielded a mighty warhammer and taught Periwyn the art of battle. When he was bitten by vampires defending their sanctuary at [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Arkayfell\|Arkayfell]], Periwyn carried out a mercy-killing—an act that left her burdened with immense psychological trauma and guilt for years.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Former student and close companion who inherited and carried his warhammer into battle.

## Location
- Sanctuary of **[[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Arkayfell\|Arkayfell]]** (Dream Memory / Spiritual Plane).

## Notes
- **Visions of Absolution ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)\|Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)]]):** Manifested as a radiant divine spirit within Periwyn's dream-trial, confronting her weaponized grief and granting complete absolution (*"You carried my death for too long... You forgave me. Now forgive yourself"*). When the vampire [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lady_Elaris\|Lady_Elaris]] attacked, Gareth intervened with blinding divine light, driving the entity back and dissolving peacefully into silver light, leaving his warhammer at Periwyn's feet.
