# John

## Description
There are two notable individuals named John. The first is a small, thick-furred northern husky belonging to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]], who adopted him as a stray three years ago. The second is a shady human informant who operates in the alleyways near the abandoned Blackfoot Guildhouse in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]]**: Owner of John the husky.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** and **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain_Irlav]]**: Met with John the informant.

## Location
- John the husky travels with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] in [[The Rift\|The Rift]] and [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].
- John the informant operates near the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Old_Blackfoot_Guild_House\|Old_Blackfoot_Guild_House]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: John the husky accompanied [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] and the party on a camping trip into the forest.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 027\|Session 027]]**: John the informant met with [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain_Irlav]] in the alleyways near the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Old_Blackfoot_Guild_House\|Old_Blackfoot_Guild_House]], handing them the journal scraps of a missing merchant who had been having nightmares about the Jarl.
