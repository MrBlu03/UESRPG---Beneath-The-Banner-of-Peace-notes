# John

## Description
This name references two separate entities documented within the campaign logs:
1. **John (Husky Companion):** A loyal, friendly stray husky dog adopted by master herbalist [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].
2. **John (Shady Informant):** A covert underworld contact operating in the alleyways of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]]**: Rescued and adopted John the dog three years prior.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] & [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain Irlav]]**: Met John the informant in Kjalgrad to receive critical intelligence scraps.

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]** (John the Dog)
- **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]** (John the Informant)

## Notes
- **John the Dog ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]):** Accompanied [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]], the party, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]], and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] on their northern camping and stag-hunting trek, walking cheerfully alongside the travelers.
- **John the Informant ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 027\|Session 027]]):** Met by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] in the shadowy alleys near the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Old_Blackfoot_Guild_House\|abandoned Blackfoot Guildhouse]] during the warehouse fires in Kjalgrad. He delivered recovered diary scraps from a missing merchant detailing terrifying nightmares and pilgrimages to the mesa.
