# Speaks-Spells-Gently

## Description
A wise, highly educated Argonian mage and arcane tutor residing in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. He is knowledgeable in magical theory, alchemy, and outer planar realms.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Serves as her primary academic instructor and magical mentor in Riften.

## Location
- Residence and laboratory in **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**.

## Notes
- **Arcane Consultations ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]):** Consulted by [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] regarding strange magical phenomena and esoteric planar anomalies.
- **Blackfeather Court Lore ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]]):** When presented with mysterious dust samples and informed of the "Court," his eyes widened in immediate recognition as he explained the nature of the [[Blackfeather_Court\|Blackfeather Court]] of crows hailing from Nocturnal's realm of [[Evergloam\|Evergloam]].
