# Milos

## Description
An Argonian assassin of the [[Info/Organizations/Other/Blacktongue_Tribe\|Blacktongue_Tribe]] and the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark_Brotherhood]]. He is the egg-brother of [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and an operative allied with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] and the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: His egg-sister, whom he reunited with in eastern Skyrim.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]]**: A Dremora steward whom he helped capture and interrogate.

## Location
- Usually operates covertly in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] and maintains a hideout in the Barrow of the Oath Breaker King.
