# Milos

## Description
Milos is a skilled Argonian shadow operative, assassin, and alchemist belonging to the [[Info/Organizations/Other/Blacktongue_Tribe\|Blacktongue_Tribe]] and the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark_Brotherhood]]. As an egg-brother to [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], he shares a deep tribal bond with her and possesses mastery over lethal venoms, covert tracking, and silent blade work.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: His beloved egg-sister and tribal kin; reunited in eastern Skyrim and shared rare alchemical lore and reagents.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]**: Operative ally with whom he collaborates on high-stakes missions against regional cult networks.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Maintained a tense yet professional relationship due to [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s moral struggles with his [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark Brotherhood]] affiliation.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Trusted ally in combat during urban and wilderness operations.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]]**: Interrogated and bound the false steward following his capture.

## Location
- Operates covertly across [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The Rift]] and [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
