# Milos

> *"Water flows where the blade commands, sister. The egg remembers, even when the river turns to ice."*

## Description
A deadly, quiet, and razor-sharp Argonian assassin belonging to the [[Info/Organizations/Other/Blacktongue_Tribe\|Blacktongue_Tribe]] and the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark_Brotherhood]]. He is the beloved egg-brother of [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], possessing lethal mastery of venoms, throat-slitting stealth, and Jel marsh-dialect. Reunited with Onasha in the frozen north, he operates as a trusted field operative for [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]], lending his silent daggers to the fight against the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile]].

*(Note: Milos is completely distinct from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige / Sadrith Indoril]]. Milos is the Argonian shadow assassin; The Vestige is the Dunmer Crow Father.)*

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: His egg-sister and closest kin. Their reunion was marked by deep emotional warmth, shared Jel greetings, and alchemical collaboration.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]**: Operates under the Crow Father's command, coordinating reconnaissance, assassinations, and prisoner holding cells.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]]**: Captured and brutally interrogated the disguised Dremora steward in the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Sewers\|Kjalgrad Sewers]].

## Location
- Operates covertly from the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Barrow_of_the_Oath-Breaker_King\|Barrow_of_the_Oath-Breaker_King]] and hidden sewer tunnels beneath [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
