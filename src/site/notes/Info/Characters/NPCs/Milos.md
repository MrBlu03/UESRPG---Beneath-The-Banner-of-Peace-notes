# Milos

## Description
An Argonian assassin of average height from the Black-Tongue tribe, recognized by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] as an egg-brother from her youth in Black Marsh. He is a lethal, stealthy blade of the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark Brotherhood]] who fights with dual daggers and short swords. He operates as an ally and operative alongside [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] in the Kjalgrad region.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Egg-brother from the Black-Tongue tribe; shares deep tribal familiarity, mutual contracts, and dark humor.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige / Crowfather]]**: Close ally and partner in shadow operations against the regional cults.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Provided intensive stealth combat, evasion, and dagger training within the ancient barrow hideout.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Answered her inquiries regarding death, and assisted her in capturing corrupt officials.

## Location
- Hidden base of operations in the **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Barrow_of_the_Oath-Breaker_King\|Barrow_of_the_Oath-Breaker_King]]** outside **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]**.

- **Reunion in Kjalgrad ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 019\|Session 019]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]]):** Sighted by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] at an Argonian caravan; later revealed as the resident contact inside the Barrow of the Oath-Breaker King working in coordination with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]].
- **Stealth Training & Tactics ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]]):** Welcomed the party into the barrow, provided shelter and provisions, and conducted two days of intensive stealth and blade training with [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]].
- **Longhouse Ambush & Interrogation ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 028\|Session 028]]):** Infiltrated the burning longhouse through a high window to capture the disguised Scaafin steward [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius Caro]], assisting in his sewer interrogation and wilderness escort.
- **Cult Facility Reconnaissance ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 029\|Session 029]]):** Scouted the fortified northern cult installation and coordinated contract negotiations and assault tactics with [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and the party.
