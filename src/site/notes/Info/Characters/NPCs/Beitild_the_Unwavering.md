# Beitild the Unwavering

## Description
A fierce, honorable Nord shield-maiden of the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] hailing from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Fallowstone_Hall\|Fallowstone Hall]]. Renowned for her unyielding courage, she wields a battleaxe with deadly precision and lives strictly by the code of Ysgramor.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod_Wraithbane]]**: Her respected battle-leader and Harbinger.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Developed a strong bond of mutual respect and martial camaraderie around the campfire.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen_the_Mer-Killer]]**: Fellow shield-brother in the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]].

## Location
- Based at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Fallowstone_Hall\|Fallowstone_Hall]], frequently stationed at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Companions_War_Camp\|Companions_War_Camp]] or Fort [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]].
