# Beitild the Unwavering

## Description
A sharp-eyed, seasoned Nord warrior and scout of [[The_Companions\|The Companions]]. Raised among the Skaal in her youth, she possessed keen wilderness instincts and specialized knowledge of necromantic wards and undead watcher thralls.

## Relationships
- **[[The_Companions\|The Companions]]**: Dedicated member who marched under [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod Wraithbane]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen the Mer-Killer]]**: Fellow veteran Companion who fought and fell alongside her at Greenwall.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Bonded around the campfire, inquiring about Kirya's Khajiiti heritage and sharing tales of her own Skaal upbringing.

## Location
- Camped outside and fought inside **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]**.

## Notes
- **Scouting at Greenwall ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]):** Formed a forward reconnaissance team with [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], successfully discovering a hidden western hatch into the fortress. Identified necromantic watcher thralls and warned the party of their ritual functions.
- **Death in Battle ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 006\|Session 006]]):** Slain during the fierce assault against the vampire forces holding the fortress. Her death prompted immense grief and anger in [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]].
- **Funeral Pyre & Remembrance ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]):** Honored with a traditional Companion funeral pyre outside Greenwall, and later toasted in solemn memory by the survivors at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Amber_Keg\|Amber Keg]] in Riften.
