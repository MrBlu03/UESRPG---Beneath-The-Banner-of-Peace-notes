# Beitild the Unwavering

## Description
Beitild the Unwavering was a fierce, sharp-eyed Nord warrior and scout belonging to the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult_of_Ysmir]]. She accompanied the supply convoy and military expedition to rescue the town of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] from [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan_Volkihar]]. Tragically, she was killed during the assault on the smuggler tunnels beneath the fort and given a traditional Nordic funeral pyre.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]]**: Her Harbinger in the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult_of_Ysmir]], whom she flanked alongside [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen]] during the expedition.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Developed a strong bond of mutual respect around the campfire, where Beitild shared stories of her youth among the Skaal.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen the Mer-Killer]]**: Her fellow warrior in the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult_of_Ysmir]] who fought and died alongside her at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]].

## Location
- Traveled from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] to the war camp at Fort [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]**: Traveled with the convoy to the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] war camp. Along the journey, she struck up a conversation with [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], sharing stories of her youth. She provided vital knowledge regarding bone hawks, explaining they were used for necromantic rituals as watchers for vampire thralls. She scouted the hidden smuggler's tunnel with the party.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 006\|Session 006]]**: Fought in the assault on [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]], where she tragically fell in battle alongside [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen]]. Her death caused [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] to erupt in furious grief, striking the ice wall with her warhammer.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]**: Honored in death with a traditional funeral pyre at the war camp. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] placed fresh flowers atop her ashes, and the party raised mugs to her memory at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Amber_Keg\|Amber Keg]] celebration.
