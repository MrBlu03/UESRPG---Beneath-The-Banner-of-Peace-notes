# Baron Jangleplume

## Description
A pompous, talking Daedric crow and titled noble of the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather_Court]] in [[Info/Locations/Other Realms/Crows_Wood\|Crows_Wood]]. Dressed in phantom finery, he barters in shiny trinkets, glittering baubles, and dangerous secrets, delivering cryptic warnings of planar upheaval.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Lore/Nocturnal\|Nocturnal]]**: Revered patroness and mistress of the Evergloam.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Communicated through cryptic prophecies regarding dragon scrolls and black feathers.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Challenged [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] during her visionary slumber in [[Info/Locations/Other Realms/Crows_Wood\|Crow's Wood]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]**: Acted as a corvid emissary between the party and the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|Crow Father]].

## Location
- Roosts in the twilight boughs of [[Info/Locations/Other Realms/Crows_Wood\|Crows_Wood]], occasionally manifesting in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The Rift]] and [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
