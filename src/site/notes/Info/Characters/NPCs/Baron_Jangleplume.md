# Baron Jangleplume

## Description
Baron Jangleplume is a rotund, glossy-feathered Daedric crow who serves as a messenger and noble of the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]]. Associated with the Daedric Prince [[Info/Lore/Nocturnal\|Nocturnal]], he is a curious and unpredictable creature capable of human speech and teleportation. He frequently interacts with the party, tracking them, delivering cryptic warnings, and serving as an envoy for [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Frequently feeds him bread and interacts with him.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle Benele]]**: Recognized him from the Clockwork City, where he once brought her a fake soup spoon.
- **[[Info/Lore/Nocturnal\|Nocturnal]]**: The Daedric Prince he is associated with.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]**: Revealed to be his master, summoning him in a burst of feathers.

## Location
- Seen tracking the party across [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The_Rift]], appearing in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], and later in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
