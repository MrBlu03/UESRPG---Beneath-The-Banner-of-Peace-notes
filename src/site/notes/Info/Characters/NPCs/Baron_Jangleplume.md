# Baron Jangleplume

## Description
A pompous, talking Daedric crow of the [[Blackfeather_Court\|Blackfeather Court]] from the realm of [[Evergloam\|Evergloam]]. He is noticeably plump, glossy-feathered, and carries himself with exaggerated aristocratic flair, constantly seeking shiny trinkets, morsels of bread, and formal recognition of his noble standing.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige / Crowfather]]**: Serves as one of the avian attendants and heralds to the Vestige.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Frequently interacted with Onasha, who lures him with bread and shiny baubles for conversation and intelligence.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle Benele]]**: Recognized by Gabrielle from past planar encounters, notably in the Clockwork City where he infamously delivered a counterfeit soup spoon.

## Location
- Manifests across **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**, **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]**, **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]**, and other regions where the [[Blackfeather_Court\|Blackfeather Court]] gathers.

## Notes
- **First Encounter ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]]):** Followed the party through Riften; [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] lured him close with bread, prompting him to grandly announce his name and court nobility.
- **Clockwork City Antics ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]):** Summoned by Onasha in Falderstad, where his past exploits in the Clockwork City were recalled by Gabrielle Benele.
- **Messenger & Herald ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 019\|Session 019]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]]):** Appeared in Kjalgrad delivering omens and heralding the arrival and revelation of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]].
