# Talking Fox

## Description
A magical, silver-furred speaking fox encountered in the quiet woodlands of the Rift. Fluent in Dovahzul (the Dragon Language), it speaks with ancient wisdom and eerie calm.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Translated her dragon-language scroll.

## Location
- Roams the deep woods of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The Rift]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]**: Translated [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s mysterious scroll, identifying the script as Dovahzul and warning that it contained a summoning ritual bound to secrecy, doom, and the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]].
