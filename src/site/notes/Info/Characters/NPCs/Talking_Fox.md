# Talking Fox

> *"A quick flash of russet fur and silver eyes—speaking riddles of Dovahzul and crow-wings to those with ears to hear."*

## Description
A supernatural, russet-furred fox possessing the uncanny ability to speak fluent Tamrielic in a soft, mocking whisper. Highly intelligent and elusive, it drifts along the edges of ancient wilderness paths, acting as a bizarre woodland oracle with deep awareness of ancient Dragon Language, runic magic, and the movements of the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Translated her mysterious dragon-bone scroll, delivering cryptic revelations about Dovahzul syntax and ancient oaths.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Teased her with warnings about the Court of Crows and the price of seeking dead gods.

## Location
- Roams the snowbound wilderness of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The_Rift]], frequently sighted along the roads between [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Fort Greenwall]], and [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].
