# Talking Fox

## Description
A mystical, intelligent talking fox and benign nature spirit that roams the woodlands of the Rift. It is attuned to primal energies and drawn to the spiritual path of [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Interacted with Onasha, translating her ancient dragon scroll and guiding her path.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**: Identified by Shaman Adda as a benevolent spirit of nature bound to Onasha's journey.

## Location
- Highways and forests between **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]** and **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**.

## Notes
- **Scroll Translation & Omen ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]):** Met the returning party on the road to Riften. Approached [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] to translate her ancient dragon-tongue parchment and issued a cryptic warning regarding "the Blackfeather."
- **Spiritual Nature ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]]):** Shaman [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] at the Hall of Ysmir confirmed the creature was a nature spirit drawn to Onasha's instincts and destiny.
