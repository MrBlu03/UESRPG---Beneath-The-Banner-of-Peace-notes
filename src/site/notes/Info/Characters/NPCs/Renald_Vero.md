# Renald Vero

## Description
An influential and distinguished Breton silk baron originally from Rivenspire (Shornhelm, High Rock), who manages commercial offices and warehouses in [[Info/Lore/Windhelm\|Windhelm]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Longtime business associate of Periwyn's father and the Lunclaire trading company.

## Location
- Commercial office in **[[Info/Lore/Windhelm\|Windhelm]]** and the Grand Market of **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]**.

## Notes
- **Silk Partnership Renewal ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]):** Attended the high-society trade gala in Kjalgrad's Jarl's Hall. Reconnected warmly with [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], reminiscing about his dealings with the Lunclaire family and promising to immediately restart lucrative silk trade connections when Periwyn visits Windhelm.
