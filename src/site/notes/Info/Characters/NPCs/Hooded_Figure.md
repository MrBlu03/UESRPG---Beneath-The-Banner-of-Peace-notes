# Hooded Figure

## Description
An enigmatic, supernatural figure draped in raven-black cowl and twilight mist, serving as a direct watcher for the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Court of Crows]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Whispered encouraging and ominous guidance during her trials.
- **[[Info/Lore/Nocturnal\|Nocturnal]]**: Mistress of the Evergloam.

## Location
- Appears unpredictably in shadowy wilderness clearings across Skyrim.

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]]**: Observed [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s unwavering resolve during the forest hunt, softly uttering 'Good girl' before vanishing into swirling crows.
