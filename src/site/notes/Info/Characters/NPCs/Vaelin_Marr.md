# Vaelin Marr

## Description
A wealthy, polished Dunmer trade advocate, diplomat, and senior factor for the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Known for his sharp silver tongue, expensive Morrowind robes, and intricate legal maneuvers.
