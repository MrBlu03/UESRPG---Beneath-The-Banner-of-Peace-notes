# Vaelin Marr

## Description
A wealthy, polished Dunmer trade advocate, diplomat, and senior factor for the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Known for his sharp silver tongue, expensive Morrowind robes, and intricate legal maneuvers, he secretly operates as an agent for the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jorven_Halliksson\|Jorven_Halliksson]]**: Guildmaster of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Silverroad]] under whom he serves.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]]**: Political ally in the longhouse.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Hired him as a mercenary and personal guard.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Proventus\|Proventus]]**: His trusted Imperial guard.

## Location
- Based in the executive offices of the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Silverroad_Guildhouse\|Silverroad_Guildhouse]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
