# Vaelin Marr

> *"Gold is the only true language of Tamriel. Everything else is mere rhetoric for priests and poets."*

## Description
A cunning, ruthless, and highly polished Imperial merchant-lord who serves as the influential Guildmaster of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Operating beneath an immaculate veneer of civic commerce, diplomatic philanthropy, and trade reform, Marr is secretly a high-ranking coordinator for the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] syndicate and a key mortal conspirator in the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]]'s economic destabilization scheme.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Maintained professional interactions with Durov in his guise as a Silverroad investigator, unwittingly allowing Durov access to sensitive guild logistics.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain_Irlav]]**: Employs and coordinates with the city guard captain, subtly steering guard deployments away from cult storage sites.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]]**: Colluded directly with the corrupt steward (revealed to be a disguised Dremora) to divert city treasury reserves into counterfeit molds.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]**: Sworn enemy of the Crow Father, who meticulously tracked Marr's financial ledgers and trade caravans across the Reach.

## Location
- The luxurious upper offices of the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Silverroad_Guildhouse\|Silverroad_Guildhouse]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
