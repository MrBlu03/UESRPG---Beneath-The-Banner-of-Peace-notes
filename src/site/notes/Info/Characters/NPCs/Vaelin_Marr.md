# Vaelin Marr (Valen Marr)

## Description
The charismatic, calculating Trade Advocate and co-leader of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Beneath his refined merchant facade, he is a high-ranking mastermind of the **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]**, wearing an Ouroboros pendant of authority and orchestrating covert operations from the northern Dwemer ruins.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jorven_Halliksson\|Jorven Halliksson]]**: Co-leader of the Guild of the Silverroad.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain Irlav]]**: His personal guard commander.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Attempted to recruit and align with Periwyn under her Lunclaire merchant lineage.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Hired Durov for covert intelligence assignments.

## Location
- Guild Headquarters in **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]** and the **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Main Facility Ruin]]**.

## Notes
- **Bounty & Counter-Offers ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 021\|Session 021]]):** Offered [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] employment to spy on "Sadrith" (The Vestige) to suppress investigations into tainted currency.
- **The Trade Gala ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]):** Attended the high-society gala wearing his concealed Ouroboros pendant, probing Periwyn regarding her political loyalties.
- **Main Facility Operations ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 028\|Session 028]]):** Met Durov and Captain Irlav inside the Dwemer ruin of Altan Mesa (accessed with his Ouroboros pendant), assigning them to investigate the abandoned [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] camp following the Kjalgrad warehouse explosions.
