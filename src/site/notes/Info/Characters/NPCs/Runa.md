# Runa

## Description
A well-known Nord huntress and marketplace meat vendor in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. She is an experienced tracker who knows the surrounding woods and provides game for the settlement.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Mirda\|Mirda]]**: Hunting partner in Falderstad.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Ingrin\|Ingrin]]**: Fellow huntress who saved Runa's life in battle.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]**: Joined Svala's organized expedition against the Skinstealer.

## Location
- Market stalls and surrounding forests in **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]**.

## Notes
- **Aiding Durov ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]]):** Directed [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] to The Soggy Beaver and later found him outside town in the snow with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Mirda\|Mirda]].
- **Hunt for the Skinstealer ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]]):** Joined [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]'s hunting party into the Cursed Grove, performing ceremonial stream cleansing before entering the dark forest.
- **Wolf Battle & Recovery ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]]):** Pinned by a corrupted wolf in the battle against the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]], suffering a bite that infected her with rock joint before [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Ingrin\|Ingrin]] decapitated the wolf. She was treated and fully recovered with local medical care.
