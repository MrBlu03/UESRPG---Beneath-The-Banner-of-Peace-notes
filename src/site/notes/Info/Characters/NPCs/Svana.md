# Svana

## Description
A Nord blacksmith in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] who co-owns and manages the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Sweaty_Steel\|Sweaty Steel]] forge alongside her brother [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorn\|Bjorn]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorn\|Bjorn]]**: Her brother and smithing partner.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]]**: Friend and neighbor whose foraging habits she was familiar with.

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Sweaty_Steel\|Sweaty Steel]]** in **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**.

## Notes
- **Smithy Trade & Information ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]):** Bought a retrieved bandit greatsword from [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]. Confirmed that alchemist [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] regularly foraged in the southern woods and expressed genuine concern when informed she had been missing for two nights, thanking the party for investigating.
