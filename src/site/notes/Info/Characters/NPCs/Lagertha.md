# Lagertha

## Description
Lagertha of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Red-Grain\|Clan Red-Grain]] was an ancient Nord noblewoman murdered during the era of the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Dragon_Cult\|Dragon Cult]]. Her clan deemed her love for [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven Cold-Moor]] shameful, leading them to flay her alive and cast her body into a village well. Her spirit remained bound to the cursed grounds of the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Haunted_Hunting_Lodge\|haunted hunting lodge]], shielding Sven from fully succumbing to a draugr curse until the party laid them both to rest.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven Cold-Moor]]**: Her tragic lover who walked into the barrow in his grief and became an undead draugr under a Dragon Priest's control.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**: The shaman who promised to protect her final resting place.

## Location
- Haunting the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Haunted_Hunting_Lodge\|Haunted_Hunting_Lodge]] east of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], before being laid to rest in the nearby ancient barrow.

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]]**: Encountered by the party as a spirit inside the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Haunted_Hunting_Lodge\|Haunted_Hunting_Lodge]]. She warmly welcomed them but did not recognize the god [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]], revealing her extreme age. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] swam into the submerged well to retrieve her skull, a lockbox, and a bone necklace given to her by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]].
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 009\|Session 009]]**: Appeared in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Kahrus_Hollow\|Khaarus Hollow]], initially melting into the form of a Dragon Priest. After the party defeated the cultists and draugr, Lagertha's spirit crossed the room to take [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]]'s hand. When he spoke the word "Krosis" (sorrow), her spirit finally faded. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] placed her remains in the same sarcophagus as Sven, adorned with her bone flower necklace, and sealed them together in peace.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: Acknowledged as finally being laid to rest, bringing an end to the haunting and returning the land to normal.
