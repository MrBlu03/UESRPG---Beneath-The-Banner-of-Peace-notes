# Lagertha of Clan Red-Grain

## Description
An ancient Nord noblewoman of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Red-Grain\|Clan Red-Grain]] who was tragically murdered during Lord [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]]'s dark [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Dragon_Cult\|Dragon Cult]] ritual. Her severed skull was cast into the well at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Haunted_Hunting_Lodge\|Haunted_Hunting_Lodge]], binding her restless spirit until the party recovered her remains and consecrated them.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven_Cold-Moor]]**: Her tragic husband whose dark rituals bound her spirit.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Dove into the well to retrieve her skull.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**: Performed the funeral rites that laid her to rest.

## Location
- Her spirit was bound to the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Haunted_Hunting_Lodge\|Haunted_Hunting_Lodge]] before ascending to Sovngarde.

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]]**: Her tragic story was recounted by the skalds [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonya]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] swam into the submerged well to retrieve her skull, breaking the seal.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 009\|Session 009]]**: With the defeat of the draugr Lord [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]], Lagertha's spirit was granted peaceful passage to Sovngarde.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: Honored in memorial prayers at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall_of_Ysmir]].
