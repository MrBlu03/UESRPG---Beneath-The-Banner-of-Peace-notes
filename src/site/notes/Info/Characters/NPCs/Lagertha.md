# Lagertha

## Description
An ancient Nord maiden of Clan Red-Grain from centuries past. Her forbidden love with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Cold-Moor\|Clan Cold-Moor]] led to a tragic clan massacre in which she was murdered and her remains cast down a well. Her sorrowful spirit haunted the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Haunted_Hunting_Lodge\|Haunted Hunting Lodge]] east of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven Cold-Moor]]**: Her beloved from Clan Cold-Moor, whose grief and draugr curse bound him to her for centuries.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Rescued her remains and laid her soul to rest.

## Location
- The well and barrow at the **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Haunted_Hunting_Lodge\|Haunted Hunting Lodge]]** near **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Kahrus_Hollow\|Kahru's Hollow]]**.

## Notes
- **Lodge Haunting ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]]):** Welcomed the adventuring party warmly at the lodge, longing for connection after centuries of isolation. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] retrieved her skull and her floral bone necklace from the bottom of the lodge's well.
- **Consecration & Rest ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 009\|Session 009]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]):** The party consecrated the burial mound and conducted a sacred funeral pyre, laying Lagertha in the same grave beside [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] with her floral bone necklace upon her neck, allowing both tortured souls to finally cross peacefully to the afterlife.
