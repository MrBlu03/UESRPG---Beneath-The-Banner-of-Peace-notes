# Donkey Khajiit Merchant

## Description
An itinerant Khajiit merchant traveling the roads of the Rift leading a pack donkey loaded with wares and alchemical concoctions.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Noticed Periwyn's visible psychological distress and offered herbal remedies.

## Location
- Highways and mountain roads near **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]** and **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistwatch\|Mistwatch]]**.

## Notes
- **Roadside Encounter ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]):** Met the returning party along the road toward Greenwall. Perceiving [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s severe post-traumatic exhaustion and distress following the Greenwall massacre, the merchant offered a mysterious soothing sleeping potion to help calm her nightmares.
