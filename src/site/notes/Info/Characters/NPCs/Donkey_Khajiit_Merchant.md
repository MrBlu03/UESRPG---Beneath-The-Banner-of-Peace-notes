# Donkey Khajiit Merchant

## Description
A nomadic Baandari Khajiit peddler traveling with a heavily laden pack donkey, trading exotic teas, spices, and trinkets along the roads of Skyrim.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Shared greetings and caravan news in Ta'agra.

## Location
- Travels the highways of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The Rift]] and Eastmarch.

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]**: Met by the party on the road north of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]; sold dried provisions and shared rumors of unusual merchant movements near the eastern border.
