# Donkey Khajiit Merchant

## Description
A traveling Khajiit merchant who travels the roads of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The Rift]] with a donkey.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Inquired with him about alchemy ingredients.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: He offered a mysterious sleeping potion for her nightmares.

## Location
- Travels along the highways, recently heading toward [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 007\|Session 007]]**: Encountered by the party on the road heading toward [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]. While he had no alchemy ingredients for [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], he did offer a mysterious sleeping potion to help with [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s nightmares.
