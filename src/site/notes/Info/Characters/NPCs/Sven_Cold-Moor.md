# Sven Cold-Moor

## Description
An ancient Nord warlord and [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Dragon_Cult\|Dragon Cult]] acolyte from [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Cold-Moor\|Clan Cold-Moor]]. Corrupted by dark rites and dragon worship, he sacrificed his bride [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] and was entombed beneath the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Haunted_Hunting_Lodge\|Haunted_Hunting_Lodge]], rising as a mighty draugr lord wielding the Thu'um.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]]**: His sacrificed bride whose spirit haunted the well.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**: Prophesied his destruction.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Defeated in combat.

## Location
- Entombed in the barrow beneath the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Haunted_Hunting_Lodge\|Haunted_Hunting_Lodge]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]]**: Awakened as a terrifying draugr lord, exploding the lodge doors inward and petrifying the party with the Thu'um (*'Faas. Ru. Maar.'*).
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 009\|Session 009]]**: Defeated in a brutal battle beneath the lodge by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], whispering *'Krosis'* as his corrupted form was finally laid to rest.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: His ancient Dragon Priest mask was claimed by the party as evidence of the ancient cult's legacy.
