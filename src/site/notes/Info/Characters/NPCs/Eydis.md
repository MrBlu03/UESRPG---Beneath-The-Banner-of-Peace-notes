# Eydis

## Description
The resilient, observant barkeep of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Sleeping_Stag\|The_Sleeping_Stag]] inn along the northern road of the Rift. Her father was killed by frost trolls near [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Nimalten\|Nimalten]], giving her a deep respect for those who hunt wilderness beasts.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]**: Regular guests at her wayside inn.

## Location
- Manages [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Sleeping_Stag\|The_Sleeping_Stag]] north of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]]**: Shared local rumors with the party regarding missing hunters and dark omens near the barrows.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]]**: Provided lodging and warm meals to the party during their regional scouting.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]]-**[[2 -  Sessions/Session 009\|Session 009]]**: Hosted the party before and after their harrowing expedition into the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Haunted_Hunting_Lodge\|Haunted_Hunting_Lodge]].
