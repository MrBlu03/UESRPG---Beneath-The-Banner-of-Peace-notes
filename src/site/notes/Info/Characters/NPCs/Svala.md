# Svala

## Description
A courageous, sharp-eyed huntress in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. Recognized the distorted, unnatural runes carved into tree trunks by the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Skinstealer]] and stood shoulder-to-shoulder with the party in the final battle.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]**: Close hunting partner and fellow tracker.
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]**: Fought alongside the party in the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Cursed_Grove\|Cursed Grove]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga_Cairn-Shield]]**: Her older sister, the town's huscarl.

## Location
- Resides in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]; ranges through the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Northern_Forest\|Northern Forest]].

## Notes
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]]**: Identified that the runes carved along the forest verge were distorted Nordic script, alerting the party to the supernatural nature of the predator. Led an initial scouting party that was forced to flee when the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Skinstealer]] howled, prompting her to rally a larger hunting party for dawn.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]]**: Tolled the Hunter's Bell to gather trackers and led the expedition into the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Cursed_Grove\|Cursed Grove]]. Helped [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] craft the protective perimeter and coordinate town defenses with her sister [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga]].
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]]**: Fought bravely during the final battle against the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]], successfully killing multiple unnatural wolves with well-aimed arrows. Survived the encounter unharmed and participated in the town's grateful celebration.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]**: Served warm mead to the party and later brought mead to the condemned [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Hlaalu\|Hlaalu]] smugglers as a final courtesy before their execution. Attended the celebratory feast at the Jarl's longhouse.
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 019\|Session 019]]**: Met the party briefly to exchange farewells alongside [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga]] as they departed [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] for [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
