# Svala

## Description
An experienced, resolute Nord huntress and leader of the hunting community in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. She is the younger sister of Huscarl [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga Cairn-Shield]]. Skilled with bow and spear, she possesses deep knowledge of forest tracking and ancient Nordic runes.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga Cairn-Shield]]**: Her protective elder sister and Huscarl of Falderstad.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]**: Collaborated closely with the elder mage to organize tactics against dark beasts.
- **The Fangs of Fjelhorst**: Led the joint expedition to hunt the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Skinstealer]].

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]** and the **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Northern_Forest\|Northern Forest]]**.

## Notes
- **Rune Identification & Rally ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]]):** Identified the distorted Nordic runes in the woods as unholy perversions of Kyne's blessing. Stood at the town podium tolling the hunting bell to assemble the hunters for the hunt.
- **Battle of the Cursed Grove ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]]):** Fought in the frontline battle against the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]], shooting down multiple attacking wolves and surviving unharmed.
- **Ceremonial Mead & Departure ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 019\|Session 019]]):** Served ceremonial warm mead to the party and condemned prisoners before the execution ritual, and bid farewell to the adventurers as they departed for Kjalgrad.
