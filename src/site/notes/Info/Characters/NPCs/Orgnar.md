# Orgnar

## Description
The gruff, stout, and dependable Nord innkeeper of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost_Gate_Hall]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Behind his stern demeanor lies a warm heart and fierce loyalty to honest travelers.

- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tarja\|Tarja]]**: Maintained discreet communication with resistance scouts.
