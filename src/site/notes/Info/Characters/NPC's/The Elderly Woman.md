# The Elderly Woman

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Unknown (referred to as "The Elderly Woman")
- **Status:** Appears only in dreams/visions
- **Location:** [[Info/Locations/Darnell Hollow\|Darnell Hollow]] manor grounds
- **First Appearance:** [[2 -  Sessions/Session 9 - 1 (Periwyn's slumber)\|Session 9 - 1 (Periwyn's slumber)]]

## Description
- **Physical:** Elderly woman with cloudy eyes that are sharp with pain
- **Demeanor:** Cryptic and mysterious, speaks in riddles
- **Nature:** Possibly a spirit tied to the manor or manifestation of [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s subconscious

## Role in Session 9.5
- **Initial Encounter:** First figure [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] meets on the road to [[Info/Locations/Darnell Hollow\|Darnell Hollow]]
- **Function:** Serves as a guide or gatekeeper figure in the dream sequence
- **Significance:** May represent the tragic history of the manor or the wisdom of those who have suffered

## Symbolism
- **The Gatekeeper:** Appears at the threshold of [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s deeper psychological journey
- **The Witness:** Represents those who have seen tragedy and carry its memory
- **The Guide:** May be leading [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] toward important revelations about herself

## Connections
- **[[Info/Locations/Darnell Hollow\|Darnell Hollow]]:** Appears connected to the manor's tragic history
- **[[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]:** Encountered during her comatose vision state
- **Dream Sequence:** Part of the larger symbolic journey through [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s psyche

## Current Status
- **Physical:** Unknown if she ever existed physically
- **Spiritual:** Exists only within [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s dream/vision
- **Purpose:** Fulfilled her role as initial guide in the dream sequence
