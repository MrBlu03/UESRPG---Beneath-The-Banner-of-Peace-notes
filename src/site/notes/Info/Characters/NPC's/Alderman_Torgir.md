# Alderman Torgir

## Description
[[Info/Characters/NPC's/Alderman_Torgir\|Alderman Torgir]] is the village leader of [[Info/Locations/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]], a fishing village that has dealt with water supply issues due to the sabotaged dam at [[Info/Locations/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod Estate]].

## Basic Information
- **Title**: Alderman
- **Location**: [[Info/Locations/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]]
- **Occupation**: Village administrator and leader

## Background
As alderman of [[Info/Locations/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]], Torgir has been dealing with the water crisis caused by the sabotaged [[Info/Locations/Snow-Shod_Dam\|Snow-Shod Dam]]. He has worked with the party to resolve this issue and owes them compensation for their services.

## Key Events
- **Early Sessions**: Involved in the water crisis investigation at [[Info/Locations/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]]
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: 
  - Party stopped to collect owed payment of 50 gold per person
  - [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] attempted to claim 60 gold per person was owed
  - Torgir correctly remembered the amount was 50 gold per person
  - Paid the party their owed compensation

## Relationships
- **The Party**: Professional relationship, contractor for village services
- **[[Info/Locations/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]] Residents**: Village leader and representative

## Personality
- Honest and fair in business dealings
- Good memory for financial obligations
- Not easily deceived by attempts to inflate owed amounts
- Practical and straightforward in approach

## Location
