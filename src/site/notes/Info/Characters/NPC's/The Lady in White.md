# The Lady in White

## Description
A dreamlike figure who haunts the chapel at Darnell Hollow in Periwyn's vision. She is said to ask for names and make people forget them, and is feared by the small girl in the grain mill.

## Appearance
- Wears white, ethereal and ghostly
- Associated with the chapel

## Role in Periwyn's Story
- Represents the danger of forgetting one's identity or past
- May be a spirit or a manifestation of Periwyn's fears

## First Appearance
- Session 9.5: Periwyn's Slumber (Darnell Hollow manor)
