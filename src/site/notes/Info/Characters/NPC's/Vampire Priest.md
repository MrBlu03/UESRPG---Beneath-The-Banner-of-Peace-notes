# Vampire Priest

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Unknown (referred to as "Vampire Priest")
- **Former Role:** Priest of [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]]
- **Current Status:** Deceased (given final rest by [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]])
- **Nature:** Vampire, but retains humanity and regret
- **Location:** Chapel at [[Info/Locations/Darnell Hollow\|Darnell Hollow]] (in dreams)
- **First Appearance:** [[2 -  Sessions/Session 009.1 (Periwyn's slumber)\|Session 009.1 (Periwyn's slumber)]]

## Description
- **Physical:** Pale and sunken features, showing the toll of vampirism
- **Clothing:** Wears the remains of [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]]'s robes, stained with wine/red blood
- **Holy Symbol:** Carries a burned and blackened holy symbol that dangles from one hand
- **Demeanor:** Tired and afraid, not aggressive or predatory
- **Notable:** Doesn't bear fangs or hiss, simply looks exhausted and tormented

## Background & Tragedy
### The Fall:
- Was once a devoted priest of [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]]
- Tried to keep his family alive "in bone and memory" after their deaths
- Was approached by an entity (likely connected to [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Loris\|Loris]]) who offered him "the power to remember them without pain"
- Accepted vampirism in exchange for this false promise
- Now feels nothing at all instead of being free from pain

### Connection to Gareth:
- **Past Guidance:** [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Gareth\|Gareth]] once tried to guide him, but the priest was "too afraid"
- **Failed Salvation:** Represents what might have happened if [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Gareth\|Gareth]] hadn't found mercy through [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]

## The Chapel Scene
### Setting:
- Found kneeling motionless at the end of the aisle behind the pulpit
- Black soul gem on the altar, pulsing with unseen veins
- Chapel's sacred air had gone stagnant "like water trapped in a crypt"

### His Story:
- **Key Quote:** *"He tried to guide me once. But I was too afraid. I kept them alive, my family, in bone and memory"*
- **The Bargain:** *"She came and offered me the power to remember them without pain. I said yes. But now I don't feel anything at all"*
- **The Realization:** *"You've been here before. You remember killing him. But there is another memory, another lesson"*

## Interaction with Periwyn
### Initial Response:
- [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s first instinct was to attack with the warhammer
- She hesitated, recognizing his humanity and suffering
- Chose mercy over vengeance, demonstrating [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Gareth\|Gareth]]'s lessons

### The Sanctification:
- [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] threw the warhammer aside
- Sanctified the altar, breaking the black soul gem
- Chapel pulsed with warm wind as his soul was freed

### His Final Words:
- **Gratitude:** *"Thank you for letting me be remembered as I was"*
- Crumbled into light, finally finding peace

## Symbolic Significance
- **Mercy vs Vengeance:** Represents the choice between destruction and compassion
- **Lost Faith:** Shows what happens when faith fails in the face of grief
- **Redemption:** Demonstrates that even the fallen can find peace through mercy
- **False Promises:** Illustrates how desperation can lead to accepting hollow bargains
- **[[Info/Characters/NPC's/Gareth\|Gareth]]'s Teaching:** Validates the lesson that mercy is more powerful than vengeance

## Relationship to Other Characters
- **[[Info/Characters/NPC's/Gareth\|Gareth]]:** Failed to accept guidance from him in the past
- **[[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]:** Recipient of her mercy and the means of his final rest
- **[[Info/Characters/NPC's/Loris\|Loris]]:** Likely connected to the entity that offered him the false bargain
- **His Family:** Motivated by grief and love for his deceased family members

## Theological Implications
- **Soul Gem:** His soul was trapped, preventing proper rest with [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]]
- **Sanctification:** [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s blessing freed his soul from Coldharbour
- **Divine Mercy:** Represents [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]]'s forgiveness even for those who have fallen
- **True Rest:** Finally achieved what he sought - peace and proper remembrance

## Current Status
- **Physical:** Destroyed/dissolved into light
- **Spiritual:** Soul freed from vampirism and given proper rest
- **Legacy:** His peaceful end validates [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s choice of mercy over vengeance
- **Memory:** Remembered as he was before his fall, as he requested
