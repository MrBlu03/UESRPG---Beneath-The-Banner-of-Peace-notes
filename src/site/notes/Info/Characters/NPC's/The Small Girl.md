# The Small Girl

## Description
A small girl with straw-colored hair encountered by Periwyn in the grain mill at Darnell Hollow during her dream/vision. She warns Periwyn about the Lady in White and seems to be a victim or witness of the manor's tragedies.

## Appearance
- Young, straw-colored hair
- Nervous, wary

## Role in Periwyn's Story
- Warns Periwyn about the Lady in White
- May represent innocence or lost childhood

## First Appearance
- Session 9.5: Periwyn's Slumber (Darnell Hollow manor)
