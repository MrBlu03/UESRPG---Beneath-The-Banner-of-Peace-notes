# Archmagus Mortgren

## Description
[[Info/Characters/NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]] is a mage who appeared briefly in Riften. His exact affiliation and position are unknown. According to the mysterious figure in [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s dream, he is much older than he appears and may be ancient.

## Key Events
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]]**: 
  - Observed in conversation with Queen Freidr about a prophecy and scroll
  - Informed [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] that her black feather was of magical origin and should be kept
  - Departed for the ruins on top of the mountain, a few days south of Riften

## Location
- Last known location: Heading to ruins south of Riften

## Notes
- First seen at the start of [[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]] in conversation with [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]], discussing a prophecy and a scroll. He mentioned heading to Fjellhorst to investigate whether "those interred within hadn't awoken.
- Has not been seen since his departure in Session 4

## Revelations from Session 9.5
According to the Mysterious Figure in [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s dream:
- **True Age:** Is "what you were before" - older than any alive, possibly ancient like Dunmer mages who are 2000+ years old
- **Alternative Names:** Referred to as "the high advisor, a Viking, Emmerich" suggesting multiple identities across time
- **Magical Specialization:** May be connected to necromancy and bone manipulation
- **Historical Connection:** Helped liberate [[Info/Locations/North_Point\|North Point]] after [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] was taken
- **Mysterious Knowledge:** The figure knows him personally, suggesting significant supernatural connections

## Implications
- His youthful appearance may be magically maintained
- May have lived through multiple historical periods
- Possibly has extensive knowledge of necromancy and undead
- Connected to [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s past and the events at [[Info/Locations/North_Point\|North Point]]
- His interest in the prophecy and scroll may be more significant than initially apparent
