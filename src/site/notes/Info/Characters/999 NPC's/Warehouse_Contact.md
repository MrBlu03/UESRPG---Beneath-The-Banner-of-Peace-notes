# Warehouse Contact

## Basic Information
**Race:** Unknown  
**Gender:** Unknown  
**Title:** None known  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District\|Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District]], [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Faction:** Unknown — suspected resistance network or Strider operative  
**Status:** Alive

## Description
A suspiciously well-dressed individual working in or around [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]'s warehouse district. This person stands out as incongruous among typical dock and warehouse workers, suggesting a cover identity. When the party investigated [[Warehouse 7\|Warehouse 7]], this contact approached and made a veiled offer, describing themselves as someone with **"friends in low places"** — language consistent with underworld or resistance contacts.

## Physical Description
- Notably well-dressed for a warehouse environment
- Appearance incongruous with the labor setting
- Specific features unknown

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]] — Warehouse 7
During the party's investigation of Warehouse 7:
- The contact made themselves known to **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]**
- Used the phrase **"friends in low places"** — suggesting ties to a criminal network, resistance cell, or the Strider network
- Did not reveal their affiliation directly
- The contact's appearance suggested they were watching the warehouse or the party

## Analysis

### "Friends in Low Places"
This phrase is notable in context. The party is operating in a city controlled by the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]], where citizens are disappearing for speaking out. A well-dressed operative in a warehouse district using this language could represent:

- A **Strider network** operative (the underground resistance helping people avoid Guild control)
- A **Thieves Guild** contact
- An operative from a rival organization monitoring the Guild's warehouse activities
- A [[Info/Organizations/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] agent

### Significance of the Encounter
The contact appeared immediately after the party discovered suspicious sealed crates containing **[[Info/1 - Party Findings/Coin_Molds\|Coin_Molds]]**, gemstones, chalk, black candles, and animal parts. The timing suggests either:
- The contact was already watching Warehouse 7 and intervened when the party got close
- The contact was tipped off to the party's presence

## Current Status
The contact's identity and allegiance remain unknown. They represent a potential ally — or a threat — depending on who they work for.

## Notes
- The party did not follow up with this contact in Session 023
- Their knowledge of "low places" suggests they may know about the disappearing citizens
- Should be approached cautiously — they may be testing the party's loyalties
