# Ingvar Snow-Shod

## Description
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Ingvar_Snow-shod\|Ingvar Snow-Shod]] is a Thane who owns [[Info/Locations/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod Estate]] near [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]].
## Role in the Story
As the owner of [[Info/Locations/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod Estate]], Ingvar was involved in the water dispute with [[Info/Locations/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]] due to the sabotaged dam on his property.
