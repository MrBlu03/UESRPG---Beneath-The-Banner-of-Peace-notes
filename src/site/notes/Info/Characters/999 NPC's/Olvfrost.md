# Olvfrost

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Male  
**Title:** Lumber Baron  
**Location:** Near **Fort Morven Scott**, Eastmarch  
**Business:** Lumber Trade  
**Status:** Alive

## Description
Olvfrost is a burly Nord lumber baron who operates near **Fort Morven Scott** in the Eastmarch region of Skyrim. He is a straightforward, somewhat drunk but friendly merchant who makes deals with anyone he considers a "wonderful mutual acquaintance" through the Guildmaster.

## Business Operations
- **Primary Trade**: Lumber
- **Location**: Operations near Fort Morven Scott
- **Market**: Sells lumber throughout Skyrim
- **Trade Routes**: Can transport lumber "all the way from here to Whiterun"
- **Volume**: Large-scale operations supplying major cities

## Personality
- **Direct and Burly**: Typical Nord merchant - physically imposing and straightforward
- **Slightly Drunk**: Was visibly intoxicated during the trade event
- **Friendly**: Warm and welcoming to fellow merchants
- **Business-Minded**: Always looking for new trade agreements
- **Formulaic**: Uses the same approach and offers with multiple potential partners

## Interaction with Periwyn (Session 022)

### Approach at the Trade Event
- Approached **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** at the Guild event with tankard in hand
- Asked if she was "another merchant here for business"
- When Periwyn mentioned restarting her family business after "some... massacre":
  - He had heard of it
  - Expressed understanding about wanting to move far from home

### The Lumber Deal
After learning Periwyn was starting fresh:
- Offered her a **signed trade agreement**
- Cited "mutual acquaintance" ([[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Jorvik_Hanison\|Jorvik Hanison]]) as reason for trust
- Document guarantees **profitable trade of lumber** from Fort Morven Scott area **all the way to Whiterun**
- Gave her the signed paper immediately
- Stumbled off to make the same offer to others

## Significance
Olvfrost represents:
- Legitimate business opportunities outside the corrupt Guild's direct control
- Access to raw materials (lumber) for Periwyn's rebuilding efforts
- The party now has a **written trade agreement** establishing credibility
- Proof that the Guild of the Silverroad facilitates normal trade alongside its corruption
- Potential supply chain for any business Periwyn establishes

## The Signed Agreement
The document Olvfrost gave Periwyn:
- Establishes a **profitable lumber trade route**
- From Fort Morven Scott area to Whiterun
- Based on "mutual acquaintance" with Guildmaster [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Jorvik_Hanison\|Jorvik Hanison]]
- Gives Periwyn tangible evidence of legitimate business dealings
- Could be used to secure loans, partnerships, or other agreements

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: New business contact - gave her a signed lumber trade agreement
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Jorvik_Hanison\|Jorvik Hanison]]**: Mutual acquaintance - Olvfrost uses this connection to establish trust
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Renald_Vero\|Renald Vero]]**: Fellow merchant at the same event

## Strategic Value
- Provides immediate legitimate trade documentation for Periwyn
- Lumber is essential for construction, crafting, and trade
- Trade route from Fort Morven Scott to Whiterun covers significant territory
- Written agreement gives Periwyn something concrete to show other potential partners
- Connection to the lumber industry opens additional business opportunities

## Notes
- Clearly intoxicated during the event but still conducted business
- Uses a standard approach with potential partners (citing Guildmaster as mutual acquaintance)
- The deal may be somewhat informal given his drunken state
- Fort Morven Scott is in the far north of Eastmarch
