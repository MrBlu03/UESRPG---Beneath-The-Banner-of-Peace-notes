# Bjorn (Hunter)

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Male  
**Occupation:** Hunter  
**Location:** [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Faction:** Independent

## Description
Bjorn is a hunter operating in the area around [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. He has a distinctive appearance with a "Green Arrow" style facial hair—featuring a slightly longer goatee and mustache combination. He wears studded leather and fur, typical of hunters in the region.

## Key Events

### [[Session 023a\|Session 023a]] - The Witch Bounty
Bjorn posted a bounty on the bounty board regarding a dangerous "witch of the woods" that had been attacking hunters and their livestock west of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. He met with [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] at the tavern to discuss the contract.

**The Attack:**
- While hunting west of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], Bjorn's horse was attacked by fire magic
- The creature has killed several people in recent days
- Bjorn and his hunting companions fled the encounter
- His horse did not survive the attack

**Information Provided:**
- Gave [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] a rough map showing the creature's territorial range
- Marked the location of their abandoned camp on the map
- Explained that the creature appears territorial and doesn't venture far from its domain
- Offered proof of completion for any hunter who could deal with the threat

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Contracted her to deal with the witch threat
- **Hunting Companions**: Fellow hunters who fled the creature with him

## Significance
Bjorn represents the local hunters affected by supernatural threats around [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. His bounty posting led to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] discovering and negotiating with the witch, establishing a potential peaceful resolution rather than combat.

## Notes
- Values practical solutions over bloodshed (accepted non-lethal resolution)
- Willing to provide proof of completion for negotiated settlements
- Operates within the hunter community around [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]
