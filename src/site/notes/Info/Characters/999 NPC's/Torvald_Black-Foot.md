# Torvald Black-Foot

## Description
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torvald_Black-Foot\|Torvald Black-Foot]] is a prominent merchant affiliated with the [[Info/Organizations/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Black-Foot Merchant Guild]]. He regularly hires [[The Fangs of Fjelhorst\|The Fangs of Fjelhorst]] for security and investigation work involving threats to his merchant operations. Known for conducting business at the Hall of Ysmir and maintaining an office in [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]].

## Relationships
- Associated with the [[Info/Organizations/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Black-Foot Merchant Guild]]
- Employer of [[The Fangs of Fjelhorst\|The Fangs of Fjelhorst]]
- Has contacts throughout the merchant network for intelligence gathering

## Recent Activities
**[[2 -  Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]**: Hired the party to investigate Dunmer criminals (Camonna Tong) harassing his dock workers and breaking into warehouses. Paid 280 gold each for eliminating the threat and recovering a mysterious lockbox. Expressed concern about ships with house dress spotted in the Sea of Ghosts.
