# Ingrin

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Female  
**Age:** Adult  
**Occupation:** Huntress  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Faction:** Independent

## Description
Ingrin is a huntress from [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] who previously discovered the bone clearing that became a key piece of intelligence about the Skinstealer's territory. She participated in the organized hunt but became one of the Skinstealer's targets through its voice mimicry ability.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]] - The Skinstealer Hunt

#### Pre-Hunt Intelligence
- Had previously found the clearing with bones
- This discovery provided crucial information about the Skinstealer's shrine location
- Her reconnaissance helped the hunting party know where to search

#### The Hunt
- Joined [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]'s organized expedition with other hunters
- Participated in journey to the grotesque clearing
- Present when [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] identified the Ragged Lord shrine
- Witnessed the bone monolith and human skin displays

### The Voice Mimicry Attack

#### During the Retreat
When the Skinstealer trapped the party in its supernatural maze:
- Stopped suddenly and asked the group, "Do you hear anything?"
- The party heard **her voice** coming from the trees ahead
- The voice whispered "over here" trying to lure people away
- **The Skinstealer was mimicking her voice to separate the group**

#### Panic Response
- Panicked when she heard her own voice being used
- Tried to run in fear and confusion
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] tackled her** to prevent separation from the group
- He calmed her down and kept her with the party
- This intervention likely saved her life, as [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]] was killed after being separated

## Character Traits
- **Brave**: Willing to venture into dangerous territory for reconnaissance
- **Resourceful**: Discovered important intelligence about the threat
- **Vulnerable to Psychological Attacks**: The voice mimicry affected her deeply
- **Survivor**: Thanks to quick intervention, avoided [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]]'s fate

## Hunting Skills
- **Scout**: Capable of finding and mapping dangerous locations
- **Explorer**: Ventured into the cursed area to find the bone clearing
- **Intelligence Gathering**: Provided actionable information to hunting party

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]**: Hunting leader who organized the expedition
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]**: Saved her from separation and likely death
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]**: Fellow hunter who used her intelligence about the clearing
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]]**: Fellow hunter who wasn't as fortunate
- **Hunting Party**: Colleague of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]]

## The Skinstealer's Tactics
Ingrin's experience revealed critical information about the Skinstealer's hunting methods:
- **Voice Mimicry**: Can perfectly replicate human voices
- **Separation Strategy**: Uses mimicked voices to isolate prey
- **Psychological Warfare**: Creates confusion and panic
- **Targeted**: Chooses specific individuals to mimic

## Significance
Ingrin represents both the value of reconnaissance and the psychological terror of facing the Skinstealer. Her prior discovery of the bone clearing was essential intelligence, but her experience with voice mimicry revealed one of the creature's most dangerous abilities. Her survival, thanks to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]'s quick action, demonstrates the importance of staying together when facing the Skinstealer.

## Survival Factors
- **Quick Reflexes of Allies**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]'s immediate response
- **Physical Restraint**: Being tackled prevented her from running into danger
- **Group Cohesion**: Staying with the party after being calmed
- **Panic Recognition**: The group recognized the danger she was in
