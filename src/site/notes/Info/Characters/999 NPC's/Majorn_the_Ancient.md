# Majorn the Ancient

## Description
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]] is a rogue vampire of [[Info/Organizations/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan_Volkihar]] lineage, believed to have been killed 20 years ago during the Planemeld but has apparently survived. Unlike typical Volkihar vampires, Majorn was always restless and impatient, seeking dominion rather than isolation.

## Relationships
- **Former Clan Volkihar Member**: Served in Harkon's court approximately a century ago
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]]**: Has personal history with Majorn, crossed paths multiple times though never as allies
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Thrysnr_the_Unsullied\|Thrysnr_the_Unsullied]]**: One of his vampire progeny, recently killed by the party
- **Possible [[Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] Connection**: May be allied with or leading the serpent cult activities

## Key Characteristics
- **Opportunistic**: Interested in Daedric Princes but not as a servant - seeks partnerships of convenience
- **Ambitious**: Always sought movement, reach, and influence beyond Volkihar's isolationist ways
- **Cunning**: Survived his supposed death and has likely been building power in secret
- **Dangerous**: According to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]], his name still carries weight in certain circles

## Current Status
- **Presumed Active**: Evidence suggests he has been building loyalty, seeding blood, and creating something in the darkness
- **Location Unknown**: Likely operating from shadows, possibly connected to recent vampire and serpent cult activities
- **Threat Level**: Extremely High - [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]] warns he cannot be slain by steel alone, requires patience, knowledge, and precision

## Historical Events
- **Century Ago**: Member of Harkon's court in [[Info/Organizations/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan_Volkihar]]
- **20 Years Ago**: Believed killed in Wittestadr during the Planemeld
- **Recent**: Revealed as sire of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Thrysnr_the_Unsullied\|Thrysnr_the_Unsullied]], indicating survival and continued vampire creation

## Warnings from [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]]
- His name should not be spoken carelessly as it may draw dangerous attention
- He does not rise for small things - his return indicates major plans
- He is not merely a beast but a predator who already knows his prey's scent
- Any confrontation requires careful preparation and knowledge

## Notes
- Represents a significant escalation in the vampire threat to the [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Rift]]
- His survival and apparent connection to the [[Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] cult suggests a major conspiracy
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]] will remain in [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] to research and prepare defenses
