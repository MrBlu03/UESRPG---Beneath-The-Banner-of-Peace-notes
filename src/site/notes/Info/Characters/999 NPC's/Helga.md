# Helga

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Female  
**Occupation:** Huscarl (Deputy/Lieutenant)  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] Jarl's Hall  
**Faction:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] Government

## Description
Helga serves as the huscarl to the Jarl of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], acting as his deputy and handling official business when he is unavailable. She represents the practical authority of the Jarl's administration and demonstrates competent leadership in managing local crises.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]] - Frozen Hunter Investigation
- Met with the party when the Jarl was unavailable due to illness (bone rot)
- Provided crucial information about the frozen hunter incident
- Directed the party to investigate [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]], the sole survivor who had fled the scene
- Described [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]] as a scruffy man with short hair and a two-braided brown beard
- Received the party's final report on their investigation findings

## Relationships
- **The Jarl**: Serves as huscarl and deputy when he is incapacitated
- **[[The_Party\|The_Party]]**: Provided official cooperation in investigating the frozen hunter incident
- **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] Guards**: Works with local security forces in managing city affairs

## Significance
Helga represents competent local governance in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], stepping up to handle critical situations when leadership is compromised. Her willingness to work with the party demonstrates the city's openness to outside assistance in solving serious threats.

- Acts with Jarl's authority when he is unavailable
- Manages crisis situations and public safety concerns
- Coordinates with visiting investigators and adventurers
- Oversees information gathering and official responses to threats
