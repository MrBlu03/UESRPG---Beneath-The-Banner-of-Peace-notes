# Darius Karo

## Basic Information
**Race:** Imperial  
**Gender:** Male  
**Title:** Steward to the Jarl of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Location:** [[Clan Morvan Longhouse\|Clan Morvan Longhouse]], [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Faction:** Clan Morvan (official), [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] (suspected)  
**Status:** Alive

## Description
Darius Karo is the Imperial steward who effectively runs [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] in place of the largely ceremonial Jarl. He operates from the [[Clan Morvan Longhouse\|Clan Morvan Longhouse]] and handles the actual governance of the city. According to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]], he is likely in the pocket of the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]], the merchant organization that bankrolls the city's guards and controls prices, tolls, and passages.

## Physical Description
- Imperial man
- Stood beside the empty Jarl's chair in the longhouse
- Projects authority despite being a steward rather than the Jarl

## Role in Kjalgrad
- **Official Title**: Jarl's Steward
- **Actual Role**: De facto ruler of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]
- **The Jarl**: Functions as a figurehead only
- **Power Base**: Likely derives from [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] backing

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]] - Meeting at the Longhouse

**Summoning the Party:**
- Sent guards to summon the [[Fangs of Fjelhorst\|Fangs of Fjelhorst]] to the longhouse
- Asked where the fourth member was ([[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]])
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] lied, claiming [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] was ill

**Assessment of the Party:**
- Found it suspicious that "merchants" were dressed like mercenaries
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] responded by invoking her family name (**Lunclaire**) and claiming to rebuild their trade empire

**The Job Offer:**
- Described as a "rat problem"
- Target: A Dunmer ([[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] in disguise) accusing citizens of Daedric dealings
- Claimed target was part of the Fighters Guild and causing trouble

**Payment Terms:**
- **750 gold per person** - if target brought in alive
- **500 gold per person** - if only his head is delivered

**Provided Information:**
- A sketch of the target ("Sadrith")
- Location: Old [[Blackfoot Merchants Guild House\|Blackfoot Merchants Guild House]]

**Party's Response:**
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] initially pushed back on being treated as mercenaries
- Eventually accepted the job
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] was visibly displeased with his disrespect and had to restrain herself

## Political Analysis

### Power Structure in Kjalgrad
According to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]]:
- The [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] bankrolls the guards, not the Jarl
- The guild controls prices, tolls, and passages
- The Jarl doesn't do much - mostly ceremonial
- Darius Karo does most of the actual governance
- **Implication**: Darius Karo is likely controlled by or aligned with the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]

### Suspicious Elements
- Hired outsiders to deal with someone investigating Daedric activity
- The target ([[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]) was actually uncovering corruption
- May be attempting to silence legitimate investigation
- Connection to [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] raises questions about his motives

## Personality
- **Condescending**: Treated the party with mild disdain
- **Pragmatic**: Willing to hire "merchants dressed like mercenaries"
- **Authoritative**: Comfortable giving orders from beside the Jarl's throne
- **Calculating**: Uses bounty hunters rather than guards for sensitive matters

## Relationships
- **Jarl of Kjalgrad**: Nominal superior, but Darius holds actual power
- **[[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]**: Suspected puppeteer
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]**: Unknowingly placed a bounty on a legendary hero

## Significance
Darius Karo represents the corruption in [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]'s leadership. While ostensibly serving the Jarl, he appears to be a tool of the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]], and his attempt to eliminate "Sadrith" suggests he may be working to protect Daedric-connected interests in the city.

## Location
- **[[Clan Morvan Longhouse\|Clan Morvan Longhouse]]**: Primary location, stands beside the Jarl's chair

## Notes
- The party accepted his bounty but discovered the target was actually [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]
- Unknown if they will report back to him or maintain their alliance with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]
- His connection to the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] makes him potentially complicit in the Cult of Clavicus Vile activities
