# Gabrielle Bonilla

## Basic Information
**Race:** Breton  
**Gender:** Female  
**Occupation:** Mages Guild Expert on Daedric Artifacts  
**Location:** En route to [[Info/Locations/Ayr\|Ayr]] (via [[Windhelm\|Windhelm]])  
**Faction:** [[Info/Organizations/Mages_Guild\|Mages Guild]]  
**Rank:** High-ranking member

## Description
Gabrielle Bonilla is a thin Breton woman who wears a distinctive **blue and gold cloak** marking her as a member of the [[Info/Organizations/Mages_Guild\|Mages Guild]]. She serves as the guild's lead expert on Daedric artifacts and relics, possessing extensive knowledge of dangerous magical items and their containment.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]] - The Harrowstorm Revelation

#### Arrival in Falderstad (10th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600)
- Arrived in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] searching for "The Fangs of Fjelhørst"
- Was directed to the Jarl's longhouse, then to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] shadowed her through the market before making contact
- Brought to meet the rest of the party at their house

#### Critical Intelligence Delivered
Revealed the true nature of the Ritual Orb discovered at [[Info/Locations/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]:
- **Identification**: The orb is a **Harrowstorm Engine Core**
- **Origin**: A remnant from the [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]] crisis 14-15 years ago
- **Grey Host Background**: A clan of vampires and werewolves who planned to create undead vampire-werewolf hybrids
- **Status**: The core is **dormant** - cannot activate without proper rituals, matrices, and energy source
- **History**: Originally taken from [[Info/Locations/Markarth\|Markarth]]; only core not retrieved until now
- **Current Containment**: Wrapped in multiple layers of **anti-magic wards**
- **Transport**: Departed [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] 1-2 days ago for [[Windhelm\|Windhelm]], then to [[Info/Locations/Ayr\|Ayr]]
- **Explanation**: The death/energy around [[Info/Locations/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] likely caused it to briefly awaken when [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] shot it

#### Encounter with Baron Jangleplume
- Needed to send a letter and asked about messenger birds
- Recognized the Daedric crows when [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] summoned [[Baron Jangleplume\|Baron Jangleplume]]
- **Identified them as**: Daedric crows - immortal, teleporting crows that can talk
- **Personal history**: Jangleplume once brought her a **fake soup spoon** when she was in the **Clockwork City**
- Mentioned the crows are curious creatures associated with **Nocturnal**

## Expertise
- **Daedric Artifacts**: Primary specialization
- **Magical Containment**: Knowledge of anti-magic wards and artifact storage
- **Historical Knowledge**: Familiar with Grey Host crisis and its remnants
- **Daedric Entities**: Recognizes Daedric creatures like the immortal crows

## Current Mission
