# Senya

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Female  
**Occupation:** Skald (Warrior Poet)  
**Location:** Travelling (visited [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] for execution)  
**Status:** Alive

## Description
Senya is one half of a pair of twin skalds, along with her sister [[Sonja\|Sonja]]. They are "Warrior Poets" who travel to important events like executions to provide ceremonial music and record events in Nordic tradition. They play the **Tagelharpa** (Swedish Horse Lyre).

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]] - The Nordic Execution

#### Meeting in the Market (9th of Sun's Dusk)
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] spotted Senya and [[Sonja\|Sonja]] in the [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] market
- Revealed they were in town for an **execution** in 2-3 days
- Explained they are "Skalds" (Warrior Poets), not just bards
- Discussed their instrument: the **Tagelharpa**
- Agreed to meet [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] at [[Info/Locations/The_Soggy_Beaver\|The_Soggy_Beaver]] later

#### Performance at The Soggy Beaver (9th of Sun's Dusk)
- Performed traditional Nordic songs with [[Sonja\|Sonja]]
- Included a humorous 16th-century song about a farmer outsmarting the church
- Took a break during which [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] performed her lute

#### The Execution Procession (11th of Sun's Dusk)
- Walked with [[Sonja\|Sonja]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Agni\|Agni]] in the procession (after [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Tarik\|Tarik]], before [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valor\|Valor]])
- Performed "The Song of the Wolf" during the march to the [[Info/Locations/Standing_Stones_Execution_Site\|Standing Stones]]

#### Post-Execution Chant
After each execution, Senya chanted:
> *"Blood for blood, oath for oath. Shor's law holds, some flesh is loath. Kyne bear the wind and the dead man's breath, to Sovngarde or to Orkey's death."*

#### Closing Words
As the bodies burned:
> *"Lore stands and blood cools. The wind bears the truth, Allfather. Skyrim sleeps unashamed. The gods have seen and nodded."*

#### Music Lessons
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] asked if Senya and [[Sonja\|Sonja]] could stay a day or two to teach her music
- They agreed to stay longer in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
