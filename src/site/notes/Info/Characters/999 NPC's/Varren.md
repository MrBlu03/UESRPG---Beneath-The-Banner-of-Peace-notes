# Varren

## Basic Information
**Race:** Imperial  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** Mid-50s  
**Occupation:** Gold Distributor / Agent  
**Location:** Currently with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] (recovering)  
**Faction:** [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]  
**Status:** Alive, turned double agent

## Description
Varren is an Imperial man in his mid-50s who was distributing **brimstone gold** (gold originating from Oblivion) throughout [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]'s markets. He works for the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] and communicated with a mysterious figure via a **Daedric speaking stone**. After being captured and interrogated by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] and the [[Fangs of Fjelhorst\|Fangs of Fjelhorst]], he was turned into a double agent.

## Physical Description
- Imperial male
- Mid-50s in age
- Appearance captured in an extremely detailed sketch by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]

## Activities in Kjalgrad

### The Brimstone Gold Operation
- Purchases goods throughout the [[Grand Market\|Grand Market]]
- Pays significantly more than items are worth (overpaying dramatically)
- Uses **brimstone-tainted gold** for all transactions
- The gold is warm to the touch and smells of sulfur/brimstone
- Looks very pleased with himself after each transaction

### Communication Method
- Uses a **Daedric speaking stone** to report to superiors
- Stone is different from normal speaking stones - etched with **Daedric runes**
- Communicates with a humanoid but inhuman projection
- Reports purchases made and gold distributed
- Meeting location: Dead-end tunnel in [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] sewers

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]] - Surveillance and Capture

**In the Market:**
- Spotted by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] buying a backpack
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] tailed him through the market
- Observed buying provisions and a coat
- Paid with large sacks of gold - far more than necessary
- Looked pleased after each transaction

**In the Sewers:**
- Entered through a sewer grate in an alleyway
- Walked hurriedly with purpose
- Reached a dead-end and activated his speaking stone
- Reported his activities to a strange projection
- The entity in the projection appeared humanoid but "weird"

**Confrontation:**
- Turned to find the party watching him
- Claimed to be from **House Tharn** (a lie)
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] paralyzed him to prevent escape

**Daedric Attack:**
- A massive bipedal Daedric crocodile creature attacked
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] appeared and killed it with a thrown dagger
- Revealed Varren had lied about House Tharn

**Interrogation:**
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] tortured and intimidated him
- Varren defiantly headbutted [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] and hurled insults
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] teleported away with him
- Returned moments later - Varren was hurled against a wall
- Varren appeared horrified and broken

**Confession:**
- Started as simple buying and provisioning
- Was told the gold was for "checking quality"
- Gold was warm to the touch from the beginning
- After a few weeks, told to meet someone in the sewers
- The contact was "higher up" in the organization
- Mentioned **"The Grand Architect"** - said "he would be pleased"
- Works for the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]

**Turned Agent:**
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] convinced him to act as a double agent
- Will continue his role as normal while reporting to the party
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] took him to recover physically and mentally
- Will return when ready to resume his cover

- **Confession**: Confirmed [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] involvement

## Significance
Varren is a low-level operative in a much larger conspiracy. His role was to distribute Daedric gold throughout the economy of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], likely as part of a broader scheme by the **Cult of Clavicus Vile**. His capture and turning provides the party with an inside source on the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]'s activities.
