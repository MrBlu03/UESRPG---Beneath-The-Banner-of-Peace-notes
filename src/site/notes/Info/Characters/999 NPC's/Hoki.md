# Hoki

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** Young  
**Occupation:** Hunter  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Faction:** Independent

## Description
Hoki is a young, impulsive hunter from [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] who lacks the experience and wisdom of veteran hunters like [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]. His eagerness and determination during the [[Info/Quests/White_Stag_Hunt\|White_Stag_Hunt]] led him into supernatural danger, resulting in a traumatic encounter that left him severely shaken.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]] - The White Stag Hunt
- Participated in hunt with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]], [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]], and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]
- Accompanied by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Brovin\|Brovin]] the hunting dog
- Demonstrated impulsive and obsessive behavior during the hunt

#### Dangerous Pursuit
- Ignored warnings from [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] about entering dangerous supernatural territory
- Pressed on despite [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]'s warning that "this forest isn't Kyne's forest"
- Continued pursuit despite environmental warning signs (carved totems, sweet rot smell)
- Ignored [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Brovin\|Brovin]]'s refusal to continue, showing poor judgment

#### Restraint Attempts
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] threw snowball to get his attention
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] attempted to use paralysis dart but missed
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] tried to physically trip him to stop his advance
- Dodged [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s second paralysis dart attempt
- Eventually stopped only when reaching the dying white stag

### Traumatic Experience
- Witnessed the dying white stag with five deep claw marks
- Present when [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] spotted supernatural blood patterns
- Saw the stag's final moments before it died
- Stood defeated with thousand-yard stare after the encounter
- Became terrified when supernatural howl echoed around them

### Aftermath
- Fled with the group when chased by unseen creature
- Remained terrified from the experience
- Received a slap from [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] while in traumatic state
- Displayed signs of severe psychological trauma

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]] - Protected from the Hunt
- Initially gathered at market when [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] tolled the hunting bell
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] worked together to dissuade him from joining the Skinstealer hunt
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] gave him a stern talking to, but her demeanor was completely different
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s approach was warm and soft, contrasting her usual cold sternness
- Successfully convinced to stay behind for his own safety
- Showed he can be reasoned with when approached with care

#### After the Hunt (Session 016)
- Approached the party when they returned from the hunt
- Informed that [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]] had been killed by the Skinstealer
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] looked very sad and regretful when speaking to him
- She told him, "There's nothing you could have done"
- This acknowledgment showed respect for his desire to help while protecting him from danger

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]] - Celebrating the Victory
**At the Market Steps:**
- Present when the party returned from defeating the Ragged Lord
- Clutching a wooden stag carving he had made
- Eyes wide with joy and relief
- **Spoke to the party**: *"You did it, didn't you? I felt it. The dogs stopped howling!"*
- Witnessed the sun's return and the forest's healing

**Interaction with [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]:**
- Approached [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] while she was sketching the aurora-lit celebrations
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] showed **rare warmth and smile** when he approached
- This contrasted sharply with her usual cold demeanor
- Demonstrated [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s protective, almost maternal care for the young hunter

## Character Development
- **Trauma Recovery**: From terrified survivor to joyful celebrant
- **Artistic Expression**: Carved wooden stag to honor the white stag's sacrifice
- **Perceptive**: Could sense the forest's healing and supernatural change
- **Resilient**: Able to celebrate despite the trauma of Session 015 and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]]'s death
- **Connection to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: One of the few people who brings out her softer side

## Character Traits
- **Impulsive**: Acts without considering consequences or heeding warnings
- **Obsessive**: Becomes fixated on objectives regardless of danger
- **Inexperienced**: Lacks wisdom to recognize supernatural threats
- **Determined**: Persistent in pursuit of goals, sometimes to his detriment
- **Vulnerable**: Susceptible to traumatic psychological impact

## Hunting Skills
- Basic tracking and hunting abilities
- Lacks experience with supernatural encounters
- Poor judgment regarding danger assessment
- Needs guidance from more experienced hunters
- Demonstrates physical capability but poor decision-making

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]**: Works under guidance of experienced hunter who tries to protect him
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Brovin\|Brovin]]**: Hunting dog shows better supernatural instincts than he does
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Party members who attempted to restrain his dangerous behavior
- **Hunting Community**: Part of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] hunting groups

## Impact of Supernatural Encounter
- Severely traumatized by cursed grove experience
- Developed thousand-yard stare and terror response
- May require time and support to recover from psychological trauma
- Experience likely to affect future hunting activities and confidence
- Serves as warning about supernatural dangers in the area

## Significance
Hoki represents the vulnerability of inexperienced individuals when facing supernatural threats. His traumatic experience during the [[Info/Quests/White_Stag_Hunt\|White_Stag_Hunt]] demonstrates the psychological impact of encounters with territorial creatures and serves as a cautionary example of what happens when warnings about supernatural dangers are ignored.
