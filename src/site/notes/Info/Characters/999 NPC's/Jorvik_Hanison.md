# Jorvik Hanison

## Basic Information
**Race:** Unknown  
**Gender:** Male (assumed)  
**Title:** **Guildmaster** of the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]  
**Location:** [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Faction:** [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] (Leader)  
**Status:** Alive

## Description
Jorvik Hanison is the **Guildmaster** of the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]], making him the ultimate authority within the guild. He commands a powerful merchant organization that has effectively taken control of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] through economic influence, controlling the city's guards, prices, tolls, and passages.

## Position and Power

### Guild Leadership
- **Guildmaster** - highest authority in the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]
- Commands the entire organization
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]** (Trade Advocate) reports directly to him
- Oversees all guild operations in the region

### Control of Kjalgrad
According to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]] ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]]):
- The guild **bankrolls the city guards** (not the Jarl)
- Controls **prices, tolls, and passages**
- The Jarl is effectively a figurehead
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]]** (Jarl's Steward) likely answers to guild interests
- The guild has purchased political control of the city

### Economic Power
- Commands a wealthy merchant organization
- Controls trade routes (the "Silverroad" likely refers to their trade network)
- Has enough money to maintain a city's entire guard force
- Can set prices and tolls throughout [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]
- Significant influence over the regional economy

## Potential Involvement in Conspiracy

### Circumstantial Evidence
While Jorvik has not been directly connected to the conspiracy, his position makes him highly suspect:

**The Brimstone Gold Operation:**
- Occurring under his guild's operations
- Distributed by guild agent [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]]
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]** (who reports to Jorvik) likely aware of finances
- Difficult to imagine this happening without Guildmaster knowledge

**Cult of Clavicus Vile Connection:**
- The guild is connected to the cult
- Someone **high-ranking** wears the **Ouroboros necklace** ([[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] symbol)
- As Guildmaster, Jorvik is the highest-ranking member
- Could be the Ouroboros wearer himself

**Political Control:**
- The guild controls [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] politically and economically
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] attempted to eliminate [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] who was investigating
- This level of corruption likely requires Guildmaster approval

**"The Grand Architect":**
- Authority figure mentioned by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]]
- Could refer to Jorvik as "architect" of guild's success
- Or Jorvik could be answering to this mysterious figure

### Unknown Factors
- His direct involvement in the brimstone gold operation
- Whether he wears the Ouroboros necklace
- His knowledge of "The Grand Architect"
- If he's aware of Daedric connections or merely a tool
- His relationship to the **Cult of Clavicus Vile**

- **Extremely dangerous** if confronted directly

- Controls the city's law enforcement
- Can make people disappear legally or otherwise

## Strategic Importance
Jorvik Hanison is the **key target** for understanding the conspiracy:
- If he's complicit, he knows about brimstone gold, the cult, and possibly "The Grand Architect"
- If he's ignorant, someone beneath him is running a massive operation without his knowledge (unlikely)
- Bringing him down could collapse the guild's operations
- But confronting him prematurely could endanger the entire party
