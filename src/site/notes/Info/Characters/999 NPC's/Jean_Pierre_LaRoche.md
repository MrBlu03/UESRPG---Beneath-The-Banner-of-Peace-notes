# Jean-Pierre LaRoche

## Basic Information
**Full Title:** His Illustrious Magnificence Jean-Pierre LaRoche, Marquis of the Sapphire Coast, Baron of the Nine Rooms, Keeper of the Royal Cocoon Seals, and Patriarch of House LaRoche  
**Race:** Breton  
**Gender:** Male  
**Profession:** Silk merchant  
**Location:** Unknown — last seen [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Status:** Missing (over one week)  
**Wife:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Lady_Aine\|Lady_Aine]]  

## Description
A Breton silk merchant operating out of [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]], identifiable by his **memorable curling moustache**. A man of some social standing — invited to the Jarl's banquets, with an extensive set of noble titles. His family business appears to be silk, and he maintains a market stall in [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

## Disappearance

### Timeline
- **Attended:** The Jarl's banquet (exact date unconfirmed, but shortly before his disappearance)
- **Noted change:** His wife reported he was mildly absent-minded following the banquet
- **Last seen:** Leaving home on the morning he was due to open his market stall
- **His stall:** Still standing in the market with **frozen fabrics** abandoned on display
- **Wife's conclusion:** He would never leave his silks unattended voluntarily

### Reported By
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Lady_Aine\|Lady_Aine]] (his wife) approached [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] at the market protest in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]] as a Guild complaints officer.

## Connection to the Conspiracy
Jean-Pierre LaRoche's name appears in the **warehouse ledger** recovered from the northeast warehouse in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 024\|Session 024]], listed under **"selection cargo"** — a category that cross-references names of the city's disappeared, most of them merchants and guests at the Jarl's events.

His profile fits the pattern precisely:
- **Breton** (consistent with "Nordic mannerisms being off" not applying)
- **Silk merchant** — prosperous, prominent
- **Guest at the Jarl's banquet** — personally selected
- **Disappeared shortly after** the banquet

He is almost certainly being held at the **main facility north of Kjalgrad**, the destination of the "selection cargo" shipments.

- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] remembered his full title without prompting at the protest — earned a free critical roll from the GM for this

- The "absent-mindedness" after the banquet may indicate he was marked or interfered with at the event itself
