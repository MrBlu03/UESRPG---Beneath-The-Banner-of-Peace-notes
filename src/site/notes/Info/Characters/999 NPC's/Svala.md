# Svala

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Female  
**Age:** Older, experienced  
**Occupation:** Hunter  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Faction:** Independent

## Description
Svala is an experienced older hunter from [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] with extensive knowledge of local lore, runes, and forest conditions. She demonstrates wisdom and caution when dealing with supernatural threats, recognizing dangerous situations that younger hunters might ignore.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]] - The White Stag Hunt
- Partnered with [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] for the [[Info/Quests/White_Stag_Hunt\|White_Stag_Hunt]]
- Brought along [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]] (young hunter) and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Brovin\|Brovin]] (hunting dog)
- Identified Nordic rune found by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] but couldn't decipher its meaning
- Suggested consulting alchemists or the Jarl's court wizard about the rune

#### Tracking and Forest Knowledge
- Spotted antler scrapes on distant trees during the hunt
- Led tracking efforts following stag's erratic trail eastward
- Recognized environmental changes indicating supernatural danger
- Warned that the cursed area "isn't Kyne's forest"

#### Leadership and Wisdom
- Attempted to restrain [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]]'s impulsive pursuit into dangerous territory
- Demonstrated knowledge of local supernatural boundaries
- Showed concern for party safety over hunt success

### Post-Hunt Organization
- Promised to have something ready for [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] by dawn
- Planned to gather people to hunt the territorial creature in the woods
- Takes responsibility for organizing community response to supernatural threat

## Expertise and Knowledge
- **Rune Identification**: Can recognize Nordic script but needs specialists for translation
- **Forest Lore**: Understands boundaries between natural and supernatural areas
- **Tracking Skills**: Expert at following animal trails and reading signs
- **Local Connections**: Has relationships with alchemists and court officials
- **Supernatural Awareness**: Recognizes dangerous supernatural territories

## Leadership Qualities
- Takes charge during hunting expeditions
- Demonstrates protective instincts toward younger hunters
- Shows wisdom in recognizing when to retreat from danger
- Organizes community responses to threats
- Balances hunt objectives with safety concerns

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]]**: Works with but tries to restrain impulsive young hunter
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Brovin\|Brovin]]**: Partners with hunting dog who shares her instincts about danger
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Collaborated with party members on hunt
- **Community Hunters**: Organizes and leads other hunters in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
- **Court Officials**: Has connections to Jarl's court wizard and local alchemists

## Community Role
- Experienced guide for hunting expeditions
- Knowledge source for supernatural and runic matters
- Organizer of community defense against threats
- Bridge between hunters and official authorities
- Mentor figure for younger hunters

## Significance
Svala represents the experienced local knowledge that bridges normal hunting activities with supernatural awareness. Her recognition of dangerous supernatural boundaries and ability to organize community responses makes her a valuable ally in addressing the territorial creature threat affecting the [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]].
