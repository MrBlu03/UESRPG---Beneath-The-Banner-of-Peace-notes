# Father Aldric

## Basic Information
**Race:** Breton  
**Gender:** Male  
**Title:** Father (priest of Stendarr)  
**Location:** [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] — Grand Library  
**Faction:** Church of Stendarr  
**Status:** Alive  

## Description
A Breton priest of Stendarr, met by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] in the Grand Library in [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] during [[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]]. Found in the restoration section browsing spell books, with a jug of mead beside him on the shelf.

Father Aldric is primarily a **restoration mage**, with additional dabbling in **destruction** and **alteration**. He is described as standing with his hands folded — very priestly — and as someone who drinks a significant amount.

The GM referenced Ivanhoe (a medieval knight) in describing his bearing.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]] — Grand Library
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] found him after [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] asked a librarian for a "curly-haired Breton." Periwyn located him in the restoration section by browsing adjacent aisles (her cover of pretending to read a book was immediately seen through — the mage noticed her at once).

She approached by noting the rarity of their shared race in this part of the country. He introduced himself and confirmed:
- Primary school: **restoration**
- Secondary schools: **destruction** and **alteration** (dabbler)
- Has **removed a Ward before** — can do so again

When Periwyn asked for help removing a Ward (framing it as a trial she had to undergo before returning to a castle), he agreed with essentially no details required:

> *"As a man of God — or as a fellow Breton — whichever ranks higher for you. Would you help us?"*
> 
> *"If it's for a bottle of mead, it's for a bottle of mead."*

He agreed to meet at the **inn tomorrow** and was to be introduced to the rest of the party and briefed (carefully) on the actual scope of the operation.

## Role in Current Plans
Father Aldric is the **backup plan for Ward removal** at the main facility's secondary entrances. The [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|Vestige]] cannot dispel the powerful Warding glyphs alone, and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s schools are destruction and restoration — not alteration.

The primary plan is an alchemical varnish requiring a **pearl** (already purchased from Julian) and a **hag raven feather** (the Vestige is retrieving this). If the varnish fails, Father Aldric is the contingency.

## Personality
- Very priestly in bearing but entirely practical in his dealings
- A significant drinker — mead jug beside him while browsing in the library
- Agreed to a potentially dangerous magical task on essentially a handshake and the word "noble cause"
- No excessive curiosity about details; comfortable with partial information

## Notes
- He does not know the full scope of what he has agreed to help with
- He will need to be carefully briefed before the assault — too much information could alarm him
- His alteration capability makes him potentially very useful if the varnish doesn't come together
- The party owes him at least a bottle of mead
