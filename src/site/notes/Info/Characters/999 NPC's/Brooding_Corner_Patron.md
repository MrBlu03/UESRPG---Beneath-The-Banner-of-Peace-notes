# Brooding Corner Patron

## Description
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Brooding_Corner_Patron\|Brooding_Corner_Patron]] is a respected human who frequents [[Info/Locations/The_Ragged_Flagon\|The_Ragged_Flagon]] and commands significant influence within the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] community. He maintains order through quiet authority and possesses valuable information about criminal activities.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Locations/The_Ragged_Flagon\|The_Ragged_Flagon]]**: Respected regular who can control other patrons
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Yannick_the_Brawler\|Yannick_the_Brawler]]**: Can stop his aggressive behavior with a simple whistle
- **The Party**: Entered into a favor agreement for information exchange
- **Underground Network**: Well-connected source of intelligence

## Key Events
- **Session 012**: 
  - Stopped [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Yannick_the_Brawler\|Yannick_the_Brawler]]'s fight attempt with a whistle
  - Negotiated favor exchange for detailed fire information
  - Revealed crucial details about the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] arson

## Information Provided
- **Perpetrators**: Three masked figures in black leathers with Daedric runes
- **Location**: Fire started near catacombs, close to [[Info/Locations/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall_of_the_Dead]] entrance
- **Method**: Oil accelerants used (burned too quickly for tavern torches)
- **Evidence**: [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] mark found on burned archways
- **Purpose**: Suspected theft operation using fire as distraction

## Favor Agreement
- **Terms**: One month obligation to answer questions or provide assistance
- **Duration**: Ends after one month with no further obligations
- **Exchange**: Detailed intelligence about the arson in return for future favor

## Personal Details
- **Drink of Choice**: Sujama (expensive drink from Vardenfell, Morrowind)
- **Demeanor**: Calm, observant, speaks only when necessary
- **Authority**: Commands respect throughout the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] community

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The_Ragged_Flagon\|The_Ragged_Flagon]]**: Regular corner table patron
- **[[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]]**: Lives within the underground community

## Notes
- His knowledge and connections suggest deep involvement in underground activities
- The quality of his drink (Sujama) indicates wealth or important connections
- His ability to control [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Yannick_the_Brawler\|Yannick_the_Brawler]] shows his social standing
- Represents the more organized and intelligent criminal element in the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]]
- May be contacted in the future to fulfill the favor obligation
