# Tharen Duleri

## Description
Tharen Duleri is a Camonna Tong leader operating criminal enterprises between Morrowind and Skyrim. He commands operatives who engage in theft, smuggling, and harassment of legitimate merchants. 

## Relationships
- Leader of Camonna Tong operatives in the [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] region
- Enemy of legitimate merchant operations, particularly [[Info/Organizations/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild]]
- Connected to wider Camonna Tong criminal syndicate in Morrowind

## Location
- Last known location: Cave in woods south of [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]], small clearing
- Base likely moved after [[2 -  Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]] elimination of his operatives
- Connected to return routes to Morrowind

## Recent Activities
**[[2 -  Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]**: His operatives were eliminated by [[The Fangs of Fjelhorst\|The Fangs of Fjelhorst]] while attempting to recover a mysterious lockbox from a seized merchant ship. The lockbox was sought due to its potential connection to ships spotted in the Sea of Ghosts.

## Notes
- Mentioned by captured subordinate during interrogation in [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] jail
- Organization appears to be involved in larger criminal operations involving maritime activities
- Status unknown following elimination of his local operatives
