# Lady Elaris

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Lady Elaris
- **Status:** Unmade/Destroyed by the Mysterious Figure
- **Nature:** Seductive supernatural entity, possibly vampire or similar creature
- **Location:** [[Darnow Hollow\|Darnow Hollow]] manor ballroom (in dreams)
- **First Appearance:** [[2 -  Sessions/Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)\|Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)]]

## Description
- **Physical:** Pale, beautiful woman wrapped in noble silks that hang like mourning veils
- **Throne:** Sits on a throne of withered roses at the far end of the ballroom
- **Movement:** Drifts like a silken ghost with no sound or weight
- **Scent:** Surrounded by the scent of crushed, wilting roses red as blood
- **Presence:** Reaches across distances without rising, commanding and seductive

## Combat Style
- **Approach:** Doesn't move like a killer, more like a lover trying to seduce to darkness
- **Weapons:** Claws capable of inflicting serious wounds
- **Tactics:** Uses seduction and psychological manipulation alongside physical combat
- **Signature Move:** Inflicted the scar on [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s left side of her face

## Dialogue & Philosophy
### Key Quotes:
- *"You've touched the void, haven't you? I can smell it on your skin"*
- *"That look in your eyes - fear, fury, cards. It's beautiful on you"*
- *"Do you even know how lovely you are when you break?"*
- *"You're already mine. That little girl with dirt on her hands whispering spells over cold flesh. That was the beginning. Just the ending"*
- *"Stay. Let the dead rest. Let your cards rot. Be beautiful with me"*
- *"You don't hate me. You hate that. Right. You ache for something to surrender to. Older than duty. Wilder than okay"*
- *"I could make it stop. Periwyn. No more craves, no more guilt. Just power. Just us"*
- *"You've worn sorrow so long you think it's skin"*

### Philosophy:
- Believes that surrender to darkness brings freedom from pain and guilt
- Sees beauty in brokenness and despair
- Claims [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] has always belonged to her since childhood
- Offers power and release from moral burdens

## Connection to Periwyn
- **Historical Claim:** References [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s childhood experiments with necromancy ("that little girl with dirt on her hands whispering spells over cold flesh")
- **Psychological Link:** Represents [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s temptation to surrender to despair and power
- **Physical Impact:** Inflicted the facial scar that [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] carries
- **Seductive Appeal:** Offered freedom from guilt, duty, and moral constraints

## Battle with Periwyn
- **Setting:** Grand ballroom of [[Darnow Hollow\|Darnow Hollow]] manor
- **Tactics:** Combined physical combat with psychological seduction
- **Injuries Dealt:** 
  - Clawed [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s face, creating her signature scar
  - Smashed [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] across the front of her neck, causing severe bleeding
- **Near Victory:** Almost succeeded in seducing [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] while she was wounded and vulnerable

## Relationship with Gareth
- **Conflict:** Hissed at [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gareth\|Gareth]]'s divine light manifestation
- **Claim:** Insisted [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] belonged to her, not to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gareth\|Gareth]]'s teachings
- **Defeat:** Was knocked back by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gareth\|Gareth]]'s divine intervention

## Final Fate
- **The Mysterious Figure:** Confronted by a cloaked entity who claimed "this was a seduction"
- **Final Battle:** Leaped at the mysterious figure with "elegant, terrible fury"
- **Unmaking:** Was caught mid-air and simply ceased to exist - not dead or gone, but unmade
- **No Resistance:** Offered no resistance to her complete destruction

## Connections
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]:** Primary target and opponent, represents her inner temptations
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gareth\|Gareth]]:** Opposed by his divine intervention and teachings of mercy
- **[[Darnow Hollow\|Darnow Hollow]]:** Connected to the manor's dark history
- **The Mysterious Figure:** Ultimately destroyed by this more powerful entity

## Current Status
- **Physical:** Unmade/destroyed completely
- **Spiritual:** No longer exists in any form
- **Legacy:** Her scar remains on [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s face as a permanent reminder
- **Symbolic:** Represents a temptation [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] has overcome
