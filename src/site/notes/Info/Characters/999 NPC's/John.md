# John

John is [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alara\|Alara]]'s small husky companion, a friendly and loyal dog who accompanies her on outings and provides emotional support.

- **Species**: Husky
- **Size**: Small
- **Owner**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alara\|Alara]]

## Background
John was originally a stray that [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alara\|Alara]] took in three years ago. Since then, he has become her devoted companion and provides comfort during difficult times.
