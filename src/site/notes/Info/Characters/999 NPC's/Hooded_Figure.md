# Hooded Figure

## Basic Information
**Race:** Unknown  
**Gender:** Presumed Male  
**Identity:** Concealed (hooded and masked)  
**Location:** Unknown (appears in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]])  
**Faction:** Unknown Court Connections

## Description
A mysterious hooded and masked individual who works with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]] on criminal operations and claims connections to high courts. The figure's voice bears similarity to someone from [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s dreams, suggesting possible supernatural or significant political connections.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]] - The Criminal Coordination
- Present during [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s final meeting with [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]
- Told [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] he was "no one of consequence" when she observed him
- Voice described as similar to figure from [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s dream encounters
- Claimed to have "connections and business" in high courts
