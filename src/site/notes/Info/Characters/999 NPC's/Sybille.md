# Sybille

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Sybille
- **Age:** Child
- **Status:** Alive (in dream/vision)
- **Location:** [[Darnow Hollow\|Darnow Hollow]] grain mill
- **First Appearance:** [[2 -  Sessions/Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)\|Session 009a (Periwyn's slumber)]]

## Description
- **Physical:** Small girl with straw-colored hair tangled at her shoulders
- **Possessions:** Wooden sword, [[Info/Items/Child's Doll\|child's doll]] (returned by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]])
- **Demeanor:** Frightened but brave, hiding from danger

## Background
- **Family:** Her mother was taken by the Lady in White
- **Tragedy:** Lost her mother to the supernatural entity haunting the manor
- **Survival:** Hiding in the grain mill to avoid the Lady in White

## Encounter with Periwyn
### The Meeting:
- Found in the grain mill, humming softly and clutching a wooden sword
- Initially startled by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s arrival but didn't run
- Asked if [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] was "the one who forgets"

### The Warning:
- Told [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] about the Lady who "takes your sadness away"
- Explained that the Lady took her mama too
- Described the Lady as wearing white, walking like she's still dreaming
- Said the Lady smells like "pretty flowers" (lilacs)
- Warned that the Lady asks for your name, and if you answer, you forget it
- Revealed the Lady lives in the chapel with the broken bell

### The Doll:
- Recognized the [[Info/Items/Child's Doll\|child's doll]] [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] found in the barn
- Revealed it was hers, given by her mama before the tragedy
- Gratefully accepted it back when [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] returned it
- Clutched it tight after receiving it

### Periwyn's Promise:
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] promised she couldn't bring her mother back
- But promised to stop the Lady and do all she could
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] gave her protective advice about hiding and not answering the Lady
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] gently brushed her hair back and tucked it behind her ear
