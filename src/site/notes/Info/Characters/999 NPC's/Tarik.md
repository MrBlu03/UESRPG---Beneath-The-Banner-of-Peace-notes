# Tarik

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Male  
**Title:** Speaker for the Dead  
**Occupation:** Religious Official / Ritual Master  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Faction:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] Government/Religious Authority

## Description
Tarik serves as the "Speaker for the Dead" in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], a religious position responsible for conducting funeral rites and execution rituals according to Nordic tradition. He is a solemn figure who ensures proper respect is paid to the gods and that Nordic customs are observed during matters of death and justice.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]] - The Nordic Execution (11th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600)

#### Pre-Execution Preparations
- Prepared [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] as the executioner in the Jarl's quarters
- Removed Durov's armor
- Painted **Nordic runes** across Durov's entire body and face using **fresh stag blood** (from a stag killed that morning)
- Dressed Durov in ceremonial garments:
  - A **wolf pelt mantle** (with wolf's head intact)
  - **Bear fur armor**
  - A **Nordic ceremonial helmet** with **stag antlers**
  - The helmet covered his face with a slit covered by cloth

#### At the Standing Stones
- Finished making a **circle of ash** around the fire pit at the execution site
- Conducted the opening invocation

#### The Ritual
**Opening Words:**
> *"Before the gods and the blood of men, the way to Sovngarde is called. Those who conspired against the clans pay the price they owe."*

Gestured to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] (the wraith/executioner):
> *"By short hand, let law be done."*

**During Each Execution:**
- Chanted in Nordic - a call to **Shor** to weigh souls and for **Kyne** to bear witness
- Oversaw the ceremonial process

**The Defiant Prisoner:**
When the fifth Dunmer spat out his mead:
- Held up his staff - everything stilled, even the wind
- Pronounced judgment:
> *"You disrespect our traditions. And so no word of you remains."*
- Guided [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] behind the prisoner (denying him the dignity of facing his executioner)
- Ordered the body carried to the edge of the woods (denied the honor of the pyre)

**Closing Ritual:**
- Dipped his hand in blood-diluted snow
- Traced a **spiral rune** upon the land
- Spoke the closing words:
> *"The deed is done. Let none speak of vengeance or guilt. The blood is paid and the gods are sated."*

## Religious Role
- **Conductor of Executions**: Leads the ritual elements of Nordic justice
- **Voice of the Gods**: Speaks on behalf of Shor and Kyne during ceremonies
- **Guardian of Tradition**: Ensures proper respect for Nordic customs
- **Judge of Honor**: Determines who receives honorable death vs. disgrace

## Notable Abilities
- Commands supernatural stillness (even the wind obeyed when he raised his staff)
- Knowledge of ancient Nordic rites and invocations
- Authority to determine treatment of the dead (pyre vs. woods)
