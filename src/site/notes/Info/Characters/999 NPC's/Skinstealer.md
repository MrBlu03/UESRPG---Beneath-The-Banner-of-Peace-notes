# The Skinstealer (Ragged Lord)

## Basic Information
**True Name:** Unknown (Called "Skinstealer" by locals)  
**Entity Type:** Ragged Lord - Aspect of Hircine  
**Nature:** Daedric supernatural creature  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]], [[Info/Locations/Cursed_Grove\|Cursed_Grove]]  
**Status:** Active threat  
**Time Active:** 3-4 weeks  
**Territory:** Inherited land with ancient sins

## Description
The Skinstealer is a manifestation of the Ragged Lord, an aspect of Hircine (Daedric Prince of the Hunt) associated with mockery and hunter's madness. It is a terrifying supernatural predator that claims the northern forest as its inheritance, creating grotesque shrines from the bones and skins of its victims while marking its territory with ancient Nordic runes.

## Physical Characteristics
- **Claws**: 45 cm long, ebony-sharp, capable of cutting through shields
- **Appearance**: Materializes out of thin air, can appear between wolves
- **Blood**: Warm, leaks from totems, blood-like consistency but supernatural origin
- **Size**: Large enough to suspend human bodies in trees
- **Visibility**: Can become invisible or partially visible as a silhouette
- **Strength**: Insanely strong, can fling armed warriors 2 meters with claw strikes

## Supernatural Abilities

### Spatial Manipulation
- **Infinite Forest**: Can trap victims in spatial loops where they walk in circles
- **Track Erasure**: Removes all evidence of footprints and passage
- **Teleportation**: "Just appears" - materializes suddenly between wolves
- **Maze Creation**: Traps entire groups in supernatural enclosed spaces

### Voice Mimicry
- **Perfect Replication**: Can exactly copy human voices
- **Luring Tactic**: Uses copied voices to say "over here" and separate prey
- **Psychological Warfare**: Creates confusion and panic
- **Targeted**: Mimicked [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Ingrin\|Ingrin]]'s voice specifically during Session 016

### Environmental Control
- **Forest Corruption**: Creates wrong, too-empty atmosphere
- **Sound Amplification**: Makes footsteps unnaturally loud
- **Atmospheric Pressure**: Air becomes thicker in its domain
- **Supernatural Cold**: Bodies freeze rapidly after death

## Territory and Markings

### The Othala Rune
- **Meaning**: Heritage, inheritance, bloodline, burden
- **Location**: Carved on back of trees throughout territory
- **Significance**: Claims the land as its inheritance
- **Theory**: Has inherited the land and its sins
- **Interpretation**: Either reclaiming ancestral territory or claiming through conquest

### The Grotesque Clearing (Shrine to Itself)
**Location**: Several hours into the Northern Forest, reached around 11 AM-noon

**Features:**
- **Ground**: No snow, no grass - supernaturally barren
- **Center**: Monolith of bones fused together with permafrost
- **Bones**: All kinds, arranged in concentric circles
- **Pattern**: Recognized by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] from old Winterhold texts as Ragged Lord markings
- **Hides**: Human skins hanging around clearing like banners
- **Tapestry**: Skins patched together in grotesque mosaic, layered like shards

**Purpose:**
- Shrine to itself (narcissistic display)
- Mockery of victims
- Territorial marker
- Ritual space for hunter's madness

### Additional Territory Markers
- **Freshly Added Totems**: Dotted throughout the forest
- **Carved Totems**: Human-like but "not quite" features from Session 015
- **Iron Nails**: Hundreds driven into trees around half-frozen stream
- **Burned Totem**: One half-turned to ash, as if struck by lightning
- **Blood Stains**: Snow stained red with the creature's warm blood leaking from totems

## Wolf Pack
- **Number**: Travels with pack of 3 wolves
- **Relationship**: Wolves are afraid and follow by compulsion, not loyalty
- **Control**: Likely commands wolves to kill
- **Status**: Serve as heralds or hunting companions under magical compulsion

## Hunting Methods

### Victim Display
- **Suspension**: Hangs bodies in trees as trophies
- **Hides**: Skins victims and displays hides like banners
- **Efficiency**: Throat cutting for quick kills
- **Freezing**: Bodies freeze rapidly in supernatural cold

### Stalking and Separation
1. Creates spatial maze to disorient prey
2. Erases tracks to prevent escape
3. Uses voice mimicry to separate individuals
4. Kills isolated targets efficiently
5. Displays bodies as warnings/trophies

### Confirmed Kills
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]]**: Woodsman, Session 016 - throat slit, suspended in trees
- **White Stag**: Session 015 - five long deep slashes
- **Unknown Hunters**: Multiple hides suggest previous victims

## Weaknesses

### Fire
**Critical Vulnerability**: The Ragged Lord is hurt by fire easily
- **Kyne's Flame**: Specifically fears the flame of Kyne
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]'s Ritual**: Fire salts caused the forest to scream in pain
- **Reaction**: Silhouette recoiled from trees when fire was lit
- **Physical Response**: Hides swayed, air trembled, creature visibly affected

### Combat Limitations
- **Retreat Before Kyne's Flame**: Did not directly attack during fire ritual
- **Forest Edge**: Limited pursuit beyond its territory
- **Final Attack**: Only struck at forest boundary, then disappeared

## Territorial Behavior
- **Active Period**: 3-4 weeks
- **Claims**: Believes it owns/inherited the land
- **Driven Away**: Natural wildlife has fled the area
- **Corruption**: Spreads supernatural influence through forest
- **Boundaries**: Recognizes forest edge as limit to direct engagement

## The Inheritance Theory
The creature appears to believe it has inherited this land and its sins:
- Othala rune specifically means inheritance/heritage
- May be reclaiming ancestral Daedric territory
- Could be responding to ancient wrongs committed on the land
- "Sins of the land" suggest historical events tied to the location
- Nordic rune usage suggests connection to ancient Nordic culture/conflicts

## Community Impact
- **Wildlife**: Driven away, affecting hunters like [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]]
- **Fear**: Community terrorized by displays and kills
- **Economy**: Hunting and trade disrupted
- **Response**: Organized community-wide effort to confront threat
- **Preparation**: Fire arrows, bonfires, guard mobilization

## Combat Strategy (Based on Intelligence)

### Known Tactics
1. Spatial manipulation to confuse and trap
2. Voice mimicry to separate prey
3. Stealth and sudden materialization
4. Display of kills to intimidate
5. Retreat from fire-based attacks

### Recommended Counter-Tactics
1. **Stay Together**: Prevent separation
2. **Fire Weapons**: Exploit critical weakness
3. **Ignore Voices**: Don't trust calls from forest
4. **Mark Paths**: Counter track erasure
5. **Kyne's Blessing**: Use sacred fire for protection

## Significance
The Skinstealer represents a major supernatural threat that has successfully claimed territory and demonstrated lethal capabilities. Its identification as a Ragged Lord aspect of Hircine suggests deeper implications about Daedric influence in the region. The discovery of its fire weakness provides hope for defeating it, but its intelligence, powers, and determination make it extremely dangerous.
