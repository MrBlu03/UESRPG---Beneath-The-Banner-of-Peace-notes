# Yannick the Brawler

## Description
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Yannick_the_Brawler\|Yannick_the_Brawler]] is a loud, half-drunk troublemaker who frequents [[Info/Locations/The_Ragged_Flagon\|The_Ragged_Flagon]] in the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]]. He spreads conspiracy theories and looks for excuses to start fights, particularly with authority figures or their representatives.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Locations/The_Ragged_Flagon\|The_Ragged_Flagon]]**: Regular patron of the underground tavern
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Brooding_Corner_Patron\|Brooding_Corner_Patron]]**: Respects the Dunmer's authority enough to stand down when whistled at
- **Authority Figures**: Hostile toward guards and anyone associated with the crown

## Key Events
- **Session 012**: 
  - Loudly blamed [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] guards for the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] fires
  - Attempted to start a fight with [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]
  - Was stopped by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Brooding_Corner_Patron\|Brooding_Corner_Patron]]'s whistle
  - Represents the anti-authority sentiment in the underground

## Personality
- **Aggressive**: Quick to anger and looking for fights
- **Conspiracy-Minded**: Spreads unfounded theories about guard involvement
- **Loud**: Dominates conversations with his accusations and theories
- **Drunk**: Frequently intoxicated, affecting his judgment and behavior

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The_Ragged_Flagon\|The_Ragged_Flagon]]**: Regular presence at the underground tavern
- **[[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]]**: Lives within the tunnel system

## Role in Community
- **Agitator**: Stirs up anti-guard sentiment among [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] residents
- **Voice of Discontent**: Represents the frustration and anger of the underground population
- **Follower**: Despite his bluster, defers to respected figures like [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Brooding_Corner_Patron\|Brooding_Corner_Patron]]

## Notes
- His conspiracy theories, while wrong about guard involvement, reflect real tensions
- Shows the delicate social dynamics within the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] community
- Demonstrates why [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]] needed outsiders to investigate rather than sending guards
- His behavior highlights the mistrust between surface authority and underground population
