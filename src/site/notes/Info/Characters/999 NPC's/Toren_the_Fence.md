# Toren the Fence

## Description
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Toren_the_Fence\|Toren_the_Fence]] is a nervous, thin information broker and fence operating in the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]]'s criminal network. He conducts business from a gated corridor in [[Info/Locations/The_Ragged_Flagon\|The_Ragged_Flagon]], always wringing his hands and maintaining detailed ledgers of contraband.

## Relationships
- **Criminal Network**: Information broker and fence in the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]]
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]**: Business partner in potential moonsugar venture
- **Underground Contacts**: Has sources throughout the criminal underworld

## Key Events
- **Session 012**: 
  - Negotiated information price down from 70 to 30 gold with [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]
  - Revealed three masked figures in black leathers with Daedric runes started the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] fires
  - Entered business partnership with [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] for moonsugar trade (500 gold investment)

## Business Operations
- **Information Brokerage**: Sells intelligence about criminal activities
- **Fencing**: Trades in illegal or stolen goods
- **Record Keeping**: Maintains detailed ledgers of contraband transactions
- **Future Venture**: Moonsugar business partnership with the party

## Location
- **[[Info/Locations/The_Ragged_Flagon\|The_Ragged_Flagon]]**: Operates from gated corridor within the tavern
- **[[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]]**: Base of operations in the underground tunnel system

## Key Intelligence Provided
- **Fire Origin**: Confirmed fire started in the Warrens near old catacombs
- **Perpetrators**: Three masked figures in strange black leathers
- **Method**: Deliberate arson using accelerants
- **Markings**: Figures wore potentially Daedric runes

## Notes
- Nervous demeanor but reliable source of information
- Willing to form business partnerships for mutual benefit
- Has connections throughout the criminal underworld that could be valuable
- Represents the organized criminal element within the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] community
