# Proventus

## Basic Information
**Race:** Imperial  
**Gender:** Male  
**Title:** Guard of the Trade Advocate  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Northeast_Warehouse_and_Main_Facility\|Main Facility Ruin]], north of [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Faction:** [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]  
**Superior:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]  
**Status:** Alive

## Description
Proventus is an Imperial guard belonging to the entourage of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]], the Trade Advocate of the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]. He is assigned to assist [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and Captain [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] in investigating the [[Info/Organizations/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna_Tong]]'s former camp.

## Background and Personality
- **Origins**: Originally from the Canton District of the Imperial City, Cyrodiil.
- **Career**: Has served [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] for approximately three years, handling general bodyguard and investigative work. Prior to this, he did bounty work around Windhelm.
- **Attitude**: Highly disciplined and formal, presenting a stiff, military-style march. This behavior earned a direct threat from [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]], who commanded him to stop marching and speak informally. 
- **Humorous Exchange**: Upon introducing himself, Durov jokingly misheard his name (*"Proventus? Prevent us from what?"*), to which Proventus replied (*"From the Imperial City, sir"*), prompting Durov to correct him (*"No, that would be from where!"*).

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 028\|Session 028]] - Assignment and Departure
- **Introduction**: Valen Marr introduced Proventus as a skilled investigator and bodyguard from his entourage to accompany Durov and Irlav.
- **The Mission**: Tasked with riding out to investigate the abandoned Camonna Tong camp to determine why they left, look for signs of attack or supplies, and locate their current encampment.
- **Durov's Command**: During their departure from the main hall of the ruin, Durov grabbed Proventus by the collar, ordering him to stop his formal marching and to not address him as "sir."

## Notes
- Experienced in investigative work similar to the bounty tracking he performed near Windhelm.
- His presence suggests Valen Marr is taking the threat from the Camonna Tong seriously, providing one of his trusted guards to assist with the investigation.
