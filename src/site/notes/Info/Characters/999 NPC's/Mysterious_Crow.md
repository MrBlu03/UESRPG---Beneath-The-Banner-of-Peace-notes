# Mysterious Crow

## Basic Information
**Race:** Supernatural Entity  
**Nature:** Unknown supernatural being appearing as a crow  
**Status:** Active  
**Location:** Follows the party  
**Faction:** Unknown

## Description
A supernatural entity that has been following the party and takes the form of a crow. This being appears to have intelligence and purpose beyond that of a normal animal, with connections to otherworldly knowledge and possibly divine or supernatural forces.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]] - Communication with Onasha
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] successfully communicated with the crow during the party's investigation
- The crow revealed that its true name cannot be spoken in mortal tongues
- When [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] asked about the scroll she received, the crow indicated she would understand its purpose "in time"
- Suggests the crow has knowledge of future events or divine providence

## Supernatural Characteristics
- Appears as an ordinary crow but demonstrates supernatural intelligence
- Can communicate with mortals, particularly [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]
- Possesses knowledge beyond normal animal understanding
- Has been following the party for unknown reasons
- Connected to mysterious scroll given to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]

## Relationship with Onasha
- Has established communication with [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] specifically
- Appears to be guiding or watching her in some capacity
- Connected to the mysterious scroll she possesses
- May represent supernatural forces taking interest in her development

## Mystery Elements
- True name and nature remain unknown
- Purpose for following the party unclear
- Connection to larger supernatural events possible
- May be messenger, guardian, or observer for higher powers

## Significance
The Mysterious Crow represents growing supernatural interest in the party's activities. Its specific connection to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] and the cryptic scroll suggests she may have a larger destiny or role to play in upcoming events. The creature's supernatural nature indicates the party is operating in increasingly significant circumstances.

## Theories
- Could be a familiar or messenger from divine forces
- May represent nature spirits or old gods taking interest
- Possibly connected to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s growing supernatural abilities
- Could be advance scout for larger supernatural intervention

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]] - The Vestige Revealed
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] was identified as the founder of the [[Info/Organizations/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] ("The Crowfather"). The crows serve as his messengers and agents; they guided the party out of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad_Sewers\|Kjalgrad_Sewers]] after the [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]] confrontation.

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]] - Note Delivered to Kirya
A crow appeared at the window of [[Info/Locations/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost_Gate_Hall]] in [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]:
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** offered the crow an apple
- The crow delivered a note in exchange
- The note read: *"Apple note from Master. Thank you."*
- "Master" refers to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] ("The Crowfather")
- The note is a receipt/acknowledgment — characteristic Blackfeather Court whimsy
