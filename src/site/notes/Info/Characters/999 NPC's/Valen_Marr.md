# Valen Marr

**Race:** Unknown

## Description
Valen Marr is the **Trade Advocate** for Guildmaster **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Jorvik_Hanison\|Jorvik_Hanison]]** of the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]. He holds significant authority within the guild, reporting directly to the Guildmaster himself. Valen handles sensitive guild operations, including financial matters and security issues.

## Role in the Guild

### Position and Authority
- **Trade Advocate** - a senior position within the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]
- Reports directly to Guildmaster [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Jorvik_Hanison\|Jorvik_Hanison]]
- Handles **financial operations** and money matters
- His men have access to guild finances and sensitive operations
- Deals with security threats to the guild

### Responsibilities
- Managing trade advocacy for the guild
- Overseeing financial operations
- Handling sensitive matters that concern the Guildmaster
- Coordinating with subordinates on guild business
- Dealing with threats to guild interests

## Significance
Valen Marr represents a critical link in the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] hierarchy. As Trade Advocate reporting directly to the Guildmaster, he likely has knowledge of:
- The **brimstone gold** operation
- The guild's connection to the **Cult of Clavicus Vile**
- **"The Grand Architect"** and the broader conspiracy
- The identity of the **Ouroboros necklace** wearer in guild leadership

Meeting him with the forged letter is extremely risky—if he sees through the deception, the party's investigation could be compromised or they could be in serious danger.

## Confirmed Coiled Serpent Membership ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]])

### The Ouroboros Pendant
**CRITICAL DISCOVERY**: **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] spotted Valen Marr wearing an Ouroboros pendant on his neck** during the trade event at the Jarl's Hall.
- The pendant confirms his membership in the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]]
- He was wearing it openly, albeit somewhat concealed
- This proves the Coiled Serpent has infiltrated the Guild of the Silverroad at the highest levels

### Interaction with Periwyn Lunclaire
At the trade event, Valen approached **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** directly:
- Welcomed her to the event and expressed that "the Lunclaire business is greatly missed"
- Offered partnership with his organization (controls trade from "trading hall up to Windhelm")
- Warned that "big changes are coming" - the High King is not feeling well
- Probed about her political alignment
- Remained cordial but clearly assessing her as a potential asset or threat

### Durov's Guard Service
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** successfully maintained his cover as Valen's guard during the event
- Valen did not recognize Durov or chose not to acknowledge their prior connection
- Valen gave Huldir spell absorption tinctures to distribute to guards (including Durov)
- At the end of the event, **Camonna Tong presence was detected** and Valen was evacuated from the city

## Evacuation from Kjalgrad
Following the Camonna Tong murders outside the Jarl's Hall:
- Valen was immediately evacuated by his guards (Huldir and Brom/Bramm)
- He is leaving Kjalgrad due to the threat (possibly today or tomorrow)
- Told Durov his payment would come by courier
- The party plans to meet with him on Monday if he hasn't left yet

## Implications of Discovery
Valen's confirmed Coiled Serpent membership changes everything:
- He is not merely a corrupt guild officer but an active cultist
- The Guild of the Silverroad's connection to the Coiled Serpent is through its Trade Advocate
- He likely knows the identity of "The Grand Architect"
- The partnership offer to Periwyn may have been an attempt to recruit her
- His warning about the High King suggests the conspiracy reaches the highest levels of Skyrim's government
