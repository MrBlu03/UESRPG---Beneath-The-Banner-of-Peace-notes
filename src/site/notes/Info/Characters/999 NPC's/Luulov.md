# Luulov

## Basic Information
**Race:** Unknown  
**Gender:** Unknown  
**Title:** Captain of the Guard, [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Location:** [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Faction:** City Guard (funded by [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]])  
**Status:** Alive  

## Description
The guard captain of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], now confirmed by name as Luulov. Like all city guards here, his force is on the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]'s payroll rather than the Jarl's. However, Luulov has been conducting an independent investigation into the missing persons situation — off the books, driven by a personal connection to a family friend who has gone missing.

Previously encountered in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]] (unnamed, referred to only as "Captain"), now fully named in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]].

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]] — The Disappearances (unnamed encounter)
After [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] de-escalated a crowd of citizens protesting the 45% tariff increases:
- Confirmed that **arrests had been made** in connection with citizens speaking out
- However, **no one was in the dungeon** — the arrested individuals had simply vanished
- There were **no dungeon records** matching the descriptions of the missing citizens
- This points to the Guild intercepting arrested citizens before they reach the dungeon

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]] — Personal Lead
Luulov had been looking for [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and found him at the [[Info/Locations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad_Guildhouse\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad_Guildhouse]] around noon:
- The missing persons situation is **far wider than he initially thought**
- He has a **personal lead** — for a family friend, not official Guild business
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] has been briefed but doesn't know the details
- He wants Durov with him because the informants he needs to speak with **don't cooperate with the Guild**, and Durov's presence is useful both socially and physically
- First stop: the **market protest**, to hear complaints and take statements

They attended the protest together, with Luulov's Silver Road insignia covered by his cloak. After taking statements from **Hana**, **Yelena**, and **Lady Aine**, they were heading toward the old **Blackfoot Guild house** to meet shady informants with a missing persons lead — when both warehouses went up in flames behind them.

## Current Status
- Was mid-approach to shady informants near the old [[Info/Locations/Blackfoot_Guild_House_Kjalgrad\|Blackfoot_Guild_House_Kjalgrad]] when the warehouse fires broke out
- His missing persons lead and the informants he needed to meet are still pending
- The session ended before they reached their destination

## Significance
Luulov represents a potential ally within the city's compromised guard structure — his investigation is personal rather than official, which means it runs counter to the Guild's interests. His willingness to cover his insignia and approach criminal informants alongside [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] suggests he is aware of the corruption around him, even if he can't address it openly.

- His "family friend" connection could be to one of the named missing — possibly Jean-Pierre LaRoche or another ledger name
