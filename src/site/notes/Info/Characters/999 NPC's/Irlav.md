# Irlav

## Description
Irlav is a city guard and contact for the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] in [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. He is cooperative with [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] but remains loyal to his duties as a guard, though he possesses a personal respect for the Queen of [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] that supersedes his allegiance to the corrupt local leadership of [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]].

## Basic Information
- **Race**: Nord (assumed)
- **Gender**: Male
- **Occupation**: City Guard / Guild of the Silverroad agent
- **Location**: [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]]
- **Faction**: [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]
- **Status**: Alive

## Key Events

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 027\|Session 027]] — Warehouse Investigation and Dungeon Duty
- **Warehouse Investigation**: Following the warehouse explosions, Irlav accompanied [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] to investigate the charred ruins. He discovered the Lower Lodge network's calling card—death bell flowers—planted in a passageway, and helped Durov track footprints with a tail drag (belonging to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]) to a sewer grate.
- **Interrogation Assist**: Irlav accompanied Durov to the dungeon of the [[Info/Locations/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]] to assist in questioning the arrested Argonian ([[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]). He carried in the confiscated gear to the evidence room for inspection.
- **Recovering the Queen's Writ**: After the corrupt steward [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] confiscated the [[Info/1 - Party Findings/Official Writ of Authority\|Queen's writ of authority]] from Periwyn's journal, Irlav recovered it from the steward's possession. He handed it to Durov to hide, stating: *"I hold respect for the Queen. The Jarl and the steward here, not so much."*
- **Jailbreak**: While Durov was checking the evidence room, Durov had to block Irlav's view to prevent him from seeing [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and Taria's rescue squad escaping through the false sewer wall.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]**: Guild colleague and companion. Irlav trusts and cooperates with Durov, sharing critical investigations and helping hide the Queen's writ.
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]]**: Jarl's Steward. Irlav holds little respect for the corrupt steward or the Jarl.
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]]**: Queen of [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]]. Irlav holds high respect for the Queen and her authority, which motivated him to save her writ from the steward.

## Notes
- Irlav is a key contact for Durov to manage his alibi following the breakout and fire at the [[Info/Locations/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]].
