# Thrysnr the Unsullied

## Description
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Thrysnr_the_Unsullied\|Thrysnr_the_Unsullied]] was a vampire of [[Info/Organizations/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan_Volkihar]] lineage who was recently killed by the party. His severed head was analyzed by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]], revealing important information about vampire activities in the region.

## Relationships
- **Sire**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]] - turned by the rogue Volkihar vampire
- **Bloodline**: [[Info/Organizations/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan_Volkihar]] but not of Harkon's direct household
- **Enemies**: The Fangs of Fjelhorst (killed by the party)

## Key Characteristics
- **Vampire Type**: Volkihar bloodline but with different lineage signature
- **Status**: Deceased (killed by party, head preserved for analysis)
- **Significance**: His blood analysis revealed the survival and continued activity of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]]

## Analysis Results
According to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]]'s examination:
- **Blood Signature**: Confirmed [[Info/Organizations/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan_Volkihar]] lineage but different from Harkon's court
- **Sire Identification**: Bloodline traces directly to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]]
- **Implications**: Proves [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]] survived his supposed death 20 years ago and has been creating new vampires

## Historical Context
- **Creation**: Turned sometime after [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]]'s supposed death during the Planemeld
- **Activity**: Part of recent vampire operations, suggesting coordinated supernatural threats
- **Death**: Killed by the party in previous session, head brought to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]] for analysis
