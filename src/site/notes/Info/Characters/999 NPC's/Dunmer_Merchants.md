# Dunmer Merchants

## Basic Information
**Race:** Dunmer (Dark Elves)  
**Gender:** Mixed  
**Occupation:** Merchant Caravan (Cover), Smugglers (Actual)  
**Status:** Mostly executed, some imprisoned  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] (captured)  
**Faction:** [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] conspiracy

## Description
A group of [[Dunmer\|Dunmer]] merchants operating a caravan that served as a front for smuggling operations connected to the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] conspiracy. They were intercepted by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] during [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s operation and revealed to be carrying illegal contraband and forged documents.

## Caravan Composition
- **6 Dunmer Guards**: Lightly armed, 3 positioned on each side of caravan
- **1 Nord Driver**: Operating the wagons
- **2 Special Guards**: Dressed in darker red, appeared nervous during inspection
- **Well-maintained Equipment**: Two wagons in good condition

## Criminal Activities

### Smuggling Operations
- **Moonsugar**: Hidden under floorboards in second wagon, sealed in waxed leather
- **Forged Documents**: Falsified paperwork with incorrect house signatures
- **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] Connection**: Ledger contained "payment, serpent mark confirmed" entries
- **House Affiliation**: Attempted to claim legitimacy through house connections

### Intercepted Goods
**First Wagon**: Cloth, mead, fish (legitimate cover goods)
**Second Wagon**: Spices, herbs (cover), moonsugar (hidden contraband)
**Additional Items**: Red gem found with silks, various trade goods

## Key Events in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]]

### The Inspection
- Stopped by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] claiming authority from [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]]
- Initially cooperative, allowing inspection of goods
- Presented forged documents when challenged
- Two guards showed nervous behavior, suggesting awareness of illegal cargo

### Interrogation and Discovery
- One guard separated for questioning by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]
- Under pressure, admitted to moonsugar hidden in second wagon
- Claimed first cart contained only legitimate goods
- Guard began yelling when cooperation broke down

### Combat and Capture
- Alert guard lunged at [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] with sword during confrontation
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] tackled, disarmed, and killed the attacking guard
- Guard spoke in unknown language before death
- Cooperative guard produced smuggling ledger revealing [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] connections
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] planted additional evidence to justify execution of entire group

### Sentencing
- Brought before [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]] the huscarl for judgment
- Offered choice: execution by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] or fight to death among themselves
- Survivors held in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] dungeons pending execution
- Caravan and all goods seized as evidence

## Intelligence Gathered

### Smuggling Ledger
- Multiple shipment records, mostly appearing legitimate
- Specific entries: "payment, serpent mark confirmed"
- [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] emblem on final page
- Evidence of organized conspiracy operations
- Payment records suggesting larger network

### Operational Methods
- Use legitimate trade as cover for illegal activities
- Forge official documents to avoid scrutiny
- Hide contraband in specially prepared compartments
- Operate with nervous guards aware of illegal cargo
- Connected to larger conspiracy network

## Conspiracy Connections
- **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]]**: Direct operational connection through ledger and symbols
- **Criminal Networks**: Working with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s organization
- **Political Corruption**: Forged documents suggest official involvement
- **Smuggling Routes**: Operating established trade routes for illegal goods

## Significance
The [[Dunmer\|Dunmer]] merchants represent the operational level of the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] conspiracy, demonstrating how the organization uses legitimate trade networks to move illegal goods and maintain funding. Their capture provides crucial intelligence about the conspiracy's methods and connections.

## Evidence Value
- Ledger reveals conspiracy payment structures
- Forged documents show corruption methods
- Moonsugar indicates drug trade involvement
- [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] symbols confirm organizational connection
- Operational methods reveal conspiracy sophistication

## Notes
- Most killed during capture, limiting interrogation opportunities
- Evidence gathered more valuable than prisoner testimony
- Demonstrates conspiracy's use of legitimate business as cover
- Shows intersection of criminal and political corruption
- Reveals funding mechanisms for larger conspiracy operations
