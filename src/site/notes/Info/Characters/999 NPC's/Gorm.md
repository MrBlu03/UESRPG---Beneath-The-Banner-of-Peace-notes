# Gorm

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Male  
**Titles:** Jarl of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Status:** Recovered from rock joint

## Description
Gorm is the Jarl of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], a Nord leader who governs with exceptional generosity and personal investment in his people's safety. Despite recovering from rock joint (a debilitating illness that also affected [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]]), he personally met with the party to reward them for defeating the Ragged Lord and protecting his citizens. He is described as a figure "broad as a bear" with a "beard like a snowdrift."

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]] - Post-Hunt Celebration
**Meeting the Heroes:**
- Personally approached the party at the market after the Ragged Lord's defeat
- Accompanied by his huscarl [[Helga Cairn-Shield\|Helga Cairn-Shield]]
- Had recently recovered from rock joint illness

**Exceptional Rewards:**
- **Paid 500 gold each** to all four party members from his personal funds
  - Total: 2000 gold distributed
  - Payment exceeded typical bounty expectations
  - Demonstrated exceptional generosity for successful hunt
- **Granted permanent use of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]]'s house**
  - Free accommodation whenever party returns to [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
  - Establishes [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] as safe haven for the party
  - Significant gift showing deep gratitude
- Thanked party for bringing [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]] back alive

## Leadership Style
- **Personal Investment**: Uses personal funds for rewards rather than only city treasury
- **Generous**: Exceptional payment (500g each) far exceeds typical bounties
- **Grateful**: Recognizes value of protecting citizens and hunters
- **Practical**: Provides ongoing benefit (free house) in addition to gold reward
- **Community-Focused**: Deep concern for citizens' safe return
- **Impressive Presence**: Described as "broad as a bear" with commanding physical presence

## Political Position
- **Title**: Jarl of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
- **Authority**: Governs eastern Skyrim city
- **Resources**: Has personal wealth to reward heroes
- **Property**: Controls distribution of housing (granted [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Nilan\|Nilan]]'s house)

## Relationships

## Health Status
- **Previously Ill**: Suffered from rock joint disease
- **Recovered**: Well enough to personally meet party by Session 017
- **Shared Condition**: [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]] also contracted rock joint during the hunt
- **Treatment Available**: [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] has healers capable of treating rock joint

## Significance
Gorm represents exceptional local leadership in Skyrim. His personal generosity (500g each from personal funds) and practical long-term thinking (free permanent housing) demonstrate investment in maintaining good relationships with capable warriors. His concern for citizens' lives over mere task completion shows moral leadership priorities.

## Resources and Authority
- **Personal Wealth**: Capable of distributing 2000 gold from personal funds
- **Property Rights**: Can grant permanent use of residential buildings
- **Military Resources**: Commands city guard and coordinates with [[Helga Cairn-Shield\|Helga Cairn-Shield]]
- **Political Power**: Authority over [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] governance and resources
