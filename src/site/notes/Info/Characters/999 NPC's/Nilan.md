# Nilan

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Male  
**Occupation:** Hunter (Former)  
**Status:** Deceased (Vampire Thrall)  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] (Found in his house basement)  
**Faction:** Independent

## Description
Nilan was a seasoned hunter from [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] who became the sole survivor of a mysterious incident that froze other hunters in the northern forests. However, his "survival" was actually his transformation into a vampire thrall before ultimately being killed by his vampire master.

## Physical Description
- Scruffy appearance with short hair
- Two-braided brown beard
- Found with bite marks on his neck
- Sunken eyes and discoloration characteristic of vampire thrall transformation
- Body showed signs of being battered and bruised

## Key Events

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]] - Discovery
- Initially reported as the sole survivor of the frozen hunter incident
- Found dead in hidden basement beneath his own house
- Body discovered by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]], and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] during investigation
- Scent of nightshade surrounded his corpse
- Single nightshade flower found at his feet

## Circumstances of Death
- Had been turned into a vampire thrall by the [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Vampire_Survivor\|Vampire_Survivor]]
- Used to gather blood for his vampire master
- Killed when no longer useful or as part of covering tracks
- Body hidden in secret basement beneath rug in his own bedroom

## Evidence Found
- Alchemy equipment showing recent nightshade preparation
- Two unique vials, one containing liquid "black as darkness"
- Mortar and pestle with fresh nightshade residue
- Bow hanging incorrectly by door (sign of struggle or non-hunter interference)

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Vampire_Survivor\|Vampire_Survivor]]**: Master who turned him into thrall and ultimately killed him
- **Other Hunters**: Colleagues who were frozen in the incident he supposedly "survived"
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]]**: Huscarl who provided information about his supposed survival

## Significance
Nilan's death represents the true scope of the vampire conspiracy affecting [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. His transformation and murder demonstrate the callous use of local residents as tools by vampire agents working for [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn]]'s organization.

## Final Disposition
- Body retrieved by the party from his basement
- Given proper funeral rites with disposal by funeral pyre
- Hunting trophies from his house recovered by the party

## Notes
- His "survival" story was a cover for his transformation into a thrall
- The alchemy setup suggests either forced cooperation or mind control in potion making
- His death marks another victim of the broader vampire conspiracy threatening the region
