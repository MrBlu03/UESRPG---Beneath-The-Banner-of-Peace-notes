# Gareth

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Gareth
- **Status:** Deceased (appears as spirit/vision)
- **Former Role:** [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s mentor and trainer
- **Former Affiliation:** [[Info/Organizations/Knights_of_Arkay\|Knights of Arkay]]
- **Location:** [[Info/Locations/Northpoint\|Northpoint]] (formerly), appears in [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] 's dreams

## Description
- **Appearance:** Silver-streaked auburn hair, scar over left eye, war-worn armor
- **Equipment:** Silver-trimmed armor, warhammer (reforged in vision)
- **Voice:** Comes slow, like it's being remembered
- **Spirit Form:** Flickers like an image on warped glass

## Background
- **Mentorship:** Trained [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] in combat and the ways of the [[Info/Organizations/Knights_of_Arkay\|Knights of Arkay]]
- **Teaching Style:** Emphasized mercy alongside vengeance, balance in justice
- **Death:** Killed by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] at [[Info/Locations/Arkayfell\|Arkayfell]] after being turned into a vampire
- **Last Words:** Begged [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] to kill him, taught her about mercy

## Key Events
### Session 009a (Dream/Vision)
- **Appearance:** Manifested in [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s dream at Darnell Hollow chapel
- **Philosophical Battle:** Engaged [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] in combat while teaching about mercy vs vengeance
- **Forgiveness:** Offered [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] absolution for his death and her past actions
- **Final Lesson:** Taught that [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] was never meant to carry the dead, only remember them
- **Closure:** Dissolved into silver light after offering forgiveness

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]:** Former student and protégé, now offers spiritual guidance
- **[[Info/Organizations/Knights_of_Arkay\|Knights of Arkay]]:** Former member and trainer
- **[[Info/Locations/Northpoint\|Northpoint]]:** Former home and training location

## Current Status
- **Physical:** Deceased, appears only in visions/dreams
- **Spiritual:** Continues to guide [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] from beyond
- **Influence:** Provides emotional closure and wisdom to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]

## Notes
- **Teaching Philosophy:** Believed in the balance between mercy and vengeance
- **Vampirism:** Was turned into a vampire before his death, begged [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] for mercy
- **Legacy:** His warhammer was reforged in [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s vision, symbolizing renewal
- **Wisdom:** Emphasizes that survival is enough, forgiveness is possible
- **Connection:** Appears to have divine/spiritual authority in [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s dreams
