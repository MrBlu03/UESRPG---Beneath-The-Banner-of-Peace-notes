# Stryder

## Basic Information
**Race:** Unknown  
**Gender:** Male  
**Occupation:** Criminal Coordinator/Information Broker  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Faction:** Independent Criminal Network

## Description
Stryder operates as an underworld contact and information broker in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], coordinating criminal activities and maintaining connections with smuggling networks. He has a debt relationship with [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and demonstrates knowledge of local criminal operations, including connections to the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] conspiracy.
