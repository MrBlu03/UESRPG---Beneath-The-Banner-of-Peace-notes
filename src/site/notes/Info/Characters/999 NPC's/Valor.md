# Valor

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord (presumed)  
**Gender:** Unknown  
**Occupation:** Official / Ceremony Assistant  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Faction:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] Government

## Description
Valor is an official in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] who assists with ceremonial duties, particularly executions. They walk alongside [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]] in formal processions and have a specific role in the execution ritual.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]] - The Execution Ceremony (11th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600)

#### The Procession
- Walked with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]] in the procession (after the skalds, before The Fangs of Fjelhørst)

#### Role in the Execution Ritual
For each of the condemned prisoners:
- Brought a **cup of mead** to the prisoner before execution
- Spoke the ritual words:
> *"Drink and meet your end with open eyes."*

This role represents the final mercy offered to the condemned - a chance to face death with clarity rather than fear.
