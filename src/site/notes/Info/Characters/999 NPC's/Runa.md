# Runa

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** Adult  
**Occupation:** Hunter and Meat Seller  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Faction:** Independent  
**Reputation:** "The goat of selling meat in the market"

## Description
Runa is a well-known hunter and meat seller in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]'s market, respected for his quality products and hunting skills. He is described enthusiastically by party members as "MY BOYY" and "the goat" (greatest of all time) at selling meat, indicating both popularity and skill in his profession. He joined [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]'s hunting expedition against the Skinstealer.
