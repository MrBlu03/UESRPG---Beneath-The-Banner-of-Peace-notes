# Hrovald

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Male  
**Occupation:** Royal Stable Master  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] Stables  
**Faction:** Riften Monarchy

## Description
Hrovald serves as the royal stable master for [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]]'s court in [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]]. He is responsible for maintaining the royal horses and assisting with the procurement of mounts for important missions.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 013\|Session 013]] - Horse Procurement
- Present alongside [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]] during the party's horse acquisition
- Assisted in the selection and preparation of horses for the party's journey
- Helped facilitate the procurement of: Rimefang ([[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]), Sunsnow ([[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]), Mossback ([[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]), and Ashen ([[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]])
- Provided professional expertise in matching horses to riders

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]]**: Serves the queen directly as royal stable master
- **The Party**: Provided professional service in equipping them with suitable mounts

## Significance
As royal stable master, Hrovald represents the practical support infrastructure of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]]'s administration. His involvement in personally outfitting the party demonstrates the high priority the royal court places on their missions.

## Horse Expertise
Successfully matched each party member with an appropriate mount:
- Recognized [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]'s need for a powerful, steady mount (Rimefang)
- Provided [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] with a nimble, warm-natured mare (Sunsnow)  
- Selected a gentle, cautious gelding for [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] (Mossback)
- Gave [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] a spirited mare matching her fierce nature (Ashen)

## Notes
- Professional and experienced in horse handling
- Works closely with royal administration
- Skilled at matching horses to riders' temperaments
