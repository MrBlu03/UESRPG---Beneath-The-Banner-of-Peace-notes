# Grieving Brother

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Male  
**Occupation:** Unknown  
**Status:** Alive  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Faction:** Independent

## Description
A local resident of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] whose brother was among the hunters who were mysteriously frozen in the northern forests. He represents the personal cost of the supernatural threats affecting the region and serves as an entry point for the party's investigation.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]] - Initial Information
- Approached by the party seeking information about the frozen hunter incident
- Revealed that his brother was one of the victims who died in the mysterious freezing
- Showed signs of grief and exhaustion from the loss
- Directed the party to speak with the Jarl for more official information
- Admitted he didn't know much detail about what had happened to his brother

## Emotional State
- Clearly grieving the loss of his brother
- Tired and worn down by the tragedy
- Limited knowledge about the specific circumstances of his brother's death
- Seeking answers but lacking official information

## Relationships
- **Deceased Brother**: One of the hunters who was frozen in the northern forest incident
- **[[The_Party\|The_Party]]**: Provided initial information and direction for their investigation
- **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] Community**: Represents the families affected by the supernatural threats

## Significance
The Grieving Brother represents the human cost of the vampire conspiracy and supernatural threats affecting [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. His loss and limited knowledge highlight how the common citizens are left in the dark about the true nature of the dangers they face.

## Role in Investigation
- Served as initial source of information about the frozen hunter incident
- Helped direct the party toward official channels (the Jarl's hall)
- Provided emotional context for the impact of the supernatural threats on local families

## Notes
- His brother's death was ultimately connected to the vampire survivor's field testing
- Represents the collateral damage of [[Majorn\|Majorn]]'s organization's activities
- His grief and lack of information show how supernatural threats affect ordinary citizens
- May have additional information about his brother's hunting activities if questioned further
