# Astrid

## Description
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Astrid\|Astrid]] is the daughter of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] and helps run the [[Info/Locations/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]] inn in [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]]. She is a skilled hunter who learned from a group of adventurers and keeps detailed records of interesting guests.

## Basic Information
- **Age**: 21 (at most)
- **Occupation**: Inn helper, skilled hunter
- **Family**: Daughter of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorin\|Bjorin]]

## Background
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] raised [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Astrid\|Astrid]] alone after her mother died during the war. Her mother was killed in an attack that collapsed a wall, though [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Astrid\|Astrid]] managed to escape. The [[Info/Locations/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]] inn was founded after her mother's death.

## Skills and Abilities
- **Hunting**: Taught by a group of adventurers a few years ago
- **Archery**: Skilled with bow and arrow
- **Trap Setting**: Knows how to set and check hunting traps
- **Record Keeping**: Maintains secret records of interesting inn visitors

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorin\|Bjorin]]**: Father and inn owner, protective relationship
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Hunting companion during camping trip

## Key Events
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: 
  - Joined the party's camping trip to the northern clearing
  - Demonstrated hunting skills by successfully tracking and killing a stag and bear
  - Shared her backstory about learning to hunt and her mother's death
  - Revealed she keeps secret records of interesting inn visitors
  - Mentioned a guest from 14 years ago who spoke about end times and dragons returning

## Location
- [[Info/Locations/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]] inn in [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]]

## Notable Records
- Keeps secret documentation of interesting people who visit the inn
- Recorded a man approximately 14 years ago who spoke about the end times and dragons being back

## Notes
- Skilled survivalist despite her young age
- Demonstrates maturity beyond her years, likely due to losing her mother early
- Her records of inn visitors could contain valuable historical information
- Maintains good relationship with the party after successful camping trip
