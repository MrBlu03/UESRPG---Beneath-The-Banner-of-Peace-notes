# Hillevi

## Description
- Nord woman, a member of the [[Info/Organizations/Companions\|Companions]]
- Practical and experienced warrior
- Has been with the Companions for 6 years
- Values glory, honor, and helping those in need

## Relationships
- Member of the [[Info/Organizations/Companions\|Companions]] organization
- Provided healing for the torture victim in Greenwall keep
- Assisted the party during the Greenwall infiltration
- Knows [[Info/Characters/Beitild\|Beitild]] and [[Info/Characters/Olen\|Olen]] who died during the Greenwall attack

## Location
- Originally encountered at the war camp outside [[Info/Locations/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]
- Participated in the attack on Greenwall with the [[Info/Organizations/Companions\|Companions]]

## Notes
- Joined the Companions seeking glory, entrance to Sovngarde, and to help people
- Shared that the Companions typically operate in The Rift region
- Their members are either descendants or recruits following [[Ysgramor\|Ysgramor]]'s traditions
- Mentioned that the Companions' homeland is now a frozen wasteland
- Has skills in healing magic or restoration
