# Torik

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** Older, clever man  
**Occupation:** Mage/Scholar  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Faction:** Independent  
**Education:** College of Winterhold

## Description
Torik is an older, clever mage who studied at the College of Winterhold. He possesses extensive knowledge of ancient lore, runic magic, and supernatural entities. Unlike many mages who focus purely on magical power, Torik combines scholarly wisdom with practical magical applications, making him an invaluable asset in confronting supernatural threats. He is notably tolerant and understanding of controversial magical practices like necromancy.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]] - The Skinstealer Hunt

#### Pre-Hunt Analysis
- Joined [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]'s hunting party to confront the Skinstealer
- Examined the Othala rune that [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] had carved into his bracer
- Identified the rune's meaning: Heritage, inheritance, bloodline, burden
- Theorized the Skinstealer was claiming the land as its inheritance
- Explained the creature has been active for 3-4 weeks
- Concluded the rune suggests the creature inherited this land and its sins

#### Periwyn's Necromancy
- Noticed [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s reaction to overwhelming sense of death in the forest
- Revealed he studied at Winterhold and knows necromancy has its uses
- Offered acceptance rather than judgment of her abilities
- Agreed to keep this knowledge between themselves
- Established mutual trust and understanding with [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]

#### The Grotesque Clearing (11:00 AM - Noon)
**Successful Lore Check:**
- Recognized the bone and skin patterns from old texts at Winterhold
- Identified the markings as belonging to the **Ragged Lord**
- **Ragged Lord Information:**
  - An aspect of Hircine (Daedric Prince of the Hunt)
  - Associated with mockery and hunter's madness
  - Creates shrines to itself
  - **Critical Weakness**: Gets hurt by fire easily
- Revealed he carried fire salts for this purpose

#### Breaking the Supernatural Maze
During the retreat, when the Skinstealer trapped the party in an infinite forest:
- Asked everyone to stand in a circle around him
- Grabbed his staff and placed it down
- Knelt in the snow
- Placed a brazier and used fire salts
- Lit them in orange flame (accounts mention blue fire falling from trees)
- **Result**: Forest screamed in a loud, pained howl
  - Hanging hides swayed violently
  - Air trembled
  - Everyone saw a silhouette recoiling from the trees
- Coughed up blood from the magical exertion
- Insisted he was "alright" despite obvious strain
- **Discovery**: The Skinstealer fears the flame of Kyne

#### Post-Hunt Preparation
- Offered his alchemy supplies to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] for battle preparations
- Mentioned materials: whiskers and blood of fox for "fox gale" (alchemical preparation)
- Scheduled to meet with them to create fire-based weapons and potions

## Significance
Torik represents the bridge between academic magical knowledge and practical supernatural combat. His identification of the Ragged Lord and discovery of its weakness to fire provides the key to defeating the Skinstealer. His acceptance of [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s necromancy shows intellectual openness and could provide her with a valuable mentor or ally in understanding her abilities.

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 017\|Session 017]] - The Final Hunt

**Morning Preparations:**
- Applied camouflage woad to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] upon request
- Applied night vision woad to party members
- Briefed party on the Ragged Lord's nature as aspect of [[Hircine\|Hircine]]
- Explained the creature is tied to the hides it hangs
- Confirmed creature likely immune to disease and poisons

**The Summoning Ritual:**
- Led party to the transformed shrine in the [[Info/Locations/Cursed_Grove\|Cursed_Grove]]
- Performed ritual to summon the Ragged Lord using its "mimic" (Daedric name/DNA)
- Spoke ancient words in borrowed language while frost crept up his arms
- Brazier burst with ash and fire, triggering the manifestation
- Successfully forced the creature to appear for confrontation

**Combat Contributions:**
- Dealt **9 damage** with fireball at critical moment
- Fire attacks exploited the creature's primary weakness
- Provided ranged magical support during battle
- Survived the entire encounter

**Post-Battle:**
- Found small antler charm (hunter's token) where the Ragged Lord fell
- Gave the charm to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] with meaningful words: *"Keep it to remember that even the gods can lose their way and still be found."*
- Will document encounter for Mages Guild/College of Winterhold
