# Harvold

## Description
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Harvold\|Harvold]] is the Captain of the [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] Guard, serving under [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]. He oversees the city's dungeons and prisoner interrogations. Known for offering drinks at the [[Info/Locations/Ember_Keg\|Ember_Keg]] to those who help extract information from difficult prisoners.

## Relationships
- Serves under [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]
- Works with [[The Fangs of Fjelhorst\|The Fangs of Fjelhorst]] on investigations requiring prisoner interrogation

## Recent Activities
**[[Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]**: Oversaw the interrogation of a captured Camonna Tong Dunmer criminal. Offered free drinks at the [[Info/Locations/Ember_Keg\|Ember_Keg]] if the party could make the prisoner talk, which they successfully accomplished.
