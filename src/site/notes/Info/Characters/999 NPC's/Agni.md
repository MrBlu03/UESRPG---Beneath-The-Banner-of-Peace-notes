# Agni

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Male  
**Occupation:** Skald / Musician  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] (visiting)  
**Faction:** Independent

## Description
Agni is a tall Nord man with a braided beard who accompanied the twin skalds [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]] and [[Sonja\|Sonja]] to [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] for the execution ceremony. He performed alongside them during the ritual procession and execution.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]] - The Execution Ceremony (11th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600)

#### The Procession
- Walked with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]] and [[Sonja\|Sonja]] in the procession order (after [[Tarik\|Tarik]], before Helga and Valor)
- Performed "The Song of the Wolf" with the twin skalds during the march to the standing stones

#### The Execution
- Provided ceremonial music throughout the ritual
- Part of the skald trio that gave the executions their traditional Nordic gravitas

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]]**: Fellow skald, performed alongside her
- **[[Sonja\|Sonja]]**: Fellow skald, performed alongside her

## Notes
- Appears to be a traveling companion of the twin skalds
- His braided beard marks him as adhering to traditional Nordic customs
