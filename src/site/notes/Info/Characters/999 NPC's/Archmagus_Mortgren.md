# Archmagus Mortgren

## Description
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]] is a mage who appeared briefly in Riften. His exact affiliation and position are unknown. According to the mysterious figure in [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s dream, he is much older than he appears and may be ancient.

## Key Events
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]]**: 
  - Observed in conversation with Queen Freidr about a prophecy and scroll
  - Informed [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] that her black feather was of magical origin and should be kept
  - Departed for the ruins on top of the mountain, a few days south of Riften
- **Session 012**:
  - Returned to [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] to analyze [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Thrysnr_the_Unsullied\|Thrysnr_the_Unsullied]]'s severed head
  - Revealed the vampire was turned by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]], not Harkon
  - Provided extensive intelligence about [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]]'s history and survival

## Location
- **Current**: [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] (temporarily returned for vampire analysis)
- **Permanent Base**: Wayrest (will return after several days)
- **Session 012**: Reinforcing wards and researching records in [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]]

## Notes
- First seen at the start of [[2 -  Sessions/Session 004\|Session 004]] in conversation with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]], discussing a prophecy and a scroll. He mentioned heading to Fjellhorst to investigate whether "those interred within hadn't awoken.
- **Personal History with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]]**: Crossed paths multiple times though never as allies, remembers him as "memorable"
- **Expertise**: Specialized knowledge of vampire bloodlines and ancient threats
- **Warnings**: Emphasizes that [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]] cannot be killed by steel alone - requires patience, knowledge, and precision

## Revelations from Session 009a
According to the Mysterious Figure in [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s dream:
- **True Age:** Is "what you were before" - older than any alive, possibly ancient like Dunmer mages who are 2000+ years old
- **Magical Specialization:** May be connected to necromancy
- **Mysterious Knowledge:** The figure knows him personally
