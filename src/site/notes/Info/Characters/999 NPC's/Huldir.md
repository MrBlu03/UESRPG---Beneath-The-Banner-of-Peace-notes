# Huldir

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** Early 30s  
**Occupation:** Personal Guard  
**Employer:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]  
**Origin:** Bruma  
**Location:** [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Faction:** [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]

## Description
Huldir is a Nord guard in his early 30s who serves as a personal guard to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]], Trade Advocate of the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]. He has a similar shield crest to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]], suggesting they may share origins from the far north. Huldir has been working with the Guild of the Silverroad for approximately three years and works directly alongside Valen Marr.

## Background
- Originally from **Bruma** in Cyrodiil
- Previously worked as a mercenary
- Has been with the Guild of the Silverroad for approximately **3 years**
- Works personally and closely with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]

## Connection to Durov
During [[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]], Huldir and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] bonded over breakfast:
- Both have similar shield crests from the far north
- Both mentioned a castle on cliffs in their homeland
- Huldir has been a mercenary like Durov
- Shared stories about their travels and work
- Durov is approximately 5-6 years younger than Huldir

## Role in Session 022

### Guard Duties
- Met [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] at the bridge before the trade event
- Escorted Valen Marr to the Jarl's Hall along with [[Brom/Bramm\|Brom/Bramm]]
- Stood guard during the trade event
- Kept his hand on his sword when the Camonna Tong threat was discovered

### The Vial
- Gave [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] a mysterious vial before the event
- Instructions: Take it when the Jarl starts speaking
- Later identified by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] as a **tincture of spell absorption**
- This suggests Huldir (or Valen) knew the Jarl's speech would have magical effects

### Camonna Tong Discovery
- Privately informed [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] about the Camonna Tong murders outside
- Revealed that the Camonna Tong is active in [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]
- Told Durov that Valen needed to be evacuated
- Arranged for payment to be delivered by courier
- Evacuated with Valen and Brom/Bramm after the event

## Personality
- Friendly and talkative with fellow guards
- Professional and loyal to Valen Marr
- Experienced mercenary who understands the trade
- Practical and security-conscious
- Trusts his employer enough to distribute magical protection

## Significance
Huldir represents:
- The personal security apparatus of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]
- A potential source of information about Valen's activities
- Evidence that someone knew about the Jarl's magical speech in advance
- A connection between the Guild and magical/alchemical resources
