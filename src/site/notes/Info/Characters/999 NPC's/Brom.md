# Brom

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord (likely)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Occupation:** Guard  
**Employer:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]  
**Location:** [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Faction:** [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]  
**Also Known As:** Bramm

## Description
Brom (also referred to as Bramm) is a guard working for [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] alongside [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Huldir\|Huldir]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]. He was assigned to guard duty at the trade event hosted by the Guild of the Silverroad at the Jarl's Hall.

## Role in Session 022

### Guard Duties
- Met [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] at the bridge meeting point with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Huldir\|Huldir]] before the event
- Escorted [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] to the Jarl's Hall
- Stood guard during the trade event alongside Huldir
- Evacuated with Valen and Huldir after the Camonna Tong murders
