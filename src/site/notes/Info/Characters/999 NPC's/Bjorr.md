# Bjorr

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** Adult  
**Occupation:** Woodsman  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Faction:** Independent  
**Status**: Killed by the Skinstealer

## Description
Bjorr was a woodsman from [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] who joined [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]'s hunting expedition to confront the Skinstealer. He represented the everyday courage of common folk willing to face supernatural threats to protect their community.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]] - The Skinstealer Hunt
- Joined the hunting party organized by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]
- One of six hunters who ventured into the [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]]
- Participated in the journey to the grotesque clearing
- Present when the Ragged Lord shrine was discovered

### Death in the Forest

#### The Supernatural Maze
During the retreat from the clearing, when the Skinstealer trapped the party in its spatial distortion:
- Suddenly vanished from the group without warning
- The party heard a snap and muffled shout
- His disappearance went unnoticed initially due to the supernatural confusion

#### Discovery of Body
- Found suspended above the party in the trees
- Throat had been cut
- Body already freezing in the supernatural cold
- First confirmed kill by the Skinstealer during the organized hunt
- His death demonstrated the creature's efficiency and cruelty

### Impact of Death
- Proved the Skinstealer could separate and kill hunters even in groups
- Demonstrated the effectiveness of the creature's voice mimicry and spatial manipulation
- Motivated the party to stay together more closely
- Showed the real cost of confronting the supernatural threat
- His body displayed as a warning/trophy by the Skinstealer

## Character Traits
- **Brave**: Willing to join dangerous supernatural hunt
- **Community-Minded**: Risked life to protect [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
- **Experienced**: Woodsman familiar with forest environments
- **Vulnerable**: Even experienced hunters can fall to supernatural threats

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]**: Hunting leader who organized the expedition
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]]**: Fellow hunter who was informed of his death upon the party's return
- **Hunting Party**: Colleague of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]], [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Ingrin\|Ingrin]], [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]], and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]]

## Significance
Bjorr's death serves as a stark reminder of the real danger posed by the Skinstealer. Unlike encounters where the party escapes unscathed, his killing demonstrates that the creature is lethal and efficient. His death also shows the Skinstealer's ability to separate victims through supernatural means, making group cohesion critical for survival.

## Method of Death
- **Separated**: Vanished from group through spatial manipulation
- **Killed**: Throat cut efficiently
- **Displayed**: Suspended in trees as trophy/warning
- **Frozen**: Body began freezing rapidly after death
- **Silent**: Minimal noise during the attack (snap and muffled shout)

## Legacy
- His death motivated [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] to increase preparations with fire defenses
- Informed [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]]'s understanding of how dangerous the hunt would have been
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s regret when telling [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]] "there's nothing you could have done"
- Demonstrated that even with preparation, the Skinstealer is deadly
- First casualty of the organized hunt, but galvanized community response

## Notes
- His profession as woodsman suggests familiarity with the forest
- Even forest knowledge couldn't protect against supernatural threats
- Death occurred despite being part of organized, prepared party
- Body was left in trees rather than consumed or hidden
- The Skinstealer's efficiency suggests it specifically targets and stalks prey
- His death proves the importance of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]'s discovery of fire weakness
