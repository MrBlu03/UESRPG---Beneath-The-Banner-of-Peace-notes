# Sonja

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Female  
**Occupation:** Skald (Warrior Poet)  
**Location:** Travelling (visited [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] for execution)  
**Status:** Alive

## Description
Sonja is one half of a pair of twin skalds, along with her sister [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]]. They are "Warrior Poets" who travel to important events like executions to provide ceremonial music and record events in Nordic tradition. They play the **Tagelharpa** (Swedish Horse Lyre).

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]] - The Nordic Execution

#### Meeting in the Market (9th of Sun's Dusk)
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] spotted [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]] and Sonja in the [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] market
- Revealed they were in town for an **execution** in 2-3 days
- Explained they are "Skalds" (Warrior Poets), not just bards
- Discussed their instrument: the **Tagelharpa**

#### Performance at The Soggy Beaver (9th of Sun's Dusk)
- Performed traditional Nordic songs with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]]
- Included a humorous 16th-century song about a farmer outsmarting the church

#### The Execution Procession (11th of Sun's Dusk)
- Walked with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Agni\|Agni]] in the procession
- Performed "The Song of the Wolf" during the march to the [[Standing_Stones_Execution_Site\|Standing Stones]]

#### Recording Names in the Book of Falderstad
After the execution, Sonja requested the party's full names for the **Book of Falderstad**:
- **Durov of Colovia**
- **Kirya Ma'Tael** (first time her surname was revealed)
- **Periwyn Lunclaire**
- **Onasha**

#### Gift to Durov
- Presented [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] with a **carved bone totem** with Nordic runes
- Quote: *"Proof of your participation in Nordic lore. May Kyne walk with you wherever your travels lead."*
- This is the party's second such totem

#### Music Lessons
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] asked if [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]] and Sonja could stay a day or two to teach her music
- They agreed to stay longer in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
