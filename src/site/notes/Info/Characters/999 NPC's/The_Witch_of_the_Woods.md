# The Witch of the Woods

## Basic Information
**Race:** Hagravenn (corrupted being)  
**Gender:** Female  
**Type:** Supernatural Creature  
**Location:** Clearing west of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Faction:** Independent  
**Status:** Alive (truce established)

## Description
The Witch of the Woods is a monstrous supernatural creature resembling a hagravenn—a corrupted fusion between an old hag and a bird. She is covered in raven feathers, possesses sharp claws, has a gigantic nose, and has a hunched, grotesque appearance. Despite her terrifying visage, she is capable of reason and negotiation.

## Physical Characteristics
- **Appearance:** Somewhere between an old hag and a bird
- **Features:** Raven feathers covering body, sharp claws, gigantic nose
- **Posture:** Hunched and monstrous-looking
- **Magic:** Capable of casting frost bolts

## Key Events

### [[Session 023a\|Session 023a]] - Periwyn's Negotiation
When [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] tracked the creature to her clearing, the witch immediately attacked with frost magic. However, after a brief combat exchange, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] called for parlay and successfully negotiated a truce.

**The Agreement:**
- The witch agreed to cease attacks on hunters and livestock
- In exchange, she requires monthly offerings of spell components:
  - Fresh human hearts
  - Eyeballs
  - Gems
- She tentatively agreed to the arrangement with a "Maybe" if humans uphold their end
- The deal establishes a buffer zone around her territory

**Motivation:**
- States: *"My power must grow, no matter the cost"*
- Seeks magical components for her spellcasting
- Prefers to be left in peace to focus on her magical studies
- Willing to coexist if provided the resources she needs

## Combat Capabilities
- **Frost Magic:** Can cast frost bolts (demonstrated 6 and 4 damage)
- **Territorial:** Defends her clearing aggressively against intruders
- **Magical Senses:** Aware of approaching creatures in her domain

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Negotiated a truce with her; potential student/teacher dynamic
- **Local Hunters:** Previously hostile relationship due to attacks on livestock

## Significance
The Witch represents a diplomatic victory for [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and demonstrates that not all supernatural threats require violence. She may become a source of magical knowledge for [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] if the truce holds. The creature establishes that there are non-human entities in the region willing to negotiate rather than fight.

## Notes
- Not a traditional human witch but a hagravenn-like supernatural being
- Territorial but reasonable when approached correctly
- Requires specific spell components that hint at necromantic or dark magical practices
- The tentative nature of her agreement suggests the truce will require ongoing maintenance
