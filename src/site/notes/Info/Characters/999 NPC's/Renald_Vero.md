# Renald Vero

## Basic Information
**Race:** Breton (likely)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Title:** Silk Baron  
**Origin:** Rivenspire (Shornhelm), High Rock  
**Current Location:** [[Windhelm\|Windhelm]] (office), travels to [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]  
**Business:** Silk Trade  
**Status:** Alive

## Description
Renald Vero is a silk baron from **Rivenspire** (also known as **Shornhelm**) in High Rock. He is an elderly, warm, and friendly merchant who had extensive dealings with the Lunclaire family in the past. He runs caravans throughout the region and maintains an office in [[Windhelm\|Windhelm]].

## Business Operations
- **Primary Trade**: Silk
- **Known For**: "The finest silks this side of the Sea of Ghosts" (or the Iliac Bay)
- **Caravan Operations**: Runs caravans throughout Skyrim and High Rock
- **Office Location**: [[Windhelm\|Windhelm]] - where he conducts most of his business
- **Networks**: Connected to major trade routes and merchant organizations

## History with Lunclaire Family
- Had **great many deals** with **Periwyn's father** when she was just a child
- Remembered Periwyn as "just a small lass" from his previous dealings
- Described the Lunclaire business as "greatly missed" after their fall
- Expressed genuine sadness about the massacre that ended the family business

## Interaction with Periwyn (Session 022)

### First Meeting at the Trade Event
- Recognized **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** immediately at the Guild event
- Expressed delight at seeing a Lunclaire again
- Asked about how things had been for her since "everything happened"

### Trade Discussion
When Periwyn mentioned restarting her family business:
- Renald expressed enthusiasm: "That would be splendid, my dear!"
- Offered to restart old trade connections
- Suggested Periwyn visit him in [[Windhelm\|Windhelm]] when the snow clears
- Gave her his office location as a contact point
- Offered to help establish new trade routes

### Personal Qualities
- Warm and paternal toward Periwyn
- Respectful of her family's legacy
- Genuine in his desire to help
- Professional but personable
- Well-connected in merchant circles

## Significance
Renald Vero represents:
- Legitimate business connections for Periwyn to rebuild the Lunclaire name
- Validation that the Lunclaire reputation still carries weight among honorable merchants
- A potential ally against the corruption of the Guild of the Silverroad
- Access to High Rock trade networks
- Proof that not all merchants are involved in the Coiled Serpent conspiracy

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Business contact - daughter of his former trading partner; wants to help her restart the family business
- **[[Jorvik Hanison\|Jorvik Hanison]]**: Acquaintance through guild events (mutual acknowledgment)
- **Lunclaire Family (Deceased)**: Former trading partners - had extensive silk trade dealings
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Olvfrost\|Olvfrost]]**: Fellow merchant at the same event

## Strategic Value
- Provides a legitimate alternative to the corrupt Guild of the Silverroad
- Connection to High Rock trade networks
- Potential source of information about the broader merchant community
- Could vouch for Periwyn's legitimacy to other merchants
- Entry point into established trade routes without guild involvement
