# Alma

## Basic Information
**Race:** Nord  
**Gender:** Female  
**Age:** Adult  
**Occupation:** Huntress  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Faction:** Independent

## Description
Alma is a huntress from [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] who joined [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]'s organized expedition to confront the Skinstealer. She demonstrated both courage in facing the supernatural threat and impatience during critical moments.

## Key Interactions

### [[2 -  Sessions/Session 016\|Session 016]] - The Skinstealer Hunt
- Joined [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]'s hunting party to confront the Skinstealer
- One of six hunters who ventured into the [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]]
- Participated in the journey to the grotesque clearing
- Present when [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] identified the Ragged Lord shrine

### At the Bone Clearing
- Witnessed the monolith of bones fused with permafrost
- Saw human skins hanging like banners
- Present for [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]'s identification of the Ragged Lord markings
- Learned the creature is vulnerable to fire

### During the Retreat
Made a notable suggestion:
- "Don't wanna sit around for too long"
- This was noted as a poor suggestion given the circumstances
- The impatience to leave the clearing area was understandable but risky
- The comment showed tension and nervousness about the situation

## Character Traits
- **Brave**: Willing to join dangerous supernatural hunt
- **Impatient**: Wanted to move quickly rather than carefully
- **Community-Minded**: Participated to protect [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
- **Nervous**: Her comment revealed underlying anxiety about the situation

## Hunting Skills
- Capable hunter willing to face supernatural threats
- Part of local hunting community
- Survived the encounter with the Skinstealer

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]**: Hunting leader who organized the expedition
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]**: Fellow hunter and mage who identified the shrine
- **Hunting Party**: Colleague of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Ingrin\|Ingrin]], [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]], and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]] (deceased)

## Significance
Alma represents the ordinary hunters of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] who must balance natural bravery with the stress of facing supernatural entities. Her impatience at the clearing shows the human response to being in a profoundly disturbing and dangerous location - the desire to leave quickly rather than linger.

## Survival
- Stayed with the group during the supernatural maze
- Survived [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]]'s death and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Ingrin\|Ingrin]]'s voice mimicry attack
- Made it back to [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] safely
- Witnessed [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]'s ritual breaking the forest enchantment

## Future Considerations
- Part of the group preparing for final confrontation
- Knows about the Skinstealer's weaknesses and tactics
- May participate in fire-based attack strategy
- Witnessed the effectiveness of Kyne's flame against the creature

## Notes
- Her suggestion to leave quickly was natural but ill-timed
- Showed nervousness through desire to move rather than plan
