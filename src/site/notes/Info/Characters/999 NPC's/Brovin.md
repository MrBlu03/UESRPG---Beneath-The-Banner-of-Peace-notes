# Brovin

## Basic Information
**Race:** Dog  
**Gender:** Unknown  
**Type:** Hunting Dog  
**Location:** [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]  
**Owner/Handler:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] and hunting party

## Description
Brovin is a hunting dog who accompanied the hunting party during the [[Info/Quests/White_Stag_Hunt\|White_Stag_Hunt]] in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]]. Despite being an animal, Brovin demonstrated superior instincts regarding supernatural danger compared to some of the human hunters, particularly [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]].
