# Camonna Tong

## Description
The Camonna Tong is a criminal syndicate originating from Morrowind that operates smuggling, theft, and extortion activities. They are known for their distinctive bone or plate masks and are willing to work as hired criminals for various clients across Tamriel.

## Key Features
- Criminal syndicate based in Morrowind
- Members wear distinctive bone/plate masks
- Engage in smuggling, theft, harassment, and extortion
- Operate maritime criminal enterprises
- Hire out services to various clients
- Use netch leather armor (exclusive to Morrowind)

- Connected to broader criminal network in Morrowind
- Members identified through mask system
