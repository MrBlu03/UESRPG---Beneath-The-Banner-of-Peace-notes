# Camonna Tong

## Overview
A ruthless, xenophobic Dunmer criminal syndicate originating in [[Info/Lore/Morrowind\|Morrowind]] that has expanded its smuggling networks, protection rackets, and illicit contraband operations into eastern Skyrim.

## Key Members & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tharen_Duleri\|Tharen_Duleri]]**: Regional syndicate boss operating along the Morrowind border.
- **Harbor Smugglers**: Infiltrated Riften docks and mountain border crossings.
- **Undercover Operatives**: Assassinated Silverroad sentries during the Kjalgrad trade gala.

## Operations & Notable Events
- **Riften Harbor Racketeering ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]]):** Harassed Argonian dock workers and seized a merchant vessel to recover a mysterious lockbox linked to House Dres ship movements; eradicated by the party.
- **Infiltration & Cover Identity ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 024\|Session 024]]):** Infiltrated Kjalgrad, murdering two guards outside the Jarl's Hall. The party utilized forged Camonna Tong identities and attire to infiltrate and raid [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Warehouse_Seven\|Warehouse_Seven]].
