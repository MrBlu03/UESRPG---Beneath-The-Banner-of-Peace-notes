# Lower Lodge Network

## Overview
A covert resistance network and underground mutual aid organization operating within [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], fighting against the corrupt monopoly of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Silverroad]] and the shadow of the cult.

## Structure
Decentralized cells operating out of safehouses and sewer tunnels.

## Goals and Activities
Protecting honest citizens, smuggling out refugees, and exposing civic corruption.

## Known Members
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tarja\|Tarja]]: Skilled ranger and chief liaison.

## Relationships
