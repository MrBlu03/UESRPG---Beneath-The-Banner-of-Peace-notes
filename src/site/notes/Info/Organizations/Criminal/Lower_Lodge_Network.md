# Lower Lodge Network

## Overview
A clandestine underground resistance movement and rebel network operating from the ancient sewer vaults beneath [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], marked by the covert **diamond-circle symbol**. Formed to oppose the corrupt monopoly of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]].

## Key Members & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tarja\|Tarja (Taria)]]**: Tactical director and resistance leader.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tabitha\|Tabitha]]**: Subterranean scout and guide.
- **Disaffected Laborers & Artisans**: Embedded contacts within city warehouses and tanneries.

## Operations & Notable Events
- **Market Strike & Sabotage ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]]):** Organized a city-wide labor strike and merchant protest, coordinating with [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] to ignite the Silverroad warehouse depots.
- **The Longhouse Jailbreak ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 027\|Session 027]]):** Staged an armed sewer rescue team led by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tabitha\|Tabitha]], providing [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] with weapons and breaching the false wall of the longhouse evidence room to rescue [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]].
