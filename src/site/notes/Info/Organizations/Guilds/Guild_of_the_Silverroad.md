# Guild of the Silverroad

## Overview
The Guild of the Silverroad is a powerful merchant organization that effectively controls the city of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] in the Rift. While ostensibly a legitimate trading guild, evidence suggests deep corruption and connections to Daedric activity, specifically the **[[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]]** and the **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]]**.

## Political Power

### Control of Kjalgrad
According to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]] ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]]):
- **Bankrolls the city guards** - not the Jarl
- **Controls prices, tolls, and passages**
- The Jarl of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] is essentially a figurehead
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Karo]] (Jarl's Steward) handles actual governance
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius Karo]] is likely in the guild's pocket

### Implications
- The guild has effectively purchased control of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]
- City policy likely serves guild interests
- Guards answer to guild money, not traditional Nordic authority
- Travelers and merchants are subject to guild-controlled tolls

## Daedric Connections

### Brimstone Gold Operation
- Distributing gold that originates from **Oblivion**
- Gold is warm to the touch and smells of brimstone/sulfur
- Agent [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varren]] was overpaying for goods throughout the market
- Purpose of flooding the economy with Daedric gold is unclear
- Connected to the **[[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]]**

### The Grand Architect
- A figure of authority mentioned by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varren]]
- *"The Grand Architect... he would be pleased"*
- Matches the "Architect" mentioned in the [[Info/Findings & Items/Documents & Notes/Reconstruction Report — Arcanaeum, College of Winterhold\|reconstructed letter]] about [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith_Velan]]
- Appears to be orchestrating events across multiple locations

### Coiled Serpent Connection
- Someone **high up** in the Guild of the Silverroad wears an **Ouroboros necklace**
- The Ouroboros is the symbol of the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]]
- This confirms infiltration at the leadership level
- Matches previous intel about a contact in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] wearing the pendant

## Known Operations

### Economic Manipulation
- Distributing brimstone gold through normal commerce
- Overpaying for goods to inject tainted currency into circulation
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varren]] paid far more than items were worth
- Long-term purpose unknown (possibly to taint the regional economy?)

### Communication Infrastructure
- Agents use **Daedric speaking stones** to report to superiors
- Stones are etched with Daedric runes
- Allow one-way communication with projections of superiors
- The entity [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varren]] reported to appeared humanoid but inhuman

### Political Control
- Jarl's Steward [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Karo]] appears to be their puppet
- Attempted to eliminate [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] (disguised as Sadrith) who was investigating them
- Use bounty hunters rather than guards for sensitive matters

### Session 023 — Tariffs, Disappearances, and Deep Operations

#### Tariff Escalation and Social Control
- **Tariffs raised to 45%** — an extreme rate causing hardship for merchants and citizens
- Citizens speaking out against the tariffs are **disappearing** — **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Guard_Captain_Kjalgrad]]** confirmed arrests were made but no one appeared in the dungeon records
- City guards on the Guild's payroll are reportedly asking questions at warehouses — described as "less than reputable people" by merchants
- A **clothing merchant with a mustache** vanished from his market stall after speaking publicly against the Guild
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** de-escalated a crowd of protesting citizens with a prepared speech, promising to investigate

#### The Warehouse Operation
At [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District\|Warehouse 7]], the party found sealed crates containing:
- **[[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Coin_Molds\|Coin_Molds]]** — matching the shape of brimstone gold coins
- Gemstones, chalk, **black candles**, and ritual animal parts (bone, sinew, pig hearts — listed only as "animal parts" on any manifest)
- A warehouse worker recognized the brimstone gold smell as matching **Daedric anchors from the Planemeld**
- The guild ledger records a **night transfer** from Warehouse 4 to [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Warehouse_Seven\|Warehouse 7]], and a **"special consignment — temple allocation"** (no temple exists in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]])

#### Intelligence from Valen's Office
The party gathered critical intelligence from [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Silverroad_Guildhouse\|Valen Marr's office]]:
- **Wall map**: Two red circles marking the warehouse district sites, plus a **mark northeast of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]** outside the city walls
- **Ledger**: Night warehouse transfers, temple allocation, and coin distribution to market
- **Cabinet letter (burnt)**: *"Jarl remains cooperative though his condition requires continued supervision"*
- **Lockbox**: Bribe payments to **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] officials** (last two weeks); letter fragments referencing **"the Coming Correction"**

#### The Partnership Offer
[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] offered [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] an 8% guild rate (vs. standard 15%) and three trade routes in exchange for carrying Guild cargo and reporting on rival merchants. He framed the Guild as a political voice for merchants with royal court connections.

#### Bribery Beyond Kjalgrad
Active bribe payments to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] officials within the past two weeks confirms the Guild is **expanding political corruption to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]** right now.

### Session 026 — Warehouses Burned; Protest; Strange Gold Pay Docking

#### Both Kjalgrad Warehouses Burned
**[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** and **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** executed a coordinated sabotage with Taria's [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Lower_Lodge_Network\|Lower Lodge network]]:
- Guard at first warehouse knocked out (war hammer, flat side; 8 damage; propped against wall)
- Death bell tripwire traps planted in both warehouses
- Kindling slung into second warehouse crates — fire spread immediately
- Both warehouses burned simultaneously via dual network action
- The Guild's [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] storage is now destroyed

#### Market Protest
A **market strike** organized by the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Lower_Lodge_Network\|Lower Lodge network]] pulled ~40–50 guards to the market perimeter (weapons sheathed). Merchants and tannery workers protested Guild tariffs and strange gold distribution:
- **Hana** (elderly merchant): Tariffs pushing sellers to losses, speaking for a group
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Yelena\|Yelena]]** (female tanner): Workers being **paid in strange smelly gold** and having pay **docked for refusing it**. Started approximately one week ago — timeline consistent with the stone going missing. A specific tannery worker was last seen at the warehouses.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lady_Aine\|Lady_Aine]]**: Her husband **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jean-Pierre_LaRouche\|Jean-Pierre LaRoche]]** (Breton silk merchant, distinctive curling moustache) has been missing for over a week, last seen leaving home to open his market stall. Was at the Jarl's banquet shortly before vanishing. Name matches the warehouse ledger's "selection cargo."

#### The Strange Smelly Gold (Brimstone Gold)
[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Yelena\|Yelena]]'s testimony confirms the brimstone gold is now being used as **workers' wages** and workers who refuse it are being **docked pay**. This represents an escalation beyond market injection — the Guild is now forcing it into the labour economy.

### Session 027 — Jailbreak & Arson of Yarls Hall

#### Interrogation and Escape
- **Interrogating [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Following the warehouse fires, Jarl's steward [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Karo]] (under suspected Guild influence) tortured [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] in the dungeon beneath the Jarl's hall. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] sent a warning message to the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] about the steward's cult and Daedric connections.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s Deception**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] arrived to interrogate [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] on "Guild orders." [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] successfully deceived the steward into leaving, found the [[Info/Findings & Items/Documents & Notes/Official Writ of Authority\|Queen's writ of authority]] in [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s confiscated journal, and hid it in his armor.
- **The Jailbreak and Fire**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and a [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|Lower Lodge]] rescue squad broke [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] out of her cell via a secret sewer wall. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] passed the Queen's writ back to [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] during the escape. Before leaving, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] threw a flame bomb in the hall, setting the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]] on fire.
