# Guild of the Silverroad

## Overview
An enormously wealthy, aggressive merchant conglomerate and mining cartel based in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. Controlling iron mines, trade tariffs, and municipal security, the guild effectively displaced local traders to rule Kjalgrad as a corporate fiefdom, secretly serving as the operational front for the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]].

## Key Members & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jorven_Halliksson\|Jorven_Halliksson]]**: Guildmaster of the Silverroad.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]]**: Trade Advocate and Coiled Serpent mastermind.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain_Irlav]] & [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Proventus\|Proventus]]**: Guard officers in guild service.

## Operations & Notable Events
- **Monopoly & Bribery ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]] – [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]]):** Imposed crushing tariffs on rival traders, bankrolled the city guard, and circulated 37,000 tainted brimstone Septims to buy civic compliance.
- **Sabotage & Collapse ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 026\|Session 026]] – [[2 -  Sessions/Session 028\|Session 028]]):** Their warehouses were burned during the synchronized market strike; their operations collapsed after the unmasking of steward [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]] and the destruction of the counterfeit mint at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan_Mesa]].
