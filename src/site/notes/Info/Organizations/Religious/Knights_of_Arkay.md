# Knights of the Order of Arkay (Knights of the Circle)

## Overview
The sacred, chivalric holy order of warrior-priests and paladins consecrated to **Arkay** (Lord of the Wheel of Life and Death), sworn to defend mortal lives from necromancy and eradicate the undead.

## Key Members & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sir_Gareth\|Sir_Gareth]]**: Late Knight Commander and combat mentor who wielded his signature warhammer.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn Lunclaire]]**: Former squire who trained at Arkayfell.

## Sacred Duties & Notable Events
- **The Tragedy of Arkayfell:** Overrun in a devastating vampire siege where Sir Gareth was infected and mercy-killed by Periwyn.
- **Weight of the Knighthood ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 031\|Session 031]]):** [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] reminded Periwyn that the heritage of the Knights of the Circle commands supreme moral weight and accountability across Tamriel, demanding discipline and selfless protection of the innocent.
