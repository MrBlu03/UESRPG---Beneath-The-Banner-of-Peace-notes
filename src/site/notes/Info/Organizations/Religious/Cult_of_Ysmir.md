# Cult of Ysmir

## Description
The [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult_of_Ysmir]] is an organization dedicated to fighting off forces if the coin is good enough, with strong spiritual knowledge and healing capabilities.

## Key Members
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir_Stormcrown]]**: Harbinger (leader), currently in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**: Shaman, currently in charge while [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir_Stormcrown]] is away
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vidir\|Víðir]]**: companion of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild]]**: Scout, uses bow and spear
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen]]**: Handles court jobs, carries two axes

## Activities
- Taking bounties
- Coordinating with local authorities
- Working on the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] situation with the party
- **Spiritual Healing**: Specialized in treating supernatural afflictions
- **Ancient Knowledge**: Understanding of [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Dragon_Cult\|Dragon Cult]] and ancient spirits
