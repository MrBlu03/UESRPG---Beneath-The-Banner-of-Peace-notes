# Cult of Ysmir

## Overview
A revered, traditional Nordic warrior-religious order in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The_Rift]] devoted to **Ysmir** (the Dragon of the North). Headquartered at the **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall_of_Ysmir]]** in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], the cult combines martial training, runic lore, and sacred ancestral rites.

## Key Members & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir_Stormcrown]]**: Towering Harbinger and martial leader.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**: High Shaman, seer, and master of woad painting.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vidir\|Vidir (Víðir)]]**: Ancient mystic warrior and temple guardian.

## Operations & Notable Events
- **The Vanguard at Greenwall ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 005\|Session 005]]):** Harbinger Gjellir led warriors alongside The Companions to relieve Greenwall fortress.
- **Spiritual Consecrations & Sanctuary ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 008\|Session 008]] – [[2 -  Sessions/Session 013\|Session 013]]):** Shaman Adda provided vital spiritual counsel regarding the Haunted Hunting Lodge, aided in laying Lagertha and Sven to rest, and hosted the party at the Hall of Ysmir.
