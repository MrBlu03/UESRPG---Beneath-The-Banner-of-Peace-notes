# Ebonheart Pact

## Overview
The historic northern alliance uniting the Nords of Eastern Skyrim, the Dunmer of Morrowind, and the Argonians of Black Marsh, formed during the Second Era to preserve regional independence.

## Key Heraldry & Lore
- **Dragon Insignia**: The united emblem of the three northern races.

## Notable Events
- **Historical Remembrance ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 023\|Session 023]]):** Evaluated during the examination of ancient alliance coinage and wartime trade treaties linking Skyrim and Morrowind.
