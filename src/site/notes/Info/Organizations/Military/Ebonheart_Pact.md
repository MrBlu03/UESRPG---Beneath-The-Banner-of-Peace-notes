# Ebonheart Pact

## Overview
The fragile, momentous alliance between Nords of Eastern Skyrim, Dunmer of Morrowind, and Argonians of Black Marsh, unified under Jorunn the Skald-King.

## Structure
The Great Moot with representatives from all three sovereign races.

## Goals and Activities
Defending eastern borders against invasion and maintaining self-determination.
