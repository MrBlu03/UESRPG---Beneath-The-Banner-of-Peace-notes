# Blackfeather Court (Court of Crows)

## Overview
An eccentric, intelligent society of talking Daedric crows native to [[Evergloam\|Evergloam]] (Nocturnal's Daedric plane) and [[Info/Locations/Other Realms/Crows_Wood\|Crows_Wood]]. Guided by the **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|Crowfather (The Vestige)]]**, they trade in secrets, shiny trinkets, and strategic planar intelligence.

## Key Members & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige (The Crowfather)]]**: Immortal leader of the independent crow murder.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron_Jangleplume]]**: Rotund, pompous Daedric crow noble.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Knave_of_Rooks\|Knave_of_Rooks]]**: Avian emissary and courier.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Mysterious_Crow\|Mysterious Crows]]**: Telepathic scouts communicating via thought.
