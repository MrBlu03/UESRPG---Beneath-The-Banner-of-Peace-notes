# Coiled Serpent

## Overview
A pervasive, sinister conspiracy and occult syndicate marked by the **triple Ouroboros insignia** (serpents devouring one another). Allied with Daedric cults, rogue vampires, and corrupt merchants to destabilize Skyrim's economy and institutions.

## Key Members & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]]**: High-ranking mastermind and Silverroad Trade Advocate.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius Caro]]**: Infiltrator steward (Scaafin Daedra).
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith_Velan]] & [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alaric\|Alaric]]**: Field operatives and saboteurs.

## Operations & Notable Events
- **Infrastructure Sabotage & Arson ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 001\|Session 001]] – [[2 -  Sessions/Session 012\|Session 012]]):** Sabotaged the Snow-Shod Dam with ungreased levers, staged highway roadblocks, and set fires in Riften to steal forensic evidence.
- **Economic Subversion in Kjalgrad ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]] – [[2 -  Sessions/Session 028\|Session 028]]):** Controlled the Guild of the Silverroad, manufactured tainted brimstone currency, and staged dark rituals from northern Dwemer ruins.
