# Clan Volkihar

## Description
[[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]] is an ancient and powerful vampire bloodline with direct connections to [[Info/Lore/Molag_Bal\|Molag Bal]]. 

## Key Features
- Pure-blooded vampire lineage
- Direct connection to [[Info/Lore/Molag_Bal\|Molag Bal]]

## Current Activities
- Suspected involvement in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]'s magical blizzard
- Possible connection to frozen soldiers at [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Harmugstahl\|Harmugstahl]]
- Evidence of a splinter faction (variant symbol discovered)

## Historical Events
- Previously took control of a Castle in the "Sea of Ghosts" approximately 2,000 years ago
- Current resurgence in activity around [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]

## Associated Locations
- [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]: Current base of operations
- [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Harmugstahl\|Harmugstahl]]: Site where their variant symbol was found

## Threats and Weaknesses
- Vulnerable to:
  - Sunlight
  - Fire
  - Silver weapons
  - Magical attacks

## Notes
- Considered extremely dangerous even by vampire hunter standards
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] has extensive knowledge of their capabilities from her time with the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Knights_of_Arkay\|Knights of Arkay]]
- Current activities suggest possible alliance with the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]
