# Clan Volkihar (Rogue Splinter Coven)

## Overview
The ancient, aristocratic vampire bloodline of Skyrim, carrying pure vampire blood from Molag Bal. While Lord Harkon's court maintains seclusion, a rogue splinter coven led by **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]]** broke away to wage aggressive campaigns across eastern Skyrim.

## Key Members & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]]**: Ancient master vampire and coven lord.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Thrysnr_the_Unsullied\|Thrysnr_the_Unsullied]]**: Volkihar commander at Greenwall.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lady_Elaris\|Lady_Elaris]]**: Vampire noble manifested in Periwyn's dream.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vampire_Survivor\|Vampire_Survivor]]**: Greenwall survivor cornered in Falderstad.

## Operations & Notable Events
- **Fort Greenwall Occupation ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 003\|Session 003]] – [[2 -  Sessions/Session 006\|Session 006]]):** Shrouded Greenwall in an unnatural eclipse blizzard using a Harrowstorm core, massacring approaching garrisons before being destroyed by the party.
- **Mortal Resilience Testing ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 014\|Session 014]]):** Captured vampire thralls in Falderstad confirmed that the Greenwall massacre was merely a field test for larger regional invasions.
