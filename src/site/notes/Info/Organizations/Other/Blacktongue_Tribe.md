# Blacktongue Tribe

## Overview
A revered and feared Saxhleel tribe from the deep swamplands of Black Marsh, known for their devotion to Sithis, mastery of natural toxins, and high proportion of born **Shadowscales** offered to the Dark Brotherhood.

## Key Members & Associated Figures
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Escaped slave and master archer from the Blacktongue tribe.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]**: Dark Brotherhood assassin and egg-brother to Onasha.

## Tribal Lore & Notable Events
- **Tribal Kinship & Intimidation ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 011\|Session 011]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 019\|Session 019]]):** [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] utilized the dread reputation of the Blacktongue tribe to extract confessions from Dunmer criminals in Riften and recognized her egg-brother [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] in Kjalgrad.
