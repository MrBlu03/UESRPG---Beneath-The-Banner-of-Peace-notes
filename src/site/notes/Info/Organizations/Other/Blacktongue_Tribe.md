# Blacktongue Tribe

## Overview
The Blacktongue Tribe is a prominent Argonian tribe hailing from the swamps of Black Marsh (Murkmire). They are renowned across Tamriel for their deep knowledge of shadow alchemy, lethal poisons, rare herbology, and devotion to Sithis.

## Structure
- Tribal elder council and shamanic leadership in Black Marsh, with widespread diaspora members retaining strong tribal bonds and mutual recognition through shared customs and dialect.

## Goals and Activities
- Preservation of traditional Argonian alchemical knowledge, venom harvesting, safeguarding tribal secrets, and defending Saxhleel against Dunmeri slaving cartels like [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Dres\|House_Dres]] and the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna_Tong]].

## Known Members
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Party chemist and alchemist, trained in Blacktongue herbalism, potion-making, and bomb-crafting.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]**: An Argonian egg-brother and operative connected to both the tribe and the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark_Brotherhood]].
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] Dockworker Contact**: A Blacktongue tribe member encountered by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] docks who provided vital intelligence on Dunmer smuggling operations.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Dres\|House_Dres]] / [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna_Tong]]**: Bitter historical enemies due to centuries of raiding, enslavement, and exploitation of Saxhleel.
- **[[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark_Brotherhood]]**: Religious and cultural reverence for Sithis creates a shared connection with shadow operatives.

## Locations
- **Black Marsh**: Ancestral homelands.
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] Docks & [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]**: Common travel and labor hubs for diaspora members in Skyrim.
