# Clan Morvan

## Overview
Clan Morvan is the ruling Nord clan of the city of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] in eastern Skyrim. Headed by the Jarl of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], the clan exercises political and administrative control over the hold, governing from the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Clan_Morvan_Longhouse]].

## Structure
- Led by the Jarl of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] with administrative assistance from the Jarl's Steward and the Hold Guard Captain ([[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain_Irlav]]).

## Goals and Activities
- Maintaining order and commerce in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], managing trade relations with rival merchant guilds ([[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild]] and [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]), and defending against external threats.

## Known Members
- **The Jarl of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]**: Ruling clan patriarch.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]]**: Former trusted steward who was secretly replaced by a Dremora shapeshifter serving the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain_Irlav]]**: Guard Captain tasked with city defense and longhouse security.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]**: Strong commercial and political ties, though strained by corruption.
- **[[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild]]**: Former commercial partners who were ousted from influence in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
- **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst]]**: Interacted with the Jarl and uncovered the cult conspiracy deeply infiltrating clan leadership.

## Locations
- **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Clan_Morvan_Longhouse]]**: Seat of power in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
