# Clan Morvan

## Overview
The historic noble Nord ruling clan of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], holding court from the **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Clan_Morvan_Longhouse]]**. Their leadership suffered subversion when their Jarl was slain and covertly replaced by a Daedric skin-changer.

## Key Members & Associated Figures
- **The Jarl (Clan Morvan)**: Replaced by a mimicry Daedra anchored by a dark runic stone.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]]**: Disguised Scaafin steward who manipulated clan governance.

## Notable Events
- **The Daedric Infiltration & Liberation ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]] – [[2 -  Sessions/Session 028\|Session 028]]):** The party uncovered the dark runic stone in the Jarl's chambers, exposed the mimicry impostor, and captured steward Darius Caro during the longhouse assault.
