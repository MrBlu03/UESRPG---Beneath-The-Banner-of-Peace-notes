# Clan Snow-Shod

## Overview
Clan Snow-Shod is an ancient and proud Old Clan of Nord landowners and farmers in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The Rift]], Skyrim. They hold considerable prestige, wealth, and land upstream from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]], centered around the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod_Estate]] and the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Dam\|Snow-Shod_Dam]].

## Structure
- Governed by clan elders and estate leaders, maintaining private guards to secure their lands, irrigation systems, and agricultural holdings.

## Goals and Activities
- Protecting and expanding agricultural prosperity in the Rift through irrigation projects, grain farming, and regional trade.

## Known Members
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Ingvar_Snow-shod\|Ingvar Snow-Shod]]**: Prominent clan figure and estate overseer.
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Dam\|Snow-Shod Dam]] Guards**: Armed retainers stationed at the dam.

## Relationships
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]]**: Loyal subjects of the Rift throne, though fiercely protective of their clan independence and property rights.
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]] Fishermen**: Tense relationship due to water disputes, nearly manipulated into full conflict by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith_Velan]] and the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] before the party mediated reconciliation.

## Locations
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod_Estate]]**: The clan's ancestral manor and farmland.
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Dam\|Snow-Shod_Dam]]**: Crucial river infrastructure built by the clan to regulate water for irrigation.
