# Clan Snow-Shod

## Overview
One of the most prominent, affluent, and influential noble merchant clans in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Rift\|The_Rift]], holding extensive agricultural lands, timber holdings, and the **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod_Estate]]** along the Treva River.

## Key Members & Associated Figures
- **Snow-Shod Guard Garrison**: Stationed at the river dam.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]**: Maintains diplomatic and commercial alliances with the clan.

## Notable Events
- **The Treva's Bend Water Feud ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 001\|Session 001]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 002]]):** Falsely blamed for withholding water from downstream farmers; cleared of wrongdoing when the party proved the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Dam\|Snow-Shod_Dam]] was sabotaged by the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] syndicate.
