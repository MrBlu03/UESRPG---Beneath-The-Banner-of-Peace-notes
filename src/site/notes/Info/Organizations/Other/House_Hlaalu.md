# House Hlaalu

## Basic Information
**Type:** Great House of Morrowind  
**Region:** Morrowind  
**Status:** Active  
**Leader:** Belyn Hlaalu (Grand Master)

## Description
House Hlaalu is one of the Great Houses of Morrowind, a major political and mercantile power in Dunmer society. The house has been implicated in smuggling operations extending into Skyrim.

## Leadership
- **Belyn Hlaalu**: Grand Master of House Hlaalu
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Garen_Hlaalu\|Garen Hlaalu]]**: Leads operations extending into Skyrim

## Criminal Operations
### Smuggling Network ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]])
Information obtained from interrogation of Dunmer prisoners in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]:
- **Moon Sugar Smuggling**: Importing moon sugar from [[Info/Locations/Morrowind/Ebonheart\|Ebonheart]] (Morrowind) into Skyrim
- **Courier Network**: Using hired couriers to transport goods and documents
- **Payment Structure**: Couriers paid 400 gold total for transporting moon sugar worth 300 gold
- **Skyrim Operations**: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Garen_Hlaalu\|Garen Hlaalu]] coordinates activities within Skyrim

## Connection to the Coiled Serpent
The captured Dunmer smugglers revealed:
- They were contacted by the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] organization
- Paid **2,000 gold in advance** to transport a ledger
- The ledger was not theirs - they were hired as couriers
- **Delivery Destination**: [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]] (location in the Rift)
- **Contact Identifier**: Individual wearing an **Ouroboros pendant**

## Evidence
### The Dunmer Ledger ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]])
Confiscated ledger contents (mostly in Dunmeri):
- General business transactions (lumber, cloth)
- A loan of **5,000 gold** from a Dunmer bank
- Dealings with **House Hlaalu**
- A single mention of **House Telvanni**
- One confirmed reference to the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]
