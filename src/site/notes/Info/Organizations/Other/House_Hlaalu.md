# House Hlaalu

## Overview
The Great House of [[Info/Lore/Morrowind\|Morrowind]] renowned for mercantile enterprise, trade diplomacy, and cross-provincial commerce with the Empire and Skyrim.

## Key Associated Figures & Events
- **Smuggler Forgery & Infiltration ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 015]], [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]):** Criminal smugglers operating across the border into Falderstad carried forged House Hlaalu trade permits and merchant crests to transport illicit contraband and weapons.
