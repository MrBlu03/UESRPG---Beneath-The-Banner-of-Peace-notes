# The Reach

## Overview
The rugged, mountainous western hold of Skyrim bordered by High Rock and Hammerfell, home to fierce Reachmen clans and ancient First Era fortresses.

- **[[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Harmugstahl\|Harmugstahl]] Investigation**: Royal mission dispatched by Queen Freidr to investigate supernatural freezing and garrison silence in the eastern Reach.
