# The Coiled Serpent

## Description
The [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] is a mysterious symbol associated with vampire activity and possibly a secret organization working with vampires.

## Significance
The symbol has been found at locations connected to vampire activities and mysterious events, suggesting it represents a group or entity involved in the vampire-related threats in the region.

## Known Appearances
- Found at the dam sabotage site at [[Snowshot_Estate\|Snowshot Estate]]
- Connected to the activities of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith Velan]]
- May be related to the vampire threats around [[Info/Locations/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]
- **[[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] Fire (Session 012)**: Serpent mark found etched on burned archways near the catacombs

## Associated Entities
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith Velan]] (deceased) seemed to be working for or with this group
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]]**: Possible connection to the rogue Volkihar vampire
- **Fire Operatives**: Three masked figures in black leathers with Daedric runes who set the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] fires
- Possible connection to vampire factions or cults

## Recent Activities (Session 012)
- **[[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] Arson**: Coordinated fire set to create chaos and cover theft operations
- **[[Info/Locations/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall_of_the_Dead]] Theft**: Stole poisoned Dunmer body and documentation under cover of the fire
- **Escalating Operations**: Moving from sabotage to direct theft of evidence, suggesting desperation or advancing timeline
