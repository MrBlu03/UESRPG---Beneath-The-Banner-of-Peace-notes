# Coiled Serpent (The Triple Ouroboros)

## Overview
An ancient, sinister occult emblem depicting three serpents or dragons consuming each other in a continuous circular knot. It serves as the recognition mark of a pervasive conspiratorial syndicate infiltrating Skyrim's politics and commerce.

## Manifestations & Crimes
- **Dam Sabotage**: Carved onto the ungreased lever mechanism that jammed the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Dam\|Snow-Shod_Dam]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Trevas_Bend]].
- **Courier Ciphers**: Found on encrypted correspondence carried by Dunmer agent [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith_Velan]].
- **Commercial Fronts**: Utilized by Trade Advocate [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin_Marr]] to coordinate contraband and tainted currency within the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]].
