# Mimicry Daedra (Scaafin & Skin-Changers)

## Overview
Lesser Daedric entities capable of adopting mortal physical forms, voices, and surface memories through dark soul-binding rituals.

## Arcane Mechanics
- **Soul-Anchoring Stones**: Inscribed with Daedric runes to anchor the mimic's illusion in Mundus. If removed, the disguise falters under direct sunlight or radiant restoration spells (*Sunbeam*).
- **Kjalgrad Imposture**: Employed by the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile]] to replace Jarl Morvan and install Scaafin steward [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]].
