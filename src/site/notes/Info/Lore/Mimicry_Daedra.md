# Mimicry Daedra

## Overview
**Mimicry Daedra** are a category of Daedric creature capable of assuming and maintaining the physical identity of a specific person. They are distinct from standard Daedric servants in that they do not belong to any specific Daedric Prince's clan — they are independent or work-for-hire Daedric entities, which makes them particularly difficult to track and identify.

The term **skin-changer** is used colloquially for these creatures. The more formal name among those who study Daedric lore is **Mimicry Daedra**.

> *"Mimicry Daedra — they don't belong to a clan or a prince, which makes this difficult. But I'd assume they'll be working with the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|cult of Clavicus Vile]] in the area."*
> — [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] ([[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]), [[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]]

## The Scathing

### What It Is
A **Scathing** is a specific type of Mimicry Daedra — tall, horned, largely humanoid, unmistakably Daedric in appearance. The party encountered one in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 024\|Session 024]] at the **northeast warehouse**, where it was serving as the warehouse manager under the employ of the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]].

### Physical Description
- Approximately Altmer height
- Large curved horns
- Mostly humanoid build but unmistakably Daedric in appearance
- Leaves behind physical remains (armor, weapon) when it departs — the one encountered at the warehouse left a body

### Abilities and Immunities
- **Immune to mind-altering magic and toxins** — this is a confirmed trait of Scathing and applies to mind-altering effects generally (not all magic)
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s frenzy bomb had no effect on it (it played along for its own reasons)
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s Calm (Illusion) spell failed against it
- **Calm, strategic, and patient** — [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|the Scathing]] at the warehouse allowed the guards to fight each other (the frenzy bomb affected the human guards, not [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|the Scathing]]), possibly to thin their numbers or because it simply did not care
- **Able to enter bargains and maintain agreements** — the warehouse Scathing negotiated its own freedom in exchange for allowing the party to search, and kept its word

### The Warehouse Scathing (Session 024)
The first Daedric creature the party encountered face-to-face:
- Emerged calmly from the warehouse interior after the last guard fell
- Declared itself a servant of **[[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]]**
- Offered a bargain: allow it to leave freely → party searches without opposition
- Gave a small wave to [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] as it departed
- Bolted when [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] lunged after it
- Was immune to [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s Calm spell
- Left behind armor, a bronze sword, and one orichalcum arrow; one horn was successfully extracted (the other broke)

**Current whereabouts:** Unknown.

## The Skin-Changer (Jarl Impersonator)

### What's Known
A **different** Mimicry Daedra — not [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|the Scathing]] — is currently posing as the **Jarl of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]**. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] confirmed this in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]] based on the **Daedric stone** (memory anchor) recovered from the Jarl's quarters in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]].

### How Skin-Changing Works
1. The skin-changer takes the physical form of the target
2. A **memory anchor** (the Daedric stone recovered by [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]) periodically transfers memories of the real person — or a stored copy — to the skin-changer
3. This allows the creature to convincingly maintain the target's identity over time: mannerisms, knowledge, relationships
4. Without the anchor, the creature begins to **lose memories** — small gaps, wrong mannerisms, behavioral inconsistencies appear over time

### Current Status of the Skin-Changer Jarl
- **The real Jarl is almost certainly dead**
- The memory anchor (stone) has been missing for approximately **one week** (since [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] stole it from the lockbox)
- The creature is now experiencing **memory degradation** — it knows the stone is gone and is actively searching for it
- It has reportedly been **more agitated than usual** according to Taria's network
- It has **not left the Yarls Hall** since the party's knowledge of it became relevant
- The [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Lower_Lodge_Network\|Lower Lodge network]] has the building surrounded; no secret exits detected
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 027\|Session 027]] Update**: During the jailbreak of [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], the Yarls Hall ([[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Clan Morvan Longhouse]]) was set on fire. The current status of the skin-changer Jarl (whether it survived the fire or fled) is unknown.

### Signs That Tipped Off Milos
> *"Whether it is the Jarl or the steward, I'm not sure. But someone is, given the Nordic mannerisms and the writing — I believe it to be likely the Jarl. The steward is an Imperial, after all."*

The Nordic mannerisms being wrong — combined with the Daedric stone, the burnt letter ("Jarl remains cooperative though his condition requires continued supervision"), and the magical effects of the Jarl's speech — all confirm the skin-changer identification.

### Immunities
Based on [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]'s confirmation that Scathing-type creatures are immune to mind-altering effects: the skin-changer Jarl is **likely immune to mind-altering magic**. Direct confrontation with illusion or charm spells may not be effective.

## Connection to Clavicus Vile
[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] identified the skin-changer operation as working **alongside** the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|cult of Clavicus Vile]] rather than being directly ordered by [[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]]. The cult (run through the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]) is using the Mimicry Daedra as tools:
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|The Scathing]] managed the warehouse
- The skin-changer occupies the Jarl's seat, maintaining the political arrangement that benefits the Guild

Whether [[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]] himself is directly orchestrating this or whether it is a mortal arrangement with Daedric tools is unclear.
