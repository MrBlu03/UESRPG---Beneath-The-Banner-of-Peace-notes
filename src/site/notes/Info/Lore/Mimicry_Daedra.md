# Mimicry Daedra (Skin-Changers)

## Overview
**Mimicry Daedra** are a category of Daedric creature capable of assuming and maintaining the physical identity of a specific person. They are distinct from standard Daedric servants in that they do not belong to any specific Daedric Prince's clan — they are independent or work-for-hire Daedric entities, which makes them particularly difficult to track and identify.

The term **skin-changer** is used colloquially for these creatures. The more formal name among those who study Daedric lore is **Mimicry Daedra**.

> *"Mimicry Daedra — they don't belong to a clan or a prince, which makes this difficult. But I'd assume they'll be working with the cult of Clavicus Vile in the area."*
> — Milos (the Vestige), Session 025

## The Scathing

### What It Is
A **Scathing** is a specific type of Mimicry Daedra — tall, horned, largely humanoid, unmistakably Daedric in appearance. The party encountered one in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 024\|Session 024]] at the **northeast warehouse**, where it was serving as the warehouse manager under the employ of the [[Cult of Clavicus Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]].

### Physical Description
- Approximately Altmer height
- Large curved horns
- Mostly humanoid build but unmistakably Daedric in appearance
- Leaves behind physical remains (armor, weapon) when it departs — the one encountered at the warehouse left a body

### Abilities and Immunities
- **Immune to mind-altering magic and toxins** — this is a confirmed trait of Scathing and applies to mind-altering effects generally (not all magic)
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s frenzy bomb had no effect on it (it played along for its own reasons)
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s Calm (Illusion) spell failed against it
- **Calm, strategic, and patient** — the Scathing at the warehouse allowed the guards to fight each other (the frenzy bomb affected the human guards, not the Scathing), possibly to thin their numbers or because it simply did not care
- **Able to enter bargains and maintain agreements** — the warehouse Scathing negotiated its own freedom in exchange for allowing the party to search, and kept its word

### The Warehouse Scathing (Session 024)
The first Daedric creature the party encountered face-to-face:
- Emerged calmly from the warehouse interior after the last guard fell
- Declared itself a servant of **Clavicus Vile**
- Offered a bargain: allow it to leave freely → party searches without opposition
- Gave a small wave to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] as it departed
- Bolted when [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] lunged after it
- Was immune to Kirya's Calm spell
- Left behind armor, a bronze sword, and one orichalcum arrow; one horn was successfully extracted (the other broke)

**Current whereabouts:** Unknown.

## The Skin-Changer (Jarl Impersonator)

### What's Known
A **different** Mimicry Daedra — not the Scathing — is currently posing as the **Jarl of Kjalgrad**. Milos confirmed this in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]] based on the **Daedric stone** (memory anchor) recovered from the Jarl's quarters in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]].

### How Skin-Changing Works
1. The skin-changer takes the physical form of the target
2. A **memory anchor** (the Daedric stone recovered by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]) periodically transfers memories of the real person — or a stored copy — to the skin-changer
3. This allows the creature to convincingly maintain the target's identity over time: mannerisms, knowledge, relationships
4. Without the anchor, the creature begins to **lose memories** — small gaps, wrong mannerisms, behavioral inconsistencies appear over time

### Current Status of the Skin-Changer Jarl
- **The real Jarl is almost certainly dead**
- The memory anchor (stone) has been missing for approximately **one week** (since Kirya stole it from the lockbox)
- The creature is now experiencing **memory degradation** — it knows the stone is gone and is actively searching for it
- It has reportedly been **more agitated than usual** according to Daria's network
- It has **not left the Yarls Hall** since the party's knowledge of it became relevant
- The Lower Lodge network has the building surrounded; no secret exits detected

### Signs That Tipped Off Milos
> *"Whether it is the Jarl or the steward, I'm not sure. But someone is, given the Nordic mannerisms and the writing — I believe it to be likely the Jarl. The steward is an Imperial, after all."*

The Nordic mannerisms being wrong — combined with the Daedric stone, the burnt letter ("Jarl remains cooperative though his condition requires continued supervision"), and the magical effects of the Jarl's speech — all confirm the skin-changer identification.

### Immunities
Based on Milos's confirmation that Scathing-type creatures are immune to mind-altering effects: the skin-changer Jarl is **likely immune to mind-altering magic**. Direct confrontation with illusion or charm spells may not be effective.

## Connection to Clavicus Vile
Milos identified the skin-changer operation as working **alongside** the cult of Clavicus Vile rather than being directly ordered by Clavicus Vile. The cult (run through the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]) is using the Mimicry Daedra as tools:
- The Scathing managed the warehouse
- The skin-changer occupies the Jarl's seat, maintaining the political arrangement that benefits the Guild

Whether Clavicus Vile himself is directly orchestrating this or whether it is a mortal arrangement with Daedric tools is unclear.
