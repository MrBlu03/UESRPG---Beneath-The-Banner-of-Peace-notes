# Wode

## Description
[[Info/Lore/Wode\|Wode]] is an ancient form of magic involving painted runes, typically applied to the face. It was used by [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Adda\|Adda]] during [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s recovery from her three-day slumber.

## Basic Information
- **Type**: Ancient magical practice
- **Application**: Runes painted on the face (primarily)
- **Purpose**: Healing, protection, or spiritual aid
- **Practitioner**: [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Adda\|Adda]] (confirmed user)

## Characteristics
- Uses painted runes as the medium for magic
- Primarily designed for facial application
- Cannot effectively be used to cover up existing markings on the body
- Represents very old magical traditions

## Known Uses
- **[[2 -  Sessions/Session 010\|Session 010]]**: [[Info/Characters/NPC's/Adda\|Adda]] applied [[Info/Lore/Wode\|wode]] runes to [[Info/Characters/The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s face during her recovery
- Used as part of healing process following supernatural trauma
- May provide spiritual protection or aid in processing visions/dreams

## Limitations
- Primarily effective on the face
- Cannot be used to disguise or cover existing body markings
- Requires knowledge of ancient magical practices

## Practitioners
- **[[Info/Characters/NPC's/Adda\|Adda]]**: Demonstrated knowledge and ability to use [[Info/Lore/Wode\|wode]]
- Likely known by other members of the [[Info/Organizations/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]]
- Represents ancient shamanic traditions

## Cultural Context
- Represents pre-modern magical practices
- Connects to ancient spiritual traditions
- Part of the deeper magical heritage of the region

## Notes
- The name suggests ancient linguistic origins
- May be connected to broader shamanic practices
- Could have applications beyond what was demonstrated
- Represents the intersection of healing and spiritual magic
