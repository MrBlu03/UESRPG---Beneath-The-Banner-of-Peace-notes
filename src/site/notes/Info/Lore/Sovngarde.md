# Sovngarde (The Hall of Valor)

## Overview
The sacred spiritual realm in Aetherius created by **[[Info/Lore/Shor\|Shor]]** as the eternal afterlife for courageous Nords who die honorably in battle.

## Cultural Reverence
- Invoked in victory toasts and funeral pyres by [[The_Companions\|The_Companions]] to honor fallen warriors like [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen]].
