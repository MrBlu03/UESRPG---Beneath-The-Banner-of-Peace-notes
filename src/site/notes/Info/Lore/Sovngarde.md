# Sovngarde

## Overview
The sacred afterlife realm of the Nordic dead, created by Shor (Lorkhan) for warriors who fall valiantly in honorable battle.

## Significance
The spiritual destination for Nord heroes and the resting place where Sven, Lagertha, and fallen Companions ascend.
