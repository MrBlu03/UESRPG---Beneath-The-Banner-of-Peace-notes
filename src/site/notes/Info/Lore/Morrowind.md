# Morrowind

## Overview
The ash-choked, eastern homeland of the Dunmer (Dark Elves), bordering eastern Skyrim across the Velothi Mountains.

## Significance
A vital commercial trade partner and member of the Ebonheart Pact, but also the origin of illicit smuggling operations led by the Camonna Tong.
