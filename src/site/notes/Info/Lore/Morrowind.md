# Morrowind (Resdayn)

## Overview
The volcanic northeastern province of Tamriel, homeland of the Dunmer (Dark Elves), governed by the Living Gods of the Tribunal and the Great Houses.

## Campaign Significance
- **Smuggling Networks**: Border smuggling routes from [[Info/Locations/Morrowind/Ebonheart\|Ebonheart]] funneling illicit contraband into Skyrim.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s Vow**: Target of the sworn liberation pact formed between Onasha and Periwyn to strike at [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Dres\|House_Dres]] plantations in Tear and rescue [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Xula\|Xula]].
