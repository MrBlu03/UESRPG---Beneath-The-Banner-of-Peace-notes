# Shor

## Overview
Shor (the Nordic aspect of Lorkhan) is the dead chieftain of the Nordic pantheon and Lord of Sovngarde. He is celebrated as the champion of mankind who led the gods in defense of mortals.

## Significance
Shor's legend inspires Nord warriors, skaldic songs, and martial honors across Skyrim. He represents courage against impossible odds and the eternal promise of the Hall of Valor.
