# Shor (God of the Underworld)

## Overview
The dead god of creation, chief of the ancient Nordic pantheon, and sovereign ruler of the sacred afterlife realm of [[Info/Lore/Sovngarde\|Sovngarde]].

## Significance in the Rift
- **Geographic Namesakes**: Celebrated through historic sites such as [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Shors_Stone\|Shor's Stone]] and [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Shors_Bastion\|Shor's Bastion]] in northern Rift.
