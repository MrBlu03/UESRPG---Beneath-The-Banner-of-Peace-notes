# Lord Harkon

## Overview
The ancient, tyrannical vampire lord of Castle Volkihar who forged a dark blood covenant with Molag Bal in the Merethic Era.

## Significance
Progenitor of Clan Volkihar whose ancient blood prophecies and cursed artifacts threaten the hold of The Rift.
