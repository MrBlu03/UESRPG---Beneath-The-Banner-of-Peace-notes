# Lord Harkon

## Overview
The ancient pureblood Vampire Lord and sovereign master of [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan_Volkihar]], ruling from Castle Volkihar in the Sea of Ghosts.

## Significance
- **Volkihar Schism**: While Harkon's central court maintains isolationism, ambitious ancient vampires such as **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]]** split away to establish rogue covens waging aggressive territorial campaigns across eastern Skyrim.
