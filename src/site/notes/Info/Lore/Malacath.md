# Malacath (Lord of the Spurned and Cursed)

## Overview
The Daedric Prince of the spurned, ostracized, broken oaths, and bloody curses. He watches over pariahs and those burdened by heavy, bitter duty.

## Connection to Periwyn
- **Planar Nightmares**: Manifested within [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s comatose dreamscape at Darnow Hollow, challenging her regarding the grief of losing her ear, her aristocratic heritage, and the burden of carrying [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sir_Gareth\|Sir_Gareth]]'s warhammer.
