# Clavicus Vile

## Overview
Clavicus Vile is a **Daedric Prince** associated with bargains, pacts, and wishes. He is known for granting desires in ways that twist the wisher's intent, often with ironic or catastrophic consequences. He typically appears as a small, jovial man or child, often accompanied by his hound **Barbas**.

## Sphere of Influence
- **Bargains and Deals**: The making of pacts and agreements
- **Wishes**: Granting desires, often with a twist
- **Trickery**: Manipulation through technically-fulfilled promises
- **Power**: Grants abilities in exchange for service

## Current Activity in the Rift

### Cult of Clavicus Vile ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 020\|Session 020]])
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] revealed he is investigating a **Cult of Clavicus Vile** operating in the region:

**Evidence of Activity:**
- **Brimstone Gold**: Currency originating from Oblivion being distributed in [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]
- Gold is warm to the touch and smells of sulfur/brimstone
- Being distributed through the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]]
- **"The Grand Architect"** mentioned as a figure of authority

**Cult Operations:**
- Agents like [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]] distribute Daedric gold
- Use **Daedric speaking stones** for communication
- Stones etched with Daedric runes
- Communication with inhuman projections

**Purpose:**
- Unknown, but likely involves some form of "bargain"
- The reconstructed letter mentioned "bargains" being set in motion
- Economic manipulation of [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] may be preparation for something larger

## Connection to Other Plots

### The Coiled Serpent
- Someone in [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] leadership wears an **Ouroboros necklace**
- The [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] has been connected to vampire activity and Daedric dealings
- May indicate cooperation between multiple Daedric-aligned factions

### The Grand Architect
- Mentioned by [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]] during interrogation
- Also referenced in [[Info/1 - Party Findings/Reconstruction Report — Arcanaeum, College of Winterhold\|reconstructed letter]] about [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith_Velan]]
- May be the leader of the Cult of Clavicus Vile
- Or a mortal agent serving the Daedric Prince

## Typical Worship
Cults of Clavicus Vile typically:
- Seek power through bargains with the Prince
- Offer services in exchange for boons
- Often don't realize the cost until too late
- May believe they can outsmart the Prince

## Known Artifacts
- **Masque of Clavicus Vile**: A helmet that enhances speech and persuasion
- **Rueful Axe**: A cursed axe (sometimes associated with Clavicus Vile)
- **Umbra**: A soul-trapping sword (formerly)

## The Vestige's Investigation
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]] has been tracking this cult's activities:
- Posed as "Sadrith Indoril" in [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]
- Accused citizens of Daedric dealings (testing reactions)
- Identified [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]] as a key operative
- Made alliance with [[Fangs of Fjelhorst\|Fangs of Fjelhorst]] to continue investigation
- Primary goal: Understand what the cult's "bargain" entails

## Significance
The presence of a Cult of Clavicus Vile in the Rift adds another layer to the conspiracy already involving:
- [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] operations
- Vampire activity ([[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]])
- [[Info/Organizations/House_Hlaalu\|House_Hlaalu]] smuggling
- [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey_Host]] artifacts

The convergence of these elements suggests a coordinated effort by multiple factions, possibly unified under "The Grand Architect."

## Notes
- Clavicus Vile's involvement suggests any apparent "deals" should be viewed with extreme suspicion
- The brimstone gold may be a form of payment for services rendered
- Or it could be a way of binding people into unwitting pacts
- The cult's true goal is likely hidden behind layers of mortal agents
