# Clavicus Vile (Lord of Bargains and Wishes)

## Overview
The Daedric Prince of bargains, pacts, wish-fulfillment, and insidious deceit. Clavicus Vile grants mortals immense power or wealth at ruinous hidden costs.

## Subversive Operations in Skyrim
- **Counterfeit Currency Crisis**: His cult in Kjalgrad utilized Daedric crucibles at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Altan_Mesa\|Altan_Mesa]] to mint 37,000 brimstone-tainted Septims that disintegrate after use.
- **Civic Replacement**: Provided Scaafin and mimicry Daedra to replace slain mortal leaders such as Jarl Morvan of Kjalgrad.
