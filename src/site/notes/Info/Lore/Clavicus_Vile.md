# Clavicus Vile

## Overview
Clavicus Vile is a **Daedric Prince** associated with bargains, pacts, and wishes. He is known for granting desires in ways that twist the wisher's intent, often with ironic or catastrophic consequences. He typically appears as a small, jovial man or child, often accompanied by his hound **Barbas**.

## Sphere of Influence
- **Bargains and Deals**: The making of pacts and agreements
- **Wishes**: Granting desires, often with a twist
- **Trickery**: Manipulation through technically-fulfilled promises
- **Power**: Grants abilities in exchange for service

## Typical Worship
Cults of Clavicus Vile typically:
- Seek power through bargains with the Prince
- Offer services in exchange for boons
- Often don't realize the cost until too late
- May believe they can outsmart the Prince

## Known Artifacts
- **Masque of Clavicus Vile**: A helmet that enhances speech and persuasion
- **Rueful Axe**: A cursed axe (sometimes associated with Clavicus Vile)
- **Umbra**: A soul-trapping sword (formerly)

The presence of a [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]] in the Rift adds another layer to the conspiracy already involving:
- [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled_Serpent]] operations
- Vampire activity ([[Info/Characters/NPCs/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]])
- [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Hlaalu\|House_Hlaalu]] smuggling

## Notes
- Clavicus Vile's involvement suggests any apparent "deals" should be viewed with extreme suspicion
- The brimstone gold may be a form of payment for services rendered
- Or it could be a way of binding people into unwitting pacts
- The cult's true goal is likely hidden behind layers of mortal agents

## Daedric Servants in the Region

### The Scathing (Session 024)
A **Scathing** — a tall, horned, largely humanoid Daedra — was found managing the **northeast warehouse** on behalf of the cult. It openly declared itself a servant of Clavicus Vile:
> *"My master. The Lord of Bargains. Clavicus Vile."*

It bargained for its release in exchange for allowing the party to search freely, kept its word, and departed. It is **immune to mind-altering magic and toxins** — including [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s frenzy bomb (it played along for its own reasons) and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s Calm spell.

See [[Info/Lore/Mimicry_Daedra\|Mimicry_Daedra]] for full Scathing details.

### The Skin-Changer Jarl (Sessions 025–026)
A **Mimicry Daedra** (skin-changer) is currently posing as the **Jarl of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]**. The Daedric stone stolen from the Jarl's lockbox ([[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]) was confirmed by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] ([[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]) in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 025\|Session 025]] to be a **memory anchor** — it periodically transferred the real Jarl's memories to the creature, allowing it to maintain the identity.

- The real Jarl is **almost certainly dead**
- Without the memory anchor, the creature is **degrading**
- It knows the stone is gone and is actively searching for it
- It has not left the Yarls Hall; is reportedly more agitated than usual

This confirms Clavicus Vile's operation includes not just economic manipulation (brimstone gold) but active **political infiltration** of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]'s government.
