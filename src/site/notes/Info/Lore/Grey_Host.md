# Grey Host

## Overview
An ancient, dreaded First Era army composed of pureblooded vampires, werewolves, and dark necromancers who forged soul-harvesting machinery to unleash catastrophic **Harrowstorms**.

## Arcane Artifacts
- **[[Harrowstorm Engine Core\|Harrowstorm Engine Core]]**: Massive occult devices that blot out the sun, generate localized blizzards, and trap mortal souls. Recovered by the party at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] and transferred by the Mages Guild to [[Info/Locations/Other Realms/Eyevea\|Eyevea]].
