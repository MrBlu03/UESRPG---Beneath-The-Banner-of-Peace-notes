# Hircine (The Huntsman / Father of Manbeasts)

## Overview
The Daedric Prince of the Hunt, tracking, beasts, and lycanthropy. Hircine tests the limits of predator and prey across the mortal realm through various animalistic aspects.

## Manifestations in the Campaign
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Nohrak, the Ragged Lord (Skinstealer)]]**: An apex predatory aspect of Hircine that established bone shrines, flayed offerings, and illusory spatial mazes in the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Cursed_Grove\|Cursed_Grove]] north of Falderstad.
