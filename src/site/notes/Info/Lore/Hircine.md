# Hircine

## Overview
Hircine is the Daedric Prince of the Hunt, the Father of Manbeasts, and Lord of the Great Game. In Skyrim, his influence is felt deeply among hunters, werecreatures, and primal wilderness shamans.

## Significance
Hircine's aspects govern the eternal cycle of predator and prey. Ritual hunts, corrupted beast totems, and lycanthropic curses in the northern wilderness stem from his supernatural realm of the Hunting Grounds.
