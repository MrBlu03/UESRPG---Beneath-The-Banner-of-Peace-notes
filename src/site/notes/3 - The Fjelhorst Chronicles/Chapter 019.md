## Chapter 19 — Where Snow Gives Way to Silver

**12th–21st of Sun’s Dusk, 2E 600**

The road to [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]] did not begin upon the road at all, but in frost, confusion, and the sour taste of mead gone stale.

On the **twelfth**, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] woke before dawn with stone at his back and smoke-stink in his lungs, sprawled beside the old tannery with no memory of how he had come there. Snow crusted his armor; his head rang like struck iron. He set himself moving by instinct rather than sense, following the city wall until an alley betrayed him with a dead end. Turning back, he wandered through the sleeping streets until the North Gate loomed familiar at last. From there he drifted through the market, empty and grey, and down toward the southern stables. There, beside the low breath of horses and the creak of leather, [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Rimefang]] lay curled and steaming faintly in the cold. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] pressed himself against the great beast’s flank, drew warmth from hide and heart, and fell back into sleep.

When [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] woke and found his bed empty, she did not panic. She counted mugs, boots, and memories, and began her search where habit suggested. The market first—where an Argonian caravan was already setting up, bright-scaled and soft-spoken. Then [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|the Soggy Beaver]], where [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]] shook his head. Then [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Wounded_Boar\|the Wounded Boar]], where [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Denskar\|Denskar]] shrugged. An hour later, at the southern gate, she caught the glint of steel beneath straw and frost. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] slept with one arm flung over [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Rimefang]]’s neck.

Her first attempt to wake him failed. Her second earned a reflexive kick in return. Only the third—louder, sharper—dragged him back to the world. What followed was less reunion than stubborn argument, ending with [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]’s refusal to move and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]’s decision to preserve her patience by leaving him there.

That same morning, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] surfaced from the Treva River some distance north of the city, stiff with cold and aching. She hurried back, scales numbed, to find [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] already stirring stew to life. Blankets, steam, and silence followed. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] returned soon after, reported [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]’s chosen stable-bound exile, and joined them by the fire.

Near noon, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] finally rose, patted [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Rimefang]], and made his way home by way of the market. There he encountered a familiar voice and a familiar lie: the cabbage merchant of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], now selling “iceberg cabbages,” which proved to be nothing more than ordinary heads left in the snow. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] accepted the explanation with good humor and continued on. At home, he took stew without comment and ignored [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]’s glare entirely.

The rest of the day passed quietly. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] studied her spellbook. Rumors were weighed and paths plotted: [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]] first, then [[Info/Lore/Shor\|Shor]]’s Stone, [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Stromton\|Stromton]], [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Nimalten\|Nimalten]]. That night they pored over the Reconstruction Report from the College—what little remained of the burned letter. Fragments spoke of an “Architect,” of brothers bound by cult or cause, of bargains tied to shifts in guard, and of someone—named only “S”—who had failed and paid dearly. When sleep finally came, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] noticed the small note [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] had left upon her bedroll, marked not by words but by a simple flower.

The **thirteenth** dawned heavy with cloud and cold. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] asked [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] to walk with her, and together they traced the edge of Faldarstad. She spoke of the execution, of how certainty had cracked into doubt, of the frightened Dunmer whose fear had lingered with her. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] offered no absolution, only perspective—that judgment must be weighed carefully, one soul at a time. They returned in shared quiet.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] spent the rest of the morning below, retrieving buried bones and performing her lesson. Dust rose, cracked, and obeyed, if only briefly. She lingered long after, turning words and thoughts over like stones in her hand.

Later, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] led them to the market. Among the Argonian caravan she recognized [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] of the Black-Tongue tribe. Greetings flowed easily, memories followed, and soon they slipped into Jel, a rapid river of sound that left [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] adrift. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sketched in the margins of her journal; [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] bought rare ingredients—snake’s eye, crocodile’s eye—tokens of preparation rather than need.

At the stables, [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Mossback]] proved as stubborn as ever, and [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]] received an excess of affection.

The days from the **fourteenth through the sixteenth** blurred together: training, mending, provisioning, rest. Snow fell. Steel was checked. Breath grew steady.

On the **seventeenth**, the Fangs packed what little they had made their own and left [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nilan\|Nilan]]’s house behind. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]] saw them off at the gate. The road south was quiet. That evening, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] hunted a fox and shared the work of skinning and harvest. Later, by firelight, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] reapplied Fox’s Guile to [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]’s back, and they spoke more openly—of [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]’s missing sister, of unanswered signs in the Velothi Mountains, of patience stretched thin by hope.

The **eighteenth** passed without incident. That night, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] attempted her lesson again, standing bare to the cold with only firewood and will to keep her warm. The dust would not obey. She fled the cold, wrapped in [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]’s cloak and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]’s stew.

On the **nineteenth**, snow weighed heavily on the march. Exhaustion set in, but that evening [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] brought down an elk—meat, pelt, and antlers to see them onward.

The **twentieth** ended early for another attempt. This time, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] succeeded. Bone dust clung to her skin for a full minute before falling away. She laughed once—short and bright—then ran for the fire. Another elk fell that night.

On the **twenty-first**, stone rose where timber had once sufficed. [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]] stood before them, walls tall and cold, purple banners bearing gold coin snapping in the wind—the mark of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Morvan\|Clan Morvan]]. Guards in mismatched armor questioned them at the gate. The Fangs named themselves hunters, and were waved through with only cursory inspection.

Inside, the city pressed close. Merchants shouted in a dozen tongues. Coin changed hands faster than glances. Argonians, Bretons, Khajiit, Dunmer—all crowded the streets. Prosperity weighed heavy here, dense and unashamed.

They found lodging at the Frostgate Hall, rich with silk and fur, its innkeep [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Orgnar\|Orgnar]] cool and appraising. Rooms were taken, windows shuttered. Upon one sill sat a crow, watching. When [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] offered bread, another arrived—[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron Jangleplume]], glossy and rotund as ever. He spoke only that they were present on business, and that the Court’s eye had turned this way.

An ill omen, some would say.

The Fangs slept beneath stone and glass that night, having left the quiet of forests behind. Ahead lay [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]]—the City of Coin—where bargains mattered more than oaths, and weight was measured not in honor, but in gold.
