## Chapter 14b: The Legionnaire

**2nd of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600**

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] woke early, the faint grey of morning filtering through the shutters. He dressed without ceremony, descended to the common room for food, and afterward went to the stables to tend to [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Rimefang]], greeting the other Fangs before they set out on their own errands.

A distant howl from the woods drew his attention; low, strained, unnatural. He followed the sound east of the palisade, through snow still soft from the night's fall. Animal hides hung high among the branches, far beyond reach.

There, in a hollow ringed by skeletal birch, wolves circled something unseen. The air shimmered — and from it stepped a creature that towered twice, perhaps thrice his height. A warped figure, draped in ragged hides, crowned with the crooked head of a stag. It moved toward a deer slain by the wolves and peeled its hide away with surgical precision, hanging it upon a branch. Then, with a hand like bone and bark, it carved a rune into the trunk beside it.

As suddenly as it had come, it was gone. The wolves fled into the trees. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] followed their trail back, finding more of those runes burned into bark, each cold to the touch.

---

Back in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], he sought answers in the market. A hunter named [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Runa\|Runa]] told him of a Colovian who sometimes passed through — a quiet man who kept to himself and bought smoked meats. He directed [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] toward a tavern called [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]], where the man was said to drink.

Inside, the tavern's owner, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]], confirmed it. The Colovian came from Chorrol, and often muttered over his cup about sin, and the "Emperor's folly." Legion talk. After a round of food and a few drinking games with the locals, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] than 3ked [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]] and set off to find him.

On the road north, a caravan bearing the banners of [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Hlaalu\|House Hlaalu]] rolled past — silks, spices, and bottles of Sujamma glinting in the lamplight; the drink of choice for [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]], the mysterious criminal he still owed a favor to.

---

The Colovian's house was near-abandoned, boards crooked, smoke long gone from the chimney. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] stood outside in the snow for nearly half an hour, silent and watchful. Eventually, the door cracked open. A man's voice — wary, aged — invited him in.

Inside sat [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gaius\|Gaius]], grey-bearded and wrapped in furs, the years heavy upon his shoulders. His eyes widened at the sight of [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s armor. He spoke of the crest etched upon it, recalling it faintly from the war — a mark once flown by the garrison families near Bruma.

Over shared mead, the two traded fragments of history. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gaius\|Gaius]] admitted his service in the Legion, his voice brittle with guilt. "War made beasts of decent folk," he said, raising his cup. "To better days — or that worse ones don't come to pass."

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] asked why he'd chosen exile in Skyrim's backwoods. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gaius\|Gaius]] only smiled thinly. "Orders break men faster than swords. When the banners fell, I followed what was left of myself."

Before [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] could speak again, his words slurred. His vision wavered. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gaius\|Gaius]]'s tone softened. "Nirnroot in the mead," he murmured. "Not to harm you — just to give you silence."

Darkness took him. Somewhere within it, a horn sounded once — mournful, distant — followed by voices calling him home.

---

**4th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600**

When [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] awoke, the world was white with snow. Two hunters — [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Runa\|Runa]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Mirda\|Mirda]] — stood over him. They had found him collapsed outside the cottage. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gaius\|Gaius]] was gone.

Inside, the house had been stripped bare. Only a note remained on the table:

_To whoever wakes: I owed debts that can't be paid in coin. Some truths are better buried with the dead. You asked about the crest — remember this: north of Chorrol, the bird once flew proud. Then came the war, and it forgot which sky it belonged to._

Beside the note lay a melted twin-bird pendant, tarnished but familiar.

He thanked the hunters, gathered his gear, and trudged back to [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|the Soggy Beaver]]. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]] greeted him with concern — and slid across the table an [[Info/Organizations/Military/Imperial_Legion\|Imperial Legion]] tag, stamped with the number zero. "You'll have better use for it than I," he said simply.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] drank deep, ate his fill of venison, and sat by the hearth until the ache in his bones dulled. Then he returned to the stables, checking [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Rimefang]]'s hooves and calming [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Mossback]], who snorted at his approach.

At [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Wounded_Boar\|The Wounded Boar]], innkeep [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Denskar\|Denskar]] told him the rest of the Fangs had taken lodging on the east side of town. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] nodded, tightened his cloak, and set off through the snow — toward the faint glow of their hearthlight, and the sound of voices within.

And thus [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s path rejoined the Fangs.
