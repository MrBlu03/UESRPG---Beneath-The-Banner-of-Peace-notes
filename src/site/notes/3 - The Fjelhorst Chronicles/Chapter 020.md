## Chapter 20 — Of Black Feathers and Broken Bargains

**22nd of Sun’s Dusk, 2E 600**

Morning came to [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]] not with birdsong, but with the grind of wheels and the ceaseless murmur of trade. Snow lay thin upon stone, already trampled and greyed by coin and boot.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] woke first, as was often his habit, and washed the night from his face before descending to the common hall. He ate sparingly, then went to the stables to see to [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Rimefang]], whose breath steamed gently in the cold. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] followed soon after, drawn as always to [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]], lingering longer than she meant to. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] woke next, wary even in daylight, peering from her window for watchers and finding only distant birds and merchants raising awnings. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] woke last, and with [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] spoke quietly of the city’s press and noise—how [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]] felt too tight, too close. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] remarked upon the Khajiit moving openly through the streets, a curious sight for a city the caravans were said to avoid. In the end, they agreed to take breakfast, sell what pelts they could, and learn the city by degrees.

That plan did not survive the morning.

As [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] lingered by the stables, guards approached her—polite, firm, unmistakably official. The Fangs were summoned to the Jarl’s Court. They locked their rooms, left their arms behind, and concealed only what could not be easily seen. Seven guards escorted them through [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]]’s arteries: past guild halls, counting-houses, and shuttered shrines, across a narrow bridge, and into the shadow of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Morvan\|Clan Morvan]]’s longhouse.

The hall loomed like a perched hawk, stone heavy and watchful. Purple banners snapped above them. Within, the opulence was immediate and exhausting—silver and corundum chandeliers, etched sconces, painted walls depicting wealth mistaken for virtue. Even to [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], whose past had brushed aristocracy, it felt hollow, a display meant to impress rather than endure.

The Jarl’s high seat stood empty. Instead, they were met by an Imperial steward, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius Caro]], sharp-eyed and colder still. He questioned them closely, noted the absence of their fourth, and did not miss the cracks in their story. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] lied by instinct. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] countered with polish and pedigree, invoking the Lunclaire name and the pretense of trade. The steward did not truly believe them—but belief, it seemed, was not required.

[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]], he explained, had a problem. A Dunmer agitator, claiming ties to the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Fighters_Guild\|Fighters Guild]] and [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Indoril\|House Indoril]], had been accusing citizens of Daedric dealings, stirring fear, and leaving guards dead in his wake. The steward wanted him silenced or brought in. Gold was offered, threats implied. Whether the Fangs were merchants or mercenaries mattered less than their usefulness.

They left the longhouse under watchful eyes, suspicion clinging like frost.

In the market district they encountered [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]], scarred but standing, her wounds from the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]] healed cleanly. She confirmed the Dunmer’s presence—his prying questions, his fixation on wealth that carried an unnatural scent. She named him at last: Sadrith.

When [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] asked about the Khajiit, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]] spoke of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]]’s false prosperity. Trade here was throttled by the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silver Road]], who controlled tolls, prices, and passage—and funded the guards who enforced them. Coin flowed upward; misery pooled below. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] listened with thinly veiled distaste. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] made her decision then: her caravan kin would not be welcome here.

They agreed that Sadrith himself was the best path forward.

Retrieving their weapons—still without [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]—they went to the abandoned Black-Foot guildhouse, a rotting structure slumped against the city wall. Through boarded windows, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] glimpsed a lone figure hunched over papers by a single lamp. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] knocked.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|Sadrith Indoril]] answered as expected: guarded, weary, irritated by yet more questions. He spoke of rumors carried to the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Fighters_Guild\|Fighters Guild]]—gold that stank of Oblivion, people growing rich too quickly, signs of cult work. He claimed experience from the days of the Planemeld, from battles in Coldharbour itself. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] nearly believed him.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] did not.

She challenged the story, questioned the logic of a Great House Dunmer donning a mercenary’s cloak. Sadrith defended himself sharply, but the strain showed. Worse still, he saw through [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]’s silence and menace with unsettling ease, naming her tribe, dismissing her performance, noting what she was not. The words struck closer than intended.

Tempers flared. Accusations followed. One name—[[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Dres\|House Dres]]—cut deeper than any blade.

In a heartbeat, Sadrith moved. His hand flashed toward [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]’s throat, then stopped short, as if reason returned too late. Magic followed instead—meant to still panic, not kindle trust. Apologies came quickly, edged with a raw hatred for the Dres that surprised even [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]].

The mask could not hold.

With a snap of his fingers and a burst of feathers, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron Jangleplume]] appeared behind him. Illusions fell away. The Dunmer’s posture shifted, his presence sharpened, and the truth stood bare.

He was [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]].

Hero of the Three Banners. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|Crow Father]]. The figure from [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]’s visions, standing now in a forgotten guildhall with ink-stained fingers and tired eyes.

Their exchange was wary, edged, but civil enough. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] saw opportunity in them: outsiders already hired to pry, unknown to the city’s deeper rot. He spoke of his own suspicions—of a [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|cult of Clavicus Vile]] threading bargains through [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]]’s power, touching both the Jarl’s court and the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silver Road]]. One man, at least, he named with certainty: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]], an Imperial buyer flooding the market with brimstone gold.

A bargain was struck, reluctant but binding. They would help one another. Knowledge, leverage, silence—all weighed carefully.

Before leaving, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] searched [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]’s worktable. No serpent revealed itself, but there was mention of an ouroboros pendant worn by an officer of the Silver Road—the same mark tied to the smuggled ledger.

As they moved back toward the market, old wounds reopened in quieter ways. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] spoke of hope, of good where it might still exist. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] spoke of scars earned too deeply to ignore. Even [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], thinking of her people’s history and the slow death of slavery in Morrowind, conceded that trust came at a cost.

They found [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] in the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Grand_Market_Kjalgrad\|Grand Market]], coin spilling from his hands as if it burned him. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] acquired one such piece under the guise of curiosity and sold pelts besides. The trio shadowed him through winding streets until he vanished into a sewer grate.

Below, the air turned damp and close. At a dead end, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] slipped unseen while [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] spoke to a projected figure—distorted, inhuman, bound to a speaking stone etched with Daedric runes. When [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] turned, he found himself cornered.

His explanations rang almost true: [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Tharn\|House Tharn]], charity, surplus wealth. Almost.

Footsteps thundered down the tunnel—something large, fast, reptilian. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] struck first, paralyzing [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]]. The charging beast fell short, skidding to a halt with a dagger buried in its skull.

From the shadows stepped [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]], cloaked now in blacker purpose. He laughed at the lie of [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Tharn\|House Tharn]] and, seeing their impasse, took control. What followed was swift and brutal—answers torn loose piece by piece, until [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] fought back with desperate strength and broke [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]’s nose.

Dark smoke swallowed them both.

When they returned, it was with screams and shattered stone. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] lived, barely. Terrified, broken, he spoke freely at last. He told of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silver Road]], of brimstone gold, of meetings beneath the city, of a voice that spoke of a Grand Architect pleased by bargains struck. Nothing more. Nothing less.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] left his fate to the Fangs. They chose leverage over blood, binding [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] as a double agent once he healed. Names were exchanged—[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Mortgren]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]—threads tied back to older horrors.

A crow guided them from the sewers. At dusk, they returned to the guildhall. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] waited, already seated. When [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] showed him the reconstructed letter, the shape of their shared enemy sharpened further.

Favors were called in. Threats were softened. Lodging was paid.

That night, the trio returned to the Frostgate Hall beneath a city that gleamed with coin and rot alike. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]’s mind reeled, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]’s hope wavered, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] felt the slow certainty of a deeper design pressing inward.

Above [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]], crows watched from the eaves.

And somewhere in the dark, bargains were being weighed.
