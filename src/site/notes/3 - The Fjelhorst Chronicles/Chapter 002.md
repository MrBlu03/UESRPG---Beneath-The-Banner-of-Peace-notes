**7th of Heartfire, 2E 600 (continued)**

[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]] sat low along the riverbank, its muddy paths and gray sky mirroring the mood of its people. When the party arrived, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Alderman Torgir]] greeted them with no warmth. Suspicion weighed heavy in his tone—until they mentioned [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]. At that, his posture straightened, and though his words remained guarded, he allowed them into his confidence. [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Snow-Shod\|Clan Snow-Shod]], he confirmed, had turned the river against them. And the rumors? True. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Torgir]] had been meeting a so-called "concerned kinsman" under cover of darkness, near the dam constructed by the Snow-Shods upstream.

The party followed the river's course to that dam, trekking along a rugged trail flanked by drooping trees and moss-covered stones. Snow-Shod guards stood posted there, stiff-backed and armed. They refused passage—until [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] stepped forward, voice firm and low, invoking the Queen's name. Reluctantly, the guards relented.

The dam reeked of tampering. Broken tools lay discarded in the reeds, and a trail of footprints cut into the mud, leading toward the dense woods. A thin curl of smoke rose above the canopy—not far. Memories of their last ambush lingered like a shadow over the party's movements. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] advanced openly, while [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] kept a loose distance with bow in hand. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] vanished into the brush, silent as moonlight.

But the clearing they reached held no foe. Only a smoldering campfire, and the lingering heat of a presence just fled. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] searched the tent and found another note bearing the now-familiar sigil of the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|coiled serpent]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] spotted drag marks near the firepit—fresh—and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] retrieved the charred fragments of another note from the embers.

The trail led them deeper into the trees. And there, at the base of a gnarled oak, they found the kinsman: a Dunmer man, face slack, limbs askew, purple staining his lips. Poison. A brutal one—concentrated nightshade. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s spines flared at the sight. She spat, calling it justice. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], with quiet understanding, offered her warhammer. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] took it without pause, and with a single strike, ensured the Dunmer would not rise again.

His name, they learned from a journal at his side, was [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith Velan]]. He had not come to help [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]]—but to inflame it. Working for the serpent faction, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith]] had blocked the flow regulators in the dam to stoke discord between the fishermen and the Snow-Shods.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] slung the corpse over his shoulder with the ease of habit, dragging it back to the dam like refuse. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] dove into the frigid water, clearing the blockages with practiced precision. The dam groaned—and the river surged anew.

Back in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Torgir]] met them with something like gratitude. He promised to reach out to [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Snow-Shod\|Clan Snow-Shod]], to attempt a fragile reconciliation. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], unimpressed by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Torgir]]'s earlier frost, asked for compensation. The alderman hesitated—then conceded. Gold would come, once the nets were full again.

They returned to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith]]'s corpse in tow, delivering it to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sigrid\|Sigrid]] in the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall of the Dead]]. The mortician agreed to study the poison and send word of her findings.

As they passed through the market, a drunken brawl had drawn a small crowd. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], ever the opportunist, placed a bet on the larger brawler—and walked away grinning, a few coins heavier.

At [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil Keep]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]] received them once more. Their reward was generous—but her unease was plain. The serpent sigil, and the deadly poison, pointed to something larger than a simple border feud. Before dismissing them, she gave a quiet warning: their service may soon be required again.

That night, the party returned to the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]]. Mead flowed freely, firelight danced across worn wood and weary faces, and—for a time—the weight of the day slipped from their shoulders.
