## Chapter 12: Embers in the Dark

**18th of Frostfall, 2E 600**

Morning broke cold over the Rift, mist clinging to the streets outside [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ysmirs_Hall\|Ysmir's hall]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] rose first, taking vegetables to a corner, still weary from the unrest with [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Dres\|House Dres]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] followed, chewing meat as she leafed through a spell tome. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] lingered by the fire, shivering against the chill. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] sat apart with his portion of meat, silence heavy in the hall.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] knelt in her usual place. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] approached, asking of woads. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] explained their markings—meant for the face, but sometimes stretched across the body. She promised to teach [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] when time allowed. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] asked after a lute, and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] spoke of Skyrim's warrior-poets and the skalds who would help her find one.

By midmorning, two guards arrived, summoning them to the war room. The wind was sharp, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] muttered at the cold. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] asked after her, but pressed no further. In the street, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik the Quick]] darted past once again, vaulting over [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s latest failed trip.

Within the war room, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]] awaited. The queen spoke of Jorunn the Skald-King, who saw shadows of the [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]] in current threats. He had sent word west to Morthal, to an old ally. The [[Info/Organizations/Other/Mages_Guild\|Mages Guild]], meanwhile, would soon arrive at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] to study the strange ritual orb. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] recognized the name [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle Benele]], though the memory eluded her.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Mortgren]] then gave his findings. The vampire head he had studied once belonged to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Thrysnr_the_Unsullied\|Thrysnr the Unsullied]], of Volkihar blood—but sired by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn the Ancient]], thought dead for twenty years. Majorn's name still carried weight, and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Mortgren]] warned them to speak it rarely. He would remain in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] for a time, sifting records and reinforcing wards. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] lingered, intent on speaking with him.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]] then told them of a fire in the Ratway. Many blamed the guards, though she suspected the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]. She asked the party to uncover the truth, and rebuild trust with the city's poorest. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] suggested bringing food; Freidr agreed, sending them to the kitchens.

The cook, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Brann\|Brann]], loaded a basket of bread, cheese, and ham. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] carried it as they set out.

At the market, they asked questions. Guards recalled smoke rising from the manholes south of the stalls, late afternoon. A cabbage-seller at first scoffed, but laughed at [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s strange cabbage-phrased queries. Amused, he directed them to Toren in the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Outlaws_Refuge\|Outlaw's Refuge]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] bought cabbages as thanks, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] paying five gold for one.

They descended into the Ratway, arriving at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ragged_Flagon\|Ragged Flagon]], a tavern of smoke and shadow. The barkeep confirmed the fire began deeper in the warrens. He pointed to a donation box, where [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] placed the food.

A drunk named Yannick loudly blamed the guards, faltering when pressed by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]. A whistle from a dark-cornered patron silenced him, the man then pointing them to Toren.

Toren, a fence, asked gold for his words. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] haggled him down to thirty. The masked arsonists had worn strange leathers, set the fire near catacombs, and slipped away. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] then struck a strange deal, giving Toren five hundred gold for a future moonsugar venture. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], meanwhile, tried to sell her old necromancy tome for fifteen hundred. The offer came back at thirty, but with [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s help she walked away with seventy, pocketing twenty and putting fifty in the shared coffer.

The cornered patron demanded a blood contract for his truth. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] signed, owing a future favor. The man then spoke: the arsonists' leathers bore runes, possibly Daedric. The blaze was fed by more than oil. A serpent mark blackened one archway. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], curious about the sudden growth of the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ragged_Flagon\|Ragged Flagon]], asked the masked patron what caused such a sudden stir. He explained that many of them came from Windhelm, where outlaw activity had been disrupted by bigger fish. 

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] guessed the fire a distraction, meant to enter the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall of the Dead]]. The group rushed there. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sigrid\|Sigrid]], keeper of the dead, tore through her shelves in panic—her autopsy notes on the fallen Dunmer were gone. There had been no forced entry. The suspicion became certainty.

They returned to Freidr, laying out what they had found. She reimbursed [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] for the gold he had spent and granted them wide authority to pursue the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]. Papers would follow in days, but for now the hunt was theirs.

The party retired to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ysmirs_Hall\|Ysmir's hall]] as the night deepened, the scent of rain and smoke still clinging to their cloaks.
