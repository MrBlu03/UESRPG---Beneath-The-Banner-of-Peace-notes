## Chapter 24 – A Web of False Faces

**Sundas, 7th of Evening Star, 2E 600**

The morning came cold, more bitter than any day before. Frost still clung to the window sills, stubborn in its refusal to yield to the passing of time.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] rose early, as he often did, and made his way downstairs for a well-earned meal. Sweet pork, as had become his habit in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]]. The warmth of the food and the noise of the inn settled him into a quiet corner, helmet covering his face in-between bites of food.

The inn was busy, but less so than he had gotten used to. Faces usually familiar had been absent, replaced with individuals that belonged a little too well, adventurers formed from a template. He had seen their faces in the last few days, hanging around and chatting up anyone with grievances. Drawing conclusions from the complaints he received, it seemed the more common folk were being priced out of lodging.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] woke not long after. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] complained of the cold before quickly retreating downstairs in search of warmth and food, while [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] lingered a moment longer, placing a small piece of bread by the window before following. They shared a simple breakfast—roasted vegetables, fresh and warm—prepared by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Orgnar\|Orgnar]], whose work never seemed to slow.

Afterwards, the two made their way to the stables, where [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] was already present. She sat beside [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]], surrounded by crumpled sheets of parchment scattered in the snow, each a failed attempt to capture her mare’s likeness. The conversation that followed was quieter, heavier.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] spoke of her encounter with a hagraven, a threat left unresolved. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] remarked that such things rarely stayed dormant for long. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] filled [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] in on the bounty completed in her absence, while [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], in turn, shared her own findings—an investigation into a house tied, unofficially, to the steward. What she found there unsettled them all: a Daedric ritual site hidden beneath, human skin, a full skeleton, and notes detailing the imitation of Nord mannerisms and speech.

Combined with what [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] had uncovered in the Jarl’s quarters, a grim thought began to take shape. Whether the Jarl was still himself—or something else entirely—remained uncertain, but the implications were clear enough.

For now, however, their attention turned elsewhere. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]’s riddle lingered in their minds, but another lead demanded action first: the warehouse marked on Vaelin’s map, situated northeast of the city.

They gathered [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], who had finished his meal and donned his armor once more, and set out.

---

On the road, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] revealed the full extent of her plan.

Days prior, she had fed false information to agents of the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]], convincing them that Vaelin possessed something of interest. Now, she intended to use that lie to their advantage. From a hidden cache within the sewers, they retrieved disguises—garb meant to resemble those same agents. Donning them outside the city walls, they would ensure any witnesses drew the wrong conclusions.

From there, they continued northward.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] briefly checked her wolf traps along the way, though none had been sprung. The forest remained still.

---

The warehouse stood alone in a clearing, pressed against a cliffside. Quiet. Too quiet.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] moved ahead, circling behind and climbing onto the roof for a better vantage point. From above, they counted four guards. One stood apart—larger, wrong somehow. Horns protruded from its head, its armor unlike anything they had seen before.

Without hesitation, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] signaled.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] nudged [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], and the two moved in openly. A crack of lightning split the air as [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] unleashed a blast of chain lightning into the guards. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] followed at his own pace, steady and unshaken, closing the distance with deliberate steps before engaging.

Another of the horned creatures emerged. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] acted quickly, tossing a frenzy bomb between them. The two creatures turned on each other in a burst of violence, buying the others time.

From above, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] struck where she could, unseen, her arrows finding their marks with precision.

The fight was swift, but not without chaos. One of the horned beings fell. The other, shaking off the frenzy, retreated into the warehouse.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], meanwhile, found himself faced not with a soldier, but a worker—unarmed, panicked, rushing him with a dagger. Rather than strike him down, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] simply tackled the man and held him there, pinning him beneath his weight with little effort.

---

Inside, the remaining creature awaited them.

It stepped out from behind the crates, revealing itself fully—no mere guard, but something else entirely. A servant of the Lord of Bargains, it claimed, its voice calm despite the bloodshed.

It offered them a deal.

The lives of the workers… in exchange for its freedom.

None of them liked it. But they accepted.

The creature left without resistance, slipping past them and out into the wilds. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] lunged after it at the last moment, but was too slow. It was gone.

---

With the threat gone, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] turned her attention to the workers. She ushered them outside, questioning them briefly. They spoke of wages from the Silver Road, but their bodies told another story—marks of shackles on wrists and ankles.

Slaves, in all but name.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]’s tone shifted. She warned them of the danger within the city, offering them a choice: flee, or seek refuge below. She directed them to the sewers, to a woman named [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tarja\|Tarja]], who would help them if they spoke her name.

They left quickly.

---

The Fangs searched the warehouse thoroughly.

Among the crates, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] found markings he recognized—wax seals identical to those he had seen during the guild’s shipments. Inside were the same materials: coin molds, animal parts, ritual components.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] uncovered a hidden ledger behind stacked crates. Its contents confirmed their fears. Shipments logged. Payments to bandits. Lists of “cargo”—names of missing people.

And a final note: goods being sent to a main facility, somewhere in the hills to the north. No precise location, only distance—half a day’s travel.

They gathered what they could—provisions, medical supplies, and anything of value. From the fallen creature, they took its armor, forged from a material none of them recognized.

---

By the time they returned to the city, the sun had begun its slow descent.

With their findings laid bare, their path forward became clearer, though no less dangerous. And still, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]’s riddle lingered in their minds.

A thread yet to be pulled.
