## Chapter 10: The Beach Episode

**8th of Frostfall, 2E 600**

The party awoke in the stone hall of the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]]. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] tended to [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], who remained unconscious, her face painted with protective runes. She had not stirred since the lodge, and her body remained pale and still.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] rose first, drawn by the scent of cooked hog and fresh mead. He ate with purpose. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], after checking on the Dragon Priest mask under her bed, wrapped it in a blanket and joined the meal with a plate of fish. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] woke last, groggy and quiet.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] stirred before midday. Confused but stable, she asked [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] where she was, then quietly mentioned the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|Crow Father]] as the last being she spoke to. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] said little.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] presented the wrapped mask to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]. She examined it, but declared its magic gone and advised [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] to keep it.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] explained the painted runes as "Woad," an old form of protective magic. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] inquired if they could be used elsewhere, but [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] said they were made for the face.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] told [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]]'s deaths. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] only asked if they got paid. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] tossed her coin, which she missed while putting on her chestplate.

---

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] suggested a lake trip to unwind. After brief hesitation, the others agreed. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] would be asked first.

As they left the hall, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] murmured something about feathers.

---

They passed [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik the Quick]] sprinting through town. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] tried to trip him for fun, but failed so badly it planted a seed for a new goal.

At [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]]'s shop, her small husky companion John greeted them. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] proposed a trip for rest, gathering, and fishing. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] accepted without hesitation.

At the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]], now home to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonya]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] asked [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] to come along. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] agreed, bringing [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] with him. A meeting was arranged at the gate.

---

The party gathered near the docks, where [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] arrived with gear. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] recommended a clearing a few hours north, often used for hunting stag.

They passed through [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]] and stopped to collect their reward. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] attempted to push for extra, but the alderman stood firm. Each received 50 gold.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] learned John was a stray, taken in by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] three years ago.

---

By late afternoon, they reached the clearing and began setting up camp.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] fished by the lake. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] joined and caught salmon with her hands.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] tried trapping, but with no success. Switching to tracking, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] showed her skill. After a long trail and several arrows, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] brought down a stag. The antlers alone were worth a small fortune.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] prepared stew while [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sketched quietly by the lakeside, recording what she remembered from her visions.

That evening, stories were shared. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] spoke of a spriggan friend and asked [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] about her tribe. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] opened up about her mother, who died in a war when a wall collapsed. She mentioned keeping notes on travelers, including one who spoke of dragons 14 years ago. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] confirmed that dragons had returned.

---

That night, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] dreamed of war. Familiar armor, comrades falling, and a snowy castle bearing the same emblem he wore.

---

**9th of Frostfall**

A cold fog greeted the dawn. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] rose early and rekindled the fire. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] checked the meat. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]], and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] followed. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] emerged from the lake, startling [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]]'s scream woke [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]].

Later, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] found a rabbit in one of their traps. A Khajiit named [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Maiq_the_Liar\|M'aiq]] appeared, muttered strange wisdom, then left.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] joined the others for a bear hunt. The group succeeded, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] helped drag it back.

That evening, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sketched Gareth. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] noticed, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] quietly shared that she had seen him during her coma—and that she had once been forced to kill him.

Each party member received 11 rations from the hunt.

---

**10th of Frostfall**

The group packed camp. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] scouted the area one final time, making sure they didn't leave anything behind that could be bad for the wildlife.

The journey back passed quietly. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] were dropped off at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]], then [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] returned to her shop with a final scratch behind John's ears.

By afternoon, the party retired once more to the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall of Ysmir]].
