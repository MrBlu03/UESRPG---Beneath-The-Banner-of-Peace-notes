## Chapter 25 – Beneath the Sealed Stone

**Sundas, 7th of Evening Star, 2E 600 — Morndas, 8th of Evening Star, 2E 600**

Snow drifted through [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]] in thin, wind-driven veils as [[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|the Fangs of Fjelhorst]] returned from the northern warehouse. The city seemed quieter than before, though whether from fear, exhaustion, or the weight of secrets gathering beneath its streets, none among them could say. The pale light of late afternoon hung low upon the rooftops, and smoke rose from hearths into a sky the color of cold iron.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] said little upon their return. He ordered a plate of sweet pork at the inn and ate with the steady silence of a soldier long accustomed to taking comfort where he could find it. The others gathered afterward within the cramped room shared by [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], where maps, scraps of parchment, and the cryptic verse they had recovered soon covered the table between them.

The riddle lingered heavily in the room.

_“When the Gate of Silver feeds the wandering road…”_

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] read the lines more than once, her voice low beneath the crackling hearth. The words carried the shape of old Nord verse — the kind meant less to instruct than to obscure. A trail hidden beneath memory and myth.

They spent the remainder of the evening chasing fragments through [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]].

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] wandered from tavern to tavern beneath gathering snowfall, listening more than she spoke. Sailors, hunters, ferrymen, and old retainers all carried half-remembered stories. She heard of feuding jarls whose names had long vanished from proper histories, of a pale figure seen standing beside the river mists south of the city, and of hunters who still used ancient standing stones to guide themselves through the forests during heavy snow.

Meanwhile, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] disappeared into the city archives and private libraries, where dust and silence ruled more faithfully than any steward.

The first clue emerged from a weathered copy of _On the Old Holds_, whose pages spoke at length upon the rivers of the Rift before the coming of the Alessian reforms. Beside one passage describing the Widow’s River, a faded note had been scrawled in cramped ink by some forgotten scholar simply marked _E._ The marginalia pointed toward another volume entirely: _Sagas of the Broken Jarls_.

There, amidst brittle pages and fragmentary lays, they found mention of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hadring_the_Unlucky\|Hadring the Unlucky]] — a jarl betrayed during a winter war whose widow was said to have wandered the southern banks in mourning until her death. The river thereafter bore her name among local clans, though the title had faded from common use generations ago.

The second trail proved narrower still.

Within the fourth lay of the same saga appeared mention of the Three Stone Brothers — not monuments, but warriors once sworn to Hadring’s banner. Another annotation, this one signed _T.E._, directed them toward _On the Early Jarls of the Rift_, wherein the brothers’ final stand was recorded beside a ridge south of the river valleys.

At last, using an old surveyor’s text — _A Survey of the Eastern Valleys of the Rift_ — they began stitching the fragments together. Rivers, ridges, abandoned roads, and forgotten cairns slowly aligned upon the map before them until one lonely region of wilderness emerged as the only place where all the clues converged.

The hall of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Oath-Breaker_King\|the Oath-Breaker King]].

And beneath it, perhaps, the dead who were never mourned.

---

They departed before dawn the following morning.

The snow had ceased during the night, though a pale fog still clung low across the valleys as they followed the Widow’s River southward. The waters twisted endlessly through the wilderness, vanishing and reappearing between black pines and snow-covered stone.

Near midday they reached a fork where the river divided around a cluster of frozen marshland. Unsure which path to follow, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] proposed they split briefly to search both banks. A coin decided it.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] turned left along the narrower stream while [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] followed the opposite branch.

The forest deepened quickly. Snow muffled their footsteps, and the world seemed to narrow into pale fog and dark trunks. Yet before long [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] found themselves halted by cliffs and tangled stone, the path ending in silence.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] muttered the words of her newly learned spell, and pale magelight shimmered faintly around her hand. The thread of Clairvoyance emerged before them like moonlight upon water, guiding them back through the trees toward the others.

They found [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] standing motionless at the edge of a dark lake.

The wind had risen sharply. Mist rolled across the water in slow sheets, and in the center of the lake stood a tall pale figure, impossibly still amidst the drifting fog.

No one spoke.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] stepped cautiously forward, eyes fixed upon the shape. At first it resembled a woman standing ankle-deep in the water, though something about its stillness felt wrong. Too perfect. Too empty.

Then the mist shifted.

The creature’s limbs stretched thin as dead branches, its face hollow and pale as drowned bone.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] stopped immediately.

_“Back away,”_ she whispered. _“Slowly.”_

None argued.

They retreated step by measured step, never turning their backs upon the thing in the lake. Only once the fog had swallowed it fully did they break into a hurried flight through the trees, branches clawing at cloaks and armor as they fled the shoreline.

Even [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] said little afterward.

Some time later, as the ridges began to rise around them, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] finally spotted the standing stones.

Three weatherworn monoliths overlooked the valley from a narrow ridge, their surfaces scarred by age and half-buried beneath snow. The markings upon them had long since eroded beyond easy reading.

It was [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] who first noticed the crow.

The bird sat silently atop the center stone, black against the white sky. It neither fled nor cried out. It simply watched them.

Then it took flight.

The Fangs followed.

The crow guided them through narrow passages between stone outcroppings and snow-choked paths scarcely visible beneath the drifts. Several times it vanished entirely, only to reappear farther ahead as though waiting for them to catch up.

At last it led them to a ruin half-buried in the mountainside.

Ancient carvings framed the entrance, worn nearly smooth by centuries of wind and ice. Comparing the structure against their maps and notes, they found little room for doubt.

This was the tomb of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Oath-Breaker_King\|the Oath-Breaker King]].

They entered with weapons drawn.

Yet the ruin remained strangely silent.

No chanting echoed through the halls. No draugr stirred from alcoves blackened by age. Only the groan of distant stone and the drip of water broke the stillness as they advanced deeper beneath the mountain.

Even so, the tomb had not been abandoned entirely to time.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] narrowly avoided several ancient traps hidden beneath dust and frost, while [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] suffered the worst of them when a concealed flail swung violently from a doorway and struck him squarely in the chest. The blow nearly dropped him where he stood, though he pressed onward after catching his breath.

Not all the ruin spoke only of death.

In one chamber they discovered a deep stone pit filled with black water disappearing into unseen depths below. While the others debated caution, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] removed her cloak and descended without hesitation.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] held magelight above the water while the Argonian vanished beneath the dark surface.

When she emerged again, soaked and grinning despite the cold, she carried old jewelry and handfuls of ancient coinage recovered from the depths — offerings perhaps cast there centuries before the fall of the old kingdom.

Far below the mountain, the dead still kept their treasures.

---

Eventually they reached a great sealed door.

Voices murmured beyond it.

The Fangs exchanged wary glances. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] knocked once upon the ancient stone before stepping back with her bow already drawn.

The door opened.

An Argonian regarded them calmly from the threshold.

Beyond him, seated at a table amidst maps, candles, and scattered notes, waited the man they knew as [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|Sadrith Indoril]] — though by then none among them truly believed that name belonged to him.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] had hidden himself deep beneath the mountains.

There, within the forgotten tomb of a long-dead king, the Fangs recounted all that had transpired since their last meeting: the ritual circle beneath the steward’s estate, the hidden warehouse of the Silver Road, the enslaved laborers, the Daedra they had fought, and the growing suspicion that a greater facility lay hidden somewhere farther north.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] listened without interruption.

Only when [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] described the ritual materials found at the estate — the skeleton, the preserved flesh, the human hide stretched for unknown purpose — did his expression darken.

He spoke then of Shadowfen during the years of the Planemeld, where alchemists had once practiced vile rites to steal the memories and identities of their victims. Those creatures, he explained, had been crude fabrications born of Dominion experimentation.

_“But this…”_ he murmured, turning the warm stone [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] had recovered over in his hand, _“this bears the touch of Oblivion.”_

He set the stone aside carefully.

When [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] revealed the horn taken from the warehouse, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] studied it only briefly before confirming their fears.

It belonged to a Skaafin.

A servant of [[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]].

Whatever role the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silver Road]] played in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]], Daedric influence now stood plainly within it.

The Fangs questioned him in turn — why he had abandoned the guildhouse so suddenly, and why he now hid among tombs and forgotten dead.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] explained that someone within [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]] had begun scrying the city. Remaining within the blind spot he had created around the abandoned guildhouse would only draw greater suspicion in time. So he had fled into lands few still remembered, where old graves and buried kings offered better shelter than walls ever could.

As for the draugr resting deeper within the ruin, he assured them [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Oath-Breaker_King\|the Oath-Breaker King]] remained no cursed tyrant.

Merely dead.

No profane magic bound him to waking.

In the end they agreed upon a course forward. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] would investigate the location hinted at within the recovered ledger while the Fangs remained hidden within the ruin until his return.

Night settled heavily over the mountain by the time they unrolled their sleeping blankets beside the cold stone walls.

Far above them, snow buried the roads of the Rift beneath white silence.

And deeper still beneath the mountain, the forgotten dead slept on, undisturbed by the living who now walked among them.
