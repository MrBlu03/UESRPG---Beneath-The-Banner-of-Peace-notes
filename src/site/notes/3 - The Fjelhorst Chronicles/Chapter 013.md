## Chapter 13: The Road to Faldarstad

**26th of Frostfall, 2E 600**

Cold clung to the Rift when the party rose. Upstairs in the hall, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] returned a book to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]], who was preparing for departure. She spoke of a sacred site in Eastmarch calling her, but assured them their paths would cross again.

Not long after, a guard stumbled into [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ysmirs_Hall\|Ysmir's Hall]], cold breath passing through his helm. Before they could set out for [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], they were summoned to the stables.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] requested warmer cloaks, so they stopped first at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Borans_Bulwarks\|Boran's Bulwarks]], selling pelts and pooling the coin into the communal coffer.

At the stables, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]] awaited with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hrovald\|Hrovald]], the stablemaster. She reminded them that her envoys could not ride swayback nags trailing merchant trains. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hrovald\|Hrovald]] had chosen finer stock. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] was paired with [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Rimefang]], a massive iron-grey gelding, calm and commanding. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] received [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Sunsnow]], a dappled cream mare with bright, curious eyes. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] was given [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Mossback]], a chestnut gelding with a gentle but wary spirit. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] took [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]], a coal-black mare with a white streak and a fierce, restless temper.

With packs lashed and tack fitted, they rode west toward [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] and whatever waited there.

The fog lifted by midday, a pale sky stretching above them, the north wind biting their cloaks. At camp that night, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] returned from a hunt with a fox pelt and meat. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] played her lute as [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sketched a harness for [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]], marked with her family's heraldry, before falling asleep against her horse.

---

**27th of Frostfall**

Heavy fog rolled from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Lake_Honrich\|Lake Honrich]], dampening their gear and bones alike. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], who had slept against [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]] rather than in the tent, woke groggy and stiff, though frostbite spared her.

By midday the fog burned away, the pale autumn sun blazing on the lake's wide waters. Near camp, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] found an old cairn slumped under moss. She excused herself despite complaints. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] knew what she was doing, but wanted to watch over her. After reassuring them, she sat by the cairn and followed the Archmagus's lessons. Even as snow drifted down from the north, she sat unmoving until sleep came.

---

**28th of Frostfall**

The world glittered under hoarfrost. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] tried teaching [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Mossback]] the "stay" command, but he stubbornly followed her whenever she walked away.

Near midday, they encountered [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Maiq_the_Liar\|M'aiq the Liar]] once more. He rambled about kerchiefs, Dunmer, and marriage, and vanished as quickly as he had come.

That afternoon, a sudden squall struck, snow whipping sideways. Just as quickly, the skies cleared. At camp, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] hunted for bear, but the snowfall had covered tracks. Instead, they gathered berries and herbs for the horses.

---

**29th of Frostfall**

The morning was bitter, the forest cloaked in ice fog. Crystals clung to cloaks, packs, and even [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s whiskers.

By midday the sun cut through, though the northeast wind bit like a knife. By late afternoon, the ice groaned across [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Lake_Honrich\|Lake Honrich]], echoing in the distance.

That night the sky erupted with green-blue auroras. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sketched the scene in her journal. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] played an upbeat tune, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] danced, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] hammered the rhythm on his armor.

---

**30th of Frostfall**

Snow fell steady and thick at dawn, muffling the forest. At last, [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Mossback]] obeyed [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s "stay," earning berries as a reward.

By midday the snowfall worsened but eased by afternoon. They camped near a small shrine to the Fox and Hawk, where a hunter left offerings. He spoke of a white stag seen in the woods, to be hunted once the snow lifted. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] offered aid, and the hunter directed them to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Denskar\|Denskar]] at [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Wounded_Boar\|The Wounded Boar]] inn.

---

**31st of Frostfall**

The dawn was savage. Every branch and blade of grass was crusted in frost, shining like glass in the sun.

By midday, they neared [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. Cresting a hill, the valley spread wide—longhouses and lodges smoking, hides drying on racks, dogs barking at the gates. The stronghold sat by the river's curve, defended by log palisades and timber towers. Banners of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Faldar\|Clan Faldar]] hung green and silver, a stag's head marked bold upon them.

The guards questioned them bluntly at the gate, but admitted them once [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] showed the Queen's writ. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl Gorm]] was too deep in clan business to see them.

They spent the day bonding with their mounts. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] demonstrated proper care, and by evening they retired to their rooms—[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] sharing one to spare the coffer. Their next step: to investigate the strange hunters who had frozen stiff on a clear day.
