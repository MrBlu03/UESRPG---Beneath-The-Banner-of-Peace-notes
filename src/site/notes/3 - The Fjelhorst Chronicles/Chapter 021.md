## Chapter 21 — Ash and Brimstone

**Tirdas, 25th of Sun’s Dusk, 2E 600**

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] woke first.

For once, he did not reach for the full weight of his armor. Only the helmet found its place, the familiar enclosure settling over his thoughts as much as his features. He went downstairs while the inn still lingered between quiet and noise, claimed a corner table, and removed the helmet only after sitting — his back turned toward the hall, as though the wall itself were preferable company.

Breakfast was taken without ceremony. Voices rose and fell behind him, unstudied and indistinct.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] had been awake long before that.

At the stables, she sat beside [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]] as if no time had passed since the day before. The mare stood patient and still, ears flicking only occasionally while [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sketched her in careful lines. There was a steadiness to it — not repetition, but reassurance — as though committing [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]] to parchment reaffirmed something unspoken.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] rose next.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] instructed [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] to fetch breakfast and wait in their room. There was intent in her voice, measured and deliberate. Some matters required fewer ears.

She sought [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] first, requesting her presence upstairs to discuss their next move regarding the steward. Then she turned to find [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] — and did not recognize him.

The man seated alone without helm or armor did not match the outline she expected. Rather than linger in uncertainty, she asked the innkeep, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Orgnar\|Orgnar]]**, whether he had seen [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] that morning. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Orgnar\|Orgnar]] gestured without hesitation toward the unarmored figure in the corner.

Recognition came with surprise.

At the sound of her approach, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] replaced his helmet before turning, the steel closing again over expression. Some habits endured regardless of circumstance.

They gathered in [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]’s room.

There, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] recounted his movements of the 22nd: the negotiations with the **[[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silver Road]]**, the presence of **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]]**, and the offer extended to him — both to investigate the one known as **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]** and the possibility of future employment within Vaelin’s sphere of influence. Whether that sphere was the Silver Road itself or something less openly named remained uncertain.

In turn, the others brought him fully into their own discoveries.

They had been hired by the steward to investigate [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]], only to uncover his true identity. They had followed [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] into the sewers and witnessed the Daedric speaking stone. They had heard confession and implication alike — threads tying the matter to the **[[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]]**, and to coin that carried the scent of brimstone.

The steward believed he was searching for a troublesome Dunmer. The truth was wider, and darker.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]’s answer was deception.

They would report that the old guildhouse had been abandoned. They would present forged correspondence suggesting a leak within the Silver Road — that someone had warned the Dunmer occupant to flee. The letter would allude to ledgers sent with a Dunmer caravan, to the distribution of strange gold, to its origin beyond Nirn, and to the involvement of the Silver Road — though never so plainly as to accuse outright. It would raise questions without offering conclusions.

Together, she and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] composed it. Care was taken with tone and phrasing, enough detail to anchor suspicion, enough ambiguity to invite investigation. The page was singed at the edges before being folded, as though rescued from attempted destruction.

By midday, they were on their way to the Long Hall.

Along the streets, the crowd pressed close. At one point, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] heard the word _n’wah_ carried through the movement — the speaker unseen, swallowed by passing forms. None lingered long enough to claim it.

Crows followed overhead.

Their presence did not feel accidental.

Guards halted the party briefly at the entrance to the Long Hall, though after inspection they were permitted within. Inside, they were received by **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius Caro]]**.

The meeting unfolded more smoothly than expected.

The steward received the letter with measured interest, though its implications of Dunmer conspiracy seemed to stir little reaction. It was only when [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] spoke the name [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]] that Caro’s composure shifted — subtly, but undeniably. Agreement followed that the letter should be placed directly into Vaelin’s hands.

Payment was discussed. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] secured eight hundred gold for each of them, save for [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], whose involvement had not been part of the original arrangement. The steward paid without resistance.

On departure, the party noted the change of guard at the entrance. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] observed that such rotations occurred frequently — a matter of security, or perhaps unease.

From there, they turned toward the abandoned Black-Foot guildhouse.

All entered without hesitation save [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], who paused briefly at the threshold before following.

Within, they asked to speak with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] — the Imperial apprehended in the sewers. While he was being summoned, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] addressed [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] with questions of her own, describing her sister’s disappearance and the rumors of witches and keeps.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] could not name a single answer, but he offered possibilities: **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistwatch\|Mistwatch]]**, a spiritual site long whispered about; **Skuldafn**, a ruin bound to old Nordic rites; and **Direfrost Keep**, abandoned by witchhunters yet not by rumor. He wished her well, though certainty eluded him.

Before granting them entry into his Crow’s Wood, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] spoke plainly about [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]]’s condition. The brief transport into the Wailing Prison had scarred him more deeply than anticipated. The trauma had fractured something essential. Whatever plans they had entertained of turning him into a double agent were no longer possible.

They found him seated at a table, eating slowly.

His answers confirmed more than they revealed. He had reported purchases and distributions to the speaking stone, yet knew nothing of its workings or the identity of the voice beyond. He had always entered the guildhouse through its front doors, unaware of hidden passages or inner workings. He was a courier of coin, not of knowledge.

Nearby stood **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**, tending quietly to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]]’s fractured state.

At [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]’s request, they brought [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] with them to recover the speaking stone. They descended once more into the sewers, passing the site of the summoning ritual and the fallen cultists. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] recounted the brief clash with the Daedroths, the thrown axe that had ended in unintended prey.

The stone was retrieved without incident.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] noted that its connection felt weak — thinner than before. Back in the guildhouse, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] confirmed the sensation. The link had not degraded; rather, it seemed to reach beyond Nirn entirely, likely into Oblivion or into some realm obscured from mortal perception.

The implication lingered heavier than the object itself.

When no further answers presented themselves, the Fangs returned to the inn.

Rest came not from resolution, but from necessity. The city continued to breathe around them — coin changing hands, guards rotating posts, crows circling unseen currents overhead.

Brimstone clung to memory.

And beneath [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]]’s commerce, something patient endured.
