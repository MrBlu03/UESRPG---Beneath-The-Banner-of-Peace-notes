## Chapter 7: The Return
**24th of Heartfire, 2E 600**  
The party made final preparations to leave [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Fort Greenwall]]. Before they departed, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod Wraithbane]] approached them with a grim warning. "You walk among dangerous powers now," he said, casting a glance toward the chamber where the orb lay. "Choose your allies with care."

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] hoisted the severed vampire's head into a cloth sack—grisly proof of their victory.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], seeking answers from the orb, released a bolt of lightning into its core. In a flash of light, she was consumed by a vision: pale moonlight filtering through a snowy glade, and within it, unnatural shapes—undead werewolves. When the others pressed her, she could not explain what it meant, only that it felt deeply wrong.

While the wounded man recovered under [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hillevi\|Hillevi]]'s care, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]] stepped forward and went pale. "I know him. That's [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Wuunferth\|Wuunferth]]. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Wuunferth\|Wuunferth the Hunter]]."

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hillevi\|Hillevi]] admitted she had done all she could, but there was one last hope: a place of ancient power deep in the Hirsing Range, known as [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistwatch\|Mistwatch]]. She would take him there in search of healing from the Old Gods.

Before they left, a hawk swept through the rafters and landed on [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod]]'s shoulder. He read the note it carried, then turned to the party. Reinforcements would arrive by the next day. The [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] would remain behind to secure [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] and bury the dead. The orb, he said, was too dangerous to leave unguarded.

He instructed the party to return to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] and report directly to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]. And once the snows melted, they were to meet [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hillevi\|Hillevi]] at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistwatch\|Mistwatch]].

As they passed the war camp, they found the still-smoking pyre of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] placed fresh flowers atop the ash. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] quietly drew a periwinkle flower in her journal, then tore out the page and cast it into the flame.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hjar\|Hjar]] met them at the edge of the woods, giving them rations and a simple farewell: "Tell my brother I still owe him a bottle of mead."

The first day on the road was difficult. Fatigue weighed on them, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] stumbled, twisting her ankle. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] struggled too, her shoulders slumping beneath her pack.

That evening, the two of them slipped away to try their forbidden spell: an attempt to resurrect the Bone Hawk. They didn't know [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] had followed, silent in the shadows.

The ritual failed.

The energies of the soul fragments lashed back at [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], filling her with agony. When she collapsed, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], hidden in the shadows, did not intervene—only watched, her eyes wide with unspoken fury. They never knew she had been there.

That night, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] dreamt of the night her family was slaughtered. The blood. The fire. Her mentor's voice lost in the chaos. But there was something more: a throne of stone, an orcish figure looming atop it, wielding a blackened maul. A voice like thunder: _"Feed into anger. Give in to vengeance."_ She awoke clutching her dagger and family pendant, tears on her cheeks.

**25th of Heartfire**  
The morning was crisp but clear. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] spoke only to [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], cooking slaughterfish in silence.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] remained in her tent, eyes hollow. She said little.

They passed the place where [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir]] had cremated the hanging victims days ago. The ashes were cold.

That evening, a squirrel snuck into camp, rifling through their packs. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] startled it, but [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] gently offered it a handful of berries, which it accepted before darting off.

**26th of Heartfire**  
On the road, they crossed paths with a traveling Khajiit merchant bound for [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] inquired after alchemy reagents, but he had none.

Noticing [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s demeanor, the Khajiit offered her a vial—a potion he claimed could produce lucid dreams. She turned it over in her hand but ultimately declined, heeding [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s wary look.

Rain fell by evening, soaking their cloaks. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] stood with her face to the sky, drinking in the moisture. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], ever resourceful, collected more berries.

**27th of Heartfire**  
[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] skipped breakfast again, but [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] insisted she eat. "You won't help anyone by starving yourself."

Later, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] confronted [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] about the growing tension between them. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] finally spoke: she felt betrayed, kept in the dark. Her younger sister had vanished years ago. She feared loss again. This admission shocked [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]—she had never considered that someone might actually care for her. In a rare moment of vulnerability, she subtly hinted that she, too, cared in her own way, though it was clear she wouldn't admit it, not even to herself. Not yet.

That night, a fox appeared at the edge of their camp. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] offered it fish, and it spoke.

It had returned.

She asked it about the scroll—the strange one given to her by one of the talking crows. The fox recognized the script as Dovahzul, the language of dragons. It explained that direct translation was impossible; the language used few words, each imbued with multiple meanings, for dragons speak not with breath but with their soul. Still, it offered an interpretation: doom, secrecy, and silence. It warned [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] to guard the scroll well, and to tell no one.

Hoping for more, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] tried to tear a piece from the scroll—but the parchment would not yield. Instead, she pressed a corner of it into the flames. Immediately, the fire turned black, roaring high into the air like a pillar of shadow. The others leapt up, weapons drawn, but the flames vanished as quickly as they had appeared. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] yanked the scroll back, unharmed. Still, from that moment on, a heavy sense of being watched lingered over them.

That night, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] dreamed again. [[Info/Lore/Malacath\|Malacath]] returned, this time seated on a throne of skulls. He struck the ground with his maul, and the stone turned to blood. She awoke in a panic.

**28th of Heartfire**  
By mid-morning, the spires of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] came into view.

Their first stop was [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]]'s shop. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] broke the news gently: none of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]]'s family had survived. She shut the shop, thanked them for their effort, and went home to grieve.

At the market, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] placed bets on another brawl. Both walked away with a few extra coins.

At the keep, they were intercepted by guards, but upon learning who they were, rushed through the halls to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]'s war room.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] delivered their report. Vampires had taken [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]. They were destroyed, but at great cost.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]] took the vampire's head from [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]. After inspection, he confirmed it belonged to an ancient, rogue member of [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]]. Not pureblooded, but powerful. He took the head for further study.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] spoke of her vision—undead werewolves and the Coldharbour-touched orb. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]'s expression darkened. She recalled the [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]], a sinister alliance of vampires and werebeasts that had once terrorized Tamriel. Over a decade ago, they had experimented with merging their curses, attempting to create hybrid monstrosities. "They were believed destroyed," she said gravely, "but if any survived... the realm must be warned."

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Mortgren]] would travel to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] to study the orb. Freidr would alert the High King and the [[Info/Organizations/Other/Mages_Guild\|Mages Guild]]. She offered them lodging at the keep.

The party first visited [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hafjorg\|Hafjorg]]'s shop, hoping to invite him and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] to join them. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hafjorg\|Hafjorg]], his voice quiet but firm, declined. "I want to mourn them in peace," he said, and gently closed the door behind them.

When they reached the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Amber_Keg\|Amber Keg]], music and laughter greeted them. A celebration was already underway—some marked the changing season, others simply toasted to being alive. The group joined in, raising mugs for [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild]], and all those lost in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]. For a few hours, grief gave way to memory, and memory to song.

And for the first time in many nights, the sky above [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] was clear.
