## Chapter 9 - The Curse of Kahru's Hollow
**4th of Frostfall, 2E 600**

In the early light of Frostfall, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] arrived in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. Her path was purposeful. First, she went to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Borans_Bulwarks\|Boran's Bulwarks]], where the smith offered her iron shards freely from a bucket of scrap. "Take what you need," he grunted. "Was gonna melt it anyway." Three shards in hand, she proceeded to the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Scent_of_Serenity\|Scent of Serenity]]. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]], having helped them before, offered the salt without question. "Seems like you need this more than I do."

Next, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] made for [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil Keep]]. On her way, she caught a glimpse of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik the Quick]]—standing still, for once. She almost didn't believe her eyes.

Inside the keep, she met with the Argonian court mage, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Speaks-Spells-Gently\|Speaks-Spells-Gently]]. After studying the sketch of the mask, he offered solemn insight. "In the days of the Dragon War, those who served the Dragons with utter devotion wore masks like these. Each held great power—symbols of their terrible rank."

When [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] asked about [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]]'s rites, Speaks nodded. "The blessing of the God of Endings is never wasted."

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] described [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]]—the spirit and the speaking draugr. Speaks's brow furrowed. "Her presence may shield him. Love or grief, it holds him back. You should speak with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]. Her wisdom goes deeper than mine. I'll wait at the stables with horses."

At [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ysmirs_Hall\|Ysmir's Hall]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] awaited her. "I saw your shadow at dawn. I knew you would return before nightfall."

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] had no name for the Dragon Priest. "Many names were buried," she said, "so fools would not seek them out." She echoed Speaks's theory: that [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]]'s love and grief shielded him. But the mask's presence troubled her. "It shouldn't be there. Something is wrong."

She summoned [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vidir\|Víðir]], one of Ysmir's sons, to join them. Together, they returned to the stables, where Speaks waited. Then they rode.

---

Back at the lodge, the air grew tense. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] emerged from the misty woods, silent but changed. He approached the well, and spoke: "Faas. Ru. Maar." Fear. Run. Terror. The words split the silence.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] froze. The Thu'um—a shout forged from true fear—gripped them. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] lifted [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] like a child, vanishing into the mist with her.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vidir\|Víðir]] arrived moments later. The shaman knelt by the well, her palm against the earth. "This land is poisoned. This isn't just a curse. It's rot—ancient rot."

She told the others to go. "Follow him to the barrow. Find her. We'll do what we can here."

With [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vidir\|Víðir]] joining them, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] ventured deep into the woods. They found the barrow—an ancient, overgrown mound. Inside, stone steps spiraled down into shadow.

They moved cautiously, clearing rooms and noting remnants of the past: coins, soul gems, weapons—and inscriptions. Runes scratched into the walls, partially translated by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vidir\|Víðir]]:

- _No peace here. Only mask. Only hunger._
    
- _Must make her whole again._
    
- _God still hungers. Find aether-touched._
    

Some notes scrawled in Cyrodilic:

- _He spoke the words again. We shouldn't be here._
    
- _The soul slipped away. I will not let her go._
    
- _Not enough. Not enough. Not enough._
    

Meanwhile, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] drifted in and out of consciousness. She saw strange visions—torches behind her, roots beneath her. A voice whispered: "Do you remember what you asked for?" She ran through endless mirrors. She stood in a ritual chamber, half-alive, half-dead.

When the party reached that chamber, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] appeared—but swiftly melted away into the form of a Dragon Priest. Sarcophagi opened. Draugr poured forth. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] stepped among them.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] planted his shield and held the doorway. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] struck from range. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] was immune to their attacks—each blow useless. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] devised a strategy: hold the others back, then target the priest's body.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vidir\|Víðir]] darted in, axes spinning, and cut through draugr with ease. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], with [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s help, slipped to [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s side. She was alive—barely. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] loosed an arrow, striking a draugr clean in the eye.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] sprinted past [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]], leaping over bones, and shoved the priest's corpse off the ritual table. The Dragon Priest's spirit shrieked. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] fell to his knees.

Then came [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] and Speaks. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] raised her staff. Her voice thundered with ancient power. The Dragon Priest's essence lifted into the air, writhing. One slam of her staff—and it was gone.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] returned. She crossed the room and took [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]]'s hand.

"Krosis," [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] whispered—sorrow—and together they vanished.

"Pain left to rot becomes a wound," said [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]. "A wound that festers."

She knelt by [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]. The ritual had brought her too close to death. "I will take her. She needs guidance."

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] placed [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] in the same sarcophagus. With reverent strength, he sealed it.

---

Back in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], the party followed [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ysmirs_Hall\|Ysmir's Hall]]. She ordered [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vidir\|Víðir]] and his kin to seal the barrow. None were to cross that threshold again.

They had earned her trust. The [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]] welcomed them fully.

After settling [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], they returned to the The [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Sleeping_Stag\|Sleeping Stag]]. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hadvar\|Hadvar]], drunk beyond reason, was barely coherent. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] seized the chance and secured double the promised gold before the man collapsed into his plate.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], youthful mischief gleaming in her eyes, suggested a prank. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eydis\|Eydis]], the innkeeper, joined in without hesitation.

Satisfied, they returned to the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] shared a drink with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] inhaled a full plate of food, ravenous. Then, she and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] returned to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ysmirs_Hall\|Ysmir's Hall]] for rest.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] stayed a little longer, downing Nord spirit with his old friend. Then, finally, he too retired—leaving behind the shadows of the hollow, if only for the night.
