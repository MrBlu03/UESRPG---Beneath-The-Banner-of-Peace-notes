## Chapter 18 — The Three Days of Snowfall

**9th–11th of Sun’s Dusk, 2E 600**

The days that followed the fall of the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]] came soft and white upon Faldarstad. Snow gathered on rooftops, muffled the streets, and softened the noise of daily life until the whole city seemed caught between breaths. Yet within the quiet, the Fangs stirred again, drawn back to their separate rhythms after the storm.

The morning of the **ninth** dawned cold and dry, the sky clear as hammered steel. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] rose first, coaxing warmth into the house with a pot of fish stew. Steam curled through the rafters; outside the windows, the world lay buried in clean snow. When [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] stepped into the square later that morning, she found [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] already awake in his shop, [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Thistle_Branch\|the Thistle Branch]]. She asked him once more of the charm he had pressed into her hand at the feast—the stag-shaped antler sliver recovered from the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]]’s ashes. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] only repeated what he had said before: it was a hunter’s token, no more and no less, a trace of what the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]] had once been before madness hollowed him out. The keeping of it, he said, belonged to her now.

Their talk turned to woad—specifically the old blend known as Fox’s Guile. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] fetched a weathered tome from his shelf, its pages stained blue and brown, and showed her the patterning and purpose: a subtle art, born of misdirection, speechcraft, and the need to pass unseen through uncomfortable company.

On her way back, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] crossed paths with [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], who had been searching for her. The fox remains from the week before were handed over, along with a long-forgotten spellbook dredged from the depths of [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]’s pack—an apology quietly exchanged between them.

Meanwhile, in the market, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] found vegetables for her stews—and found, too, familiar faces from the south. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonya]], the Skald twins of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], stood laughing beneath a stall’s awning. They revealed they had been summoned by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl Gorm]] for the coming execution and spoke readily of their Faldarstad roots. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] asked after local instruments, hoping to carry some Northern sound back to Anequina. The twins suggested the taglharpa and welcomed her to their performances at [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|the Soggy Beaver]].

By midday, the Fangs returned home. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] set to her work immediately, boiling the fox bones according to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Mortgren]]’s grim treatise. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] shared what she had learned of the upcoming execution, prompting speculation about the captured Dunmer smugglers and their ties to the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]. Old threads resurfaced—[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]]’s favor, vanished ledger pages, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]’s vision of the Dunmer in her coma.

Later, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] asked [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] into the cellar. There, behind the closed hatch, she revealed the true reason she sought help with the woad: the marks she wished to hide were not on her face but carved across her back. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] asked gently, then let the matter rest when [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] said she was not yet ready to speak of them. As [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] worked, each noted aloud their unfamiliarity with the other’s kind—fur soft beneath scaled hands, scales rough beneath furred ones—an unguarded moment between two travelers far from home.

When they emerged, a courier knocked. A letter from J’zargo left [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] muttering over riddles and half-truths. Evening settled in with pale light, and the Fangs made for [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|the Soggy Beaver]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] was already inside, losing at cards to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] stopped to speak with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]], asking him to keep watch for strange symbols, suspicious Dunmer, vampiric signs, or sudden cold. The boy agreed eagerly, proud to be entrusted with such a task.

The Twins played Bonden og Kragen that night, their voices sweeping through the rafters like wind off the barrows. When the last notes faded, the Fangs returned home through powdery snow.

---

The **tenth** dawned beneath a steady snowfall. Within the house, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] ate; [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sketched, bone-dust still beneath her nails.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], restless, stepped out early in search of the armorsmith. In the marketplace he noticed a robed Breton woman asking after the Fangs. Slim, sharp-featured, dressed in the blue and gold of the [[Info/Organizations/Other/Mages_Guild\|Mages Guild]]—she cut an out-of-place figure among the furs and iron. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] shadowed her as she questioned [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Runa\|Runa]], then watched as she made her way to the Longhall in growing frustration.

Inside, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl Gorm]] pointed directly to [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] the moment she announced her business. She introduced herself as **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle Benele]]**, antiquarian and Guild scholar of Daedric relics. Whatever she wished to say would not be said in a public hall.

Back at the house, she briefed them: the orb from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] was a dormant Harrowstorm engine core, a remnant of the [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]’s visions of bone and fur were echoes of that old calamity, not a present threat. The artifact had stirred only because of the mass death at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]. It was now sealed in layers of wards and en route to [[Info/Locations/Other Realms/Eyevea\|Eyevea]]. Before leaving, Gabrielle asked for a messenger. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] produced a crust of bread—soon stolen by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron Jangleplume]] himself, rotund and glossy-feathered. Gabrielle recognized the Daedric crow at once and entrusted him with her letter. As he departed, she warned them that crows of [[Info/Lore/Nocturnal\|Nocturnal]] were as curious as they were unpredictable. When she finally left, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] suggested buying more bread, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] eagerly complied.

That afternoon the Fangs visited the armorsmith to line their gear with fur for the journey ahead. From there they returned to the Longhall to interrogate the imprisoned Dunmer. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga]] handed them the ledger—written in fluent Dunmeri. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], raised among the sharp vowels of Tear, translated enough to discern its threads: ordinary trade… and then less ordinary dealings. A Hlaalu merchant network under one **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Garen_Hlaalu\|Garen Hlaalu]]**. A loan from a Kragenmoor bank. A single Telvanni mention. A symbol of the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]].

The prisoners themselves offered more, once [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] applied his particular contrast of comfort and denial. They were Hlaalu merchants—smugglers, but not Serpent agents. A Temple Guard in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Fultheim\|Fultheim]] had paid them to transport the ledger south to [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjallgrad]], where someone bearing an Ouroboros pendant would receive it. The moon sugar was merely a side operation, purchased from [[Info/Locations/Morrowind/Ebonheart\|Ebonheart]] smugglers. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] scolded them for the stupidity of their choices; [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] confirmed that no Khajiiti caravans were involved.

On their way back, the ever-running [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik]] arrived with the College’s report on the burned letter fragments. Before he could sprint off, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] at last succeeded in tripping him—her tail cutting across his path. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik]] blamed a rock and fled, confused.

Night fell quietly upon the snowbound city.

---

The **eleventh** began with the sounding of a horn—low, distant, resonant. Drums followed from the Longhall. Where song had filled the last days, this one began in ritual silence.

The Fangs rose. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]] visited with warm mead, and they drank to their continued survival, to long roads, to never meeting the chopping block themselves.

When the horn sounded thrice, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga]] summoned them.

In the Longhall, preparations were already underway. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] knelt before the fire, spreading a ring of ash. The Skald twins and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Agni\|Agni]] tuned their instruments. The Dunmer waited in chains.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] stepped forward. He was taken aside, stripped of armor, painted in Atmoran runes with stag’s blood, clothed in bear-hide and wolf mantle, crowned with antlers from that morning’s kill. When he returned, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl Gorm]] placed in his hands the executioner’s blade—an old Nord sword, worn smooth by centuries of sentence and ceremony.

Their procession wound through the city: Jarl, Lawspeaker, Skalds, Huscarls, Fangs, and townsfolk. Warriors wore their clan furs; hunters carried charms. Snow crunched beneath hundreds of feet. Ravens circled above. The Skalds played a song of Suurin, the hunger-wolf.

They walked until they reached the field of standing stones—half-sunken in snow, ringed by grey braziers whose smoke drifted in slow coils. There [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] declared the **Wergeld** called.

One by one, the condemned knelt. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] invoked [[Info/Lore/Shor\|Shor]] to weigh their souls, and [[Info/Lore/Kyne\|Kyne]] to bear witness. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]] brought each a cup of mead, a final courtesy before judgment.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]’s cuts were swift—three clean strokes. Yet with each, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]’s anticipation drained into something heavier, quieter. By the fourth, she no longer smiled.

The final prisoner—the one who had spat at [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]—refused the mead and spat again, this time upon the ground before [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] raised his staff. The wind ceased; the forest stilled. No words remained for one who rejected the rites. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] was guided behind him, denying the condemned the dignity of meeting his death face-forward. The cut was slower, harsher; bone resisted; breath caught. This body was carried not to the pyre, but to the forest’s edge, left for beasts and rot.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] traced a spiral rune in bloodied snow. The law was fulfilled.

When the pyres were lit and blue smoke rose above the trees, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] received a carved bone token from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonya]]—proof of his participation in the ancient rites.

A feast awaited them in the Longhall. Stag meat from the morning’s kill filled the boards, surrounded by vegetables and heavy mead. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] played alongside the Skalds. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonya]] collected their names for the Book of Faldarstad. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] lingered only briefly before slipping out into the snowbound quiet, the weight of the day pressing her into solitude. She later curled to rest beside the slow-moving river, its icy surface misting in the evening chill.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], drunk from a contest with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl Gorm]] (lost by only a few mugs), wandered off and collapsed near the tannery. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] returned home; finding [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] missing, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] left a small note at her resting spot inside—an assurance of company had she need of it, marked by a flower she had drawn many times before.

Thus ended the three days of snowfall—quiet, heavy, and full of reckonings—marking the close of the Fangs’ time in Faldarstad, and the slow turning of their road toward whatever waited next.
