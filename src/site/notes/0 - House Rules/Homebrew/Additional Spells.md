## Destruction

### Black Hex

An old curse used by witches around the continent due to its unfortunate yet harmless effect.

| Level  | 3   | 6   |
| ------ | --- | --- |
| Cost   | 8   | 14  |
| Sp Str | 1   | 2   |
Qualities: Direct

Effect:
	The target must make a **Wp** test to resist the effects. If they fail, the target rerolls their next test "**SpStr**" times and uses the worst result.

### Widfire

This is a spell believed to have been created by the Ashlanders of Morrowind.

| Level  | 1   | 2   | 3   | 4    | 5   | 6   | 7    |
| ------ | --- | --- | --- | ---- | --- | --- | ---- |
| Cost   | 6   | 7   | 8   | 9    | 10  | 11  | 12   |
| Sp Str | 1d4 | 1d6 | 1d8 | 1d10 | 2d6 | 2d8 | 2d10 |
Qualities: Upkeep, Range (30m), AoE (2m, flat)

Effect:
	The target location sets on fire, any creature within the affected radius takes "**Sp Str**" damage. This damage is resolved as an **AoE**. The affected area will persist for 3 rounds and will **increase** in size by 1m every round.

### Curse of Notorgo

A spell used by witches to aid in escaping the ignorant.

| Level  | 1   | 2   | 3   | 4   | 5   | 6   | 7   |
| ------ | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cost   | 6   | 9   | 12  | 15  | 18  | 21  | 24  |
| Sp Str | -1  | -2  | -3  | -4  | -5  | -6  | -7  |
Qualities: Direct, Upkeep

Effect:
	Reduces the target's **Speed** & **Initiative Rating** by "**Sp Str**" for 1 minute.

### Healing Light

An upgraded form of healing that is used to aid a group of targets.

| Level  | 1   | 2   | 3   | 4   | 5   | 6   | 7   |
| ------ | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cost   | 8   | 10  | 12  | 14  | 16  | 18  | 20  |
| Sp Str | 2   | 4   | 6   | 8   | 10  | 12  | 14  |
Effect
	All **allies** to the caster within a 3m radius centered on the caster are healed for **"Sp Str" HP.**
