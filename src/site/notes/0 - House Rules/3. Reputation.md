# Reputation

Here you will find general details on your reputation with various factions and peoples.

---

## General

This section is dedicated to factions that aren't bound to single kingdoms or locations, but instead have members all across Tamriel.

---

### Organizations

#### The Dark Brotherhood

> To most, a rumor. To others, a last resort. To walk with the Brotherhood is to move in silence and shadow, where every whisper carries weight. To cross them is to invite death with no warning, and no mercy.

#### The Blackfeather Court

The Crows are never far behind. You are being watched.

---

## The Rift

The people and factions of the Kingdom of the Rift, the southeastern Hold of Skyrim, ruled by Queen Freidr the Patient of Clan Volng.

---

### People

#### City of Riften

The people of Riften recognize your helpful nature. They speak of you as they do of most local mercenaries: someone reliable in times of need, but motivated by coin.

---

### Factions

#### The Court of Clan Volng

Your continued aid to the people of Riften and dedication to investigating the Coiled Serpent and rogue Volkihar vampires have earned you the favor of Queen Freidr and her court. While they see you as mercenaries, they value their allies and reward them generously.

- **Unfettered access** to the entire Hold; you may travel its lands without interruption or delay.
	
- **Call upon the Riften Guard** for assistance when the situation requires it.

---

#### Cult of Ysmir

The Warriors of Ysmir recognize the weight you carry — the same burden they bear with honor. You’ve earned your place among them. In their hall, you’ll find flame against the cold, a cup raised in your name, and comrades who will stand beside you when the wind turns sharp. This is your hearth now, for rest, for strength — and for the forging of greater deeds.

- **Free lodging** is always available in their hall.
    
- **Training in the following skills is discounted by 25%**: _Acrobatics, Athletics, Combat Style, Evade, Lore, and Survival._
    
- **They offer information and work** whenever opportunities arise.
    
- **Members of the cult may assist you** in your endeavors, provided you ask and their help is practical for the task or situation.

---

#### The Companions of Fallowstone Hall

Your deeds during the occupation of Greenwall have earned the respect of the Companions.
