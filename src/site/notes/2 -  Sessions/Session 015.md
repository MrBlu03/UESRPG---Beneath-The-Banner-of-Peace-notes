# Session 015 - Divided Paths and Dark Discoveries


## Summary
[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] returns after his mysterious absence and reveals his encounter with a massive creature in the woods. The party splits into two groups: [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] pursue the [[Info/Quests/White_Stag_Hunt\|White Stag Hunt]] with local hunters, leading them into a [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Cursed_Grove\|cursed grove]] where they discover the wounded stag and face a territorial supernatural creature. Meanwhile, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] fulfill [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s favor by intercepting a [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Dunmer_Merchants\|Dunmer]] caravan involved in [[Info/Quests/Coiled_Serpent_Conspiracy\|Coiled Serpent]] smuggling operations. The session reveals deeper connections to the conspiracy threatening the region.

## Detailed Recap

### Day 1  Morning Confrontations (4th of Sun's Dusk)

#### Durov's Return
[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] appeared later that afternoon after being absent the previous day. When [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] questioned his whereabouts, her tone somewhere between command and concern, he attempted to avoid the conversation but eventually revealed:
- Had encountered a massive creature in the woods, the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Skinstealer]]
- Attempted to draw the creature but produced a very poor sketch, only managing to copy a rune carved into the bark of a pine
- Learned about a [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Dunmer_Merchants\|Dunmer]] merchant caravan passing through the area
- Found a rune near the creature's location (later identified by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]] as Nordic but unreadable)
- Was drugged with Nirnroot by a Colovian named [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gaius\|Gaius]], who then vanished
- Acquired a pendant and a Legion tag marked with the number zero from the encounter

#### Personal Conflicts
[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] pointed out that [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] had left her emotional letter in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Nilans_House\|Nilan's]] basement. This led to a confrontation where:
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] warned that [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s guilt and regret could cloud the judgment of even the finest archer
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], her voice trembling though firm, replied that her skill and her pain were not [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s to measure
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] left with a swirl of her cloak, walking off for several hours to be alone and breathe without the weight of eyes upon her

### Day 2  - The Warmest Morning (5th of Sun's Dusk)

The day began foggy but with the warmest temperature in days (-12°C). [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] woke first to silence and studied a new passage in *On the Custody of the Dead*. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] tended to the hearth, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] broke his fast on salted rations, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] tended to the old horse, [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Mossback]]. 
When [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] stepped outside to stretch his legs, he discovered an arrow quivering in the doorframe with an attached note.

#### Stryder's Summons
The note from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]] called in the favor [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] owed him:
- Requested "muscle" and someone who could fight up close
- Meeting location: abandoned guild house on NE side of town
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] showed the letter to [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and they prepared to leave

#### Party Split
The group divided into two teams for concurrent missions:
- **Team 1**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] prepared for the [[Info/Quests/White_Stag_Hunt\|White Stag Hunt]]
- **Team 2**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] went to fulfill [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s favor

## The White Stag Hunt

### The Hunting Party
[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] met with the local hunters:
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]**: Older experienced woman, knowledgeable about local lore
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]]**: Young impulsive man, eager hunter
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Brovin\|Brovin]]**: Hunting dog

### Investigation of the Rune
[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] asked [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]] about the rune [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] had found. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]] identified it as Nordic in origin but wrong and distorted, as if the words resisted meaning. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] asked about the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Skinstealer]], and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]] mentioned rumors of flayed hides with surgical precision and a creature mocking [[Info/Lore/Kyne\|Kyne]]'s hunts.

### Tracking the White Stag
The hunting party headed north. The woods began bright, but darkened into amber and smoke the deeper they went. They found:
- Stag tracks that were highly erratic, as if running from something
- Thin streaks of blood frozen on stone surfaces
- Antler scrapes on distant trees marked by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]

### Journey into the Cursed Grove
After an hour of tracking eastward, the environment changed dramatically:
- Trees grew closer together, ground dipped down
- Air became heavy, silence grew heavy
- Totems carved into tree trunks, some with holes, appearing human but "not quite"—faces eyeless, mouths in silent screams
- Cut marks from claws visible on the totems, some painted in blood or hung with hide
- Sweet rot smell permeated the area
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Brovin\|Brovin]] the dog stopped cold and refused to continue

#### The Pursuit Crisis
Despite [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]'s warning that "this forest isn't [[Info/Lore/Kyne\|Kyne]]'s forest" and her breath shaking, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]] pressed on obsessively, calling the others cowards:
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] threw a snowball to get his attention
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] attempted to use a paralysis dart but missed
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] tried to physically trip [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]] to stop him
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s second paralysis dart was dodged

### Discovery of the White Stag
In a hollow clearing, they found the white stag, not as a proud prize but a dying creature:
- Lying on its side, breathing hollow and rasping
- Five perfect, deep slashes across its side
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] spotted patterns in the bloodstains around the stag—deliberate, cruel spirals
- The stag turned its head toward [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], sighed deeply, and died

### Encounter with the Forest Creature
After the stag's death:
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]] stood defeated and fell to his knees in total silence
- A weird howl echoed around them in the distance—low, drawn, neither wolf nor man
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]] raised her bow, hands trembling, as another howl answered closer
- The entire party fled back toward the city as the trees seemed to lean inward
- They were chased by an unseen creature, its howls following like laughter from the dark
- The creature slowly faded into the distance as they reached the frozen road to [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]] remained pale, shaking, and terrified, unable to speak, while [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] drew him close in rare compassion

### Resolution
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]] spat into the snow, declaring this was no beast of [[Info/Lore/Kyne\|Kyne]] and wore her blessings as a mask
- She promised to gather people to hunt the monster in the woods—the tolling of the Hunter's Bell at dawn would mark their stand
- The pair headed to [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]] tavern for information and food, their resolve firm

## Stryder's Favor

### The Meeting Location
[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] arrived at the [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Abandoned_Guild_House\|Abandoned Guild House]] (former tannery):
- Building had a collapsed roof due to snow
- Mostly empty structure
- Narrow staircase led to the basement

### The Basement Meeting
The basement was damp with shale rock construction and smelled horrible. At the far end:
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]]** stood over a table with a map of the Rift
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hooded_Figure\|Hooded Figure]]**: Masked individual present during negotiations

#### The Assignment
[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]] presented a silver coin with a triple ouroboros (dragon devouring eagle devouring lion devouring dragon) and explained:
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Dunmer_Merchants\|Dunmer]] traders flying [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Hlaalu\|Hlaalu]] colors were coming through, cutting off road guards
- Their task: intercept under pretense of royal inspection and uncover their cargo
- Intelligence: 6 guards, lightly armed, one Nord driver, rest Dunmer
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s men would provide backup from the trees
- After completion, they would meet back at the same location

### The Caravan Interception
South of the city along the river, they found:
- Well-maintained caravan with two wagons
- 6 Dunmer guards (3 on each side) plus 1 Nord driver
- 2 guards dressed in darker red, appearing nervous

#### The Inspection
[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] claimed authority to check for illegal goods in a voice of command, invoking the full name and titles of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr the Patient]] (Jarl of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], Matriarch of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Volng\|Clan Volng]], Shield of the Eastmarch Border, Bear-Mother of the Rift, Keeper of the Watchfires, and Blood-Daughter of Ysgramor):

**First Wagon**: Cloth, mead, fish - nothing suspicious
**Second Wagon**: Spices, herbs - appeared legitimate on surface

#### Interrogation and Discovery
[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] separated one nervous guard for questioning:
- The guard initially claimed all goods were legal with proper paperwork, but his lies came quick and sloppy
- Documents were in Dunmeri but clearly forged with missing seals; [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] mockingly asked, "Do they not have wax where you're from?"
- Under pressure, the guard revealed moonsugar hidden under floorboards in sealed waxed leather
- Promised nothing illegal in the first cart

#### Combat and Evidence Planting
- The interrogated guard shouted something in Dunmeri, alerting his [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|companions]]
- One guard lunged with a sword
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] already had him in a headlock, demanding answers, but when the others stared and one coldly said "Do it", she snapped the man's neck
- The cooperative guard produced illegal goods and a ledger
- Ledger revealed multiple shipments, with entries in Hlaalu script stating "payment, serpent mark confirmed"
- [[Info/Quests/Coiled_Serpent_Conspiracy\|Coiled Serpent]] emblem appeared on the last page
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] planted additional evidence (a vial of moon sugar) beneath the boards of the first wagon to justify executing all merchants

### Return and Sentencing
At the city gates:
- They informed guards and summoned [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga Cairn-Shield]] the huscarl
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga]] did not mourn for Dunmer smugglers, and offered execution options: [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] could execute them, or they could fight to the death among themselves with the survivor facing [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]. The Jarl would confirm executions in due time.
- Prisoners were held in dungeons until a decision was made

### Final Meeting with Stryder
Returning with both carts and goods:
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]]** and **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hooded_Figure\|Hooded Figure]]** were present
- When [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] demanded a name, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hooded_Figure\|Hooded Figure]] told them he was "no one of consequence"
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] stepped forward blocking the stairs; [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] told [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] to stand down. The [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hooded_Figure\|Hooded Figure]] chuckled, mocking, and said, "Good girl," making [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] freeze. (Voice similar to figure from her dreams)
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga]] had an understanding about goods with certain marks ([[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Hlaalu\|House Hlaalu]] and ouroboros symbols)
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hooded_Figure\|Hooded Figure]] claimed connections in high courts

## Evening Reunion at The Soggy Beaver

Both teams met at [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]] tavern to compare notes and share their discoveries under a dim lamp. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] vented frustration over [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]]'s recklessness. The bartender, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]], mentioned meeting [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] a couple days ago when he was "looking for a guy." Before the night ended, they convinced [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]] to spread word through town that hunters and warriors alike were needed at dawn to face the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Skinstealer]] in its lair.
