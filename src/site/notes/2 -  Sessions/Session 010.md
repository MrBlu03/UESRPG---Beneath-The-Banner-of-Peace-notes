# Session 010 - 2025-08-02
**8th of Frost Fall, 2E 600**

## Summary

The party awakens on the morning of the 8th of Frost Fall at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall of Ysmir]]. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] tends to [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], who has been unconscious for three days following the events at the haunted lodge. As the party recovers from their ordeal, they decide to take a camping trip to decompress, recruiting several [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] locals including [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]], and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]].

The group travels north to a hunting clearing, where they spend time fishing, hunting, and sharing stories around the campfire. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] processes her trauma through art, drawing scenes from her visions, while [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] experiences significant dreams about his forgotten past. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] reveals her hunting skills and shares knowledge about interesting inn visitors from years past, including someone who spoke of dragons returning.

The trip proves therapeutic for the group, allowing them to bond with local NPCs and gain valuable provisions through successful hunting. They return to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] with renewed spirits and several new leads to investigate.

## Detailed Recap

### Morning at the Hall of Ysmir

The party awakens at the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]] hall. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] tends to [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], who lies unconscious on a fur cot, still recovering from her three-day slumber following the lodge incident.

- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] wakes first, grabbing the largest piece of hog meat and mead he can find, sitting in a corner to eat
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] wakes up and waves at [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], who waves back. She checks that the dragon priest mask is still under her bed, wraps it in a blanket, and grabs fish for breakfast
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] wakes and grabs an apple before joining the others for food
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] finally awakens, immediately asking for her gear and picking up her book first. She asks [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] where she is and reveals the last person she spoke to in her dreams was the "[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|Crow Father]]"

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] presents the wrapped dragon priest mask to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] for examination. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] declares it "just a trophy now" since its master is gone and its magic has faded, encouraging [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] to keep it.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] explains the runes painted on [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] 's face during her recovery, calling them "Woad" - an ancient form of magic. When [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] asks if they can cover markings, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] explains they're primarily for facial use.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] informs [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] that [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] are dead and the land is returning to normal. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] simply asks if they got paid. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] throws her share of gold, but she misses it while reequipping her gear.

### Planning the Lake Trip

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] proposes a lake trip to decompress. Though skeptical, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] agrees. They decide to recruit [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] first since she's closest.

As they leave, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] makes cryptic comments about "feathers."

**Recruiting [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]]:**

En route to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]]'s shop, they spot [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik the Quick]] sprinting across town. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] attempts to trip him for amusement but fails spectacularly, inspiring a new personal quest.

At [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] 's shop, they meet her small husky companion, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/John\|John]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] proposes the camping trip for alchemy component gathering, hunting, fishing, and relaxation. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] accepts enthusiastically and is happy to come along with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/John\|John]].

At the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonya]] prepare their instruments (having moved from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Sleeping_Stag\|The Sleeping Stag]]). [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] invites [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] camping, who accepts and asks [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] to join them. They arrange to meet at the city gate.

### Journey to the Clearing

The group meets at the gate near the docks. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] arrive with camping gear, and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] suggests a clearing a few hours north where they often hunt stag.

**Stop at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]]:**

They travel past [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]], stopping to collect the 50 gold per person still owed by the alderman. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] attempts to claim 60 per person, but [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Alderman Torgir]] correctly remembers the actual amount and pays 50 gold each.

During the walk, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] learns that John is a stray [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] adopted three years ago.

### Camp Activities - Day One

**Setting Up Camp:**

After hours of forest travel, they reach the clearing and establish camp.

**Fishing and Hunting:**

- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] fish on the lake (though they catch no fish), while [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] catches salmon with her hands
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] attempt trap hunting (unsuccessful) then track a stag
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] demonstrates her hunting skills learned from adventurers years ago
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] successfully brings down the stag after multiple shots, claiming antlers worth ~100 gold
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] prepares stew while [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sketches the scenery and journals her visions

![drawing made during session 10.png](/img/user/Z%20-%20other%20bullshit/Z%20-%20Image%20Assets/drawing%20made%20during%20session%2010.png)

**Evening Conversations:**

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] discusses her spriggan friend and asks [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] about her tribal connections. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] shares her background: raised by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] alone after her mother died when a wall collapsed on the building they were sheltering in during a war. Both [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] escaped, but her mother didn't survive. She reveals keeping secret records of interesting inn visitors, including a man who spoke of dragons returning ~14 years ago - specifically noting a "schizo guy talking about dragons returning." [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] confirms dragons have returned and killed her father.

### Durov's Dream

During the night, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] experiences a vivid dream:
- A muddy battlefield with [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|companions]] in matching armor bearing the same insignia as his own
- A fellow soldier falling with a spear wound to the chest
- A distant large, snowy castle looming in the background, bearing banners with the same insignia

### Second Day Activities

**Morning Routine:**

A foggy, chill morning sees [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] wake first to restart the fire, followed by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] checking the meat, then [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]], and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] emerges from the lake bottom where she slept, startling [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] who reaches for his sword. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] wakes last due to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]]'s terrified screaming.

**Hunting and Encounters:**

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] check traps, finding a caught rabbit. A mysterious Khajiit named [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Maiq_the_Liar\|M'aiq]] appears suddenly at their camp, rambling about not liking taxes and claiming he's tired, then departs as mysteriously as he arrived without elaborating further.

The hunting party expands to include [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] for a bear hunt, successfully bringing down the animal with help from [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] to drag it back.

**Art and Reflection:**

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sketches [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sir_Gareth\|Gareth]] as she saw him in her visions. That evening, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] observes her drawing, prompting [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] to open up about seeing [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sir_Gareth\|Gareth]] in her coma, their relationship, and having to kill him.

![Gareth drawing based on coma visions.png](/img/user/Z%20-%20other%20bullshit/Z%20-%20Image%20Assets/Gareth%20drawing%20based%20on%20coma%20visions.png)

The hunting expedition yields 11 rations per party member.

### Return Journey

The next morning, everyone packs up. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] conduct a final sweep, making sure they didn't leave anything behind that could be bad for the wildlife. They return to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] via [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]], dropping off [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]] first, then [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] (with farewells to John), finally reaching the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall of Ysmir]] by afternoon.
