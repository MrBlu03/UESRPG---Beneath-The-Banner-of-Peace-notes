# Session 23a - 2026-03-14

## Summary

On a bitterly cold morning (-18 degrees), [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] spends time studying ancient texts at the stables before taking up a bounty to hunt a "witch" terrorizing hunters west of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. She tracks the creature to its lair, discovers it's a hagravenn-like being (a monstrous hybrid of hag and bird), engages in brief combat, and ultimately negotiates a truce. The [[Info/Quests/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|witch]] agrees to cease attacks on hunters in exchange for monthly offerings of spell components (hearts, eyeballs, and gems), potentially establishing a lasting peace.

---

## Detailed Recap

### Morning Studies

The session begins with [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] awakening to a frigid morning (-18°C) in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. She spends time studying "On the Old Holds," an ancient text (over 600 years old) detailing the history and landmarks of eastern Skyrim. While reading at the stables next to her horse, she overhears stable boys and locals discussing growing tensions with the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild_of_the_Silverroad]] and notices suspicious hooded figures passing by.

---

### Taking the Bounty

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] checks the bounty board and finds a posting about a "[[Info/Quests/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|witch of the woods]]" that pays well and involves magic—two things that interest her greatly. She visits the tavern and speaks with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorn_Hunter\|Bjorn]], a hunter with a distinctive "Green Arrow" style goatee and mustache. He explains that while hunting west of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], his horse was attacked by fire magic, and the creature has killed several people in recent days. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorn_Hunter\|Bjorn]] provides a rough map showing the creature's territorial range and the location of their [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Abandoned_Hunter_Camp\|abandoned camp]].

---

### Investigation

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] rides west for approximately two hours to investigate. She first finds [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorn_Hunter\|Bjorn]]'s dead horse, which has severe claw marks across its body and a distinct scorch/burn mark near the neck. Using her magical senses, she attempts to detect necromantic energy but experiences a disturbing backlash—hearing voices echoing through her mind (successful Willpower test prevents worse effects). 

At the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Abandoned_Hunter_Camp\|abandoned hunter camp]], she finds an iron short sword, a bow, dried provisions, and rope. She tracks humanoid footprints northeast to a small [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Witchs_Clearing\|clearing]].

---

### The Encounter

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] spots the "[[Info/Quests/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|witch]]"—revealed to be a monstrous creature resembling a hagravenn: a corrupted being somewhere between an old hag and a bird, covered in raven feathers, with sharp claws, a gigantic nose, and a hunched, grotesque appearance. The creature immediately attacks with frost bolts.

During combat:
- The [[Info/Quests/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|witch]] lands two frost bolts (6 and 4 damage) before [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] can act
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] breaks line of sight using trees and calls for a parlay
- The [[Info/Quests/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|witch]] ceases hostilities to hear [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s proposal

---

### Negotiation

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] proposes a truce: if the [[Info/Quests/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|witch]] stops attacking people, the hunters will leave her territory alone and provide monthly offerings of spell components. The [[Info/Quests/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|witch]] demands specific items—fresh human hearts, eyeballs, and gems—for her magical practices. After tense negotiations, the [[Info/Quests/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|witch]] tentatively agrees to the arrangement, stating "Maybe" if the humans uphold their end. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] emphasizes that this arrangement protects the [[Info/Quests/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|witch]] from eventual mob retaliation while allowing her to focus on her magical studies in peace.

## NPCs Met

- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorn_Hunter\|Bjorn]]:** A hunter who posted the bounty. He wears studded leather and fur, sports a distinctive goatee/mustache combo, and lost his horse to the [[Info/Quests/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|witch]]'s attack. He provides intel and a map.
- **[[Info/Quests/The_Witch_of_the_Woods\|The Witch of the Woods]]:** A hagravenn-like creature—a monstrous fusion of hag and bird with raven feathers, sharp claws, and powerful frost magic. Territorial but willing to negotiate for resources. Currently residing in a [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Witchs_Clearing\|clearing]] west of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
