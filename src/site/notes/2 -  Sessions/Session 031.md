## Detailed Recap

### Part 1 — The Balcony Aftermath & Infiltration

The session opened immediately following the magical ward explosion outside the **Alton Mesa Cult Facility**. On the 4-metre-high walkway, the warded Dwemer doors remained intact, while the **unnamed priest of Stendarr** lay comatose on the snow below after suffering catastrophic magical backlash.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] stared up at [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] from below with burning fury, his intimidating glare leaving her visibly shaken and panicked. Distraught over getting an innocent man crippled, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] clutched her head in distress.

The **Vestige** emerged silently from the shadows on the walkway. Without acknowledging [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], he produced a **tincture brewed from hagraven feathers and claws** and poured it over the *secondary* door leading into the facility's ventilation system, dissolving the purple ward completely. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] leaped down to [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], confirmed the priest was alive, and instructed [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] to maintain his guard disguise while leaving **red cloth markers** inside to guide him.

When 4 to 5 cult guards emerged from the main gates to investigate the blast, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] misdirected them into the surrounding wilderness, allowing [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] to slip into the ventilation network unnoticed.

---

### Part 2 — Navigating the Dwemer Vents

Inside the facility, the infiltration team crawled and navigated through the expansive **Dwemer ventilation shafts**. The ducts were tall enough to crouch-walk comfortably and dimly lit by ancient brass mechanical fixtures.

Along the route:
- High-pressure steam pipes lined the shafts; a ruptured valve vented scalding steam onto [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s arm, dealing **1 point of damage**.
- The party observed circular, iris-toothed wall chutes built for deploying automated Dwemer defenses.
- Through floor grates, the team tracked guard movements, noting around 16 guards stationed in this wing of the complex.
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] placed strips of red cloth near floor grates to signal the safe route to [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] as he advanced through the lower maintenance corridors.

---

### Part 3 — The Ritual Chamber & The Sacrificial Captives

The vents led to an elevated viewpoint overlooking the **Central Ritual Chamber** — a large, pillared Dwemer hall lined with copper piping and drainage grates converging around a glowing sacrificial circle.

Tied up and unconscious on the chamber floor were several missing citizens from Kilgard, slated to be sacrificed to power the **[[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]]'s** ritual sustaining the **37,000 counterfeit Septims** flooding the economy. Among the captives, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] recognized the eccentric clothes merchant **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jean-Pierre_LaRouche\|Jean-Pierre LaRouche]]** (Marquis of the Sapphire Coast, Baron of the Nine Looms).

Presiding over the altar and flanked by two armored guards was the ritual commander: a towering **Xivilai** with silvery-blue skin and horns. 

Warning the party of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|the Xivilai]]'s immense physical power, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] slipped away into the shadows to eliminate adjacent guards and prevent reinforcements from flanking the room.

---

### Part 4 — Durov's Drinking Contest Gambit

To buy time for [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] to reach ambush positions above the guards, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] walked into the ritual chamber**, posing as an oblivious sentry searching for a drink.

He challenged [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|the Xivilai]] to a **drinking contest** with half-litre flagons, establishing a rule where each flagon had to be slammed, drained, and flipped upside down before pouring the next. 

During the contest, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|the Xivilai]] noticed the crest on [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s shield and remarked that it seemed familiar. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] smoothly brushed off the inquiry as a simple family crest. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] matched the Daedra drink for drink, keeping the creature's full attention focused on the table while [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] crawled into position directly above the two guards.

---

### Part 5 — The Ambush & Guard Executions

With [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|the Xivilai]] distracted, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] initiated a surprise ambush:

1. **Paralysis Bombs:** [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] dropped paralysis bombs through the overhead vents onto the two flanking cult guards, instantly paralyzing them.
2. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s Strike:** [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] dropped from the vent, dashed 9 metres across the floor, and cleanly decapitated the first paralyzed guard in a swift drive-by sword attack.
3. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s Strike:** [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] stepped forward and executed the second paralyzed guard on the ground.

With the lackeys dead before they could act, the fight immediately shifted to the enraged Xivilai.

---

### Part 6 — Boss Battle: Slaying the Xivilai

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|The Xivilai]] drew a massive **Orcish Greataxe** and initiated combat.

#### Battle Climax:
- After surviving the *Brutal Cleave* with only 1 HP remaining, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] drank an **Electro-Healing potion** and launched two consecutive overcharged **Sunbeam** spells, searing the Daedra with radiant fire.
- When [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|the Xivilai]] dropped his greataxe during the melee, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] used a sprint action to dash 18 metres across the chamber, snatching the weapon to prevent the boss from reclaiming it.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] engaged the disarmed Daedra in close quarters, tackling the creature to the stone floor and locking him in the **Restrained** condition.
- From the overhead vent, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] lined up a **Silver Arrow** and fired, scoring a critical hit straight through the side of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|the Xivilai]]'s neck.
- As the Daedra began choking on his own blood, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] shoved his armored fingers into the neck wound and ripped [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|the Xivilai]]'s head and upper spine completely free from the body.

---

### Part 7 — Rescuing the Captives & The Facility Aftermath

With [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Xivilai\|the Xivilai]] dead, the party tended to the unconscious prisoners tied up around the sacrificial circle:
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] used smelling salts from her medkit to revive the noble merchant **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jean-Pierre_LaRouche\|Jean-Pierre LaRouche]]**.
- Upon waking, a tearful and disoriented LaRouche began pompously reciting his full titles before being gently calmed by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]].
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] handed LaRouche a map marked with a route back to Kilgard, advising him to exercise caution given the civil unrest and protests currently rocking the city.
- The party untied the remaining captives, ensuring they were stabilized and able to escape the complex.

Moving to check the adjoining wing:
- Opening the heavy doors, the party found the room drenched in blood and littered with the bodies of nearly a dozen guards and cultists.
- The **Vestige** was leaning against a Dwemer pillar, bloodied and exhausted from wiping out the garrison single-handedly.
- The party secured their spoils, including the **Xivilai's Head** (kept by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] as a trophy), the **Orcish Greataxe**, and **2 full sets of steel armor, longswords, and a shield** from the guards.

---

### Part 8 — The Comatose Priest & The Letter of Guilt

The party gathered the comatose **Priest of Stendarr** and rode back through the night to the **Vestige's Dwemer ruin hideout**.

At the ruin:
- The horses (**[[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Mossback]], Rhymefang, and [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]]**) were stabled, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] fed [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Mossback]] her last red apple.
- The priest was laid on an examination table. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] cast detection magic on his aura, observing his unresponsive, vacant state.
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] diagnosed the injury: the ward's backlash had locked the priest in a **vegetative mental prison**, wandering the halls of an empty, inaccessible mental manor surrounded by an endless black void.
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] summoned a **messenger crow** and dispatched a written request to bring experienced healer mages to the ruin.

#### Periwyn's Letter:
Racked with guilt for dragging an unprepared priest into mortal danger, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] wrote an extensive letter of apology detailing the events, explaining that healer mages were on their way, and taking full responsibility for his plight. She signed and stamped the letter with both her **family seal** and the **[[Info/Organizations/Religious/Knights_of_Arkay\|Knights of the Circle]] emblem**, tucking the parchment securely inside the priest's robes so he would find it upon waking.

---

### Part 9 — The Weight of the Circle

Witnessing [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] slip the letter into the priest's clothes, the **Vestige delivered a severe reprimand**:
- He reminded [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] that even far away in Skyrim, the emblem of the **[[Info/Organizations/Religious/Knights_of_Arkay\|Knights of the Circle]]** carries immense reverence, weight, and history.
- He emphasized that regardless of her personal feelings toward her heritage, she still bears the mark and must take responsibility for what it represents rather than answering it with reckless decisions.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] accepted the criticism in silence, acknowledging that her own internal guilt far exceeded any external scolding.

Before resting, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] placed a reassuring hand on the priest's shoulder, stating quietly that the nightmare would be over soon. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sat together discussing how to properly evaluate [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|companions]] in the future and make amends once the priest recovers. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] noted that he was glad [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] didn't attempt to resolve an innocent man's near-fatal trauma with mere gold.

The session concluded with the party resting safely in the ruin.
