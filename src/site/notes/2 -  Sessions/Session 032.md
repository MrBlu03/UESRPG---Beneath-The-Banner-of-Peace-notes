# Session 32 - 2026-09-05

## Summary
The party recuperates at the Oathbreaker King's tomb following the raid on the Alton Mesa cult redoubt. Durov and Onasha salvage Dwemer oil, scrap metal, and supplies from the complex while melting down the counterfeit Septim presses. Periwyn, wracked with guilt over the unconscious cleric, attempts a forbidden communion rite on the slain Xivilai—dragging her across the threshold into Daedric realms where she encounters Clavicus Vile, Meridia, and Malacath. She refuses the bargain and her mother's sword is shattered by divine wrath. The group learns only one master smith in the north can reforge it, but the winter snows have closed the passes for four months.

## Detailed Recap
- **Morning at the Tomb:** Periwyn sleeps fitfully on Ashen's back, torished by guilt over the cleric's coma. Durov confronts her coldly—"I hope this is a one-time thing"—before resuming his silent vigil at the cleric's bedside. Onasha urges the group to act before the unguarded cult redoubt is looted by brigands.
- **Salvaging the Mesa:** Durov, Onasha, and Periwyn return to the Dwemer complex. Durov destroys the counterfeit coin presses by dumping scrap and coins into the magma crucible. Onasha harvests 7 vials of Dwemer oil from ruined spider constructs and strips 13 pieces of metal scrap, cogs, and gyros. Durov claims an orichalcum greataxe from a fallen cult champion.
- **Durov Unmasked:** While working the smelter, Durov removes his helmet for the first time—revealing a young, broad-jawed man with no gray at his temples, a stark contrast to the battered veteran his armor projects. Onasha notices.
- **Periwyn's Communion Rite:** Alone in the ritual hall, Periwyn uses Morvayn's grimoire to perform the communion of the dead on the Xivilai's corpse. The current drags her into a void of six freestanding doors—realms of Daedric Princes. She enters Clavicus Vile's Fields of Regret (a meadow where the cleric stands with eyes of charcoal and ash), Meridia's Colored Rooms (where she fills a flask with glowing crimson magicka), and Malacath's Ashpit. In the Ashpit, she battles three vampires, then encounters Malacath himself, who offers his power to exterminate all vampires in exchange for her fealty. She refuses; he shatters her mother's sword with his bare hand.
- **The Broken Sword:** Periwyn's blade—her mother's only legacy—is reduced to four jagged shards pulsing with malevolent orange ember-light. The Vestige confirms no ordinary smith can mend it; only a master artisan north of Windhelm who understands soul-binding and ancient reagents might succeed. The Pale passes are snowed shut until Rain's Hand.
- **Kjalgrad Streets:** Durov walks the city, noting the garrison fracturing after Steward Caro's disappearance. He reads in a bookstall and then works the floor at Frost Gate Hall for two hours, earning tips and a free meal from Orgnar.
- **Night Conversation:** Onasha sits with Periwyn in the stables. Periwyn reveals the full story of the vision and her refusal of Malacath's bargain. She confesses her deepest fear: that her violent past drives away those who get close, citing an alchemist companion who abandoned her years ago. Onasha reassures her—"I am not afraid of your demons, Periwyn. Because I carry an army of my own." The bond between them deepens significantly. Durov returns and commits to marching for Windhelm once the snow thaws.

## Items Acquired
- **Dwemer Oil (7 vials):** Viscous amber oil harvested from ruined spider constructs. Potentially useful for brewing explosives capable of breaching iron gates.
- **Dwemer Metal Scrap:** 13 pieces of bronze cogs, gyros, and plating stripped from construct chassis.
- **Orichalcum Greataxe:** Broad-bladed, green-tinted weapon taken from a fallen cult champion. Durov strapped it across his back.
- **Wool Blankets & Dried Rations:** Recovered from the cult barracks stores.
- **Flask of Meridia's Colored Rooms Liquid:** Glowing crimson fluid thrumming with raw magicka, scooped from a pool in Meridia's realm during Periwyn's vision. Unknown properties.
- **Broken Sword Shards:** Four jagged pieces of Periwyn's mother's longsword, pulsing with Daedric corruption. Cannot be reforged by mortal smithcraft.

## Quests
### New
- **Reforge Periwyn's Mother's Sword:** Seek the master smith in the crags north of Windhelm. The artisan who crafted the Vestige's weapons and cuirass is the only one who might mend the Daedric-corrupted steel. Blocked until Rain's Hand (4 months) when the Pale passes thaw.
### In Progress
- **Cleric Recovery:** The Breton cleric remains unconscious after the Daedric ward's backlash. The Vestige has sent word to the mages; their arrival is pending. Periwyn's failed attempt to reach his mind through the communion rite has yielded no progress.
- **Destroy Cult Infrastructure:** Counterfeit presses destroyed and coins melted down. The Dwemer complex is dismantled, but the gear and oil still need to be put to use.
### Completed
- **Destroy Counterfeit Septim Presses:** Coins melted in the magma crucible. Machinery dismantled. Presses and coin stock destroyed to prevent economic poisoning of Skyrim's markets.

## Player Notes
- **Durov:** Revealed to be surprisingly young beneath his plate armor—broad jaw, dark stubble, no gray at the temples. His rugged veteran persona is entirely the product of the armor and bearing. He is protective in a stern, judgmental way and maintains a silent vigil over the cleric.
- **Periwyn:** Deeply shaken by the encounter with Malacath and the loss of her mother's sword. Her guilt over the cleric's condition is intense and personal. Her backstory is further revealed: she had a close companion—an alchemist—who abandoned her after witnessing the depth of her violent obsession with vampires.
- **Onasha:** Her relationship with Periwyn deepens significantly this session. She shares the weight of their similar traumatic pasts and offers genuine emotional support without pity. She is resourceful and practical, focusing on what can be done rather than what can't.

## Next Steps

- March for Windhelm once the Pale passes clear in Rain's Hand to find the master smith.
- Continue monitoring the cleric's condition and await the mages' arrival.
- Deploy the Dwemer oil for explosives or other tactical purposes as needed.
