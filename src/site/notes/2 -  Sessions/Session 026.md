# Session 026

## Summary

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] is gone.** Well past midday, a message arrived for her — brought by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jzargo\|J'zargo]], a Khajiit waiting beyond the ruin with prepared horses. It was a fresh and time-sensitive lead on her missing sister. She explained the situation to the party. Before departing, she returned all guild ledgers and investigation notes to the others. She offered the black feather token back to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]], but he told her to keep it. She rode east with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jzargo\|J'zargo]].

The party is now three: **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**, and **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]'s scouting report was grim but actionable: the old Nordic ruin north of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] (up by the edge of the mesa) is the main facility, sporadically guarded — that's manageable. The problem is the **secondary entrances**, which the cult has Warded shut with powerful glyphs that he cannot dispel alone. To break them, the party needs either a capable conjuration/alteration mage or a special alchemical **varnish** made from a **pearl** and a **head-raven feather**. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] offered to retrieve the feather himself from the head-raven [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] previously dealt with. The pearl could be bought from the alchemist in town.

The session then split across two fronts:

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** returned to [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] on foot ([[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] had technically already gone ahead, via the scenic route). They visited the **Grand Library** to find an alteration mage as a backup plan, and met **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Father_Aldric\|Father Aldric]]** — a Breton priest of Stendarr, restoration specialist, alteration dabbler, and enthusiastic drinker — who agreed to help. They then went to **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Granny_Mummu\|Granny Mummu]]'s alchemist shop**, bought the pearl, and had an extended visit with the elderly woman (flowers placed in the vase again). Finally, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] led [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] through the sewers to the **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|Lower Lodge]]**, where contact **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tarja\|Tarja]]** briefed them on the protest operation: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tarja\|Tarja]]'s network had organised a market strike to pull guards away from the warehouses, and needed the party to deliver encrypted letters to embedded contacts and set traps inside both warehouses using oil jars, ropes, and kindling. At the first warehouse, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] distracted the guard while [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] struck him from behind with her warhammer — accidentally killing him instantly. They planted the trap, then moved to the second warehouse where [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] burned it to the ground by slinging kindling into the crates. Both warehouses went up in flames.

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** exited the barrow, turned right, and then navigated approximately 90 minutes of snowbound mountain forest through a series of navigate and observe tests that should not have worked, and absolutely did. He arrived not at the south gate (which he left from), but at the west gate — roughly half an hour off course — where he immediately spotted his horse, gave it a pat, and headed to the Silver Road Guildhouse. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]] greeted him briefly, confirmed the guard [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain Irlav]] had been looking for him, and left [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] to the refreshments. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] grabbed hummingbird mead, brought mugs to the two door guards (one accepted reluctantly, one let it spill), and waited. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] found him at roughly midday, pulled him aside into the alleys, and briefed him: the missing persons situation is far wider than expected, the lead is personal (for a family friend), and it's not officially Guild business. They attended the protest together, where [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] took statements from **Hana** (elderly merchant, tariff complaints), **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Yelena\|Yelena]]** (tanner, strange gold/docked pay), and **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lady_Aine\|Lady Aine]]** (the wife of **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jean-Pierre_LaRouche\|Jean-Pierre LaRouche]]** — a silk merchant missing since the Jarl's banquet). As they were heading to meet [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]]'s shady informants near the old Black-Foot Guild house, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] spotted two massive plumes of smoke rising from the warehouse quarter.

Session ends there.

## Detailed Recap

### Morning — The Barrow of the Oath Breaker King

The party woke in the barrow. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] had, as expected, fallen asleep face-down in her necromancy book. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] ate a ration for what she noted was probably the first time. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] called them into the main chamber.

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] — absent**: Before the debrief began, the GM flagged that during the night, a message had arrived via [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s caravan contact — a fresh and time-sensitive lead on her sister that would be lost if not followed immediately. She had already left. The remaining party: [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]].

**[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]'s report**: He returned overnight, bloodied, but alive (enough). He sat down, recovered, then laid out what he found.

> *"The directions you gave me led me to the remnants of an old Nordic ruin north of here, up by the edge of the mesa. It is guarded sporadically. That is not the main problem. Normally, ruins like that have several entrances apart from the main one scattered about. It seems that the cult has Warded them quite well. Powerful Warding glyphs that I, unfortunately, with my limited capabilities in conjuration, cannot dispel."*

He then turned to [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] — her schools are **destruction** and **restoration** (she barely looked up from her book). Not conjuration or alteration.

**Two options:**
1. Find a mage nearby skilled in **conjuration or alteration** who can dispel the Wards
2. Brew an alchemical **varnish** that will dissolve them — requires a **pearl** (easily purchased from the alchemist) and a **hag raven feather** (harder to source in this area)

On the hag raven feather — [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] disclosed she is acquainted with a **hag raven** from a prior solo session. She had tracked the **Witch of the Woods** — a hagravenn, part hag part bird, covered in raven feathers, wielding powerful frost magic — west of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023a Periwyns witch hunt\|Session 23a]]. She took a frost bolt to the face, broke line of sight behind a tree, and called for a parlay. Rather than fight, she brokered a **truce**: the Witch would stop attacking hunters and their livestock; in exchange, the hunters of the nearby town would deliver **monthly offerings of fresh hearts, eyeballs, and gems** for the creature's magical practices. The Witch agreed, tentatively — *"Maybe"*, if the humans held up their end. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s pitch was that the arrangement protected the Witch from eventual mob retaliation while letting her study in peace. Whether the townspeople are still delivering those offerings is an open question.

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] responded to this with barely concealed exasperation (*"I swear. Bretons and hag-ravens."*). After some debate about whether to just kill it, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] offered to handle retrieving the feather himself — he can approach it directly and either negotiate or take what he needs. The party agreed.

**Message by crow**: A [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] crow arrived with two messages:
- **For [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: The guard captain Luulov is looking for him — some quest or task for the guild. He can't reach the barrow, so it can wait, but [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] should check in.
- **For [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: *"Some women are asking about you, peddling in hidden places and filthy places."* — Unclear but acknowledged. She thanked the crow.

**The plan**: The party splits. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] retrieves the feather tonight. The party returns to [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]:
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]: library (mage backup), alchemist (pearl), [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|Lower Lodge]] (network contact)
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]: Silver Road Guildhouse (check in with Valen, meet Luulov)

All three exit the barrow. It is snowing. No horses (off-road terrain, same reasoning as coming out). Their tracks have been completely buried by two days of snowfall.

### Late Morning — Durov's Navigation

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] exited the barrow, turned right, and kept going.

The GM ran him through a sequence of **Navigate** tests across the mountain forest:

- Right for five minutes. **Navigate**: failed — ended up slightly off-course.
- Kept going anyway. Another **Navigate**: ended up at a small frozen pond (the same one the party had skirted on the way in, where the Wisp Mother lives). He crouched at the edge and drank some of the water. **Endurance** test (disease, from unknown water): passed — no hentai virus.
- Left from the pond. Another **Navigate**: continued to meander roughly south.
- Periodically [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], walking behind at distance with [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], used **Clairvoyance** to track him by targeting him directly — received a purple-glowing magical arrow in her palm indicating his direction. She and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] followed at a safe distance and watched.
- After about an hour and a half, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] emerged not at the south gate — but at the **west gate** of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], approximately half an hour off course from the exit point.

The GM confirmed: every single failed navigate roll still consistently sent him toward the city. He spotted the stables with his horse immediately upon clearing the treeline. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s Clairvoyance had tracked him perfectly throughout. Neither she nor [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] said anything. They just watched and followed at distance, laughing.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] greeted his horse, confirmed it had been fed, and headed for the Silver Road Guildhouse.

### Late Morning — The Grand Library (Onasha and Periwyn)

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] split off toward the Grand Library — a grand two-storey wooden building with sections, fires, reading areas, and colour-coded shelves by school of magic.

A librarian on the ground floor pointed them to the second floor for mages. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] asked specifically for a **curly-haired Breton**. She was directed toward the second floor.

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s approach**: Picked up a random book from the illusion section and pretended to browse. The curly-haired mage noticed immediately. She moved aisle to aisle checking mages by race from a distance (observe test to see race: passed). Eventually landed in the **restoration** section, where she spotted a **Breton mage** browsing.

She approached obliquely, mentioning the rarity of their people in this part of the country. The mage introduced himself: **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Father_Aldric\|Father Aldric]]**, a **priest of Stendarr**, primarily a **restoration mage**, dabbler in **destruction** and **alteration**. He had a jug of mead beside him on the shelf. He was standing with his hands folded, very priestly.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] asked if he could remove a Ward. He said he had done it before, yes.

> *"As a man of God — or as a fellow Breton — whichever ranks higher for you. Would you help us?"*
>
> *"If it's for a bottle of mead, it's for a bottle of mead."*

She confirmed: they needed to break into a place, for a noble cause, tied to a trial she had to undergo before she could return to the castle (?). He agreed, without excessive detail. She told him to meet her **tomorrow at the inn** — she'd introduce him to the others then. He bowed, returned to his books and his mead.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] aside: She asked if [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] (nearby and listening) had any human hearts in her pack. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] confirmed she did not have any human hearts, then immediately reconsidered the question. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] confirmed: she wanted to restock her alchemical ingredients. Human heart is an ingredient.

### Midday — Julian's Alchemist Shop

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] arrived at Julian's — recognisable from the lavender smell from the street. The elderly woman was mortar-and-pestling something with her eyes closed (or very nearly).

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] picked up the flowers she'd been carrying and, while Julian went to the back to find the pearl, slipped a **red mountain flower** into the vase — noticing that the **previous flower** she'd placed was still in it, doing well. She added the second one beside it.

Julian returned and sold her **one pearl for 8 gold**. Mention of the Silver Road's effect on prices: Julian was calm. *"Life finds a way. It will take care of itself."* [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] confirmed she was sure Julian's time wasn't coming soon. Julian sent them off warmly.

**Pearl confirmed as alchemy ingredient**: dispel and frost, according to [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s quick check. Combined with the head-raven feather (from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]), this makes the Ward-dissolving varnish.

### Midday — The Lower Lodge (Onasha and Periwyn)

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] took [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] through the sewers — the familiar diamond-circle symbol on the entrance — and into the **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|Lower Lodge]]**. It was noticeably **busier** than last time. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] spotted several of the **warehouse refugees** she had sent here after the raid, now talking with other people and seeming settled.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] made her way to **Taria's** desk. Taria looked up from her journal.

**Taria's briefing**: The network had been busy. A **market protest** was being organised — every Silver Road-affiliated merchant was being called out to the market by her people, pulling guards to keep the peace. Estimated **40–50 guards** at the market perimeter, weapons sheathed. The protest is primarily working merchants (those whose income is being squeezed) and is making noise about tariffs, unfair trade, and the general state of the city economy.

**Warehouse task**: With workers out protesting and most guards occupied, the warehouses would be lightly defended. Taria's plan:
1. **Deliver encoded letters** to two embedded contacts inside the warehouse district — identifiable by the network's mark (diamond with circle)
2. The contacts would supply **death bell tripwire traps** and **kindling**
3. Plant the traps and burn at least one warehouse

**Death bells**: Taria confirmed these are the network's calling card — tripwires set near pillars that will hit suspicious crates if triggered. *"Our people that are tripwires."*

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] (now listening in) was introduced as associated with [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and not the Night of Arc.

Additional intel from Taria on the skin-changer situation:
- **The Jarl (skin-changer) has not left the Yarls Hall** — Taria's people have the building surrounded and have access to the sewers. No secret exits or entrances detected.
- **He has been more agitated lately** — consistent with the missing memory anchor.

### Midday — Silver Road Guildhouse (Durov)

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] arrived at the **eastern gate** and found the Silver Road Guildhouse just outside it, with two guards on the door who didn't recognise him.

> *"I don't think your boss wants to be kept waiting."*

One guard went inside. Five minutes later: confirmed and waved through. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] paused to give the first guard a look. The guard didn't break. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] shrugged.

Inside, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Valen Marr]]** greeted him briefly, noting Luulov had been looking for him and had gone out to search for him in the city. No extended conversation — Valen had business to attend to. He pointed [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] toward the refreshments and headed upstairs.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] found **hummingbird mead**. Grabbed the jug and three mugs. Brought two mugs out to the door guards. Guard one (willpower test): reluctantly grabbed it, held it stiffly. Guard two (willpower test: failed): did not grab it. The mug fell, shattered on the stone, mead spilled on his boots. He continued standing completely still. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] clinked his mug with the first guard and drank alone, waiting.

**Luulov arrived around noon**, approached [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], and said he'd been looking for him. They took a walk through the alleys to somewhere secluded.

**Luulov's briefing**:
- The missing persons situation is far wider than he initially thought
- He has a **personal lead** — for a family friend, not official Guild business (Valen has been briefed but doesn't know the details)
- He wants [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] with him: the informants they need to speak with are people who don't exactly cooperate with the Guild, and having [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] along is useful both socially and physically
- First stop: the **protest**, to hear complaints and take statements (some protesters have missing loved ones)

They walked together, Luulov's Silver Road insignia covered by his cloak.

### Afternoon — The Market Protest (Durov and Luulov)

Roughly **40–50 guards** maintained a loose perimeter around the market, weapons sheathed. Merchants and workers with torches and wooden boards. Loud but not violent.

**Three complainants approached [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] (in his capacity as guild complaints officer):**

**1. Hana** — elderly male merchant, appeared to be a general goods trader. Tariffs have pushed several merchants to a point where they're selling at a loss or going hungry. He had not lost any people personally but was speaking on behalf of a group. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] promised continued attention and not to be silenced.

**2. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Yelena\|Yelena]]** — female tanner, representative of the eastern tannery. The Guild has been paying workers in **strange gold** (smelly, discoloured) that some workers have refused to accept. The guild's response to refusal has been **docked pay**. Several tannery workers have had to resort to "unpleasant means" to afford food. She mentioned a specific worker — a man who had been at the warehouses — as someone whose pay had been docked. She thought he was still at the warehouses somewhere. The docked pay started approximately **one week ago** (timeline consistent with the stone going missing). [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] took note.

**3. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lady_Aine\|Lady Aine]]** — posh, well-dressed woman of some standing. Arrived with a cane and a frock. Her husband is **His Illustrious Magnificence [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jean-Pierre_LaRouche\|Jean-Pierre LaRoche]], Marquis of the Sapphire Coast, Baron of the Nine Rooms, Keeper of the Royal Cocoon Seals, and Patriarch of House LaRoche** — a silk merchant in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. He went missing **over a week ago**, last seen leaving home to open his market stall. His stall is still standing, frozen fabrics abandoned. She last recalls him being somewhat absent-minded since the Jarl's banquet. His description: a Breton, memorable curling moustache.

After the protest, Luulov ushered [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] toward the old **Blackfoot Guild house**, where shady informants were waiting in the shadows with their missing persons lead. As they approached, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] heard commotion from behind him. He looked back.

Two massive columns of smoke rose from the warehouse district.

### Afternoon — Warehouse Sabotage (Onasha and Periwyn)

After leaving [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|the Lower Lodge]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] made for the warehouses.

**First warehouse**: Guard at the front, slightly inattentive and wobbly. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] distracted the guard with a theatrical Deceive — *"Over there! They tried to tackle you!"* — just barely convincing enough. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] slipped behind the guard, rolled combat style, and hit him over the head with her **war hammer** (flat side) for 8 damage. He collapsed. She stuffed cloth deep into his throat to prevent him waking up or alerting anyone, then propped him up against the wall to look like he was napping.

Inside: crates, leftover work equipment, signs of people who left quickly. An observe test spotted a shady miscreant in the corner — a Blackfeather network contact. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] flashed the diamond-circle hand signal; he responded. He directed her to a **pillar**, told her the foreman walked through underneath it regularly, and handed her a **death bell tripwire trap** and a **yellow death bellflower**.

She checked the upstairs office: standard warehouse inventory. Some **pages ripped from the manifest** — missing. Couldn't find them anywhere in the office.

Set up the tripwire near the pillar. Engineering +20: passed. The trap was correctly rigged.

Left the first warehouse.

**Second warehouse**: No guard visible at the front — only a blood stain and drag marks in the snow where the previous guard had been standing. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] covered it with fresh snow. Inside: another contact, this one moving openly and already setting things up with death bells. He confirmed receipt of his letter, showed her where every trap was already placed, and handed her **kindling**.

She checked the second office as well: same layout. **Manifest pages ripped here too**, not in the office. Investigation test: failed — nothing else found.

Back outside. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] confirmed the plan — everyone out, next building down. Contact whistled, cleared the building.

**The burn**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] asked if [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] wanted her to do it. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] already had a sling. She wound up and let the kindling fly from the next building over. It landed in the crates. The fire spread immediately — all the pre-set traps and death bells going off in sequence, firebombs dropping from crates, the entire warehouse going up at once.

Shortly after: the first warehouse also went up, as heard from the distance. Taria's network agents had triggered their own set. Both warehouses burned simultaneously.
