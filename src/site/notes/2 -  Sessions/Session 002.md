# Session 002 - 2024-12-20

## Summary
The party investigated tensions in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]], uncovering dam sabotage by a Dunmer agent named [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith Velan]] working for the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] faction. They restored the dam, returned the body to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], and reported to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]].

## Detailed Recap

### Treva's Bend
- Arrived at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]], a low settlement along the riverbank with muddy paths
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Alderman Torgir]] greeted them with suspicion until the Queen's name was invoked
  - Confirmed Clan [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod]] had turned the river against them
  - Admitted to meeting a so-called "concerned kinsman" under cover of darkness near the dam upstream
  - Noted Eldar's interaction with a strange man
  - Fishermen had stopped trading

### Snow-Shod Dam Investigation
- Trekked along a rugged trail flanked by drooping trees and moss-covered stones to the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Dam\|Snow-Shod Dam]]
- Two Snow-Shod guards refused passage — [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] stepped forward, voice firm and low, invoking the Queen's name; the guards reluctantly relented
- The dam showed signs of tampering: broken tools in the reeds, footprints in the mud leading toward dense woods
- A thin curl of smoke rose above the canopy nearby
- **Camp Investigation:**
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] examined the still-burning, smoldering campsite
  - Found another note bearing the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] sigil (no text)
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] spotted fresh drag marks near the firepit
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] retrieved charred fragments of a note from the embers (burnt paper scraps potentially recoverable by magic)
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] scouted the perimeter, tracking a heavy object carried northward
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] vanished into the brush, silent as moonlight

### Discovery of Adrith Velan
- Found a dead Dunmer at the base of a gnarled oak: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith Velan]]
  - Face slack, limbs askew, purple staining his lips
  - Poisoned with concentrated nightshade extract
  - A single nightshade flower on the body
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s spines flared at the sight — she called it justice
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], with quiet understanding, offered her warhammer
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] took it without pause and struck to ensure the Dunmer would not rise again
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith]]'s journal revealed critical information:
  - He had not come to help [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]] but to inflame it
  - Working for the serpent faction, he had blocked the flow regulators in the dam to stoke discord between the fishermen and the Snow-Shods
  - Mentions of mysterious footsteps near his camp

### Restoring the Dam
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] slung [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith]]'s corpse over his shoulder and dragged it back to the dam
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] dove into the frigid water and cleared the blockages with practiced precision
- The dam groaned — and the river surged anew; water levels returned to normal

### Return to Treva's Bend
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Torgir]] met them with something like gratitude
- Promised to reach out to [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Snow-Shod\|Clan Snow-Shod]] to attempt a fragile reconciliation
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], unimpressed by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alderman_Torgir\|Torgir]]'s earlier frost, asked for compensation
- The alderman conceded: gold would come once the nets were full again
  - 50 gold per party member from Aldermen (to be collected later)

### Riften Investigations
- Returned to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith]]'s corpse
- Reported to the city barracks — Captain [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Harvold\|Harvold]] recognized [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith Velan]]'s name and directed them to bring the corpse to the morgue
- Delivered the body to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sigrid\|Sigrid]] at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall of the Dead]] — she agreed to study the poison and send word of her findings
- Met with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]] at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil Keep]]
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] delivered [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith]]'s diary
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] provided a detailed report
  - Reward: 200 gold from the Queen
  - The Queen's unease was plain — the serpent sigil and deadly poison pointed to something larger than a simple border feud
  - She gave a quiet warning: their service may soon be required again
- Consulted Court Wizard [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Speaks-Spells-Gently\|Speaks-Spells-Gently]] (Argonian)
  - Unable to recover the burnt paper scraps
  - Suggested [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Winterhold\|Winterhold]] mages might help
- **Social Interactions:**
  - A drunken brawl drew a small crowd in the market
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], ever the opportunist, placed a bet on the larger brawler — and walked away grinning, a few coins heavier
  - Merchants directed to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]] tavern
- That night, the party returned to the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]] — mead flowed freely, firelight danced across worn wood and weary faces
