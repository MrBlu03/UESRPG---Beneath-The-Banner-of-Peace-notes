# Session 027

## Summary

**The warehouse district is burning.** As two massive plumes of smoke rise from the eastern side of town at 4:00 PM, the party's paths diverge as they navigate the immediate fallout of the sabotage. 

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]]** chose to prioritize their contact first. In the alleys near the [[Info/Locations/Blackfoot_Guild_House_Kjalgrad\|abandoned Blackfoot Guildhouse]], they met a shady informant named John, who handed them journal scraps from a missing merchant. The scraps detailed nightmares about trade, hearing the Jarl's voice in his head, and a final note about going to see the mesa that afternoon. Afterward, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] investigated the charred remains of the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District\|warehouses]]. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] discovered death bell flowers left as a calling card, while [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] tasted oily residue on jar shards, confirming it was standard flammable lamp oil. They found footprints with a tail drag ([[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s signature) leading to a sewer grate, which [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] marked on his map for later exploration.

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** attempted to escape by blending into the panicking crowd heading north on the main street. However, they were intercepted by a large patrol of city guards. While [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] tried to play the role of a terrified witness, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] attempted to cast a Charm spell on a guard. Forgetting that magic is highly visible in this setting, the cast alerted the entire patrol. Both were stripped of their gear and detained, though they had luckily left their most incriminating items (such as the necromancy book and guild ledger) back at the [[Info/Locations/Barrow_of_the_Oath_Breaker_King\|barrow]]. Along the way, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] tricked a guard into untying her hands for a bathroom break, knocked him out with a shock spell, and escaped into the alleys. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] was dragged to the dungeons beneath the [[Info/Locations/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]].

**In the dungeon**, the Jarl's corrupt steward [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] interrogated and tortured [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]], beating her and confiscating [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s journal. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] arrived shortly after, sent by the guild to interrogate the "suspicious Argonian." [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] successfully deceived the steward into leaving, claiming he had no connection to the prisoner. Left alone to inspect the confiscated gear, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] discovered the [[Info/1 - Party Findings/Official Writ of Authority\|Queen's writ of authority]] in [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s journal and hid it in his armor chest compartment to protect the party. 

**The jailbreak** was initiated by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], who regrouped with Taria at the Lower Lodge sewer hideout. Armed with a magicka potion, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and a Lower Lodge rescue squad led by Tabitha navigated the old [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad_Sewers\|sewers]] to a false wall in the dungeon's evidence room. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] confronted them briefly but quickly shut the door to block [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]]'s view, slipping the [[Info/1 - Party Findings/Official Writ of Authority\|Queen's writ]] back to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]. After [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] left, the rescue squad opened the false wall again, stunned the remaining guards, and freed [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]. Seeking revenge for the torture, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] refused to leave empty-handed. As they escaped, she threw a flame bomb into the hallway of the [[Info/Locations/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]], setting the curtains and carpets ablaze. The session ends with the hall burning and the party escaping into the night.

## Detailed Recap

### Afternoon — The Warehouse Aftermath & Shady Leads (Durov and Irlav)

* **Meeting the Shady Contact**: At 4:00 PM, as explosions rocked the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District\|warehouse district]], [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] stood in the alleyways near the [[Info/Locations/Blackfoot_Guild_House_Kjalgrad\|abandoned Blackfoot Guildhouse]]. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] chose to stick to the meeting first. They met John, a shady informant, who handed them journal scraps from a nameless missing merchant.
* **The Merchant's Journal Scraps**: ramblings of a merchant who had been inspired by the Jarl's speech but soon began experiencing nightmares and hearing the Jarl's voice in his head.
	> *"It seems to be some journal scraps of said merchant. Some notes about being really inspired by the Jarl's speech. Followed by increased ramblings about hearing the voice in his head here and there on off days. Then some nightmares involving trade... and then abruptly, a line about going to see the mesa this afternoon."*
* **Investigating the Ruins**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] headed to the warehouse district via back roads to avoid the crowds. The warehouse [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] had worked at was in ruins, with 30 city guards attempting to contain the fire.
* **Death Bells and Lamp Oil**: 
	* [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] ushered [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] to a charred passageway and pointed out recently planted death bell flowers.
	* [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] investigated jar shards covered in oily residue. He licked the oil and passed a survival test to identify it as bog-standard flammable lamp oil.
	* They contained the fire by laying stones and snow around the perimeter.
* **Tracking the Footprints**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] rolled an observe test (succeeding under 20) and spotted footprints with a tail drag leading away from the fire. They tracked them through the alleys to a sewer grate. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] marked the location on his map for a future expedition, and they returned to help the guards at the main warehouse.

---

### Late Afternoon — Detention & The Illusion Failure (Onasha and Periwyn)

* **Running with the Crowd**: Following the explosions, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] fled north along the main street to blend in with the panicked crowd. One of Taria's men split off into the alleys, telling them to meet back at the Lower Lodge hideout.
* **Intercepted by Guards**: They ran directly into a patrol of city guards heading toward the warehouse district. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] tried to look frantic and scream about the explosions to direct them away, while [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] ran into one of the guards to sell the panic.
* **The Magic Blunder**: A guard stopped them to ask why they were running from the district. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] fabricated a story about looking to buy or rent storage space. When the guard grew suspicious and decided to escort them to the barracks, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] attempted to cast a Charm spell on him.
* **Arrested and Stripped**: Because magic is highly visible, the surrounding guards immediately noticed the cast. They drew their weapons and surrounded them. The party was stripped of their gear and bound with ropes. Luckily, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] had left her necromancy book and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] had left the guild ledger back at the [[Info/Locations/Barrow_of_the_Oath_Breaker_King\|barrow]].
* **The Outhouse Escape**: While being marched to the dungeon beneath [[Info/Locations/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]], [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] convinced a guard she urgently needed to relieve herself. The guard escorted her to a wooden outhouse on the poorer side of town and untied her hands.
* **Lightning Bolt and Flight**: As she came out, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] tried to cast a spell on the guard. The guard immediately lunged, punching her (1 damage). [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] fought back, pushing him against the outhouse and casting a shock spell that dealt 6 damage. With the guard stunned and convulsing, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] dashed into the alleys and successfully broke line of sight, though the crackle of her lightning alerted guards across the city.

### Evening — Interrogation & The Vault (Onasha, Durov, and Irlav)

* **Torture in the Dungeon**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] was taken to the dungeon beneath the [[Info/Locations/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]] and chained to the wall. The corrupt steward [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] arrived with guards and taunted her. Recognizing she had no Shadowscale training or markings, he decided they would bypass official charges and simply execute her. The guards punched and tortured her, dealing 2 damage.
* **Interrogating the "Argonian"**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] arrived at the dungeon around 5:30 PM, sent by the guild to question the detained suspect. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] walked into the cell and confronted the steward, telling him he would handle the interrogation.
* **Deceiving the Steward**: The steward questioned [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]'s relationship with the Argonian, bringing up the party's presence during the forged letter incident in Session 21. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] managed to successfully deceive the steward, arguing they had arrived separately and had no official connection. The steward, convinced, left to check on other matters, leaving [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] to handle the interrogation.
* **Securing the Queen's Writ**: The steward instructed [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] to check the confiscated belongings in the next room. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] brought the backpacks and gear in. While sorting through [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s journal, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] discovered the [[Info/1 - Party Findings/Official Writ of Authority\|Queen's writ of authority]] from [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]]. Knowing it was highly incriminating, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] pocketed the letter and hid it in a compartment in his chest armor.
* **Message by Crow**: While the steward was away, a purple-hued crow flew through the window bars of [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s cell. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] whispered an urgent message to the bird for the [[Info/Organizations/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]], warning them that the steward is corrupt and connected to the cult, with possible Daedric dealings. The crow flew out to deliver the message.

### Night — Jailbreak & The Yarls Hall Fire (Onasha, Periwyn, and Durov)

* **Planning at the Lodge**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] arrived at the Lower Lodge in a panic, explaining that [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] was captured and her journal was confiscated. Taria gave [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] a magicka potion and gathered a rescue team.
* **Sewer Infiltration**: Led by Tabitha, the rescue squad navigated the sewer ruins beneath the [[Info/Locations/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]], arriving at a false wall behind the evidence room. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] slipped on a ledge during the approach, taking 1 damage from the fall.
* **Confrontation and Secret Handshake**: Tabitha pulled a candle sconce to open the false wall into the evidence room. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]], who was inside checking the gear, drew his sword. Recognizing [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and Taria's men, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] quickly shut the door to block [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]]'s view. He slipped the [[Info/1 - Party Findings/Official Writ of Authority\|Queen's writ]] back to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], told them to wait, and closed the false wall. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] then left the dungeon.
* **The Breakout**: Once the guards left, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] opened the false wall again. Two dungeon guards entered the hallway, one rattling his sword against the bars to mock [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] cast a shock spell (rolling a 1 on d6, dealing 1 damage) to stun the guard. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] was freed and reclaimed her gear.
* **Revenge and Fire**: Refusing to leave the steward alive after the torture, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] decided to burn the hall. As they ran out of the dungeon into the main corridors, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] grabbed a flame bomb and hurled it at the curtains. The fire spread rapidly across the carpeted hallway and [[Info/Locations/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]], sending guards into a panic as the party bolted into the night.
