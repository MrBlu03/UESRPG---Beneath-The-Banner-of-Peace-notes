# Session 027

## Summary

**The warehouse district is burning.** As two massive plumes of smoke rise from the eastern side of town at 4:00 PM, the party's paths diverge as they navigate the immediate fallout of the sabotage. 

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]]** chose to prioritize their contact first. In the alleys near the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Old_Blackfoot_Guild_House\|abandoned Blackfoot Guildhouse]], they met a shady informant named John, who handed them journal scraps from a missing merchant. The scraps detailed nightmares about trade, hearing the Jarl's voice in his head, and a final note about going to see the mesa that afternoon. Afterward, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] investigated the charred remains of the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District\|warehouses]]. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] discovered death bell flowers left as a calling card, while [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] tasted oily residue on jar shards, confirming it was standard flammable lamp oil. They found footprints with a tail drag leading to a sewer grate, which [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] marked on his map for later exploration.

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** attempted to escape by blending into the panicking crowd heading north on the main street. However, they were intercepted by a large patrol of city guards. While [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] tried to play the role of a terrified witness, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] attempted to cast a Charm spell on a guard. Forgetting that magic is highly visible, the cast alerted the entire patrol. Both were stripped of their gear and detained, though they had luckily left their most incriminating items (such as the necromancy book and guild ledger) back at the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Barrow_of_the_Oath-Breaker_King\|barrow]]. Along the way, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] tricked a guard into untying her hands for a bathroom break, knocked him out with a shock spell, and escaped into the alleys. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] was dragged to the dungeons beneath the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]].

**In the dungeon**, the Jarl's corrupt steward [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Karo]] interrogated and tortured [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], beating her and confiscating [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s journal. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] arrived shortly after, sent by the guild to interrogate the "suspicious Argonian." [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] successfully deceived the steward into leaving, claiming he had no connection to the prisoner. Left alone to inspect the confiscated gear, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] discovered the [[Info/Findings & Items/Documents & Notes/Official Writ of Authority\|Queen's writ of authority]] in [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s journal and hid it in his armor chest compartment to protect the party. 

**The jailbreak** was initiated by [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], who regrouped with Taria at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|the Lower Lodge]] sewer hideout. Armed with a magicka potion, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and a [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|Lower Lodge]] rescue squad led by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tabitha\|Tabitha]] navigated the old [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Sewers\|sewers]] to a false wall in the dungeon's evidence room. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] confronted them briefly but quickly shut the door to block [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]]'s view, slipping the [[Info/Findings & Items/Documents & Notes/Official Writ of Authority\|Queen's writ]] back to [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]. After [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] left, the rescue squad opened the false wall again, stunned the remaining guards, and freed [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]. Seeking revenge for the torture, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] refused to leave empty-handed. As they escaped, she threw a flame bomb into the hallway of the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]], setting the curtains and carpets ablaze. The session ends with the hall burning and the party escaping into the night.

## Detailed Recap

### Afternoon — The Warehouse Aftermath & Shady Leads (Durov and Irlav)

* **Meeting the Shady Contact**: At 4:00 PM, as explosions rocked the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District\|warehouse district]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] stood in the alleyways near the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Old_Blackfoot_Guild_House\|abandoned Blackfoot Guildhouse]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] chose to stick to the meeting first. They met John, a shady informant, who handed them journal scraps from a nameless missing merchant.
* **The Merchant's Journal Scraps**: ramblings of a merchant who had been inspired by the Jarl's speech but soon began experiencing nightmares and hearing the Jarl's voice in his head.
	> *"It seems to be some journal scraps of said merchant. Some notes about being really inspired by the Jarl's speech. Followed by increased ramblings about hearing the voice in his head here and there on off days. Then some nightmares involving trade... and then abruptly, a line about going to see the mesa this afternoon."*
* **Investigating the Ruins**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] headed to the warehouse district via back roads to avoid the crowds. The warehouse [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] had worked at was in ruins, with 30 city guards attempting to contain the fire.
* **Death Bells and Lamp Oil**: 
	* [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] ushered [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] to a charred passageway and pointed out recently planted death bell flowers.
	* [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] investigated jar shards covered in oily residue. He licked the oil and passed a survival test to identify it as bog-standard flammable lamp oil.
	* They contained the fire by laying stones and snow around the perimeter.
* **Tracking the Footprints**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] rolled an observe test (succeeding under 20) and spotted footprints with a tail drag leading away from the fire. They tracked them through the alleys to a sewer grate. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] marked the location on his map for a future expedition, and they returned to help the guards at the main warehouse.

---

### Late Afternoon — Detention & The Illusion Failure (Onasha and Periwyn)

* **Running with the Crowd**: Following the explosions, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] fled north along the main street to blend in with the panicked crowd. One of Taria's men split off into the alleys, telling them to meet back at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|the Lower Lodge]] hideout.
* **Intercepted by Guards**: They ran directly into a patrol of city guards heading toward the warehouse district. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] tried to look frantic and scream about the explosions to direct them away, while [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] ran into one of the guards to sell the panic.
* **The Magic Blunder**: A guard stopped them to ask why they were running from the district. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] fabricated a story about looking to buy or rent storage space. When the guard grew suspicious and decided to escort them to the barracks, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] attempted to cast a Charm spell on him.
* **Arrested and Stripped**: Because magic is highly visible, the surrounding guards immediately noticed the cast. They drew their weapons and surrounded them. The party was stripped of their gear and bound with ropes. Luckily, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] had left her necromancy book and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] had left the guild ledger back at the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Barrow_of_the_Oath-Breaker_King\|barrow]].
* **The Outhouse Escape**: While being marched to the dungeon beneath [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] convinced a guard she urgently needed to relieve herself. The guard escorted her to a wooden outhouse on the poorer side of town and untied her hands.
* **Lightning Bolt and Flight**: As she came out, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] tried to cast a spell on the guard. The guard immediately lunged, punching her (1 damage). [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] fought back, pushing him against the outhouse and casting a shock spell. With the guard stunned and convulsing, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] dashed into the alleys and successfully broke line of sight, though the crackle of her lightning alerted guards across the city.

---

### Evening — Interrogation & The Vault (Onasha, Durov, and Irlav)

* **Torture in the Dungeon**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] was taken to the dungeon beneath the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]] and chained to the wall. The corrupt steward [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]] arrived with guards and taunted her. He openly admitted that it was he who had paid the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] to capture her weeks before. Recognizing she had no Shadowscale training or markings, he decided they would bypass official charges and simply execute her. The guards punched and tortured her, dealing 2 damage.
* **Interrogating the "Argonian"**: By sunset, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] descended into the dungeon, sent by the guild to question the detained suspect. Their arrival sparked sharp words between [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]] concerning jurisdiction. Quite quickly, Caro relented and left.
* **Securing the Queen's Writ**: Left to interrogate the prisoner and check the confiscated belongings, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] searched [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s journal. A folded parchment slipped onto the floor — the [[Info/Findings & Items/Documents & Notes/Official Writ of Authority\|Queen's writ of authority]] from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] attempted to dismiss it casually, but [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] was unconvinced. Taking the document, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] departed briefly to verify its authenticity.
* **Message by Crow**: While [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] was away, a purple-hued crow flew through the window bars of [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s cell. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] whispered an urgent message to the bird for the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]], warning them that the steward is corrupt and connected to the cult, with possible Daedric dealings. The crow flew out to deliver the message.
* **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]]'s Loyalty**: Moments later, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] returned. His expression had changed entirely. Without ceremony, he handed the writ back to [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]. *"Keep it hidden,"* he said quietly. His respect for [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Volng\|Clan Volng]] outweighed whatever questions lingered in his mind. Gathering what they required, the two men departed to answer [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Valen Marr]]'s summons.

---

### Night — Jailbreak & The Yarls Hall Fire (Onasha, Periwyn, and Durov)

* **Planning at the Lodge**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] arrived at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Lower_Lodge\|the Lower Lodge]] in a panic, explaining that [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] was captured and her journal was confiscated. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tarja\|Tarja]] listened without interruption, then handed [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] a magicka potion and gathered a rescue team.
* **Sewer Infiltration**: Led by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tabitha\|Tabitha]], the rescue squad navigated the sewer ruins beneath the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]], arriving at a false wall behind the evidence room. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] slipped on a ledge during the approach, taking 1 damage from the fall.
* **Confrontation and Secret Handshake**: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tabitha\|Tabitha]] pulled a candle sconce to open the false wall into the evidence room. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], hearing the scrape, drew his sword and discovered them. He quickly lowered his blade. *"Wait,"* he whispered, explaining that leaving immediately would jeopardize his standing with the Guild. The next watch would change soon, which would be their moment. He closed the false wall, and they waited.
* **The Breakout**: Two fresh guards entered the dungeon. One amused himself by dragging his sword along the iron bars of [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s cell. The hidden passage opened again. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] cast a shock spell (dealing 1 damage) to instantly drop the first guard. The second guard merely raised his hands — he belonged to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tarja\|Tarja]]'s people. To preserve appearances, they struck him soundly before departing. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] was freed and reclaimed her gear.
* **Revenge and Fire**: Refusing to leave the steward alive after the torture, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] chose another road. She and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] climbed toward the upper halls of the Jarl's residence. As they passed, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] hurled a flame bomb into the corridors. Curtains caught first, then timber. By the time they reached the upper floors, smoke was already following close behind. And somewhere above the growing fire, Steward [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius_Caro]] remained. The session ends with the hall burning and the party escaping into the night.
