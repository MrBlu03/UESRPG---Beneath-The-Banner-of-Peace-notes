# Session 028

## Summary

**The Jarl's steward has been captured.** While the [[Info/Locations/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]] burns, the party acts swiftly on multiple fronts to secure critical intelligence and adapt to a changing tactical situation.

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** launched a surprise assault on the office of the corrupt steward, **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]]**. After a swift and bloody battle in which both of his guards were eliminated, [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] was knocked unconscious as he attempted to flee. As they went to search his body, his form shifted, revealing his true identity as a **Dremora** (specifically a **Scaafin**-like Daedra in service to the [[Cult of Clavicus Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]]). The Dark Brotherhood assassin, **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|Milos]]**, arrived from the window to collect the steward under specific orders. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] convinced him to allow a brief interrogation in the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad_Sewers\|sewers]] as they fled the collapsing, burning hall. During the interrogation, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] scorched the Dremora's face with *Sunbeam* magic to bypass his Daedric ward, forcing him to confess that he hired the [[Info/Organizations/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna_Tong]] due to "mutual interest" to eliminate the party and the Dunmer for being "too nosy." In a fit of rage, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] slashed his cheek with her steel dagger, and they transported him to the [[Info/Locations/Northeast_Warehouse_and_Main_Facility\|Main Facility Ruin]] north of Kjalgrad to lock him up.

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]]** rode through the night to the same [[Info/Locations/Northeast_Warehouse_and_Main_Facility\|Main Facility Ruin]] (which Irlav opened using [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]'s Ouroboros pendant of authority). There, they met with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] inside the main hall. Valen revealed that due to the Kjalgrad warehouse explosions, he would be leaving the city soon. Before his departure, he tasked [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] with investigating a former [[Info/Organizations/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna_Tong]] camp that had been abandoned under suspicious circumstances. Valen assigned one of his trusted Imperial guards, **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Proventus\|Proventus]]**, to accompany them. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] commanded the formal, marching guard to stop marching and speak informally as they prepared for their mission.

## Detailed Recap

### Night — The Steward's Office & Interrogation (Periwyn and Onasha)

* **Breaching the Office**: At the burning [[Info/Locations/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Yarls Hall]], [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] kicked down the door to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]]'s office to catch him and his guards by surprise.
* **The Office Combat**: 
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] threw a paralysis bomb into the room, paralyzing Guard 1 while Guard 2 resisted the effect.
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] performed a swift Coup de Grace on Guard 1, cutting off his leg at the knee, then his arm, and finally his head. 
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] moved to engage Guard 2 with her longsword, dealing damage. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] threw a flame bomb, dealing fire damage to Guard 2.
  - Guard 2 struck back, dealing 3 damage to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] (reduced by her armor). [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] disengaged to avoid further hits, and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] handed her an Elixir of Healing (+12 HP) from her inventory.
  - [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] cast Firebolt twice at [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]. One hit dealt 1 damage (reduced by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s Breton magic resistance), while she absorbed the second.
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] struck Guard 2 with a final, devastating blow, twisting her longsword into his chest using a Penetrate Armor advantage to kill him.
* **True Identity Revealed**: As [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] tried to flee, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] knocked him unconscious with a pommel strike to the back of the head. Upon closer inspection, his illusion broke, and his body reverted to a horned, Scaafin-like **Dremora** associated with the [[Cult of Clavicus Vile\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]].
* **Meeting the Assassin**: The Dark Brotherhood assassin, **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|Milos]]**, arrived through the window to collect the steward under mutual orders. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] convinced him to allow ten minutes of questioning. The party and the assassin dragged the Dremora steward into the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad_Sewers\|sewers]] just as the building's staircase collapsed behind them.
* **Sewer Interrogation**: 
  - Bound securely in the sewers, [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius_Karo]] initially refused to answer questions, using a Daedric ward to block [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s dagger strike.
  - The assassin offered [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] a potion of replenishment (+8 magicka), which she used to cast a high-level *Sunbeam* spell, scorching the Dremora's face.
  - Terrified and burned, the Dremora confessed that he hired the [[Info/Organizations/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna_Tong]] out of "mutual interest" to hunt down the party and the Dunmer ([[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The_Vestige]]) for being "too nosy."
  - Enraged by his taunting, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] slashed his cheek with her steel dagger. They then transported him out of the city to the [[Info/Locations/Northeast_Warehouse_and_Main_Facility\|Main Facility Ruin]] to be secured by the [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|Vestige]].
* **A Shared Past**: While resting, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] shared the tragic details of her past, describing the loss of her brother, her training with the [[Info/Organizations/Knights_of_Arkay\|Knights_of_Arkay]], and her solemn oath to hunt down vampires.

### Night — Dwemer Ruin & A New Assignment (Durov and Irlav)

* **Riding to the Ruin**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] rode north from Kjalgrad through the snowy woods in the middle of the night to meet [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]].
* ** پشت-Passage of Authority**: They arrived at the main door of the ruin, which was a solid Dwemer metal door. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] held up [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]]'s Ouroboros pendant of authority, and the heavy doors creaked open.
* **Exploring the Ruin**: Inside, they snaked through massive stone corridors lined with Dwemer metal, stone stools, and pipes. The ruins were guarded by numerous but undisciplined and under-equipped bandits dressed in furs and leather.
* **Meeting Valen Marr**: They met [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Valen_Marr\|Valen_Marr]] in a large room. Valen explained that with the city warehouses destroyed and public opinion turning against him, the powers that be decided he would leave Kjalgrad soon.
* **The Camonna Tong Camp Mission**: Valen tasked [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] with investigating a former camp of the [[Info/Organizations/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna_Tong]] that had been abandoned not by choice. He wanted them to search for left-behind clues, determine why they fled, and locate their new camp.
* **Proventus Assigned**: Valen assigned an Imperial guard from his entourage, **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Proventus\|Proventus]]**, to accompany them. Proventus, originally from the canton district of the Imperial City, had served Valen for three years after doing bounty work near Windhelm.
* **Durov's Threat**: As they departed, [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Proventus\|Proventus]] marched stiffly and formally. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] grabbed him by the collar and threatened to throw him around the room if he did not stop his formal marching and stop addressing him as "sir."
* **Crest of Bruma**: During their ride, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] asked [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Irlav\|Irlav]] about the eagle crest on his shield. Irlav explained it is the **Bruma Crest** (representing a Colovian noble house from Bruma, Cyrodiil). Irlav also detailed the Planemeld Crisis, when a Daedric anchor fell directly into Bruma's market in front of the Cathedral of Saint Lucia, and mentioned his father Colleux (a smith) and mother Leona.
