# Session 023

## Summary

Three days after the feast at the Jarl's Hall, the party resumed their investigation in **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]**. While **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** carried out a warehouse inspection for [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain Irlav]], he uncovered suspicious sealed crates containing **[[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Coin_Molds\|coin molds]], Daedric materials, and black candles**—all consistent with the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]]'s brimstone gold operation. Across town, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**, and **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** met with **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]]** at the Guild guildhouse, where [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] played the role of a young merchant seeking a trade partnership. While Vaelin stepped away, the party rifled through his office and discovered **bribe payments to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] officials**, references to **"the Coming Correction"**, and a burnt letter confirming the **Jarl is being supervised** by the Guild. The session closed with a message from the **[[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]]** arriving by crow.

---

## Detailed Recap

### Morning — Frost Gate Hall

**Settling In After Three Days of Downtime:**

The party had spent three days at **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost_Gate_Hall]]** following the trade feast at the Jarl's Hall. During that time, the team had been recovering, studying, and keeping a low profile.

- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** had previously boarded up the windows of her and **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s** shared room out of lingering paranoia following her abduction during their earlier downtime. She unboarded them this morning, a small but telling sign of easing tension.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** spent the morning feeding her horse **[[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]]** at the stables and practicing her newly acquired spells. She had studied **Sunbeam** (a Destruction spell) during the three days of downtime and was also working through two Illusion spell tomes: one for **Charm**, and one that conjures floating **light orbs**. The morning practice in the forest gave her a chance to demonstrate Sunbeam to the group—a dramatic flash of blinding light that impressed her [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|companions]]. She confirmed she had completed studying the light orb spell during the session.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** and **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** woke up together and prepared for the day.

---

### Morning — Stables Intelligence

While at the stables, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** overheard stable hands muttering in hushed but frustrated tones. She gathered the following:

- The **[[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]]** had **raised tariffs unexpectedly and significantly** (later confirmed at 45% on food and general goods)
- Citizens speaking out against the tariffs were being **arrested or disappearing entirely**—no bodies, no jail records
- A **clothing merchant** notable for his distinctive mustache had **vanished from his stall** after making his opinions public
- The city guards were widely believed to be **on the Guild's payroll**
- "Less than reputable people" had been **asking questions around the warehouses**—possibly a resistance or opposition network forming

---

### Morning — Tracking the Crow Contact

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** spotted a figure in the treeline outside the city—the same hooded figure associated with their **[[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]]** contact, accompanied by crows. The party headed into the forest to investigate.

- Tracks were found at a clearing but split in multiple directions, as if deliberately scattered
- Tracking attempts failed (difficult terrain, heavy snow cover, a -20 penalty applied)
- After approximately fifteen minutes of searching, the party gave up and returned to the city
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** also attempted to gather **alchemical ingredients** during the search but found nothing—everything was buried under deep snow
- The party returned to [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] by approximately 9am

---

### Morning — Durov and the Crowd

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** met with his employer **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain Irlav]]** early at the inn and was assigned a routine **warehouse inspection job** in the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District\|warehouse district]].

On the way to the warehouses, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] encountered an **angry crowd confronting a city guard** over the Guild's 45% tariff increases on food and goods. Tensions were high. Someone in the crowd threw a rock—which [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] intercepted with a raised arm before it struck. Rather than escalate, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] stepped forward and delivered a composed, prepared speech to calm the crowd:

- He acknowledged their grievances were legitimate
- He promised to personally look into the **disappearances** of those who had spoken out
- He told them to direct complaints to him and promised to find a proper channel for them

The speech worked. The crowd dispersed peacefully (successful Persuade roll).

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] followed up with **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Captain Irlav]]** who confirmed:
- **Arrests had been made**, but none of the arrested were in the dungeon
- The people had simply **vanished**—no official record of what happened to them
- The captain had no explanation

---

### Midmorning — Warehouse Inspection (Durov)

At **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Warehouse_Seven\|Warehouse 7]]** in the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Warehouse_District\|warehouse district]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] carried out his inspection of a caravan's cargo.

**The Suspicious Crates:**

Among the standard goods, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] found a set of crates sealed with wax and **carved sigils**. They were exceptionally heavy. When he opened one, the contents were unmistakably unusual:

- **[[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Coin_Molds\|Coin molds]]**—matching the shape and likely the source of the brimstone-smelling gold that [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varren]] had been distributing in the market
- **Strange materials**: gemstones, chalk
- **Black candles**
- **Bone, sinew, and pig hearts** (animal parts)—listed vaguely in the paperwork as "animal parts" under provisions, not accurately declared

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] refused to let the crates pass inspection without supervisor approval. He sent for a supervisor, who never returned.

The **caravan master** refused to accept payment in the brimstone-smelling gold that was offered. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], to prevent a confrontation and keep the crates held, paid **500 gold of his own conventional coin** to the caravan master from his personal funds.

During the standoff, one of the **warehouse workers was shoved to the ground by a guard** and struck his head. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] gave the man a **healing potion**. In gratitude, the worker mentioned that the brimstone-scented gold *"smells like the Daedric anchors from the war"*—referencing the **Mooring Anchors** dropped during the Planemeld by [[Info/Lore/Molag_Bal\|Molag Bal]]'s forces, suggesting the man had lived through or heard stories of that conflict roughly 18 years prior.

Later, another warehouse worker—one who seemed oddly well-presented and out of place—turned out to be a contact. He introduced himself as having *"friends in low places"*, suggesting ties to the underground network that had been making inquiries around the warehouses.

### Late Morning — Meeting with Vaelin Marr

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**, and **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** headed to the **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Silverroad_Guildhouse\|Guild of the Silverroad guildhouse]]** to meet with **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin_Marr]]**.

**Arrival:**
- A guard at the entrance gave **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** a pointed look and warned against "funny business"—a reminder that Khajiit were not entirely welcome in Guild spaces
- They were escorted to Vaelin's private office, with **no guards posted inside**

**The Meeting:**

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] played the role of an eager, somewhat naive young merchant from the Lunclaire family looking to re-establish a trade presence after years away from the family business. The performance was intentional—playing to Vaelin's apparent interest in the Lunclaire name.

Vaelin offered a **trade partnership**:
- A reduced **8% guild rate** (down from the standard 15%), justified by the Lunclaire family's former standing
- Priority placement on **three trade routes**: [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] → Morrowind, the rest of the Rift, and Rift → Falkreath → Bruma/Cyrodiil
- A **permanent warehouse** in her name in southeast Skyrim
- Faster passage through guild checkpoints, fewer inspections, and armed escorts
- In exchange: periodic transport of Guild cargo, and reporting on the plans and movements of competing merchants

Vaelin framed the Guild as an organization that gave merchants a *"voice in politics"*, with connections in royal courts and mercenary guilds across the region.

**The Interruption:**

Midway through the meeting, a guard entered and whispered something to Vaelin, who stepped out briefly—almost certainly a report about the warehouse incident [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] had caused. This gave the party a critical window.

**The Office Search:**

While Vaelin was away, the party moved quickly and systematically:

- **Map on the wall**: Two **red circles marked in the warehouse district** (one likely [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Warehouse_Seven\|Warehouse 7]]) and a **third mark northeast of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]**, outside the city walls
- **Open ledger** on the desk, reviewed by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] (who memorised all contents):
  - *"Transfer warehouse 4 to [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Warehouse_Seven\|warehouse 7]] — escorted shipment, night delivery"*
  - *"Special consignment — temple allocation"* — notable because **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] has no temple**
  - *"Coin distribution — market circulation"*
  - All entries were dated within the last few weeks
- **Locked cabinet** (picked by [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]): A slightly burnt letter reading — *"jarl remains cooperative though his condition requires continued supervision"*
- **Lock box** (also picked by [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], under significant pressure before Vaelin returned):
  - Silver Road trade contracts
  - **Bribe payments to officials in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**, dated within the last two weeks
  - Letter fragments referencing **"the Coming Correction"**

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] memorised all documents. Everything was replaced exactly as found before Vaelin returned.

**The Pendant:**

After the business was concluded, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** asked about the pendant Vaelin wore—the same **Ouroboros pendant** [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] had spotted in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 022\|Session 022]]. He answered openly:

- It was given to **survivors at the White Gold Tower** during the **Planemeld**, approximately 18–20 years ago
- It represents the **three alliances** that held the line: one side bears the symbol of the **[[Info/Organizations/Military/Ebonheart_Pact\|Ebonheart Pact]]**, another the **[[Info/Organizations/Military/Aldmeri_Dominion\|Aldmeri Dominion]]** (a griffon), and the third the **[[Info/Organizations/Military/Daggerfall_Covenant\|Daggerfall Covenant]]** (a lion)
- He knew of **one other person** who wore the same pendant—someone in **Bruma**

This is the Ouroboros pendant—Vaelin's explanation gives it a plausible surface identity as a Planemeld survivor's keepsake, which may be how he wears it openly without raising suspicion.

The meeting concluded amicably. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] narrowly passed a Deceive roll when Vaelin noticed she hadn't seemed to enjoy the wine—but he did not press the point. The party was escorted out.

### Midday — Regrouping at the Inn

The group reunited at **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost_Gate_Hall]]** around midday. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] had returned from the warehouse district.

**Intelligence Sharing:**

- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** reported: the crowd speech and disappearances, the suspicious sealed crates at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Warehouse_Seven\|Warehouse 7]] (coin molds, Daedric materials, animal parts), the brimstone gold smell, and the contact with "friends in low places" in the warehouse district
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** reported: the map with two warehouse circles and the northeast marker, the ledger entries referencing warehouse transfers and temple allocations with no temple in the city, the bribes to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] officials, the "Jarl" letter, and the "Coming Correction" references

**Plans Discussed:**

- Investigate the **northeast marker** on Vaelin's map—the location outside the city walls
- Potentially use **frenzy bombs** to engineer a conflict between the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild]] and the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]], framing the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] for an attack on the warehouse to cover the party's tracks
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** revealed she had previously misdirected the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]], telling them that [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]] held the **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Old_Blackfoot_Guild_House\|Blackfoot Guild House]] lockbox** they were seeking—effectively pointing them at the Guild as enemies

**The [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] Message:**

As the party debriefed, a **crow** arrived at the inn window—the same bird that had previously guided them from the sewers. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** traded the crow a piece of apple for the note it carried.



A message from **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]** via the **[[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]]**. The note was brief, a riddle of sorts, with an addendum written in careful script: *"Seek the knowledge of dust and dried ink."* Its precise contents and instructions would form part of the party's next steps.

## Important Revelations

- **[[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Coin_Molds\|Coin molds]] at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Warehouse_Seven\|Warehouse 7]]**: Active production of brimstone gold is confirmed—this is not just distribution of existing Daedric currency, but minting of new coin within [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] itself
- **"The Coming Correction"**: A phrase found in letter fragments in Vaelin's lockbox. Its precise meaning is unknown but the language suggests a planned large-scale event, not merely ongoing operations
- **The Jarl is being "supervised"**: The burnt letter in Vaelin's cabinet states the Jarl *"remains cooperative though his condition requires continued supervision"*—confirming the Jarl is not acting freely. Something is wrong with him and the Guild knows it.
- **Temple allocation with no temple**: Goods or funds are being routed to a "temple" that does not officially exist in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]—likely a cover for a cult facility
- **Bribe payments to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] officials**: The [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]]'s influence reaches beyond [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] into [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]'s civic structure, dated within the last two weeks
- **Vaelin's Planemeld pendant**: He was present at the White Gold Tower during the Planemeld, approximately 2E 582—meaning he is at minimum 18–20 years older than he may appear. His long history may explain his deep involvement in the current conspiracy
- **Northeast marker**: An unknown location outside [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] is marked on Vaelin's private map alongside the two warehouse circles—likely a significant site
