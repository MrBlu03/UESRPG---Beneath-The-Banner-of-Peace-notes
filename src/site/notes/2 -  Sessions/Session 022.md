# Session 022 - The Guild's Gambit

## Summary

The party executed a daring infiltration of the **Jarl's Hall** during a prestigious trade event hosted by the **[[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]]**. While **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** and **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** attended as invited guests to gather intelligence and make trade connections, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** infiltrated through the sewers to search the Jarl's private quarters for evidence of his suspicious activities. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** worked undercover as a guard for **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]]**, maintaining his cover while protecting the party's interests.

During the event, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** spotted **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]] wearing an Ouroboros pendant**—confirming he is a member of the **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]**. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** successfully reconnected with her family's old trading partners, securing lucrative trade deals with silk baron **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Renald_Vero\|Renald Vero]]** and lumber baron **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olvfrost\|Olvfrost]]**. Meanwhile, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** discovered a [[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Bloody Fur Cloak\|bloody fur cloak]] and a **[[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Dark Runic Stone\|dark runic stone]] radiating heat** hidden in the Jarl's quarters.

The Jarl delivered a speech with a strange magical effect that was neutralized by spell absorption tinctures the party had taken. Shortly after, **two guards were found murdered outside**—victims of the **[[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]]**, who have infiltrated the city. The session ended with the party regrouping at the inn to discuss their findings and planning their next moves, including infiltrating the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]] further and investigating the mysterious stone.

### Kirya's Sewer Infiltration

**Entering the Sewers:**
- Entered [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad_Sewers\|Kjalgrad Sewers]] near the bridge to Jarl's Hall
- Rolled lucky on Observe—found a corpse with arm pinned to wall by a dagger
- The dagger pointed to a sign reading "old sewers"
- Found a pouch with a rusted key on the corpse

**Navigation:**
- Navigated through sewers (marked paths with mud), initially went wrong way into ancient architecture
- Backtracked and picked a locked gate (barely succeeded)
- Found sign confirming "old sewers" direction
- After 15-20 minutes of navigating, reached a door leading to a large wine cellar beneath the Jarl's Hall

**The Wine Cellar:**
- Picked another lock to enter a massive wine cellar
- Found a stairwell leading up
- Took her chameleon tincture before proceeding

**Upper Floor Infiltration:**
- Stealthed upstairs past the sound of the event below
- Saw the **Jarl** being escorted by 4 guards to a balcony—well-dressed man in furs
- Took her spell absorption tincture as the Jarl prepared to speak
- Found a single locked door at end of hall (the Jarl's quarters)
- Picked the lock and entered a large bedroom

**Discoveries in the Jarl's Room:**
- Blood trail from a closet to a window
- In the closet: an old, [[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Bloody Fur Cloak\|bloody, stinking fur cloak]] (smelled of soot and wet dirty fur)
- A **lockbox** on an end table containing a **[[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Dark Runic Stone\|dark stone with runes etched on it]]** that radiated heat
- She wrapped the stone in cloth (didn't touch it directly), put it in her pouch, relocked the lockbox
- On the way out, stole some wine from the cellar (fruity and spicy varieties)
- Exited through sewers, slightly tipsy from sampling wine

### The Jarl's Speech and Aftermath

**The Speech:**
- The Jarl stood on the balcony—something seemed "off" about him, shadows were weird and shifty
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] tried to discern if it was magical but rolled unlucky
- Speech content: talked about [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] being built by hands/merchants/families, trade is shared strength, warned against division, praised the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]], asked for cooperation and investment

**Magical Effect:**
- During the speech: everyone felt a brief **warmth wash over them** followed by a **pressure change in the air**
- Then it dissipated (the spell absorption tinctures protected the party)
- After the speech, the party observed but noticed no obvious behavioral changes in the crowd

**The Murders:**
- Guards were suddenly called to the front door—commotion
- **Two guards found dead outside** the Jarl's Hall—the same ones from [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s morning shift
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] went to check, saw bodies being examined by guards and a healer
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Irlav\|Irlav]] told [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] privately: **[[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] is in the city**
  - They need to get Vaelin out, possibly leaving today or tomorrow
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s payment will come by courier
  - Told [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] to guard the front/gates
- Guards ushered all guests to leave—event cut short due to the murders
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] helped move bodies inside for examination
