# Session 017

## Summary
The party departed [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] at dawn to hunt **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Nohrak]], the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]]**, a Daedric aspect of [[Info/Lore/Hircine\|Hircine]]. After discovering corrupted totems bearing their own faces, they reached a transformed shrine where [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] performed the summoning ritual.

The battle progressed through three phases: **Seven wolves** attacked first—swiftly eliminated by coordinated party efforts. Then the **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]] manifested**: an 8-9 meter tall horror of stitched hides and antlers. He grievously wounded [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]] but her fire-varnished counterattack permanently burned him. When he teleported to attack [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] intercepted, taking 10 damage but enabling devastating counterstrikes that triggered the 50% threshold—the hides burned away, revealing skeletal horror beneath.

Trapped by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s Unrelenting talent and slowed by [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s arrows, the creature fought desperately. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] parried his whip attack, severed his right arm (17 damage), and nearly severed his left. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] delivered the **killing blow**—splitting her own embedded arrow to shatter his kneecap. His stag skull split, revealing a scarred human face that whispered *"End it."* [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] drove her silver blade through his heart as he whispered *"Hunter..."* and burned to ash.

[[Info/Lore/Kyne\|Kyne]]'s peace returned to the forest. [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] erupted in celebration. Jarl [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Gorm]] paid **500 gold each** and granted permanent use of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Nilans_House\|Nilan's house]]. The evening ended with aurora-lit festivities, character bonding, and a mysterious leaf falling at their door.

## **The Battle of the Ragged Woods**

### **Phase One: The Summoned Wolves (7 Wolves)**

As [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]'s voice rose in ancient power, speaking words in a language not entirely his own, frost crept up his arms from the brazier. The runes circling the brazier glowed faintly green and white. He spoke: *"Veit ek þik, veiðimaðr villr. Veit ek þik, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Nohrak]], flǫkna herra [[Info/Lore/Kyne\|Kyne]]!"*

Those last words left his mouth: *"I call the last hunter who turned from the chase. I call the one who wears the skin of the slain. I call the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]] to answer for his madness."*

The brazier shattered—a sound like steel drawn through bone, hurling ash and fire skyward. Far off in the treeline, a howl echoed low and guttural. Then another answered. And another.

Seven wolves with glowing faintly green eyes materialized from the darkness—more than the expected three.

**Combat Summary:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: One-shot two wolves with devastating sneak attacks (10 damage each with splitting arrows)
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]]**: Hit for 10 damage after missing first shot
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]**: Killed a wolf before it reached [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Missed chain lightning, repositioned defensively
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Obliterated a wolf with fire-varnished sword (14 damage total: 8+2 fire+4 overkill)
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Runa\|Runa]]**: Wounded but fighting
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Frozen first round (failed willpower check), then recovered and killed wolf with arrow through its mouth (16 damage total)
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Ingrin\|Ingrin]]**: Decapitated attacking wolf with longsword, saving [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Runa\|Runa]]

All seven wolves fell within minutes. Their corpses remained for later salvaging.

### **Phase Two: Nohrak, The Ragged Lord Appears**

The trees groaned in agony. The hides around the clearing ripped loose from their branches, spinning like wings. The party knew the true battle was about to begin.

**Boss Title Appeared:** *"[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|NOHRAK]] - THE [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|RAGGED LORD]]"* (Dark Souls style)

From the tree line emerged a nightmare: **8-9 meters tall**, constructed of stitched hides and antlers. Faces stretched and twisted across his chest, eyes rolling beneath translucent flesh. Steam rose from every seam. Black antlers cracked in the heat. He spoke with dozens of overlapping voices.

**First Strike:**
- Teleported behind [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]], mimicking her stance before slashing with 30-40cm claws
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]] failed to evade: **12 damage** + **Grievous Wound** to chest (lost 1 AP, near death)
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]]'s attack of opportunity with fire-varnished blade: Permanent burning wound, creature howled in pain

**Party Response:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Slow arrow hit (5 damage), reduced his speed significantly
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]]**: Critically wounded, bleeding out
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Stabilized [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]]'s bleeding with survival check on her turn, then fired arrow into [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]]'s kneecap (embedding it for later)
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]]**: After stabilization, drank healing potion, retreated to altar (wounded but stable)
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]**: Killed additional wolf (Wilfred)
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** & **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**: Held actions defensively

### **Phase Three: The Turning Point**

The [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]] teleported aggressively toward [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] activated held action**, swapping positions and blocking with his shield. The attack penetrated his defense: **10 damage to left arm**.

**Counter-Attack:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** struck back twice:
  - First hit: Left leg, 13+ damage with fire
  - Second hit: Left leg again, **17 total damage**
  - **THRESHOLD CROSSED: 50% HP**

The creature collapsed to one knee, howling. Fire consumed him from every wound—leg, chest, arrows. He screamed with voices of man and beast, succumbing to flames.

**Transformation:** The hides burst away. What remained was skeletal horror—sinew over bone, frost steaming from joints, stag skull fused to flesh with glowing orange antlers. Every breath was like a furnace.

He rose and **screamed at [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**.

### **Final Phase: The Kill**

Trapped by **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s Unrelenting talent** (cannot disengage), slowed by arrows, surrounded, and on 1 AP.

**The [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]] formed hides into a whip and struck at [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] parried successfully** (used Lucky die)
- Gained advantage: Overextend (opponent -10 next test)

**Final Assault:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Fire arrow, 9 damage
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]]**: Two shots, 6 damage
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]**: Fireball, 9 damage  
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Two devastating strikes:
  - Hit right arm: **17 damage** (6+5 fire+4 slashing+2 silver) - **ARM SEVERED**
  - Hit left arm: Major sinew damage (nearly severed)
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Runa\|Runa]]**: Two shots narrowly missed (rolled 77, 81 vs 70 target)
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: **KILLING BLOW** - Split her own previous arrow in his kneecap, shattering it (11 damage)

The [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]]'s leg fell away, shin crumbling. He collapsed. The stag skull split, revealing a scarred human face with hollow eyes. He whispered: *"Hunter..."*

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] stepped forward**, driving her silver-varnished blade upward under his ribs into his heart. With his claws, he gripped the blade and pulled himself deeper onto it, whispering one final time: *"Hunter..."*

His body burned to ash from the silver fire.

### **The Aftermath**

The forest **exhaled**.

- Trees no longer groaned
- Oppressive weight lifted
- Clouds parted—revealing sunny afternoon sky (first time since arrival)
- Air smelled of sap, pine, and ash. No more blood.
- A hawk cried—**no wolf answered** for the first time
- Totems vanished from trees
- Old, rotten hides remained but no supernatural presence
- Birdsong returned
- Snow crunched normally again
- Winds moved freely through branches

**[[Info/Lore/Kyne\|Kyne]]'s peace returned to the woods.**

### **Loot & Salvage**

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** mechanically skinned and gutted the wolves while processing the adrenaline. The party harvested from the fallen wolves:
- **8 Wolf Pelts** (one failed to recover)
- **24 days of wolf rations** (7 rations per party member, distributed)
- **9 Wolf Hearts** (alchemy ingredients: restore fatigue, cure disease)

The [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]] left only ash—no claws, no physical remains. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] found one item where he fell: **A small antler charm** carved as a running stag, wrapped with hide and sinew. A hunter's token, perhaps from before the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]] went mad. He gave it to [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], saying: *"Keep it to remember that even the gods can lose their way and still be found."*

### **Return to Falderstad**

The journey back was peaceful—sunlight streaming through trees, no howls, only the sounds of a living forest.

By the time they saw the city's watchtowers, the sun was setting, amber and red spilling across the river like melted gold.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] attempted to whistle an alert, but the **hunter's bell was already tolling**—the city had seen the sun return.

Folk emerged from homes and stalls, following the party through the streets in a growing wave. At the market steps stood young [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]] clutching a wooden stag carving, eyes wide: *"You did it, didn't you? I felt it. The dogs stopped howling!"*

**Jarl [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Gorm]] of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]** (recovered from rock joint) approached with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga]] Cairn-Shield, his huscarl:

- Thanked them for bringing [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Runa\|Runa]] back alive

**Healer** treated [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]]'s grievous wound and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Runa\|Runa]]'s wolf bite (rock joint confirmed but treatable).

Evening turned to night. The square filled with torches, banners, laughter. Meat roasted on open flames, mead flowed freely. Folk returned to song.

Above, the aurora returned—streaks of gold and violet swirling like silk.

**Key Moments:**
- Children chased through snow wearing painted wooden masks (hawks, stags, foxes)
- A new totem rose at the square's center—a hawk carved from a rune-tree
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] lit incense beneath it as offering of gratitude (not fear)

**[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]]** appeared to congratulate the party, his presence cryptic as always, before departing toward the old tannery.

**[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga]]** thanked [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] for bringing her sister [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]] back alive.
