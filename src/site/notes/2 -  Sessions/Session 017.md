# Session 017

## Summary
The party departed [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] at dawn to hunt **Anorak, the Ragged Lord**, a Daedric aspect of [[Hircine\|Hircine]]. After discovering corrupted totems bearing their own faces, they reached a transformed shrine where [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] performed the summoning ritual.

The battle progressed through three phases: **Seven wolves** attacked first—swiftly eliminated by coordinated party efforts. Then the **Ragged Lord manifested**: an 8-9 meter tall horror of stitched hides and antlers. He grievously wounded [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]] but her fire-varnished counterattack permanently burned him. When he teleported to attack [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] intercepted, taking 10 damage but enabling devastating counterstrikes that triggered the 50% threshold—the hides burned away, revealing skeletal horror beneath.

Trapped by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]'s Unrelenting talent and slowed by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s arrows, the creature fought desperately. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] parried his whip attack, severed his right arm (17 damage), and nearly severed his left. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] delivered the **killing blow**—splitting her own embedded arrow to shatter his kneecap. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] drove her silver blade through his heart as he whispered *"Hunter..."* and burned to ash.

[[Kyne\|Kyne]]'s peace returned to the forest. [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] erupted in celebration. Jarl [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Jorrun\|Jorrun]] paid **500 gold each** and granted permanent use of Dylin's house. The evening ended with aurora-lit festivities, character bonding, and a mysterious leaf falling at their door.

## **The Battle of the Ragged Woods**

As Torik's voice rose in ancient power, speaking words in a language not entirely his own, frost crept up his arms from the brazier. The runes circling the brazier glowed faintly green and white. He spoke: *"Veidt exec v mother Villa Veidt exec no rock fluke no Kane..."*

The brazier burst, throwing ash and fire skyward. The trees groaned in agony. The hides ripped loose from branches, spinning like wings.

### **Phase One: The Summoned Wolves (7 Wolves)**

Before the Ragged Lord manifested, seven wolves with glowing green eyes materialized—more than the expected three.

**Combat Summary:**
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: One-shot two wolves with devastating sneak attacks (10 damage each with splitting arrows)
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]]**: Hit for 10 damage after missing first shot
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]**: Killed a wolf before it reached [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Missed chain lightning, repositioned defensively
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]**: Obliterated a wolf with fire-varnished sword (14 damage total: 8+2 fire+4 overkill)
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]]**: Wounded but fighting
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Frozen first round (failed willpower check), then recovered and killed wolf with arrow through its mouth (16 damage total), provided medical aid to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]]
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Ingrin\|Ingrin]]**: Decapitated attacking wolf with longsword, saving [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]]

All seven wolves fell within minutes. Their corpses dissolved into ash.

### **Phase Two: Anorak, The Ragged Lord Appears**

**Boss Title Appeared:** *"ANORAK - THE RAGGED LORD"* (Dark Souls style)

From the tree line emerged a nightmare: **8-9 meters tall**, constructed of stitched hides and antlers. Faces stretched and twisted across his chest, eyes rolling beneath translucent flesh. Steam rose from every seam. Black antlers cracked in the heat. He spoke with dozens of overlapping voices.

**First Strike:**
- Teleported behind [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]], mimicking her stance before slashing with 30-40cm claws
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]] failed to evade: **12 damage** + **Grievous Wound** to chest (lost 1 AP, near death)
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]]'s attack of opportunity with fire-varnished blade: Permanent burning wound, creature howled in pain

**Party Response:**
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Slow arrow hit (5 damage), reduced his speed significantly
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]]**: Drank healing potion, retreated to altar (wounded but stable)
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Stabilized [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]]'s bleeding with survival check
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]**: Killed additional wolf (Wilfred)
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** & **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]**: Held actions defensively

### **Phase Three: The Turning Point**

The Ragged Lord teleported aggressively toward [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]. **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] activated held action**, swapping positions and blocking with his shield. The attack penetrated his defense: **10 damage to left arm**.

**Counter-Attack:**
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** struck back twice:
  - First hit: Left leg, 13+ damage with fire
  - Second hit: Left leg again, **17 total damage**
  - **THRESHOLD CROSSED: 50% HP**

The creature collapsed to one knee, howling. Fire consumed him from every wound—leg, chest, arrows. He screamed with voices of man and beast, succumbing to flames.

**Transformation:** The hides burst away. What remained was skeletal horror—sinew over bone, frost steaming from joints, stag skull fused to flesh with glowing orange antlers. Every breath was like a furnace.

He rose and **screamed at [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**.

### **Final Phase: The Kill**

Trapped by **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s Unrelenting talent** (cannot disengage), slowed by arrows, surrounded, and on 1 AP.

**The Ragged Lord formed hides into a whip and struck at [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]:**
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] parried successfully** (used Lucky die)
- Gained advantage: Overextend (opponent -10 next test)

**Final Assault:**
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Fire arrow, 9 damage
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]]**: Two shots, 6 damage
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]**: Fireball, 9 damage  
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Two devastating strikes:
  - Hit right arm: **17 damage** (6+5 fire+4 slashing+2 silver) - **ARM SEVERED**
  - Hit left arm: Major sinew damage (nearly severed)
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]]**: Two shots narrowly missed (rolled 77, 81 vs 70 target)
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: **KILLING BLOW** - Split her own previous arrow in his kneecap, shattering it (11 damage)

The Ragged Lord's leg fell away, shin crumbling. He collapsed. The stag skull split, revealing a scarred human face with hollow eyes. He whispered: *"Hunter..."*

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] stepped forward**, driving her silver-varnished blade upward under his ribs into his heart. With his claws, he gripped the blade and pulled himself deeper onto it, whispering one final time: *"Hunter..."*

His body burned to ash from the silver fire.

### **The Aftermath**

The forest **exhaled**.

- Trees no longer groaned
- Oppressive weight lifted
- Clouds parted—revealing sunny afternoon sky (first time since arrival)
- Air smelled of sap, pine, and ash. No more blood.
- A hawk cried—**no wolf answered** for the first time
- Totems vanished from trees
- Old, rotten hides remained but no supernatural presence
- Birdsong returned
- Snow crunched normally again
- Winds moved freely through branches

**Kyne's peace returned to the woods.**

### **Loot & Salvage**

The party harvested from the fallen wolves:
- **8 Wolf Pelts** (one failed to recover)
- **24 days of wolf rations** (7 rations per party member, distributed)
- **9 Wolf Hearts** (alchemy ingredients: restore fatigue, cure disease)

The Ragged Lord left only ash—no claws, no physical remains. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] found one item where he fell: **A small antler charm** carved as a running stag, wrapped with hide and sinew. A hunter's token, perhaps from before the Ragged Lord went mad. He gave it to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]], saying: *"Keep it to remember that even the gods can lose their way and still be found."*

### **Return to Falderstad**

The journey back was peaceful—sunlight streaming through trees, no howls, only the sounds of a living forest.

By the time they saw the city's watchtowers, the sun was setting, amber and red spilling across the river like melted gold.

[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] attempted to whistle an alert, but the **hunter's bell was already tolling**—the city had seen the sun return.

Folk emerged from homes and stalls, following the party through the streets in a growing wave. At the market steps stood young [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]] clutching a wooden stag carving, eyes wide: *"You did it, didn't you? I felt it. The dogs stopped howling!"*

**[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Jorrun\|Jorrun]] of [[Clan Valor\|Clan Valor]]** (the Jarl, recovered from rock joint) approached with [[Helga Kieran-Shield\|Helga Kieran-Shield]]:

**Healer** treated [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]]'s grievous wound and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]]'s wolf bite (rock joint confirmed but treatable).

Evening turned to night. The square filled with torches, banners, laughter. Meat roasted on open flames, mead flowed freely. Folk returned to song.

Above, the aurora returned—streaks of gold and violet swirling like silk.

**Key Moments:**
- Children chased through snow wearing painted wooden masks (hawks, stags, foxes)
- A new totem rose at the square's center—a hawk carved from a rune-tree
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] lit incense beneath it as offering of gratitude (not fear)

**[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]]** appeared to congratulate the party, his presence cryptic as always, before departing toward the old tannery.

**[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]]** thanked [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]], [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]], and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] for bringing her sister [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] back alive.
