# Session 010a - Downtime

**Session Notes - Periwyn & Adda Conversation**

**Key Revelations:**

• Adda had prophetic dreams about Periwyn long before they met 
• She saw visions of Periwyn's childhood - a young girl desperately trying to raise the dead through necromancy 
• Witnessed Periwyn's relationship with a knight figure who protected her, then was killed by Periwyn herself ("hammer in hand, breaking the wall yourself... because love demanded it") 
• Adda sees Periwyn's future as violent and filled with pursuit of shadows

**Periwyn's Response:**

• Shocked and disturbed that Adda knows her deepest secrets 
• Angry about the invasion of privacy but accepts Adda didn't choose to see these visions 
• Confirms she made a promise to someone (her brother) that she wouldn't give up 
• Has a journal containing important personal information

**Agreement Reached:**

• Adda promises to keep Periwyn's necromantic past secret 
• Will not reveal Periwyn's history to her companions 
• Periwyn will decide when/if to share these secrets herself
