# Session 010a - Downtime

**Session Notes - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] & [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] Conversation**

**Key Revelations:**

• [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] had prophetic dreams about [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] long before they met 
• She saw visions of [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s childhood - a young girl desperately trying to raise the dead through necromancy 
• Witnessed [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s relationship with a knight figure who protected her, then was killed by [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] herself ("hammer in hand, breaking the wall yourself... because love demanded it") 
• [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] sees [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s future as violent and filled with pursuit of shadows

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s Response:**

• Shocked and disturbed that [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] knows her deepest secrets 
• Angry about the invasion of privacy but accepts [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] didn't choose to see these visions 
• Confirms she made a promise to someone (her brother) that she wouldn't give up 
• Has a journal containing important personal information

**Agreement Reached:**

• [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] promises to keep [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s necromantic past secret 
• Will not reveal [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s history to her [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|companions]] 
• [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] will decide when/if to share these secrets herself
