# Session 029 — The Long Night

## Summary

- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] & [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** share a rare, vulnerable late-night heart-to-heart 
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** wakes in the small hours and explores the Dwemer ruin alone. He inadvertently activates an elevator and descends into **Blackreach**, where he witnesses a living **dragon** silhouetted against the cavern's artificial sun, before carefully mapping his way back out.
- The following morning, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and Preventers investigate an **abandoned Camona Tongue camp** southeast of the ruin. They find evidence of a violent raid (blood, bodkin arrows, shattered bomb vials, a note reading *"Attacked. New camp. East."*) and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] quietly deduces [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] was likely involved.
- Back at the ruin, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] conduct a brutal interrogation of the **captured Daedra** (the fake Steward), extracting extensive intelligence through intimidation, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s "accidentally" slippery throwing daggers, and — ultimately — a **cleverly worded Daedric contract**.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] negotiates with the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]: **800 gold** for the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark Brotherhood]] to kill some body

---

## Detailed Recap

### Part 1 — The Heart-to-Heart (Night)

The session opened with [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] continuing a late-night walk from the previous session, ending up by a **small frozen pond** under a yellow-green aurora at around 1am.

**What they talked about:**

- **Anger vs. exhaustion.** [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] asked how [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] keeps finding the energy to be angry. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] explained that her rage is inseparable from a **promise she made to herself and her brother** — that as long as she can keep that promise, she can keep living. She admitted it isn't healthy and explicitly told [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] not to be like her.

- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s brother.** He was a magic prodigy — far more gifted than [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], who was always more of a books person. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s journal contains drawings of every member of her family, including multiple portraits of her brother at different ages that eventually shift from memory to *imagined* portraits of how he would have looked had he survived. She keeps a **dried periwinkle flower pressed between the pages** — her mother used to love picking them. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] draws the flower both as a memorial and as a reflex.

- **Zula** [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] revealed for the first time the name of **the girl she left behind** when she escaped slavery, would now be around **16** — she was 9 when [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] fled, seven years ago. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] is haunted by the fear that they haven't forgiven her for leaving. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] pushed back gently, saying someone like [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] wouldn't be easily forgotten.

- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s memory of her family.** Despite having a near-perfect memory, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] admitted she **struggles to recall her parents' faces clearly** — a sign of trauma-repressed memory

- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] (Dunmer).** The real thread running through the conversation was how each of them copes with being faced with the things they hate most. For [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] it's vampires; for [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] it's Dunmer. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] noted, uncomfortably, that [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] has only helped them and hasn't wronged her personally — she's struggling to hold onto fear and hatred of someone who hasn't actually been monstrous to her. She confessed she has **never once looked [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] in the eyes**; every time she looks at him, she panics.

- **Rebuilding the family legacy.** [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] admitted part of her crusade is also about honouring what her family built — a trade network that fed and clothed people across the world. She'd like to rebuild it once her "chapter" is done, but she doesn't know if she'll want to see her old friend (a past travel companion she showed [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] a portrait of in her journal — a human woman, not drawn in [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s art style, who eventually left because of [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s relentless crusade and nightmares).

**The scene ended** with [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] attempting (and catastrophically failing) to draw a periwinkle in the snow — [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] had to physically guide her hand to produce something resembling a flower. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] eventually fell asleep leaning on [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s shoulder at the pond's edge, at which point [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] gently woke her after about 20 minutes and suggested they head back inside to sleep before the Daedra woke up.

### Part 2 — Durov in Blackreach (Pre-dawn)

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] woke in the early hours and began wandering the Dwemer ruin's corridors with pen and paper, tracing a rough schematic. After mapping the sleeping quarters area, he stumbled onto a door he hadn't opened before — an elevator shaft — and accidentally triggered it, descending into **Blackreach**.

There, silhouetted against the enormous artificial sun of the vast underground cavern, he saw the unmistakeable shape of a **dragon** — wings spread, partially eclipsing the glowing orb as it circled overhead. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] stood in silence and watched until it disappeared from view. He then carefully traced his way back up, bluffed past a confused guard ("I must not have had enough sleep"), and emerged just past sunrise. He groomed Rhymefang and waited for the others.


### Part 3 — Investigating the Abandoned Camp (Morning)

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] accompanied Preventers on a ~45-minute trek southeast to a derelict camp abandoned approximately **two to three weeks** ago. Evidence gathered:

- **Multiple bodkin/splitting arrows** embedded in trees — not target practice. One tree had dried human blood consistent with someone having been shot against it.
- **Shattered glass** from alchemical bomb vials (gas-dispersal type — likely frenzy bombs based on the lack of scorch marks).
- A **half-buried tent** containing a note: *"Attacked. New camp. East."*
- A **broken-down cart** — too damaged to move, left behind.
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Proventus\|Proventus]] found **broken branches at chest-height** along the treeline, leading northeast.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] concluded with a quiet private smile that a familiar party member had likely been here. He pocketed one bodkin arrow. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] told Preventers to write everything down.

### Part 4 — The Daedra Interrogation (Morning)

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] were roused by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] with the news that the Daedra was waking. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] briefed them outside the door:

- A **ring** on his person suppresses the Daedra's magic while he remains nearby (outside the room).
- Three weapons are available on a table: a **dueling sword** (causes "existential pain" to Daedra, 1d6+5), an **assassin's dagger** (kills Daedra permanently — do not use yet), and **three enchanted throwing daggers** (painful but non-lethal to Daedra; passed through its armour).
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] stepped outside to play solitaire.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] slapped the Daedra awake with a full haymaker. The interrogation began. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] asked questions; [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] hovered close with daggers. Key intelligence extracted:

### Part 5 — The Daedric Contract

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] stepped out to speak privately with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] about commissioning a hit. She then returned with a proposal for the Daedra:

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s offer:**
- Let it leave the ruin unharmed (by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]).
- Ensure the Komodo Tongue agents who failed it are dead.
- Full truthful answers to all remaining questions.

A **Daedric contract materialised on parchment**. Careful negotiation over wording ensued:

- "No harm from allies" was narrowed to **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] by name** — [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]/[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] are not covered.
- "While leaving the ruin" limits the protection to the premises only — outside, all bets are off.
- The contract does **not** specify Delos Nazi must be alive when the Daedra arrives. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] has separately paid the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark Brotherhood]] 800 gold to get there first.

The contract was signed in blood (using daggers that had already been inside the Daedra — [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s suggestion) and sealed in blue flame. The Daedra's soul is also forfeit if it violates its terms.

**Outcome of the deal:** [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] confirmed this was only the second time he had seen a mortal outsmart a Daedra by exploiting its arrogance. The Daedra will be held in the ruin for roughly **three months** (time for the Brotherhood to reach Tier) before being released per the contract.

Separately, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] confirmed: the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark Brotherhood]] can take regular assassination contracts outside of a formal Black Sacrament. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] paid 800 gold. She is now nearly broke.
