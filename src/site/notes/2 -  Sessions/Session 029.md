# Session 029 — The Long Night

## Summary

- **Onasha & Periwyn** share a rare, vulnerable late-night heart-to-heart 
- **Durov** wakes in the small hours and explores the Dwemer ruin alone. He inadvertently activates an elevator and descends into **Blackreach**, where he witnesses a living **dragon** silhouetted against the cavern's artificial sun, before carefully mapping his way back out.
- The following morning, Durov and Preventers investigate an **abandoned Camona Tongue camp** southeast of the ruin. They find evidence of a violent raid (blood, bodkin arrows, shattered bomb vials, a note reading *"Attacked. New camp. East."*) and Durov quietly deduces Onasha was likely involved.
- Back at the ruin, Onasha and Periwyn conduct a brutal interrogation of the **captured Daedra** (the fake Steward), extracting extensive intelligence through intimidation, Periwyn's "accidentally" slippery throwing daggers, and — ultimately — a **cleverly worded Daedric contract**.
- Onasha negotiates with the Milos: **800 gold** for the Dark Brotherhood to kill some body

## Detailed Recap

### Part 1 — The Heart-to-Heart (Night)

The session opened with Onasha and Periwyn continuing a late-night walk from the previous session, ending up by a **small frozen pond** under a yellow-green aurora at around 1am.

**What they talked about:**

- **Anger vs. exhaustion.** Onasha asked how Periwyn keeps finding the energy to be angry. Periwyn explained that her rage is inseparable from a **promise she made to herself and her brother** — that as long as she can keep that promise, she can keep living. She admitted it isn't healthy and explicitly told Onasha not to be like her.

- **Periwyn's brother.** He was a magic prodigy — far more gifted than Periwyn, who was always more of a books person. Periwyn's journal contains drawings of every member of her family, including multiple portraits of her brother at different ages that eventually shift from memory to *imagined* portraits of how he would have looked had he survived. She keeps a **dried periwinkle flower pressed between the pages** — her mother used to love picking them. Periwyn draws the flower both as a memorial and as a reflex.

- **Zula** Onasha revealed for the first time the name of **the girl she left behind** when she escaped slavery, would now be around **16** — she was 9 when Onasha fled, seven years ago. Onasha is haunted by the fear that they haven't forgiven her for leaving. Periwyn pushed back gently, saying someone like Onasha wouldn't be easily forgotten.

- **Onasha's memory of her family.** Despite having a near-perfect memory, Onasha admitted she **struggles to recall her parents' faces clearly** — a sign of trauma-repressed memory

- **The Vestige (Dunmer).** The real thread running through the conversation was how each of them copes with being faced with the things they hate most. For Periwyn it's vampires; for Onasha it's Dunmer. Onasha noted, uncomfortably, that the Vestige has only helped them and hasn't wronged her personally — she's struggling to hold onto fear and hatred of someone who hasn't actually been monstrous to her. She confessed she has **never once looked the Vestige in the eyes**; every time she looks at him, she panics.

- **Rebuilding the family legacy.** Periwyn admitted part of her crusade is also about honouring what her family built — a trade network that fed and clothed people across the world. She'd like to rebuild it once her "chapter" is done, but she doesn't know if she'll want to see her old friend (a past travel companion she showed Onasha a portrait of in her journal — a human woman, not drawn in Periwyn's art style, who eventually left because of Periwyn's relentless crusade and nightmares).

**The scene ended** with Onasha attempting (and catastrophically failing) to draw a periwinkle in the snow — Periwyn had to physically guide her hand to produce something resembling a flower. Onasha eventually fell asleep leaning on Periwyn's shoulder at the pond's edge, at which point Periwyn gently woke her after about 20 minutes and suggested they head back inside to sleep before the Daedra woke up.

### Part 2 — Durov in Blackreach (Pre-dawn)

Durov woke in the early hours and began wandering the Dwemer ruin's corridors with pen and paper, tracing a rough schematic. After mapping the sleeping quarters area, he stumbled onto a door he hadn't opened before — an elevator shaft — and accidentally triggered it, descending into **Blackreach**.

There, silhouetted against the enormous artificial sun of the vast underground cavern, he saw the unmistakeable shape of a **dragon** — wings spread, partially eclipsing the glowing orb as it circled overhead. Durov stood in silence and watched until it disappeared from view. He then carefully traced his way back up, bluffed past a confused guard ("I must not have had enough sleep"), and emerged just past sunrise. He groomed Rhymefang and waited for the others.


### Part 3 — Investigating the Abandoned Camp (Morning)

Durov accompanied Preventers on a ~45-minute trek southeast to a derelict camp abandoned approximately **two to three weeks** ago. Evidence gathered:

- **Multiple bodkin/splitting arrows** embedded in trees — not target practice. One tree had dried human blood consistent with someone having been shot against it.
- **Shattered glass** from alchemical bomb vials (gas-dispersal type — likely frenzy bombs based on the lack of scorch marks).
- A **half-buried tent** containing a note: *"Attacked. New camp. East."*
- A **broken-down cart** — too damaged to move, left behind.
- Proventus found **broken branches at chest-height** along the treeline, leading northeast.

Durov concluded with a quiet private smile that a familiar party member had likely been here. He pocketed one bodkin arrow. Durov told Preventers to write everything down.

### Part 4 — The Daedra Interrogation (Morning)

Onasha and Periwyn were roused by the Vestige with the news that the Daedra was waking. The Vestige briefed them outside the door:

- A **ring** on his person suppresses the Daedra's magic while he remains nearby (outside the room).
- Three weapons are available on a table: a **dueling sword** (causes "existential pain" to Daedra, 1d6+5), an **assassin's dagger** (kills Daedra permanently — do not use yet), and **three enchanted throwing daggers** (painful but non-lethal to Daedra; passed through its armour).
- The Vestige stepped outside to play solitaire.

Periwyn slapped the Daedra awake with a full haymaker. The interrogation began. Onasha asked questions; Periwyn hovered close with daggers. Key intelligence extracted:

Periwyn's daggers found multiple homes throughout the interrogation (leg, foot, shoulder). The enchanted weapons caused visible distress. Periwyn also received information about the armourers responsible for the vestige's enchanted weapons and armour, written down on a slip of paper.

### Part 5 — The Daedric Contract

Onasha stepped out to speak privately with the Vestige about commissioning a hit. She then returned with a proposal for the Daedra:

**Onasha's offer:**
- Let it leave the ruin unharmed (by Onasha and Periwyn).
- Ensure the Komodo Tongue agents who failed it are dead.
- Full truthful answers to all remaining questions.

A **Daedric contract materialised on parchment**. Careful negotiation over wording ensued:

- "No harm from allies" was narrowed to **Onasha and Periwyn by name** — Milos/the Vestige and Durov are not covered.
- "While leaving the ruin" limits the protection to the premises only — outside, all bets are off.
- The contract does **not** specify Delos Nazi must be alive when the Daedra arrives. Onasha has separately paid the Dark Brotherhood 800 gold to get there first.

The contract was signed in blood (using daggers that had already been inside the Daedra — Periwyn's suggestion) and sealed in blue flame. The Daedra's soul is also forfeit if it violates its terms.

**Outcome of the deal:** The Vestige confirmed this was only the second time he had seen a mortal outsmart a Daedra by exploiting its arrogance. The Daedra will be held in the ruin for roughly **three months** (time for the Brotherhood to reach Tier) before being released per the contract.

Separately, the Vestige confirmed: the Dark Brotherhood can take regular assassination contracts outside of a formal Black Sacrament. Onasha paid 800 gold. She is now nearly broke.
