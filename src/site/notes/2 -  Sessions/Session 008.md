# Session 008 - 2025-06-21

## Summary
The party accepted a contract from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hadvar\|Hadvar]] of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Cold-Moor\|Clan Cold-Moor]] to investigate a [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Haunted_Hunting_Lodge\|haunted hunting lodge]] east of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. After gathering information from the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]] and local twin bards, they discovered the lodge was built on ancient ground where a massacre occurred. At the lodge, they encountered the spirit of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] and the undead [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] returned to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] for supplies to consecrate the site while the others prepared a funeral pyre for the spirits. The party discovered the remains of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] in the well, including her head and a necklace, and found a locked box that [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] requested be burned.

## Detailed Recap

### Morning at the Keep (4th of Frostfall)
- **7:00 AM:** The morning in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] was grey and cold. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] woke first and stood quietly in the corridor, waiting.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] woke next and was stopped by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] in the hallway, followed by [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]
- The three had a subdued conversation laced with vulnerability about [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s concerns regarding their secretive necromancy attempt:
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], her voice tense, advised [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] not to be overly attached, saying "That only gets you hurt." But the words betrayed a flicker of care she refused to admit.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], with arms crossed and sharp eyes, told [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] to mind her own business.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], calm and unwavering, spoke of the sister she had lost and her desire to look out for the group so the same thing wouldn't happen again. "I care about you. Both of you. I didn't think I would, but I do." Her voice cracked slightly, leaving a moment of heavy silence.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] woke and headed to the great hall for meats
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] barely touched her food, focused entirely on scribbling in her journal

### Contract Details
- Contract reward: 200 gold each
- Location: Hunting lodge east of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], near the fork in the road where woods thicken ("[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Kahrus_Hollow\|Kahru's Hollow]]")
- Haunting phenomena: Doors opening on their own, hearth lighting itself, whispers from rafters, bear rug growling, bed moving at midnight
- Payment: 200 gold, one bottle of Cold-Moor Reserve, items from root cellar (excluding cursed items)
- Contact: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hadvar\|Hadvar]] of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Cold-Moor\|Clan Cold-Moor]] at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Sleeping_Stag\|The Sleeping Stag]]

### Journey to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Sleeping_Stag\|The Sleeping Stag]]
- Pleasant morning, slightly chilly but not cold
- Saw fishermen heading to work and people with carts
- Felt watched since the contract piece was burned by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]

### Arrival at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Sleeping_Stag\|The Sleeping Stag]]
- Located on a quieter side of town, carrying the scent of burning pine
- Massive elk head above the hearth
- Bards (skalds) chatting by the hearth on the right of the entrance
- Well-dressed man ([[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hadvar\|Hadvar]]) in corner with boots on chair, wine half gone, plate of lamb and veggies lazily picked at, looking out of place in every sense

### Meeting with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hadvar\|Hadvar]]
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hadvar\|Hadvar]] explained the haunted house situation without much courtesy
  - Strange occurrences started two nights ago at his newly acquired ancestral lodge
  - Heard ghostly voices whispering his name from rafters
- House location: Near [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Kahrus_Hollow\|Khaarus Hollow]], at the fork in the road
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hadvar\|Hadvar]] attempted to draw a horrendous map and waved them off
- Appalled, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] redrew a more accurate map in her journal from his ramblings

### Information from the Bards
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonya]]** (Twin skalds and keepers of song/story):
  - Suggested [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hadvar\|Hadvar]] probably scared himself while drunk
  - Lodge abandoned longer than most people have been alive
  - Known for cold air and strange noises, everybody knows about the lodge
  - The well at the lodge never dries up, even during frost
  - They told the tale of Clan Coldmoor: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven Cold-Moor]] had loved [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Red-Grain\|Clan Red-Grain]]. The love was deemed shameful. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] was flayed alive and thrown in the village well. In his grief, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] walked into the barrow. That night, the entire clan was found dead.

### Additional Information from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eydis\|Eydis]]
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eydis\|Eydis]] mentioned there used to be a whole village in the lodge area
- The story mentioned the First Empire (at least 4,000 years old)
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] noted the massacre was definitely not vampires (too early in history)
- No further information about Clan Coldmoor; everything else struck from history

### Visit to the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]] at Ysmir's Hall
- **Time:** Around 8:30-9:00 AM
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]] still in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]
- Few cult members training outside, others inside
- Directed to shaman [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]], currently in charge
  - She was younger than expected, her eyes covered by a blindfold
  - Wore a headdress adorned with antlers and feathers, runes marked her arms
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]'s insights (cryptic but precise):**
  - Lodge lies on disturbed ground; no blessing of the gods has ever taken hold there (cursed)
  - Dealing with creatures bound by pain and rage, yet mercy might reach them
  - Should try to understand them
  - Presence unlike anything else in Skyrim; spirits might be reasoned with, though swords might still be useful
  - Recognized [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] as the merchant's daughter, unnerving [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] into silence
  - Told [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] offering quiet hope: "Your sister will come. When she can."
  - Explained the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Talking_Fox\|talking fox]] as a spirit of nature drawn to [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s path

### Journey to the Lodge
- **Time:** Around 9:30 AM departure
- Guards at east gate told them no guards go near the lodge after dark
- Walked for several hours as forest grew denser, air turned clammy with moss and bark scent
- **Encounter with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olgierd\|Olgierd]]:**
  - Small hut with farming area near woodland edge
  - Warned his goat had vanished into the trees near the lodge, and wasn't foolish enough to follow
  - Described weird noises, trees leaning wrong way, fog coming quickly
  - "They say if you listen closely to the well, you can still hear her crying or laughing"
- **Disused shrine:** Overgrown and covered in leaves, long forgotten
- **Fork in the road:**
  - Stone kern (cairn) in the middle, worn and half-toppled
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] recognized its resemblance to Wyrd magic (Nordic origin)
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] compared it to the warding stones of Black Marsh
  - Faint leftover magic, set up to protect from evil spirits. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] marked it on her map
- **Fog:** Rolled in very densely after passing the fork, winding between the trees like something alive
- **Hunter's corpse:** Recent body spotted out of corner of eye; marked on map
- **Disembodied voices:** Echoed in the fog: "She still weeps," followed by "He loved her"

### Arrival at the Lodge
- **Time:** Around 2:00 PM
- Though surrounding buildings lay in ruin, the lodge stood intact, untouched by time
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] whispered it felt as though the Divines themselves had turned their eyes away from this place
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] checked the well, found tension on rope and skeleton at far end
- **Pale figure:** Spotted in lodge window
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] waved, figure waved sheepishly back
  - Front door creaked open as they asked who she was

### Inside the Lodge
- **Common room:** Hearth was lit, open wine bottle on table
- Door slammed shut once inside
- Spirit identified herself as [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]], welcoming them warmly as it had been so long since she'd spoken with the living
- Spirit didn't recognize [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]] or his description ("I know no god of that name.")
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] paled; [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]] was far older than they thought, older than most vampires
- Looked puzzled when told gods don't look at this place

### Sven's Arrival
- **Armor clang:** Heard outside, cutting the conversation short
  - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]]'s demeanor changed: "He doesn't like when I have guests."
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] banged on door, then the door exploded inward
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] barely dodged in time
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]]'s appearance:** A draugr clad in rusted, mangled armor with glowing blue eyes like cold ice
- He demanded they leave, but the party saw the truth in him and offered to help
- He relented: "Until morning. Then this ends." (Time limit until dawn to put them to rest)
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] said he prayed to be put to rest to whoever was listening, but whatever answered left him in current state

### The Mask Discovery
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] led them to master bedroom, pointing to wardrobe furthest from door
- Wardrobe had drag marks
- After everyone failed to move it, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] moved it away
- **Locked chest:** Found behind wardrobe
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] unlocked it
- **Contents:** Pouch of ancient coins and a strange metal mask
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] carefully sketched the mask in her journal, choosing not to disturb it for now
- As a Knight of [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] suggested consecration to prevent resurrection, but lacked ingredients
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] ran off back to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] with:
  - Map [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] drew
  - Sketch of the mask to ask questions to the court mage
  - List of items for [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]]'s rite: Salt, Iron shard

### Cellar Investigation
- **Smell:** Unbearable; both [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] threw up/retched
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] somehow fine
- **Skeleton:** Found with key and parchment written in Nordic
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] said it was an excerpt from his journal, and the skeleton belonged to a person who got closest to helping
- Entry explained the ritual [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] did

### Funeral Pyre Preparation
- Went back outside and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] cut wood for the funeral pyre
- Assembled the pyre
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] pulled [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Lagertha\|Lagertha]]'s body from the well, but her head wasn't there (it had fallen in)

### Well Investigation
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], able to breathe underwater, went into well with rope tied around waist
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] held the rope tight
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] descended 12 meters down into a big, extremely dark cavern
- **Found:** The skull/head, a necklace [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] gave her, and a small lockbox
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sven_Cold-Moor\|Sven]] asked them to burn the lockbox
- Everything set up, waiting for [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] to return
- As the sun dipped low, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] went to meditate near the lodge while they waited
