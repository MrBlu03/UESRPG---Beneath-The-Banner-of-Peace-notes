# Session 003 - 2025-02-16 (9th of Heartfire, 2E 600)

## Summary
The party ventured out to find missing alchemist [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]], successfully locating her in the woods with a protective spriggan. They also received findings about the murdered [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith Velan]]'s body from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sigrid\|Sigrid]] and learned of a mysterious magical blizzard surrounding [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]], which [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] recognized as the work of the ancient [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]] vampires.

## Detailed Recap

### Morning at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]]
- Some days had passed since the audience with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]].
- Party woke up in sequence: [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]].
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] was seen hastily putting his helmet back on.
- Grabbed food to go at [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]]'s.
- Regrouped by the bounty board in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]'s square, where a hurriedly penned notice caught their eye.

### Investigation in Town
- Learned that [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] (owner of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Scent_of_Serenity\|Scent of Serenity]]) had not returned home for 2-3 days from her usual herb-gathering.
- Her son [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hafjorg\|Hafjorg]] was visibly worried and feared the worst, offering a 200 gold reward to find her.
- Visited [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Sweaty_Steel\|Sweaty Steel]] blacksmith, run by siblings [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svana\|Svana]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorn\|Bjorn]].
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] sold off a greatsword retrieved from a bandit.
  - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svana\|Svana]] confirmed [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]]'s routine wanderings but grew concerned upon hearing she had been gone for two nights, thanking the group for taking it seriously.
- Additional information gained about the southern area:
  - Sabercats have made nests near the hills.
  - [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Shadow_Stone\|Shadow Stone]] standing stone in the area.
    - Old relic from when Nords settled in Skyrim.
    - Grants power to those who attune to it.
    - Located next to old ruins from the Dragon War, which have undead and draugr.
    - Ruin is built around the stone.

### Search in the Southern Woods
- Passed by docks and fish market (smelled fishy).
- Saw Mistvail Keep with [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Honed_Edge\|The Honed Edge]] in front.
- Visited a windmill surrounded by fields.
- Gathered information from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eydis\|Eydis]], a rugged hunter hauling a freshly killed elk carcass.
  - He had sold meat to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] two nights prior.
  - He warned of wolves, sabercats, and darker tales of rare Frost Trolls from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mount_Fjelhorst\|Mount Fjelhorst]]. Decades ago, frost trolls had descended and ravaged [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Nimalten\|Nimalten]], killing [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eydis\|Eydis]]'s father.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] tracked a trail finding signs of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]]:
  - Crushed flowers and trampled undergrowth.
  - Piece of torn fleece fabric caught on a thorn bush.
  - Torn herb basket near a scratched tree. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] inspected the bark and recognized spriggan claw marks.
  - Blood trail leading south, parallel with the mountains.
  - Saw lights at the top of the mountain.
- Following the blood, they found a pack of 6 wolves feeding on an elk (avoided by slowly retreating).
- Near the base of the mountain, found an active, modest campsite with a simmering potato and venison stew (burning for about 2 days).
- Located footprints leading to a large collection of rocks and a possible cave.

### [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] Found
- Party waited at camp for 30 minutes.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] took care of weapons while waiting.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] slipped into the woods to gather botanical ingredients of her own.
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] emerged from the cave after 20 minutes, basket in hand, flanked by an unusually placid spriggan.
- She was unharmed, in good spirits, and greeted them warmly, inviting them to share her stew.
- Discovered [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] has been friends with the spriggan for about 20 years.
- Received a dark blue ribbon to show to her son as proof.
- During the meal, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] mentioned:
  - Tales of old Skyrim, including ruins of Fjelhorst atop the mountain (a [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Dragon_Cult\|Dragon Cult]] stronghold where Draugr still wander).
  - She noticed [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s shield emblem. When [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] confessed his amnesia, she mentioned an old Colovian acquaintance in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Faldarstad]] in the North who might know more.
  - Three Banners War (580-589, started about 20 years ago).
  - [[Info/Quests/Investigate_Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile_Cave\|Cult of Clavicus Vile]] (Lord of Bargains) in a cave.
  - Information about Kaalgrontiid, a dragon leader who rebelled against Alduin.

### Return to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] said she would be home by dinner.
- Flagged down by the spry city courier [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik the Quick]] with a message from mortician [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sigrid\|Sigrid]].
- Delivered the ribbon to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hafjorg\|Hafjorg]], who teared up, thanked them profusely, and busied himself preparing the home. (He had previously been attacked by a spriggan, making his mother's alliance ironic).
- Heard the town crier in the central market announce [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]'s mysterious blizzard situation:
  - The town was swallowed by an unnatural blizzard with walls of dense ice.
  - Travelers going north advised to take the road through [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Faldarstad]].
  - Monstrous shapes were reported moving within the ice.

### Hall of the Dead
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sigrid\|Sigrid]] led them straight to the Dunmer's body and revealed troubling findings:
  - Healed wounds that formed scar tissue.
  - Body was dragged to the tree after death, ruling out suicide. No signs of struggle.
  - Poisoned with extremely potent nightshade in food.
  - She had brought a blood sample to the skilled alchemist [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Ralvard\|Ralvard]].
  - The poison was complex and contained exotic ingredients to mask the nightshade:
    - Vampire dust (from killing vampires with silver weapons).
    - Shadow ichor (from hellhounds in Oblivion planes).
    - Twilight poison (from poison glands of winged twilight, also from Oblivion).
  - Suggests involvement of a powerful perpetrator who can enter the planes of Oblivion (or hunt there).
  - The Queen's guard had already been informed. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sigrid\|Sigrid]] warned them to tread carefully.

### Meeting with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]]
- Visited the mead hall of the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]]. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]], towering above even [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], shared grim news.
- The ice around [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] is unnatural. People approaching the barriers had vanished, snatched by creatures phasing through the ice.
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Wuunferth\|Wuunferth]] (vampire hunter from [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s quest) stranded in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]].
- Mentioned the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Fallowstone_Hall\|Fallowstone Hall]] (ancient, powerful Nord warriors). Their harbinger, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod Wraithbane]], had established a war camp nearby to rescue the town.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] recognized the signs and paled. These were not just monsters—they were [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]], an ancient lineage feared among the undead, unseen in Skyrim for centuries.
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir]] offered the use of [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]] facilities for training, repair, and food.
