# Session 004 - 2025-03-02 (15th of Heartfire, 2E 600)

## Summary
The party was summoned by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]] to investigate a military keep called [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Harmugstahl\|Harmugstahl]] that had gone silent three days ago. Upon arrival, they discovered the keep was eerily frozen, with soldiers and objects frozen solid. The party encountered mysterious voices, a frostbitten hand in the well, and a trail of black feathers similar to the one [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] found earlier. They also uncovered a logbook from the frozen overseer, which revealed the last entry: "It is inside now." The party found a cracked mirror in the dungeon, which reflected a mysterious Khajiit figure. They also discovered two symbols etched into the ground: the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] and a variation of the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]] symbol. After reporting their findings to the queen, they received their reward and were invited to become the "[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|Fangs of Fjelhorst]]."

## Detailed Recap

### Morning at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]] & Town
- The party woke up, ate and drank food and ale. They had been training with the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]] for the past few days.
- Before heading to the queen at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil Keep]], the party visited **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Honed_Edge\|The Honed Edge]]** blacksmith to acquire more silver weapons.
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** and **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** each bought a bundle of 50 silver arrows for 100 gold, using a discount offered to those heading out to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]].
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** shared knowledge about **[[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]]**, the purest form of vampires, who are weak to sunlight and fire, resistant to non-magical damage, and undead.
  - The blacksmith owner, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hilldis\|Hilldis]]**, revealed that during the early First Era, her ancestral clan owned a castle off the coast of Haafingar that was besieged and captured by **[[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]]**. The survivors fled and resettled in the mountains of the Reach.

### Meeting with the Queen at Mistveil Keep
- Overheard conversation between [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]] and a darkly dressed man, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]].
  - Discussed a prophecy, a scroll, and someone named Harkon.
  - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Mortgren]] mentioned heading to the ruins atop Fjelhorst to ensure nothing buried there had awakened.
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]] spoke with [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]].
  - Confirmed the black feather's magical nature.
  - Advised her to keep it close as it may become useful.
  - Departed for the mountain and has not been seen since.
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]'s demeanor was cold as she explained the situation at [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Harmugstahl\|Harmugstahl]]:
  - A garrison and training ground for [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]'s scouts that had gone silent three nights prior.
  - Scouts sent to investigate never returned.
  - The hunter **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eydis\|Eydis]]** (from the previous session) had passed the area and reported no signs of battle, only an eerie stillness and a strange chill. He was later found dead in his home—frozen from the inside out, despite no trace of snow or struggle.

### Journey to [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Harmugstahl\|Harmugstahl]]
- The party embarked on a 5-hour walk southeast to the keep.
  - They were tracked by mysterious crows.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] lured one closer with bread, discovering it could speak. It introduced itself as **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron Jangleplume]]** from the **[[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Court of Crows]]** (messengers from the **[[Info/Lore/Nocturnal\|Nocturnal Realm]]**).
  - The crow issued cryptic warnings: "You're too late," "The keep is Hollow," "He waits beneath the bones," before vanishing into the trees.

### Investigation of [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Harmugstahl\|Harmugstahl]]
- Upon arrival, the party found the keep eerily lifeless.
  - Construction seemed old but completely abandoned.
  - No footprints apart from their own. Snow lay undisturbed in the courtyard.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] discovered a severed, frostbitten hand in the well bucket, and the water was frozen solid all the way to the bottom.
  - Stables: Trough was frozen, hay was almost frozen, no signs of horses.
- **Barracks (Side Door):**
  - Soldiers were frozen solid mid-action: some drawing swords, some kneeling in prayer, some writing letters.
  - Torches glowed dim and cold, giving off nearly no heat.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] approached one figure—its eyes moved. They were alive, yet trapped.
  - The party heard malicious whispers directed at [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]: "Gareth's little stray, still chasing ghosts?"
  - In fury, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] threw a dagger, shattering one of the soldiers.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] noticed movement in a mirror, but saw nothing when she turned—only a soft, sorrowful whisper: "You shouldn't be here."
- **Main Hall & Overseer's Quarters:**
  - Food sat rotting on the table, drinks frozen in mugs, and more frozen guards watched in silence.
  - Upstairs, the overseer sat frozen over his journal. The final line written: "It is inside now."
  - A voice, cold and smooth, filled the room: "All that faith, all that fire, and still you are afraid, you reek of [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]]'s prayers."
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] glimpsed a reflection in the window—a dark-furred, shadowy Khajiit standing in the snow, which disappeared when she blinked.
  - A force rattled the shutters closed despite no wind.
  - The scent of fresh blood filled the air, though no source was found.
  - Frozen soldiers' eyes began to track them. They saw breath exhaled from the darkest corner—ice-cold and invisible.
  - The party moved the frozen overseer to the entrance of the main hall (he was too heavy to carry far).
- **Dungeon:**
  - Air grew heavy, as if crushed beneath invisible weight.
  - A trail of black feathers led to a cracked mirror in a lone open cell.
  - The mirror reflected the same shadowy Khajiit figure, this time behind [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]. The figure said, "They left them for you." Only [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] could see the figure during this supernatural encounter.
  - A wind rushed through the hall, extinguishing all torches and plunging them into darkness.
  - Rustling sounds filled the silence—movement, not footsteps.
  - In front of [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] saw two symbols appear in the dust, as if drawn by an invisible hand:
    - The [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] symbol.
    - An unfamiliar variation of the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]] symbol, suggesting a splinter faction.
  - A second, stronger gust slammed doors shut, rattled chains, turned the torches back on, and left silence. The presence had left.
  - Upon returning upstairs, the frozen soldiers were gone—only a fine, snow-like dust remained.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] gathered a sample of the dust.

### Return to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]
- The party reported their findings to the queen, who was at a war table with a map of the Rift.
  - She realized this was the first blow in something far worse.
  - She addressed the party individually, noting their unique skills and resilience.
  - The queen offered them a name: the **[[Info/Organizations/Other/The_Fangs_of_Fjelhorst\|Fangs of Fjelhorst]]**. Hunters of the serpent, sworn to defend the weak, with her coin and backing.
- The party received their reward of **400 gold per person** from housecarl [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Rigi]].
- They handed over the journal found in the keep.
  - The captain/overseer was revealed to be [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Rigi]]'s brother.
- The party gave the dust samples to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Speaks-Spells-Gently\|Speaks-Spells-Gently]] for investigation.
  - When the "Court" was mentioned, his eyes widened in recognition.
  - Explained the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Court of Crows]] from [[Info/Lore/Nocturnal\|Everglow]]:
    - From [[Info/Lore/Nocturnal\|Nocturnal]]'s realm.
    - Friendly to mortals.
    - A pocket realm was captured and unanchored from [[Info/Lore/Nocturnal\|Everglow]].
    - [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Court of Crows]] now resides there.
    - Their allegiance is to the people of Tamriel.
    - Established by a Dunmer known as "[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]" (connected to [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s backstory).
  - Mentioned [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]] as a correspondent who could provide more information.
- The party had drinks with [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Rigi]] at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Amber_Keg\|Amber Keg]] tavern, where they met [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Grelka\|Grelka]].
- That night, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] had a dream of a large metal object like an insanely huge flail falling in the middle of a market square, destroying everything.
