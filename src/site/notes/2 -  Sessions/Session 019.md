# Session 019 - Downtime, Training & The Road to Kjalgrad

## Summary
Following the execution witnessed in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]], the party spent additional downtime in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] before departing for [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** mysteriously woke near [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s abandoned warehouse and wandered lost through the city before falling asleep by the stables. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** searched for him across town, eventually finding him and engaging in a stubborn argument. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** spent the night submerged in the river and drifted downstream. The party discussed travel plans and routes toward [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]. Over the following days, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] practiced her necromancy lessons, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] reconnected with an old egg-brother at the market, and the group prepared for departure. The five-day journey to [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] tested the party with harsh cold—[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] both suffered fatigue from exposure. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] hunted successfully, bringing down two elks and providing substantial rations. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] completed **Sub-Lesson III: The Bone Ward** after an initial failure left her frostbitten. Upon arrival in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], a prosperous mining town controlled by **[[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Morvan\|Clan Morvan]]**, the party secured rooms at the **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost Gate Hall]]**. A **murder of crows** visited [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s window, including the rotund **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Baron_Jangleplume\|Jangleplume]]**, stating they had business to discuss.

## Detailed Recap

## Day 4 of Downtime: 12th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600

### Early Morning - Durov's Disorientation

**5:30 AM - Near [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s Warehouse:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** woke up disoriented near [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s abandoned warehouse with no memory of how he arrived
- Decided to follow the walls and turned right into the first alleyway he found
- The alley led to a dead end
- Turned around and took the first left, accidentally retracing his exact route back
- Kept walking until something looked familiar
- Eventually reached the northern gate and entered the city
- Wandered onto the main street of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]
- Made his way through the market toward the stables
- Spotted a **large caravan of Argonians** entering the city
- Found the party's horses at the stables
- Lay down next to his sleeping horse, **[[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Rimefang]]**, and fell back asleep

### Morning - Periwyn's Search

**7:00 AM - The House:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** woke and noticed [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] was missing
- Decided to search for him systematically

**The Search Route:**
1. **The Market (First Stop):**
   - Heard a cabbage merchant yelling
   - Spotted [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Runa\|Runa]] at his stall
   - Saw the caravan of Argonians arriving

2. **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]] (Second Stop):**
   - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]] was serving drinks
   - He didn't know where [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] had gone

3. **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Wounded_Boar\|The Wounded Boar]] (Third Stop):**
   - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Denskar\|Denskar]] was serving food
   - Also had no information about [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s whereabouts

4. **South Gate Area (Final Stop):**
   - **8:00 AM** by this point
   - On the way, spotted a glint of armor next to a horse at the stables
   - Found [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] sleeping with one arm flung over **[[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Rimefang]]**'s neck

**The Wake-Up Confrontation:**
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] stood over [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and lightly kicked his leg
- He didn't wake up
- In his slumber, he kicked back
- She kicked him again and yelled **"Wake up!"**
- He finally woke and kicked [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] back
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] shoved his leg in response
- They argued back and forth about whether he should come home
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** stubbornly refused
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] eventually gave up and returned home alone

### Morning - Onasha's Return

**Early Morning - The River:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** woke slowly, floating to the surface of the river where she had slept
- She had drifted a decent distance downstream from her original sleeping spot
- Took 30 minutes to walk back to [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]

**9:00 AM - The House:**
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] arrived at the northern gate and headed back to the house
- Found **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** already awake and cooking

**Morning at the House:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** was preparing food for everybody
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] burst through the door and immediately went to the fire to dry off
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] handed her a bowl of stew and a warm drink
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] hung up all her clothes to dry and wrapped herself in blankets
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** returned home shortly after [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] informed the others of [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s stubborn refusal to come home
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] grabbed food and joined them by the fire

### Late Morning and Afternoon

**11:00 AM - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] Finally Wakes:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** woke at the stables
- Sat and petted his horse for a while
- Eventually decided to stroll back to the house, taking his time

**On the Way Home:**
- Passed through the market
- Bumped into the **cabbage merchant** they had met before
- The merchant was now selling "iceberg cabbages"—which proved to be nothing more than ordinary heads left in the snow. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] accepted the explanation with good humor and continued on.

**Noon - Arrival at the House:**
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] arrived around noon
- Completely ignored [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s visible annoyance
- Grabbed a bowl of food without comment

**Afternoon Activities:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** and **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** had a conversation with [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** continued studying the **Jack of Trades spellbook** [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] had given her in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 018\|Session 018]]
- The group discussed travel plans and route options:
  - **Proposed Route**: [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] → [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Shors_Stone\|Shor's Stone]] → [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] → [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Stromton\|Stromton]] → [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Nimalten\|Nimalten]]
- The party pored over the **Reconstruction Report** from the College of [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Winterhold\|Winterhold]] (what little remained of the burned letter fragments). It spoke of an "Architect," of brothers bound by cult or cause, of bargains tied to shifts in guard, and of someone named "S" who had failed and paid dearly.
- Before sleeping, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** finally noticed the small note [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] had left upon her bedroll, marked not by words but by a simple flower.

### Evening - Necromancy Practice

**Sub-Lesson III: The Bone Ward (First Attempt)**

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] attempted the third necromancy lesson from her training:

> **"To shape bone is to mirror your own nature. What you bind too tightly will break. What you treat too loosely will fall apart."**

**Exercise Instructions:**
1. Grind bone into fine powder
2. Stand in open air, no robes, no armor
3. Breathe slowly and call the dust upward with will alone
4. Let it drift against the skin—not as armor, but as weight
5. Hold the dust in place for one full minute without movement
6. Whisper its name: _"REMAIN"_

**Result:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** failed the lesson
- Gained **minor frostbite** from extended exposure in the cold
- Sprinted back to camp to warm up by the fire
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] helped treat the frostbite
