# Session 012 - 2025-08-30
**18th of Frostfall, 2E 600**

## Summary

The party awakens at the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall of Ysmir]] to urgent news from Windhelm regarding vampire activities and receives a new mission to investigate mysterious fires in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]'s Ratway. Learning about the rogue vampire [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn the Ancient]] and his connection to recent events, the party ventures into the underground criminal network to uncover the truth behind the fires. Their investigation reveals evidence of a conspiracy involving the theft of the poisoned Dunmer body's autopsy notes from the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall of the Dead]], with serpent cult involvement becoming increasingly apparent. The Queen grants them wide authority to pursue the cult.

## Detailed Recap

### Morning at the Hall of Ysmir

The party wakes in sequence on a cold morning with unsettling mist hanging over the town:

- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** wakes first, gets vegetables and sits in a corner, still weary from the unrest with [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Dres\|House Dres]]
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** grabs meat and reads her spell tomes upstairs, wanting to be left alone
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** heads upstairs and sits close to the fire, shivering against the chill
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** goes upstairs, grabs meat, and sits in a different corner in heavy silence

**Learning Opportunities:**
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] approaches [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] (who is meditating) to learn about woads, asking if they could cover magical marks. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] explains that most woads are used on the face for combat or diplomacy, but could extend over the body though less effectively. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] promises to teach [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] the ways of the woad when time allows.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] inquires about where to acquire a lute, and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]] speaks of Skyrim's warrior-poets and the skalds who would help her find one.

### Royal Summons

At midmorning, two [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] guards arrive requesting the party's immediate presence in the war room - there's news from Windhelm. The party makes their way through the cold city:

- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] mutters at the cold. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] asks after her, but presses no further.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] attempts to trip [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik the Quick]] again but fails as he vaults over her
- People are dressed warmer due to the cold weather

### War Room Briefing

At [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil Keep]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]] deliver crucial intelligence:

**News from Windhelm:**
- Skald-King Jorunn recognizes similar threats to the ancient [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]]
  - A messenger has been sent to Morthal to contact an old ally who fought the [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]]
  - The [[Info/Organizations/Other/Mages_Guild\|Mages Guild]] is sending experts to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] to study the strange ritual orb, including [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle_Benele]] (a name [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] recognizes as Breton but can't fully place), who specializes in Daedric relics
- The guild members left 12 days ago and will arrive on the 31st of Frostfall, just before Witches Festival

**[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Mortgren]]'s Vampire Analysis:**
- The severed head belongs to **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Thrysnr_the_Unsullied\|Thrysnr_the_Unsullied]]**
  - [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]] blood but not turned by Harkon or his court
- Actually turned by **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]]**, believed killed 20 years ago during the Planemeld
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]] was a former member of Harkon's court who grew tired of their isolationism
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Mortgren]] warns that Majorn's name carries weight in certain circles and should not be spoken carelessly. He intends to remain in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] to sift records and reinforce wards. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] lingers, intent on speaking with him.

### The Ratway Investigation

**Mission Briefing:**
[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]] assigns the party to investigate fires in the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ratway\|Ratway]] that occurred two nights prior. Rumors blame her guards, but she suspects the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] cult's involvement. The party must find the truth to restore trust between the crown and [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]'s underground population. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] suggests bringing food; Freidr agrees, sending them to the kitchens.

### Preparation and Market Intelligence

**At the Kitchen (11 AM):**
- Head chef **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Brann\|Brann]]** provides a large basket of food (bread, cheese, ham) for the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ratway\|Ratway]] inhabitants
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] carries the basket

**Market Investigation:**
- Fewer stalls than usual due to cold weather
- Guards report the fire happened south of the market, with smoke coming from manholes, occurring late afternoon
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Cabbage_Guy\|Cabbage_Guy]]** initially scoffs at their questions but laughs at [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s strange cabbage-phrased queries. Amused, he directs them to Toren in the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Outlaws_Refuge\|Outlaw's Refuge]].
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] buy cabbages as thanks, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] paying five gold for one. (Session note: [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] buys 5 cabbages, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] 1)

### The Outlaw's Refuge

**[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ragged_Flagon\|The_Ragged_Flagon]]:**
The party finds the hidden entrance near a boathouse and descends into the torch-lit tavern of smoke and shadow:

- **Barkeep** confirms fires started somewhere deeper in the Ratway warrens and points to a donation box where [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] places the food.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Yannick_the_Brawler\|Yannick_the_Brawler]]** (drunk) loudly blames guards but falters when pressed by [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]
- **Brooding Corner Patron** (respected Dunmer) whistles to stop Yannick and directs party to Toren

**Negotiating with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Toren_the_Fence\|Toren_the_Fence]]:**
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] negotiates information price down from 70 to 30 gold
- **Key Intelligence**: Three masked figures in strange black leathers with potentially Daedric runes started the fire in the warrens (side passages leading to catacombs) and slipped away
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] offers future moonsugar business partnership for 500 gold investment
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] tries to sell an old necromancy tome for 1500 gold. The offer comes back at 30, but with [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s help she negotiates it to 70 gold (keeps 20, puts 50 in communal coffers)

**The Brooding Patron's Information:**
In exchange for a blood contract (a future favor/one month obligation), the masked Dunmer provides crucial details:
- Fire starters wore black leathers with runes (possibly Daedric)
- Fire began near the catacombs, close to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall of the Dead]] entrance
- The blaze was fed by more than oil - deliberate accelerant used
- **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] mark** blackened one of the burned archways
- When [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] asks about the sudden growth of the Flagon, the patron explains many came from Windhelm, where outlaw activity had been disrupted by bigger fish.

### [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall_of_the_Dead]] Investigation

Following [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s theory about the poisoned Dunmer body from [[2 -  Sessions/Session 002\|Session 2]]:
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sigrid\|Sigrid]]** tears through her shelves in panic — her autopsy notes on the fallen Dunmer are gone.
- No signs of forced entry, nothing else missing
- Confirms [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s suspicion that the fire was a diversion for the theft

### Debrief with Queen Freidr

The party returns to report their findings. The evidence points to a coordinated operation by the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] cult to steal the Dunmer body's documentation under cover of the deliberately set fire. The Queen reimburses [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] for the gold spent and grants them wide authority to pursue the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]], with papers to follow in days.
