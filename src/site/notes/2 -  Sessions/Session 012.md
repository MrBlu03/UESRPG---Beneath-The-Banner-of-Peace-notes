# Session 012 - 2025-08-30
**18th of Frostfall, 2E 600**

## Summary

The party awakens at the Hall of Ysmir to urgent news from Windhelm regarding vampire activities and receives a new mission to investigate mysterious fires in Riften's Ratway. Learning about the rogue vampire Majorn the Ancient and his connection to recent events, the party ventures into the underground criminal network to uncover the truth behind the fires. Their investigation reveals evidence of a conspiracy involving the theft of the poisoned Dunmer body from the Hall of the Dead, with serpent cult involvement becoming increasingly apparent.

## Detailed Recap

### Morning at the Hall of Ysmir

The party wakes in sequence on a cold morning with unsettling mist hanging over the town:

- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** wakes first, gets vegetables and sits in a corner to observe
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** grabs meat and reads her tomes upstairs, wanting to be left alone
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** heads upstairs and sits close to the fire, agitated by the cold
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** goes upstairs, grabs meat, and sits in a different corner from [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]

**Learning Opportunities:**
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] approaches [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Adda\|Adda]] (who is meditating) to learn about woads, asking if they could cover magical marks. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Adda\|Adda]] explains that most woads are used on the face for combat or diplomacy, but could extend over the body though less effectively. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Adda\|Adda]] agrees to teach [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] the ways of the woad.
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] inquires about where to acquire a lute

### Royal Summons

At 9 AM, two Riften guards arrive requesting the party's immediate presence in the war room - there's news from Windhelm. The party makes their way through the cold city:

- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] is bothered by the unsettling chill and low-hanging mist
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] attempts to trip [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik the Quick]] again but fails as he jumps over her
- People are dressed warmer due to the cold weather

### War Room Briefing

At Mistveil Keep, [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus_Mortgren]] deliver crucial intelligence:

**News from Windhelm:**
- Skald-King Jorunn recognizes similar threats to the ancient [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]]
  - A messenger has been sent to Morthal to contact an old ally who fought the [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]]
  - The [[Info/Organizations/Mages_Guild\|Mages Guild]] is sending experts to [[Info/Locations/Greenwall\|Greenwall]], including [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle_Benele]] (a name [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] recognizes as Breton but can't place), who specializes in Daedric relics
- The guild members left 12 days ago and will arrive on the 31st of Frostfall, just before Witches Festival

**Mortgren's Vampire Analysis:**
- The severed head belongs to **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Thrysnr_the_Unsullied\|Thrysnr_the_Unsullied]]**
  - [[Info/Organizations/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]] blood but not turned by Harkon or his court
- Actually turned by **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]]**, believed killed 20 years ago during the Planemeld
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn_the_Ancient]] was a former member of Harkon's court who grew tired of their isolationism
- Mortgren warns that Majorn's name carries weight in certain circles and should not be spoken carelessly

### The Ratway Investigation

**Mission Briefing:**
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Queen_Freidr\|Queen_Freidr]] assigns the party to investigate fires in the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] that occurred two nights prior. Rumors blame her guards, but she suspects the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] cult's involvement. The party must find the truth to restore trust between the crown and Riften's underground population.

### Preparation and Market Intelligence

**At the Kitchen (11 AM):**
- Head chef **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Brann\|Brann]]** provides a large basket of food (bread, cheese, ham) for the [[Info/Locations/Ratway\|Ratway]] inhabitants
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] carries the basket

**Market Investigation:**
- Fewer stalls than usual due to cold weather
- Guards report the fire happened south of the market, with smoke coming from manholes, occurring late afternoon
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Cabbage_Guy\|Cabbage_Guy]]** initially dismisses their questions but eventually suggests talking to Toren about black market matters
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] buys 5 cabbages, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] buys 1

### The Outlaw's Refuge

**[[Info/Locations/The_Ragged_Flagon\|The_Ragged_Flagon]]:**
The party finds the hidden entrance near a boathouse and descends into the torch-lit tavern:

- **Barman** confirms fires started somewhere deeper in the Ratway
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Yannick_the_Brawler\|Yannick_the_Brawler]]** (drunk) loudly blames guards and tries to start a fight with [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]
- **Brooding Corner Patron** (respected Dunmer) whistles to stop Yannick and directs party to Toren

**Negotiating with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Toren_the_Fence\|Toren_the_Fence]]:**
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] negotiates information price down from 70 to 30 gold
- **Key Intelligence**: Three masked figures in strange black leathers with potentially Daedric runes started the fire in the warrens (side passages leading to catacombs)
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] offers future moonsugar business partnership for 500 gold investment
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sells old necromancy tome for 70 gold (keeps 20, puts 50 in communal coffers)

**The Brooding Patron's Information:**
In exchange for a future favor (one month obligation), the Dunmer provides crucial details:
- Fire starters wore black leathers with runes (possibly Daedric)
- Fire began near the catacombs, close to Hall of the Dead entrance
- Oil burned too quickly for normal torches - deliberate accelerant used
- **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] mark** found on one of the burned archways

### [[Info/Locations/Hall_of_the_Dead\|Hall_of_the_Dead]] Investigation

Following [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s theory about the poisoned Dunmer body from Session 2:
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Sigrid\|Sigrid]]** cannot locate the documentation on the examined Dunmer
- No signs of forced entry, nothing else missing
- Confirms [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s suspicion that the fire was a diversion for the theft

### Debrief with Queen Freidr

The party returns to report their findings. The evidence points to a coordinated operation by the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] cult to steal the Dunmer body and its documentation under cover of the deliberately set fire.

|---|---|
|**Fire Location:** Fire started in the Warrens, near the catacombs/Hall of the Dead.|The proximity suggests the fire was set to create chaos, providing cover for stealing the Dunmer body.|

|**No Forced Entry:** No signs of break-in at the Hall of the Dead.|The thieves used the fire as distraction to enter undetected or had inside knowledge.|
|**Missing Documentation:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Sigrid\|Sigrid]] cannot find the records on the poisoned Dunmer.|The thieves took both body and documentation to cover their tracks.|
|**Suspects:** 3 masked people in black leathers with Daedric runes.|Suggests organized cult activity, possibly with necromantic or Daedric interests.|

|**Coiled Serpent Mark:** Found on archways in the burned area.|Confirms serpent cult involvement and territorial marking.|
