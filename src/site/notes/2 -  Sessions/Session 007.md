# Session 007 - 2025-05-10

## Summary
After defeating the vampires at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]], the party transported the decapitated vampire head and informed [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod Wraithbane]] of their [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark Brotherhood]] encounter. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] interacted with the mysterious crimson orb and received a terrifying vision of undead werewolves. The party was instructed to return to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] and report to the Queen while the injured victim, identified as [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Wuunferth\|Wuunferth]], was taken to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistwatch\|Mistwatch]] for healing. After paying respects to their fallen [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|companions]] [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild]], they began their five-day journey back to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], which was marked by difficult travel conditions, strained relationships after a failed necromancy attempt, encounters with strange creatures including a [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Talking_Fox\|Talking Fox]], and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s recurring nightmares featuring both her traumatic past and the Daedric Prince [[Info/Lore/Malacath\|Malacath]]. Upon reaching [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], they reported their findings to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]], and received payment for their work.

## Detailed Recap

### Final Moments at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] Keep (24th of Heartfire)

- The party discussed what to do with the decapitated vampire head
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] hoisted it into a cloth sack to transport it—grisly proof of their victory
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod Wraithbane]] warned them about the dangers of associating with the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark Brotherhood]], saying "You walk among dangerous powers now. Choose your allies with care."
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] investigated the mysterious crimson orb:
  - Shot it with lightning, feeling a strange pull on her body and mind
  - When her hand touched the orb, she received a vision:
    - A pale moon in the sky filtering through a snowy glade
    - Pale creatures with yellow eyes, taller than [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]
    - Undead werewolves with exposed muscle and rotting flesh
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]] went pale upon seeing the victim and identified him as [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Wuunferth\|Wuunferth]] the Hunter ([[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s personal quest)
- A hawk swept through the rafters and delivered a message to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod Wraithbane]] about reinforcement timelines
  - Reinforcements would arrive by the next day, and the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] would remain behind to secure the keep and the dangerous orb
- The party (known as "The Fangs") was instructed to return to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] to report directly to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]
  - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hillevi\|Hillevi]] agreed to take [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Wuunferth\|Wuunferth]] to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistwatch\|Mistwatch]] for medical attention from the Old Gods
  - The party could meet them there after the snow melts

### Departure from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]

- Back at camp, they found a still-smoking funeral pyre with the bodies of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild]]
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] placed fresh flowers atop the ash
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] drew a periwinkle flower in her journal, tore out the page, and tossed it onto the flames
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hjar\|Hjar]] met them at the edge of the woods and provided rations for their journey
  - Asked them to give his regards to his brother [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torvald_Black-Foot\|Torvald Black-Foot]]: "Tell my brother I still owe him a bottle of mead."
- The party assigned travel tasks for the journey:
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]: Assisting
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]: Healing
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]: Scouting
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]: Guarding

### Journey to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] - Day 1

- The entire party struggled with their travel tasks (travel toll reached 18)
  - Fatigue weighed on them, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] stumbled and suffered a minor injury (twisted ankle), preventing travel actions the next day
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] struggled too, shoulders slumping, becoming more susceptible to injuries
- At the evening camp:
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] slipped away to attempt necromancy on the bird
  - While waiting for the others to sleep, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] read her ingredient book and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sketched [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] in her journal
  - Unknown to them, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] had silently followed and watched from the shadows
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s resurrection spell failed and backfired
    - The energies of the soul fragments lashed back at [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], filling her with agony and she collapsed
    - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] watched with wide eyes full of unspoken fury but did not intervene
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] experienced a harrowing nightmare that night:
    - Relived being nine years old during the vampire attack that killed her family
    - Saw her twin brother with bite marks, asking "Why didn't you save us?"
    - The dream shifted to her mentor dying of vampire infection while she held a silver dagger
    - As the nightmare ended, she heard [[Info/Lore/Malacath\|Malacath]]'s voice: "Feed that anger, give in to vengeance, be my pawn."
    - She saw a large orc-like figure with a blackened maul on a throne of stone, recognizing it as [[Info/Lore/Malacath\|Malacath]]
    - She woke clutching her silver dagger and moonstone pendant, silently crying

### Journey to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] - Day 2 (25th of Heartfire)

- The morning was crisp and clear
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] cooked slaughterfish by the fire
  - She spoke only to [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], avoiding [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] spent the morning crying in her tent, remaining hollow-eyed
  - She barely composed herself for departure
- They passed the extinguished pyre where [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] had cremated the hanging victims (the ashes were cold)
- Evening camp activities:
  - They spotted a squirrel rummaging through their backpacks
    - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] startled it, but [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] gently offered it berries instead, which it accepted
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] searched for ingredients separately
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] stared blankly into the campfire
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] had no nightmares but struggled to sleep

### Journey to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] - Day 3 (26th of Heartfire)

- Weather was slightly cloudy with no significant events initially
- They encountered a [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Donkey_Khajiit_Merchant\|Donkey Khajiit Merchant]] traveling toward [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] inquired about alchemy ingredients, but he had none
  - The merchant noticed [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s distress
    - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] explained about her nightmares
    - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] remained unresponsive, focused on her journal
  - He offered a vial with a mysterious potion he claimed could produce lucid dreams
    - After heeding [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s wary look, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] turned it over but declined
- At the evening camp:
  - Storm clouds gathered and rain began falling, soaking their cloaks
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] stood with her face to the sky, drinking in the moisture
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] gathered berries while [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] collected alchemy ingredients
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] remained silent and withdrawn

### Journey to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] - Day 4 (27th of Heartfire)

- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] nearly skipped breakfast again, having not eaten for two days
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] insisted she eat: "You won't help anyone by starving yourself."
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] continued her silence toward [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] finally addressed the tension after the failed necromancy attempt
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] expressed feeling betrayed by their secrecy
  - She revealed her younger sister vanished years ago and she feared loss again
  - This admission shocked [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], who had never considered someone might actually care for her. In a rare moment of vulnerability, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] subtly hinted she cared too, though she wouldn't admit it fully.
- The rain stopped around midday
- Evening encounter with a [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Talking_Fox\|Talking Fox]]:
  - The fox approached their campfire again
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] fed it fish, after which it spoke
  - It offered to interpret [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s dragon language (Dovahzul) scroll
  - It explained that direct translation was impossible; the language used few words, imbued with multiple meanings, for dragons speak not with breath but with their soul
  - The interpretation: doom, secrecy, and silence. It warned to guard it well and tell no one, mentioning "the Blackfeather" whose founder values secrecy
  - When a piece of the parchment was burned in the flames to test it, the fire turned black, roaring high into the air like a pillar of shadow. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] yanked the unharmed scroll back.
  - The group felt a heavy sense of being watched from that moment on
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] experienced another nightmare:
  - She saw [[Info/Lore/Malacath\|Malacath]] on a throne of skulls
  - He stomped his hammer, creating a pool of blood beneath her
  - She jolted awake in a panic with a small cut on her forehead from hitting something as she woke
  - Still clutched her silver dagger and moonstone pendant

### Arrival in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] - Day 5 (28th of Heartfire)

- By mid-morning, the spires of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] came into view
- They headed directly to the alchemy shop to inform [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] about [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] gently delivered the news that no one from her family survived
  - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]], devastated, closed the shop for the day and went home to grieve
- At the market, they observed a drunken brawl
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] placed bets (10 and 5 gold) and won a few extra coins
- Meeting with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]:
  - Initially intercepted by guards, they were eventually rushed inside the war room
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] reported that [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] was overrun by vampires but the threat was eliminated
  - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]] examined the vampire head
    - Confirmed it belonged to an ancient, rogue member of [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Clan_Volkihar\|Clan Volkihar]]. Not pureblooded, but powerful.
    - He took the head for further study
  - The party received 250 gold each as compensation
  - They reported on the mysterious orb and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s vision of undead werewolves
  - The Queen shared information about similar past incidents:
    - In Western Skyrim, the [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]] (a sinister alliance of vampires and werebeasts) had experimented with merging their curses into hybrid monstrosities
    - She promised to send word to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]
    - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]] would travel to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] to study the orb
    - The High King and [[Info/Organizations/Other/Mages_Guild\|Mages Guild]] would be informed
  - The party was offered free lodging at the keep

### Evening in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]

- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] suggested getting drinks to unwind
  - They visited [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hafjorg\|Hafjorg]]'s shop, hoping to invite him and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]]
  - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hafjorg\|Hafjorg]], his voice quiet but firm, declined: "I want to mourn them in peace," and gently closed the door
- At the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Amber_Keg\|Amber Keg]] tavern:
  - They found a celebration in progress marking the changing season and toasting to being alive
  - The group joined in, raising mugs for [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild]], and all those lost in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]
  - For a few hours, grief gave way to memory, and memory to song
- Before retiring, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] checked a noticeboard
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] spent the next day or two in her quarters practicing lightning meditation
- For the first time in many nights, the sky above [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] was clear
