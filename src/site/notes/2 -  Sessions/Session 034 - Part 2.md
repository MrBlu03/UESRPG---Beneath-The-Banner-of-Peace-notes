# Session 034 - Part 2 - The Silver Road Takeover, Royal Summons & The Winter Sled

## Summary
A continuation of Session 34 in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] breach Vaelin Marr's private office, slamming the door into [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] (disguised as bounty hunter Danel Serrano), who reveals the guild's coffers were emptied and that Vaelin fled to coordinate with [[Info/Quests/Majorn_the_Ancient_and_the_Volkihar_Threat\|Majorn the Ancient]]'s vampires in Eastmarch. Meanwhile, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] recovers at the Kjalgrad library after her alley fight, receiving healing from Head Librarian Anise and being directed by the Vestige to help at the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Blackfoot]] soup kitchen at the Eastern Gate. At the camp, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] entertains the refugee children, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] infiltrates in civilian clothes and tests the stew, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] delivers the paymaster's ledger to Hedrek Blackfoot. Recovered letters prove Silver Road Guildmaster Jorvan Haliksson's treason with Clan Shatter-Shield; he is executed in a traditional Weregild trial by combat by guard Ranvig. A royal messenger hawk arrives with an urgent missive from [[Queen_Freydis\|Queen Freydis]], commanding the Fangs of Fjelhorst to return to Mistveil Keep in Riften by the 29th of Evening Star for the Old Life and New Life councils. The next morning, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] haul a makeshift timber sled north with [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Rhymefang]] to retrieve Durov's 100 kg portable forge from the frozen Camonna Tong campsite before the party packs up at the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Barrow_of_the_Oath-Breaker_King\|Barrow]] to march south.

## Detailed Recap

### Vaelin Marr's Office & The Vestige
- **Breaching the Office**:
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** picks the lock to Vaelin Marr's second-floor private study in the Silver Road Guild House.
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** breaches with his tower shield raised, inadvertently slamming the door into someone hiding behind it and drawing a sharp grunt of pain.
  - The lurker is **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]**, operating incognito in his bounty hunter persona, **Danel Serrano**.
- **Comparing Notes**:
  - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] was hired by the "rats and vermin" of the **[[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Lower_Lodge_Network\|Lower Lodge Network]]** to sweep the study under strict orders of confidentiality.
  - He notes that if the local criminal network learns the party got the intelligence before he delivered it, suspicion of betrayal would fall on him.
  - The office was found thoroughly scrubbed: bookshelves cleared, records taken, and the treasury vault completely emptied.
  - Unlike the ransacked lower guildhall, Vaelin's private quarters were methodically sanitized days prior.
- **Recovered Evidence & Intelligence**:
  - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] produces personal letters he pried from beneath the floorboards under Vaelin's desk.
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** attempts to pickpocket the letters from his coat while **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** makes small talk; the Vestige bats her claw away without breaking stride.
  - **Syndicate Trail**: Vaelin Marr and the **[[Info/Quests/Coiled_Serpent_Conspiracy\|Coiled Path]]** fled either west into the Rift or north into Eastmarch.
  - **Vampire Alliance**: The syndicate is collaborating with the remnants of **[[Info/Quests/Majorn_the_Ancient_and_the_Volkihar_Threat\|Majorn the Ancient]]'s** vampire coven, who retreated into Eastmarch's volcanic sulfur wastes and geysers near Windhelm.
- **Survival & Contract Warnings**:
  - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] warns the party of **lake-effect snow** off Lake Honrich: advancing blizzard walls that can bury Riften under meters of snow in hours and reduce visibility to under 200 paces.
  - Confirms he will remain in Kjalgrad until the binding contract between [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and the imprisoned Scaafin/Dremora steward in the barrow expires.

### Periwyn's Sanctuary at the Library
- **Aftermath of the Alley Ambush**:
  - One hour earlier, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** limped through Kjalgrad's streets after being ambushed and beaten by four armed crooks in an alleyway.
  - Channeled basic self-healing spells along the retreat to patch lacerations and cuts.
- **Sanctuary with Anise**:
  - Arrived bloodied at the **Kjalgrad Library**.
  - **Head Librarian Anise** took her into a secluded rear annex housing carved wooden shrines to Nordic deities (Kyne).
  - Anise treated her with restorative magic for twenty minutes, mending broken ribs and cuts to bring her back to full vitality.
- **Sorting History Stacks**:
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] offered to help sort books to take a break from adventuring.
  - Reshelved Nordic history texts, reading passages on King Olaf One-Eye trapping Numinex in Dragonsreach and treatises on **[[Sanguine\|Sanguine]]**.
- **The Vestige's Tip**:
  - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] approached in a heavy fur mercenary disguise, inquiring about Silver Road trade treaties.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] recognized his voice.
  - The Vestige advised her to head out the Eastern Gate to the **[[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Blackfoot]]** camp, noting that a Knight of the Circle would provide valuable public relations at their soup kitchen.

### The Blackfoot Encampment & Soup Kitchen
- **The Camp Setup**:
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** exited the Eastern Gate and reached the Blackfoot camp: multiple hearths, bubbling cauldrons of hot stew, guards, and children having a snowball fight.
  - Met **Hedrek Blackfoot**, who presented **[[Queen_Freydis\|Queen Freydis]]'s royal decree** authorizing the Blackfoot Merchants Guild to absorb Silver Road holdings.
  - Hedrek explained that [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] were distributing wage arrears and letters to guard families to enable a peaceful transition.
- **Periwyn at the Soup Kitchen**:
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] embraced the role, helping carry potato crates, gathering five refugee children in line, and dishing out hot broth.
  - Joined the children in their snowball fight against the garrison guards, smiling and laughing openly.
- **Durov and Onasha's Deliveries**:
  - Arrived at the Eastern Gate on the wagon pulled by **[[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Rhymefang]]** (1400 kg draft horse).
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** delivered a letter to Guard Commander **Baedig** from his wife.
  - Spoke privately with guard **Ranvig**, passing along a message from his mother and confirming Durov had repaired her broken home door. Ranvig was deeply touched and had the portcullis raised.
  - Drove the wagon out to the Blackfoot encampment.

### The Snowball War & Durov Unmasked
- **Snowball Ambush**:
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** spotted [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] laughing with the children and launched a surprise snowball; [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] deftly leaned aside.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] egged on the children; a boy charged with a large snowball. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] attempted a tackle, slipped in the slush, and faceplanted into a snowdrift with snow wedged on her snout.
- **Durov in Civilian Clothes**:
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** stepped into a tent, removed his heavy plate armor, helmet, and visor, and re-emerged in plain woolen civilian clothes.
  - Revealed as a broad-jawed, athletic Colovian veteran under thirty with short-cropped dark hair.
  - Walked up to the soup line incognito and tasted the stew.
  - **Culinary Palate**: Accurately named every ingredient by taste: potatoes, carrots, black pepper, thick cream, and wild hare.
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s Reaction**: Noticed his posture, stature, and hair. Realized it was Durov pretending to be a random traveler, but kept quiet to tease him later.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] slipped away and donned his plate armor again.
  - Guard families arrived for soup, including **Gretka** (the paymaster's wife), Baedig's wife, and Ranvig's mother.

### Standoff, Embezzlement Evidence & The Weregild Trial
- **The Standoff**:
  - Commander **Baedig** arrived leading city guard contingents alongside Guildmaster **Jorvan Haliksson**.
  - **Sylla** sheltered the children inside the main pavilion and copied the paymaster's ledger.
- **Durov's Report to Hedrek**:
  - **Good News**: Silver Road is no longer an operational threat; Vaelin Marr has fled.
  - **Bad News**: The underground vault was completely cleaned out; zero gold left in coffers.
  - **Resolution**: Delivered the paymaster's ledger. Hedrek confirmed the Blackfoot Guild would cover all guard wage arrears directly from merchant funds.
- **Jorvan's Defense & The Incriminating Letters**:
  - Jorvan Haliksson claimed he had been in an alcoholic stupor for weeks and had no knowledge of recent corruption.
  - Danel Serrano arrived and handed over the letters salvaged from Vaelin's office.
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s Handwriting Analysis**: Using her photographic memory, Onasha confirmed the handwriting, flourishes, and seals perfectly matched Jorvan's authentic signature.
  - **Letter Contents**:
    - Endorsed keeping Kjalgrad's populace in poverty to maintain profit margins.
    - Ordered guard wages withheld.
    - Formally authorized Vaelin Marr to liquidate and empty the guild treasury.
- **Jorvan's Confession**:
  - Cornered by evidence, Jorvan admitted Vaelin Marr promised him "order, stability, and control" under an overarching "Correction."
  - In exchange for the treasury, Jorvan was promised total rule over Kjalgrad, backed by **Clan Shatter-Shield** of Windhelm.
- **The Weregild Execution**:
  - The guards demanded traditional Nord justice: a **Weregild** (trial by combat).
  - Guard **Ranvig** dropped a broadsword before Jorvan's feet.
  - When Jorvan raised the blade, Ranvig lunged and ran him through the chest in a single swift thrust.
  - Guards carried Jorvan's body into the woods.
  - The tension dissolved into a camp-wide snowball fight between guards and children, with [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] planting his shield as a bunker wall.

### Royal Summons from Queen Freydis
- **The Messenger Hawk**:
  - A large royal messenger hawk landed directly on [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s planted tower shield.
  - Carried a sealed vellum dispatch from **[[Queen_Freydis\|Queen Freydis]]** addressed to the **Fangs of Fjelhorst** ([[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]).
- **Contents of the Royal Dispatch**:
  - Acknowledged Jarl Gorm's praise regarding their deeds in Falderstad.
  - Requested an honest eyewitness account of Kjalgrad: who truly rules (Jarl vs. Guild), whether Dunmer are accepted, and if the people would tolerate an appointed Jarl from outside.
  - **The Summons**: Ordered the party to return south to Riften, arriving at **Mistveil Keep by the 29th of Evening Star** (the day before Old Life).
  - Demanded their presence at the royal table for Old Life (30th) and New Life (1st), and in the war room to deliver full reports on Falderstad, Tear, and the Coiled Serpent.
  - **Ominous Warning**: The Queen noted she sent warnings regarding the Serpent to every hold in Skyrim, but only *two* answered: *"I am not a woman given to dread. I am giving myself that liberty now."*
  - Missive was dated 9th of Evening Star, 2E 600, having taken roughly 8-10 days to reach them through severe mountain blizzards.

### Preparations & Retrieving the Travel Forge
- **Evening Logistics**:
  - Facing a 10-day horseback trek through worsening winter conditions, the party prepared for immediate departure.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] alerted **Tarja** of their departure and purchased healing potions and bomb reagents from **Grandma Mumu**.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] insisted on recovering his travel forge from the northern woods before leaving.
- **Morning Sled Run (Next Day)**:
  - Continued heavy snowfall overnight.
  - Leaving [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] to pack the wagon at the **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Barrow_of_the_Oath-Breaker_King\|Barrow]]**, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] took **[[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Rhymefang]]** north with a repurposed timber tabletop used as a makeshift sled.
  - Reached the burnt **Camonna Tong campsite**:
    - Found the frozen pyre of 9 charred Dunmer corpses preserved beneath ice.
    - Pinned against the cliff was the scout [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] had brutally interrogated (jaw burned, gut punctured, 16 vertebrae shattered). [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] averted her eyes in visible discomfort and shame.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] loaded his **100 kg portable forge and anvil** onto the sled.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Rhymefang]] hauled the sled back through the snowdrifts with ease.
- **Departure Readiness**:
  - Returned to the Barrow by early afternoon.
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] collected [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s perimeter wolf traps and finished loading provisions onto the cart.
  - The party stood fully prepared to begin the winter march south toward Riften via Chaos Crossing and Fort Greenwall.

## NPCs Encountered (Summary)
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] (Danel Serrano)**: Infiltrated Vaelin Marr's study for the Lower Lodge; salvaged incriminating letters; advised Periwyn to aid the soup kitchen; cautioned the party regarding lake-effect snow and Majorn's vampires.
- **Hedrek Blackfoot**: Head of the Blackfoot Merchants Guild in Kjalgrad; orchestrated the civic takeover under Queen Freydis's royal writ; pledged merchant gold to pay guard arrears.
- **Sylla**: Blackfoot lieutenant; sheltered children in the camp pavilion and copied the paymaster's master ledger.
- **Jorvan Haliksson**: Nominal Guildmaster of the Silver Road; exposed for conspiring with Clan Shatter-Shield and draining guild coffers; slain in Weregild trial by combat by Ranvig.
- **Commander Baedig**: Captain of the Kjalgrad city guard; accepted Blackfoot surrender terms and back wages following receipt of his wife's letter.
- **Ranvig**: Veteran city guardsman; thanked Durov for repairing his mother's door; executed Jorvan Haliksson in trial by combat.
- **Head Librarian Anise**: Curator of the Kjalgrad Library; healed Periwyn's injuries using Nordic healing arts in the Kyne shrine annex.
- **[[Queen_Freydis\|Queen Freydis]]**: Queen of Skyrim and Jarl of Riften; summoned the party via messenger hawk to Mistveil Keep for the Old Life / New Life councils.
- **Gretka**: Wife of the Silver Road paymaster; reunited with guard families at the soup kitchen.
- **Grandma Mumu**: Kjalgrad alchemical merchant; sold healing draughts and bomb-crafting reagents to Onasha.
- **Tarja**: Lower Lodge network contact in Kjalgrad; briefed by Onasha on the party's departure for Riften.
