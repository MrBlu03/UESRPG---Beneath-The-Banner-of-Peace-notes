# Session 016 - The Hunt for the Skinstealer

> **DUROV HAS A PORTENT LUCKY ROLL (69) TO BE USED WHENEVER**

## Summary
The party wakes before dawn to join [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]'s organized hunt for the supernatural creature terrorizing the [[Info/Locations/Northern_Forest\|Northern_Forest]]. Meeting at the market, they learn the creature is called the Skinstealer and has been active for 3-4 weeks. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]], a clever mage, identifies the Othala rune as meaning "heritage" or "inheritance," suggesting the creature is claiming the land. The hunting party - including [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]], [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]], [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]], [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Ingrin\|Ingrin]], [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]], and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]] - ventures deep into the cursed forest to find a grotesque clearing where bones and human skins are arranged in ritual patterns. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] identifies this as a shrine to the Ragged Lord, an aspect of Hircine associated with mockery and hunter's madness. On the return journey, the Skinstealer traps them in a supernatural maze where [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]] is killed. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] uses fire salts to break the enchantment, revealing the creature fears Kyne's flame. The Skinstealer attacks [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] at the forest edge, but [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] shields her, taking the blow. The party returns to plan a final confrontation with fire-based tactics. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] make a pact to help each other with their respective revenge quests - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] reveals she must save a young girl from House Dres slavery.

## Detailed Recap

### Dawn - 5:00 AM Preparation

#### Morning Routines
The party woke simultaneously in the pre-dawn darkness:
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Woke groggy, drinking in the morning, dipped her head in a bucket of water to wake up
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: also used cold water to wake up fully
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Prepared tea for the group
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]**: Unusually energetic despite the early hour

### Early Morning - The Market Rally

#### The Hunting Bell
The market streets were nearly empty in the early hours. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] stood on a small podium tolling the hunting bell, signaling the gathering of hunters. Around her, torn animal hides left to dry hung as grim evidence of the creature's presence.

#### The Hunting Party Assembly
Six hunters gathered with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]:
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]**: Clever older mage who studied at Winterhold
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]]**: Woodsman
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Ingrin\|Ingrin]]**: Huntress who had previously found the clearing with bones
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]]**: Huntress
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]]**: Hunter, well-known meat seller in the market
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]]**: Initially present but removed from expedition

#### Hoki's Departure
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] worked together to dissuade [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]] from joining the dangerous mission:
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] gave him a stern talking to
- Her demeanor shifted completely from her usual cold sternness to warm and soft when speaking with him
- He was convinced to stay behind for his own safety
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] went to chat with [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]] while [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] handled [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]]

#### Pre-Hunt Briefing
**[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]'s Strategy**: Gather all known information about the creature and exploit its weaknesses

[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] dragged [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] by the arm to show [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] and the group the rune he had carved into his bracer.

##### The Othala Rune Analysis
**[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]'s Identification:**
- The rune is called "Othala"
- Meaning: Heritage, inheritance, bloodline, burden
- **Theory**: The Skinstealer is claiming the land as its own inheritance
- **Alternative Theory**: The creature is trying to reclaim the land from humans because it believes it owns it
- **Timeline**: The Skinstealer has been active for 3-4 weeks
- The rune suggests the creature has inherited this land and its sins

#### Known Facts About the Skinstealer
The hunting party compiled their intelligence:
- Hangs up animal and human hides like trophies or banners
- Carves the Othala rune into the back of trees throughout its territory
- Travels with a pack of 3 wolves
- The wolves appear afraid and follow by compulsion, not loyalty
- Possesses insane strength
- Has 45 cm long ebony-sharp claws
- A clearing with bones exists several hours into its territory
- "Just appears" - materializes out of thin air between the wolves
- Likely commands the wolves to kill

#### War Paint Ritual
One of the hunters applied woad (traditional war paint) to everyone joining the expedition. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] turned away from the party to open his visor for the application.

## The Hunt Begins

### Journey into the Northern Forest

#### Environmental Changes (First Hour)
As the party ventured deeper into the forest:
- The air felt wrong, too empty
- Footsteps sounded far too loud, as if the forest was amplifying sound
- Tracks covered the ground going in all directions
- Freshly added totems dotted the landscape
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] confirmed theories about the rune's significance

#### The Frozen Stream
A half-frozen stream appeared with disturbing features:
- Surface gleaming red - hundreds of iron nails driven into trees around the bank
- One totem was half-turned to ash, as if struck by lightning
- Nearby snow stained red with strange sap
- The sap had blood consistency but wasn't from a plant
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] licked it and discovered:
  - It was warm
  - It was the Skinstealer's blood leaking from the totems

[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Runa\|Runa]] ran his hand through the water and cleansed his face (did not die, passed his observe check).

#### Deepening Corruption (Hours Later)
- A smell of copper and rot hit the party
- The Othala rune's specific meaning became clearer: inheritance, blood, burden
- Confirmed theory that the creature inherited this land and its sins

##### Periwyn's Necromantic Awareness
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] felt an overwhelming sense of death through her necromancy abilities:
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] noticed her reaction
- Revealed he studied at Winterhold and knows necromancy has its uses
- They agreed to keep this knowledge between themselves

### The Clearing (11:00 AM - Noon)

#### Arrival at the Shrine
The party arrived at a clearing with horrifying features:
- **Ground**: No snow, no grass
- **Center**: Monolith of bones fused together with permafrost
- **Hides**: Human skins hanging around the clearing like banners
- **Arrangement**: Bones arranged in concentric circles
- **Skins**: Patched together in a grotesque tapestry, layered like a mosaic

#### Torik's Identification
**[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]'s Lore Knowledge (successful check):**
- Recognized the patterns from old texts at Winterhold
- Identified as markings of the **Ragged Lord**
- **Ragged Lord**: An aspect of Hircine
- **Associated with**: Mockery and hunter's madness
- **Nature**: The creature creates a shrine to itself
- **Weakness**: Gets hurt by fire easily
- **Torik's Preparation**: Has fire salts with him

## The Retreat and the Forest's Trap

### Return Journey - The Vanishing Trail
On the way back, the party noticed disturbing changes:
- Their tracks were completely gone, as if they had never stepped there
- The air grew thicker over time
- The forest didn't become sparser despite walking toward the edge
- They were somehow walking in circles despite heading straight

#### Alma's Impatience
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]] suggested they shouldn't sit around too long (noted as a poor suggestion given the circumstances).

### The Skinstealer's Maze

#### Ingrin's Panic
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Ingrin\|Ingrin]] stopped suddenly and asked, "Do you hear anything?"
- The party heard her voice coming from the trees ahead, whispering "over here"
- She panicked and tried to run
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] tackled her to calm her down and prevent her from being separated

#### Bjorr's Death
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]] suddenly vanished from the group
- A snap and muffled shout echoed through the forest
- The party found [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Bjorr\|Bjorr]]'s corpse suspended above them
- His throat was cut, body already freezing
- This was the first confirmed kill of the Skinstealer during the hunt

#### The Infinite Forest
Despite continuing to walk, the forest didn't end:
- Trees remained thick
- They were trapped in circles
- Traditional navigation was useless

### Torik's Ritual - Breaking the Enchantment

#### The Fire Salt Ritual
[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] asked everyone to stand in a circle around him:
- Grabbed his staff and placed it down
- Knelt in the snow
- Placed a brazier and grabbed fire salts
- Lit them in orange flame (blue fire falling from trees in one account)

#### The Forest's Reaction
For a heartbeat, the forest screamed in a loud, pained howl:
- The hanging hides started swaying violently
- The air trembled
- Everyone saw a silhouette recoiling from the trees
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] coughed up blood but insisted he was "alright"

#### Key Discovery
**The Skinstealer fears the flame of Kyne** - this fire-based magic can harm it

### Escape to the Forest Edge
As dusk approached:
- The lights of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] became visible
- The party hurried toward the forest boundary
- A howl sounded very close behind them

#### The Final Attack
At the forest edge, the Skinstealer struck:
- It slashed at [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], who wanted to investigate it (2-3 meters away)
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] jumped between them, back turned to the creature
- His shield blocked the claws
- Both [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] were flung forward 2 meters
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] landed on their backs
- The Skinstealer looked at them, howled once more, and disappeared
