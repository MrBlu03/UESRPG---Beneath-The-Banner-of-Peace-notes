# Session 024

## Summary

The party finally investigated the **northeast warehouse** marked on [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Valen Marr]]'s map, disguised in **[[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] regalia** to frame the opposing faction for the intrusion. Travelling three hours northeast through the forest, they reached the warehouse — a sturdy building backed against a cliff — and found it guarded by four armed men plus a hidden fifth figure. Using **chameleon tinctures**, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] scouted the perimeter and took position on the rooftop. The party opened with **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s frenzy bomb**, turning two guards against each other, and fought the remaining guards in a brutal extended combat. After the last guard fell, a figure emerged from inside: a tall, horned **Daedra** — a Scathing — who revealed itself to be the warehouse manager and a servant of **[[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]]**. It allowed the party to search freely in exchange for its release, and departed. Inside, the party discovered **enslaved warehouse workers**, a cache of **ritual supplies** matching the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Warehouse_Seven\|Warehouse 7]] shipments [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] had previously flagged, and a **ledger** containing shipment records, names of the disappeared, and directions to a **main facility** several hours further north. The enslaved workers were freed and directed to Strider's sewer refuge. The party chose not to burn the warehouse — at [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s urging — to preserve the shipping chain as a future intelligence trail.

## Detailed Recap

### Morning — Frost Gate Hall

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** woke early and placed a small piece of bread by the window before heading downstairs. She raised the topic of the **Daedric stone** recovered from the Jarl's steward's cellar. She was insistent — it was potentially tied to a Daedric ritual, possibly time-sensitive, and needed to be handed to their contact as soon as possible. The group agreed: finding their [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] contact and following the riddle would happen soon.

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** woke shortly after, complaining of the cold before retreating downstairs. They shared a simple breakfast of roasted vegetables prepared by **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Orgnar\|Orgnar]]**. The brimstone gold refusal problem came up at breakfast when **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** tried (jokingly) to pay with it — [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Orgnar\|Orgnar]] refused. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] noted the tavern had fewer regulars than usual: hunters specifically had largely stopped coming, replaced with generic-looking individuals chatting up those with grievances. He spotted three familiar suspicious-looking individuals — two Nords and one Imperial in their thirties — scattered around the common room, watching the space.

Afterwards, the group met at the stables where **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** was surrounded by crumpled parchment from failed attempts to sketch [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]]. The conversation grew heavier:
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** spoke of an encounter with a hagraven, a threat left unresolved. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] remarked such things rarely stayed dormant.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** filled [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] in on a bounty completed in her absence.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** relayed her investigation into a house tied unofficially to the steward. She found a Daedric ritual site hidden beneath, human skin, a full skeleton, and notes detailing the imitation of Nord mannerisms and speech. Combined with [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s findings in the Jarl's quarters, the grim thought took shape: the Jarl might no longer be himself, or something else entirely.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** also relayed the findings from [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023b\|Session 23b]] — the **horned mask letter** found on the bandit mage. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] identified the symbol: *"a depiction of the famed mask of [[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]]"*.

The group confirmed the plan for the day: **disguised infiltration of the northeast warehouse** from Valen's map, using [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] regalia retrieved from the guild cart hidden in the sewers, to frame the Tong for the break-in.

### Late Morning — Sewers and Disguise

The party collected their **[[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] disguises** from the hidden cart in the sewer: a mask and a long cowl-style garment draped over one shoulder. They exited through the sewer tunnel that led outside the city walls, skirting the guards and watchtowers.

- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] tucked her tail under the cowl — uncomfortable, but necessary
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] helped [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] manage the garment due to the Khajiit's anatomy
- The party set off northeast on foot, carrying their disguises

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** briefly checked her **wolf traps** set near the northeastern treeline on the way out — none had been sprung. The forest remained still.

---

### Late Morning / Midday — Approaching the Warehouse

After approximately two and a half to three hours of travel through increasingly dense and snowy forest, the party arrived at the foot of a small hill. Through the trees they saw the warehouse: a large block-shaped building **backed against a cliff face**, with rudimentary fortifications and a cart outside.

**Scouting:**

- Both **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** and **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** drank **chameleon tinctures** (2-minute duration) and circled the building
- They counted **four guards** visible — two directly patrolling, two near the cart and entrance
- A fifth figure was briefly visible near the entrance but hard to place
- The warehouse had no windows; the roof could be accessed by crawling from the cliff above, roughly 1.5m clearance

**Tactical decision:**

The party elected to climb onto the roof via the cliff access and position themselves at the edge for a height advantage, then use a **frenzy bomb** to cluster and confuse the guards before engaging in direct combat.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] would approach from ground level to distract while the others took the high ground.

---

### Combat — The Northeast Warehouse

**Encounter:** 4 guards, including **two horned Daedric creatures** acting as overseers.

**Opening:**

- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] opened with **Chain Lightning** the moment she had line of sight, hitting the guards. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] advanced openly, closing the distance with deliberate steps.
- **Another of the horned creatures emerged.** [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] threw a **frenzy bomb** between the two horned creatures — the two creatures turned on each other in a burst of violence, buying the party time.
- From above, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] fired silver fire arrows from stealth, killing one guard with a precise sneak attack through the back.

**Round-by-round summary:**

- The frenzied Daedric creatures dealt significant damage to each other. One of the horned beings fell. The other, shaking off the frenzy, retreated into the warehouse.
- A crossbow-wielding guard on elevated ground fired at [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] — she **evaded** successfully; a later bolt struck her for **8 damage** before she drank a healing potion.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] engaged guards directly: landed a critical hit dealing **9 damage to the leg** (arterial bleed, lethal), killed another with a warhammer strike to the chest — *"ribs caved in, crushing the heart"*.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] then faced a warehouse worker—unarmed, panicked, rushing him with a dagger. Rather than strike him down, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] simply tackled the man and held him there, pinning him beneath his weight.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] attempted Sunbeam but missed; chain lightning again dealt further damage to grouped targets.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] threw a **fear bomb** at one guard — he failed his willpower test and became frightened, unable to approach.

**The Remaining Scathing Emerges:**

Inside the warehouse, the remaining horned creature awaited them. It stepped out from behind the crates, revealing itself fully — a servant of the Lord of Bargains.

*"You may look through everything. I will not stop you."*

It offered a deal: The lives of the workers... in exchange for its freedom.
None of them liked it, but they accepted.

When pressed on its identity:
> *"I have my own agenda. As does my master."*
> *"Your master being—"*
> *"Come now. You haven't put two and two together yet?"*
> *"...My master. The Lord of Bargains. [[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]]."*

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** investigated (Observe roll) — the Daedra was telling the truth.

She dismissed the Sunbeam she had ready and stepped aside, letting it leave. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|The Scathing]] left without resistance, slipping past them. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] lunged after it at the last moment, but was too slow. It was gone. ([[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] had attempted Calm earlier, but the GM revealed post-combat: **Scathing are immune to mind-altering magic and toxins**, which is why the frenzy bomb earlier had only worked because the creatures played along for reasons of their own).

### The Warehouse — Discovery

**Enslaved Workers:**

Inside, the party found several warehouse workers — **not willing employees**. They bore the marks of shackles. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] noted they had been coerced or enslaved, likely by the Guild or the Daedra's operation.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] ushered them out firmly, asking one who seemed like the natural leader where they had been hired from: *"The [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Silver Road Guild]] house."* She directed them to the **city sewers** and told them to seek out **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Tarja\|Tarja]]** (Strider), giving them the memorised sewer map and saying to invoke her name for safe harbour.

She then sprinted to catch up to the group, as she worried whether the workers would be safe returning to [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] given the city's current state.

**The Ledger:**

Tucked behind a crate in a corner of the warehouse, the party found a **warehouse ledger**. Key contents:

- **Standard inventory entries**: provisions, medical supplies, general cargo — largely unremarkable
- **Ritual shipment confirmations**: matching the same crates [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] had flagged at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Warehouse_Seven\|Warehouse 7]] — coin molds, chalk, gemstones, bone and sinew
- **"Selection cargo" entries**: with a handful of **names matching records of the city's disappeared** — largely merchants and people of means, including some names that appeared in the guest list for events at the **Jarl's Hall**
- **Payment records to bandits**: confirming the financial connection between the Guild's operation and the eastern road bandit group the party cleared in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023b\|Session 23b]]
- **Direction to the main facility**: a location *"several hours further north"* of the current warehouse — where the shipments were being routed

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** used **Clairvoyance** on the crates to imprint their appearance for future tracking; she argued persuasively against burning the warehouse immediately — if the crates continue moving, they could trace the full supply chain to the main facility.

**Other Loot:**

- Medical supplies (bandages, basic kit — refilled [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s kit)
- Some silks and provisions taken from the general stores to simulate a robbery
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|The Scathing]]'s **armor** (looted from the body it left behind — composition unknown, but taken)
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|The Scathing]]'s **bronze sword** (taken by [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]])
- A **wooden crossbow** recovered and taken by [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] to learn over time
- One **orichalcum arrow** recovered from the Daedra's body
- One Scathing **horn** extracted (survival roll with [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s aid) — the other broke during extraction. The horn is intact and could be carved into a vessel or used as an alchemical component

**Post-combat healing:**
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] cast **Healing Touch** on herself (recovering HP after the crossbow bolt), and a brief pulse on [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|The Scathing]]'s **body** was picked at by a crow when the party went to loot it — [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] approached carefully. Whether it was a [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] crow or ordinary was not established

**The party chose not to burn the warehouse.** [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] was deeply reluctant but accepted [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s argument — tracking the supply chain to the main facility was worth more than destroying this staging point. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] extracted a promise: *"When we find the person behind this, I get to burn the building with them in it."* [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] agreed.

## Important Revelations

- **[[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]] is directly involved**: A Scathing — a Daedra in service to [[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]] — was physically managing this warehouse. This is not a peripheral cult; the Prince's servants are embedded in the operation. The horned mask bandit letter, the coin molds, the ritual supplies, the stone — all are now connected to a single patron.
- **The disappeared are "selection cargo"**: The ledger lists names of missing persons (merchants, guests at the Jarl's events) under the heading "selection cargo." These people were deliberately selected, likely for ritual use, and are being transported north.
- **The main facility is further north**: A primary site exists several hours north of the warehouse. This is almost certainly where selected people and ritual supplies are being taken. It has not yet been investigated.
- **The Guild paid the eastern road bandits**: The ledger confirms the Silver Road was financing the bandit operation disrupted in [[2 -  Sessions/Session 023b\|Session 23b]], via the same mage bearing the [[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]] symbol. The Guild, the cult, and the bandits are one operation.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|The Scathing]] played along with frenzy**: Two guards fought each other during [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s frenzy bomb — but [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|the Scathing]] (immune to the effect) apparently allowed this or encouraged it. Why? It may have been content to thin its own guards rather than fight the party directly.
- **[[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] cover may hold**: The party infiltrated in Tong disguise. With the guards dead and the commoners freed, no one inside the warehouse survived to clearly identify the party. The Guild should blame the Tong for this intrusion — deepening the manufactured conflict [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] set up.
- **The three suspicious men at the inn**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] has now spotted the same trio (two Nords, one Imperial, thirties, leather-clad, suspiciously generic) across multiple mornings. They watch the common room. Their faction and purpose are unknown.
