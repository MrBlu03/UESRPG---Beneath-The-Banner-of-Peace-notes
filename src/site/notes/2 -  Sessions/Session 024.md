# Session 024

## Summary

The party finally investigated the **northeast warehouse** marked on Valen Marr's map, disguised in **Camonna Tong regalia** to frame the opposing faction for the intrusion. Travelling three hours northeast through the forest, they reached the warehouse — a sturdy building backed against a cliff — and found it guarded by four armed men plus a hidden fifth figure. Using **chameleon tinctures**, Onasha and Kirya scouted the perimeter and took position on the rooftop. The party opened with **Onasha's frenzy bomb**, turning two guards against each other, and fought the remaining guards in a brutal extended combat. After the last guard fell, a figure emerged from inside: a tall, horned **Daedra** — a Scathing — who revealed itself to be the warehouse manager and a servant of **Clavicus Vile**. It allowed the party to search freely in exchange for its release, and departed. Inside, the party discovered **enslaved warehouse workers**, a cache of **ritual supplies** matching the Warehouse 7 shipments Durov had previously flagged, and a **ledger** containing shipment records, names of the disappeared, and directions to a **main facility** several hours further north. The enslaved workers were freed and directed to Strider's sewer refuge. The party chose not to burn the warehouse — at Periwyn's urging — to preserve the shipping chain as a future intelligence trail.

## Detailed Recap

### Morning — Frost Gate Hall

**Onasha** woke early and immediately raised the topic of the **Daedric stone** recovered from the Jarl's steward's cellar. She was insistent — it was potentially tied to a Daedric ritual, possibly time-sensitive, and needed to be handed to their contact as soon as possible. The group agreed: finding their Blackfeather Court contact and following the riddle would happen soon.

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** woke shortly after and went to the stables. The brimstone gold refusal problem came up at breakfast when **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** tried (jokingly) to pay with it — the innkeeper refused. Durov noted the tavern had fewer regulars than usual: hunters specifically had largely stopped coming. He also spotted three familiar suspicious-looking individuals — the same two Nords and one Imperial in their thirties he had seen over the past few days — scattered around the common room, watching the space. They were dressed to blend in but clearly generic; Durov noted they knew each other but were pretending not to.

**Onasha** and **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** sat at the bar together, sharing honey-glazed carrots and mulled wine. Kirya asked the barkeep for gossip — more disappearances, and a small gathering at the southern gate the previous evening where residents had protested the Guild tariffs, some baiting guards into aggression.

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** relayed the findings from Session 23b to Onasha — the **horned mask letter** found on the bandit mage. Onasha read it and immediately identified the symbol: *"a depiction of the famed mask of Clavicus Vile"* — a Daedric Prince whose cult was reportedly active somewhere in Kjalgrad. She connected it to the Daedric stone, and the group half-jokingly discussed slipping the letter into their contact's pocket when they next met him.

The group confirmed the plan for the day: **disguised infiltration of the northeast warehouse** from Valen's map, using Camonna Tong regalia retrieved from the guild cart hidden in the sewers, to frame the Tong for the break-in.

The party collected their **Camonna Tong disguises** from the hidden cart in the sewer: a mask and a long cowl-style garment draped over one shoulder. They exited through the sewer tunnel that led outside the city walls, skirting the guards and watchtowers.

- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] tucked her tail under the cowl — uncomfortable, but necessary

**Kirya** checked one of her **Survival traps** set near the northeastern treeline on the way out — it had been sprung but empty; the animal saw it.

### Late Morning / Midday — Approaching the Warehouse

After approximately two and a half to three hours of travel through increasingly dense and snowy forest, the party arrived at the foot of a small hill. Through the trees they saw the warehouse: a large block-shaped building **backed against a cliff face**, with rudimentary fortifications and a cart outside.

**Scouting:**

- Both **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** and **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** drank **chameleon tinctures** (2-minute duration) and circled the building
- They counted **four guards** visible — two directly patrolling, two near the cart and entrance
- A fifth figure was briefly visible near the entrance but hard to place
- The warehouse had no windows; the roof could be accessed by crawling from the cliff above, roughly 1.5m clearance

**Tactical decision:**

The party elected to climb onto the roof via the cliff access and position themselves at the edge for a height advantage, then use a **frenzy bomb** to cluster and confuse the guards before engaging in direct combat.

[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] would approach from ground level to distract while the others took the high ground.

### Combat — The Northeast Warehouse

**Encounter:** 4 guards (two Nords and two others), plus a hidden warehouse manager later revealed to be a Daedra

**Opening:**

- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] opened with **Chain Lightning** the moment she had line of sight, hitting the first guard (2 damage, failed evade)
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] threw a **frenzy bomb** between two grouped guards — one failed the willpower test and went berserk, turning on his ally
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] advanced on the remaining guards while the frenzied guard began attacking his companion

**Round-by-round summary:**

- The frenzied guard dealt significant damage to his ally — **14 damage** in one strike — nearly killing him outright before the frenzy ran out
- A crossbow-wielding guard on elevated ground fired at [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] — she **evaded** successfully; a later bolt struck her for **8 damage** before she drank a healing potion
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] fired silver fire arrows from stealth on the roof, killing one guard with a **sneak attack through the back** (arrow struck body, killed him as he fell)
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] engaged guards directly: landed a critical hit dealing **9 damage to the leg** (arterial bleed, lethal), killed another with a warhammer strike to the chest — *"ribs caved in, crushing the heart"* — max damage, 7 points straight through armor
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] attempted Sunbeam but missed; chain lightning again dealt 2 further damage to grouped targets
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] threw a **fear bomb** at one guard — he failed his willpower test and became frightened, unable to approach
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] used **Intimidate (Persuade via Strength)** to force a guard's attack to miss — same mechanic as Session 23b
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] restrained the final **warehouse worker commoner** who had been coerced into fighting; the man barely attacked and ultimately just stopped once combat ended

**The Scathing Emerges:**

As the last guard fell, a figure walked calmly out of the warehouse interior: **a tall humanoid Daedra** — approximately Altmer height, large curved horns, mostly humanoid but unmistakably Daedric. The party had not seen its kind before.

*"You may look through everything. I will not stop you."*

When pressed on why:

> *"I have my own agenda. As does my master."*
> *"Your master being—"*
> *"Come now. You haven't put two and two together yet?"*
> *"...My master. The Lord of Bargains. Clavicus Vile."*

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** investigated (Observe roll) — the Daedra was telling the truth.

She dismissed the Sunbeam she had ready and stepped aside, letting it leave. The Scathing walked past calmly, **gave a small wave to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** — slightly too smug — and bolted once [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] lunged after it. Kirya attempted Calm (Illusion) to stop it; the spell failed. The GM revealed post-combat: **Scathing are immune to mind-altering magic and toxins**, which is also why the frenzy bomb earlier had not forced the two guards to kill each other (they played along for reasons of their own).

### The Warehouse — Discovery

**Enslaved Workers:**

Inside, the party found several warehouse workers — **not willing employees**. They bore the marks of shackles. Onasha noted they had been coerced or enslaved, likely by the Guild or the Daedra's operation.

Kirya ushered them out firmly, asking one who seemed like the natural leader where they had been hired from: *"The Silver Road Guild house."* She directed them to the **city sewers** and told them to seek out **Strider**, giving them the memorised sewer map and saying to invoke her name for safe harbour.

She then sprinted to catch up to the group, as she worried whether the workers would be safe returning to Kjalgrad given the city's current state.

**The Ledger:**

Tucked behind a crate in a corner of the warehouse, the party found a **warehouse ledger**. Key contents:

- **Standard inventory entries**: provisions, medical supplies, general cargo — largely unremarkable
- **Ritual shipment confirmations**: matching the same crates Durov had flagged at Warehouse 7 — coin molds, chalk, gemstones, bone and sinew
- **"Selection cargo" entries**: with a handful of **names matching records of the city's disappeared** — largely merchants and people of means, including some names that appeared in the guest list for events at the **Jarl's Hall**
- **Payment records to bandits**: confirming the financial connection between the Guild's operation and the eastern road bandit group the party cleared in Session 23b
- **Direction to the main facility**: a location *"several hours further north"* of the current warehouse — where the shipments were being routed

**Periwyn** used **Clairvoyance** on the crates to imprint their appearance for future tracking; she argued persuasively against burning the warehouse immediately — if the crates continue moving, they could trace the full supply chain to the main facility.

**Other Loot:**

- Medical supplies (bandages, basic kit — refilled [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s kit)
- Some silks and provisions taken from the general stores to simulate a robbery
- The Scathing's **armor** (looted from the body it left behind — composition unknown, but taken)
- The Scathing's **bronze sword** (taken by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]])
- A **wooden crossbow** recovered and taken by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] to learn over time
- One **orichalcum arrow** recovered from the Daedra's body
- One Scathing **horn** extracted (survival roll with Kirya's aid) — the other broke during extraction. The horn is intact and could be carved into a vessel or used as an alchemical component

**Post-combat healing:**
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] cast **Healing Touch** on herself (recovering HP after the crossbow bolt), and a brief pulse on [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]
- The Scathing's **body** was picked at by a crow when the party went to loot it — Kirya approached carefully. Whether it was a Blackfeather Court crow or ordinary was not established

**The party chose not to burn the warehouse.** [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] was deeply reluctant but accepted Periwyn's argument — tracking the supply chain to the main facility was worth more than destroying this staging point. Onasha extracted a promise: *"When we find the person behind this, I get to burn the building with them in it."* Periwyn agreed.

## Important Revelations

- **Clavicus Vile is directly involved**: A Scathing — a Daedra in service to Clavicus Vile — was physically managing this warehouse. This is not a peripheral cult; the Prince's servants are embedded in the operation. The horned mask bandit letter, the coin molds, the ritual supplies, the stone — all are now connected to a single patron.
- **The disappeared are "selection cargo"**: The ledger lists names of missing persons (merchants, guests at the Jarl's events) under the heading "selection cargo." These people were deliberately selected, likely for ritual use, and are being transported north.
- **The main facility is further north**: A primary site exists several hours north of the warehouse. This is almost certainly where selected people and ritual supplies are being taken. It has not yet been investigated.
- **The Guild paid the eastern road bandits**: The ledger confirms the Silver Road was financing the bandit operation disrupted in Session 23b, via the same mage bearing the Clavicus Vile symbol. The Guild, the cult, and the bandits are one operation.
- **The Scathing played along with frenzy**: Two guards fought each other during Onasha's frenzy bomb — but the Scathing (immune to the effect) apparently allowed this or encouraged it. Why? It may have been content to thin its own guards rather than fight the party directly.
- **Camonna Tong cover may hold**: The party infiltrated in Tong disguise. With the guards dead and the commoners freed, no one inside the warehouse survived to clearly identify the party. The Guild should blame the Tong for this intrusion — deepening the manufactured conflict Onasha set up.
- **The three suspicious men at the inn**: Durov has now spotted the same trio (two Nords, one Imperial, thirties, leather-clad, suspiciously generic) across multiple mornings. They watch the common room. Their faction and purpose are unknown.
