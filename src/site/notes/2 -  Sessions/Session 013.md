# Session 013 - Journey to Falderstad


## Summary
The party acquires horses from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]] and embarks on a six-day journey from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] to [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]]. They purchase cold weather gear, encounter [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Maiq_the_Liar\|M'aiq]] the Liar speaking cryptically, witness a spectacular aurora, and meet a hunter planning a white stag hunt. Despite harsh winter weather and heavy snow, they arrive safely in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], showing the Queen's writ to gain entry. They prepare to investigate the mysterious hunters who were found frozen stiff on a clear day.

## Detailed Recap

### Departure from Riften
Cold clung to the Rift when the party rose. Upstairs in the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Hall_of_Ysmir\|Hall of Ysmir]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] returned a book to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]], who was preparing for departure to a sacred site in Eastmarch but assured their paths would cross again.

Not long after, around 8 AM, a guard summoned them to the stables, cold breath passing through his helm. Walking through [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]], they noticed the rivers had frozen over, signaling the harsh winter ahead. They first visited [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Borans_Bulwarks\|Boran's Bullwarks]] as [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] requested warmer cloaks - they purchased three bear cloaks at 75 gold each (discounted from 100) and sold 100 gold worth of bear fur plus a smaller 10 gold fur, pooling the coin into the communal coffer.

At the stables, they met [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hrovald\|Hrovald]] the stable master. The Queen personally oversaw their acquisition of horses, reminding them her envoys could not ride swayback nags trailing merchant trains.

### Horse Acquisitions
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** received **[[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Rimefang]]** - a massive iron-grey gelding with calm, commanding presence
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** received **[[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Sunsnow]]** - a dappled cream mare, light-footed and curious 
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** received **[[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Mossback]]** - a deep chestnut gelding, gentle but wary spirit
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** received **[[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]]** - a coal-black mare with fierce, restless temperament and white streak on muzzle

### Travel Day 1 (26th of Frostfall)
The fog lifted by midday, a pale sky stretching above them. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] hunted small game, returning with fox pelt and meat. Evening activities included [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] playing lute, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sketching horse harness designs with crescent moons and her family's heraldry while feeding [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]] berries. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] inquired about [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s family heraldry. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] fell asleep against her horse rather than in the tent.

### Travel Day 2 (27th of Frostfall)
Heavy fog rolled from [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Lake_Honrich\|Lake Honrich]], dampening their gear. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] woke groggy and stiff from sleeping against [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]], though frostbite spared her. By midday the fog burned away. Near camp, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] found an old cairn slumped under moss. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] wanted to watch over her, but after reassuring them, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sat by the cairn and followed the Archmagus's lessons, meditating with the dead until sleep came, despite snow drifting down.

### Travel Day 3 (28th of Frostfall)
The world glittered under hoarfrost. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] tried teaching [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Mossback]] the "stay" command, but he stubbornly followed her. Encountered [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Maiq_the_Liar\|M'aiq]] around midday, wearing orange-yellow robes and rambling about kerchiefs, dark elves, and marriage ("hands in chains"). Afternoon brought a sudden squall, with snow whipping sideways before clearing. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] attempted bear hunting unsuccessfully as snow covered tracks, gathering berries and alchemy ingredients for the horses instead.

### Travel Day 4 (29th of Frostfall)
The morning was bitter with ice fog, crystals clinging to cloaks and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s whiskers. Sun broke through by midday, though the northeast wind bit sharply. By late afternoon, they heard the ice groan across [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Lake_Honrich\|Lake Honrich]] in the distance. Evening brought a spectacular green-blue aurora that awed the entire party. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sketched the scene in her journal. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] played an upbeat tune, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] danced, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] hammered the rhythm on his armor.

### Travel Day 5 (30th of Frostfall)
Snow fell steady and thick at dawn. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] finally succeeded in training [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Mossback]] to stand still, earning him berries. At evening camp, encountered a hunter leaving offerings at a small shrine to the old gods (Fox and Hawk). The hunter revealed plans to hunt a white stag seen in the woods once the snow clears. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] offered assistance - meeting arranged at [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Wounded_Boar\|The Wounded Boar]] inn in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] through [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Denskar\|Denskar]].

### Travel Day 6 (31st of Frostfall) - Arrival at Falderstad
The dawn was savage, with every branch crusted in frost shining in the sun. Arrived at midday in -14°C weather, the valley revealing smoking lodges and hides drying on racks. The stronghold sat by the river's curve, defended by palisades and timber towers displaying banners of [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Faldar\|Clan Faldar]] (green and silver with a bold stag's head). Guards questioned them bluntly at the city gates, but admitted them once [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] showed the Queen's writ. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl Gorm]] was too deep in clan business to see them. They spent the day bonding with their mounts, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] demonstrating proper care. They retired to the inn, with [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] sharing a room to spare the coffer.
