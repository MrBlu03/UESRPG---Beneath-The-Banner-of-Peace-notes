# Session 025

## Summary

The morning after the warehouse raid, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** woke the party at the crack of dawn — insisting they act immediately on the riddle leading to the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] contact. After decoding the clues from the campaign's reference texts (the **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Widows_River\|Widow's River]]**, the **Three Brothers** standing stones, the **Barrow of the Oath Breaker King**), the party departed in snowfall, on foot, leaving their horses behind due to the icy terrain. They split at a fork in the river, with **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** using Clairvoyance to find the others, then reunited and continued together. They encountered a **Wisp Mother** floating in a frozen pond — and wisely backed away. Shortly after, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** spotted a crow leading them to a barrow carved into the mountainside. Inside, after navigating through a pitch-black Nordic ruin full of traps, a lever puzzle, a flooded corridor, and locked doors (many broken open with an axe), they knocked on the final large chamber door and were let in by **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]** — [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]], their long-sought [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] contact. The party delivered the Daedric stone and shared all gathered intelligence. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] revealed that the stone is a **memory anchor** keeping a skin-changer (a Daedric **mimicry creature**) able to maintain the Jarl's identity — and that the Jarl himself is likely already dead. The party agreed on a two-day plan: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] scouts the **main facility in the northern mountains**, while the party rests and prepares at the barrow using his alchemy lab. The session closed with the party bedding down in the barrow.

## Detailed Recap

### Early Morning — Inn, Kjalgrad

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] was awake before dawn, notes spread out and fire under her. The moment she could, she shook [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] awake — *"It's basically five, which is basically six, which is basically seven. Let's go."* [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] had apparently been up already. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] was, for once, found sleeping in her room rather than in the stables — [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] poked her until she moved.

The party gathered from memory and the campaign's reference texts to decode the **[[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] riddle**:

> *"When the Silver Gate fed the wandering road... seek not the trader's beaten trail nor hunter's path through wood and vale... walk where the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Widows_River\|widow's river]] bends... beneath the watch of brothers three whose names were lost to memory... there lies the hole no song will keep, where oath-baker king was laid to sleep... through there, seek out the door, nodes sealed tight in buried stone and hidden night..."*

The GM walked through the solution:
- **The Silver Gate** = the south gate of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]
- **The [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Widows_River\|Widow's River]]** = a small frozen stream near [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], named in historical documents for a Jarl's wife who walked into it in grief after her husband's death in war — the stream they had been passing since arriving in the city
- **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Three_Brothers\|The Three Brothers]]** = three standing stones in the mountains, referenced in *On the Early Years of the Rift* — three brothers who sealed an oath-breaking king away, leaving stones in their place
- **The Barrow** = the tomb of that oath-breaking king, located near the three stones

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] asked about using **Clairvoyance** to navigate (too vague — the spell needs a known target). The party confirmed: no horses (snowfall plus off-trail terrain = broken legs), travel on foot, leave at dawn, cover tracks not needed (wind and snow would cover them naturally).

Breakfast was on the road. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] grabbed honeyed carrots and turnips. The party stepped out into light snowfall as the sun crept up.

---

### Morning — The Widow's River and the Fork

The party exited the south gate and quickly found the small, mostly-frozen stream. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] (and others) made Observe tests — someone spotted saber cat tracks crossing the river. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]: *"We don't follow those."*

After **30–40 minutes of walking**, the stream split. No way to tell which fork was correct.

**Plan**: Split up — [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] take one fork, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] uses Clairvoyance to find [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] (who went ahead) on the other, then they link back up.

- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s fork**: led to a small dead-end pond after 15 minutes. She cast **Jack of All Trades** (reroll next test), then **Clairvoyance** targeting [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] — received a compass-like magical direction, vaguely southwest. After ~20–25 minutes, she tracked [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] down and rejoined the group following the **right fork**.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s re-entry**: appeared out of nowhere in lockstep beside [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] — [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] nearly tumbled into the river from the jump scare.

On the subject of [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s stone and the Daedric connection, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] asked **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] directly** what she knew about it. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] confirmed she'd noticed signs of it on the Jarl for a while — the stone was her priority alongside the slave operation.

---

### Mid-Morning — The Frozen Pond and the Wisp Mother

Continuing along the right fork, the party came upon a **larger frozen pond**. In the middle, a glowing, floating figure — half-transparent, draped in pale wrappings, emitting a high-pitched multi-note hum.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] identified it on a successful Observe: a **Wisp Mother** — a magical being, capable of speech and illusion, that can spawn up to six Wisps in combat.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] walked toward it anyway because she was curious. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] got one step behind her, hand ready to pull her back. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] was two steps behind that, watching.

At about 20 metres, the Wisp Mother turned toward them. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] stopped. The creature was floating, humming — and watching. The party voted (unanimously): *"Let's go."* They backed off and returned to the path without engaging. The Wisp Mother did not pursue.



---

### Late Morning — The Three Brothers and the Barrow

After another **30 minutes** of walking, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** spotted (on a successful Observe, -10 penalty) a **stone outcropping to the left**, up a gentle slope. The path to it was old — once carved, now just a worn channel in the rock.

At the top stood **three standing stones** — [[Info/Locations/The Rift/The_Three_Brothers\|the Three Brothers]] — with barely-visible carvings, badly eroded. One carving might have been a crown; most had become indistinguishable from weather damage.

As the party examined the stones, a **crow landed on one** — eyes glowing dark purple. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] offered it dried meat from her pocket. It ate, then flew off slowly toward the mountains. She addressed it: *"We are looking for your friend. We are following the path of the riddle he gave us."* The crow led them onward like a beacon.

Whether this was Jango (the distinctive Blackfeather crow who wears a little hat and stands on a coin in his token) was unclear — any identifying marks weren't confirmed.

The crow led them to a **barrow** built into the cliff face — what appeared to be just natural stone at a glance, but recognisably the collapsed entrance to an old Nordic structure. As they stepped inside, the crow flew through the doorway and vanished. In front of them: a metal door. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] pushed it. It didn't move. He pulled it. It opened.

### The Barrow of the Oath Breaker King — Interior

**Darkness**: pitch black. Only **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** could see (darkvision). [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] cast **Light I** (10m radius magical orb) to allow the rest of the party to navigate. Torches were lit as backup once they got oriented.

**Layout navigation**: The party adopted a "hug the right wall" approach so they wouldn't get lost.

**Room 1 — Entry Hall**:
- One skeleton near the far wall — [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] examined it, Alchemy +10: recovered **one dose of Bone Meal**
- A gate on the left (lever-operated, opened fine) leading to a corridor
- A door ahead

**Corridor and Trap**:
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] rushed ahead into an unexplored corridor
- The GM called for an **Evade** test
- She barely passed — a huge log trap swung from the ceiling and nearly hit her
- Trap noted: *2d10 crushing* if it had connected. Subterfuge (Observe) established as the skill for spotting further traps

**Round Room**:
- Pots around the perimeter, another skeleton — [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] checked it (Alchemy), failed

**Door Bashing**:
- Several locked wooden doors encountered
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] began using his **axe** to simply break them down rather than wait for [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] to lockpick
- One door: Athletics test for bashing — succeeded, burst through, found a corridor with side entrances and debris at the end

**Lever Puzzle Room**:
- Four levers on the wall, each with an animal symbol above it
- Levers: **Hawk**, **Moth**, and two others (**Bear** and **Whale**) — but for Bear and Whale, the carved markers had crumbled and fallen
- The door had a **Hawk** and a **Bear** carved on it
- Engineering -20: [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] attempted to understand the mechanism — failed, couldn't see behind the wall
- Logic test: confirmed that the two broken markers came from above the remaining levers — so it's a 50/50 guess for the Bear
- **Hawk was identified** (leftmost)
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] flipped a coin** to determine which of the two unknown levers was the Bear — and pulled correctly
- The door opened with a mechanism somewhere in the wall

**Chest Behind the Door**:
- Wooden chest — [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] broke it open
- Contents: a **spell book** (hard to read — cipher or unknown script), **six lockpicks**, and **strange old coins** matching those found in the previous ruin the party explored

**The Water Room**:
- A flooded room with a submerged section
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** dived in to check the bottom (she can breathe underwater)
- Found a skeleton with a **ring** — she retrieved it and surfaced
- She then attempted a stealth approach from underwater to jump-scare someone; her tail gave her away with noise

**The Final Door**:
- Arrived at a **large reinforced wooden door** — no lock visible, but seemingly barred from the other side
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** (Observe -20, succeeded with a 69): heard **two distinct voices** from beyond the door
- After deliberation (someone suggested knocking), **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] knocked** — full fist, decisive
- Footsteps approached
- The door opened from the inside with a satisfying mechanical clunk

**The Shortcut**:
- As they were leaving at the end, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] showed them the path back — a single short corridor that connected the entrance directly to the throne room
- The party had taken the long way entirely around

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and the Exit Trap**:
- On the way out, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] spotted a closed wooden door and bashed through it with his axe
- Failed the **Evade** test
- Took **15 crushing damage** from a massive flail trap (2d10, rolled 15)
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] healed him with a touch — *"The stab of healing"*
- He stood back up as if nothing had happened

### Meeting Milos — The Vestige

The door opened to reveal the party standing before **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]** — an Argonian, roughly average height, previously briefly encountered by [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] in the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] marketplace. He confirmed his name and gestured to the room.

The **throne room** was dominated by a large sealed **stone sarcophagus** — the coffin of the Oath Breaker King — at the top of which [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] had apparently been standing or sitting when they arrived.

**The debrief** — the party laid everything out:

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] summarised the mission: found a ritual circle, stone, lockbox; warehouses with enslaved workers bearing shackle marks; a Daedra (Scathing) serving [[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]] openly running operations; a ledger confirming bandit payments, the names of the disappeared as "selection cargo," and a pointer to the **main facility north**; the stone + rusted key recovered from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Valen Marr]]'s office during the Jarl's party.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] provided a rough sketch map of the city with marked locations. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] noted [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|the Scathing]]'s horn (the one recovered) by pulling it out and examining it mid-briefing.

**[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] on the stone:**

> *"What perplexes me is what you have found — the stone."*
> *"The ritual is... I've only seen something like this once before. This is a ritual of what we call skin-changers. Mimicry Daedra — they don't belong to a clan or a prince, which makes this difficult. But I'd assume they'll be working with the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|cult of Clavicus Vile]] in the area."*

The **Daedric stone** is a **memory anchor**:
- The skin-changer (a Daedric mimicry creature) uses the stone to retain the memories of the person whose form it wears — in this case, **the Jarl**
- The stone periodically **transfers memories** from the real person (or their stored copy) to the skin-changer, allowing it to convincingly maintain the identity
- With the stone missing for **approximately one week**, the skin-changer will have been losing memories — small inconsistencies, gaps, wrong mannerisms

**[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]'s assessment of who is a skin-changer:**

> *"Whether it is the Jarl or the steward, I'm not sure. But someone is, given the Nordic mannerisms and the writing — I believe it to be likely the Jarl. The steward is an Imperial, after all."*

The Jarl is likely already dead. The creature wearing his skin is starting to degrade without the anchor.

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s question — the stone**:
[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] confirmed: she stole the stone, locked the box again, replaced it exactly where it was. No one on the guest list knows who took it. The skin-changer likely only noticed its absence recently — about now, in fact, which means they are currently looking for it.

**On the horned mask / [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|cult of Clavicus Vile]]**:

[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] confirmed he'd already been told about the "horned mask" — the [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Cult_of_Clavicus_Vile\|cult of Clavicus Vile]] — and their connection to the bandits via the ledger. He identified the situation as dealing with **two overlapping operations**: the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] (the Guild corruption) and the cult (the Daedric ritual/skin-changer element).

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] on informing the Queen of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]**:
[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] advised against waiting for the queen's forces — by the time a message reaches [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] and anyone responds, the main facility would have already acted. Better to resolve it now. Report to the queen after.

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] on necromancy**:
[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], now settling in, asked [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] directly about necromancy. He disclosed: he is a vestige because a necromancer stabbed him in the heart, killed him, and imprisoned him in Coldharbour. He doesn't care if [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] practices necromancy — *"Do whatever you want."* She promptly pulled out her **black tome** and began studying.

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] on the plan**:
[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] asked [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] about his combat style — daggers and short sword, good stealth. She asked if he could train her in stealth-based combat during downtime. He agreed.

**[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]'s plan:**
- He will **solo-scout the main facility** to the north (tonight)
- The party will **not follow him** — too many people draws too much attention
- The party uses the **two days of downtime** to recover, prepare, and use his resources
- His barrow includes an **alchemy lab** and **enchanting table**, both offered freely
- He will then return with intelligence and they go in together: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] distracts the main target, the party handles the rest

The party agreed to stay in the barrow rather than walk back — provisions, bedrolls, and a fire in the central room were sufficient. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] made healing potions before departing.

## Important Revelations

- **The Jarl is likely dead — a Daedric skin-changer wears his form**: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] confirmed that the ritual circle, the stone, and the Nordic mannerisms all point to a **mimicry Daedra** — a skin-changer — currently posing as the Jarl. The stone was the anchor for its memory of who the Jarl was. Without it, the creature is now degrading. The real Jarl has almost certainly been dead since before the party arrived in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
- **The Daedric stone is a memory anchor, not a summoning focus**: Its purpose was not to summon or bind anything, but to **house and periodically transfer memories** so the skin-changer could maintain its cover. This is a slow-burn operation — the Jarl's identity has been sustained over weeks or months.
- **The skin-changer knows the stone is missing — right now**: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] estimated the stone has been gone long enough (approximately one week) that the creature would be noticing memory degradation. The cult is actively looking for it.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] is a Vestige**: He was killed by a necromancer — stabbed in the heart — and his soul was held in Coldharbour. He is no longer fully mortal. This explains his unusual presence in a barrow, his [[Info/Organizations/Cults/Blackfeather Court\|Blackfeather Court]] connections, and his comfort operating in complete isolation.
- **The main facility is the final target**: The party now has [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]]'s confirmation of the northern location, the ledger evidence, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s Clairvoyance imprint of the ritual crates. Everything converges there.
- **Skin-changers are immune to mind-altering magic**: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Milos\|Milos]] confirmed [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|the Scathing]]'s immunity from [[2 -  Sessions/Session 024\|Session 24]] is a trait of this type of Daedra (it applies to mind-altering effects, not all magic). The frenzy bomb worked on the human guards; [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Scathing\|the Scathing]] simply played along.
