# Session 009a - Periwyn's Slumber - 2025-07-26

## Summary
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] experiences a vivid dream/vision while in her comatose state following the Dragon Cult ritual. She finds herself on a road leading to an old contract location - Darnow Hollow manor - where she encounters a mysterious elderly woman and explores the abandoned estate. The dream leads her to a chapel where she faces a manifestation of [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gareth\|Gareth]] (her former mentor) in a philosophical battle about mercy versus vengeance, followed by a confrontation with a figure called "Lady Elaris" who attempts to seduce her into darkness. The sequence culminates with the appearance of a mysterious cloaked figure who offers her a black feather and cryptic advice about writing her own story rather than following fate or prophecy.

---

## Detailed Recap

### Part 1: The Road to Darnow Hollow
- **Arrival at the Manor:**
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] finds herself on a familiar road leading to an old contract location
  - Atop a hill stands [[Darnow Hollow\|Darnow Hollow]] manor - crumbling yet regal
  - Sign post reads "Darnow Hollow"
  - Place appears deserted and sealed off

- **The Elderly Woman:** [[The Elderly Woman\|The Elderly Woman]]

### Part 2: Exploring the Estate
- **The Barn:**
  - Door split open by enormous claw marks
  - Hay soaked with blood
  - Pile of bones neatly stacked in the corner
  - Small glint next to bones reveals a child's doll wrapped in cloth
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] takes the [[Child's Doll\|child's doll]]

- **The Grain Mill:**
  - Soft humming coming from within
  - Small girl named [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Sybille\|Sybille]] with straw-colored hair inside
  - Girl warns about a lady in white from the manor who lives in the chapel
  - Lady asks for names and makes people forget them
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] gives the [[Child's Doll\|child's doll]] back to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Sybille\|Sybille]], as it belonged to her

### Part 3: The Chapel
- **Exterior:**
  - Red light pulsing from stained glass windows
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] hears a voice: "We are still here beneath her teeth"
  - Chapel of Stendarr appears like a wound in the forest
  - Bear footprints leading into the chapel
  - Graves to the left of the entrance: Cerulian and Saphirelle
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] has a sad moment at the graves

- **Interior:**
  - Once sacred air gone stagnant like water trapped in a crypt
  - Blood candles flicker on every surface, casting shadows that twist into shapes of kneeling men
  - Altar defaced - symbol of Stendarr carved out, but beneath it faintly scratched: [[Info/Lore/Arkay\|Arkay]]'s seal
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] recognizes her own seal, made years ago but she doesn't remember when
  - Figure kneels motionless at the end of the aisle behind the pulpit

### Part 4: Gareth's Manifestation
- **Gareth's Appearance:**
  - Shadows behind the altar deepen unnaturally, then detach like a cloak pulled from a corpse
  - Forms a hollowed man wrapped in remnants of silver-trimmed armor, helm in hand
  - Left eye socket in ruins of torn flesh (not just a scar)
  - Warhammer drags behind him, worn down to a ragged haft
  - Voice comes slow, like it's being remembered: "You've come a long way, little flower"

- **The Initial Confrontation:**
  - Gareth: "But you still carry me, don't you? Not just my voice, but what you never said to me"
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] is stunned, tries to collect herself and slowly backpedals "You shouldn't be here. You can't be here."
  - Gareth: "I died by your hand"
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]: "You begged me"
  - Gareth: "I did. You remember my last words very well, but not my last lesson"

### Part 5: The Philosophical Battle
- **The Core Dispute:**
  - Gareth: "Vengeance isn't mercy. Mercy isn't the absence of vengeance. It is the memory of love given even when it hurts"
  - Gareth: "You never forgave yourself. And that's the one thing I couldn't teach you"
  - Gareth lifts his warhammer and swings at [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] refuses to draw her weapon, tries to dodge instead

- **Flashbacks During Combat:**
  - Flash of [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] at arkayfell, Gareth showing her how to brace her stance
  - Gareth: "You came here to stop her, but you already know how you kill the hollow. You burn the rot. You swing until nothing moves. That's easy. But can you lay her to rest?"
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]: "Creatures like that don't deserve to rest"
  - Flash of rainy training day, Gareth's coy laughter when [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] slipped

- **The Argument Continues:**
  - Gareth: "I watched you become the finest of us and the most alone. You hunted the undead like they owed you something"
  - Gareth: "But you gave your heart to vengeance, not mercy, not even me"
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]: "How can I give my heart to a God that never did anything for me?"

- **The Cure Revelation:**
  - Gareth: "What if I turned but didn't feed? What if I came back to our castle and asked for a cure?"
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]: "You know better than any of us that there is no cure"
  - Gareth: "There is a cure. Always has been. I've seen it myself. Pure blooded vampires turned from the beginning, becoming mortal once more"
  - Gareth: "And even then, if I don't feed, if I don't hurt anyone, what wrong have I done to deserve death other than being bitten?"

- **The Chapel Transformation:**
  - Chapel shifts, becoming [[Info/Locations/Arkayfell\|Arkayfell]], walls trembling as if under siege
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sees herself younger, kneeling beside Gareth's dying body
  - Gareth: "You remember what you did. But do you remember why?"
  - As their blades clash, Gareth pauses and drops his weapon
  - Gareth: "Was it mercy or just escape?"

### Part 6: Gareth's Forgiveness
- **The Final Moment:**
  - Gareth drops his warhammer between them, arms wide
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] drops her sword, takes out her dagger
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] holds the dagger against Gareth's neck but doesn't follow through
  - Gareth moves her arm away and kneels down
  - Chapel grows silent, blood candles stop flickering

- **Gareth's Final Lesson:**
  - Gareth: "You carried my death for too long. Let this be my final lesson"
  - Gareth: "You were never meant to carry us. Only to remember"
  - Gareth reaches out and touches [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s shoulder
  - Gareth: "You forgave me. Now forgive yourself"
  - Gareth's form dissolves into silver light, becoming part of the air
  - One blood candle relights, burning clean white

- **The Warhammer:**
  - At [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s feet lies Gareth's warhammer, the one she's been carrying all these years
  - It appears reforged, new

### Part 7: The Vampire's Plea
- **The Second Vampire ([[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Mirel\|Mirel]]):**
  - After Gareth fades, someone emerges from the chapel shadows
  - Pale and sunken, wearing remains of Arkay's robes stained with wine/red blood
  - Holy symbol dangles from one hand, burned and blackened
  - Doesn't bear fangs or hiss, simply looks tired and afraid

- **The Vampire's Story:**
  - "He tried to guide me once. But I was too afraid. I kept them alive, my family, in bone and memory"
  - "She came and offered me the power to remember them without pain. I said yes. But now I don't feel anything at all"
  - On the altar: black soul gem pulsing with unseen veins
  - Whisper: "You've been here before. You remember killing him. But there is another memory, another lesson"

- **The Choice:**
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] could help him rest, let his soul free from Coldharbour
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] initially swings at him with the warhammer but hesitates
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] throws the warhammer aside and sanctifies the altar
  - Soul gem breaks, chapel pulses with warm wind
  - Vampire crumbles into light: "Thank you for letting me be remembered as I was"

### Part 8: Return to the Manor
- **The Transition:**
  - After the chapel sequence, reality shifts
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] finds herself on the way to the manor she saw at the beginning
  - Cliff side breathes salt and rot
  - Once grand manor half sunk into stone like it's trying to bury itself
  - Ivy chokes the walls but can't mask the scent of blood clinging to the air

- **The Manor Interior:**
  - Front garden with white flowers blooming pure and defiant like [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s mother's back in [[Info/Locations/Northpoint\|North Point]]
  - Flowers shouldn't be growing in soil this dark
  - Long foyer unfolds with marble underfoot, blackened portraits on the wall
  - Grand chandelier swings as if from some unseen breath
  - Place remembers feasting, bigger than even Luneclaire estate back in [[Info/Locations/Northpoint\|North Point]]

### Part 9: The Battle with Lady Elaris
- **Lady Elaris:** [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Lady Elaris\|Lady Elaris]]
  - Sits at the far end of the ballroom on a throne of withered roses
  - Doesn't rise but her presence reaches across the distance
  - Pale, beautiful, wrapped in noble silks that hang like mourning veils
  - Silver goblet untouched beside her, as if waiting
  - Voice: "You've touched the void, haven't you? I can smell it on your skin"

- **The Seductive Combat:**
  - Lady Elaris drifts down like a silken ghost, no sound, no weight
  - Scent of roses follows her, crushed, wilting red as blood
  - Lady Elaris doesn't move like a killer, more like a lover trying to seduce to the dark
  - "That look in your eyes - fear, fury, cards. It's beautiful on you"
  - "Do you even know how lovely you are when you break?"
  - Multiple strikes and parries as [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Lady Elaris\|Lady Elaris]] attempts to seduce [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] into darkness

- **The Face Scar:**
  - Lady Elaris lunges with claws into [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s face, causing her to spin and stumble
  - This creates the scar on [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s left side of her face
  - Lady Elaris: "You're already mine. That little girl with dirt on her hands whispering spells over cold flesh. That was the beginning. Just the ending"

- **The Final Blow:**
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] swings for kneecap, hits [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Lady Elaris\|Lady Elaris]]'s thigh instead, causing her to fall
  - Lady Elaris gets up and moves away: "Stay. Let the dead rest. Let your cards rot. Be beautiful with me"
  - Lady Elaris lunges again, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] tries to parry but too late
  - Lady Elaris smashes [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] across the front of her neck
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] hits the floor, weapon wrenched from her hand

- **The Seduction Attempt:**
  - Lady Elaris descends slowly like a petal falling from a dying rose
  - Kneels beside [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], gloved hand still bloody, brushes blood from her lip
  - "You don't hate me. You hate that. Right. You ache for something to surrender to. Older than duty. Wilder than okay"
  - "I could make it stop. Periwyn. No more craves, no more guilt. Just power. Just us"
  - "Say the word. Let it all rot. And I will show you what it means to be truly free"
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]: "Should have stayed in the plains of oblivion"
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] tries to reach for her dagger to slash [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Lady Elaris\|Lady Elaris]]'s throat

### Part 10: Gareth's Intervention
- **Divine Light:**
  - Every candle in the room suddenly alights
  - As if Arkay's own presence graced the halls
  - Shadows peel away like curtains, letting in light the manor hasn't seen in centuries

- **Gareth's True Appearance:**
  - Silver-streaked auburn hair, scar over left eye
  - Same old war-worn armor, proud posture
  - Figure flickers like an image on warped glass
  - Voice comes slow, like it's being remembered

- **The Confrontation:**
  - Gareth addresses [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]: "You've come for a little flower, far enough to mistake vengeance for justice"
  - Lady Elaris hisses at the light, claims [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] is hers
  - Lady Elaris lunges at Gareth but is knocked back by divine light
  - Gareth knocks her back to her throne of roses, knocking over a goblet

- **Gareth's Message:**
  - Kneels beside [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] as he once did in the ruins of North Point
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s carotid artery was cut, blood staining the floor
  - Gareth tells her she carries too much grief, calls it "weaponized"
  - "You don't have to dig them up again. You don't have to answer every scream with steel"
  - "You survived. That was enough. And I forgive you for what you did. For what you couldn't do"
  - Fades into light with final words: "Let the dead rest, little flower"

### Part 11: The Mysterious Figure
- **Lady Elaris's Return:**
  - Lady Elaris appears above [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] once more
  - Attempts to seduce her again: "You've worn sorrow so long you think it's skin"
  - Offers to "kiss it off piece by piece"
  - Touches the gaping wound in [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s neck
  - "Come, die a little and rise with me"

- **The Figure's Arrival:**
  - Darkness devours everything - not shadow, but devouring
  - Candles die, wind goes still, manor seems to forget it exists
  - Figure cloaked in something darker than night appears from the threshold
  - Black that repels memory, shadows move out of the way like they fear him
  - Face hidden, eyes catch moonlight like glass knives
  - Air grows heavier, [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Lady Elaris\|Lady Elaris]] stiffens

- **The Unmaking:**
  - Figure addresses [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Lady Elaris\|Lady Elaris]]: "I told you this was a seduction"
  - [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Lady Elaris\|Lady Elaris]] claims this is her dream, asks who the figure is to steal [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] from her
  - [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Lady Elaris\|Lady Elaris]] leaps at the figure with elegant, terrible fury
  - Air thickens, shadows decide, [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Lady Elaris\|Lady Elaris]] is caught mid-air
  - No resistance - she simply ceases to exist
  - Not dead, not gone, just unmade

- **The Transition:**
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] finds herself elsewhere with no transition
  - Standing upright, fully healed
  - World around her is not Tamriel but remembers it enough to mock it

- **The Other Realm:**
  - Trees twist like bone fingers, bark splintered and white as dead coral
  - Sky like cracked obsidian with stars like eyes that never blink
  - Air hums like a breath held too long
  - Black water covers the ground, doesn't ripple or reflect [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]
  - Only reflects the stars
  - Divines don't look here, Princes can't
  - Place has been "cut loose" from reality
  - Single crow coasts overhead, then another, then silence

### Part 12: The Conversation
- **The Figure's Identity:**
  - Claims to be "no one of consequence"
  - Mentions countless names, none that speak to him
  - References crows that used to belong to [[Info/Lore/Nocturnal\|Nocturnal]]
  - Calls this place his "fixer upper" - a pocket created to be away from prying eyes

- **The Feather:**
  - Offers [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] a long, [[Black Feather\|black feather]] faintly humming with energy
  - Air around it shivers
  - Mentions the Argonian ([[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]) carries another or two
  - "If you wake, keep it. Might keep you next time. If not, I recycle"

- **The Vision:**
  - As [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] touches the [[Black Feather\|feather]], she sees a vision
  - [[Info/Lore/Malacath\|Malacath]]'s tusked mask, fractured but not ruined
  - As if something tried to break it and wasn't quite there
  - Pieces hold just barely

- **The Warning:**
  - "The first to carry dead like armor. But if you play it smart, you might be the last"
  - "I'll be watching you, Periwyn Luneclaire"
  - "Heed this: this isn't about fate or prophecy"
  - "It's about you and what kind of story you are willing to bleed for"
  - "Write your own fetching ending. Don't let them do it"
  - "Especially not [[Info/Lore/Malacath\|Malacath]]. We don't want another one of him running around"

### Part 13: Tea and Revelations
- **The Tea:**
  - Figure offers tea, claims it's just tea
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] accepts despite suspicions
  - Place shifts around them to a table with teacup already poured

- **About the Group:**
  - Figure asks about [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s companions
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] describes them:
    - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]: "Makes for a damn good meat shield"
    - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]: "Steals things, saves my skin more times than I'd like to admit"
    - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]: "Useful. Don't tell them"

- **About Archmagus Mortgren:**
  - Figure reveals he knows Mortgren
  - Calls him "the high advisor, a Viking, Emmerich"
  - Claims Mortgren is "what you were before" - older than any alive
  - Mentions Dunmer mages that are 2000 years old
  - Hints at necromancy and bone manipulation
  - Reveals he helped liberate North Point after [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] was taken

- **The Departure:**
  - Figure advises [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] to write her own story
  - "Don't let the gods or princes or anyone write a story that you don't want"
  - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] asks how to reach him again
  - "I'll find you. My crows are everywhere"
  - Figure slowly scatters into crows that vanish into the canopy
  - Trees fold inward, session ends
