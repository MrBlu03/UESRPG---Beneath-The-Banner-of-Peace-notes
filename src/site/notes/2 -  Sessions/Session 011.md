# Session 011 - 2025-08-17
**17th of Frostfall, 2E 600**

## Summary

The party awakens at the Hall of Ysmir on a rainy morning when [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torvald_Black-Foot\|Torvald Black-Foot]] arrives with an urgent job. Dunmer criminals have been harassing Argonian dock workers and broke into a warehouse, seeking a mysterious lockbox. The party investigates the docks, interrogates a captured Dunmer linked to the [[Info/Organizations/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] criminal syndicate, and tracks the criminals to their hideout. They discover the criminals have seized a merchant ship containing the sought-after lockbox. After eliminating the Dunmer operatives in a violent confrontation, the party recovers the lockbox and delivers it to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torvald_Black-Foot\|Torvald Black-Foot]], learning it may be connected to suspicious ship movements in the Sea of Ghosts.

## Detailed Recap

### Morning at the Hall of Ysmir

The party wakes on the morning of the 7th of Frostfall to pouring rain outside. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Adda\|Adda]] is absent from the hall as they wake in sequence:

- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] wakes first, eating fish for breakfast
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] wakes second, enjoying fruit salad  
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] wakes third, grabbing meat
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] wakes last, taking meat and examining her destruction tome before joining the others

### The Job Offer

A hooded figure enters the hall, dripping wet from the rain. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torvald_Black-Foot\|Torvald Black-Foot]] reveals himself and approaches [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]], ushering the party to the main table for a job proposal:

- **Payment**: 250 gold each (negotiated up to 280 by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]])
- **Problem**: Dunmer criminals harassing Argonian workers at the docks, asking about shipments
- **Incident**: Warehouse break-in a few nights ago, one Dunmer captured, others scattered
- **Current Situation**: The targeted shipment is halted and guarded a few hours south of [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]]
- **Tasks**: Question the Argonian workers and interrogate the captured Dunmer in [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]] jail

### Equipment and Preparation

The party stops at the [[Info/Locations/Sweaty_Steel\|Sweaty Steel]] for equipment. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] again attempts to trip [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik the Quick]] as he speeds by but fails spectacularly as he jumps over her and continues running. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] purchases a short sword at the shop.

### Investigation at the Docks

Arriving at the docks around 10 AM, the party finds them quieter than usual. They investigate the targeted warehouse and question the Argonian workers:

**From the Workers:**
- Dunmer claimed the shipment belonged to their house (unspecified)
- One Dunmer wore a bone or plate mask - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] recognizes this as Camonna Tong (criminal syndicate)
- "Yourgrim drops" were mentioned (likely smuggler terminology)
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sketches the mask description for future reference

**From the Guards:**
- Break-in occurred while the foreman was absent
- Entry through top window
- Criminals likely didn't find what they sought, as they returned
- Dunmer headed north after the incident

**Suspicious Argonian:**
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] spots an Argonian worker acting suspiciously - shorter than others, broad shoulders, avoiding eye contact, with similar markings to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]. Under interrogation by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] (including threats of shoulder dislocation), the worker reveals:

- He lied about being in quarters during the break-in
- Mentions "the dread father" when begging [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] for help
- Reveals the Dunmer were seeking a journal or lockbox
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] admits she was lying about not recognizing certain elements but [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] trusts her reasoning

**Warehouse Inspection:**
Inside the warehouse, some crates were moved or broken into, but nothing was stolen.

### Interrogation at Riften Jail

The party descends into the dungeons where [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Harvold\|Harvold]], the guard captain, offers free drinks at the [[Info/Locations/Ember_Keg\|Ember Keg]] if they can make the prisoner talk. 

The captured Dunmer's reaction to the party is telling:
- Spits at [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]
- Eyes widen upon seeing [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]], keeps glancing at her nervously
- Becomes intimidated when [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] stands back

**Information Extracted:**
- They sought a lockbox "taken in a storm"
  - The prisoner is with the [[Info/Organizations/Camonna_Tong\|Camonna Tong]] criminal syndicate
  - His boss is [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Tharen_Duleri\|Tharen Duleri]]
  - Their base: cave in woods south of [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]], small clearing (likely abandoned by now)
- The location serves as meeting point for return to Morrowind

After the interrogation, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] enters the cell, calls the prisoner "a fool," and knocks him unconscious with a punch that breaks his nose. She later explains to [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] that her tribe (the Blacktongue) has a reputation for [[Info/Organizations/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark Brotherhood]] recruitment, explaining the Dunmer's fear.

### Tracking the Criminals

The party heads south through the city. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] fails another attempt to trip [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik the Quick]]. After 2-3 hours of walking in the early afternoon, they reach the suspected hideout:

**At the [[Info/Locations/Abandoned_Cave_South_of_Riften\|Cave]]:**
- Located at the foot of mountains south of [[Info/Locations/Riften\|Riften]]
- Obviously recently abandoned with extinguished campfire (only hours old)
- Food scraps and gear impressions visible
- In the distance, they spot a ship's mast near the water

### The Ship Assault

Approaching the ship in early evening (5-6 PM), the party observes:
- Small rowboat nearby
- Ship's lanterns unlit, covered with reeds for concealment
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] identifies Dunmer wearing netch leather armor (exclusive to Morrowind)

**Systematic Elimination:**
The party employs stealth tactics to eliminate the guards:

1. **First Guard**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] throws pebble to lure him away, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] uses paralysis dart, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] slits his throat
   - **Loot**: 14 gold, healing potion, weapons, armor (taken by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]])

2. **Second Guard**: Same tactic repeated
   - **Loot**: 27 gold

3. **Third Guard**: Called out about noise, lured closer with pebbles and bird whistle
   - **Loot**: 11 gold, torch

1. **Masked Dunmer Leader**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] throws pebble at his face, he goes invisible
   - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] exits cover as distraction
   - [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] swims underwater to other side of boat

**Ship Interior:**
- Upper deck littered with crew bodies
- Muffled screaming from crew quarters (stops abruptly)
- Several dead people tied up, two still alive
- **Masked Dunmer Leader** discovered

**Final Confrontation:**
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] throws paralysis bomb (fails to affect target)
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] attempts tackle and stabbing
- Dunmer drops fireball at his feet, speaks in Dunmeri, and dies
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] catch fire, jump through window into water
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] heals herself and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] retrieves the lockbox (fails to pick lock)
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] damages ship hull
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] recovers the Dunmer's body and gear

**Final Loot from Leader:**
- Camonna Tong mask
- 78 gold
- Partial armor (unrecognized material, mostly cloth)
- Ledger written entirely in Dunmeri

### Mission Completion

Returning to [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torvald_Black-Foot\|Torvald Black-Foot]]'s office, the party reports their findings and delivers the lockbox. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torvald_Black-Foot\|Torvald Black-Foot]] expresses concern about the implications:

- Ships spotted in the Sea of Ghosts bearing house colors
- Lockbox may have been recovered from one of these vessels
- He will investigate through his contacts
- **Total Party Payment**: 280 gold each (10 gold contributed to communal savings)
