# Session 012a - Periwyn's Necromancy Apprenticeship
**18th of Frostfall, 2E 600**

## Summary
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] approaches [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Archmagus Mortgren]] privately to reveal her knowledge of his necromantic abilities and request training. After testing her motivations, Mortgren agrees to teach her through conjured tomes rather than direct instruction.

## Detailed Recap

### Private Meeting
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] found Mortgren in his quarters translating documents and requested a private conversation about an "extremely illegal" matter. She revealed her knowledge that he is a necromancer and asked to become his student.

### The Black Feather Test
When Mortgren challenged her assumptions, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] produced the mysterious black feather from her near-death visions. Mortgren immediately recognized its significance, confirming both his necromantic nature and that "the crows circle you already."

### Motivation Assessment  
Mortgren questioned whether [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sought necromancy to counter enemies or resurrect the dead. She honestly admitted her original goal was resurrection but had learned "the dead should rest" and now sought defensive applications against supernatural threats.

### Training Agreement
Impressed by her honesty about past failures with resurrection attempts, Mortgren agreed to teach her. He conjured a black leather tome using pallid flames and ancient runes, explaining she would learn from written instruction rather than direct mentorship.

### Practical Guidance
[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] asked about avoiding persecution when using necromancy defensively. Mortgren explained the "spectrum" of necromantic arts - that bone-shaping and soul-warding could be disguised as alteration or conjuration, but raising the dead must never be witnessed.

### Departure
The session ended with Mortgren providing contact methods (crows or [[Info/Organizations/Mages_Guild\|Mages Guild]]) and [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] returning to her quarters to begin studying the conjured tome.
