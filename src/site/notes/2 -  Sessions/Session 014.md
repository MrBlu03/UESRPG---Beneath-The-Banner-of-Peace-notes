# Session 014 - Investigation of the Frozen Hunters

## Summary
The party investigates rumors of frozen hunters in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]], discovering a vampire survivor from the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] incident who has been conducting necromantic experiments. They uncover a connection between the frozen hunters, vampire thralls, and a larger conspiracy involving [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn the Ancient]] and his cohorts. The investigation leads to the discovery of a territorial creature in the northern forests and reveals more about the ongoing vampire threat to the region.

## Detailed Recap

### Morning Activities
The Fangs awoke to pale light cutting weakly through the shutters. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] checked her weapons and spent quiet moments bonding with her horse [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]], running a brush along her mane. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] similarly tended to their horses [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Mossback]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Sunsnow]] respectively. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s absence hung like a faint question none voiced aloud.

### Investigation Begins
At the market, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] sold a rack of antlers she'd taken from the woods days before to local merchants for 95 gold, adding to the party's communal funds. The group then began investigating rumors of frozen hunters that had been circulating in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].

### Gathering Information
- Met with a local hunter, a [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Grieving Brother]] whose sibling had been among the frozen dead
- Still raw with grief, the brother offered little but directed them to speak with the Jarl for more official information
- At the Jarl's Longhouse, they were received not by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Jarl Gorm]], but by his Huscarl.

### Meeting with Helga the Huscarl
- The Jarl was unavailable due to illness—bone rot, what the locals called Rockjoint. The people were wary, their memories of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] still bleeding fresh.
- Spoke with **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga Cairn-Shield]]**, the Jarl's huscarl, who urged them to keep their work quiet
- Learned that one hunter, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nilan\|Nilan]]**, had survived the encounter and come to report what happened, but vanished soon after. Guards sent to his home found nothing.
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga]] handed [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] a key, suggesting the Fangs might fare better than her men, and pointed northeast toward the incident site.

### Investigation of Nilan's House
The party proceeded to investigate [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nilan\|Nilan]]'s residence, half-buried in frost with its chimney long cold:
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] circled the outside, finding no disturbances but noting spoiled stew through the window
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]], focusing as [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Mortgren]] taught her, felt the faint pulse of death lingering near the back wall
- The key turned stiffly in the lock as the party entered the sour-smelling house.

#### Inside the House
- The air was sour with spoiled stew on the table, dust settling over the room
- Trophies lined the walls: wolves, bears, saber cats
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] noticed a bow hung wrong near the door—not the hand of a seasoned hunter
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] followed the pull of her sense to the bedroom, finding a hatch beneath a rug. The hinges groaned as she lifted it, releasing a wave of rot.

#### The Hidden Basement
Beneath the rug, a flickering candle burned in a cramped cellar:
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nilan\|Nilan]]'s body**: Bound against the wall, bruised, bloodied, and long dead. Sunken eyes and bite marks on his neck indicated he had been turned — a thrall to a vampire.
- The sharp scent of nightshade filled the air. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] recognized it as the poison that killed [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adrith_Velan\|Adrith Velan]] months before.
- A nearby table held a mortar stained black and two vials. One held liquid as dark as pitch.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] gathered the vials and a fallen nightshade petal before leaving for the woods.

### Northern Forest Investigation
The forest north of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] lay in uneasy silence (no birds, no rustle):
- Spotted a shred of animal hide tangled high in a tree
- Found the carcass of a deer, skinned clean and abandoned
- The party felt the woods were watching them

#### Discovery of the Frozen Hunters
In a shallow clearing, the party found two men frozen mid-motion (one with sword drawn, the other with bow half-pulled):
- Frost had claimed them long ago
- Surrounding battlefield showed splintered boughs and deep gashes carved into bark — too broad for bear, too long for wolf.
- Followed a clawed path that led them deeper.

### The Ruined Hunting Lodge
Following the trail, the party discovered the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Ruined_Hunting_Lodge\|Ruined Hunting Lodge]]:
- A run-down, half-collapsed abandoned hunting lodge
- Strong odors of dust, rot, and blood hung in the air

#### Encounter with the Vampire Survivor
Inside the lodge, they found a [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vampire_Survivor\|Vampire Survivor]]:
- The vampire did not attack; he leaned against a wall clutching a deep wound carved across his side by whatever beast left the marks in the woods.
- Boasted of serving [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Majorn_the_Ancient\|Majorn the Ancient]]'s Coven — a survivor of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] sent to test mortal strength.
- [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]], he said, was only a field experiment, a taste of what was to come.
- He turned [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nilan\|Nilan]] into a thrall merely to feed while he healed.
- When [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] mentioned [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nilan\|Nilan]]'s fate, his grin faltered, but he hardened again: "Fear spreads faster than fire. Soon all of them will break."
- The Fangs shared a glance, realizing there was nothing more to learn. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] drew first, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] followed, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s lightning struck last.
- His dying words: "My work here is done."

#### Significant Revelations
On the road back, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] questioned [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s hesitation.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] shared details of her coma and the dreams/visions she'd had.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] dismissed it as fevered imagining, until [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] showed the feather given by the stranger in black.
- Crows watched from the treeline. One hopped closer and spoke in thought, confirming its master, the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|Crow Father]], deals in omens and feathers, leaving tokens for those he marked.
- Overwhelmed, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] tried to return the cryptic scroll to the bird. The crow replied, "You will understand in time."
- A low, pained, unnatural howl rolled from the deeper woods, prompting them to leave. Pale blue eyes watched them from the treeline until the road swallowed them.

### Return Journey and Reporting Back
- Retrieved a few hunting trophies from [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Nilans_House\|Nilan's House]] still worth selling
- Properly disposed of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nilan\|Nilan]]'s body in a funeral pyre behind his house
- Around 4 PM, the party returned to report their findings to **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga Cairn-Shield]]** the huscarl.
- They advised her to keep the townsfolk's spirits high, to remind them of the Nords' strength in the face of fear. She agreed to let them occupy [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Nilans_House\|Nilan's house]] for the time being, pending the Jarl's approval.

### Evening Activities
That night, they made the house their own. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] gathered wood for the hearth. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] conducted her second necromancy lesson in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Nilans_House\|Nilan's]] basement, opening [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Mortgren]]'s book. She whispered the names of the dead — her mother, her father, her brother, her mentor. Voices whispered back, but she held steady until they faded. She wrote a letter filled with what she felt—regret about how vengeance had consumed her soul and memories of the good in her life—and left it on the table. Upstairs, they settled near the fire as the wind carried faint echoes of the howl.

### Durov's Solo Investigation (2nd-4th of Sun's Dusk)
While the others investigated the frozen hunters, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] followed the strange, unnatural howl into the woods east of the palisade.
- In a birch hollow, he witnessed a creature towering twice his height, draped in ragged hides and crowned with a stag's head, peel a deer's hide with surgical precision and carve a cold rune into a tree trunk.
- Returning to the market, a hunter named **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Runa\|Runa]]** directed him to **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]]** tavern to find a Colovian from Chorrol who often muttered about Legion talk.
- Tavern owner **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]]** confirmed this, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] found the Colovian, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gaius\|Gaius]]**, at a near-abandoned house.
- Over mead, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gaius\|Gaius]] recognized the crest on [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s armor from the war (garrison families near Bruma). [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gaius\|Gaius]] admitted his guilt over his Legion service.
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gaius\|Gaius]] drugged [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] with Nirnroot in the mead "to give him silence."
- On the 4th of Sun's Dusk, hunters [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Runa\|Runa]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Mirda\|Mirda]] found [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] outside the cottage in the snow. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gaius\|Gaius]] was gone, leaving a note about debts that can't be paid and a melted twin-bird pendant.
- Back at [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]] gave [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] an [[Info/Organizations/Military/Imperial_Legion\|Imperial Legion]] tag stamped with the number zero.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] checked on the horses and learned from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Denskar\|Denskar]] at [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Wounded_Boar\|The Wounded Boar]] that the Fangs had taken lodging on the east side of town, where he finally rejoined them.
