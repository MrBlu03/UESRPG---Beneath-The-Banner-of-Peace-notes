# Session 021 - Deception and Discovery

## Summary
The morning after their encounter with **The Vestige**, the party regrouped at **Frost Gate Hall** to plan their next move. **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** gathered the group to discuss how to handle **[[Darius Karo\|Darius Karo]]**, the Jarl's Steward, and decided on a bold deception: they would forge evidence of a Dunmer conspiracy to give themselves cover to investigate the **Guild of the Silverroad**. **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** revealed he had been sent to hunt The Vestige by **[[Valen Marr\|Valen Marr]]**, the Trade Advocate for Guildmaster **[[Jorvik Hanison\|Jorvik Hanison]]** of the Guild of the Silverroad, and had already applied for a position within the guild. **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** and **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** forged a partially burned letter implicating a Dunmer caravan carrying ledgers related to the brimstone gold conspiracy. The party delivered their fabricated story to [[Darius Karo\|Darius Karo]], claiming they found evidence at the abandoned guild house but no sign of Sadrith. They convinced him they were owed 800 gold each and were instructed to bring the letter to [[Valen Marr\|Valen Marr]] instead. Strange events occurred during their journey—**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** heard Dunmer voices mocking her though none were visible, and crows continued to follow them. Returning to the guild house, they found **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]]** in worse mental condition than expected—too traumatized to continue as a double agent. The Vestige provided information about the Guild of the Silverroad's structure and confirmed **[[Valen Marr\|Valen Marr]]** as a key figure. **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** and **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]]** returned to the sewers to retrieve the **Daedric speaking stone**, passing by the corpses of cultists slain by [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] and The Vestige, along with a **summoning circle** used to call forth Daedra. The Vestige examined the stone and determined its weak connection was because it linked to **another realm**—likely Oblivion itself.

## Important Revelations

- **The Guild of the Silverroad's Leadership**: [[Jorvik Hanison\|Jorvik Hanison]] is the Guildmaster, with [[Valen Marr\|Valen Marr]] serving as his Trade Advocate. [[Valen Marr\|Valen Marr]] has significant authority and access to the guild's financial operations.
- **Durov's Secret Mission**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] was sent to hunt The Vestige by the very organization the party is now investigating. His failed mission has become an asset.
- **The Speaking Stone's True Nature**: It doesn't communicate within Tamriel—it creates a connection to **another realm**, almost certainly Oblivion. This confirms direct Daedric involvement.
- **Active Cult Presence**: Multiple cultists were operating in the sewers. Their corpses and summoning circles prove the Cult of Clavicus Vile is actively working in [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
- **Varren's Failure as a Double Agent**: The psychological toll has made him unusable for espionage. The party must find another way to gather intelligence.
- **Phantom Voices**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] experienced auditory hallucinations of Dunmer mockery with no visible source. This could be magical, psychological, or a sign of something watching her.
- **The Crows' Protection**: The Vestige's crows continue to follow and watch over the party, providing a measure of security.
