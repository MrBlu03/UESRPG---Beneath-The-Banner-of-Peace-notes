# Session 021 - Deception and Discovery

## Summary
The morning after their encounter with **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]**, the party regrouped at **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost Gate Hall]]** to plan their next move. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** gathered the group to discuss how to handle **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius Caro]]**, the Jarl's Steward, and decided on a bold deception: they would forge evidence of a Dunmer conspiracy to give themselves cover to investigate the **[[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]]**. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** revealed he had been sent to hunt [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] by **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]]**, the Trade Advocate for Guildmaster **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jorven_Halliksson\|Jorvik Hanison]]** of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]], and had already applied for a position within the guild. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** and **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** forged a partially burned letter implicating a Dunmer caravan carrying ledgers related to the brimstone gold conspiracy. The party delivered their fabricated story to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius Caro]], claiming they found evidence at the abandoned guild house but no sign of Sadrith. They convinced him they were owed 800 gold each and were instructed to bring the letter to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]] instead. Strange events occurred during their journey—**[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** heard Dunmer voices mocking her though none were visible, and crows continued to follow them. Returning to the guild house, they found **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]]** in worse mental condition than expected—too traumatized to continue as a double agent. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] provided information about the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]]'s structure and confirmed **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]]** as a key figure. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** and **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]]** returned to the sewers to retrieve the **[[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Daedric Speaking Stone\|Daedric Speaking Stone]]**, passing by the corpses of cultists slain by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]], along with a **summoning circle** used to call forth Daedra. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] examined the stone and determined its weak connection was because it linked to **another realm**—likely Oblivion itself.

### Afternoon - Retrieving the Speaking Stone

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s Request:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** asked **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]]** to accompany her back to the sewers to retrieve the **[[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Daedric Speaking Stone\|Daedric Speaking Stone]]**

- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** pointed out a nearby sewer entrance
- The group descended into the tunnels

**Disturbing Discovery:**
- As they navigated the sewers, they stumbled upon **multiple corpses**
- On the floor near the bodies were **ritual markings**—a **summoning circle** designed to call forth Daedra
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] had dealt with these **cultists** previously
- The bodies were evidence of the Cult of [[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]]'s activities

**Reaching the Dead End:**
- Eventually they arrived back at the location where they'd confronted [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] the previous day
- The **[[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Daedric Speaking Stone\|Daedric Speaking Stone]]** was still there, untouched

**Examining the Stone:**
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]]** picked up the stone and handed it to **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**
- She studied it carefully:
  - Physically, it felt like a **normal speaking stone**
  - However, the magical connection felt unusually **weak**

**Returning to the Guild House:**
- The group made their way back through the sewers
- Emerged at the abandoned guild house

**[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]'s Analysis:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** gave the stone to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] for examination
- His findings:
  - The connection was weak, but **not because the stone was degraded**
  - The stone was functioning properly
  - The weakness was because it **links to a different realm**
  - Almost certainly **Oblivion** itself

## Important Revelations

- **The [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]]'s Leadership**: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jorven_Halliksson\|Jorvik Hanison]] is the Guildmaster, with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]] serving as his Trade Advocate. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vaelin_Marr\|Vaelin Marr]] has significant authority and access to the guild's financial operations.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s Secret Mission**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] was sent to hunt [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] by the very organization the party is now investigating. His failed mission has become an asset.
- **The Speaking Stone's True Nature**: It doesn't communicate within Tamriel—it creates a connection to **another realm**, almost certainly Oblivion. This confirms direct Daedric involvement.
- **Active Cult Presence**: Multiple cultists were operating in the sewers. Their corpses and summoning circles prove the Cult of [[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]] is actively working in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]].
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]]'s Failure as a Double Agent**: The psychological toll has made him unusable for espionage. The party must find another way to gather intelligence.
- **Phantom Voices**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] experienced auditory hallucinations of Dunmer mockery with no visible source. This could be magical, psychological, or a sign of something watching her.
- **The Crows' Protection**: [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]'s crows continue to follow and watch over the party, providing a measure of security.
