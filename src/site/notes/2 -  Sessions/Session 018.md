# Session 018 - Downtime, Investigation & The Nordic Execution

## Summary
A three-day downtime session in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] following the defeat of the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]]. The party shopped, gathered intelligence, and witnessed a traditional Nordic execution ritual. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] learned the Fox's Guile woad, commissioned a bow for [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]], and recruited him as an informant. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] (full name revealed: **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] Ma'Tael**) received a cryptic letter from her best friend and bonded with the visiting skalds **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]]** and **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonja]]**. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] received the Jack of Trades spellbook and struggled emotionally with the execution. **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle Benele]]** of the [[Info/Organizations/Other/Mages_Guild\|Mages Guild]] arrived with news that the Ritual Orb is a **Harrowstorm Engine Core** from the [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]]. The party interrogated the Dunmer prisoners, learning of their connection to **[[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Hlaalu\|House Hlaalu]]** and the **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]**. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] served as executioner in a full Nordic ritual, earning a place in the Book of [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] and a bone totem proving his participation in Nordic Law.

## Day 1: 9th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600

### Morning at the House
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** woke up first and spent the morning sketching the previous day's battle with the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]] (the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Nohrak]] fight), specifically trying to capture the party's "badass" moments.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** prepared a hearty breakfast using fish rations, making a broth/stew.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** woke up and joined the others, commenting on [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s sketches.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** slept in, having fallen asleep on the couch the night before. Later went to the inn to play cards (Solitaire).

### Shopping and Errands

**[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/The_Thistle_Branch\|The Thistle Branch]] ([[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]'s Shop):**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** visited [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] to learn the **Fox's Guile** woad. She also asked him about the **stag-shaped antler sliver** recovered from the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]]'s ashes that he had pressed into her hand at the feast. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] explained it was simply a hunter's token, a trace of what the [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Nohrak\|Ragged Lord]] once was, and that it now belonged to her.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]** taught her the woad for **30 Gold**.
  - *Visual*: Two slanted lines across each cheek from mouth to cheekbone, and a Gebo rune (X-shape) painted between the eyebrows.
  - *Effect*: Enhances social interactions and deception.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** then ran into **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** outside the shop. She gave [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] the **fox remains** from the week before, along with a spellbook she had been carrying (found in previous ruins): **"Jack of Trades"**.
  - *Spell Details*: 3rd Level, Cost: 9 Magicka. Allows the caster to roll the next test twice and take the best result.

**The Fletcher:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** visited the local fletcher/woodworker.
- Commissioned a **Custom Bow**:
  - *Design*: Engraved with a white stag, leaves, and trees. Decorative "wall piece" quality but functional.
  - *Arrows*: 10 Silver Arrows included.
  - *Cost*: Negotiated down to **210 Gold** (from 220).
  - *Delivery*: To be delivered to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]] in 3-4 days with a note.

**The Market:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** went to the market to stock up on supplies.
- Bought vegetables (potatoes, carrots, onions, leeks) and 2 garlic bulbs (for alchemy/curing).
- *Cost*: **17 Gold**.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** used *Observe* to find a cabbage merchant (failed) but spotted **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]]** and **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonja]]**, the two bards/skalds.
- **Interaction with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]] & [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonja]]**:
  - They revealed they are in town for an **execution** taking place in 2-3 days.
  - They are "Skalds" (Warrior Poets), not just bards.
  - Discussed instruments: They play the **Tagelharpa** (Swedish Horse Lyre).
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] expressed interest in learning their customs and music. They agreed to meet later at **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]]**.

### Regrouping & The Smugglers
- The party regrouped at the house. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** immediately set to work boiling the fox bones according to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Mortgren]]'s grim treatise.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** shared the news about the execution.
- Discussion turned to the **Dunmer Smugglers** (from [[2 -  Sessions/Session 015\|Session 15]]) who are scheduled for execution.
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** recounted the events: They were smuggling moon sugar (a crate's worth) and a ledger. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] killed one when they attacked; the survivors were handed over to the Huscarl.
- **Investigation Plan**:
  - The group suspects a connection to the **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]**.
  - Debated questioning the prisoners or demanding to see the ledger/evidence.
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** revealed she saw **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Stryder\|Stryder]]'s companion** (a Dunmer) in her coma/dream, implying a connection or at least significance.

### Woad Application & Bonding
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** asked **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** to help apply the **Fox's Guile** woad to her back.

- **Interaction**:
  - A moment of cultural curiosity occurred when [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] touched [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s fur and [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] touched [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s scales.
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** noticed a **blue beaded bracelet** on **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s** wrist.
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** explained it's a family identifier. If seen with a *pinkish hue*, it belongs to her sister. Her brother has a different color. This is a clue she is searching for.

### The Letter
- A **Courier** arrived at the house with a letter for **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**.
- It was from her **best friend, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jzargo\|J'zargo]]**.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** read it by the fire, visibly frustrated by the riddles and half-truths.
- *Quote*: "More riddles."

### Evening Activities

**Recruiting [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]]:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** invited **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** to accompany her to visit **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]]**.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** agreed, bringing her journal.

- **Mission**: Watch for specific threats:
  - The **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]** symbol (**[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** showed him the sketch from her journal).
  - Suspicious Dunmer activity.
  - Strange disappearances or gatherings.

  - Unexplained cold weather (similar to the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] snowstorm).
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hoki\|Hoki]]** agreed enthusiastically. He revealed that **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]** has been teaching him alchemy and he is learning to make potions.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** praised his potential and entrusted him with this mission.

**[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Soggy_Beaver\|The Soggy Beaver]]:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** and **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** went to the tavern where **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]]** and **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonja]]** were performing.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjalfi\|Bjalfi]]** (bartender) was serving food while **[[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Elsa]]** ran drinks.

- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** sat close to the front, attentively watching the performance.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** played Solitaire at a table.

- When [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonja]] took a break, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** asked to play her lute and performed an instrumental piece.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** made progress learning the 3rd level spell (requires 3 days of study).

## Day 2: 10th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600

### Weather Change
- Overnight, snow began falling heavily.
- **Temperature**: -13°C
- [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] was blanketed in snow, muffling all sound.
- The party awoke to a winter landscape.

### Discussing Fur-Lined Armor
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** noticed the snowfall and suggested the party might need to add fur to their armor.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** agreed to help with the modifications using pelts from previous hunts.

### The Mages Guild Visitor - Gabrielle Benele
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** left the house to head to the blacksmith.
- He noticed a thin **Breton woman** in a **blue and gold cloak** asking around for the "Fangs of Fjelhørst."
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] shadowed her through the market, watching as she questioned **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Runa\|Runa]]** and was directed to the Jarl's longhouse.
- At the longhouse, **Jarl [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Gorm]]** pointed her toward [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] the moment she announced her business.
- **Introduction**: She is **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle Benele]]**, an antiquarian and high-ranking member of the **[[Info/Organizations/Other/Mages_Guild\|Mages Guild]]** and their lead expert on **Daedric artifacts**.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] brought her to meet the rest of the party.

### News About the Harrowstorm Engine Core
**[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle]]** delivered critical information about the Ritual Orb:
- It is a **Harrowstorm Engine Core**, a remnant from the **[[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]]** crisis 14-15 years ago.
- The [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]] was a clan of vampires and werewolves working together, with plans to create undead vampire-werewolf hybrids.
- All their research was destroyed and key members were killed during the "Dark Heart of Skyrim" crisis.
- **The orb is dormant** - without the proper rituals, matrices, and energy source, it cannot activate.
- It was originally taken from **[[Info/Lore/Markarth\|Markarth]]** and was the only one not retrieved until now.
- It has been wrapped in multiple layers of **Anti-Daedric wards**.
- It departed for **[[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Winterhold\|Winterhold]]** 1-2 days ago, and will eventually be transported to the main guild hall in **[[Info/Locations/Other Realms/Eyevea\|Eyevea]]** for safe storage.
- The energy/death around [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] likely caused it to briefly awaken when [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] shot it.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle]]** mentioned someone named **Vanus** who owes her a favor (related to the orb's storage).

### The Crows (Daedric Creatures)
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle]]** needed to send a letter and asked about messenger birds.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** pulled out bread and summoned **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron Jangleplume]]**, the rotund Daedric crow.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle]]** recognized the crows as **Daedric crows** - immortal, teleporting crows that can talk.
- She mentioned they are curious creatures associated with **[[Info/Lore/Nocturnal\|Nocturnal]]**.
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron Jangleplume]] once brought her a fake soup spoon when she was in the **Clockwork City**.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** suggested feeding them more bread to make friends with them.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** bought more bread (1 Gold) to keep summoning the crows.

### Fur-Lining the Armor
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** used his smithing skills to fur-line everyone's armor at the blacksmith's forge.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** and the others had to remove their armor and wrap in cloaks while waiting.
- **Smithing Tests**: All successful (using Physical Exertion for +20 bonus).
- **Materials Used**: Pelts from previous hunts.
- **Result**: All party armor is now **fur-lined** for cold weather protection.

### Interrogating the Prisoners
- The party visited the **Jarl's longhouse** to investigate the captured Dunmer smugglers.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga]]** led them to the basement cells.
- Five Dunmer remained, chained to the walls on their knees.
- One immediately spat on **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** when they entered. She clenched her fists in silent fury.

#### Obtaining the Ledger
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga]]** provided the confiscated **Dunmer Ledger**.
- It bore the **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]** symbol.
- Contents (mostly in Dunmeri, which **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**, raised among the sharp vowels of Tear, translated):
  - General business transactions (lumber, cloth)
  - A loan of **5,000 gold** from a **Kragenmoor** bank
  - Dealings with **[[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Hlaalu\|House Hlaalu]]**
  - A single mention of **House Telvanni**
  - One confirmed reference to the **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]**
- Since it wasn't needed for the trial, the party was allowed to **keep the ledger**.

#### The Interrogation
**[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** took the lead, employing intimidation tactics:
- Dragged a chair into the cell making a scraping noise.
- Sat close to the prisoners, establishing dominance.
- The guard watched with interest, eating meat.

**Key Information Obtained**:
- **Employer**: **Belyn Hlaalu**, Grand Master of [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Hlaalu\|House Hlaalu]] (one of the Great Houses of Morrowind).
- **Direct Superior**: **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Garen_Hlaalu\|Garen Hlaalu]]**, who leads operations into Skyrim.
- **Moon Sugar**: Worth 300 gold; they would have been paid 400 gold total for transport. Smuggling moon sugar usually just results in fines, not execution.
- **The [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] Connection**:
  - They didn't "join" the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] - they were contacted by them.
  - The [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] paid **2,000 gold in advance** to transport the ledger.
  - The ledger was not theirs - they were hired couriers.
  - **Delivery Destination**: [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] (a location in the Rift), where a contact wearing an **Ouroboros pendant** would meet them.
- **The Contact**: A man resembling a **Temple Guard** met them in **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Fultheim\|Fultheim]]** to give them the ledger. He smelled of sulfur (possibly from being near Morrowind's volcanoes). The meeting happened in the market; they departed the next day.

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s Approach**:
- Offered small comforts (sitting in a chair, resting legs) in exchange for cooperation.
- Used the contrast between the cooperative prisoner's comfort and the uncooperative one's continued suffering.
- Asked philosophical questions about their choices and whether they deserved their fate.
- The scared prisoner ("the little guy") cooperated readily after initial intimidation.

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s Input**:
- Delivered a speech about poor business decisions leading to their current predicament.
- Called them "morons" for their choices.

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s Question**:
- Asked how they obtained the moon sugar.
- Answer: Smugglers in **[[Info/Locations/Morrowind/Ebonheart\|Ebonheart]]** (Morrowind). They bring it from there to Skyrim.
- She confirmed the Khajiit caravans were not involved.

### [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik]] the Courier
- On the way back from the longhouse, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik the Quick]]** (the perpetually running courier) approached the party.
- He delivered a **letter from the College of [[Info/Locations/Other Skyrim/Winterhold\|Winterhold]]** about reconstruction.
- Before he could sprint away, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** tripped him with her tail.
- He stumbled, stopped for the first time, blamed a stone, and ran off.
- **Mission Accomplished**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] finally tripped him.

## Day 3: 11th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600 - The Execution

### Morning Preparations
- A **horn** sounded in the distance - the signal for the execution day.
- [[Info/Locations/Falderstad/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] fell unusually quiet. No music, no bells, just the horn.
- **Temperature**: -6°C
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** woke first and attempted to help with breakfast (unsuccessfully): *"Food goes into fire, right?"*
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** reminded her there was leftover stew.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** woke with a smile, excited for the day.

### [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]'s Visit
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]** arrived with a tray of four cups of **warm mead**.
- *Quote*: "Today the Law takes blood. May this keep you warm for the day."
- The party toasted: "To our good health. May we not end up in their position ever."

### The Summons
- After two hours, the horn sounded three times.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]** knocked once and entered, summoning them to the longhouse.

### Preparing the Executioner
**[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** was taken to the Jarl's quarters by **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]**:
- His armor was removed.
- He was painted with **Nordic runes** across his entire body and face using **fresh stag blood** (killed that morning).
- He donned ceremonial garments:
  - A **wolf pelt mantle** (with the wolf's head intact)
  - **Bear fur armor**
  - A **Nordic ceremonial helmet** with **stag antlers** from the morning's kill
  - The helmet covered his face with a slit covered by cloth - only his eyes visible.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** took a moment to look at his regular helmet before setting it down.

### The Procession
**Order of March**:
1. Jarl [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Gorm]]
2. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]] (Lawspeaker)
3. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonja]], and **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Agni\|Agni]]** (a tall Nord man with a braided beard)
4. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Helga_Cairn-Shield\|Helga]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]
5. The Fangs of Fjelhørst (Party)
6. Everyone else

**The Walk**:
- People lined the path from the eastern gate to the longhouse, then out of the city.
- Warriors stood in clan furs; hunters wore charms.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonja]], and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Agni\|Agni]]** played "Suurin."
- The condemned were brought forth in chains.
- The party walked for nearly an hour through crunching snow.
- Ravens circled overhead.
- More people joined the procession along the way.

### The Holmgang Field
- The execution site: A **circle of standing stones** half-buried in frost.
- **Braziers** burned at each cardinal direction with thick grey smoke.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]** finished making a circle of ash around the fire pit.
- The crowd formed a ring around the circle.

### The Executioner's Blade
- **Jarl [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Gorm]]** presented the blade - a claymore of **Stalhrim** (ancient ice-like material).
- *Quote*: "Let it be known that this blade is not yours. It belongs to the law. When the law is done, you will return it clean or not at all."
- The blade was ancient, from the time when **Ysgramor walked the land**.
- Noticeably blade-heavy, designed for executions.

### The Ritual
**[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]'s Opening Words**:
- *"Before the gods and the blood of men, the Wergeld is called. Let those who conspire against the Clans pay the price they owe."*
- He gestured to the wraith ([[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]): *"By [[Info/Lore/Shor\|Shor]]'s hand, let law be done."*

**The Ceremony** (for each of the first four prisoners):
1. The condemned was forced to knees before a stone.
2. **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]** chanted in Nordic - a call to **[[Info/Lore/Shor\|Shor]]** to weigh souls and for **[[Info/Lore/Kyne\|Kyne]]** to bear witness.
3. **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Svala\|Svala]]** brought a cup of mead: *"Drink and meet your end with open eyes."*
4. **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** placed a hand on their shoulder, promising a quick death.
5. The execution (horizontal swing while kneeling).
6. **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Senya\|Senya]]'s Chant**: *"Blood for blood, oath for oath, [[Info/Lore/Shor\|Shor]]'s law holds, through flesh is loath. [[Info/Lore/Kyne\|Kyne]] bear the wind, and the dead man's breath, to Sovngarde or to [[Info/Lore/Orkey\|Orkey]]'s death."*
7. The crowd bowed their heads.

**The Executions**:
- **First Dunmer** (cooperative one from interrogation): [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] promised a quick death. Clean cut.
- **Second Dunmer**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] said nothing, just placed his hand on the shoulder. Very clean cut.
- **Third Dunmer**: Same procedure. Clean.
- **Fourth Dunmer** (the scared one, "the little guy"): [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] looked him in the eye. Through his gaze alone, he communicated regret that it came to this, but acknowledgment of the wrong path taken. The prisoner nodded and faced him straight. Clean cut.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s Reaction**: Her excitement faded with each execution. By the fourth (the scared one), her face had fallen. It became "a bit too real."

**The Fifth Dunmer** (the defiant one who spat on [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]):
- When given the mead, he **spat it out** onto the rocks.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]** held up his staff. Everything stilled - even the wind.
- The mead was taken from him.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]'s Words**: *"You disrespect our traditions. And so no word of you remains."*
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] was guided behind the prisoner (denied the dignity of facing his executioner).
- **The Execution**: [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] noticed [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s distress but proceeded. The blade **struggled through the bone**, taking longer - a less clean, more painful death than he deserved.
- The body was carried to the **edge of the woods** and left there (denied the honor of the pyre).

### After the Executions
**[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torik\|Torik]]'s Closing**:
- Dipped his hand in blood-diluted snow.
- Traced a spiral rune upon the land.
- *"The deed is done. Let none speak of vengeance or guilt. The blood is paid and the gods are sated."*
- The bodies (except the last) were carried to a pyre in the distance and burned.

**Jarl [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Jarl_Gorm\|Gorm]]** approached [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]:
- Shook his hand.
- *"You have the hand of a just man. Skyrim will remember your deed."**

**The Skalds' Gift**:
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Sonya\|Sonja]]** presented [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] with a **carved bone totem** with Nordic runes.
