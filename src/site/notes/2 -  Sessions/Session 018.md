# Session 018 - Downtime, Investigation & The Nordic Execution

## Summary
A three-day downtime session in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] following the defeat of the Ragged Lord. The party shopped, gathered intelligence, and witnessed a traditional Nordic execution ritual. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] learned the Fox's Guile rune, commissioned a bow for [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]], and recruited him as an informant. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] (full name revealed: **Kirya Ma'Tael**) received a cryptic letter from her best friend and bonded with the visiting skalds **Senya** and **Sonja**. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] received the Jack of All Trades spellbook and struggled emotionally with the execution. **Gabrielle Bonilla** of the Mages Guild arrived with news that the Ritual Orb is a **Harrowstorm Engine Core** from the Grey Host. The party interrogated the Dunmer prisoners, learning of their connection to **House Hlaalu** and the **Coiled Serpent**. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] served as executioner in a full Nordic ritual, earning a place in the Book of Falderstad and a bone totem proving his participation in Nordic lore.

## Day 2: 10th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600

### Weather Change
- Overnight, snow began falling heavily.
- **Temperature**: -13°C
- Falderstad was blanketed in snow, muffling all sound.
- The party awoke to a winter landscape.

### Discussing Fur-Lined Armor
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** noticed the snowfall and suggested the party might need to add fur to their armor.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** agreed to help with the modifications using pelts from previous hunts.

### The Mages Guild Visitor - Gabrielle Bonilla
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** left the house to head to the blacksmith.
- He noticed a thin **Breton woman** in a **blue and gold cloak** asking around for the "Fangs of Fjelhørst."
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] shadowed her through the market, watching as she was directed to the Jarl's longhouse.
- At the longhouse, **Jarl Gorm** pointed her toward [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]].
- **Introduction**: She is **Gabrielle Bonilla**, a high-ranking member of the **Mages Guild** and their lead expert on **Daedric artifacts**.
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] brought her to meet the rest of the party.

### News About the Harrowstorm Engine Core
**Gabrielle** delivered critical information about the Ritual Orb:
- It is a **Harrowstorm Engine Core**, a remnant from the **Grey Host** crisis 14-15 years ago.
- The Grey Host was a clan of vampires and werewolves working together, with plans to create undead vampire-werewolf hybrids.
- All their research was destroyed and key members were killed during the "Dark Heart of Skyrim" crisis.
- **The orb is dormant** - without the proper rituals, matrices, and energy source, it cannot activate.
- It was originally taken from **Markarth** and was the only one not retrieved until now.
- It has been wrapped in multiple layers of **anti-magic magic** and wards.
- It departed for **Windhelm** 1-2 days ago, and will eventually be transported to the main guild hall in **Ayr** for safe storage.
- The energy/death around Greenwall likely caused it to briefly awaken when [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] shot it.
- **Gabrielle** mentioned someone named **Vanis** who owes her a favor (related to the orb's storage).

### The Crows (Daedric Creatures)
- **Gabrielle** needed to send a letter and asked about messenger birds.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** pulled out bread and summoned **Jangleplume**, the rotund Daedric crow.
- **Gabrielle** recognized the crows as **Daedric crows** - immortal, teleporting crows that can talk.
- She mentioned they are curious creatures associated with **Nocturnal**.
- Jangleplume once brought her a fake soup spoon when she was in the **Clockwork City**.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** suggested feeding them more bread to make friends with them.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** bought more bread (1 Gold) to keep summoning the crows.

### Fur-Lining the Armor
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** used his smithing skills to fur-line everyone's armor at the blacksmith's forge.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** and the others had to remove their armor and wrap in cloaks while waiting.
- **Smithing Tests**: All successful (using Physical Exertion for +20 bonus).
- **Materials Used**: Pelts from previous hunts.
- **Result**: All party armor is now **fur-lined** for cold weather protection.

### Interrogating the Prisoners
- The party visited the **Jarl's longhouse** to investigate the captured Dunmer smugglers.
- **Helga** led them to the basement cells.
- Five Dunmer remained, chained to the walls on their knees.
- One immediately spat on **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** when they entered. She clenched her fists in silent fury.

#### Obtaining the Ledger
- **Helga** provided the confiscated **Dunmer Ledger**.
- It bore the **Coiled Serpent** symbol.
- Contents (mostly in Dunmeri):
  - General business transactions (lumber, cloth)
  - A loan of **5,000 gold** from a Dunmer bank
  - Dealings with **House Hlaalu**
  - A single mention of **House Delveri**
  - One confirmed reference to the **Coiled Serpent**
- Since it wasn't needed for the trial, the party was allowed to **keep the ledger**.

#### The Interrogation
**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** took the lead, employing intimidation tactics:
- Dragged a chair into the cell making a scraping noise.
- Sat close to the prisoners, establishing dominance.
- The guard watched with interest, eating meat.

**Key Information Obtained**:
- **Employer**: **Beryn Hlaalu**, Grand Master of House Hlaalu (one of the Great Houses of Morrowind).
- **Direct Superior**: **Garen Hlaalu**, who leads operations into Skyrim.
- **Moon Sugar**: Worth 300 gold; they would have been paid 400 gold total for transport. Smuggling moon sugar usually just results in fines, not execution.
- **The Coiled Serpent Connection**:
  - They didn't "join" the Coiled Serpent - they were contacted by them.
  - The Coiled Serpent paid **2,000 gold in advance** to transport the ledger.
  - The ledger was not theirs - they were hired couriers.
  - **Delivery Destination**: Kil Great White (a location in the Rift), where a contact wearing an **Ouroboros pendant** would meet them.
- **The Contact**: A man resembling a **Temple Guard** met them in **Fultheim** to give them the ledger. He smelled of sulfur (possibly from being near Morrowind's volcanoes). The meeting happened in the market; they departed the next day.

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]'s Approach**:
- Offered small comforts (sitting in a chair, resting legs) in exchange for cooperation.
- Used the contrast between the cooperative prisoner's comfort and the uncooperative one's continued suffering.
- Asked philosophical questions about their choices and whether they deserved their fate.
- The scared prisoner ("the little guy") cooperated readily after initial intimidation.

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s Input**:
- Delivered a speech about poor business decisions leading to their current predicament.
- Called them "morons" for their choices.

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s Question**:
- Asked how they obtained the moon sugar.
- Answer: Smugglers in **Haven** (Valenwood). They bring it from there to Skyrim.
- She confirmed the Khajiit caravans were not involved.

### Erik the Courier
- On the way back from the longhouse, **Erik** (the perpetually running courier) approached the party.
- He delivered a **letter from the College of Winterhold** about reconstruction.
- Before he could sprint away, **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** tripped him with her tail.
- He stumbled, stopped for the first time, blamed a stone, and ran off.
- **Mission Accomplished**: Onasha finally tripped him.

## Day 3: 11th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600 - The Execution

### Morning Preparations
- A **horn** sounded in the distance - the signal for the execution day.
- Falderstad fell unusually quiet. No music, no bells, just the horn.
- **Temperature**: -6°C
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** woke first and attempted to help with breakfast (unsuccessfully): *"Food goes into fire, right?"*
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** reminded her there was leftover stew.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** woke with a smile, excited for the day.

### Helga's Visit
- **Helga** arrived with a tray of four cups of **warm mead**.
- *Quote*: "Today the Lord takes blood. Let this keep you warm for the dead."
- The party toasted: "To our good health. May we not end up in their position ever."

### The Summons
- After two hours, the horn sounded three times.
- **Helga** knocked once and entered, summoning them to the longhouse.

### The Procession
**Order of March**:
1. Jarl Gorm
2. Tarik (Speaker for the Dead)
3. Senya, Sonja, and **Agni** (a tall Nord man with a braided beard)
4. Helga and Valor
5. The Fangs of Fjelhørst (Party)
6. Everyone else

**The Walk**:
- People lined the path from the eastern gate to the longhouse, then out of the city.
- Warriors stood in clan furs; hunters wore charms.
- **Senya, Sonja, and Agni** played "The Song of the Wolf."
- The condemned were brought forth in chains.
- The party walked for nearly an hour through crunching snow.
- Ravens circled overhead.
- More people joined the procession along the way.

### The Standing Stones
- The execution site: A **circle of standing stones** half-buried in frost.
- **Braziers** burned at each cardinal direction with thick grey smoke.
- **Tarik** finished making a circle of ash around the fire pit.
- The crowd formed a ring around the circle.

### Preparing the Executioner
**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** was taken to the Jarl's quarters by **Tarik**:
- His armor was removed.
- He was painted with **Nordic runes** across his entire body and face using **fresh stag blood** (killed that morning).
- He donned ceremonial garments:
  - A **wolf pelt mantle** (with the wolf's head intact)
  - **Bear fur armor**
  - A **Nordic ceremonial helmet** with **stag antlers** from the morning's kill
  - The helmet covered his face with a slit covered by cloth - only his eyes visible.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** took a moment to look at his regular helmet before setting it down.

### The Executioner's Blade
- **Jarl Gorm** presented the blade - a claymore of **Stalhrim** (ancient ice-like material).
- *Quote*: "Let it be known that this blade is not yours. It belongs to the law. When the law is done, you will return it clean or not at all."
- The blade was ancient, from the times of the Jarl's grandmother.
- Noticeably blade-heavy, designed for executions.

### The Ritual
**Tarik's Opening Words**:
- *"Before the gods and the blood of men, the way to Sovngarde is called. Those who conspired against the clans pay the price they owe."*
- He gestured to the wraith (Durov): *"By short hand, let law be done."*

**The Ceremony** (for each of the first four prisoners):
1. The condemned was forced to knees before a stone.
2. **Tarik** chanted in Nordic - a call to **Shor** to weigh souls and for **Kyne** to bear witness.
3. **Valor** brought a cup of mead: *"Drink and meet your end with open eyes."*
4. **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** placed a hand on their shoulder, promising a quick death.
5. The execution (horizontal swing while kneeling).
6. **Senya's Chant**: *"Blood for blood, oath for oath. Shor's law holds, some flesh is loath. Kyne bear the wind and the dead man's breath, to Sovngarde or to Orkey's death."*
7. The crowd bowed their heads.

**The Executions**:
- **First Dunmer** (cooperative one from interrogation): [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] promised a quick death. Clean cut.
- **Second Dunmer**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] said nothing, just placed his hand on the shoulder. Very clean cut.
- **Third Dunmer**: Same procedure. Clean.
- **Fourth Dunmer** (the scared one, "the little guy"): [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] looked him in the eye. Through his gaze alone, he communicated regret that it came to this, but acknowledgment of the wrong path taken. The prisoner nodded and faced him straight. Clean cut.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s Reaction**: Her excitement faded with each execution. By the fourth (the scared one), her face had fallen. It became "a bit too real."

**The Fifth Dunmer** (the defiant one who spat on [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]):
- When given the mead, he **spat it out** onto the rocks.
- **Tarik** held up his staff. Everything stilled - even the wind.
- The mead was taken from him.
- **Tarik's Words**: *"You disrespect our traditions. And so no word of you remains."*
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] was guided behind the prisoner (denied the dignity of facing his executioner).
- **The Execution**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] noticed [[Onasha's\|Onasha's]] distress but proceeded. The blade **struggled through the bone**, taking longer - a less clean, more painful death than he deserved.
- The body was carried to the **edge of the woods** and left there (denied the honor of the pyre).

### After the Executions
**Tarik's Closing**:
- Dipped his hand in blood-diluted snow.
- Traced a spiral rune upon the land.
- *"The deed is done. Let none speak of vengeance or guilt. The blood is paid and the gods are sated."*
- The bodies (except the last) were carried to a pyre in the distance and burned.

**Jarl Gorm** approached [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]:
- Shook his hand.
- *"You have the hand of a just man. Falderstad will remember your deed."*

**The Skalds' Gift**:
- **Sonja** presented [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] with a **carved bone totem** with Nordic runes.
- *"Proof of your participation in Nordic lore. May Kyne walk with you wherever your travels lead."*
- The party now has **two totems** (including one from a previous session).

### The Return & The Feast
**The Walk Back**:
- As the sun sank, the bodies still burned on the pyre.
- Blue smoke rose above Falderstad.
- **Senya's Voice** carried across the wind: *"Lore stands and blood cools. The wind bears the truth, Allfather. Skyrim sleeps unashamed. The gods have seen and nodded."*

**The Feast** at the Longhouse:
- A feast was prepared in the Jarl's longhouse.
- Attendees: The party, the skalds, Tarik, Helga, Valor, and Jarl Gorm.
- **Food**: The stag killed that morning was the only meat, served with many sides.
- **Drink**: Horse Blood Mead (hints of juniper berries and smoked salt) from The Wounded Boar.

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]'s Drinking Contest with Jarl Gorm**:
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] matched the Jarl drink for drink.
- After 14-15 drinks (nearly a liter of mead), [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] burned through all his stamina.
- Failed his navigation roll while trying to walk home.
- **Passed out** in the street near the old tanning guild house (where they met [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]]).

**Other Party Members**:
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** asked to play her lute with the skalds, providing ambient background music.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** stayed for politeness but left early after about 10 minutes, emotionally affected by the execution.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** observed but remained troubled.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] left [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] a letter** at her sleeping spot: *"I saw you get shook from today. If there's anything you want to get off your chest, I will understand more than anyone."* (Unsigned, but with a flower drawing she would recognize as Periwyn's style.)

### Recording Names in the Book of Falderstad
**Sonja** requested the party's full names for the **Book of Falderstad**:
- **Durov of Colovia**
- **Kirya Ma'Tael** (first time her surname is revealed to everyone)
- **Periwyn Lunclaire**
- **Onasha**

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s Music Training**:
- Before the evening ended, [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] asked **Senya** and **Sonja** if they could stay a day or two to teach her music.
