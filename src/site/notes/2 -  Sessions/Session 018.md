# Session 018 - Downtime, Investigation & The Nordic Execution

## Summary
A three-day downtime session in [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] following the defeat of the Ragged Lord. The party shopped, gathered intelligence, and witnessed a traditional Nordic execution ritual. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] learned the Fox's Guile woad, commissioned a bow for [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Hoki\|Hoki]], and recruited him as an informant. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]] (full name revealed: **Kirya Ma'Tael**) received a cryptic letter from her best friend and bonded with the visiting skalds **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]]** and **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Sonya\|Sonja]]**. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] received the Jack of Trades spellbook and struggled emotionally with the execution. **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle Benele]]** of the [[Info/Organizations/Mages_Guild\|Mages Guild]] arrived with news that the Ritual Orb is a **Harrowstorm Engine Core** from the [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]]. The party interrogated the Dunmer prisoners, learning of their connection to **[[Info/Organizations/House_Hlaalu\|House Hlaalu]]** and the **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]**. [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] served as executioner in a full Nordic ritual, earning a place in the Book of Falderstad and a bone totem proving his participation in Nordic Law.

## Day 2: 10th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600

### Weather Change
- Overnight, snow began falling heavily.
- **Temperature**: -13°C
- [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] was blanketed in snow, muffling all sound.
- The party awoke to a winter landscape.

### Discussing Fur-Lined Armor
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** noticed the snowfall and suggested the party might need to add fur to their armor.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** agreed to help with the modifications using pelts from previous hunts.

### The Mages Guild Visitor - Gabrielle Benele
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** left the house to head to the blacksmith.
- He noticed a thin **Breton woman** in a **blue and gold cloak** asking around for the "Fangs of Fjelhørst."
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] shadowed her through the market, watching as she was directed to the Jarl's longhouse.
- At the longhouse, **Jarl [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gorm\|Gorm]]** pointed her toward [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]].
- **Introduction**: She is **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle Benele]]**, a high-ranking member of the **[[Info/Organizations/Mages_Guild\|Mages Guild]]** and their lead expert on **Daedric artifacts**.
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] brought her to meet the rest of the party.

### News About the Harrowstorm Engine Core
**[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle]]** delivered critical information about the Ritual Orb:
- It is a **Harrowstorm Engine Core**, a remnant from the **[[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]]** crisis 14-15 years ago.
- The [[Info/Lore/Grey_Host\|Grey Host]] was a clan of vampires and werewolves working together, with plans to create undead vampire-werewolf hybrids.
- All their research was destroyed and key members were killed during the "Dark Heart of Skyrim" crisis.
- **The orb is dormant** - without the proper rituals, matrices, and energy source, it cannot activate.
- It was originally taken from **[[Info/Locations/Markarth\|Markarth]]** and was the only one not retrieved until now.
- It has been wrapped in multiple layers of **Anti-Daedric wards**.
- It departed for **[[Info/Locations/Winterhold\|Winterhold]]** 1-2 days ago, and will eventually be transported to the main guild hall in **[[Info/Locations/Eyevea\|Eyevea]]** for safe storage.
- The energy/death around [[Info/Locations/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] likely caused it to briefly awaken when [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] shot it.
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle]]** mentioned someone named **Vanus** who owes her a favor (related to the orb's storage).

### The Crows (Daedric Creatures)
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle]]** needed to send a letter and asked about messenger birds.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** pulled out bread and summoned **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron Jangleplume]]**, the rotund Daedric crow.
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gabrielle_Benele\|Gabrielle]]** recognized the crows as **Daedric crows** - immortal, teleporting crows that can talk.
- She mentioned they are curious creatures associated with **[[Info/Lore/Nocturnal\|Nocturnal]]**.
- [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron Jangleplume]] once brought her a fake soup spoon when she was in the **Clockwork City**.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** suggested feeding them more bread to make friends with them.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** bought more bread (1 Gold) to keep summoning the crows.

### Fur-Lining the Armor
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** used his smithing skills to fur-line everyone's armor at the blacksmith's forge.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** and the others had to remove their armor and wrap in cloaks while waiting.
- **Smithing Tests**: All successful (using Physical Exertion for +20 bonus).
- **Materials Used**: Pelts from previous hunts.
- **Result**: All party armor is now **fur-lined** for cold weather protection.

### Interrogating the Prisoners
- The party visited the **Jarl's longhouse** to investigate the captured Dunmer smugglers.
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]]** led them to the basement cells.
- Five Dunmer remained, chained to the walls on their knees.
- One immediately spat on **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** when they entered. She clenched her fists in silent fury.

#### Obtaining the Ledger
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]]** provided the confiscated **Dunmer Ledger**.
- It bore the **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]** symbol.
- Contents (mostly in Dunmeri):
  - General business transactions (lumber, cloth)
  - A loan of **5,000 gold** from a Dunmer bank
  - Dealings with **[[Info/Organizations/House_Hlaalu\|House Hlaalu]]**
  - A single mention of **House Telvanni**
  - One confirmed reference to the **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]**
- Since it wasn't needed for the trial, the party was allowed to **keep the ledger**.

#### The Interrogation
**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** took the lead, employing intimidation tactics:
- Dragged a chair into the cell making a scraping noise.
- Sat close to the prisoners, establishing dominance.
- The guard watched with interest, eating meat.

**Key Information Obtained**:
- **Employer**: **Belyn Hlaalu**, Grand Master of [[Info/Organizations/House_Hlaalu\|House Hlaalu]] (one of the Great Houses of Morrowind).
- **Direct Superior**: **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Garen_Hlaalu\|Garen Hlaalu]]**, who leads operations into Skyrim.
- **Moon Sugar**: Worth 300 gold; they would have been paid 400 gold total for transport. Smuggling moon sugar usually just results in fines, not execution.
- **The Coiled Serpent Connection**:
  - They didn't "join" the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] - they were contacted by them.
  - The [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]] paid **2,000 gold in advance** to transport the ledger.
  - The ledger was not theirs - they were hired couriers.
  - **Delivery Destination**: [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]] (a location in the Rift), where a contact wearing an **Ouroboros pendant** would meet them.
- **The Contact**: A man resembling a **Temple Guard** met them in **[[Info/Locations/Fultheim\|Fultheim]]** to give them the ledger. He smelled of sulfur (possibly from being near Morrowind's volcanoes). The meeting happened in the market; they departed the next day.

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]'s Approach**:
- Offered small comforts (sitting in a chair, resting legs) in exchange for cooperation.
- Used the contrast between the cooperative prisoner's comfort and the uncooperative one's continued suffering.
- Asked philosophical questions about their choices and whether they deserved their fate.
- The scared prisoner ("the little guy") cooperated readily after initial intimidation.

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s Input**:
- Delivered a speech about poor business decisions leading to their current predicament.
- Called them "morons" for their choices.

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]'s Question**:
- Asked how they obtained the moon sugar.
- Answer: Smugglers in **[[Info/Locations/Ebonheart\|Ebonheart]]** (Morrowind). They bring it from there to Skyrim.
- She confirmed the Khajiit caravans were not involved.

### [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik]] the Courier
- On the way back from the longhouse, **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik the Quick]]** (the perpetually running courier) approached the party.
- He delivered a **letter from the College of [[Info/Locations/Winterhold\|Winterhold]]** about reconstruction.
- Before he could sprint away, **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** tripped him with her tail.
- He stumbled, stopped for the first time, blamed a stone, and ran off.
- **Mission Accomplished**: Onasha finally tripped him.

## Day 3: 11th of Sun's Dusk, 2E 600 - The Execution

### Morning Preparations
- A **horn** sounded in the distance - the signal for the execution day.
- [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]] fell unusually quiet. No music, no bells, just the horn.
- **Temperature**: -6°C
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** woke first and attempted to help with breakfast (unsuccessfully): *"Food goes into fire, right?"*
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** reminded her there was leftover stew.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** woke with a smile, excited for the day.

### [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]'s Visit
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]** arrived with a tray of four cups of **warm mead**.
- *Quote*: "Today the Law takes blood. May this keep you warm for the day."
- The party toasted: "To our good health. May we not end up in their position ever."

### The Summons
- After two hours, the horn sounded three times.
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]** knocked once and entered, summoning them to the longhouse.

### Preparing the Executioner
**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** was taken to the Jarl's quarters by **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]**:
- His armor was removed.
- He was painted with **Nordic runes** across his entire body and face using **fresh stag blood** (killed that morning).
- He donned ceremonial garments:
  - A **wolf pelt mantle** (with the wolf's head intact)
  - **Bear fur armor**
  - A **Nordic ceremonial helmet** with **stag antlers** from the morning's kill
  - The helmet covered his face with a slit covered by cloth - only his eyes visible.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** took a moment to look at his regular helmet before setting it down.

### The Procession
**Order of March**:
1. Jarl [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gorm\|Gorm]]
2. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]] (Lawspeaker)
3. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]], [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Sonya\|Sonja]], and **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Agni\|Agni]]** (a tall Nord man with a braided beard)
4. [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]] and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]
5. The Fangs of Fjelhørst (Party)
6. Everyone else

**The Walk**:
- People lined the path from the eastern gate to the longhouse, then out of the city.
- Warriors stood in clan furs; hunters wore charms.
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]], [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Sonya\|Sonja]], and [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Agni\|Agni]]** played "Suurin."
- The condemned were brought forth in chains.
- The party walked for nearly an hour through crunching snow.
- Ravens circled overhead.
- More people joined the procession along the way.

### The Holmgang Field
- The execution site: A **circle of standing stones** half-buried in frost.
- **Braziers** burned at each cardinal direction with thick grey smoke.
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]** finished making a circle of ash around the fire pit.
- The crowd formed a ring around the circle.

### The Executioner's Blade
- **Jarl [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gorm\|Gorm]]** presented the blade - a claymore of **Stalhrim** (ancient ice-like material).
- *Quote*: "Let it be known that this blade is not yours. It belongs to the law. When the law is done, you will return it clean or not at all."
- The blade was ancient, from the time when **Ysgramor walked the land**.
- Noticeably blade-heavy, designed for executions.

### The Ritual
**[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]'s Opening Words**:
- *"Before the gods and the blood of men, the Wergeld is called. Let those who conspire against the Clans pay the price they owe."*
- He gestured to the wraith (Durov): *"By Shor's hand, let law be done."*

**The Ceremony** (for each of the first four prisoners):
1. The condemned was forced to knees before a stone.
2. **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]** chanted in Nordic - a call to **Shor** to weigh souls and for **Kyne** to bear witness.
3. **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]]** brought a cup of mead: *"Drink and meet your end with open eyes."*
4. **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]** placed a hand on their shoulder, promising a quick death.
5. The execution (horizontal swing while kneeling).
6. **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]]'s Chant**: *"Blood for blood, oath for oath, Shor's law holds, through flesh is loath. Kyne bear the wind, and the dead man's breath, to Sovngarde or to Orkey's death."*
7. The crowd bowed their heads.

**The Executions**:
- **First Dunmer** (cooperative one from interrogation): [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] promised a quick death. Clean cut.
- **Second Dunmer**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] said nothing, just placed his hand on the shoulder. Very clean cut.
- **Third Dunmer**: Same procedure. Clean.
- **Fourth Dunmer** (the scared one, "the little guy"): [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] looked him in the eye. Through his gaze alone, he communicated regret that it came to this, but acknowledgment of the wrong path taken. The prisoner nodded and faced him straight. Clean cut.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s Reaction**: Her excitement faded with each execution. By the fourth (the scared one), her face had fallen. It became "a bit too real."

**The Fifth Dunmer** (the defiant one who spat on [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]):
- When given the mead, he **spat it out** onto the rocks.
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]** held up his staff. Everything stilled - even the wind.
- The mead was taken from him.
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]'s Words**: *"You disrespect our traditions. And so no word of you remains."*
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] was guided behind the prisoner (denied the dignity of facing his executioner).
- **The Execution**: [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] noticed [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s distress but proceeded. The blade **struggled through the bone**, taking longer - a less clean, more painful death than he deserved.
- The body was carried to the **edge of the woods** and left there (denied the honor of the pyre).

### After the Executions
**[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]]'s Closing**:
- Dipped his hand in blood-diluted snow.
- Traced a spiral rune upon the land.
- *"The deed is done. Let none speak of vengeance or guilt. The blood is paid and the gods are sated."*
- The bodies (except the last) were carried to a pyre in the distance and burned.

**Jarl [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gorm\|Gorm]]** approached [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]:
- Shook his hand.
- *"You have the hand of a just man. Skyrim will remember your deed."**

### The Return & The Feast
**The Walk Back**:
- As the sun sank, the bodies still burned on the pyre.
- Blue smoke rose above [[Info/Locations/Falderstad\|Falderstad]].
- **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Senya\|Senya]]'s Voice** carried across the wind: *"Law stands, and blood cools. The wind bears the truth. Faldar's kin sleep unshamed. The gods have seen, and nodded."**

**The Feast** at the Longhouse:
- A feast was prepared in the Jarl's longhouse.
- Attendees: The party, the skalds, [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Torik\|Torik]], [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Helga\|Helga]], [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Svala\|Svala]], and Jarl [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gorm\|Gorm]].
- **Food**: The stag killed that morning was the only meat, served with many sides.
- **Drink**: Boar's Blood Mead (hints of juniper berries and smoked salt) from [[Info/Locations/The_Wounded_Boar\|The Wounded Boar]].

**[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]]'s Drinking Contest with Jarl [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Gorm\|Gorm]]**:
- [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] matched the Jarl drink for drink.
- After 14-15 drinks (nearly a liter of mead), [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Durov\|Durov]] burned through all his stamina.
- Failed his navigation roll while trying to walk home.
- **Passed out** in the street near the old tanning guild house (where they met [[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Stryder\|Stryder]]).

**Other Party Members**:
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** asked to play her lute with the skalds, providing ambient background music.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]** stayed for politeness but left early after about 10 minutes, emotionally affected by the execution.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** observed but remained troubled.
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] left [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]] a letter** at her sleeping spot: *"I saw you get shook from today.
