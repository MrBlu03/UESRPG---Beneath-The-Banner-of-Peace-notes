# Session 001 - 15/12/2024

## Summary
First session. The party — [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] — are summoned to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil Keep]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]] to investigate a water dispute between [[Info/Organizations/Other/Clan_Snow-Shod\|Clan Snow-Shod]] and the fishermen of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]]. On the road south, they are ambushed by hired bandits bearing the seal of the [[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]].

## Detailed Recap
- The party was summoned to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Mistveil_Keep\|Mistveil Keep]] in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]]
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Queen_Freidr\|Queen Freidr]] explained the situation: a river near [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] had run low, causing a bitter feud between [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Snow-Shod_Estate\|Snow-Shod Estate]] (an Old Clan of proud farmers upstream) and the fishermen of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]] downstream
- The Queen's guards were tangled in old loyalties, so she sought outsiders with no stake in the feud
- The Queen's steward privately briefed them: the Snow-Shods had dug new irrigation tunnels upstream, causing the river to thin downstream — starving the fishermen's nets and drying the banks
- Before departing, the party explored [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]]'s streets
  - Met [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Torvald_Black-Foot\|Torvald Black-Foot]] of the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Black-Foot Merchant Guild]] — he has a gem the party might want to buy once appraised
  - Met [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hafjorg\|Hafjorg]] at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Scent_of_Serenity\|Scent of Serenity]] (son of the shop owner) — sells healing potions: 2 HP / 6 HP / 10 HP
  - A cabbage merchant, paid for information, shared a curious tale: villagers meeting with a cloaked stranger beneath the moonless sky, and none willing to speak of it in daylight
  - Note: "Eldar was talking to a strange man"
  - Fishermen had stopped trading
- Headed south along the river's thinning edge toward [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Trevas_Bend\|Treva's Bend]]
- **Bandit Ambush**: Figures burst from the brush on the road south
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] was the storm — her warhammer rising and falling in brutal rhythm, caving in the bandit leader's skull
  - The last surviving bandit surrendered and gave his name: **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alaric\|Alaric]]**
  - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alaric\|Alaric]] revealed they hadn't come for gold — they'd been hired to block the road
  - The seal on their instructions bore a **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]**, etched in wax
- Party turned back to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alaric\|Alaric]] bound
  - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Harvold\|Captain Harvold]] took [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alaric\|Alaric]] into custody and paid the bounty
  - [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Captain_Harvold\|Harvold]] urged the group to bring the serpent mark to the Queen directly — whatever this was, it reached beyond a simple feud
