# Session 006 - 2025-04-26

## Summary
The party infiltrated [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] through a forgotten smuggler's tunnel from the Three Banners War while the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] provided a distraction with trebuchet fire. Inside the town, they discovered abandoned buildings, evidence of vampire attacks, and a disturbing Black Sacrament ritual site that summoned a member of the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark Brotherhood]]. After striking a deal with the assassin to eliminate the source of the magical darkness, they explored the keep, finding a horrific ritual altar to [[Info/Lore/Molag_Bal\|Molag Bal]] with a mysterious crimson orb. The magical darkness lifted as the assassin completed his contract, and they rescued a barely alive man who had been crucified and suspended above the altar.

---

## Detailed Recap

### War Camp Preparations
- The party was awakened from uneasy dreams by [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hillevi\|Hillevi]], a [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] healer, who brought warm bread and porridge despite the unnatural darkness of the eclipse
- Many trebuchets had already been reassembled overnight, acting as silent sentinels
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] carefully sketched the war tent—every fold and crease—to find a sense of order while the group ate breakfast
  - The breakfast provided a strength bonus (+5 to strength checks) that would help them during their mission
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] discussed the bone hawk specimen in private:
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] revealed she knows minor necromancy and suggested they might repurpose the creature as a scout
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s distrust was immediate, but they agreed to experiment with reanimating it later, away from other mages and away from the rest of the party
  - Agreed to use it for alchemical ingredients if they decided against necromancy
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] learned from [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hillevi\|Hillevi]] about the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]]:
  - She had been with them for 6 years
  - They typically operate in The Rift region
  - Their members are an ancient order of warriors spread across Skyrim, either descendants or recruits following Ysgramor's traditions
  - Their homeland is now a frozen wasteland
  - She joined seeking glory, entrance to Sovngarde, and to help people. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] found the lack of hidden motive unsettling, but [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hillevi\|Hillevi]]'s demeanor remained open and sincere
  - The [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] operate from a large mead hall

### The Infiltration Plan
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod Wraithbane]] summoned the party to the war tent and outlined the plan beneath heavy canvas and maps inked with battle-lines:
  - The party would enter [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] through a forgotten tunnel, a remnant of the Three Banners War
  - The [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] would launch flaming projectiles as a distraction
  - Once inside, they should head for the keep where the darkness likely originated
  - They received a sketched map of the area
  - Chances of finding survivors were deemed minimal, but any found should be sent through the tunnel into the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Western_Woods\|western woods]]
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod]] clasped their shoulders one by one, saying, "Gods give you strength. And may we meet again in Sovngarde."

### Entering the Tunnel
- The tunnel entrance was hidden under a bed of fallen leaves on a quiet hilltop, a weathered wooden trapdoor
- A 4-meter wooden ladder led down to a flickering torch-lit passage
- Marching order: [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] taking the lead with shield raised, [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] (trailing behind with hesitant steps due to anxiety)
- The tunnel was narrow with barely enough room to move past [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] if he stood in the middle

### Tunnel Exploration
- They found a small dusty storage room where [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] discovered three books:
  - "Herbalist's Guide to Skyrim"
  - "Sithis"
  - "Herbane's Bestiary: Hagravens"
- A smuggler's logbook was found on a table
  - Detailed ledger of smuggled goods including skooma and a chilling list of slaves
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s expression hardened, her scales drying in silent revulsion
- Further exploration revealed a larger room that appeared to be the smugglers' living quarters
  - Contained bedrolls and rusted shackles amid crumbling walls
  - Initially seemed like a dead end until [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] noticed part of the wall was slightly offset
- The tunnel ended at a false wall (wooden panel) that [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] brought his axe crashing through
- This led to the basement of the local inn, among cracked casks and broken shelves, accessible via a bookshelf door

### Inside [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] paused to sample an old keg of mead in the cellar and noted it had a sharp, berry tang
- The inn's main floor was worse, filled with dead bodies showing claw marks and overturned furniture
- As they explored, trebuchet fire hit nearby buildings, shaking the walls
- The party maintained their formation as they stacked up to climb the stairs from the basement
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] found the innkeeper's room containing a metal safe
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] snapped a few lockpicks before opening it, revealing:
  - 100 gold
  - A business ledger and journal with concerning entries:
    - The captain of [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] had been absent-minded
    - Four cloaked strangers claiming nobility paid for a week in advance 3 days prior, only to vanish that night
    - The town felt "wrong"
    - Reports of ghost lights in the hills on the 7th of Last Seed
    - Hunters reported empty woods on the 14th of Last Seed
    - The "nobles" were listed by name on the 20th of Last Seed: A. Vettien, M. Carcette, I. Tharn, V. Alvuin (at least two were Breton names, and all names were suspected aliases)
      - It was noted that I. Tharn couldn't be legitimate as the only Tharns are a powerful Cyrodiilic family, high on the Elder Council
- Outside, they observed permafrost-covered ground and flaming debris raining from above
- Clouds of bats were seen fluttering in the sky toward the war camp, acting as scouts, but eventually ceased
- The keep was visible on a small hill across the central courtyard

### House Explorations
- They detoured through nearby homes in search of survivors
- Blacksmith's house (attached to a smithy):
  - Held only chaos with shattered tables and scattered belongings
  - Main floor: Three doses of dried elves ear herb hanging by the extinguished hearth
  - Basement: Smithing supplies and 4 corundum ingots (taken by [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]])
- Final house before the keep:
  - Door was locked; [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] failed to pick it
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] broke it down
  - Main floor: The upstairs still held some semblance of order with furniture largely intact
    - Small items were knocked over, but larger furniture remained in place
  - Basement: Contained a scene of horror - the Black Sacrament

### The [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark Brotherhood]]
- The basement contained a ritual circle:
  - A circle of candles connected by dried blood
  - Human remains in the center
    - Included a skull, flesh, heart, and pieces of ribcage
  - A dull ebony dagger etched with strange Daedric runes
    - Similar to ones seen at statues in the Velothi mountains north on the road between Eastmarch and the region bordering Morrowind
  - A book titled "A Kiss, Sweet Mother"
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] froze in panic, recognizing this as the Black Sacrament ritual to summon the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark Brotherhood]]
  - Explained in hushed tones that the ritual requires the remains of the intended victim
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] rushed in to coax her back and calm her down
- As they turned to leave, a shadow moved at the top of the stairs, descending slowly, foot by foot, cloaked in silence and menace
- The figure passed by their drawn weapons to the ritual site, kneeling and touching the circle with a single motion to reignite the extinguished candles
  - Green flames erupted, and the air thickened as the figure solidified—an assassin answering the Sacrament
  - He was about the size of [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]
  - Appeared to be a Dunmer, though his face was obscured
  - He spoke calmly and precisely, explaining the sacrament had called him, and offered his services since the summoner was absent
  - The assassin uttered [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s family name. Startled, she demanded to know how he knew it, and he gave a cryptic reply: "A name like theirs carries its weight across all harbors, even those less visited."
  - The room fell into tense deliberation, and the party contracted him to kill the mage responsible for [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]]'s darkness, shifting from [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s vengeance
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] signed the contract parchment with his blood
  - The assassin nodded once and dissolved into shadow, saying "And so begins a contract, bound by blood."

### Lifting the Darkness
- Fearing the keep still held too many dangers, the party waited inside the building while the assassin worked
- After 15 minutes, the thunder of trebuchet fire faded
- After 30 minutes, a faint, golden light pierced the clouds. Sunlight.
- Shrieking death-cries were heard from both the war camp and keep areas
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] recognized them as vampires burning in the dawn (she loved the sound of that)
- They rushed to the keep

### The Keep
- Inside the keep, they found only horror:
  - Bones and flesh spiraled in deliberate patterns across the stone floor
  - Flayed skin hung from broken spears and symbols daubed in blood coated the walls
  - An altar of black stone to [[Info/Lore/Molag_Bal\|Molag Bal]], the God of Domination, leering in monstrous glory, with blood flowing from the statue's gaping maw into a basin beneath
  - A crimson orb pulsing with faint, malevolent light suspended above the basin
  - Behind the altar hung a man—crucified on rusted spears. Barely alive.
- The victim:
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] rushed to his side
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] steadied his shallow breath with a touch of healing magic
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] uncorked a potion and tipped it between his lips
  - For a moment there was hope, then his body convulsed, going rigid before falling deathly still. Paralyzed, but alive.
  - They decided to lay him gently against the wall while they explored further
- The crimson orb:
  - Radiated the unmistakable presence of Coldharbour ([[Info/Lore/Molag_Bal\|Molag Bal]]'s realm)
  - Resisted movement attempts by [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s silver arrows bounced off it like pebbles, emitting white-red lightning
- The [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] arrived, reporting the orb emitted no detectable magic
- They freed the victim, with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hillevi\|Hillevi]] gasping and providing healing but noting he was barely clinging to life
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] attempted to access side doors, but thick ice sealed them shut
- Upon learning [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen]] had fallen in the assault, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s grief erupted in fury and her warhammer struck the ice wall, sending cracks spidering across it
- A heavy thud broke the stillness—a fanged, ancient vampire's head dropped from the ceiling, confirming the assassination was complete
- High on a torch sconce, the [[Info/Organizations/Criminal/Dark_Brotherhood\|Dark Brotherhood]] assassin stood, poised and silent, performing impressive parkour moves
- He didn't ask for payment, instead lingering with quiet interest. He said, "My masters find your story fascinating. They see great potential in you. We will certainly meet again."
- Before he departed, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] flipped him a single gold coin, saying "This will do—for now." The assassin then vanished into shadow.
