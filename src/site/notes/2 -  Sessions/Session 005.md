# Session 005 - 2025-03-29 (18th–23rd of Heartfire, 2E 600)

## Summary  
The party embarked on a six-day journey to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]], encountering unsettling events along the way, including undead bone hawks and hanged corpses. Upon arrival, they found the [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] war camp devastated by a vampire ambush. The ice wall surrounding [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] remained unyielding, but a potential smuggler's tunnel was discovered. Plans were made to infiltrate the town and restore sunlight by disrupting the unnatural eclipse.

---

## Detailed Recap  

### Preparation in [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Riften\|Riften]] (18th of Heartfire)
- The party woke early at [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Wanderers_Rest\|Wanderer's Rest]].  
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** was first to rise, followed by **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** and **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**, who ate a hearty breakfast prepared by the innkeeper's daughter, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Astrid\|Astrid]]**.  
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** ate breakfast alone on the upper balcony, sketching the view quietly in her journal. When the others returned, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] tossed a pebble up to catch her attention. Mildly annoyed, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] called out that she would've heard them if they'd just called her name, tucked her journal away, and joined them.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Eirik_the_Quick\|Eirik the Quick]]** delivered a message: The party (referred to as "The Fangs") was expected at the stables by **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hjar\|Hjar of Clan Black-Foot]]** within an hour.  
- The group visited **[[Info/Locations/The Rift/Scent_of_Serenity\|Scent of Serenity]]** to restock:  
  - Firebombs/Fire Salts were unavailable or too expensive to acquire, but [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alara\|Alara]] offered supplies to craft them (declined).  
- At the stables, they met the innkeeper **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Bjorin\|Bjorin]]** overseeing provisions, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hjar\|Hjar]]**, and the travel [[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]]: **Reyloth**, **Gronkar**, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Ralof\|Ralof]]**, and **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Hrongar\|Hrongar]]**.  
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** assisted with packing supplies onto the cart.  
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]]** arrived with heavy, metal-clad footsteps, flanked by the sharp-eyed **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild the Unwavering]]** and the lean, hawkish **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen the Mer-Killer]]** (members of the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Cult_of_Ysmir\|Cult of Ysmir]]).
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir]] outlined travel logistics:  
  - Pace: 5 km/hour, 40 km/day, 8 hours of walking daily.  
  - Assigned roles:  
    - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** and **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**: Guard duty.  
    - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**: Campsite preparation.  
    - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**: Healing.  
- The convoy set off just as sunlight crested the Velothi Mountains.

### Journey to [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] (6 Days)  
**Day 1:**  
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** foraged ingredients en route through birch forests and craggy passes.  
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild]]** questioned **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** about her travels as a Khajiit. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] replied quietly that she was searching for someone, declining to elaborate.
- That evening, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild]] prodded more, sharing stories of her own youth among the Skaal.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** had a nightmare about her parents as bloodfiends.  

**Day 2:**  
- Under bright skies, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** shared the reins with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen]], discussing tactics and past battles. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] revealed his helmet-wearing was due to paranoia about headshots and admitted he could recall little before his mercenary days. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir]] observed that the crest on [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s shield resembled Bruma's old noble lines.  
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** asked **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** about her missing ear. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] plainly explained she lost it in a vampire attack defending the temple of the [[Info/Organizations/Religious/Knights_of_Arkay\|Knights of Arkay]].  
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** dreamed of her decaying twin brother's voice repeating only, *"Survive."*  

**Day 3:**  
- A cold rain fell and fog drifted low over the road.  
- Huddled around a struggling campfire, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]]** shared a grim, reverent story of a company of Nord warriors who died to the last man fighting to earn a place in Sovngarde.  
- No nightmares for **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**.  

**Day 4:**  
- Fog lingered. Undead **bone hawks** were spotted circling ominously, watching the caravan.  
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** shot one cleanly from the sky; **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** sketched its twisted corpse for study.  
  - **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild]]** explained their use in necromancy rituals as watchers for vampire thralls.  
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** collected and kept the bone hawk remains.  

**Day 5:**  
- Discovered **17 hanged, rotting corpses** executed and left along the roadside in the woods.  
  - The grim sight triggered a sharp panic attack in **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**, who fell to her knees shaking. 
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Olen_the_Mer-Killer\|Olen]], and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir]] solemnly cut the bodies down and built a pyre to cremate them.  
- That night, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** was startled awake by a vivid nightmare, striking her head against the tent pole. 
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] approached first, kneeling beside her. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] admitted she suffered from frequent nightmares, fearing sleep as much as she needed it, constantly staying up late to avoid memories. 
  - [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] revealed she too lost loved ones in Elsweyr, offering fragile solidarity: "We can't let the things that took them win twice."
  - Watching silently from her tent, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** noticed a crow land on the cooking rack. It sat perfectly still, unblinking, staring at **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**.  

**Day 6:**  
- By early afternoon (2:30 PM), the sun vanished behind unnatural clouds. A deep darkness fell, mirroring the eclipse during the **Night of Crimson Tears** (linked to **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s** past trauma in Northpoint).  
- Crested a ridge and arrived at the **[[Info/Organizations/Military/Companions\|Companions]] war camp**, now a bloody, sparse ruin.  
  - The Harbinger **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod Wraithbane]]** reported: 100 soldiers reduced to 5 battered survivors after a vampire ambush.  
  - The ice wall surrounding [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Greenwall\|Greenwall]] was unmeltable.  
  - **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** unleashed lightning on vampire corpses in a rage.  
- A scouting team (**[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]**, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]**, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Beitild_the_Unwavering\|Beitild]]**) found a hidden hatch to the west beneath leaves—a forgotten smuggler's tunnel from the Three Banners War leading beneath the fort.  
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]**, still shaken, stayed behind. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod]] instructed her to investigate the remains of the old scattered trebuchets.
- **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Vigrod_Wraithbane\|Vigrod]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Gjellir_Stormcrown\|Gjellir Stormcrown]]'s Plan:**  
  - Rebuild trebuchets to launch burning debris and bombard the fort as a distraction, forcing vampires into the open.  
  - Infiltrate via the tunnel during the assault to locate and disrupt the source of the unnatural eclipse.
