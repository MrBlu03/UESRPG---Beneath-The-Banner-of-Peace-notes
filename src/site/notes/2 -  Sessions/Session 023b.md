# Session 023b

## Summary

Two days after the events at the Guild guildhouse, the party took a morning bounty from the local blacksmith to deal with an increasingly bold bandit gang attacking caravans on the eastern road. The bandits were tracked to a forest camp approximately two hours east of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], led by a powerful **battle-mage** who had been financing and directing the operation. In a swift fight, three of the four bandits were killed; the fourth, a woman who had been a beggar before turning to banditry, was captured and ultimately spared. The party looted a hidden stash near the camp and discovered a **sealed letter bearing an unknown horned mask symbol**—suggesting outside investment in the bandit operation. The party returned to [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] to collect their reward and deliver a recovered emerald pendant to the [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Silver_Antler_Inn\|Silver Antler Inn]].

## Detailed Recap

The session opened with a brief recap of downtime before picking up in the early morning at [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost_Gate_Hall]].

**Pre-session downtime and inventory work:**

- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** had crafted **frenzy bombs** using materials prepared with [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Silver_Antler_Inn\|Nashu]]'s assistance before her departure. The party now carries two frenzy bombs, plus blind and poison variants. She also purchased **three chameleon tinctures** (2-minute duration, 20 gold each) from an alchemist and spent downtime gold on spell learning.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** received the **Bone Ward** minor power from her earlier necromancy lesson — a once-per-day ability that creates a bone ward absorbing one hit before fading. She also finally obtained her **Clairvoyance** spell (Illusion school), which had been in progress. She has been investing heavily in spells and is now at roughly 2,408 gold remaining.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** purchased provisions and is actively building toward the **Unstoppable Might** talent (Expert Strength, 450 XP) which would allow him to wield his longsword one-handed while retaining two-handed damage values — effectively making his shield and sword combo even more devastating.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** had her three silver throwing daggers added to inventory, confirmed her **Knife Fighter** talent, and received a small item update from the GM.

**Kira wakes first**, as usual. She also thanked [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Silver_Antler_Inn\|Nashu]] for the bomb-making assistance before [[Info/Locations/The Rift/Silver_Antler_Inn\|Nashu]]'s quiet departure.

### Morning — The Bhandari Caravan

Before breakfast, **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** approached the **Bhandari caravan** that had been camped near the eastern gate for several weeks and was preparing to depart that morning.

**Conversation with the Bhandari:**

- The Khajiit traders confirmed they were heading south — passing through **Kyal's Crossing** on their way back toward the Rift.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] offered to courier a **package and amulet** northward for them; they in turn asked if she might deliver a **package to the innkeeper at Kyal's Crossing** — a man named **Robin** — on their behalf, as they would no longer be passing through there.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] accepted the delivery task.
- The merchants had been in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] for approximately three weeks and had done good business, noting that merchants in the city were willing to trade despite the Guild's grip.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] shared directions to her family's plantation in **Elsweyr** and offered the caravan hospitality there if they ever returned south — a quiet piece of character warmth.

### Morning — Breakfast and the Brimstone Gold

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** came downstairs and breakfasted at the inn. He attempted (jokingly) to pay with **brimstone gold** — the innkeeper flatly refused. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] asked about where the brimstone gold could be spent.

The innkeeper spoke plainly:
- People were getting tired of the **Guild's control** of the city — the tariffs, the price-fixing, the inability for merchants to set their own prices
- More and more people were **refusing to accept brimstone gold** — it had begun circulating only a few weeks ago, described as a more "efficient" mint, but something felt wrong to those who handled it regularly
- The innkeeper suspected the Guild was devaluing the coin while keeping prices artificially high — squeezing the city from both ends

[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]], sitting nearby, overheard this exchange.

---

### Late Morning — The Bounty Board & the Blacksmith

The party (minus [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] who was doing her own thing nearby) checked the **bounty board**, which was mostly missing-persons notices — almost entirely merchants. They had expected this.

The most actionable notice came from the local **blacksmith**: bandits on the eastern road had grown increasingly bold, now attacking even well-guarded caravans. A recent shipment of **silver ore** — one of his suppliers — had been hit; the cart was still by the roadside. **Reward: 250 gold per person.**

The party went to the blacksmith directly for more information:

- Bandits had been operating on the eastern road for some time, but previously targeted only lone travelers
- They had recently started attacking caravans with four or five guards — using **ambush tactics**, with reports of horses being **spooked** and **fire magic**
- The last incident was approximately **two hours east of the city gate** on horseback
- Dead and injured from the latest attack had been recovered by guards; survivors mentioned the fire and the spooked horses
- The blacksmith also mentioned a **merchant at the market** who had reported a **gold and emerald pendant** stolen in one of the raids — he asked the party to recover it if possible: *"A gold emerald pendant. Quite easy to spot."*

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** negotiated her share of the reward as **smithing apprenticeships** instead of gold — the blacksmith (an Adept) could bring her up at least one level, effectively halving her training costs.

The party also asked the blacksmith his opinion on the brimstone gold — consistent with what the innkeeper said, he refused it outright, adding:
> *"It's a little too soft to the touch. As a man who's worked metal for all these years, something's wrong. It does have a...distinct stench to it."*

He speculated the softness suggested it might be **corundum** quality at best — not actual gold — which could theoretically be melted and worked into steel, but would be useless as true currency.

### Late Morning — Approaching the Bandit Camp

The party mounted up and rode east. Approximately two hours out, they reached the **ruined cart** from the silver shipment. Very little remained — all cargo had been taken, but the containers (sacks and crates) were left behind.

**Investigation at the cart site:**

- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** scouted ahead on stealth, scanning the treeline; she found no one present at the cart
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** examined burn marks on the cart — and felt strong magical residue. The fire had been **incredibly hot and sustained**, consistent with a high-tier Destruction spell, not a torch or mundane fire. She warned the group: *"Whatever mage cast this can conjure a lot more power than I can. If we get hit by it, it is going to hurt a lot."*
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** examined tracks leading north into the woods — approximately **three to four people**, heading toward denser forest
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** cast **Clairvoyance** to help track the footprints, providing a directional trail that [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] was able to follow (passed Survival, aided by the spell's +10 bonus)

After approximately 20 minutes of careful movement through increasingly dense forest, the party **heard the sound of a blacksmith's hammer on an anvil**.

**Tactical approach:**

- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] moved into stealth, scouting ahead. She spotted **one sentry** on an elevated wall — the camp had rudimentary fortifications with two gate openings and a cart inside for cover
- The party agreed [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] would find a concealed position while [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] approached more openly, letting [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] draw attention
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] successfully hid behind a cart inside the camp perimeter without being detected

### Combat — The Bandit Camp

**Encounter: 4 bandits** — 2 warriors (greatsword + greataxe), 1 archer/poacher, 1 battle-mage (leader)

**Round 1:**

- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] was spotted by two sentries who drew their weapons (greatsword and greataxe). He raised his hands: *"I was trying to find [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]."* They were not buying it.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** immediately fired **Sunbeam** (Destruction, 30m line, 1m wide) — both sentries failed to evade. Direct hit on both. (Cost: 12 magicka, 2 AP)
- She followed with **Chain Lightning**, which jumped between three targets — 2 damage each. (Magicka depleted for the round)
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]**, hidden in stealth behind the cart, fired a **silver fire arrow** at the sentry on the wall. The bandit failed his shock test — **arrow to the knee**, literal. The sentry's leg was crippled; he was slowed and barely mobile.

**Subsequent rounds:**

- The battle-mage emerged from his tent and moved toward [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]], casting a **ranged fire spell** — [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] used his **Intimidate** ability (Persuade vs. Strength) to force the mage to miss. The shot went wide and hit the rock in front of him.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] closed distance and **bashed** the nearest warrior with his shield — the warrior was knocked back, fell prone, and lost all AP.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** fired another silver fire arrow at the prone warrior — 8 damage total, not quite enough to finish him.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] used her refreshed AP to cast **Chain Lightning** again — 2 damage to three targets.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] drove his longsword into the warrior he'd bashed — **10 damage**, striking internal organs. The warrior suffered the **Organ Damage** wound. He killed him shortly after with a finishing stab, setting him ablaze.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] moved to engage the mage — missed once (mage evaded barely), took **3 damage** from a retaliatory fire bolt.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] (still hidden) fired at the mage — **14 damage**, wounding his body. Then a follow-up shot **severed his left leg entirely** — a bodkin arrow clean through. The mage collapsed.
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] finished the crippled knee archer with a **10 damage sword strike** to the body — organs again. The man died.
- The remaining warrior saw [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] walk toward him — he threw down his weapon and **surrendered**, collapsing on the anvil, barely alive.

**Post-combat:**

The surviving bandit turned out to be a **woman** — the archer/poacher. She was placed on the anvil. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] gave her a small healing touch (6 HP) to keep her alive for questioning. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] began searching the camp for the amulet.

---

### Interrogation — The Poacher

With the hammer nearby and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] standing over her, the woman was questioned:

- **Why the raids?** — *"To make money. We sell what we steal to whoever takes it."* (Observe: she was telling the truth — no organised fence, purely opportunistic)
- **Where's the stash?** — *"Everything here. Everything we haven't sold."*
- **Who was the mage?** — He had recruited her from the streets of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] some time back — she had been **begging** prior to joining the group. He assembled them to work the roads; she thought it beat what she was doing.
- **Why not leave the city?** — *"Have you seen the state of this city? And travel with a bounty on my head, with no horse, no food?"*

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** pushed her on whether there was something she'd have preferred to do instead of banditry. The poacher admitted: **she had always wanted to be a hunter**. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] pointed out that her bow, her combat skills, and her tracker's instincts were all transferable.

**The offer:**

The party offered the poacher a deal:
- The bounty board would report **three bandits** killed, not four — her name would not appear
- [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] would write a letter of introduction to a contact at **Faldar's Tooth** (a hunter's post south of [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]], in the direction the Bhandari were heading)
- The **Bhandari caravan** was leaving that evening — they could take her as a passenger, getting her out of the Hold without needing to enter the city
- In return: she hands over everything she has, cooperates fully, and genuinely attempts to change

The poacher accepted. [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] gave her **three rations** and returned her **50 gold personal share**, gave back her **bow**, and fashioned her a rough walking stick from the treeline.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] cast a second healing touch before they left.

### The Stash and the Letter

In the camp's **hollow tree stump** near the gate, the party found the bandit mage's personal stash:

- **~100 gold**
- A **silver ring**
- A **sealed letter** — addressed to the mage, discussing *"additional investment coming to the operation"*, signed with a symbol: **a horned mask with curved horns**

None of the party recognised the symbol. The poacher claimed she had never seen it either — the stash had been the mage's personal space.

**Also recovered:**
- The **gold and emerald pendant** (found by [[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]] in the tent, on an end table)
- **One sapphire, two amethysts** (on the mage's body)
- ~40 gold from the other bandits' persons
- Miscellaneous stolen trade goods: silks, dried meat, preserved food

Total from stash and bandits: approximately **140 gold** (placed in communal fund).

## Character Development

### Periwyn
- Clairvoyance proved immediately useful in the field — the spell helped track the bandits through forest terrain and contributed directly to finding the camp
- Her Sunbeam and Chain Lightning combo dealt significant opening damage, establishing her as a serious Destruction combatant despite her lower magicka pool
- She expressed moral concern over the poacher's situation and provided healing beyond what was tactically necessary — consistent with her character's more considered ethics
- Smithing lessons as bounty reward shows forward planning about costs; she's actively working around her gold shortage

### Kirya
- Led the interrogation's compassionate angle — her personal history (street life, caravan pickup, now living better) gave her genuine authority when offering the poacher a way out
- Her silver fire arrows proved devastating — she effectively took a man's leg in a single shot and remained hidden throughout most of the fight
- Is now managing two delivery tasks simultaneously (Bhandari package to Robin; the poacher to Faldar's Tooth) alongside her own quests — she's becoming the party's informal logistics anchor
- Returned the poacher's 50 gold and gave rations from her own stock — consistent with her character's generosity

### Durov
- His Intimidate (Persuade via Strength) ability worked against the mage's fire bolt — a genuinely surprising and effective use of the mechanic that changed the flow of the fight
- Shield bash into prone successfully set up multiple follow-up attacks and demonstrated his growing tactical awareness
- His willingness to let the poacher go — and to write a false bounty report to protect her — suggests he sees the world in more complex terms than his stoic exterior suggests

## Important Revelations

- **Brimstone gold is increasingly being refused**: Merchants and tradespeople across [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]] are rejecting it — the blacksmith and innkeeper both independently confirmed they won't take it. The Guild's monetary control is beginning to erode its own credibility with the city's working population.
- **The Horned Mask Symbol**: A previously unseen sigil — a horned mask with curved horns — appears on a letter in the bandit mage's private stash, referencing external "investment" in the bandit operation. The mage was not an independent actor. Someone was backing him. This is new and unconnected to any known faction so far.
- **The bandits were backed externally**: The mage had resources, talent, and organisation beyond what a typical bandit leader would have. The letter suggests this was an investment arrangement — someone wanted the eastern road disrupted.
