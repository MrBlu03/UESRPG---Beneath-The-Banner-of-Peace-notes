# Session 020 - The Vestige's Bargain

## Summary
The party was summoned to the **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Clan Morvan Longhouse]]** by the Jarl's steward, **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Darius_Caro\|Darius Karo]]**, and offered a bounty to capture or kill a troublesome Dunmer named **Sadrith** who had been accusing citizens of Daedric dealings. After gathering intel from **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Alma\|Alma]]** about the political situation in [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]—specifically how the **[[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]]** essentially controls the city—the party tracked down Sadrith at the abandoned **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Old_Blackfoot_Guild_House\|Blackfoot Merchants Guild house]]**. In a shocking revelation, Sadrith revealed himself to be **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]** (also known as the Crowfather), a legendary hero from the Planemeld. He explained he was investigating a **Cult of [[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]]** and suspicious gold that smells of brimstone. The party agreed to help him in exchange for future favors. They followed **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]]**, an Imperial distributing the tainted gold, into the sewers where they witnessed him communicating with a strange humanoid projection via a **Daedric speaking stone**. After a confrontation involving a **Daedric crocodile creature** and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]'s intervention, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] confessed to working for the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]] and mentioned "**The Grand Architect**." [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] convinced [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] to act as a double agent. The party also discovered that someone high up in the [[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]] has been spotted wearing an **Ouroboros necklace**—the symbol of the **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]**. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] agreed to cover the party's lodging for a week.

### The Vestige Revealed

**Initial Conversation with "Sadrith":**
- He introduced himself as **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|Sadrith Indoril]]**
- Explained his investigation:
  - Daedric activity in the area
  - Gold appearing that potentially originates from **Oblivion**
  - The gold smells of **brimstone**
  - People getting rich too quickly—possible cult involvement

- He claimed to have been part of the **[[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Fighters_Guild\|Fighters Guild]]** during the **Planemeld**
  - He personally infiltrated **Coldharbour**

**The Confrontation:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Kirya\|Kirya]]** challenged his story, questioning the logic of a Great House Dunmer donning a mercenary's cloak.
- Sadrith defended himself sharply but the strain showed. He saw through **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s** deceptive behavior and silence, naming her tribe, dismissing her performance, and noting what she was not.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** became agitated as tempers flared. One name—**[[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Dres\|House Dres]]**—cut deeper than any blade, bringing up old hatreds.
- In a heartbeat, Sadrith suddenly lunged at **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s** throat. He stopped short, realizing his mistake, and retreated. He used magic meant to still panic, not kindle trust.
- Apologies came quickly, edged with a raw hatred for the Dres that surprised even [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]].
- He called **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** *"little flower"*
- He **summoned his crows**, with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Baron_Jangleplume\|Baron Jangleplume]] appearing in a burst of feathers, and revealed his true identity:
  - **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]**
  - Also known as: **The Crowfather**

- The party jumped down after him

**The Projection:**
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** recognized the device as a **[[Info/Findings & Items/Relics & Evidence/Daedric Speaking Stone\|Daedric Speaking Stone]]** (one-way communication)

- *The entity in the projection*: Not entirely human—humanoid but strange/distorted

**Confrontation:**
- The projection ended

### Daedric Attack

**The Creature:**
- A **massive bipedal crocodile creature** came charging down the sewer tunnel toward the party
- It was clearly **Daedric** in origin
- Suddenly it recoiled, fell, and skidded to a stop at their feet
- A **dagger** was embedded in the back of its head

**[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] Returns:**
- Standing in the tunnel behind them was **[[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]**
- He stated that [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] had lied about being [[Info/Organizations/Other/House_Tharn\|House Tharn]]

**Interrogation:**
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] began threatening and torturing [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]], tearing answers loose piece by piece
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] fought back with desperate strength, defiantly headbutting him and breaking [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]'s nose
- Dark smoke swallowed them both as [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] vanished with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]]
- They reappeared moments later with screams and shattered stone—[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]] was hurled against the wall at terrible speed
- [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]], slightly winded, allowed the party to question the now-terrified [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Varran\|Varran]]
- Before the Fangs left, names were exchanged with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]—**[[Info/Characters/NPCs/Archmagus_Mortgren\|Mortgren]], [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Adda\|Adda]]**—tying threads back to older horrors.
