# Session 020 - The Vestige's Bargain

## Summary
The party was summoned to the **Clan Morvan Longhouse** by the Jarl's steward, **[[Darius Karo\|Darius Karo]]**, and offered a bounty to capture or kill a troublesome Dunmer named **Sadrith** who had been accusing citizens of Daedric dealings. After gathering intel from **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]]** about the political situation in [[Kjalgrad\|Kjalgrad]]—specifically how the **Guild of the Silverroad** essentially controls the city—the party tracked down Sadrith at the abandoned **Blackfoot Merchants Guild house**. In a shocking revelation, Sadrith revealed himself to be **[[The Vestige\|The Vestige]]** (also known as the Crowfather), a legendary hero from the Planemeld. He explained he was investigating a **Cult of Clavicus Vile** and suspicious gold that smells of brimstone. The party agreed to help him in exchange for future favors. They followed **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]]**, an Imperial distributing the tainted gold, into the sewers where they witnessed him communicating with a strange humanoid projection via a **Daedric speaking stone**. After a confrontation involving a **Daedric crocodile creature** and the Vestige's intervention, Varren confessed to working for the Guild of the Silverroad and mentioned "**The Grand Architect**." [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] convinced Varren to act as a double agent. The party also discovered that someone high up in the Guild of the Silverroad has been spotted wearing an **Ouroboros necklace**—the symbol of the **Coiled Serpent**. The Vestige agreed to cover the party's lodging for a week.

- He saw through **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s** deceptive behavior

- He is investigating a **Cult of Clavicus Vile**

  - Pays exclusively in **brimstone-tainted gold**

**The Deal:**
- The Vestige handed the party an **extremely detailed sketch of Varren**
- *Terms*: Talk to Varren and learn what happens to the gold he distributes
- *In exchange*: The party can call in a favor from the Vestige later
- *"The more you can do for me, the more I will do for you."*
- The party shook on the deal and the Vestige vanished

**After His Departure:**

### The Sewers

**Following Into the Depths:**
- The party jumped down after him
- Varren walked hurriedly with clear purpose
- Eventually they rounded a corner to find him at a **dead end**
- He was speaking to a **projection**—some kind of one-way communication

**The Projection:**
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** recognized the device as a **speaking stone** (one-way communication)
- However, this one was different—etched with **Daedric runes**
- *The entity in the projection*: Not entirely human—humanoid but strange/distorted
- *Overheard*: Varren reported on his purchases and the amount of gold distributed

**Confrontation:**
- The projection ended
- Varren turned around and saw the party
- When questioned, he claimed to be from **House Tharn**—one of the wealthiest and most powerful families in all of Tamriel
- **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Kirya\|Kirya]]** shot him with a **Paralyze** arrow on him to prevent escape

### Daedric Attack

**The Creature:**
- A **massive bipedal crocodile creature** came charging down the sewer tunnel toward the party
- It was clearly **Daedric** in origin
- Suddenly it recoiled, fell, and skidded to a stop at their feet
- A **dagger** was embedded in the back of its head

**The Vestige Returns:**
- Standing in the tunnel behind them was **The Vestige**
- He stated that Varren had lied about being House Tharn

**Interrogation:**
- The Vestige began threatening and torturing Varren
- Varren defiantly headbutted him and hurled insults
- The Vestige handed **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** a dagger to hold, then vanished with Varren
- They reappeared moments later—Varren was hurled against the wall at terrible speed
- The Vestige, slightly winded, allowed the party to question the now-terrified Varren
