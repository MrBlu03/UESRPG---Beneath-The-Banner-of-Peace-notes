# Session 020 - The Vestige's Bargain

## Summary
The party was summoned to the **[[Info/Locations/Clan_Morvan_Longhouse\|Clan Morvan Longhouse]]** by the Jarl's steward, **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Darius_Karo\|Darius Karo]]**, and offered a bounty to capture or kill a troublesome Dunmer named **Sadrith** who had been accusing citizens of Daedric dealings. After gathering intel from **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Alma\|Alma]]** about the political situation in [[Info/Locations/Kjallgrad\|Kjalgrad]]—specifically how the **[[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]]** essentially controls the city—the party tracked down Sadrith at the abandoned **[[Info/Locations/Blackfoot_Guild_House_Kjalgrad\|Blackfoot Merchants Guild house]]**. In a shocking revelation, Sadrith revealed himself to be **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]]** (also known as the Crowfather), a legendary hero from the Planemeld. He explained he was investigating a **Cult of [[Info/Lore/Clavicus_Vile\|Clavicus Vile]]** and suspicious gold that smells of brimstone. The party agreed to help him in exchange for future favors. They followed **[[Info/Characters/999 NPC's/Varren\|Varren]]**, an Imperial distributing the tainted gold, into the sewers where they witnessed him communicating with a strange humanoid projection via a **Daedric speaking stone**. After a confrontation involving a **Daedric crocodile creature** and the Vestige's intervention, Varren confessed to working for the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]] and mentioned "**The Grand Architect**." [[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] convinced Varren to act as a double agent. The party also discovered that someone high up in the [[Info/Organizations/Guild_of_the_Silverroad\|Guild of the Silverroad]] has been spotted wearing an **Ouroboros necklace**—the symbol of the **[[Info/Lore/Coiled_Serpent\|Coiled Serpent]]**. The Vestige agreed to cover the party's lodging for a week.

- He claimed to have been part of the **Fighters Guild** during the **Planemeld**

- He saw through **[[Info/Characters/001 The Fangs of Fjelhorst/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s** deceptive behavior

**Confrontation:**
