<# [[2 -  Sessions/Session 030\|Session 030]] — Bones, Grief, and a Priest off a Cliff

## Summary

- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** completes two necromancy lessons: she learns the **Bone Shards** spell (Level 1, requires a corpse within 10m) after repeated failures, then travels to the frozen lake where she channels the dying emotions of the Lady of the Lake — experiencing overwhelming grief and agony that leaves her silently weeping for over an hour.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** have a tense conversation about whether necromancy can truly bring back the dead. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s necromancy book physically reacts — pulling away when she insists "it will work because there is no other option" — revealing that the book's condition for staying is pursuit of knowledge, not resurrection.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** sneak into Kilgard through the sewers and visit **Tara's resistance hideout**, which is buzzing with armed protest preparations. Tara reports the **[[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Blackfoot Merchants Guild]]** is moving in on the Silver Road's lost holdings. Tara's people plan to raid the Guild House. They also meet the contact from the warehouse fires, who shows them jars of oil.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]** meets the **unnamed priest of Stendarr** at the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost Gate Hall]] and secures his help breaking wards at the cult facility, promising payment after the job is done and pledging her family name.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]** arrives in Kilgard from the cult facility, melts down captured armor sets into steel ingots, eats sweet pork at the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost Gate Hall]], and has a touching farewell with the **innkeeper**, who gives him the **sweet pork recipe** before leaving to join the protests. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] finds [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s note, gathers all horses ([[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Mossback]], [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]], Rhymefang) plus a cart, and heads to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]'s ruin.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]** have a deep philosophical conversation on the road — covering [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]'s age (112, middle-aged Dunmer), his soul being trapped in Cold Harbour, [[Info/Lore/Molag_Bal\|Molag Bal]], [[Info/Lore/Nocturnal\|Nocturnal]]'s crows, [[Info/Characters/NPCs/Marjon\|Marjon]] the vampire, Arch-Magus Morgan, and whether people can truly be brought back from the dead.
- The party reunites at the **cult facility** (Alton Mesa). [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] talks his way into a guard shift, sneaks the party to the entrance, and throws [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] and the mage up to a 4-metre-high walkway to reach the warded doors.
- The **priest of Stendarr attempts to break the wards** but fails catastrophically — the ward's magic overpowers his restoration beam, creeps up his staff, and **violently flings him off the balcony**. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] catches him. The priest is unconscious with unknown magical residue on him. The session ends on this cliffhanger, with [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] reappearing in smoke to check on the priest — and it's revealed he had a **tincture to dissolve the wards the whole time**.

## Detailed Recap

### Part 1 — Necromancy Lesson: Bone Shards (Afternoon, at the Ruin)

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sat cross-legged in the main chamber of [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]'s ruin with her books and prepared bones laid out. The lesson required her to break a bone, channel magicka into the fracture until the shards strained against one another, then release — the fragments would fly forward, driven by residual energy.

She rolled **Strength** to break the bone (success), then began a series of **Necromancy** rolls at escalating difficulty:

| Attempt | Modifier | Result |
|---------|----------|--------|
| 1 | −10 | Fail — turquoise/green energy formed between the bones but fizzled |
| 2 | −10 | Fail — energy jumped like lightning between the bones, then shot off harmlessly |
| 3 | −20 | Fail — energy burrowed into the bones (like the Infinity Stones creeping onto Tony's hand) but couldn't hold |
| 4 | −25 | **Success** — the bones pulled toward each other, energy surrounded them, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] lost grip as the fragments hovered |

With increasing frustration [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] threw away the first bone and grabbed a new one, smashing it against the wall. By attempt 4, the GM noted that anger was *increasing* the difficulty — the spell requires emotional control.

Once the bone shard was floating, [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] aimed it at the **Vestige**, who was sitting in the room with his back to her. She rolled **Combat Style (Agility) at −20** and succeeded — the shard whizzed past [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]'s bare face, barely grazing him. He didn't even turn around: *"Were you trying to aim it at me? … You'll get there eventually."*

**Lesson complete.** [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] gained the **Bone Shards** spell (Level 1 Necromancy). Requirements: a corpse within 10 metres. The GM also mentioned a future **Bone Armor** spell that similarly requires a nearby corpse, and that a corpse cannot be used twice.

### Part 2 — The Lake: Feeling the Weight of Death (Afternoon)

For the next lesson — *"Seek out a grave or a site of violent death where the spirit has not found rest"* — [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] recalled the **Lady of the Lake**, the ghost of an old king's wife who drowned herself in the river near the ruin. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] asked [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] to come along.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] handed [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] a **healing potion** she'd crafted during downtime and reluctantly agreed to the trip. They spent roughly **1–1.5 hours** retracing their steps through the forest, past the Three Stone Brothers, to the frozen lake. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] navigated using her perfect memory of their previous route.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sat at the lake's edge, dug her hands into the ground, and channelled necromantic energy. The GM asked Blu a critical question: **"What is [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s mindset about necromancy right now?"** Blu answered with a **60/40 split** — 60% driven by the hope of bringing back her family, 40% by genuine fascination with magic and knowledge. The GM noted he would ask this question at every subsequent lesson.

**Necromancy roll (straight):** Success — [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] found the right spot.

**Necromancy roll (−10):** Success — she felt the dead woman's last emotions. First came **grief** — the grief of losing someone to an unknown fate. It burrowed into every part of her psyche, then was immediately destroyed by **pure, willful agony** — the physical pain of drowning, chosen freely, because the woman's husband (the Ahl of the village) was gone.

**Second Necromancy roll (straight):** Success — the emotion dissipated, leaving [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] with the knowledge that the woman died of her own free will.

**Willpower test (+20):** **Failed.** The dead woman's grief pulled a string in [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s mind she didn't want pulled. Her own grief — for her brother, her family — flooded back. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] sat motionless at the lake's edge, tears streaming silently down her cheeks, staring at nothing, for **over an hour**. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] knelt in front of her after ~40 minutes, keeping a respectful distance, asking *"Hello? Is this normal?"* Eventually [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s eyes refocused. She said simply: *"Let's head back. I think I've had it here."*

The GM confirmed that 2 more successful attempts at other death sites are needed before [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] masters this ability.

### Part 3 — "It Has to Work" — The Book Reacts

On the walk back, [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] pressed [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] on whether necromancy could actually bring someone back — whole, with their memories, as they were. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] insisted it must work. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] pointed out that the only known case of someone returning from the dead was [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]], and that wasn't through magic — it was because he became a Daedra.

When [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] said **"It will work because there is no other option,"** the **necromancy book physically reacted** — shaking and pulling away from her, as if trying to escape. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] noticed it glowing between its pages. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] panicked: *"Can we not talk about this before this fucking book flies away and I lose my one shot?"*

This revealed that the book's bond with [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] is conditional — it was given to her under the pretence of *knowledge for knowledge's sake*, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]]'s obsessive fixation on resurrection threatens that arrangement.

### Part 4 — Into Kilgard: Tara, Protests, and the Priest

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] led [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] through the **southern sewer entrance** into the Ratways. At **Tara's hideout**, they found the resistance **actively preparing for armed action** — people sparring, sharpening weapons, Tara visibly stressed. Tara explained:

- Guards had turned violent during the protests after the warehouse fires; **people died**.
- The Ahl is presumed dead or fled after the hall burned.
- The **[[Info/Organizations/Guilds/Black-Foot_Merchant_Guild\|Blackfoot Merchants Guild]]** has begun claiming Silver Road holdings through legal charters.
- Tara's people are preparing to **raid the Guild House** with weapons and oil if public sentiment supports it.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] exited through a sewer passage into a cellar near the **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost Gate Hall]]**. They were guided by Tara's contact from the warehouse fires, who showed them **jars of oil** being stockpiled.

At the [[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost Gate Hall]], [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] met the **unnamed priest of Stendarr** — the Breton mage she'd recruited in a previous session. She apologised for the delay, explained they needed help breaking wards at a facility, and pledged her **family name and insignia** as guarantee of payment. The priest agreed to meet them at the **western gate** outside the city. They also left a **note under [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s door** at the inn, telling him to find them at the ruin.

### Part 5 — Durov's Day: Steel, Sweet Pork, and a Recipe

Meanwhile, [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] was at the cult's facility. He **smelted down captured armor sets** (4 partial iron sets, 24 pieces total — the GM deferred exact ingot calculations). He then rode Rhymefang toward Kilgard.

At the **[[Info/Locations/Kjalgrad/Frost_Gate_Hall\|Frost Gate Hall]]**, the innkeeper looked exhausted — guards had conducted a surprise inspection after the fires, turning the inn upside down. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] ordered his usual **sweet pork** (8 gold, paid 16). The innkeeper sat beside him with a **war axe** and delivered a quiet farewell:

- He gave [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] a **folded piece of paper** — the **sweet pork recipe**.
- He explained the inn might not survive the coming upheaval.
- He was arming citizens and about to join the protests.
- *"May the gods watch over you, friend."*

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] promised to spread the recipe to every inn in every city. They embraced. The innkeeper left for the market with his axe.

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] found [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]'s note, collected **all three horses** (Rhymefang, [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Mossback]], [[Info/Characters/Party/The Horses\|Ashen]]) and a **cart** with his gear, and headed to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]'s ruin.

### Part 6 — Durov and the Vestige: A Walk and a Talk

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] arrived at the ruin just as [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] was leaving for the cult facility. They walked together, and [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] seized the opportunity for a rare, extended conversation:

| Topic | What [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] revealed |

| **Age** | 112 years old — middle-aged for a Dunmer (lifespan ~200–300) |
| **How he became [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]]** | His soul was trapped by a necromancer threatening Tamriel (~18 years ago). He escaped, but his soul stayed in Cold Harbour. His body returned; whenever he died, his body re-materialized at wayshrine anchors or soul gem locations |

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] also showed his face to [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] (removing his helmet for the second time that day). [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] estimated him as less than 30 years old.

### Part 7 — Reunion at the Cult Facility

The party converged at the **Alton Mesa cult facility** at nightfall. [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] arrived with the priest; [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] and [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] came from the ruin. [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|The Vestige]] immediately **vanished** upon seeing the priest, leaving a note: *"I'll be back when the mage is gone."*

[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] talked the **gate guard** into letting him take over the shift (the guard was exhausted), effectively placing himself as the only sentry. He signalled the party in the bushes.

**Reconnaissance (from [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s earlier exploration):** ~10–11 guards awake at any given time, staggered shifts.

The **warded doors** were on a ~4-metre-high walkway carved into the mountainside — two doors, both sealed by **purple-glowing wards**, leading to a ventilation system. There was no path up; the party used **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]]'s strength**:

- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] threw [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] up** (Athletics check — success). She caught the ledge and climbed.
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] threw the priest up** with [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] catching from above (Athletics with advantage — success).
- **[[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]]** was thrown up next (success).

### Part 8 — The Ward Backfires

The priest of Stendarr examined the wards, set up a ritual circle with candles and his staff, and began praying. A beam of light from his staff struck the ward's centre and began dissolving it from the middle.

**[[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] rolled Restoration** to assess the situation — and failed. The ward's magic was **fighting back and winning**. [[Info/Characters/Party/Periwyn\|Periwyn]] grabbed [[Info/Characters/Party/Onasha\|Onasha]] and pulled her back: *"You don't want to be near this."*

The purple enchantment crept up the beam of light, reached the staff, and **erupted violently** — sending the priest flying off the 4-metre balcony. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] (below) caught him with an **Athletics roll** (success, barely). The priest was **unconscious with unknown magical residue** on him. [[Info/Characters/Party/Durov\|Durov]] stashed him in a bush, rolled in the mud near the horses to create an alibi, and resumed his guard post.

The **Vestige** reappeared as smoke near the priest to check on him — and the session ended with the revelation that [[Info/Characters/NPCs/The_Vestige\|the Vestige]] had a **tincture to dissolve the wards the entire time**. The party had never needed the priest.
