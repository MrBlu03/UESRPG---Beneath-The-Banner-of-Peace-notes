Esteemed Lady Lunclaire,

Your family's past deeds have long reached ears far and wide across this continent. From Wayrest to Windhelm, the name Lunclaire has been spoken with respect in halls where coin weighs as heavy as steel. With you arrival in our city, and your interest in building trade relations, we believe this to be a time of opportunity for us both.

I, Guildmaster Jorven Halliksson of the Guild of the Silver Road, hereby invite you to a private benefit gathering in support of Kjallgrad's mercantile future. This event will take place this Turdas in the Jarl's Grand Hall, with which I am told you are already acquainted.

The purpose of the evening is simple in appearance and profound in intent: to secure stronger ties between established trading houses and those who would see Kjallgrad flourish as the beating heart of commerce in the north. Select merchants, caravan masters, and financial stewards will be present; men and women whose signatures can redirect entire trade arteries across Skyrim.

It would honor us greatly if you would attend, accompanied by your trusted guards. In times such as these, visible strength and loyalty are virtues well regarded.

There will be discussion of:
- Expanded caravan routes between High Rock and the Rift
- Preferential tariffs for contributing houses
- Investment opportunities in northern lumber and ore ventures
- And the formation of a more unified mercantile charter for the coming year

Kjallgrad stands at the edge of great prosperity, Lady Lunclaire. Yet prosperity favors those who stand within the circle when the terms are written, not those who arrive after the ink has dried.

Your insight, lineage, and experience would lend the evening a weight befitting its ambition. 

I trust you will consider this invitation not merely as a courtesy, but as a sign of mutual respect between our houses.

May your roads remain profitable and your ledgers ever balanced,

**Jorven Halliksson**
Guildmaster of the Silver Road
Kjallgrad
